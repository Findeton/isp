## 1. What the primary construction establishes

Benincasa and Dowker introduce a one-parameter family of retarded operators on
causal-set scalar fields.  On causal sets approximating four-dimensional
Minkowski spacetime, the operators are Lorentz invariant and nonlocal, carry a
nonlocality scale, and approximate the continuum d'Alembertian on fields that
vary slowly on that scale.  On suitable curved-spacetime approximations they
approach `Box-R/2`, permitting an approximately local causal-set curvature
action:

<https://arxiv.org/abs/1001.2725>

Thus the construction has important physical content beyond D16's arbitrary
interval family: continuum matching constrains a particular linear
combination of interval abundances.
