# D11 literature audit — complete histories, intervention, and extinction

**Search date:** 2026-07-11. This audit was performed after the D11 protocol,
exact engine, and numerical outcome were frozen.

**Round-1 correction:** D11 implements an ordinary Markov growth kernel on an
augmented typed-history state, with a globally normalized event race and
incidence-scoped instruments. It does not implement Barandes's more general
indivisible non-Markovian dynamics, a decentralized local click law, or an
integrated generated-history gauge construction.

## Direct comparisons

1. Jacob Barandes, [The Stochastic-Quantum Correspondence](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.10778)
   and [Quantum Systems as Indivisible Stochastic Processes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.21192).
   These establish the relevance of general non-Markovian stochastic laws and
   Hilbert-space representations. A primitive full-history measure would
   already determine conditional next outcomes. They do not derive SHARD's
   variable carrier types, births, joins, ownership, or token activities from
   sealed records.

2. Chiribella, D'Ariano, and Perinotti,
   [Theoretical framework for quantum networks](https://arxiv.org/abs/0904.4483).
   Quantum combs and link products already provide constructive and axiomatic
   composition of memory-bearing quantum networks. D11's instruments and
   endpoint composition are not a new general formalism.

3. Barrett, Lorenz, and Oreshkov,
   [Quantum Causal Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/1906.10726).
   This makes interventions and quantum processes central to causal
   structure. It supports D11's refusal to identify positivity or correlation
   with causation. D11's growing record ontology is a separate model choice.

4. Classical critical branching theory already proves eventual extinction
   under standard hypotheses; see, for example,
   [Extinction probability for a critical general branching process](https://doi.org/10.1016/0304-4149(74)90021-0).
   D11 does not claim the general extinction theorem. Its contribution is the
   exact reduction of the frozen SCIR token race to a supermartingale with an
   even stronger local downward bias whenever a JOIN is enabled.

## Originality boundary

The individual mathematics is precedented. The defensible internal result is
the diagnosis that a complete global sequential kernel can pass a separate
Lorentz-gauge template, instrument, ownership, prefix-normalization, and
algebraic-containment checks while failing universe growth by an exact
population theorem. No external priority
claim is made for the synthesis, the common-future formula, or the proposed
`SEAL -> COMMIT` type repair until a dedicated broader search and peer review.

## Consequence for Barandes/ISP

Full-history non-Markovianism answers “what happens next?” only after the
history space and its measure are supplied. It does not say which objects can
be born, whether a durable record has a continuation output, or which local
incidences exist. D11 makes that remaining freedom explicit. The extinction
result is therefore not a criticism of ISP; it is a falsification of one
particular globally raced variable-record kernel built on top of it.
