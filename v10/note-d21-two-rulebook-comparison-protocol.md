# D21 protocol — two complete finite rulebooks at one record interface

**Frozen before executable construction and numerical evaluation:** 2026-07-12.

## Goal

Construct two genuinely inequivalent, mathematically complete finite
whole-history laws that create three interacting sealed records through the
same operational interface without a global commit clock; verify every shared
structural gate; and identify the first question on which their predictions
differ.

The broader universe-law objective is not declared complete unless the finite
packets are lifted to independently calibrated physical generators with
variable carrier birth, matter, gravity, units and untouched empirical data.

**Post-audit scope disposition:** the frozen phrase “three interacting sealed
records” was not achieved literally.  The Q source contains two explicit
carrier interactions and both candidates create three sealed, correlated
spacelike outputs, but those output records do not dynamically act on one
another.  The protocol wording is preserved here; the unmet clause remains an
opening rather than being silently redefined.

## Frozen carrier and causal organization

One source region `S` has three mutually spacelike output regions `A,B,C`.
Each output admits a locally chosen Pauli instrument `X`, `Y` or `Z` and emits
one durable binary record `a,b,c in {-1,+1}`.  The source precedes all three
outputs; the outputs have no causal order among themselves.  Local outcome
projectors act on separate tensor factors and must therefore commute.  Any
serial evaluation order is bookkeeping gauge.

The finite query interface is

```text
(local settings x,y,z; any already sealed outcome subset)
    -> joint/marginal probabilities
    -> every positive next-record conditional
    -> final protected classical outcome registers.
```

1. Hermiticity, unit trace and positivity.
2. Complete normalization for all `3^3` local setting triples.

   every proper-subsystem observable.

SHARD holdout.  They may adjudicate the parameter-free microscopic hard-seal
