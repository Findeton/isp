## 1. What (H1) is, and why the previous route died

**(H1):** at d42a scope, for all histories `h, h'` of ANY depth,
`sigma(h) = sigma(h')` implies `menu(h) = menu(h')` as renamed
event-multisets with exact weights.

**REFUTED ROUTE — do not re-walk it.**  D46a tried to define per-actor
own-view abstractions `tau_A, tau_B` (the `sigma` construction applied
to the own view) and prove `tau` a function of `sigma`.  Its own round
refuted the framing: **`tau` is not an own-view object**, and the menu
view strictly exceeds the noop cone on **1,016 of 12,942** actor-
histories at depth <= 5 (7.9%), by at most 4 events, **all
OPPONENT-AUTHORED**.

## 2. The structural reading this pin is built on

Read from the committed admission layer rather than assumed:

- `admissible(acts, e)` builds `view = View(acts+[e], pred, pred[j])`
  — **the view generated by the CANDIDATE EVENT'S OWN CAUSAL PAST.**
  For an idle `('n', a)` that is a's noop cone; for `('p', a, b, x)`
  and `('r', a, C, W)` it additionally pulls in the wires the event
  touches, which is exactly why the menu view can exceed the cone.
- The menu reads that view through **exactly four projections**:
  `view.holdings(a)`, `view.superseded`, `view.live` / `view.props`,
  and `view.components()`.

`sigma` records exactly those four kinds of data, on the FULL view.

> **THEREFORE (H1) REDUCES TO ONE QUESTION, and it is much coarser
> than the refuted one: are the FOUR MENU-RELEVANT PROJECTIONS,
> evaluated on each candidate's own view, determined by `sigma(h)`?**
> `tau` being sigma-determined would have sufficed; it is false.  This
> is weaker and may still be true — that is the whole bet.

## 3. Pre-registered forms, and the decision rule

- **MV-STRONG.**  For every candidate `e`, the four projections
  evaluated on `pred[e]` **equal** those evaluated on the FULL view.
  Then menus are full-view functions and (H1) is immediate.
  *Pre-registered expectation: FALSE for idles*, whose view is the
  bare cone and can genuinely miss an opponent's live proposal.
- **MV-WEAK.**  The four projections on `pred[e]` are a function of
  `(sigma(h), a, event-shape)` — determined, though not equal to the
  full-view values.  **This is the target.**  It suffices for (H1).
- **MV-FAIL.**  Two histories with equal `sigma` give different
  menu-relevant projections for some candidate.  Then (H1) is FALSE
  as stated at d42a scope, residue 1 does not close by this route,
  and **the counterexample is the deliverable.**

**Decision rule, binding:** the receipt reports which of the three
holds; no form may be claimed whose gate did not run at a non-empty
stratum; and the all-depth statement requires the §5 structural
argument discharged, never the sweep alone.

## 5. The structural argument to discharge (the depth-free part)

Computation at any finite depth is evidence, not proof.  The proof
obligation is:

> **For a candidate event `e` by actor `a` touching base `b`, the view
> `pred[e]` contains every live proposal on `b`** — because those
> proposals are carried on the version wire `b`, `e` touches wire `b`,
> and the event poset is the carrier-wise wire closure.  Hence the
> only data missing from `pred[e]` lives on wires irrelevant to the
> four projections at `b`, and **the lag is MENU-INVISIBLE.**

If that holds for `p`/`r` candidates, and the idle weight's
cone-level `has_p`/`has_r` are separately shown sigma-determined,
(H1) follows **at every depth with no induction on depth**.  That is
what makes this route worth attempting: it removes the depth
parameter rather than climbing it.
