# D13 literature audit — how much of the action is forced?

**Status:** final three-round-reviewed primary-source audit, 2026-07-11.

## 1. Boundary amplitudes solve the clock problem, not selection

Oeckl's [general-boundary formulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0306025)
associates state spaces to boundaries and amplitudes to finite spacetime
regions; its developed axioms recover ordinary quantum mechanics as a
special case and impose composition/gluing
([foundations](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0509122)).  This is almost exactly
the mathematical type a sealed-diamond theory needs: no preferred global
time, tensor composition for disjoint regions, and contraction over a shared
boundary.  The axioms permit many quantum field theories.  They define what a
law is, not which functor nature uses.

The D13 boundary-amplitude proposal is therefore not claimed as original.
The corpus-specific point is the identification of SHARD's lower/upper
screens and eventless collars with the input/output/general boundaries, and
of its construction-order gauge with functorial gluing independence.

## 2. Histories and durable records

Gell-Mann and Hartle's [strong-decoherence
condition](https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/9509054) makes generalized records
permanent as histories are extended.  It supplies exactly the distinction D13
needs: amplitudes of unresolved histories interfere, while sufficiently
decoherent recorded alternatives obey probability sum rules and retain a
stable past.  It does not select the Hamiltonian, initial state, coarse
graining, or record operators.

Quantum combs and process tensors similarly give complete containers for
multi-time, memory-bearing dynamics—respectively
[Chiribella–D'Ariano–Perinotti](https://arxiv.org/abs/0904.4483) and the
process-tensor construction of
[Pollock et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1512.00589).  They support D12's result:
tomography can identify a supplied non-Markov process, but the container does
not generate its physical entries.

## 3. Locality and consistency strongly restrict sectors

Deser's [self-interaction
derivation](https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/0411023) shows that locality and
gauge consistency bootstrap the nonlinear Einstein and Yang–Mills structures
from their linear gauge fields.  This is a genuine selector once the massless
spin, representation, derivative assumptions and starting fields are fixed.
It does not select the field list, Newton scale, cosmological constant,
matter couplings, vacuum, or UV completion.

Lovelock's [metric uniqueness theorem](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.1665613)
likewise isolates Einstein plus cosmological terms in four
dimensions under a metric-only, local, divergence-free, second-order set of
hypotheses.  It is a low-derivative gravity uniqueness theorem, not a theorem
that nature must satisfy those hypotheses.  Effective field theory admits
higher-curvature operators.

The correct interpretation is: consistency can uniquely complete a chosen
massless gauge seed; it does not choose the seed or its numerical couplings.

## 4. Effective field theory proves the coefficient problem is real

Donoghue's [gravity effective-field-theory
construction](https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/9512024) separates universal
long-distance quantum effects from unknown short-distance coefficients.  The
Standard Model EFT has many independent gauge-invariant higher operators;
the [Warsaw basis](https://arxiv.org/abs/1008.4884) is an explicit complete
dimension-six classification modulo redundancies.  These are not defects in
the formalism.  They are physically inequivalent coefficients that symmetry
allows and experiment constrains.

Thus the most general empirically licensed low-energy packet has schematic
form

```math
S_{EFT}=\int d^4x\sqrt{-g}\left[
{M_P^2\over2}(R-2\Lambda)+\mathcal L_{SM}
+\sum_i {c_i\mathcal O_i\over\Lambda_{UV}^{d_i-4}}
\right]+S_{boundary}.
```

The formula fixes an architecture and power counting.  It does not derive
`M_P`, `Lambda`, Standard Model masses/mixings/couplings, the Wilson
coefficients `c_i`, or the cosmological state.

## 5. Causal sets supply a serious geometric action, not a complete universe

Benincasa and Dowker construct a causal-set d'Alembertian and scalar curvature
whose continuum behavior yields an approximately local causal-set action
([paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1001.2725)).  Rideout and Sorkin derive a
family—not a single member—of classical sequential-growth laws from discrete
general covariance and causality
([paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/9904062)).  Together they show both sides
of the D13 issue:

- order-based covariance can motivate a gravitational action;
- covariance and causality still leave couplings, measure choices and whole
  families of dynamics.

The BD action also assumes or selects a dimension, discreteness/nonlocality
scale, summation measure and contour before becoming a quantum law.  It does
not provide the Standard Model, boundary state, or durable-record rule.

## 6. Information actions are inference after physical statistics

Maximum entropy/caliber and KL projection select a least-committal
distribution relative to specified constraints and a specified reference.
[Jaynes's original formulation](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRev.106.620)
makes this dependence on stated information explicit.
D12 already gives the exact refusal: without an interaction constraint,
maximum entropy selects independence; supplying the correlation supplies the
missing physics.  V7's RN/KL center work is therefore a principled inference
or effective-action construction once physical response channels and masses
are known, not a microscopic selector.

## 7. Amplitude bootstrap and empirical identification

Unitarity, analyticity, crossing, factorization, positivity and soft behavior
can isolate special amplitudes in restricted sectors.  They do not generally
collapse the full theory space to one point; the
[S-matrix bootstrap survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.02421) documents an
allowed-space program with special boundary theories.  The exact D13
exchange family provides a finite sealed-diamond version of the same fact.

Empirically, the Standard Model plus general relativity is the strongest
low-energy action packet.  Calling it the complete click law would exceed the
evidence: quantum gravity, cosmological boundary data, dark sectors, the
measurement/record ontology and the UV action are not thereby fixed.

## 8. Live selector programs checked after the initial audit

Two routes deserve more than a one-line dismissal.

First, asymptotic safety can turn some infrared parameters into predictions if
the ultraviolet fixed point and its finite-dimensional critical surface are
established.  The Higgs-mass analysis of
[Shaposhnikov and Wetterich](https://arxiv.org/abs/0912.0208) is a concrete
example of this ambition, and
[gravity–Yang–Mills fixed-point work](https://arxiv.org/abs/1710.04669)
exhibits nontrivial ultraviolet restrictions.  This is a possible future
empirical selector, not a current derivation of the complete action: the
matter content and theory space are inputs; relevant directions remain;
truncation, regulator and scheme stability must be controlled; a boundary
state and record instrument are outside the fixed-point calculation.  D13
therefore keeps asymptotic safety as a named opening rather than claiming it
has been refuted.

Second, causal-set phase dynamics has evidence for suppressing large bad
classes.  [Carlip, Carlip and Surya](https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.00327) show
strong path-integral suppression of Kleitman–Rothschild orders and evidence
for a wider class.  This directly addresses V8–V9's manifoldlikeness wall and
is more relevant than a classical Gibbs action.  It still does not uniquely
select the full causal-set quantum measure, matter content, boundary
condition, scales, or record rule, and suppression of known bad classes is
not yet a theorem that the surviving phase is our `3+1` universe.  It is the
strongest external reason not to declare the geometric-action avenue dead.

Quantum reconstructions select the probability calculus/state-space
architecture from operational axioms.  Like SHARD's complex-cone results,
they do not ordinarily determine a Hamiltonian or the numerical interaction
couplings.  They support the architecture/coefficients distinction rather
than close it.

## 9. Originality boundary

The following pieces are established mathematics or physics and carry no D13
priority claim:

```text
general-boundary amplitude gluing;
decoherence-functional records;
polar/RN decomposition of complex amplitudes;
EFT operator families;
Lovelock/Deser low-derivative consistency;
causal-set BD and sequential-growth frameworks.
```

The core iSWAP-family nonuniqueness witness was already established in D12;
D13 claims no priority for it.  D13's contribution is narrower: the fixed-
operational-interval action reading, the corpus/literature action-route audit,
and the boundary-amplitude proposal as a bridge from physical dynamics to
sealed records.  Its universality is not established.
