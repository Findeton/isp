## 1. The question

chart with large overlaps — a TILING — the way its courier records
carry one rich sky?  v9's toys did this by construction outside the
law; the question is whether the law itself admits it.

## 2. The committed blueprints (constructions, not searches)

- **CRYSTAL-1D (the brick wall).**  m ring-adjacent actors.  Opening:
  A1 proposes on v0 and self-arbitrates, minting v1; v1 is broadcast
  along the ring by chained deliveries.  Then the BRICK CIRCUIT:
  alternating rounds of re-deliveries of v1 between even pairs
  (A1↔A2, A3↔A4, …) and odd pairs (A2↔A3, …).  Each delivery covers
  its two wires' previous events — the standard 1+1 brick lattice
  worn as a record.  Re-delivery is admissible and physical (d42b1
  docstring), so the circuit can run indefinitely.
- **CRYSTAL-2D (the grid).**  k×k actors; four phase types cycling
  (row-even, row-odd, column-even, column-odd deliveries of v1).
  The 2+1 analogue.

Every event menu-selected (the corrected D55 instrument: restricted
menus INCLUDE the delivery receiver; membership-equivalence gated).
