# D15 protocol — select the maximal physical action packet evidence warrants

**Status:** frozen before D15 derivations, numerical fits, and holdout access,
2026-07-11.

## 1. Question

D13 proved record principles do not select a local kernel.  D14 proves, at
most conditionally, how supplied regional amplitudes and instruments generate
durable projective histories.  D15 asks the next honest question:

> What is the simplest local generally covariant regional action packet that
> independent physical evidence actually selects, at what energy/length
> scope is it unique, and exactly where do UV dynamics, records, couplings,
> dimension and scales remain underdetermined?

D15 may identify a maximal low-energy effective rulebook without calling it a
fundamental law.  It may report fundamental nonselection if multiple UV
packets remain empirically viable.

## 4. Frozen gates

### S0 — effective versus fundamental scope

Every theorem must specify energy/length domain, regulator, truncation and
whether spacetime dimension/signature are inputs or predictions.

### S1 — gravity normal form

Within the stated low-energy assumptions, derive the maximal uniqueness
claim supported by locality, massless spin two, gauge consistency and
diffeomorphism covariance.  Distinguish the Einstein-Hilbert leading term
from the symmetry-allowed higher-curvature EFT tower.

### S2 — matter normal form

Given the observed 3+1 field representations and gauge group, enumerate the
renormalizable Standard Model action and the symmetry-allowed higher-
dimension operators.  Field content, gauge group, Yukawas, mixing angles,
theta terms, masses and Wilson coefficients may not be called derived unless
an independent selector fixes them.

### S3 — coupling and scale ledger

Classify every parameter as:

```text
fixed by normalization/convention;
related by symmetry/consistency;
renormalization-group initial datum;
measured empirical coefficient;
predicted from a UV fixed point/model;
still free.
```

In particular determine whether `G`, the cosmological constant, Higgs scale,
gauge couplings and record/coarse-graining scales are set or measured.

### S4 — action-to-D14 dictionary

Construct at least one regulated nontrivial regional action example whose
boundary kernel, gluing, state and local record instrument enter the repaired
D14 bridge.  Gauge fixing, edge modes and record environment must be explicit.
An `exp(iS)` slogan does not pass.

### S5 — autonomous records

Either derive the pointer/decoherence instrument from the same action, state
and environment, or grade it as an extra supplied coarse graining.  Exact
unitarity plus an arbitrarily declared pointer basis is insufficient.

### S6 — locality, joining and no global clock

Regional composition must use only owned boundary data and explicit join
entitlements.  Evaluation-schedule gauge is not a generative support law.
State whether the packet sums complete diagrams or generates them locally.

### S7 — 3+1, cones and influence

State separately whether `3+1` and Lorentzian signature are assumed, selected
by a phase, or predicted.  Derive the candidate's influence dispersion and
cone observable before any V9 comparison.  A continuum action that assumes a
Lorentz metric does not explain emergent round cones or dimension.

### S8 — empirical selection

Use independent data to reject or constrain candidates.  Parameter fitting
and model selection must be separated.  No dataset used to choose a model,
operator basis, threshold or coefficient may be called a holdout.

### S9 — untouched prediction

Before opening a designated holdout, freeze the observable, units, nuisance
handling, pass/fail threshold and candidate predictions.  If no candidate is
complete enough to make a non-tunable prediction, the holdout remains closed.

### S10 — UV survivor audit

Exhibit whether two inequivalent UV actions can share every established
low-energy coefficient and tested observable.  If so, fundamental uniqueness
is not proved even when the effective action is selected.

### S11 — hostile closure

Independent mathematics/EFT, ontology/locality and clean-room/empirical
reviews must attack assumptions, parameter counting, action dictionaries,
data leakage and verdict scope.  New openings are repaired before the next
round.
