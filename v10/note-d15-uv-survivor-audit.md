**Status:** pre-hostile-review candidate ledger, 2026-07-11.

## 1. Decision rule

A candidate survives if present evidence does not refute it.  It passes D15
only if it supplies every field in the frozen action packet, rejects live
rivals by independent evidence and makes a non-tunable untouched prediction.
Survival is much weaker than selection.

## 3. `EFT4`: selected form, unselected completion

The low-energy gravity sector is constrained because a local massless spin-two
field cannot be coupled consistently while keeping only its linear theory;
self-coupling completes it to Einstein gravity at leading derivative order.
Gravity then functions as an effective field theory at ordinary energies.
The observed matter representations and gauge group similarly determine the
renormalizable Standard Model operator form, while leaving its empirical
parameters.

This is the only candidate in the ledger already selected across a broad
experimental domain.  It nevertheless assumes the continuum `3+1`
Lorentzian carrier whose emergence SHARD wants to explain.  It cannot use its
own round continuum cone as evidence that a record graph dynamically becomes
round.

Primary anchors:

- <https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/0411023>
- <https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/9512024>
- <https://arxiv.org/abs/1008.4884>
- <https://arxiv.org/abs/1710.05834>

## 4. `BDQ`: closest ontology, missing quantum selector

The causal-set route is closest to SHARD's event/record web ontology.  The
Benincasa–Dowker construction gives retarded Lorentz-invariant nonlocal
operators on causal sets approximating four-dimensional Minkowski spacetime
and an approximately local curvature action on manifoldlike sets:
<https://arxiv.org/abs/1001.2725>.

But an action functional is not a completed quantum measure.  Classical
sequential growth itself yields a family under covariance/causality axioms,
not one selected dynamics:
<https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/9904062>.

For D15, `BDQ` still needs one selected complex quantum measure, a viable
matter/state sector, autonomous records, a component-join law, a physical
nonlocality/discreteness scale and a demonstrated stable `3+1` Lorentzian
phase.  Until then it cannot open the V9 holdout.

## 5. `ASQ`: strongest coefficient-selector program, not closed

Asymptotic safety is the clearest live route by which apparently free EFT
coefficients could become coordinates on a finite-dimensional UV critical
surface, with some low-energy quantities predicted rather than fitted.  The
2026 review reports robust Euclidean pure-gravity fixed-point evidence,
increasingly realistic matter studies and recent Lorentzian progress:
<https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.21522>.

D15 must not turn that progress report into a theorem of a unique universe
trajectory.  A passing packet would need regulator/truncation stability,
realistic Lorentzian matter and state, a regional-amplitude map, autonomous
records, calibrated scales and a frozen prediction not used to choose the
trajectory.  Those fields are not presently filled in the SHARD corpus.

## 6. `SFQ`: regional amplitudes without selected continuum physics

Spin foams directly implement regional boundary amplitudes, making them a
natural D14 source architecture.  The EPRL precursor derives a four-
dimensional vertex from a quantized Regge discretization of general
relativity and matches loop-gravity boundary kinematics:
<https://arxiv.org/abs/0708.1236>.

That establishes neither a unique refinement limit nor the observed matter
sector, cosmological state, record instrument or measured couplings.  Four
dimensions are part of this candidate's construction, not a prediction from
sealed records.  Consequently `SFQ` survives but does not select the goal law.

## 7. Pairwise discriminability

All four physical candidate classes can be arranged to share the same
leading Einstein–Standard-Model effective action over an observed low-energy
domain.  If their higher operators lie below present bounds, established
low-energy data cannot distinguish them.  This is not yet a formal theorem
that two **complete** UV packets share all observations, because the candidate
packets are incomplete.  It is enough to block a claim that current IR
agreement uniquely selects one UV action.

The next empirical step must therefore target an observable for which:

```text
candidate A and candidate B both make fixed predictions;
their nuisance/coupling fits are frozen elsewhere;
the predictions differ by more than the stated uncertainty;
the comparison data were not used to construct either packet.
```

No such cross-candidate SHARD cone/dimension observable is currently
available.  The correct S9 action is to keep the V9 holdout closed.
