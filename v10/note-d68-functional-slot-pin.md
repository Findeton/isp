## 1. The question

functional; the generated line has, at closed scope, a measure
(μ_Ẑ) on record classes and nothing underneath it.  **Does the
generated law admit a functional level at all — and if so, how big
is the space of them?**  Concretely: over the finite truncated
history space, measure the set of Hermitian forms `D(h, h')` that

- **(C1, the diagonal demand)** reproduce the generated record
  measure on the record diagonal: `Σ_{h,h' ∈ r} D(h, h') = μ_Ẑ(r)`
  for every record class `r` (the decoherent-sum reading), AND the
  per-class variant (`D` block-diagonal across record classes with
  block traces `μ_Ẑ(r)`) — BOTH conventions computed and reported;
- **(C2, strong positivity)** are positive semidefinite;
- **(C3, cylinder consistency)** are consistent across one depth

  history extension, in the paper-29 refined-cylinder sense —
  operationalized and printed);
- **(C4, equivariance)** are invariant under the layer's renaming
  group (the canon automorphisms).

The deliverable is the EXISTENCE and the DIMENSION structure of
that set at truncation depths 3 and 4 (5 if tractable): empty (a
wall — the generated law admits no quantum extension of this
