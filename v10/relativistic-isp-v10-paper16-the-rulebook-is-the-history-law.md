## Abstract

SHARD begins with finite sealed records, local evidence, diamonds and a demand
that physical evolution not depend on a universe-wide commit clock.  The
remaining generative question is whether those principles select the law by
which new interacting records arise.  This paper gives a negative answer at
the fundamental-selection level and a positive answer at the conditional
operational level.

First, one fixed relabeling-invariant finite causal action is shown to admit
inequivalent positive boundary/orbit kernels that propagate through actual
owned causal extensions, local carried memory and durable records into
different projective non-Markov laws.  The action therefore does not select
the history measure.  Second, on a finite typed event algebra, a supplied
normalized strongly-positive decoherence functional determines every
decoherent event probability and positive-cylinder conditional; no second
click lottery is needed.  Third, an exact rank-null construction proves that
all one- and two-record observations can agree while a higher history
correlation and future conditional differ.  Finite local shadows therefore
need not identify the complete history law, much less its physical generator.

The strongest supported rulebook is consequently conditional: a whole-history
functional is the operational law, while a local explanatory construction also
requires a history/field domain, gauge measure or contour, action and
coefficients, boundary state and record dynamics.  Present evidence selects
the Standard Model plus Einstein-Hilbert effective baseline over its tested
domain, not a unique ultraviolet completion or SHARD derivation of `G`, 3+1
spacetime or round cones.  The V9 geometry holdout must remain closed.

## 1. The question

If a complete non-Markov probability law `mu` on record histories is primitive,
then

```math

```

already is the next-record law.  The universe does not first consult `mu` and
then roll a second click die.  The derivation problem exists only if SHARD
wants to obtain `mu` from sealed records, evidence and diamond composition.

For quantum histories the corresponding primitive is not generally an
ordinary fine-path probability.  It is a decoherence functional `D`, with
ordinary probabilities licensed on decoherent durable-record partitions.
The physical question is therefore:

```text
Do SHARD kinematics select D or a local generator of D?
```
