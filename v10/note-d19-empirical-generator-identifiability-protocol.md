# D19 protocol — can independent evidence select the generator?

**Status:** frozen before D19 outcomes, 2026-07-12.

## 1. Question

D17 proves that an action does not select the history kernel.  D18 identifies
the finite operational core `(E,D)` and separates it from a local explanatory
generator.  D19 asks:

> Can existing independent evidence select one physical equivalence class of
> complete generators, or only a low-energy/observational equivalence class?

The target generator fields are

```text
G = (history/domain and fields, gauge measure/contour, local action and
     coefficients, boundary/cosmological state, record dynamics,
     operational unit/scale dictionary).
```

## 2. Identification map

Let

```text
generator G -> history functional D_G -> observed data vector y_train.
```

Two generators are observationally equivalent on the training evidence when
they give the same `y_train`, after quotienting actual gauge/field-redefinition
equivalence.  Unique empirical selection requires this map to be injective on
the frozen candidate class.  A simplicity score may rank survivors only after
its description language and prior are frozen; it is not a physical theorem.

## 3. Frozen gates

### I0 — candidate class before data reuse

List the generator class and every adjustable field before applying the
training evidence.  Adding a field after seeing a miss is model revision, not
prediction.

### I1 — equivalence quotient

Separate:

```text
gauge/relabel/field-redefinition equivalence;
same observed data but different holdout prediction;
same finite EFT but different ultraviolet completion;
genuinely different state or record dynamics.
```

### I2 — exact finite identifiability theorem

For a finite history carrier, represent observations as linear/affine
functionals of `D`.  Compute the rank and null space exactly.  A positive
interior functional perturbed along a nonzero invisible Hermitian direction
must remain valid for a finite interval and change a declared holdout.

### I3 — physical evidence ledger

For every generator field, label it:

```text
independently measured;
fixed only within a chosen EFT/convention;
bounded but not identified;
unobserved/open;
derived by a proved theorem.
```

The Standard Model plus Einstein-Hilbert EFT may be credited only at its
tested energy/curvature scope.  Its field content, coefficients, state and
cutoff status must remain visible.

### I4 — state versus law

Do not fit cosmological initial/boundary state parameters and call them action
coefficients.  A universe prediction needs both.

### I5 — record/coarse semantics

Show whether durable record variables follow dynamically from the candidate
or are supplied as the observational interface.  Tomography of an accessible
process does not select inaccessible cosmic histories.

### I6 — locality and covariance

The selected generator must have regional factorization/sewing and no
observable dependence on a global construction order.  Agreement only on one
preferred sampler does not pass.

### I7 — scale and `G`

Separate dimensionless record odds from the operational unit bridge.
Measured `G`, `hbar` and `c` can calibrate a low-energy packet; calibration is
not emergence from dimensionless records.

### I8 — holdout discipline

Freeze at least one prediction not used for candidate invention, parameter
fit, architecture choice or stopping.  A post-selected V9 cone/dimension
result is training evidence unless an untouched dataset remains.

### I9 — geometry gate

Influence speed, cone score `F`, dimension, scale ladder and gravity may be
tested only when cross-candidate values are fixed before access.  If 3+1,
Lorentz signature or Einstein gravity is in the candidate definition, its
appearance is recovery/calibration, not emergence.

### I10 — hostile closure

Independent mathematics, physical-identifiability and clean-room reviewers
must attack null directions, parameter reuse, equivalence classes, data
leakage and overclaims.

## 5. Verdicts

```text
FUNDAMENTAL-GENERATOR-IDENTIFIED
  one physical equivalence class survives and passes untouched predictions;

EFFECTIVE-GENERATOR-IDENTIFIED
  one low-energy equivalence class is selected at a declared cutoff/scope,
  while state or ultraviolet completion remains open;

EMPIRICAL-GENERATOR-NONSELECTION
  explicit inequivalent positive generators agree on all frozen training
  evidence and differ on a legal holdout;

INCOMPLETE-INVESTIGATION
  class, rank/null proof, evidence ledger, reviews or holdout is missing.
```

An empirical nonselection theorem would not prove that nature has no exact
law.  It would prove that the present corpus and evidence do not uniquely
identify it, locating precisely which new observation must discriminate the
survivors.
