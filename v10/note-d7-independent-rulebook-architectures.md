# D7 independent construction ledger — candidate rulebook architectures before external search

**Frozen:** 2026-07-11, after the v1–v9 internal audit and before the targeted
external literature/priority search. These constructions are not claimed
original. The later literature section must classify their precedents and
cannot rewrite this timestamped ledger to manufacture novelty.

## 1. Internal-corpus diagnosis

Across v1–v9, the same gap appears in different languages:

```text
v1-v3: endpoint/projective kernels and exchange defects are supplied;
v3-v4: actual-law tables, supports, scores, and residual values are missing;
v5: full non-Markovian law is ontologically allowed but not selected;
v6: complete history cochains identify a supplied law; commitment is
    conditional on primitive modes/evidence;
v7: martingales, sufficiency, centers, shadows, and h-transforms constrain a
    supplied candidate domain/weight;
v8: the click law answers when given content; placement/root/branching remains
    the deepest open sector, and Gibbs move scores are model choices;
v9: a quotient-covariant marked Poisson-support process exists conditional on
    its support/exposure law; shared allocation, transfer, root, and branch
    remain free.
```

The missing physics is therefore not another readout. It is the law that
assigns a null-inclusive measure to typed extension packages.

## 2. Architecture A — direct covariant extension kernel

Take the D7 specification literally. Define the physical state as a canonical
unlabeled typed marked history `H`, define its physical extension space
`Ext(H)`, and posit or derive

$$
K_H(d\xi).
$$

This is the minimal answer. It does not require an auxiliary global scheduler.
If `K` is measurable, normalized, locally finite, isomorphism-covariant, and
projectively consistent, it generates a path measure from a root law.

### Strengths

1. It contains proposal, null mass, root, bridge, marks, and outcomes in one
   typed object.
2. It permits full-history dependence without assuming finite Markov memory.
3. It makes construction order auxiliary by working directly on canonical
   histories.

### Failure as a derivation

`K` is the rulebook itself. This architecture characterizes closure but gives
no principle selecting its values.

## 3. Architecture B — local transfer weights plus a positive harmonic correction

Define a positive local transfer weight for a proposed extension:

$$
w_H(\xi)
=
a_{\tau(\xi)}
\mathbf 1_{\rm adm}(H,\xi)
\exp[-\Delta_\xi\Phi(H)]
c_H(\xi).
$$

Here:

- `a_tau` is a root/type/bridge activity;
- `1_adm` enforces typed incidence and conservation;
- `Delta Phi` is a record-carried collar/action increment;
- `c_H` is any conditional commitment/mark factor already justified.

Let the transfer operator be

$$
(Tf)(H)=\sum_{\xi\in\operatorname{Ext}(H)}w_H(\xi)f(H+\xi).
$$

If a positive function `h` and level factor `lambda_H` solve

$$
Th(H)=\lambda_H h(H),
$$

then the normalized extension law is the positive transform

$$
K_H(\xi)
=
\frac{w_H(\xi)h(H+\xi)}{\lambda_Hh(H)}.
$$

This factorization unifies four internal objects:

```text
D5 local supplied factors -> w
v6 commitment/evidence    -> part of c
v7 positive h-transform   -> h-normalization
v9 marked support process -> extension types and exposures.
```

### Construction-order condition

For two gauge-commuting packages, the `h` factors telescope. Equality of the
two physical path weights reduces to flatness of the local transfer cocycle:

$$
w_H(\xi)w_{H+\xi}(\eta)
=
w_H(\eta)w_{H+\eta}(\xi),
$$

plus equality of any level normalizers shared by the two paths. A nonzero
defect means either:

1. the extensions are not gauge-commuting and their order is physical; or
2. the proposed local weights fail construction-order gauge.

### Strengths

1. It separates local physical increments from global/projective
   normalization.
2. The `h` field can represent a future boundary condition or projective
   consistency class without placing the entire future in one local record.
3. Exact factor messages may compute `h` locally when a supplied factorization
   has finite separators.
4. Root and bridge activities are visible rather than hidden.

### Open/free data

The activities, extension type system, action `Phi`, commitment factor, and
positive harmonic branch are not selected. If `h` is simply computed from a
supplied full path measure, the construction is circular. If `h` requires an
unbounded global future lookup with no recorded separator implementation, it
violates the locality target.

### Provisional assessment

This is the strongest structural architecture. It is a rulebook compiler and
consistency factorization, not yet a unique physical rulebook.

## 4. Architecture C — closure-defect-driven proposals

Let `epsilon_H(xi)` measure the no-silent boundary defect that would remain if
candidate `xi` were absent or present. Propose extensions using, for example,

$$
w_H(\xi)
=a_{\tau(\xi)}F(\epsilon_H(\xi)),
$$

or an action improvement

$$
w_H(\xi)=a_{\tau(\xi)}
\exp[-\beta(\Phi(H+\xi)-\Phi(H))].
$$

### Attractive idea

Records would be born where existing collars fail to screen future response.
This makes “no silent history” active rather than merely diagnostic.

### Exact obstructions

1. `epsilon_H(xi)` cannot be evaluated until `xi` is enumerated.
2. Future relevance is defined relative to a future law, creating a circular
   dependence if that law is not independently available.
3. A complete/empty seed may have zero defect and never nucleate its first
   record or bridge.
4. The function `F`, activity, scale `beta`, norm, and tie law remain free.
5. Minimizing defect can drive lookup-like boundary growth rather than finite
   physical records.

### Verdict

Useful as a correction/eligibility term inside Architecture B, insufficient
as the first proposal source.

## 5. Architecture D — residual-capacity reaction law

Give each record a finite residual capacity vector `r_i`. A typed extension is
a reaction consuming compatible ports and producing new records:

$$
\sum_i\nu^-_{i\alpha}R_i
\longrightarrow
\sum_j\nu^+_{j\alpha}R_j.
$$

Assign propensity

$$
\lambda_\alpha(H)
=k_\alpha
G_\alpha(\{r_i\}_{i\in\partial\alpha})
\mathbf1_{\rm adm}.
$$

The next reaction and no-event survival follow from the total propensity. A
multileg reaction is an explicit bridge package. Disjoint reactions commute
at the record level; overlapping reactions record order through shared ports.

### Strengths

1. Candidate scope and physical carrier legs are explicit.
2. Conservation and capacity are naturally typed.
3. Local finiteness can follow from finite ports and bounded propensities.
4. Root/immigration reactions can be stated without pretending they are
   derived from old support.

### Failures

The reaction list, rate constants `k_alpha`, capacity pairing functions, and
root reactions are primitive. Current SHARD capacity results do not give a
uniform arity bound or these propensities. The architecture is classical
unless augmented by a coherent pre-seal sector.

### Verdict

A physically transparent realization class for the missing rulebook, not a
selection theorem.

## 6. Architecture E — self-exciting marked support process

Extend v9 Paper 9 from fixed support exposures to history-dependent marked
intensities:

$$
\lambda_S(H)
=\lambda^{root}_S(H)
+\sum_{e\in H}g_{S,\tau(e)}(B_H(S),e).
$$

Private, shared, and bridge supports are marks. Conditional on integrated
intensity, survival is exponential. Root terms allow first birth; excitation
along recorded ancestry allows propagation without a universe scheduler.

### Strengths

It produces an actual null-inclusive point-process rule once the intensity
functionals are supplied, and naturally separates raw scheduler order from
the canonical marked partial history.

### Failures

The base intensities and response kernels are exactly the missing interaction
law. Without a pregeometry-native collar argument, the kernels smuggle a
metric or arbitrary support affinity. Classical intensities do not by
themselves generate quantum-strength correlations.

### Verdict

The closest direct extension of v9's conditional family, but it relocates
rather than derives the free coupling kernels.

## 7. Architecture F — projective decoherence/instrument rulebook

Replace the classical path probability by a strongly positive decoherence
functional or quantum instrument on finite extension cylinders. Require
bi-additivity, normalization, projective consistency, and record-level
no-signaling. A seal selects a recorded alternative; before sealing,
alternative extension packages can interfere.

### Strengths

1. It can represent outcome-dependent, no-signaling quantum correlations
   without forcing a Bell-local classical support model.
2. It treats the full indivisible history as primitive and makes the classical
   click kernel a recorded conditional shadow.
3. It distinguishes coherent pre-seal alternatives from durable records.

### Failures

The decoherence functional/instrument is the rulebook. Positivity and
consistency leave large families. Conditional probabilities can depend on the
chosen record partition/instrument. Root, bridge, coupling, and continuum
selection remain open.

### Verdict

Likely required for the quantum-complete theory, but not a derivation of the
functional.

## 8. Architecture G — maximum caliber or uniform admissibility

Choose the maximum-entropy distribution over allowed extensions, perhaps
relative to a count measure and subject to conservation/evidence constraints.

### Refusal

This is an inference rule unless the reference measure and constraints are
themselves established physical facts. Different coordinates, proposal
menus, or priors change the answer. V4 already showed that uniform admissible
rows and residual minima are model-only without a source theorem; v8 showed
count measures anti-select the desired geometry.

## 10. Independent synthesis before the literature search

The strongest candidate is not one of the architectures alone. It is a
four-layer rulebook:

```text
Layer 1: typed extension grammar
  finite root, continuation, and multileg bridge packages;

Layer 2: record-local transfer cocycle
  admissibility, conservation, local factor/evidence increments;

Layer 3: positive harmonic/projective completion
  normalization, whole-history boundary condition, construction-order
  quotient, and profinite consistency;

Layer 4: record instrument/seal
  coherent or classical outcome functional whose committed shadow supplies
  D5 tokens.
```

In a classical finite arena the proposed formula is

$$
K_H(\xi)
=
\frac{
w_H(\xi)h(H+\xi)
}{
w_H(\varnothing)h(H)+
\sum_{\eta\in\operatorname{Ext}(H)}w_H(\eta)h(H+\eta)
}.
$$

This is only a class until the extension grammar, local weights, harmonic
boundary, and seal instrument are selected. The important conceptual move is
the separation:

```text
local physical coupling lives in w;
global/projective law class lives in h;
construction gauge lives in the quotient/cocycle;
physical outcome lives in the instrument.
```

The literature audit must now determine which parts are established under
other names, whether their combination is standard, and whether any existing
theorem already characterizes or selects the missing data.
