# D8 — selection gates for a complete interacting record rulebook

**Status:** frozen before the D8 candidate derivation and new literature
search, 2026-07-11.

## 1. What “find the rulebook” can honestly mean

D7 proved that the established SHARD/ISP principles do not uniquely select a
law.  D8 therefore distinguishes three achievements:

1. **Mathematical completion:** give an explicit law that assigns every
   allowed finite history a root/null/continuation/bridge/outcome rule and
   generates a consistent full-history measure.
2. **Physical compression:** derive that law from a shorter set of local
   physical postulates and finitely stated primitive types/couplings, without
   hiding a table of all future probabilities.
3. **Empirical identification:** show that the selected member reproduces
   quantum/no-signalling behavior and the downstream spacetime diagnostics.

A complete explicit law with declared couplings counts as a rulebook even if
its constants require measurement; ordinary physical laws have couplings.
An unspecified functional `K_H`, action, Hamiltonian, decoherence functional,
or “choose the member that works” does not count.

## 2. Required output object

The fundamental output must be either:

$$
(\rho_0,\{K_H\}_{H\in\mathsf{Hist}})
$$

for a classical committed-history law, or a quantum-capable projective
instrument/decoherence family whose sealed shadow supplies those kernels.

For every generative history, it must define:

```text
candidate extension grammar;
root/immigration alternatives;
continuation alternatives;
multileg bridge alternatives;
explicit null/no-click alternative;
relative numerical weights or amplitudes;
normalization/no-event survival;
marks and outcome probabilities;
commitment/seal operation;
provenance and exactly-once ownership;
off-support behavior or a declared restricted domain.
```

## 3. Nonnegotiable gates

### G1 — finite specification, not full-law lookup

The primitive input must be a finite rule schema plus finitely stated
couplings/types or a convergent computable generator.  It cannot contain one
independent number for every history.

### G2 — no preferred construction order

Auxiliary orders of commuting extensions must have equal physical amplitude
or probability and push forward to one canonical marked history.  Physical
protocol orientation and noncommuting order must remain observable.

### G3 — no background metric

Eligibility and local weights may use only typed incidence, recorded collars,
factor support, capacity, and sealed numerical fields.  Metres, seconds,
dimension, light cones, or the V9 target geometry may appear only in tests.

### G4 — root and bridge closure

The law must start from the empty/root boundary and must either create typed
multileg bridges or prove that disconnected components never join.  A hidden
global pairing oracle is forbidden.

### G5 — locally finite/no explosion

Every finite history must have finite total extension intensity or a
normalized countable extension distribution.  Infinite candidate grammars
need a proved summability bound.

### G6 — recorded locality and relativistic causality

Future response may depend on the complete recorded past but must be mediated
by an explicit collar/predictive state.  Sealed spacelike choices must obey
operational no-signalling.  Bell-local classical factorization is not imposed
on pre-seal alternatives.

### G7 — quantum capability

A fundamental classical local hidden-variable support process is insufficient
unless it is explicitly only the committed shadow of a quantum-capable
instrument.  The rulebook must state where interference lives and how a seal
produces durable alternatives.

### G8 — projective/profinite consistency

Finite cylinder/instrument laws must agree under admissible end deletion and
coarse graining, with retained residue for modes that still affect the future.

### G9 — capacity and ownership

Every primitive mark, factor, port, bridge leg, and outcome must be carried in
finite records with exactly-once provenance.  No uniform arity bound is
assumed without proof.

### G10 — nontrivial downstream risk

The candidate must make predictions capable of failing the V9 cone,
dimension-stationarity, scale-ladder, propagation, and quantum tests.  Those
tests cannot define its local weights retroactively.

## 4. Candidate selection principles to investigate

The following are hypotheses, not accepted answers:

1. **Covariant causal growth plus a renormalization fixed point.**  Test
   whether general covariance, causal locality, and coarse-graining stability
   collapse causal-set growth couplings to a small or unique family.
2. **Local quantum rewrite generator.**  Treat record birth as typed
   creation/bridge operators with a local completely positive instrument;
   enforce commuting disjoint generators and projective gluing.
3. **Whole-history action/decoherence law.**  Assign amplitudes to canonical
   histories using a local additive sealed-holonomy action, then derive the
   committed kernel by the instrument.
4. **Self-consistent evidence fixed point.**  Let a proposed law determine its
   own predictive contrasts and require its extension weights to equal the
   click/evidence response.  Test existence, uniqueness, and root bootstrap.
5. **Canonical groupoid base measure.**  Use automorphism-corrected counting
   over typed extensions and determine whether capacity and local action make
   it summable and coordinate-free.
6. **Minimal predictive-state dynamics.**  Ask whether causal-state/minimal
   sufficiency plus a universal update principle fixes more than a
   representation.

Maximum entropy, algorithmic probability, uniform admissibility, and geometry
fitting are controls.  They may be useful inference tools but are rejected as
fundamental unless a physical reference measure is independently derived.

## 5. Success ladder

```text
FAIL:
  another conditional comparison or an unnamed generator.

COMPLETE-POSITED:
  an executable full law with every primitive exposed.

COMPLETE-COMPRESSED:
  the law follows from a finite local quantum rewrite/action schema and
  declared measurable couplings.

PHYSICALLY-SUPPORTED:
  it passes quantum causality and the V9 geometry/scale tests without tuning
  those tests into its definition.

UNIQUE:
  all alternative laws satisfying the same frozen postulates are proved
  equivalent on committed records.
```

D8 will not call a result `UNIQUE` merely because only one candidate was
implemented.
