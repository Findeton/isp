# A complete global record kernel that cannot grow a universe

## Abstract

Earlier work separated a Sealed Causal Instrument Rewrite (SCIR) architecture
from the conditional fact that the positive cone of a complex qubit is the
`1+3` Lorentz cone. Here they are combined in a globally normalized sequential
kernel with incidence-scoped instruments. A separate template proves dual
`SL(2,C)` covariance; SPLIT, sibling-MERGE, and terminal-SEAL instruments
generate every finite-prefix probability once the global race is supplied;
one disjoint-SPLIT cell commutes in state and probability; and intervention
is kept distinct from ancestry and positivity. Seventy-three exact checks over
`Q(sqrt(2),i)` verify the algebra, instruments, typed durable outcomes,
ownership, prefix normalization, the selected schedule cell, algebraic
containment, negative controls, and an independent finite `H/T` orbit.

The resulting cosmology nevertheless fails exactly. If `p` ports and `j`
sibling joins are open, equal token activities give expected population
increment `-j/(2p+j)`. The open population is a nonnegative supermartingale,
has one-half immediate-extinction probability at the root, and becomes
extinct almost surely. All 72 frozen numerical histories terminate before
cutoffs 512--2048; none violates the declared algebraic cone, but too few
realize the influence, direction-support, or rank gates. Hostile review
confirms extinction but finds that decentralized clicking, generated
multi-frame covariance, general bridge birth, and canonical history
pushforward remain open. The frozen primary verdict is therefore
`INCOMPLETE-PACKET`. Retaining a successor after a seal is a motivated next
candidate, not a derived correction.

## 1. Question and claim boundary

The target is not another hand-built causal set and not another shape fit. It
is a complete decentralized local probability law answering, for every finite marked
history:

1. which next rewrites are possible;
2. which records and ports own them;
3. with what activity a candidate fires;
4. with what probability its quantum outcome occurs;
5. what new records, positions, links, and opportunities result.

The paper asks whether such a complete law can use the same local algebra for
quantum records and Lorentzian causal displacements and then typically grow a
large, four-factor, angularly filled influence cone.

The complex rank-two algebra, the root, the instrument matrices, unit
activities, and the click scale are primitive packet data. This paper neither
derives them from sealed records nor claims that nature uses them. It tests
their joint consistency and generative consequences.

## 2. Ordered event algebra and dual gauge

Let

`V=Herm_2(C)`, `V_+={X:X>=0}`.

Every Hermitian matrix has a unique expansion

`X=t I+x sigma_x+y sigma_y+z sigma_z`.

Its determinant is `t^2-x^2-y^2-z^2`, and positivity is equivalent to
`t>=sqrt(x^2+y^2+z^2)`. Thus `V_+` is exactly the future Lorentz cone. In the
root gauge `e=I`, a normalized pure state `rho` produces the declared
increment

`Delta Y=2 rho=(1,r)`, `|r|=1`.

For `A in SL(2,C)`, event and effect representations must transform dually:

`X'=A X A^dagger`,

`E'=A^{-dagger} E A^{-1}`, `e'=A^{-dagger} e A^{-1}`.

Then

`p(E|X,e)=Tr(EX)/Tr(eX)`

is frame invariant. An instrument leg from endpoint `a` to endpoint `b`
transforms as `K'=A_b K A_a^{-1}`, and completeness is

`sum_o K_o^dagger e_b K_o=e_a`.

The exact receipt verifies these identities under a nonunitary boost and
noncommuting local frames. Link paths transform only at their endpoints, and
diamond holonomy transforms by conjugation. A physical-filter control changes
the probability when the effect is not transformed, so operational change is
not erased as gauge. This is a covariance template; the generated history is
still executed in one root frame and is not an integrated multi-frame test.

## 3. Globally raced, incidence-scoped rewrite packet

An open carrier stores its record identifier, owner, qubit state, event
position, explicit parent ports, local order unit, link to the anchor frame,
and open status. Provenance is distributed over immutable records rather than
copied into a growing ancestry word. A token stores its rule, exact owner
ports, anchor, unit activity, and actual instrument matrices. Candidate
creation is incidence-local; next-event normalization is global.

### 3.1 SPLIT

SPLIT consumes one open carrier. Its two equally weighted isometry legs are

`K_g |psi> = 2^{-1/2}(U_g|psi>) tensor |0>`, `g in {H,T}`.

It produces a transformed carrier `rho_a=U_g rho U_g^dagger` and a fresh
ancilla `rho_b=P0` at

`Y_a=Y_o+2rho_a`, `Y_b=Y_o+2P0`.

It also emits exactly one sibling-MERGE token owned by these siblings and anchored at
`o`. This is state transfer plus ancilla preparation, not cloning.

### 3.2 Sibling-MERGE (`JOIN` in the frozen protocol)

Sibling-MERGE is enabled only while both named siblings remain open. At the frozen D9
angle `pi/4`, it applies partial-iSWAP and measures/discards its second output:

`J_b=(I tensor <b|)U_pSWAP`, `b in {0,1}`.

The continuing output is placed at

`Y_c=Y_a+Y_b-Y_o`.

Consequently

`Y_c-Y_a=Y_b-Y_o in V_+`,

`Y_c-Y_b=Y_a-Y_o in V_+`.

The placement formula is incidence-local because the shared anchor is carried
by the token; there is no scan for arbitrary compatible records. It cannot
join unrelated components. The exact instrument
depends on both inputs and can transfer an intervention into a later seal
probability.

### 3.3 SEAL and the path measure

SEAL consumes one open carrier, creates an immutable typed projective `P0/P1`
outcome record, and emits no continuation. Every new port emits one SPLIT and
one SEAL candidate; every SPLIT emits its one sibling-MERGE. Consuming a port
invalidates all other tokens that name it.

All activities equal one. The executable enumerates every enabled token and
normalizes one global race, then applies the selected instrument's Born law.
This supplies a normalized next-history kernel and finite-prefix tree;
bounded linear opportunity growth supplies nonexplosion for the associated
auxiliary continuous-time process. It does not prove that this global
earliest-event presentation is gauge-equivalent to decentralized proper-time
clicks.

One tested pair of disjoint SPLIT instruments has the same canonical state and
exact presentation probability `1/140` in either schedule. Overlapping
SPLIT/sibling-MERGE operations do not commute. A general probability
pushforward from sequential presentations to canonical marked histories is
not executed; construction-order gauge remains open.

## 4. Four relations, not one

The construction distinguishes:

1. rewrite ancestry;
2. positivity of coordinate displacement;
3. the `(t,x,y,z)` coordinate shadow;
4. interventional influence, defined by a changed later seal distribution.

Every generated ancestry edge is positive by construction. The converse is
false: the exact search finds a carrier record and a branch-disjoint ancilla
descendant whose displacement is positive but whose state and seal law remain
unchanged under the root intervention. A deliberately naive JOIN placement at
one endpoint fails the other input's future-cone test. These controls prevent
the built-in cone containment from being overread as derived causal
equivalence.

## 6. Population theorem

Let `P_n=p>0` denote the open-port population after committed event `n`, and
let `J_n=j` be the number of enabled sibling joins. Each join owns two ports,
so `0<=j<=p/2`. Equal activities give `p` SPLIT, `p` SEAL, and `j` JOIN
candidates. Hence

`Pr(Delta P=+1|H_n)=p/(2p+j)`,

`Pr(Delta P=-1|H_n)=(p+j)/(2p+j)`

and

`E[Delta P|H_n]=-j/(2p+j)<=0`.

`P_n` is a nonnegative supermartingale absorbed at zero. For the stopping time
at `{0,M}`, optional stopping bounds the probability of reaching `M` before
zero by `1/M` from the root population one. Survival forever within a bounded
positive set also has probability zero: the total SEAL probability is at
least `2/5`, uniformly in every nonempty state, giving a uniform positive
absorption chance in bounded blocks. Taking `M` to infinity proves:

> **The equal-activity D11 universe becomes extinct almost surely.**

At the root only SPLIT and SEAL are enabled, so immediate extinction has exact
probability one-half. JOIN does not rescue growth; whenever enabled it makes
the drift strictly more negative.

## 7. Frozen 72-history campaign

Without survivor selection, 24 fresh seeds were run at each cutoff
`512,1024,2048`. Every history terminated early. Longest lifetimes were
`49,171,47`; median lifetimes were `1,3,1`.

There were no ancestry- or influence-cone violations. A stricter repaired
statistic—changed downstream `P0/P1` seal distribution after sibling-MERGE—
appeared in `1/24`, `4/24`, and `5/24` histories, proving a working
local mechanism but failing the frozen `20/24` population-level gate. Four
positive covariance eigenvalues appeared in only `3/24`, `5/24`, and `4/24`.
Median directional support was `-1.0000,-0.8516,-1.0000`; a negative value
means the realized directions did not even cover every hemisphere. These are
extinction readings, not continuum estimates.

The inherited shape statistic refused almost all generated clouds for lack of
valid projections. Same-pipeline M4 controls were valid under the physical
time-axis convention, with mean `F` about `1.08`, while the diagonal-axis
convention refused. No roundness result is assigned to the generated packet.

The frozen registry verdict is `INTERACTION-INERT`. That phrase is not a valid
mechanism diagnosis: the mechanism is interaction-capable but
population-extinct / interaction-sparse.

## 8. Relation to ISP and existing literature

Barandes's stochastic--quantum correspondence permits general indivisible,
non-Markovian stochastic dynamics and Hilbert-space representations. If a
complete full-history measure is primitive, its conditionals already answer
the next-outcome question. It does not by itself choose D11's changing record
types, continuation boundary, incidence grammar, or activities.

Quantum combs and quantum causal models already supply extensive formalisms
for memory-bearing composition and intervention-defined causation. Critical
branching extinction is classical probability theory. The Lorentz-spinor
algebra is standard. The present contribution is a SHARD-specific synthesis
and falsification, not a priority claim for those components.

The new lesson inside this program is that **a complete global kernel, a
separate covariance template, and constructed algebraic containment do not
imply cosmogenesis or a local spacetime law**. One must separately constrain
event selection, frame transport, canonical histories, and continuation.

## 9. The constrained next law

Increasing SPLIT activity would manufacture supercriticality by a free dial.
One structural candidate is to declare that a durable commit need not destroy
the continuation carrier. Replace terminal SEAL by

`COMMIT: one input carrier -> one sealed outcome + one successor carrier`.

The same projective maps already have quantum outputs; D11 discards them.
Retaining them changes population increments to SPLIT `+1`, COMMIT `0`, JOIN
`-1`. The empty boundary becomes unreachable from a single root, without
altering any rate. With unit activities,

`E[Delta P|H_n]=(p-j)/(2p+j)>0`.

This does not make COMMIT the law of nature, and neither SHARD nor Barandes
forces it. Terminal observation, a separate birth token, and other
continuation ontologies remain possible. COMMIT is a motivated next packet,
to be frozen and tested for excessive growth, full-history
non-Markovian consistency, angular support, influence, cone shape, and
dimension. The decisive question is now concrete: **is sealing termination,
or is it the birth of a successor record?**

## 10. Verdict

The primary verdict under the frozen gates is

`INCOMPLETE-PACKET`.

D11 supplies a complete globally raced conditional kernel with a separately
verified Bloch--Lorentz covariance template and an exact extinction theorem.
It does not supply the final decentralized interacting click law. Its failure
is exact and useful: continuation semantics is now a sharp issue, alongside
event-selection gauge, integrated frame transport, canonical history
pushforward, and general bridge birth.
