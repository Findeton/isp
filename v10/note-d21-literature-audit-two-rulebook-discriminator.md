# D21 literature audit — complete histories, multipartite coherence and objective events

**Status:** final primary-source audit, 2026-07-12.

## Question audited

Does the D21 construction identify a new physical discriminator, or does it
instantiate known quantum-versus-collapse structure in SHARD's sealed-record
language?

## Regional and whole-history antecedents

Feynman's sum over histories assigns amplitudes to alternatives, while a
complete prediction additionally requires a state, measure and query.
Gell-Mann and Hartle's decoherent-histories program makes a decoherence
functional and decoherent coarse histories central
([Hartle, arXiv:gr-qc/9210004](https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/9210004)).  Oeckl's
general-boundary formulation assigns state spaces and amplitudes to arbitrary
region boundaries and composes regions without requiring a preferred global
time ([Oeckl, arXiv:hep-th/0306025](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0306025)).
These are direct predecessors of Candidate Q's regional, construction-order-
free organization.

Operational multi-time quantum processes are completely represented, at a
fixed intervention interface, by process tensors.  Pollock et al. give a
necessary and sufficient operational Markov condition and measures tied to
detectable memory
([arXiv:1801.09811](https://arxiv.org/abs/1801.09811)); process-tensor
tomography has also been formulated experimentally
([arXiv:2106.11722](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.11722)).  D21's finite table is
therefore not claimed as a new general theory of multi-time processes.

## Objective-collapse antecedents

GRW modifies quantum dynamics with spontaneous stochastic localization
([Ghirardi, Rimini and Weber, Phys. Rev. D 34, 470](https://journals.aps.org/prd/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevD.34.470)).
Relativistic flash models exist in restricted settings
([Tumulka, arXiv:quant-ph/0406094](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0406094)).
Collapse phenomenology is generally scale dependent and can differ from
ordinary quantum mechanics in matter-wave visibility, spontaneous radiation
or heating; interferometric calculations explicitly cover GRW, CSL and
non-Markovian variants
([Toroš and Bassi, arXiv:1601.02931](https://arxiv.org/abs/1601.02931)).

The D21 Candidate C is only the exact hard-dephasing endpoint.  It is not a
faithful full GRW, CSL, Diósi-Penrose or relativistic flash theory.  Replacing
its `eta=0` by a physical `eta(M,Delta x,T,...)` requires a model-specific
collapse generator and independently fixed coefficients.  Current
experiments bound substantial parts of such parameter spaces rather than
establishing a unique collapse law; examples include Majorana's spontaneous-
radiation search
([arXiv:2202.01343](https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.01343)) and levitated-
optomechanics bounds on dissipative variants
([arXiv:2401.04665](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.04665)).

## Multipartite discriminator antecedents

The GHZ/Mermin discriminator is established physics, not a D21 invention.
For the coherent GHZ state the Mermin combination reaches `4`; complete
dephasing removes the relevant three-body coherences.  Experiments have
observed the predicted conflict with local-realistic or incoherent models.
A strict-locality three-photon experiment reported `2.77 +/- 0.08`, nine
standard deviations above the Mermin local bound
([Erven et al., Nature Photonics 8, 292–296](https://www.nature.com/articles/nphoton.2014.50)).
Earlier three-photon work directly compared the `YYX`, `YXY`, `XYY` and `XXX`
predictions
([Pan et al., Nature 403, 515–519](https://www.nature.com/articles/35000514)).

Consequently the parameter-free microscopic hard-seal endpoint is already
empirically untenable at the photon/qubit scale.  Those experiments do not
adjudicate all weak mass-dependent collapse models, nor do they constitute an
untouched SHARD holdout.

## Important non-equivalences

1. A three-record Mermin correlator is **not** Sorkin third-order
   interference.  Standard quantum mechanics remains grade-2 at the quantum-
   measure level; D21 uses an ordinary multipartite observable.
2. `XXX` is a complete-support phase witness, but is not automatically a
   geometrical Wilson loop or a measured SHARD sealed holonomy.  Calling it a
   closed-history analogue does not establish that identification.
3. A no-signalling density operator is not by itself a local relativistic
   ontology.  Candidate C still needs a covariant event account; Candidate Q
   still takes the entangled source state as supplied boundary data.
4. Agreement of every proper reduced state does not imply agreement of the
   full state.  This is the standard GHZ marginal phenomenon and is the
   quantum realization of the D19 empirical null direction.

## Conservative priority statement

No originality is claimed for GHZ states, Mermin inequalities, dephasing,
collapse phenomenology, decoherent histories, general-boundary amplitudes or
process tensors.  The corpus contribution is narrower: D21 promotes D19's
abstract lower-shadow non-identifiability to two executable, complete
fixed-carrier record-instrument packets; freezes their common interface before
evaluation; and locates the first identifying query at complete-history order.
The physical scale-ladder proposal is a synthesis of established
multipartite-coherence and collapse-testing logic, not a new experimental
principle.
