## 1. D31A — the graph-blind stationarity no-go [EXACT]

**Theorem (to be gated at exhibit grade, proof in-note):** in the none-free, unbounded-growth grammar, a stationary path-covariant kernel whose per-labelled-opportunity weights depend only on the unsealed-register count u cannot assign positive weight to interactions; the unique such kernel is pure birth, w_b(u) = 1/u.
*Proof:* at count u there are u labelled births and u(u−1) labelled directed interactions. For an independent (commuting) birth/interact pair, path-covariance demands w_b(u)·w_i(u+1) = w_i(u)·w_b(u) (the interact leaves u unchanged; the birth raises it). Birth positivity (w_b(u) > 0) forces w_i(u+1) = w_i(u) = c. Normalization u·w_b(u) + u(u−1)·c = 1 with nonnegativity forces c < 1/(u(u−1)) for every reachable u; unbounded u forces c = 0, hence w_b(u) = 1/u. ∎
**The conclusion at its honest width:** a stationary path-covariant kernel with interactions must consult **richer state than u** — graph structure, degree, age, motifs, component data, or even global invariants. **Locality is NOT specifically forced** (the review's narrowing). Combined with D28's T2 (pure birth has no common futures): *the stationary + path-covariant + graph-blind class contains no diamond-forming kernel.*
**Printed limits:** none-ops excluded (the equation changes with a none arm); bounded-growth models escape the collapse (c > 0 admissible below the u-cap); birth positivity is a hypothesis (a birthless stationary covariant kernel is not addressed); per-labelled-opportunity weights with the stated counts assumed; the pair types bb and ii are trivially covariant (u unchanged or symmetric), bi carries everything — enumerated and gated.
**Receipt gates (d31a):** the three pair types instantiated at u = 2..6 (bb/ii trivial, bi forcing — explicit path-product pairs); a non-constant-w_i kernel violates covariance on an exhibited pair; a constant-c kernel is covariant on all tested pairs but positivity fails at the computed u* (exhibited); the pure-birth kernel w_b = 1/u passes full path-covariance on the enumerated depth-3 domain (every path to every web, equal products); the diamond-free corollary stated with its D28 T2 pointer.
