# D13 candidate-class adjudication

**Status:** final after three hostile rounds at narrowed scope, 2026-07-11.

## A — primitive path measure

Mathematically complete.  For a supplied full measure `mu`, disintegration
gives every next click.  Writing `S=-log(dmu/dnu)` merely changes coordinates.
This is D12's endpoint, not a derivation.

**Grade:** complete container; primitive dynamics.

## B — finite local quantum rewrite action

A typed grammar plus local isometries/unitaries/Kraus maps, boundary state and
record instruments constructs a valid finite process.  The D13 exact witness
shows a continuous family survives all structural gates.

**Grade:** complete conditional architecture; primitive kernels and grammar.

## C — holonomy/topological action

Closed holonomy supplies the phase coordinate and complete holonomy/RN
ledgers identify a finite law.  A physical theory must still choose the group,
representations, local face/diamond weights, level/coupling, matter labels and
boundary state.  Pure topological actions also lack local propagating degrees
of freedom unless extended.

**Grade:** serious phase architecture; no unique physical content.

## D — evidence/information action

KL/Fisher/RN actions give the modulus/effective-likelihood face.  Convexity can
select a unique point after the reference, constraints, sufficient statistics
and coefficients are declared.  Maximum entropy without an interaction
constraint selects independence.

**Grade:** principled inference/effective action; microscopic selection fails.

## E — symmetry/EFT action

Locality, Lorentz/diffeomorphism and internal gauge symmetry, unitarity and
cluster structure determine the allowed operator basis.  Deser/Lovelock and
anomaly constraints can uniquely complete restricted low-derivative sectors.
The full EFT retains field choices, relevant parameters and infinitely many
irrelevant coefficients.

**Grade:** strongest general low-energy organization; primitive/empirical
coefficients.

## F — geometric/gravitational action

Einstein–Hilbert is selected in the metric-only two-derivative `3+1` sector;
Benincasa–Dowker is the strongest order-native candidate and its complex path
weight can suppress important non-manifold classes.  Neither route supplies a
complete quantum measure, matter action, boundary state, record instrument or
absolute scale.  V6 independently proves the record-only `G` scale no-go at
its audited scope.

**Grade:** live gravity candidate, not a complete universe action.

## G — amplitude bootstrap

Factorization, soft limits, crossing, analyticity, positivity and unitarity
can isolate amplitudes after dimension, spectrum and symmetry data are fixed.
They generally define allowed regions and boundary points.  The finite D13
family is an exact counterexample to universal one-point selection.

**Grade:** conditional sector selector; no universal field/coupling selector.

## H — empirically identified effective action

Standard Model EFT plus Einstein gravity, measured parameters, a cosmological
boundary state and ordinary environment/measurement instruments is the best
empirical low-energy packet.  It recovers the quantum and relativistic data
that motivated D13.  It has no independently validated SHARD-to-field map, no
selected actual-record ontology and no established UV completion.

**Grade:** `EFFECTIVE-ACTION-ONLY`.

### D9 partial empirical identification

D9 shows the strongest corpus example of conditional parameter selection:
within its frozen partial-iSWAP source/preparation family, maximal Bell data
select `theta=pi/4`.  That does not select the family, grammar, fields, state
or record ontology.  Its independent geometry holdout then refutes identifying
the same raw parameter with `g_geometry=1/2`.  D13 therefore distinguishes
`PARAMETER-IDENTIFIED-WITHIN-MODEL` from
`EMPIRICALLY-IDENTIFIED-DIAMOND-ACTION`.

## Live selector openings

The audit does not close all future physics.  The most credible openings are:

1. **asymptotic safety:** a verified unique UV fixed point with a fully
   controlled critical surface could predict some currently free couplings;
2. **causal-set complex phase:** a fully defined quantum measure whose phase
   suppresses all non-manifold entropy and selects a stable `3+1` phase could
   choose the geometric sector;
3. **new empirical discriminator:** an untouched record-sensitive experiment
   could select collapse/actual-record dynamics against unitary decoherent
   histories;
4. **a new representation-invariant simplicity principle:** it would have to
   survive field redefinitions and assign a unique prior/complexity to whole
   amplitude functors;
5. **a derived matter/diamond dictionary:** it must map sealed record types to
   field representations and predict at least one coupling before fitting.

None is presently closed in the corpus or established literature.

## Decision

No candidate class reaches `UNIQUE-DERIVED-DIAMOND-ACTION` or
`EMPIRICALLY-IDENTIFIED-DIAMOND-ACTION`.  Classes B–E describe the strongest
candidate envelope found, but D13 has not proved that every generalized
process/action architecture reduces to it.  Class H is the strongest
empirically known realization.  The exact finite nonuniqueness theorem blocks
unique kernel selection, while the unproved architecture census keeps the
formal protocol verdict at `INCOMPLETE-INVESTIGATION`.
