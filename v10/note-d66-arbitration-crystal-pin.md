## 1. The question, in three convergent halves

Three terminal results point at the same event species:
(i) **width** — chart width past 4 at d = 2 REQUIRES a 3+-register
event (W4b), and arbitration is the only one; (ii) **gauge** — the
known trivialization of transition classes exploits the delivery's
two-port symmetry, which arbitration breaks; (iii) **measure** — the
descent coboundary is generated exactly where conflict groups become
visible.  The unit builds the object all three ask about: **a forced,
tiling record whose engine is CONFLICT — propose/arbitrate cycles —
rather than delivery**, and runs the three instruments on it.
