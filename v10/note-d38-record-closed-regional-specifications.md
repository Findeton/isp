## 1. Question

Paper 26 closes the supplied-carrier problem.  Given typed opportunity roles,
conflicts and boundary data, it constructs normalized regional conditionals,
records arbitration clicks and admits compatible global classical measures.
It leaves two statements deliberately open:

```text
the chosen D34b law is not proved to have the regional presentation;
the regional law does not generate the opportunity roles or their incidence.
```

D38 asks whether the generator and boundary language can be one object:

> Can a regional specification generate and authenticate the records that its
> own later conditionals require as boundary data?

The target name is **record-closed regional specification**.  “Self-
generating” will mean closure after a declared finite seed, not creation from
nothing and not unique law selection.

## 2. The correction that governs the campaign

The following proposed theorem is false and is excluded before receipt work:

> real birth or component growth obstructs every uniformly finite-range
> regional specification.

Papers 21--23 prove the opposite separation.  D34b has actor-star-local
generator rows and bounded touched/write arity.  Paper 22 gives a radius-one
distributed record boundary for the coarse and role-labeled A-wire futures.
What grows without a uniform bound is its width.  Paper 23 proves a stronger
obstruction only for the projective family of exact, unlimited-horizon,
full-ancestry futures: the minimal predictive quotient is the rooted marked
component class and has arbitrarily large information width.

D38 will keep three objects separate:

```text
local update collar       records read or written by one law event;
sufficient boundary       record carrier screening one declared future query;
predictive quotient       minimal equivalence class for that law and query.
```

The intended separation theorem is:

```text
bounded local update arity  DOES NOT IMPLY  bounded predictive width.
```

It is not a theorem that the local kernel reads the whole component before
each click, and it is not a no-go for finite collars.

## 3. Record-closed specification

Let `H` be a typed append-only record history, `D` a finite causal region and
`b_D(H)` its incoming/lateral boundary.  A record-closed specification consists
of regional kernels `gamma_D`, causal realizations `F_D` and boundary updaters
`U_D` satisfying all of the following.

### C1 — physical boundary realization

Every field read by `gamma_D(.|b_D)` is carried by a typed record or typed
incidence in the admitted record DAG.  A behavioral histogram or demand bit
may be used only when a distributed record realization and ownership map are
given.  No analyst-only oracle field receives physical credit.

### C2 — screening

For the declared query and stopping scope, conditional future laws factor
through `b_D(H)`.  Sufficiency is law-, query- and stopping-relative.  It is
not inferred from graph radius alone.

### C3 — recursive generation

Starting from the declared seed boundary, every later boundary record required
by the kernel is emitted by an admitted earlier law event.  The updater

```text
U_D : (b_D, local typed event) -> b'_D
```

uses only owned or authenticated incident records and emits a finite typed
delta.  Exogenous couplings and random marks may be part of the law; an
exogenous opportunity carrier after the seed may not be silently supplied.

### C4 — authentication and ancestry integrity

Boundary records bind type, owner, wire, causal parents, structural role and
payload under the D36 ideal-authentication discipline.  Missing-parent,
foreign-root, retargeting, omitted-field, forged-signature, replay and
disconnected-lookalike mutations reject before durable mutation.  D38 does
not claim Byzantine or cryptographic security.

### C5 — restriction and update cocycle

For `D contained in E contained in F`, record restriction is parent-closed and
boundary updates commute with nested transport.  In insertion notation,

```text
U_b(eta_1 then eta_2) = U_(U_b(eta_1))(eta_2),
r_(F,D) = r_(E,D) o r_(F,E).
```

This is the record version of the insertion/transport cocycle.  Pairwise
agreement alone is not enough; Paper 26's triple-cover counterexample remains
a permanent gate.

### C6 — closure fixed point

The output record support of the law is an admitted input boundary support for
the next kernel.  Equivalently, after the seed the specification does not need
to import a new boundary ontology to continue.  This is the precise content of
“the boundary is made of records.”

### C7 — disclosed capacity and physical upkeep

Graph radius, actor/record/port count, parent arity, identifier cost,
continuous marks and total information width are reported separately.  A
finite record is not promoted to a uniform finite-capacity theorem.
Boundary-maintenance births are physical and inherit D26's coherence cost;
pre-existing coherence-neutral TOKEN capacity is a distinct control.

## 4. Positive target: an induced D34b presentation

D38 will use the chosen D34b law as an existence example, not as a law of
nature.  Its finite seed, Poisson construction marks and coefficients remain
supplied model data.  Birth, idle and interaction events then generate the
actor rows, edges, wire tips, event records and opportunity roles used by all
later updates.

At every positive finite cylinder, condition the chosen history measure on
the complete typed incoming record boundary.  These conditional laws are
normalized regional kernels.  Nested conditional expectation supplies the
tower law on the positive-cylinder algebra.  The local generator supplies the
recursive updater:

```text
birth        touches one parent and one new child;
idle         touches one actor wire;
interaction  touches two existing actor wires.
```

For the coarse and role-labeled A-wire queries, Paper 22's distributed typed
star is the physical boundary realization.  The root owns its row, counters
and ports; neighbors own their degree/birth data; edges carry incidence.  The
star is radius one and recursively updated, but its row/port/identifier count
is unbounded.

The theorem target is deliberately limited:

- positive finite cylinders, not an arbitrary pointwise version on null
  boundaries;
- the chosen timed D34b law, not a derivation of its rates;
- a regional presentation of that law, not identification with Paper 26's K1,
  K2, K3 or joint transaction family; and
- classical record histories only.

```text
inverse-limit exact predictive boundary = rooted marked component class.
```

D38 will restate this as an exhaustion theorem for record-closed

This does not imply one actor privately stores the component.

## 6. Criterion theorem and load-bearing assumptions

The obstruction will be stated conditionally.  A law/query pair enters the
criterion only when all of the following hold:

```text
P  typed old records persist immutably in wire ancestry;
G  reachable connected histories have unbounded distinguishable growth;
R  a finite positive-probability local transport path can return every old
   attachment to the distinguished future boundary;
A  a fresh authenticated anchor makes the return path an observable cylinder;
Q  the query asks exact typed ancestry at unlimited horizon;
S  no irreversible seal, exact horizon or zero-influence cut blocks return.
```

Under `P,G,R,A,Q,S`, the anchored tomography proof makes the complete rooted
marked component class the exact predictive quotient.  The `2^M` family gives
unbounded worst-case information width.  If the law also has bounded local
update arity, both facts hold simultaneously; that is the separation theorem.

The receipt must show that the assumptions are load-bearing, not decorative.

### Escape E1 — finite horizon

A horizon-capped query can ignore a witness whose first possible return lies
beyond the cap.  This can bound the exact operational boundary at fixed
horizon without deleting the old record.

### Escape E2 — irreversible operational seal

A seal may preserve the record ontologically while forbidding every later
read/transport at the declared alphabet.  This breaks `R` or `S`.  No Silent
Erasure still protects the global record; it does not require the future A
interface to serve it.

### Escape E3 — attenuation

Nonzero attenuation alone does **not** bound an exact infinite-precision
predictive quotient: arbitrarily small differences remain different.  It can
bound an epsilon-operational boundary after a declared alphabet/resolution
threshold.  The receipt must include both controls.

### Escape E4 — bounded growth

A fixed finite population bounds capacity for that model.  It does not refute
component identity on the finite state space; it removes the uniform-in-growth
quantifier.

## 7. Ontological and operational width

D38 will report two widths.

```text
W_ont   information required to identify the persistent rooted marked
        component history up to gauge;

W_op(A) information required to predict the future at a declared operational
        alphabet A, stopping rule and error tolerance.
```

Under exact unsealed Branch F, `W_ont=W_op` at the component quotient.  A
genuine seal or an epsilon attenuation threshold can make `W_op` smaller while
`W_ont` remains NSE-protected.  This mirrors D31's structural order versus
alphabet-relative operational influence.  Operational screening is not
physical deletion.

## 8. Boundary upkeep and the D26 falsifier

If maintenance of a record-closed boundary creates `N` BORN summary carriers
on one probed parent degree with coupling `g`, D26 gives

```text
V_N/V_0 = (1-g)^(N/2),
B_N = -ln(V_N/V_0) = N[-(1/2)ln(1-g)].
```

At `g=9/25`, the exact visibility factor is `(4/5)^N`.  A measured visibility
budget therefore constrains the product of boundary-maintenance birth count
and coupling.  A coherence-neutral dormant TOKEN contributes factor one but
requires pre-existing capacity.  Bounded per-record capacity plus unbounded
predictive width therefore requires unbounded record count, physical births,
or a declared escape from the exact query.

This is not a universal rate.  A rate needs a record-to-system dictionary,
monitored duration and environmental-decoherence control.

## 9. Selection and root-free balance

Record closure constrains coupling data because the law must sustain the
boundary records used by its own later conditionals.  It does not guarantee a
unique solution.  Paper 16's action-bridge warning and Paper 26's K1/K2/K3
nonselection remain in force.

A root-free random generated network needs more than local row normalization.
The mass-transport principle/unimodularity is the relevant invariance language:
expected mass sent from a random root equals expected mass received at it for
every nonnegative covariant transport.  D38 will include finite balance
controls and cite Aldous--Lyons, but it will not claim to derive a unique
unimodular completion or select D34b's coefficients.  That is the sharpened
selection target for a successor.
