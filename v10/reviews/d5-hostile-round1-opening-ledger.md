## Dispositions

### D5-O1 — signed weights entered the declared probability arena

**Finding:** `Table` accepted negative entries and normalized any table with
positive total.

**Disposition:** repaired. The executable separates strictly positive
primitive factors, nonnegative support-control factors, derived messages, and
the identity. Every negative entry is rejected. An explicit signed control is
refused before normalization.

### D5-O2 — structural zeros were not typed

**Finding:** one equality example did not constitute a zero-support category.

**Disposition:** narrowed and expanded. Equality support with zero boundary
fibers composes; all-zero messages refuse normalization; equality and
inequality support multiply to an explicit impossible message. These remain
controls only. General fiberwise support compatibility is open.

### D5-O3 — missing parenthesization and branching cells

**Finding:** the theorem was sound, but the executable omitted the promised
right-associated contraction and branching tree.

**Disposition:** repaired. Direct, left-associated, right-associated, and
reverse-schedule chain contractions agree. A six-factor branching tree's
leaf-to-root messages equal direct elimination.

### D5-O4 — provenance did not construct physical typing or ownership

**Finding:** a `FrozenSet[str]` detects literal duplicates but supplies no port
type, direction, primitive/derived distinction, or ownership derivation.

**Disposition:** both repaired and narrowed. Primitive factors and derived
messages are now separate table kinds. A supplied separator factor may be
allocated left or right exactly once with identical results; provenance union
survives elimination and refuses nested reuse. The theorem is explicitly for
a supplied complete factor partition with unique allocated IDs. Semantic
duplication under fresh IDs and physical typed ownership remain open.

### D5-O5 — factor scope was conflated with direct carrier

**Finding:** listed variables can be dummy, a two-variable table can factor
into unary terms, and eliminating shared ancestry can create joint scope.

**Disposition:** repaired. Exact controls separate listed scope, essential
variables, separable versus irreducible pair interaction, primitive
factor-hyperedge incidence, and a derived correlated message. Directed
physical transport is not implemented and is no longer claimed.

### D5-O6 — construction-order gauge overclaim

**Finding:** variable-elimination order on one supplied factor multiset is not
physical record-birth-order gauge.

**Disposition:** repaired. Every claim now says elimination-schedule
invariance for one fixed identified factor multiset. Duplicate elimination
schedules are refused. Physical birth-presentation pushforwards remain open.

### D5-O7 — conditional state prediction was called birth/click

**Finding:** the executable receives `Z` and its factors and predicts only
`Z=-1/+1`; it has no no-birth candidate, proposal measure, eligibility, or
seal.

**Disposition:** repaired. The operation is `predict_candidate_state` and its
output is a conditional state distribution for an already supplied candidate.
Proposal/no-birth, evidence, eligibility, and seal laws remain absent.

### D5-O8 — universal ray minimality lost its test-class quantifier

**Finding:** the separating test can be a full-collar high-arity factor.

**Disposition:** repaired. The positive ray is universally minimal only
against arbitrary strictly positive full-separator tests. It is a diagnostic
target; a restricted physical proposal class may have a coarser quotient.

### D5-O9 — rank/relabeling statement was false and ranks were conflated

**Finding:** copying does not raise rank, but adding a symmetry-translated
observable can. Covariance/Gram and Fisher ranks require a declared screen and
model.

**Disposition:** repaired. The receipt separately verifies duplicate
dependence, whole-model swap invariance, and rank increase when the translated
coordinate is added. All rank claims are screen-relative; Fisher trace, KL,
participant count, and encoding size remain distinct.

### D5-O10 — the `1/12` witness was broader than its resource

**Finding:** weak-channel KL does not pay for scopes, rational parameter
descriptions, IDs, metadata, or carrier tokens.

**Disposition:** repaired. Explicit binary distributions now reconstruct TV
and chi-square. The theorem is only that bounded additive KL/evidence from the
chosen null does not bound channel count absent a per-carrier floor. It is not
a universal finite-record encoding theorem.

### D5-O11 — exact-state growth used a noncanonical representative

**Finding:** unnormalized numerator growth might be removable by positive-ray
rescaling.

**Disposition:** repaired. The executable gates the recurrence, gcd-one
primitive ray, 32 distinct rays, primitive integer bit growth `2->45`, and
normalized rational bit growth `3->46`. The paper distinguishes this exact
table-description growth from optimal algorithmic description and physical
