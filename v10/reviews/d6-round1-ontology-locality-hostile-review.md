## Accepted mathematical core

The following conditional facts survive hostile review:

1. Given strictly positive finite laws `P,Q` on one supplied atom space,
   `R=P/Q` reconstructs `P` from `Q`, inverts under swapping, and multiplies
   under independent products.
2. Directed KL is a scalar expectation of the supplied log-likelihood ratio;
   a reversal-symmetric pair can have equal KL magnitudes and inverse RN
   fields.
3. Three-bit parity twins can agree on every proper one/two-coordinate
   marginal while differing in a triple interaction.
4. A complete Walsh log-density coordinate list reconstructs a supplied
   positive finite law; dropping a load-bearing coordinate leaves
   nonidentifiability.
5. Endpoint laws and their RN field can be fixed while hidden-history lifts
   change a future readout from `7/12` to `5/12`.
6. The supplied normalized parity parameterization has the audited positive
   root `0.6093778634360062315...`.
7. Candidate symmetry does not fix proposal null mass, and an h-transform
   does not select its domain or weights.
8. Independent tensor-product RN fields have zero cross determinant, while
   the registered joint parity ratio is irreducible.
9. Two Bernoulli cylinder families satisfy the audited end-deletion equations
   and remain different.

These are results about supplied finite laws and coordinates. Their physical
interpretation needs the repairs below.

## Numbered openings and exact repair demands

### O1 — “Sealed holonomy” is not an executed object

The code begins with two dictionaries `p` and `q` on a common binary atom
space and forms their ratio. It contains no executable representation of:

- a diamond boundary or center;
- two typed input/output screens;
- a closed exchange loop;
- a holonomy cochain/cocycle condition;
- a committed outcome;
- a sealing witness;
- or a record produced by the comparison.

The RN identities are therefore generic finite measure theory. Calling the
result “sealed holonomy reconstructs change” presupposes that `P_AB,P_BA`
already are the laws of a sealed diamond—the physical premise D6 is supposed
to audit.

The paper does say the laws and atom space are supplied, but Theorem 11.1 is
still named the `conditional sealed-holonomy token theorem`, and the receipt
header says `SEALED-HOLONOMY FACTOR-TOKEN`. Those names outrun the arena.

**Exact repair:** rename the proved object `conditional ordered-law RN factor
reconstruction`. If a sealed-holonomy theorem is desired, add typed diamond
data, boundary maps, exchange orientations, a closure/seal witness, and prove
that the compared laws are carried by that object. Until then `sealed` and
`holonomy` may appear only as the supplied interpretation or future physical
target.

### O2 — `R=1` is a neutral comparison, not an eventless token or null proposal

When `P=Q`, the RN factor is one and KL is zero. This proves only that the two
supplied probability laws are statistically identical. A physical event may
still occur; the two compared orders may simply have the same law. It also
says nothing about whether a proposal opportunity exists.

The note defines

```text
null = R == 1
```

and R3 calls this an `eventless conditional token`. The code check likewise
says `eventless R is one`. This conflicts with the paper's otherwise correct
separation between comparison and proposal/null mass.

**Exact repair:** use `RN-neutral comparison` or `no statistical change
between the supplied laws`. Remove `eventless` and do not place `R==1` in a
proposal-null field. The null/no-proposal alternative remains missing and is
represented only in the separate proposal arena.

### O3 — Essential RN coordinates are presentation-relative, not intrinsic physical scope

For a field on a fixed product presentation, D6 correctly computes the
coordinates on whose fibers the field varies. That is a unique minimal
functional-dependence set relative to those coordinate labels.

It is not yet:

- a set of participating records;
- a causal support;
- a directed carrier scope;
- or an invariant under arbitrary changes of history coordinates.

An invertible recoding of the atom space can redistribute apparent
coordinate dependence. The three-bit parity result shows high-order
dependence in the supplied binary presentation; it does not prove that one
primitive physical three-record carrier exists.

**Exact repair:** call the field `presentation-relative essential coordinate
scope`. Keep physical participant scope, primitive factor incidence, and
directed carrier scope unset. Add a coordinate-change audit if any
`intrinsic-scope` claim is retained.

### O4 — The commitment coefficient is conditional on mode normalization and is not intrinsic

The root of

$$
\tanh h=e^{-h}
$$

is reproduced to high precision for a supplied parity observable normalized
to values `+/-1` and its chosen natural parameter `h`. D6 correctly shows
that reversing the mode mirrors the law and changing parity scope yields
another admissible mode.

There is a further dependence: rescaling/reparameterizing the primitive
contrast changes the numerical coefficient equation unless a canonical unit
for the cochain is already fixed. The executable does not derive that unit.
Thus `h_*` is not an intrinsic universal record constant; it is a conditional
coefficient in the frozen normalized one-mode presentation.

**Exact repair:** state the observable normalization and parameterization in
every coefficient claim. Replace `intrinsic division-event commitment` with
`conditional commitment for a supplied normalized oriented mode`, unless a
separate theorem derives primitive mode units.

### O5 — Expected KL, realized evidence, survival, division, and sealing are conflated

The code computes

$$
I=D(P\Vert Q)=E_P[\log R]
$$

from two supplied laws. This is an expected evidence scalar. It is not the
realized log-likelihood evidence of an actual history atom, nor an accumulated
record carried by a physical event.

The code then evaluates

```text
survival = exp(-I)
```

and verifies it lies in `(0,1)`. Paper 7 calls `S=e^{-I}` both a survival law
and, in the abstract, a conditional seal probability; Section 7 separately
calls `1-e^{-I}` the division probability. If sealing corresponds to the
division/record event, its probability is `1-S`, not `S`. If sealing instead
means survival, that ontology must be stated. At present the token census
merely marks `seal: conditional-on-evidence` without resolving the branch.

No Bernoulli seal variable is sampled in the executable.

**Exact repair:** distinguish:

1. pointwise RN action `A(omega)=log R(omega)`;
2. expected KL `E_P A`;
3. actual accumulated record evidence;
4. survival/no-division probability `S`;
5. division/commitment probability `1-S`;
6. the physical event, if any, called `seal`.

Rename the current result a conditional numerical survival/division weight.
Do not claim an actual seal law until the evidence carrier, random decision,
and accepted outcome are modeled.

### O6 — The reconstructed RN field is not yet a complete D5 factor token

D5's accepted arena requires a supplied positive table **and** a unique
allocated identity in a complete factor partition. D6 reconstructs the
positive numerical field `R`, but it explicitly leaves primitive ownership
missing. It also lacks a physical factor-cover assignment and primitive versus
derived type.

Theorem 11.1 nevertheless says D6 supplies “an exact positive factor suitable
for D5 composition.” At most it supplies the numerical table that could enter
D5 after an external identity/ownership/cover allocation.

**Exact repair:** say `D5-compatible numerical factor field pending supplied
ID, ownership, primitive/derived status, and complete-cover allocation`.
Do not call the reconstructed field a complete factor token.

### O7 — Algebraic irreducibility does not prove a cross-component physical carrier

The determinant witness is valid: the registered joint `2x2` RN table is not
a product of unary factors, while an independent product ratio is. This
distinguishes irreducible statistical dependence from independent tensor
factorization.

But the executable has no record graph or component ontology. The two binary
coordinates are not demonstrated to be disconnected old record components.
An irreducible joint law can reflect a common cause, conditioning, a global
constraint, hidden history, or a primitive joint sector; the determinant does
not identify which.

Therefore the sentence “an irreducible cross carrier therefore requires an
irreducible joint proposal law” is too physical. The arithmetic requires a
nonproduct joint law relative to the selected tensor split. It neither
nucleates nor proves a carrier event.

**Exact repair:** use `independent tensor sectors` and `irreducible joint RN
field`. Keep disconnected physical components, first bridge, direct carrier,
and nonlocal influence unset until typed incidence and history are supplied.

### O8 — `AB/BA` must be distinguished from construction-order gauge

D6 compares `P_AB` and `P_BA` and calls their swap orientation reversal. The
paper does not explain whether `AB/BA` are:

- physically different oriented protocols; or
- two construction/presentation orders of one physical history.

This matters. If they are construction-order gauge, their canonical physical
pushforwards should agree and the physical RN comparison is neutral. A
nontrivial `R` would then live only on the presentation cover. If they are
physical protocols, no construction-order gauge conclusion follows.

The executable only swaps supplied law dictionaries. It performs no quotient
over natural labelings or birth schedules.

**Exact repair:** type `AB/BA` explicitly as physical protocol orientation or
presentation order. If the latter, compute the canonical pushforward before
RN comparison. State that D6 proves no physical construction-order gauge.

### O9 — The “complete holonomy ledger” is executed only as a complete Walsh log-density ledger

The code computes a Walsh/Fourier expansion of `log(P/mu)` on a supplied
binary cube. It verifies full linear rank and high-precision reconstruction.
It does not execute:

- closed paths;
- cochain boundaries;
- cocycle/flatness equations;
- diamond composition;
- or a physical contrast-measurement process.

Thus the mathematical result is finite identifiability from a complete
log-density coordinate ledger. The paper correctly says this has the same
dimension as the simplex and does not select a law. It should not call the
ledger physically supplied by sealed holonomy without separately constructing
that correspondence.

**Exact repair:** use `complete supplied Walsh/log-density ledger` for the
executed theorem. State the additional mapping required from closed-history
holonomy observations to these coefficients, including primitive contrasts,
orientation, units, and measurement/seal provenance.

### O10 — Proposal, eligibility, ownership, and accepted birth are correctly refused, but “physical” language must remain conditional

This is the strongest part of the paper. The two symmetric proposal laws show

These results support:


They do not establish a physical token census; the executable line

**Required preservation:** keep proposal opportunity, eligibility, exactly-
once physical ownership, accepted outcome, and record birth marked missing in

### O11 — Barandes ISP permits history dependence; it does not force every law to be history-dependent

The paper is right that a primitive full-history measure can make non-Markovian
conditionals fundamental, and that state compression must not erase predictive
history silently.

However, Section 12 says the extension-opportunity law **must** be history-
dependent to respect Barandes-style indivisibility. That is too strong.
Barandes-style indivisibility does not select D6's filtration, force every
candidate law to be non-Markovian, or prove that no Markov representation is
available. It permits irreducible full-history dependence and warns against
unjustified factorization.

A measure on completed histories also supplies conditional kernels only
relative to a chosen filtration and almost surely; null/no-proposal must be in
the state space, and versions on measure-zero histories are not automatically
unique.

**Exact repair:** say the law `may be history-dependent and must not be assumed
Markovian without derivation`. Specify the marked-history space, filtration,
null alternative, and conditional-version scope before claiming a primitive
path measure supplies every extension field.

### O12 — Profinite compatibility remains end-deletion hosting, not locality or selection

The Bernoulli tower result is sound. The analytic product family extends to
all finite prefix lengths, and standard consistency then hosts distinct
whole-history measures.

The executable checks only the finite shadow through length five and only
end-deletion prefix maps. This is not:

- arbitrary-subset restriction naturality;
- D1 marked restriction;
- a physical local specification;
- a construction-order quotient;
- or a selector of `p`.

The paper gets nonselection right. It should explicitly preserve the D3
terminology `end-deletion prefix consistency` and not let `projective` or
`profinite` imply locality. The relation between finite ledgers and a whole
process also requires compatibility across levels; a complete ledger at each
level alone is insufficient.

### O13 — V9 geometry stays downstream, but D4's accepted terminology regresses

The geometry section is otherwise well scoped. It correctly says v9 results
remain properties of the particular builder laws, not consequences of
holonomy; metres/seconds calibration cannot change dimensionless anisotropy;
and the strict replication, dimension-ruler, stationarity, and scale-ladder

No speed, horizon, cone, dimension, gravity, quantum, or absolute scale is
derived.

However, the abstract says D4 “collapses to a global shock.” D4's accepted
ontology permits only `empty/full universal-precursor mixture` or
`global-incidence branch`; “shock” adds unproved physical content.

**Exact repair:** restore D4's accepted terminology and retain the full
geometry refusal.

## Verdict

**MAJOR REVISION.** D6 has a strong negative result and a useful conditional
positive result, but the latter is narrower than “sealed holonomy reconstructs
a factor token.” What is actually constructed is the RN field and associated
coordinate/evidence diagnostics of two already supplied finite laws.

The paper correctly concludes that no proposal opportunity, eligibility law,
ownership decomposition, null mass, cross-component birth sector, accepted
record, or final click law is selected. It must now apply the same discipline
to `sealed`, `holonomy`, `intrinsic`, `scope`, `seal`, `carrier`, and
`construction order`.

The accepted headline after repair should be:

> Supplied ordered finite laws determine a canonical relative RN field and a
> complete supplied log-density ledger identifies a law. A supplied normalized
> mode admits the registered commitment coefficient and numerical
> survival/division weights. None of these constructions proposes, owns,
> seals, or births the next physical record.
