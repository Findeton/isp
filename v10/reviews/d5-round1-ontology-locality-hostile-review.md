## Major findings

### M1 — A “sealed factor” posits the missing local law and does not yet implement a seal

The paper is commendably explicit that `phi_f` is supplied numerical law data
and that covariance plus contraction selects neither its scope nor its value.
The two-table witness and three invariant scope families support that refusal.

However, the primitive object

```text
(scope, positive rational table, unique provenance)
```

does not contain or verify:

- an evidence/Radon–Nikodym increment;
- the v7 survival/click test;
- a committed outcome;
- a screen or collar port type;
- a sealed-holonomy witness;
- or a record-birth event.

Calling the supplied table a **sealed local factor record** therefore imports
the very operation still missing. The paper later admits that arbitrary SHARD
diamonds have not been reduced to these tables, but the primitive noun has
already promoted them.

**Exact repair demand:** rename the base object `supplied factor token`,
`candidate factor record`, or equally conditional language. Reserve `sealed
factor record` for an object that carries a seal/outcome/evidence witness and
passes the v7 acceptance law. State in the abstract and verdict that D5 is a
conditional factorization theorem, not a derivation of record factors.

### M2 — The executable has unique provenance IDs, not the typed collars claimed by the paper

The code represents provenance as

```python
provenance: FrozenSet[str]
```

and duplicate refusal checks only set intersection. There is no executable
type for:

- primitive carrier versus derived message;
- source/screen/collar role;
- orientation or input/output port;
- factor ownership by one region;
- direct versus inherited provenance;
- or compatibility of separator interfaces.

Likewise, `Table.variables` is only a tuple of names. Joint relabeling tests
name covariance, not compatibility of physical boundary types. D2 proved a
pushout only after actual typed spans were supplied; D5 may not silently use
string identity as the missing type system.

This matters under nested reuse. The current algebra correctly refuses the
same provenance twice, but it has no canonical rule assigning an overlapping
factor to one side of a gluing. Two physical collars may overlap legitimately.
Refusing every duplicate detects double counting; it does not construct the
factor ownership/refinement needed to compose an overlapping cover.

**Exact repair demand:** either downgrade the theorem to a
`unique-ID factor-partition contraction theorem`, or implement a typed
provenance object with carrier kind, orientation, primitive/derived status,
ownership, separator compatibility, and an overlap/refinement rule. State
that the current theorem assumes a complete provenance-disjoint factor cover
has already been assigned.

### M3 — Factor scope, essential dependence, direct incidence, shared ancestry, and correlation are not the same relation

D5 makes a valuable distinction in the ancestry control:

```text
H-A and H-B factors
    -> correlated A,B boundary message
    -/-> primitive A-B factor.
```

That survives review. But the general carrier criterion remains syntactic.
`has_direct_factor` returns true whenever two variable names occur in one
table. A table may list a variable on which its values do not depend, or may
factor exactly into lower-scope tables. Thus listed scope is not necessarily
essential support, and a common factor is not automatically a directed causal
carrier between every pair in its scope.

There are at least five different objects:

1. listed table scope;
2. minimal/essential statistical dependence scope;
3. primitive factor-hypergraph incidence;
4. a derived joint message produced by eliminating shared ancestry;
5. directed physical transport through a typed carrier.

The receipt distinguishes items 3 and 4 in one control, but the ontology does
not yet type all five. A derived `M(A,B)` has joint scope even when no direct
`A-B` carrier existed; downstream code must not reclassify it by scope alone.

**Exact repair demand:** define an essential-scope test or quotient out dummy
legs and exact lower-scope factorizations. Type primitive factors separately
from effective messages. Use `direct factor-hyperedge incidence` for the
executed relation and reserve `direct carrier/transport` for a directed typed
record law.

### M4 — Elimination-order invariance is not the physical construction-order gauge

The exact chain verifies

```text
sum out S, then T = sum out T, then S.
```

This is variable-elimination scheduling invariance, following from finite
associativity and distributivity. It is not a comparison of two physical
record-birth histories, two global commit orders, or their canonical
pushforward to one unlabeled completed record web.

Calling C2 “construction-order gauge” risks claiming that D5 has solved the
global-order question inherited from v9 Paper 9. It has not. All factors and
variables are already supplied before either elimination order is chosen.

**Exact repair demand:** rename the result `elimination-order invariance` or
`contraction-schedule gauge`. Explicitly deny that it proves physical
record-construction-order gauge. A physical gauge theorem requires distinct
birth sequences, their weights, and an exact quotient/pushforward comparison.

### M5 — The receipt predicts a supplied candidate's binary state, not whether a record is born

`predict_new_state` takes an already supplied new variable `Z` and supplied
birth-factor tables, sums the boundary, and normalizes the two values
`Z=-1,+1`. L1 establishes a conditional binary outcome distribution. It does
not include:

- a no-birth/null candidate;
- competing record proposals with different scopes;
- a probability or rate that any candidate arises;
- eligibility selection;
- a seal/survival event;
- or normalization over inequivalent new-record extensions.

Accordingly, “normalized local click weights” and “local next-record
probability” are stronger than the executed gate. The factor table can score
the state of a proposed record; it does not create that proposal or show that
a click occurs.

**Exact repair demand:** rename L1 and theorems to `conditional candidate-state
weights` at the current scope. If a birth law is desired, add an explicit
candidate space including refusal/no-birth, a scope/eligibility measure,
factor ownership, seal acceptance, and exact normalization across candidate
extensions. Keep the v7 survival law separate until its evidence input is
constructed.

### M6 — “Local” currently means factor-graph/separator local, not spacetime local

The finite arena permits arbitrary factor scopes, including a single
high-arity parity factor meeting ten or arbitrarily many variables. It has no
metric, causal speed, bounded hop range, screen distance, or directed
propagation rule. The encoder also receives the complete list of factors
assigned to the region; the executable does not show how a decentralized
record can discover that this list is complete without a global registry.

Thus D5 proves locality only relative to a **supplied factor hypergraph and
factor partition**: outside dependence screens through a separator if the
assumed factorization has that separator property. It does not prove physical
record locality.

**Exact repair demand:** qualify every `local composition` verdict as
`conditional factor-graph composition`. State that completeness and local
readability of the factor cover are additional premises. A later physical
locality gate must bound/type scopes and provide a record-local way to find
all cut-touching factors.

### M7 — Connected-collar-only growth and the connected seed are domain assumptions

The component invariant is correct:

- a new hypergraph vertex attached only to one old component cannot join two;
- an explicit proposal with legs into two components joins them;
- starting from a connected seed and accepting only single-component parent
  sets preserves connectedness.

None of the italicized inputs is selected by sum-product composition. The
connected-collar-only proposal domain is chosen; the first connected seed is
chosen; and the multileg joining proposal, when allowed, is supplied.

`CONNECTED-SEED-ONLY` is therefore ambiguous. It may sound as if D5 derived a
unique connected seed or a seed-only law. The theorem is only closure of a
declared domain from a supplied seed.

**Exact repair demand:** rename the verdict
`CONNECTED-DOMAIN-CLOSURE-GIVEN-SEED`. Preserve the explicit refusal that D5
does not derive the primordial seed, first join, or a principle deciding
whether cross-component proposals are legal.

### M8 — The augmented message respects non-Markovian history only under a supplied factorization premise

This section is largely correct. D5 does not claim that the unmarked primitive
process becomes Markovian. It says the accumulated message is a function of
history and may be sufficient for a declared continuation class. This is
compatible with Barandes-style indivisibility and full-history conditioning.

The load-bearing conditional is:

> the complete path law must admit the supplied factor records, and all
> external dependence must meet the eliminated region through the retained
> separator variables/factors.

That premise is a conditional-independence/factorization assertion. General
non-Markovian or indivisible dynamics need not have a finite sufficient
separator. The two-history receipt proves sufficiency for one common external
factor, while Theorem 5.2 proves positive-ray minimality only against the
arena's unrestricted positive external tests. A later physically restricted
birth-factor class may induce a coarser quotient.

**Required wording:** say `factorization-relative augmented-state
sufficiency`, not that non-Markovian history in general has been locally
screened. If two histories with the same permitted collar have different
futures, record this as factorization failure rather than silently enlarging
the message with global history.

### M9 — `DISTRIBUTED-BOUNDARY-GROWTH` overclaims an unbuilt physical encoding

The receipt proves two exact storage facts:

1. a generic width-`b` binary separator table has `2^b` entries and
   `2^b-1` normalized degrees of freedom;
2. a width-one chain keeps two entries while exact numerator size grows from
   2 to 45 bits through 32 steps.

These establish **separator-table state growth** along width and precision
axes. They do not provide:

- a decomposition of an arbitrary table into bounded-capacity primitive
  records;
- an addressing/routing layout for those records;
- a local update rule for that distributed encoding;
- or compatibility with a per-record evidence ceiling.

The paper acknowledges the phrase is provisional. A provisional
overstatement should not remain in the receipt verdict.

**Exact repair demand:** replace `DISTRIBUTED-BOUNDARY-GROWTH` with
`EXACT-SEPARATOR-STATE-GROWTH` or `WIDTH/PRECISION-STATE-GROWTH`. Reserve
`distributed boundary` for a constructed per-record encoding and update
algebra.

### M10 — Rank and capacity are mostly separated, but rank must remain screen-relative

The mathematical conclusions survive:

- complete parity ledgers have covariance rank `2^n-1` under the uniform
  full-support screen;
- duplicate observables do not increase that rank;
- one many-body parity observable has rank one at arbitrary arity;
- independent biased coordinates have Fisher/covariance rank `n` and trace
  `3n/4`;
- arbitrarily many nonzero weak independent channels fit below the KL upper
  budget `1/12`.

This correctly refutes a raw participant or rank bound from finite content
alone. It also correctly refuses to equate a scalar `W_*` with a Fisher
matrix.

One terminology qualification is needed. Covariance/Fisher rank depends on
the chosen probability screen and support; it is not an intrinsic rank of a
mere observable list. With structural zeros, directions may become null.
`Operational channel rank` should therefore always specify the screen and
quotient. Rank is a dimension count, trace is one selected scalarization, KL
is a finite contrast, and exact storage is a coding resource. None may slide
into another.

**Required wording:** use `screen-relative covariance/Fisher rank` in theorem
and receipt summaries. A clearer verdict label is
`FINITE-CONTENT-DOES-NOT-BOUND-RANK-OR-ARITY`.

### M11 — Structural-zero controls do not yet supply the typed support theory they require

The positive base theorem is clean. The deterministic equality example
correctly shows that some zero tables compose, and the all-zero table is
correctly refused at normalization.

That is not a general structural-zero extension. With zeros, composition may
make only some boundary assignments impossible, conditional normalization may
fail on a fiber, and support compatibility must be checked during gluing. The
current `Table` type does not distinguish positive factors, nonnegative
support factors, and invalid signed tables; its constructor does not enforce
the base theorem's positivity premise.

**Exact repair demand:** keep all theorems positive unless a separate typed
support category is implemented. Add explicit support objects/fiberwise
compatibility and refusal rules before generalizing. At executable level,
separate positive constructors from nonnegative structural-zero controls and
reject negative factor weights.

### M12 — Projective/profinite and spacetime/horizon language stays within scope

**PASS, with the stated ceiling preserved.** The two Bernoulli families are
compatible finite product towers and remain parameter-distinct. The analytic
product formula plainly extends beyond the eight audited widths, while the
receipt itself is only a finite shadow. D5 correctly concludes that
projective compatibility hosts supplied laws and selects none.

It does not call the inverse limit a bounded physical record, a local encoder,
or a source of factor values. It also does not transfer D4's standard
profinite-integer discontinuity result to this different tower.

The spacetime and horizon sections are explicitly counterfactual and
downstream. D5 derives no metric locality, signal speed, cone, horizon,
dimension, Lorentz invariance, black-hole law, quantum theory, or absolute
scale. Factor-hypergraph connectivity is not promoted to directed causal
reachability.

## Mandatory openings before round 2

Round 2 should not begin until the production artifacts:

1. **Downgrade or construct sealing.** Treat factor tables as supplied
   candidate tokens unless actual seal/evidence/outcome data are added.
2. **Implement or downgrade typing.** Replace string-ID provenance by a typed
   carrier/interface/ownership model, or state the theorem only for
   provenance-disjoint factor partitions.
3. **Separate scope relations.** Distinguish listed, essential, primitive,
   derived-message, correlated, and directed-carrier relations.
4. **Repair the gauge claim.** Call C2 elimination-order invariance; do not
   infer physical construction-order gauge.
5. **Repair the birth claim.** Call L1 conditional candidate-state weighting
   unless a genuine candidate/no-birth/seal law is added.
6. **Qualify locality.** State that the result is factor-graph local relative
   to a supplied complete factor cover, not yet spacetime or decentralized
   record locality.
7. **Relabel connected closure.** Mark the connected-collar domain and seed as
   assumptions; rename the verdict accordingly.
8. **Preserve Barandes.** Keep augmented sufficiency conditional on the
   factorization and declared continuation class.
9. **Rename storage growth.** Do not claim a distributed-boundary encoding
   before constructing one.
10. **Type rank/capacity.** Use screen-relative covariance/Fisher rank and keep
    it separate from trace, KL, participant count, and exact storage.
11. **Type structural support.** Keep zeros outside the positive theorem until
    fiberwise support compatibility and nonnegative constructors exist.
12. **Retain the downstream ceiling.** No projective selector, bounded
    profinite record, cone, horizon, speed, geometry, quantum, or final click
    law may be promoted.

## Verdict

**MAJOR REVISION.** D5 has successfully shown that non-Markovian memory is not
by itself an obstruction to exact finite factor-message composition. It has
also sharpened the missing datum: scope, factor value, provenance ownership,
and the proposal domain must be supplied before contraction begins.

The accepted theorem is conditional finite factor-graph sum-product. The
rejected promotion is from that theorem to already sealed, typed, physically
local record birth with a distributed boundary implementation.

No final dynamic interacting click law is selected. In particular, D5 does
not yet say how a new record arises, which scope becomes eligible, why its
factor has one numerical value, whether it seals, or how arbitrary exact
separator state is stored in finite primitive records.
