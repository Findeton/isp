## 1. Bare history functional versus records

D20 now gives the operational packet as

```text
typed event algebra E
+ normalized strongly-positive D
+ supplied record semantics and protected future algebra
-> probabilities on the declared decoherent durable-record sector.
```

It then states in prose that bare `(E,D)` gives event probabilities but does not
identify durable records.  This is the necessary repair.  D14/D17 record
ownership, sealing and future protection are supplied finite structures; they
are not consequences of arbitrary strong positivity.

Paper 16 independently preserves the same distinction through three layers:

1. the operational history core `(E,D)`;
2. the interpretation/query layer selecting physical records and protected
   future algebra;
3. the explanatory local generator containing domain, measure/contour, action,
   state, instruments and units.

Its phrase “ordinary probabilities licensed on decoherent durable-record
partitions” is conditional on such a partition being supplied.  The subsequent
layer separation makes that condition unambiguous.  No record-emergence theorem
is smuggled into `D`.

## 2. Effective baseline versus complete physical packet

D20 now says exactly what the audit required.  Standard Model plus
Einstein–Hilbert EFT is the leading tested low-energy **baseline**.  A predictive
packet is obtained only after context-specific state, contour/prescription and
record/apparatus data are fixed.  The document expressly denies that this is
one empirically selected complete generator.

Paper 16's abstract likewise restricts empirical selection to the effective
baseline over its tested domain.  Section 8 says the action is used together
with a state, gauge/renormalization prescription and physical record
environment, and immediately leaves state parameters and several coefficients
measured, bounded or open.  Read with the generator tuple in Section 2, this is
a template for context-complete predictions, not evidence that cosmology has
selected one state/contour/record packet.

The documents therefore do not promote EFT success into a unique ultraviolet
completion or a SHARD derivation of the carrier, couplings or records.

## Non-blocking wording edits

For maximum manifest identity, two final sentences could be tightened before
publication.

1. D20 ends with “the experiment needed to select it.”  The body correctly
   establishes that no particular experiment is yet fixed.  Prefer:

   > a sharply characterized missing generator plus a protocol for completed
   > survivors to determine a future discriminator.

2. Paper 16 ends with “the higher-order/cross-candidate experiment required to
   move further.”  “Higher-order” is a demonstrated discriminator class in the
   finite example, not a uniquely forced physical observable.  Prefer:

   > explicit countermodels to proposed selectors and the cross-candidate
   > discriminator program required to move further.

These changes would remove the last local mismatch between the precise future-
work sections and their rhetorical conclusions.  Because the qualified bodies
control the claim and no actual experiment is named or opened, these are not
manifest blockers.
