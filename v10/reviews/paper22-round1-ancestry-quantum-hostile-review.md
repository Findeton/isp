## 2. Branch-F theorem audit

### 2.1 The carrier class is typed narrowly enough

The manuscript defines `C_r` as the complete record-native restriction owned
by actors in the closed actor-graph ball of radius `r` about A.  It includes
owned actor rows, tips, counts, ports, complete events touching owned wires and
opaque predecessor identifiers.  It explicitly forbids dereferencing an
outside identifier, because that would cease to be a radius-`r` carrier.

This is the same complete-radius family closed by terminal D34e.  The theorem
headline, proof and post-theorem scope sentence all quantify over
`{C_r:r finite}`.  No sentence silently upgrades that result to a no-go for:

- arbitrary bounded carriers;
- adaptive non-radius frontiers;
- all local encodings;
- all physical realizations of the C/L quotient; or
- the whole class of SHARD laws.

### 2.2 The paired pasts really have one common selected event

For each finite `r`, the construction places D at distance `r+1` from A and
adds leaf E.  The two histories differ only in D's next event: idle versus
interaction with E.  Both events have the same structural D role and the same
pre-stop D-wire ordinal `k`, while the immutable event kind differs.  Because
D and E lie outside the owned ball, the complete `C_r` carriers agree.

The future predicate is not “whatever happens to be D's tip later.”  It names
the exact pre-stop event by structural role plus ordinal and asks that the
selected inward chain carry it into A's future ancestry with kind `idle`.
This is one common, gauge-invariant Branch-F event in both conditional laws.
Later D idles, interactions or births cannot rewrite that sealed event.  The
paper therefore retains the endpoint-pinning repair that closed D34e round 2's
moving-tip counterexample.

### 2.3 The all-`r` probability is exact and correctly scoped

The witness component has `r+3` active actors.  Each of the `r+1` prescribed
child-to-parent interactions has a degree-two initiator.  At one embedded
step, its exact selection probability is therefore

```text
[1/(r+3)] * [1/(4*2)] = 1/[8(r+3)].
```

Hence the consecutive inward subcylinder has mass

```text
p_r = [1/(8(r+3))]^(r+1) > 0.
```

The printed exact cases agree:

```text

r=1  1/1024
r=2  1/64000
r=3  1/5308416
```

On the idle past, this subcylinder is contained in the selected event, so the
paper correctly writes a lower bound, not an equality.  On the interaction
past, the same ordinal already has immutable kind `interaction`, so the
selected idle-kind event has probability exactly zero.  The timed `Delta=1`
numbers are likewise described only as subcylinder lower bounds obtained by
the Erlang completion factor, never as total event probabilities.

The theorem is thus a conditional-future-law distinction at every finite
radius, not merely a graph-separation or present-rate observation.

## 3. Component ceiling and locality audit

The B4 statement is correctly one-sided.  In the chosen D34b law:

- birth attaches a new actor inside the parent's component;
- interaction traverses an existing edge;
- idle touches one actor; and
- no row joins two previously disconnected components.

Independent actor clocks therefore factor over disconnected components at
continuous construction time and component-local stops.  Retaining the full
current configuration of A's component retains the persistent event records,
predecessor references, tips and actor data needed to generate Branch F.
Terminal D34e's full typed regional composition gate covers those fields, so
Paper 22 does not rely on the rejected earlier actor-row-only composition
check.

The manuscript repeatedly calls B4 a “sufficient ceiling” and says explicitly
that its necessity is unproved.  It does not trigger Paper 21's stronger
`WHOLE-COMPONENT ONLY` row.  The apparent slogan “smallest causal boundary” in
the conclusion states the desired predictive principle; the immediately
following sentences say only that D34e constructs *one* such boundary for C/L
and leaves the minimal adaptive F frontier open.  It does not overclaim
minimality.

The locality language is equally disciplined.  “Local” means radius-one
actor-graph read scope and bounded touched/write support per event.  The paper
explicitly says this does not establish bounded memory, Lorentz locality or a
spacetime light cone.  It also denies that the actor construction requires a
global physical commit clock.
