not a localized certificate theorem.


action classes.  Conversely, state-potential increments are not automatically

matrix, print connected-component count and quotient dimension, and add a

whether endpoint potentials are physically quotiented depends on the declared

## MINOR m2 — the complete-tail convention is only implicit


**Repair.**  Print and gate the exact `sum_(r>R)` identity and state the
retained-through-R convention.  Keep the nonsummable shell control separate.
