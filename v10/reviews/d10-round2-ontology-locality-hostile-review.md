## Detailed closure findings

### 1. Positive functionals are no longer promoted to clocks

**Status:** **PASS at repaired scope**

The live abstract calls `Tr(P_u X)` a `directional clock-shadow evaluation`
and a positive functional. The investigation explicitly says these are not
operational clocks until a click/time/unit map is supplied. The normalized
qubit time-scale opening remains load-bearing.

The paper title and Corollary 2.3 retain “many clocks, four factors,” but the
surrounding text makes this an inherited slogan and a conditional shadow.
This legacy title is not a blocker as long as the explicit functional/time
qualification remains adjacent and the verdict uses `FOUR-FACTOR-
DIRECTIONAL-POSITIVE-EVALUATIONS`.

### 2. Generated directions and the external sphere diagnostic are separated

**Status:** **PASS**

The independent finite-hull value is also separated from the sampled support
diagnostic. Finite outer approximations pass; infinite convergence is

### 3. Finite grammar has been downgraded from a capacity theorem

**Status:** **SUBSTANTIVELY CLOSED, ONE TEXT BLOCKER REMAINS**

The repaired code and paper now distinguish:

- three opportunity-token names;
- finite word depth;
- finite projector count at each depth;
- a possible distribution of one gate mark per history record;
- exact amplitude/word/provenance description;
- per-record evidence/KL capacity;
- and total collar/history storage.

The live verdict says
`FINITE-ALPHABET/FINITE-DEPTH-PROJECTOR-REFINEMENT` and explicitly marks

However, Section 3 of `note-d10-bloch-celestial-investigation.md` still says:

### 9. Full SL(2,C) gauge and Born invariance remain open

**Status:** **PASS ON REFUSAL; ONE NEW EXACT WORDING BLOCKER**

The repaired package continues to prove only determinant preservation,
nonunitarity, and trace change for one exact boost. It does not turn the boost
into a harmless normalized-state gauge transformation or claim Born-weight
invariance. The required event/effect dual law remains the next falsifier.

However, Paper 11 now says:

> The quotient `PSL(2,C)` double-covers the proper orthochronous Lorentz group.

This is false. The correct relation is:

```text
SL(2,C) -> SO^+(1,3) is a two-to-one covering homomorphism
with kernel {+I,-I};
PSL(2,C)=SL(2,C)/{+I,-I} is isomorphic to SO^+(1,3).
```

This is both a group-theory and gauge-kernel distinction. Quotienting by the
kernel removes the double cover; the quotient does not still double-cover the
Lorentz group.

**Exact blocker repair:** correct the sentence and the repair ledger entry.
No executable change is required.
