## Round-1 closure audit

### 1. Supplied factor tokens do not masquerade as sealed records — pass

The base object is now a **supplied factor token** with:

- a listed variable scope;
- a positive rational table;
- one unique allocated identity under a supplied ownership convention.

The paper explicitly says these data are not seals, outcomes, evidence
increments, directions, carriers, or record births. It also says that the
factor algebra is only something a later sealed-diamond construction might
instantiate. Scope and numerical value remain supplied law data.

The opening ledger and conclusion retain the missing physical operation:
sealed holonomy would still have to derive the token's scope, direction,
evidence, ownership, value, eligibility, and seal/no-birth rule. Thus the
factor table no longer answers the problem by renaming it.

### 2. Unique-ID bookkeeping is not called physical typing — pass

The exact theorem is now for a **supplied complete factor partition with
unique allocated IDs**. The artifacts say what those IDs do and do not prove:

- they detect literal duplicate counting;
- provenance unions survive elimination;
- reintroducing an already accumulated ID is refused;
- one separator factor may be allocated left or right exactly once with the
  same result;
- fresh-ID semantic duplication is not detected;
- physical interface types, orientation, ownership derivation, and a complete
  factor registry remain open.

This accurately matches the code. `provenance` remains a set of opaque IDs;
the manuscript no longer promotes it to a typed carrier. The composition
theorem assumes factor-cover completeness and ownership rather than deriving
them.

The final hardening patch also closes a small kind leak: `effective_message`
always returns `kind="message"`, even for a one-factor region with no
elimination. A primitive factor token therefore cannot masquerade as a
derived separator message merely because the algebra performed no sum.

### 3. Listed scope, dependence, interaction, incidence, and correlation are separated — pass

The repaired B5 control now distinguishes:

1. a listed two-variable scope with one dummy leg;

3. a table depending on both variables but exactly separable into unary
   factors;

The ancestry control still shows that `H-A` and `H-B` may correlate `A,B`

separate runtime kinds, and factor-component calculations refuse derived
messages.

Directed physical transport remains a seventh, unimplemented relation and is
explicitly excluded. The connectedness results therefore concern syntactic
primitive factor-hypergraph incidence only.
