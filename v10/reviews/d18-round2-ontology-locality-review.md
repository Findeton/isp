**Date:** 2026-07-12

## Decision

The repaired theorem makes the essential distinction correctly.  On a finite
typed event algebra, a normalized strongly-positive decoherence functional
`D` determines every licensed decoherent-event probability and conditional.
No second probability lottery is mathematically required after `D` is given.
That finite operational statement is exact and should be accepted.

Supplying primitive `D` does not discover or explain the interactive record
law.  It supplies that law wholesale.  In general `D` is a global table or
functional containing every interference term and completed-history
correlation.  Calling it “the whole-history law” is accurate; calling it a
derived local click dynamics would merely rename the missing object.  The
theorem explicitly admits this: primitive `D` solves the next-click problem
by stipulation rather than deriving it from sealed records.

The separation into three layers is therefore load-bearing:

```text
operational probability core       (E,D)
record/query/unit interpretation    supplied semantics and reporting
physical explanatory generator     domain, fields, measure, action, state,
                                    instruments and sewing
```

The exact source verifies the first layer.  It illustrates pieces of the
other two layers, but it does not derive a local generator, a preferred-record
ontology, construction-order independence, or nature's unique law.

Several protocol gates consequently remain open:

- the interferometer functional and the causal projective functional are
  still separate examples rather than one quantum causal-record family that
  simultaneously has interference, D14 durability, causal projectivity and
  the non-Markov conditional;
- orthogonal record vectors in D18 do not themselves prove record semantics
  or future durability;
- a global `D` need not admit a bounded-collar local generator and can encode
  a preferred foliation or nonlocal ledger just as easily as a covariant law;
- D18 does not compare all admissible sampler orders or construction
  filtrations; and
- depth-one-to-six compatibility is not a sigma-algebra extension theorem for
  a quantum decoherence functional.

The theorem and JSON mostly state these ceilings honestly.  In particular,
D18 does not claim `MINIMAL-COMPLETE-CONDITIONAL-RULEBOOK`, does not claim
`UNIQUE-FUNDAMENTAL-RULEBOOK-SELECTED`, and does not open a geometry holdout.
Accordingly these are formal-thread openings, not reasons to reject the
narrow finite sufficiency theorem.

## 1. What finite `D` sufficiency actually proves

For a finite event algebra, `D` contains the bilinear weight of every pair of
fine histories.  Once a question is represented as a partition or event map,
the corresponding coarse functional is obtained by block summation,

```math
D'=M D M^\dagger.
```

If the chosen partition decoheres, its probabilities are the diagonal entries
of `D'`, and conditionals are ratios of positive cylinder values.  This is a
complete operational recipe on the declared finite domain.

The exact interferometer makes the extra content beyond an ordinary path
probability visible.  The unrecorded and recorded constructions have the same
four fine-history diagonals but different off-diagonal terms.  Consequently
their output laws differ:

```text
unrecorded path       output (1,0)
orthogonal path mark  output (1/2,1/2).
```

Thus a classical probability measure on fine paths cannot replace the full
quantum functional.  The path and output questions of the same `D` also return
different, correctly typed laws.  A nondecoherent partition is rejected rather
than having its diagonal silently interpreted classically.

Strong positivity is not merely inferred from a sampled grid.  Each matrix is
a Gram matrix of branch vectors, so for arbitrary complex `c`,

```math
c^\dagger D c=\left\|\sum_\alpha c_\alpha b_\alpha\right\|^2\ge 0.
```

All fifteen partitions of the four-element history set are exact regression
coverage for coarse closure.  These mathematical facts support the candidate
subresult.

The exact conclusion is best worded as:

> Once the complete finite quantum history law `D` and its argument domain are
> supplied, no additional probability postulate is needed to answer licensed
> questions.

“No additional probability postulate” is safer than an unqualified “no
sequential click law is needed,” because a computational sampler or local
physical implementation may still be required.

## 2. Primitive `D` renames rather than derives the missing law

The operational economy of `(E,D)` is real but can be misleading.  `D` is not
a short universal principle merely because it is one mathematical symbol.  On
a large history algebra it can contain as much information as the complete
universe law, including all global boundary correlations, phases, couplings,
record effects and non-Markov dependence.

Nothing in Hermiticity, normalization, strong positivity and additivity
selects one particular `D`.  D17 already supplies inequivalent positive
kernels for the same action and grammar.  D18's own interventions show that a
different boundary state or envelope produces a different functional while
the local interferometer action remains fixed.

Therefore these statements must remain distinct:

```text
D is sufficient to calculate probabilities          proved
D is selected by the action                          disproved at finite scope
D has a local explanatory factorization              additional hypothesis
D is nature's actual functional                      empirical/open
D is uniquely selected                               not shown
```

The repaired theorem handles this well.  It calls the richer generator

```text
G = (C,F,nu,S,rho_boundary,J,coarse,units)
```

and explicitly says primitive `D` solves the next-click problem by
stipulation.  The semantic JSON likewise says the generator remains supplied.

The phrase “minimal” should not migrate back from the protocol title into the
accepted subresult.  `(E,D)` is operationally compressed, not proved minimal
as a physical explanation, local algorithm, or empirical parametrization.

## 3. Record semantics and durability are not inside `D`

A decoherence functional can tell whether a declared partition decoheres and
with what probabilities.  It cannot, without additional interpretation, tell
which event variable is a physical sealed record, where its carrier lives, or
which future operations are forbidden from overwriting it.

In the interferometer example, `recorded_branches()` enlarges each branch
vector by an orthogonal path label.  This is a correct mathematical model of a
recording interaction.  It does not use:

- a D14 `Port` with owner and `record_id`;
- a sealed-map correspondence;
- a live collar;
- an explicit local CP instrument; or
- a nontrivial future algebra demonstrating durability.

Accordingly source check 9's phrase “the local path commit” is stronger than
its predicate.  The predicate establishes orthogonal path marking and changed
interference.  Locality and durable-record ontology are supplied by how that
vector factor is interpreted, not by the matrix alone.

The hash-pinned D17 dependency does contain one finite owner-safe D14 record
network, exact isometry, complete reset and protected seals.  But D18 imports
only `causal_nodes`, `causal_tower` and `is_projective_binary`.  It converts the
tower tables directly into diagonal matrices.  It does not call the D17 local
record network or derive those matrices from its record morphisms.

The theorem's repaired interpretation layer is therefore essential.  Record
semantics and future durability are physical-generation/interpretation data,
not redundant probability inputs after `D` is supplied.  The operational core
passes only conditional on those questions being licensed externally.

## 4. The “one exact packet” gate remains open

Round 1 objected that the interferometer functional and causal tower were
parallel.  The repair lifts every D17 cylinder table to a diagonal
decoherence functional `D_n` and verifies exact restriction

```math
D_n=M D_{n+1}M^\dagger
```

through depth six.  This is a useful repair: the non-Markov conditional now
really is the diagonal conditional shadow of one projective functional family,
not a probability table outside the D formalism.

It does not make the interferometer and causal constructions one packet.  The
source still has:

```text
d_un/d_rec    four fine histories; nontrivial interference; no causal tower
D_n           actual D17 causal cylinders; diagonal/classical; no off-diagonal
              quantum interference or D18-executed D14 commit.
```

No single family in D18 simultaneously exhibits amplitudes, interference,
physical D14 record birth and durability, causal projectivity, and the visible
non-Markov conditional required by protocol Q1.

This does not weaken the algebraic theorem that arbitrary finite `(E,D)` is
operationally sufficient.  It prevents D18 from claiming that the full frozen
protocol's integrated local sufficiency witness has been built.  The theorem
correctly withholds `MINIMAL-COMPLETE-CONDITIONAL-RULEBOOK`.

## 5. Global correlations are not a global update machine—but neither are they locality

The theorem's statement that a global state is not a global update machine is
conceptually sound.  A boundary state and whole-history functional specify a
joint law; they need not describe a machine committing every record in one
universal sequence.

However, this distinction does not by itself give local generation.  An
arbitrary strongly-positive `D` can encode:

- correlations requiring unbounded past information;
- a preferred foliation hidden in its event-domain construction;
- direct correlations between disconnected regions;
- a nonlocal constraint evaluated only on completed universes; or
- the exact contents of a universe ledger in tabular form.

All of those objects are operationally sufficient.  Strong positivity and
projectivity do not distinguish them from a functional obtained by local
regional sewing.

To earn locality, the explanatory generator must separately show that
regional class operators compose on owned unsealed boundaries, that spacelike
operations satisfy the intended independence/commutation conditions, and
that every correlation has either boundary-state support or an admitted
whole-history origin.  The formula

```math
D(\alpha,\beta)=\operatorname{Tr}(C_\alpha\rho C_\beta^\dagger)
```

does not establish those facts by itself; arbitrary global class operators
also fit it.

D17 supplies one finite local-memory realization of its `000/101` correlation.
That proves compatibility of one record law with local carried memory.  D18's
primitive diagonal `D_n` does not uniquely encode or select that realization.
The same `D_n` could be presented as a global correlation table.  Local
generator structure is additional ontology.

The protocol and theorem acknowledge that arbitrary conditionals need not be
computable from a bounded collar.  That ceiling must remain prominent.

## 6. No global clock is not proved by omitting a clock variable

A whole-history functional can avoid treating construction order as physical
time.  Its probabilities may be defined directly on covariant events.  This
is a promising architecture for avoiding a universe-wide commit ledger.

But the absence of an explicit time index in `D` does not prove:

- invariance under all admissible linear extensions;
- equality across different construction filtrations of one completed order;
- foliation independence of the event algebra;
- relativistic no-signalling; or
- existence of a local proper-time sampler.

Protocol Q3 demands comparison of sampler orders.  D18 runs no such
comparison.  Its D17 dependency proves compatible element-label covariance
within one supplied filtration and explicitly leaves alternate filtration
sewing open.  Therefore the theorem sentence that D17 “realizes that
distinction exactly at finite scope” must be read narrowly: D17 distinguishes
construction labels from records on its chosen filtration.  It does not close
construction-order gauge in the stronger protocol sense.

The defensible D18 claim is:

> The operational law need not be formulated as a physical global commit
> sequence.  Full construction-order/foliation independence remains a
> generator and event-domain condition to be proved.

That is enough to motivate the architecture, not to certify covariance of an
arbitrary primitive `D`.

## 7. Profinite and sigma-algebra ceiling

The exact source constructs diagonal functionals only at depths one through
six and verifies their prefix restrictions.  D17 contains a deterministic
continuation schema that can generate any requested finite depth.  This
supports the intended classical cylinder-family induction at the supplied
finite scope.

For the durable classical record subalgebra, a complete compatible family of
finite discrete cylinder probabilities can be placed on the corresponding
inverse-limit/profinite space and extended by the ordinary probability
machinery under the usual hypotheses.

That does not imply that an arbitrary quantum decoherence functional on the
cylinder algebra extends to the full generated sigma algebra.  The cited
Dowker–Johnston–Surya result makes extension a separate vector-measure
question.  Strong positivity alone is not an automatic extension theorem.

The theorem states this correctly:

```text
finite/cylinder event domain           claimed
classical record inverse-limit move    conditional/standard
full quantum sigma extension           open
profinite space selects D              false
```

Profinite completion is therefore a domain that can host compatible shadows.
It is neither a dynamical selector nor a proof that the shadows come from a
local law.

## 8. Literature and priority audit

I checked the cited primary arXiv records.  Their abstracts support the audit's
high-level use:

- Oeckl assigns amplitudes to arbitrary spacetime regions and states to their
  boundaries in the general-boundary framework;
- Gell-Mann and Hartle make quasiclassical domains depend on both initial
  condition and action;
- Sorkin treats a quantum history measure as a basic law-like object;
- Dowker, Johnston and Surya make sigma extension a separate vector-measure
  problem;
- Boes and Navascues single out strong positivity through composability;
- Rideout and Sorkin derive a family, not one numerically unique, of classical
  sequential-growth dynamics; and
- Srivastava and Surya's 2026 analysis reports commutativity for two natural
  orderings and an inconsistent attempted two-dimensional Pauli realization.

The literature audit draws the right architectural conclusion: these
formalisms represent, constrain, compose or learn supplied dynamics; none of
the cited work selects SHARD's unique state, couplings, record ontology and
scale dictionary.  Its no-priority statement for the general formalism is
appropriate.

The audit should continue distinguishing “no general solution is known” from
“no solution exists.”  It does so in the current wording.

## Required next step

Do not add another arbitrary primitive `D` and call it local.  The next
construction should build one finite **quantum causal-record family** in which:

1. off-diagonal causal-history interference is present before a local D14
   record commit;
2. the commit is an owned CP morphism that emits and durably protects a record;
3. the post-commit `D_n` family is projective across actual causal cylinders;
4. the non-Markov next record is generated from carried boundary memory;
5. two admissible sampler orders or construction presentations are compared;
6. the same family is obtained by explicit regional sewing rather than global
   table insertion; and
7. all generator choices remain visibly supplied unless independent evidence
   selects them.

That would close Q1/Q3/Q4 at finite scope.  It still would not identify
nature's law without empirical selection.

## Final verdict

**PASS `FINITE-DECOHERENCE-FUNCTIONAL-SUFFICIENCY` at finite typed-event
scope.**  The operational theorem and exact arithmetic are sound, and the
repaired layer separation prevents a primitive `D` from being advertised as
an action-derived local generator.

**Formal D18 remains `INCOMPLETE-INVESTIGATION`.**  Primitive `D` supplies the
missing law rather than explaining it; record semantics and durability live
outside `(E,D)`; no global clock is avoided architecturally but not disproved
as hidden structure; the exact quantum and causal witnesses remain separate;
and profinite/sigma extension and nature-law selection remain open.
