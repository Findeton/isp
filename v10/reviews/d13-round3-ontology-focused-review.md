```text

```

and verifies `U_mem^dagger U_mem=I`.  Starting from the equal classical
mixture of `|0000>` and `|1000>`, the actual matrix evolution produces the two



round-2 concern that the receipt had hard-coded the history table while calling
it an executed reversible circuit.

The ontology conclusion remains deliberately small.  The cell proves that a
