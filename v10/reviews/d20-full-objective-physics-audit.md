## Decision

The D20 bottom-line verdict is correct and compelled by the reviewed corpus.
No artifact derives or independently selects one complete local generally
covariant sealed-diamond generator together with its state, measure, record
dynamics, couplings, scale dictionary and untouched geometry prediction.
Several exact countermodels show that the principles tested so far leave
physical directions open. The full objective therefore is not achieved.

D20 correctly withholds construction/refoliation equivalence, a selected
complete quantum functional, universal record emergence, derivation of
`3+1`, Lorentz signature, `G`, `Lambda`, masses or mixings, a selected
ultraviolet completion, and access to the V9 cone/dimension holdout.

Four central formulations nevertheless overreach their receipts:

1. Bare `(E,D)` does not imply **durable-record** probabilities. It gives
   probabilities only for supplied licensed decoherent questions. Record
   semantics, ownership, sealing and future durability are separate
   interpretation/generator data in D18.
2. Standard Model plus Einstein-Hilbert EFT, a generic state, contour and
   record/environment dynamics are not one empirically selected complete
   generator. The low-energy action baseline is extensively tested; the
   complete state/contour/record packet is not identified.
3. Unit conversion with measured `G`, `hbar` and `c` cannot round an
   already fixed dimensionless cone, but `G` used inside the dynamics can
   affect dimensionless correlations. D15 proves the former, not a categorical
   statement that `G` cannot affect cone shape.
4. The structure of a future empirical program is forced, but no particular
   next experiment or causal-order architecture is. D19 establishes the need
   for an independently frozen discriminator, not its physical identity.

Two further scope issues remain. The objective's “simplest” clause has no row,
even though D13 leaves the description language/prior and representation-
invariant simplicity measure open. D8's final 24-seed geometry result is also
described too warmly as a “protocol-level cone result”: its `m4` leg passed,
the `dom` leg and strict conjunction failed, and dimension was excluded.

These repairs do not change `NOT ACHIEVED`.

## 1. Locality, covariance and time

The finite corpus establishes genuine components:

- D10 supplies conditional Bloch/celestial cone kinematics and supplied
  connection covariance.
- D14 supplies one-owner finite morphisms, join checks, sealed-record
  preservation and local memory/reset.
- D16 supplies relabeling-invariant finite causal-order actions and typed
  quotient sewing examples.
- D17 supplies one owner-safe causal-extension packet on a **supplied
  filtration**, including compatible element-label covariance.

These justify saying that a universal global commit race is not logically
mandatory. They do not prove continuum diffeomorphism/refoliation invariance,
equivalence of all construction filtrations, universal spacelike locality, a
complete regional sewing theorem, or a locally computable sampler for every
conditional.

D20's statement that construction order is “not automatically physical time”
is correct. It must not be shortened to “construction order is gauge.” D17
explicitly leaves alternate-filtration sewing open.

Likewise, “regional sewing and whole-history conditionals need no universal
commit race” is valid as an architectural possibility. A primitive global
functional can omit a clock variable while still encoding a preferred
foliation or nonlocal correlation. Absence of an explicit clock is not a
locality theorem. D20's status column preserves that ceiling.

## 2. Records and the operational rulebook

D20 currently writes:

```text
typed event algebra E
+ normalized strongly-positive whole-history functional D
-> decoherent durable-record probabilities
-> all positive next-record conditionals.
```

This exceeds D18. Bare `(E,D)` can evaluate finite quantum measures, test a
supplied partition for decoherence, assign probabilities when it decoheres,
and condition positive classical cylinders. It cannot identify which
variables are physical records, prove their carriers sealed, or specify the
future algebra protecting them. A strongly-positive `D` need not have any
nontrivial decoherent record partition.

D14/D17 supply one finite durable-record generator/interpretation, but that
structure is not contained in arbitrary `(E,D)`.

The honest diagram is:

```text
typed finite/cylinder event algebra E
+ normalized strongly-positive D
+ supplied record semantics/future algebra
+ declared decoherent classical record sector
-> probabilities on that licensed sector
-> conditionals for positive record cylinders.
```

Or D20 can state only the probability core:

```text
(E,D) is sufficient for every supplied licensed decoherent question;
record meaning and durability belong to interpretation/generation.
```

“All” must mean all positive conditionals inside the declared finite/cylinder
decoherent record sector—not every quantum or continuum event.

D20 correctly says that once a full classical record law or licensed

It also correctly says this does not derive the law. Primitive `D` contains

The attached ceiling must remain:

```text

```

With the record diagram repaired, this part of D20 is faithful to D18.

## 4. The strongest low-energy physical packet

The evidence ledger supports:

> Given the observed `3+1` Lorentzian carrier and field representations,
> Einstein-Hilbert gravity plus the Standard Model is the leading extensively
> tested low-energy local EFT baseline, with measured/bounded coefficients and
> allowed higher operators.

It does not support calling the following one empirically selected complete
generator:

```text
generic prepared/cosmological state
generic gauge measure/contour
generic renormalization prescription
generic record/environment dynamics.
```

Laboratory states and instruments are selected for individual experiments. A
complete cosmological boundary state is not uniquely identified. Gauge
fixing and renormalization include conventional choices; a nonperturbative
gravity contour is not selected. Universal durable-record emergence is open.

The strongest honest block is:

```text
observed 3+1 Lorentzian carrier
+ leading Einstein-Hilbert + Standard Model EFT baseline
+ measured/bounded coefficients at declared scales
+ experiment-specific prepared states and record instruments
-> best-tested effective predictions in validated domains,

while cosmological state, universal records, UV contour/completion and higher
operators remain open.
```

This is an effective generator template plus context-specific packets, not one
selected complete generator. D20's sentence “the second is predictive and
local over its tested domain” should become “its leading local EFT dynamics
are predictive over tested domains.” Completed correlations can still be
global through the state.

This is an additional reason for `NOT ACHIEVED`.

## 6. EFT, UV, G, 3+1 and Lorentzian cones

D20 is correct that EFT4 assumes observed `3+1` Lorentzian spacetime, higher
operators remain partly open, BDQ lacks a selected quantum measure/state/
record/scale packet, asymptotic safety lacks one uniquely controlled realistic
Lorentzian trajectory plus state and records, and four-dimensional spin-foam
models build dimension into their construction.

It is also correct that measured `G`, `hbar` and `c` define combinations
such as

```math
\ell_\mathrm{Pl}=\sqrt{\hbar G/c^3}.
```

One sentence needs qualification:

> Physical metres, seconds and `G` do not round a dimensionless cone.

Changing only the reporting dictionary—converting a computed graph distance
to metres or Planck units—cannot change dimensionless anisotropy. That is the
D15 result. But if `G` enters the generator dynamics, changing the relevant
dimensionless coupling ratios can change the history ensemble and cone.

The closed statement is:

> Post hoc unit conversion with measured `G`, `hbar` and `c` cannot round
> a fixed dimensionless cone. Only selected dynamics/refinement can change
> anisotropy; the dynamical role of gravitational couplings is generator-
> dependent.

These do not prove that no deeper principle or observation can select a law.
They prove that this corpus's premises and evidence have not done so.

The final paragraph should say:

> Changing the verdict requires a new independently justified selection
> principle or new discriminating evidence. Using target phenomenology to
> invent or tune that principle would not count as an untouched test.

That is more complete than saying the only alternatives are inventing a
principle or tuning to the target.

## 9. Is the next experiment scientifically forced?

For an empirical-selection route, this structure is forced:

1. freeze a complete candidate class and common observational interface;
2. make survivors complete enough to predict the same observable;
3. fit nuisance parameters only on training data;
4. freeze a discriminator on which survivors differ;
5. evaluate genuinely untouched data; and
6. replicate hostilely.

D20's future-work sequence correctly instantiates that structure.

What is not forced is the first causal-order/BDQ architecture, exactly two
candidates, or any listed discriminator class. A genuinely derived unique
principle plus an untouched prediction is another route. No experiment can
yet be uniquely chosen before completed candidates print stable differing
predictions and uncertainties.

D19's toy theorem shows that higher-order observations can separate one
low-order training fiber. It does not prove that nature's open direction is a
cubic record correlation or that such an experiment is optimal.

Thus the scientifically forced next action is **candidate completion and
discriminator design**, not one named experiment. The suggested observables
are a research menu. Calling one “the experiment needed to select” is
premature.

## Final verdict

**THE FULL OBJECTIVE IS `NOT ACHIEVED`.** That conclusion is forced by missing
fields and exact nonselection countermodels. V10's strongest durable result is
a precise factorization of the missing problem: history law, generator, state,
records, units, ultraviolet completion and empirical discriminator cannot be
collapsed into one action coefficient.

**D20 needs targeted major scope edits before becoming the final closure note.**
Bare `(E,D)` does not generate durable records; the full low-energy
state/contour/record packet is not empirically selected; `G` may have a
dynamical role distinct from unit conversion; simplicity remains open; and the
exact next experiment is not determined.

After these edits, D20 will faithfully express the corpus: the objective was
not achieved, the reasons are proved at finite scope, and the route to changing
the verdict is constrained but not uniquely chosen.
