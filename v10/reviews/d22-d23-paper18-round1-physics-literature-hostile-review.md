## Front-1 verdict (F5, priority): novel, renaming, or strengthening?

**Strengthening-by-localization, not a new principle.** Clause (i) = Everettian unitarity (renaming). Clause (ii) "sealing = dispersal into records" is, inside unitary QM, an established **theorem**, not an axiom: Halliwell (PRD 60, 105031 (1999), fetched) proves medium decoherence ⇔ existence of records for pure initial states ("the known connection between decoherence of histories and the existence of records"); Gell-Mann–Hartle's strong decoherence (gr-qc/9509054, fetched) is *defined* by permanence of generalized records; Zurek's existential interpretation/quantum Darwinism supplies the mechanism (redundant environmental records). As an **axiom**, (ii)+(i) is Chiribella–D'Ariano–Perinotti's purification principle (PRA 81, 062348 (2010), fetched: "every physical process can be regarded as arising from a reversible interaction of the system with an environment, which is eventually discarded"). The claimed deltas grade out as:
- **(a) record-dilation formalization — real but unbuilt, and it is the entire delta.** Stinespring dilation is trivially available for *any* CP map, including Candidate C's Delta_Z; all content lives in "record-sector INSIDE the ontology." Unformalized, (a) is CDP-purification-localized-to-the-declared-record-ontology. That localization is a defensible specific strengthening — if F1 is actually executed (see O1).
- **(b) scale axiom — partially prior + internally bugged (M2).** Priors: Dirac via Duff (hep-th/0208093, fetched: dimensionful constants "merely human constructs... no operational meaning"; "no absolute meaning unless that quantity is dimensionless"), Dicke 1962 unit-covariance, Barbour/shape-dynamics ratios-only relationalism, and Bell's classic complaint that GRW buys definiteness with "new constants of nature." Rovelli's RQM relationalizes *states*, not scale parameters — no prior there. The record-certification *form* is corpus-original (paper 57).
- **(c) ladder-null packet — not novel as physics.** Residual-zero-after-calibration is standard QM's null hypothesis, the default of the entire collapse-testing literature (Bassi et al. RMP 85, 471 (2013); Carlesso et al., Nat. Phys. 18, 243 (2022)). Parameter-free-null-from-a-principle even has an exact structural precedent: Sorkin-parameter tests (Sinha et al., Science 329, 418 (2010) — QM predicts identically zero third-order interference). Genuinely distinctive: only the factor-number axis *n* (complete-support Mermin order) of the (M, Delta x, T, n) ladder (see O3).
