## The make-or-break front (F1), adjudicated

The defender of C says: the erased coherence IS received — the hard seal creates an objective fact s, and the ZZZ records correlate perfectly with s. **The defense fails, on three independent grounds, but P18 §2 never engages it and its current "precise sense of silent" is self-contradictory as written.** The sharp analysis:

(b) The **occurrence** of erasure: within C's own record algebra the post-seal world is indistinguishable from one that was never coherent; occurrence is certifiable only against an independently certified coherent preparation — which is exactly what GHZ experiments and the falsifier do. (c) The erased **content** — the eta-distinction between preparations, itself operationally defined by clicks (`P_eta(abc|XXX) = (1+eta*abc)/8` differ): the map from eta to the *entire post-seal ontology* (branch law 1/2 1/2, branch states, every shadow, s itself) is **constant**. Zero mutual information between the lost distinction and every record including s. Reception requires the receiver's state to depend on what is received; s is fresh randomness, not a receipt.
- **The no-new-receiver form (strongest):** every record statistic C produces is already a Q statistic (all 37 proper shadows identical, ZZZ law identical); C's total record content is a strict coarse-graining of Q's. A dynamics whose only effect on the record algebra is deletion, adding no sector, cannot claim its deletion was received — a receipt needs a receiver Q lacks. And the dilemma: either s just *is* the ZZZ record content (not an extra receiver), or s is a hidden variable (silent unrecorded structure directly).
- **Well-formedness / smuggling:** "coherence must be received" IS well-formed provided the normative statement is pitched at the ensemble/click level, never at off-diagonals: *distinction* = two preparations some admissible instrument separates (eta qualifies via XXX clicks — no Hilbert space needed to state it); the principle = the closure dynamics never renders record-distinct preparations record-indistinct on the total algebra; local loss must be compensated by a named complementary record sector. Clause (i) as printed ("total content is conserved (the closure is unitary)") **does** smuggle the formalism and courts circularity — "closure unitary" is nearly a synonym for "not C-class." Within the quantum carrier, Wigner/Kadison turns ensemble-level distinguishability-preservation into unitarity as a *theorem*; (i) should be derived, not asserted (O1).
