## Abstract

Paper 19 left the identified interactive click law with one unspecified organ: the interaction-opportunity kernel K — which records a young carrier may couple to. This paper reports the first systematic campaign on K's geometry. Three results. **(1) A no-go complex [THEOREM]:** in the none-free unbounded-growth grammar, the graph-blind (u-only) class of stationary path-covariant kernels is pure birth; degree, distance-degree, and tree-substrate motif gradings are obstructed at every horizon U ≥ 3; component-size gradings are obstructed at unbounded growth only, with the bounded-horizon escape exhibited exactly. The covariance premise itself is a physical fork (order-as-gauge vs order-as-recorded-history), stated and left open. Either the law consults structure richer than everything tested, or construction order is physical history rather than gauge. **(2) An instrument line [MEASURED]:** a frozen, environment-pinned validation card; an order-only spatial *ranking* estimator certified at referee grade (rank monotonicity only — no calibrated distance); and the finding that the natural deliverable of order-geometry measurement is a **signature pair** (d̂_MM, d̂_mid) — the two estimators disagree by a statistically real margin on grown webs, so no single "dimension" exists that every manifold estimator must recover. **(3) The compression regularity [MEASURED]:** across every axis tested — interaction range, interaction weight, horizon-covariance at its priced coupling, and anti-aligned directional structure — grown record webs slide along one approximate curve between the chain limit (d̂ → 1) and the pure-birth-tree limit, and no kernel enters any manifold band (nearest approach ≥ 10.4σ). This is an empirical regularity of static birth-tree-metric collar kernels — labeled and scoped, not a universal law. Interaction never lifts effective dimension in these families; the one directional family tested *anti-lifts* at matched interaction fraction. The dimension-lifting mechanism demanded by M⁴ — if the program's grammar is right — must therefore reorganize interaction placement at a healthy interaction rate, or change what interactions do to the underlying relational geometry, or live on the other side of the covariance fork. Falsifiers and the successor arms are printed.

## 1. The objects

**The kernel question.** The identified law (papers 18–19) specifies what clicks are and which histories generate them, but not which records a newborn record may subsequently interact with. D28 named this organ: the interaction-opportunity kernel K assigns weights to the interaction opportunities present at each growth step. Everything in this paper is about what K's choices do to the *order geometry* of the resulting web.

**The measured order [THEOREM, frozen].** All geometry here is measured on one structural object, frozen before any dimension was measured: the event order of a grown web — the transitive closure of "j is the next operation on one of i's wires." Two independently defined candidates (the port-dependency poset and the wire-DAG) are the same order: both are the closure of the same next-op-on-wire generator, and totality of the port map holds at the pinned couplings because every controlled-rotation entry kicks back (the D28 kickback lemma). The operational-influence graph is a distinct, alphabet-relative diagnostic; it can lose edges through exact cancellation and is never used as the order. (d31b [EXACT]; note-d31 §2/§5.)

**The grammar.** Webs grow from the three-register chain seed (R sealed) by two act types: births (a fresh record on an unsealed parent; isometric, collar-local, the paper-19 kernel) and interacts (a controlled rotation between two present unsealed records at the pinned couplings). A none-op carries idle weight where a family needs it. Opportunity sets and their weights are the kernel family under test.

**The estimators.** d̂_MM: the Myrheim–Meyer dimension of the maximal interval's sub-order, with whole-order fallback below |I| = 64 — the fallback status is disclosed per cell wherever readings are quoted, here included. d̂_mid: the midpoint-scaling dimension on the maximal interval (+1 endpoint convention pinned in-code); where starved webs shrink intervals below the midpoint floor, quoted d̂_mid values are subset means and are flagged as such. Chain statistics (longest chain, matched-d chain constant, 3-chain ratio) ride along and carry verdict weight of their own. [EXACT conventions; MEASURED values.]

## 2. The no-go complex

**2.1 The graph-blind theorem [THEOREM].** In the none-free unbounded-growth grammar, a stationary, path-covariant, birth-positive kernel whose per-opportunity weights depend only on the unsealed count u is pure birth: w_b(u) = 1/u, no interactions. (The commuting birth–interact pair forces the interact weight constant in u; normalization with unbounded u forces it to zero. Birth positivity is load-bearing — the birthless interacting kernel w_i = 1/(u(u−1)) is covariant.) With D28's tree no-go (pure birth admits no common operational futures), *the stationary + path-covariant + graph-blind class contains no diamond-forming kernel.* (d31a 5/5 [EXACT]; note-d31 §1.)

**2.2 The graded exhaustion [THEOREM at the stated scopes].** Does richer-than-u sensitivity rescue covariance? For interact-inert class maps the two-op covariance closure reduces in layers: births beside a disjoint interact force Z-invariance (linear); overlapping pairs then force class-merges; exact Fourier–Motzkin decides positivity. On the u ≤ 5 horizon from the seed (the reachable states are exactly the rooted trees on u nodes — A000081: 1, 2, 4, 9):

- **Simple-degree and distance-degree gradings: obstructed at every horizon U ≥ 3** — by *single* all-nonnegative birth rows (a deg-1-parent birth strictly enlarges the weighted census), so the kills are convention-robust (they survive disjoint-only independence with no merges), closure-depth-independent (every two-op equation is a necessary condition), and horizon-monotone (deeper systems contain these rows). (d31c2 D2/D3 [EXACT].)
- **Tree-substrate motif gradings (distance × common-neighbor): obstructed** — leaf births never reclassify existing pair distances or common-neighbor counts, so motifs are birth-inert and fall to the census-growth mechanism directly. (D4 [EXACT].)
- **Component-size gradings: obstructed at unbounded growth only.** Connectivity makes the grading u-only, so the graph-blind theorem applies as u grows without bound — but at every finite horizon U the family is *feasible*, and the witness is exact: w_i = c for any c < 1/(U(U−1)), w_b(s) = (1 − (s−1)(s−2)c)/(s−1), Z ≡ 1, path-covariant on every independent two-op pair. The witness is the priced bounded-growth escape: its interact rate encodes the size cap — a finite record universe, extra physics against the grammar. (D5/D5b [EXACT].)

**2.3 The covariance fork [POSITED, open — O7].** Everything in 2.1–2.2 presumes path-covariance is physical: that accretion order is gauge. If the order of operations is itself recorded physical history, the covariance quotient is a hypothesis about what the record contains, not a mathematical necessity, and the whole axis reopens. This is the deepest qualifier on the complex and is carried as a named open problem, not resolved here.

**What 2 licenses.** Within everything tested, a *stationary* path-covariant interacting kernel at unbounded growth does not exist. The record's terminal landscape: graph-blind (u-only) killed by the D31A theorem; d_cover, static-age, and component-indicator gradings obstructed (the birth-inert mechanism, d31c C2/C3); degree, distance-degree, and motif gradings obstructed at every horizon U ≥ 3 (d31c2 D2–D4); component-size horizon-feasible but unbounded-obstructed (D5/D5b); none-absorbing kernels covariant on bounded domains only; multi-radius profiles, age-degree products, and the exists-side beyond u ≤ 5 undecided. Exactly two covariant interacting objects exist in the record, both priced: the component-size witness (cap-priced — its coupling encodes the size bound) and **K_flat** (teleological — path-uniform weights w = Φ(H+o)/Φ(H), exactly path-covariant at every finite horizon but horizon-dependent and non-stationary; D28b R5). This is paper 19 §9's F12 trichotomy made quantitative: stationary-and-violating, covariant-and-teleological, or stationary-and-covariant — with the third leg now closed across every tested grading at unbounded growth. Every stationary interacting *grid* kernel measured in §§4–5 accordingly carries a VIOLATING covariance column; the witness cell's column reads COVARIANT-AT-CAP, its price stated above.

## 3. The instrument line

**3.1 The card [MEASURED, frozen].** All verdicts run against a frozen instrument card: sprinkled causal sets at d ∈ {2,3,4} × N ∈ {256, 512, 1024} × 20 seeds, box-4 and percolation controls at every N, per-cell SDs for every carded statistic, fixed-significant 12-digit serialization, the numerical environment and the generator's own hash recorded in-card. Terminal freeze: sha256/16 `e7bc80f0be08f537` (v2.2). Card v1 (`af5f6b011d3c48f8`) remains the untouched historical freeze consumed by the earlier record.

**3.2 The order-only spatial estimator [MEASURED — a ranking instrument].** For spacelike pairs with a common diamond, the 0.10-quantile of longest-chain lengths through sampled common intervals is certified as an order-only *ranking* of spatial separation: canonical tie-averaged Spearman against invariant proper distance, pooled over 12 independent sprinklings × 10 pairs per dimension, with sprinkling-level (cluster) bootstrap and jackknife uncertainty — d = 3: ρ = 0.844 [0.775, 0.896]; d = 4: ρ = 0.790 [0.699, 0.861]; cluster-level P(ρ < 0.60) = 0.000 both. The certification is rank monotonicity over common-diamond pairs, and nothing more: no absolute scale, no calibrated distance, no scaling law. The naive-minimum proxy is disclosed as unstable (it grows with sprinkling density). (d32a P2 [MEASURED]; the card carries the conventions, CIs, and domain.)

**3.3 The signature pair [MEASURED — the deliverable object].** On the frozen collar web ensemble (the D30 seeds), d̂_MM = 1.756 ± 0.101 and d̂_mid = 1.630 ± 0.127; the paired gap 0.126 ± 0.150 is 2.66 SE from zero, and calibrated instrument bias at matched interval size explains only ≈ −0.017 of it. The two estimators genuinely disagree on grown webs while agreeing on sprinkled manifolds (midpoint validation: 1.992/3.024/4.009 against truths 2/3/4). The honest deliverable of order-geometry measurement is therefore the ordered pair (d̂_MM, d̂_mid) — a multi-exponent signature — and single-dimension narration is barred throughout this paper. Fresh-seed replication reads (1.908 ± 0.215, 1.779 ± 0.243): the kernel-level seed spread is ≈ 0.2 (the frozen ensemble was a P ≈ 0.012 left-tail draw), the M²-exclusion travels on the chain constant (11.2σ) and 3-chain ratio (9.6σ) rather than the d̂-leg, and ~half of fresh draws fail the 0.15 concordance tolerance — the pair's *gap* is a property of the object, not a fixed constant. (d32a P4, d32b B3 [MEASURED].)

## 4. The compression regularity

The kernel-mechanism map (d32b, 240 growths [MEASURED]): the collar family with interaction radius r ∈ {1, 2, 3} (static birth-tree metric; interacts are metric-inert by choice — the family scope) and interact-weight ratio λ ∈ {½, 1, 2}, 12 seeds × N ∈ {256, 512}, verdicts against the card at Bonferroni-corrected entry bands.

**The result: interaction compresses.** d̂_MM falls strictly monotonically with both r and λ — all 36 adjacent comparisons, at both sizes, for *both* exponents of the signature pair: from (2.11 ± 0.35, 1.95 ± 0.39) at (r=1, λ=½, N=512) down to (1.107 ± 0.027, 1.058 ± 0.020) at (r=3, λ=2), the corner readings being upper bounds under the instrument's bracket floor. No cell enters any manifold band; the minimum joint entry threshold across all cells and dimensions is 10.4σ. The signature gap is positive at every cell mean. Mechanically: more interaction (in range or weight) means more comparability, and more comparability drives the event order toward chain-like totality — away from M-like partial order, not toward it. The ≥7-same-pair-touch census (the exception bookkeeping any influence reading owes, alphabet-scoped) rides per cell and peaks at 12.0/web in the high-λ corner.

**The two calibration bounds.** Pure-birth webs anchor the starved end as a *family, not a point*: the D30 pure-birth control reads d̂_MM ≈ 4.6–5.2 at its web-level record loci while midpoint scaling stays near 1 — a branching-tree signature, not a five-dimensional universe. The covariant-at-cap witness kernel, run as a growth process, is birth-dominated by construction (its cap-safe coupling keeps the realized interaction fraction ≈ 7%) and sits in that family as a lightly perturbed member: (4.54 ± 0.09, 1.93 ± 0.21) at N = 256 and (5.13 ± 0.06, 1.98 ± 0.21) at N = 512, both read whole-order (12/12 fallback; every maximal interval below the |I| = 64 floor). It bounds covariance's effect at the priced coupling only — no test of covariance at high interaction density exists yet, and the witness's interaction range (complete-graph) differs from the grid's collar, so its column is a bound, not a controlled comparison. Seed-forgetting holds (star and triangle seeds match the chain seed within 0.92 SE at three cells), so the map is a kernel property, not a seed memory. (d32b [MEASURED]; LEDGER #146–#148 carry the terminal scopes.)

## 5. The S² test: a positive mechanism test, diagnosed confounded

The one direction the corpus's own history nominates for dimension-lifting is directional structure (the "why S²" question). D32C tested a mechanism: registers carry 12-vertex icosahedral directions (child inherits, scatters to a neighbor vertex with probability p ∈ {0, ¼, ½}); interact opportunities on the r = 1 collar are weighted by anti-aligned compatibility ((1 − u·u′)/2)^κ, κ ∈ {0, 1, 2}, with κ = 0 the direction-blind control and direction draws consumed identically in every cell. It was designed as a positive test of the lifting hypothesis; what it became is the campaign's sharpest negative.

**The formal outcome and its autopsy.** All six live cells cleared the pre-registered lift bar (d̂_MM above the control by up to 41.5 pooled SE, κ-monotone, replicated on the pinned fresh seed family) — and the bar was confounded, because it lacked a matched-interaction-fraction control. Direction inheritance aligns the population; anti-aligned weights then collapse; realized interaction fraction falls from the control's 0.667 to 0.001–0.22 by the exact mean-field law fr = 2p·0.276^κ/(1 + 2p·0.276^κ); and interaction-starved webs revert to the pure-birth-tree signature (midpoint readings collapse toward 1 as *subset means* — starved intervals fall below the midpoint floor, with n_mid down to 4/12; the one genuinely estimator-mixed cell's strata are whole-order 4.747 ± 0.192 / interval 1.829 ± 0.115). A direction-blind matched-fr control predicts 101–126% of every cell's elevation: the entire "lift" is the starvation. **The residual at matched fr is negative at all six cells** (Stouffer ≈ −4.8; traced to the cross-clan seed-pair channel): anti-aligned icosahedral compatibility *anti-lifts*, and the family cannot express sphere geometry in bulk beyond the single scalar 0.276^κ. No cell enters any band (minimum entry threshold 11.2σ). (d32c [MEASURED]; the bar's design failure and the confound analysis are LEDGER-carried, #149–#151.)

**What this did and did not test.** It tested one local directional mechanism: direction labels plus a compatibility weight on collar opportunities. It did **not** test the paper-11-class bridge — neither that arbitrarily many clock readings compress into a few latent factors, nor that such factors generate the structural and influence cones. The directional *structure* the corpus ultimately needs sits far downstream of the directional *mechanism* killed here.

**The lesson, pinned:** lift claims must hold the interaction rate fixed *by construction* — directional weights should redistribute a fixed interaction budget among candidates, bracketed by shuffled-direction and direction-blind controls (post-hoc fr-matching is weaker, and is in fact unreachable inside the anti-aligned family, which saturates at fr ≈ 0.36).

## 6. What this does and does not claim

**Does [at terminal scope]:** the event order of record webs is a well-defined frozen structural object; its geometry is measurable with validated, environment-pinned instruments whose honest deliverable is the signature pair; within the static-metric collar family neither interaction range nor weight, nor anti-aligned icosahedral direction, produces manifold-band order; horizon-covariance is bounded at its priced coupling only (a complete-graph kernel, a bound rather than a controlled comparison); every tested kernel lies on one approximate compression/starvation curve between the chain and birth-tree limits; and no *stationary* path-covariant interacting kernel exists at unbounded growth in any tested grading class.

**Does not:** claim any kernel is nature's click law (every stationary grid kernel violates the covariance column; the two covariant objects are priced — cap and teleology); claim a single fractional dimension for any web (the pair is the object); claim covariance is physically required (O7); claim the compression relation is exact (matched-fr residuals are measurably nonzero — an approximate regularity, not a law of the grammar); test the paper-11-class clocks-to-factors bridge (§5); reach dynamic-metric families (interacts that alter the substrate metric are a different family, untested); or decide the exists-side of gradings beyond u ≤ 5.

**The F12 status.** The kernel-selector problem is now quantitative and cornered. Within the present static growth grammar, neither more interaction nor the first directional mechanism generates spacetime: interaction makes the order too chain-like; removing interaction makes it too tree-like. The missing mechanism must reorganize interaction *placement* while preserving a healthy interaction rate — or change what interactions *do* to the underlying relational geometry (the dynamic-metric fork) — or dissolve the covariance constraint from the other side of O7.

## 7. Falsifiers and successor arms

**Falsifiers of this paper's claims:** (i) any kernel in the tested families entering a manifold band under the frozen card and corrected joint rule (the record carries 10.4–11.2σ margins — a reproducible band entry falsifies the compression claim); (ii) a positive matched-fr directional residual in the anti-aligned family (the record carries −4.8σ); (iii) a *stationary* path-covariant interacting kernel with positive weights **at unbounded growth** in any tested grading class, or at any horizon U ≥ 3 in the horizon-certified classes (degree, distance-degree, motif — the record carries exact infeasibility certificates; graph-blind and component-size are horizon-feasible — the constant-c kernel and the exhibited witness — and excluded only at unbounded growth); (iv) MM/midpoint concordance on grown collar webs at precision excluding the 0.126 gap (the record carries 2.66 SE).

**Successor arms, in order of leverage:** (1) the aligned directional variant under the fixed-budget design standard — a fixed interaction budget redistributed by direction, with shuffled-direction and direction-blind controls: the one arm that actually tests directional geometry at healthy interaction rate; (2) dynamic-metric interacts (edge-insertion), the family the D30 tail kernel foreshadowed — changing what interactions do to the relational geometry; (3) the O2 unified positivity-collapse lemma (the no-go complex's single-mechanism form — the census-growth kills are its instance data); (4) O7, the covariance fork — the one move that changes the rules rather than the family; (5) the 48-point sphere refinement and the exists-side of richer gradings at deeper horizons.
