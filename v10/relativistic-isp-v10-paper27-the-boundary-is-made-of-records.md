## Abstract

Paper 26 constructed classical regional specifications on a supplied typed
opportunity carrier.  It did not prove that the chosen D34b generator has such
a regional presentation, and it did not make the boundary data part of the
record ontology generated by the law.  Papers 21--23, meanwhile, identified
the chosen generator's predictive boundaries without placing them inside the
regional-specification language.  The two constructions were one interface
apart.

This paper closes that interface at a deliberately finite and classical
scope.  A **record-closed oriented finite-cylinder presentation** consists of
a typed append-only event history, a query-relative record boundary, positive
finite-cylinder kernels, record restriction with typed external-parent
and external-frontier references, and an updater whose output is admitted
input for the next cylinder.  Every current discrete actor row, wire head and
simple-edge incidence is derived from authenticated records.  Birth emits a
canonical Ulam child row, edge, two ports and child clock address.  Regional
append is accepted only when it advances an explicit complete finite
authenticated-history witness.  This proves existence; it does not prove a
smaller localized admission certificate.  Realized future exponential marks,
historical occurrence times and a stored global port map are not reconstructed.

For the chosen D34b law, one proved sufficient root carrier is the D34e
distributed star.  Its compact kernel state carries the root's carrier, ring,
birth and wire counts, each neighbor's identity, degree and own-birth count,
and elapsed time relative to the conditioning stop.  The complete record
witness carries the incidence from which its edges and ports are reconstructed.
Neighbor idle and off-root interaction events are silent for the compact
quotient.  The relevant holding rate is

```text
q_A = 1 + degree(A)/4 + sum_(x adjacent A) 1/[4 degree(x)].
```

The induced depth-n action cylinders form a projective family under prefix
restriction.  D38b checks 12 normalizations, 12 direct and four staged
restrictions, 1,760 positive atoms, 220 exact competing-exponential
integrals and 30 elapsed/no-event updates.  Its record layer rejects all
18 registered whole-history or candidate failures without mutation and
passes nested restriction, update naturality, disjoint insertion and a
same-frontier branch rejection.

The main structural theorem is a separation, not a no-go for local kernels:

```text
bounded local event support does not imply bounded predictive width.
```

D34b events touch at most two actors.  One proved sufficient C/L carrier has
graph radius one and unbounded width over growth; neither a bounded alternative
nor the weak/timed minimal C/L quotient is ruled out.  For the stronger exact
unlimited-horizon Branch-F ancestry query, Paper 23 proves at every licensed
finite stop that the minimal predictive quotient is the current rooted marked
component class.  Its `2^M` family necessarily forces at least M worst-case
bits.  This is a finite-stop theorem; a timed or infinite predictive
inverse-limit identification remains open.

An abstract anchored-prefix criterion isolates what the ancestry proof uses:
component sufficiency, persistent reconstructible records and, for every two
different component classes, a measurable fresh-anchor prefix with positive
order q for the target and zero or order at least q+1 for the competitor.
D34f supplies that property for the chosen law.  The criterion's assumptions
are sufficient, not proved individually minimal.

Finite horizons, coarse alphabets, epsilon thresholds, finite marked-history
caps and a hypothetical exact-zero operational cut can change the boundary
question.  D38b does not identify the last item with V6 sealing.  Nonzero
attenuation alone does not merge an exact infinite-precision quotient.
Likewise D26 prices maintenance only if a future bridge identifies particular
records with repeated same-line BORN carriers; no such bridge or maintenance
rate is derived here.

The result therefore gives D34's generated law an oriented boundary
presentation architecturally analogous to Paper 26's language, but it does not
prove a D37/D38b comparison map, select D34b's rates, construct spatial DLR
kernels, prove null-boundary versions, identify a smaller admission
certificate, derive an infinite unimodular completion, or supply a quantum
regional join.

## 1. The answer in ordinary language

A law cannot repeatedly ask an analyst to hand it the boundary information it
needs.  If a neighbor's degree changes a future click rate, some physical
record must carry that degree.  If a birth creates a new actor, the law must
emit the child's identity and incidence.  If two regions share a causal
parent, restriction must preserve that parent internally or expose it as
typed boundary data.  And if an event is appended, the resulting records must
be valid input for the next conditional law.

That fixed-point requirement is what “the boundary is made of records” means.
It is not creation from nothing.  The finite seed, the rates, construction
marks and record grammar remain supplied.  After the seed, however, the
chosen process generates the records its next finite cylinders use.

The second result prevents a familiar shortcut.  Local event dynamics do not
promise small predictive memory.  One D34b event touches one actor or one
incident pair.  One proved sufficient physical carrier for a coarse A-wire
future is a radius-one star, whose number of neighbors, ports and identifiers
is unbounded.  Whether a different bounded C/L carrier exists remains open.
If the future query is upgraded to complete persistent ancestry forever, the
minimal exact predictor is necessarily the whole current component history up
to rooted marked isomorphism.

Those statements concern different widths of the same law.  They are not a
contradiction:

```text
event support          actors changed by one transition;
screening boundary     records sufficient for one declared future query;
predictive quotient    minimal equivalence class for that law and query.
```

## 2. Exact scope

### 2.1 The chosen D34b law

Each active actor owns a rate-one Poisson clock.  On its own ring it chooses

```text
birth                         probability 1/4;
one eligible neighbor        aggregate probability 1/4;
idle                          probability 1/2.
```

The chosen neighbor is uniform.  Birth creates a fresh parent-local Ulam
child.  Interaction touches both causal wires but does not consume the
receiver's clock or mark.  Idle touches only the initiator wire.  Edges and
event ancestry persist.  The connected finite seed is `A--B`; the accepted
D34b nonexplosion theorem makes every licensed stop land in a finite
configuration almost surely.

These coefficients and the seed are model data.  Nothing in this paper
selects them as nature's law.

### 2.2 Four different objects

The proof keeps four objects separate:

1. the complete typed record history `H`;
2. the current discrete actor rows, wire heads and simple-edge incidence
   reconstructed from `H`;
3. the D34e actor-star boundary `B_A(H)` for the C/L queries; and
4. the rooted marked component class `[K_A(H)]_g` for Branch F.

The complete history can be a validation witness without being the minimal
boundary.  The star can be a sufficient boundary without being bounded in
capacity.  The component class can be the minimal Branch-F quotient without
being read before every local click.

The reconstruction claim is discrete.  Historical event occurrence times and
already realized future exponential deadlines or marks are not stored or
recovered.  Clock addresses seed the chosen law's future exponential sampling;
relative elapsed time is a separate oriented-boundary coordinate.

### 2.3 Classical and oriented

The positive construction is an oriented future-cylinder family from a
conditioning stop.  It is not a spatial Dobrushin--Lanford--Ruelle theorem,
not a kernel version on probability-zero boundaries and not an identification
with Paper 26's K1, K2, K3 or joint transaction family.

The construction is classical.  It defines no intervention-indexed quantum
process, no quantum join operation and no quantum record-closed specification.
Any future quantum join inherits D23's in-degree-at-least-two
click-identifiability ceiling.

## 3. Record-closed finite-cylinder presentation

### 3.1 Physical event records

Every record binds

```text
kind, owner, touched wires, causal parents, payload and ideal signature.
```

Seed actor records carry the initial row and clock address.  The seed edge
carries its endpoints and both ports.  A later event is accepted only if its
parents are exactly the current heads on every previously existing touched
wire and its payload is the exact state transition derived from those heads.

For birth by actor y at own-birth ordinal j, the one event record carries

```text
child identity             y/j;
parent and child post-rows;
new edge                   {y,y/j};
two oriented ports         y->y/j and y/j->y;
child clock address        (ACTOR_CLOCK,y/j,0).
```

For interaction, the initiator's ring count advances; the receiver's does not.
Both carrier bits and wire counts advance.  For idle, only the initiator row
advances.

There is no authoritative cached state or tip map.  Validation topologically
replays the authenticated DAG, requires one comparable successor chain per
wire and derives the unique discrete rows, heads and simple-edge incidence.  A
monotone used-event set prevents rollback from turning a used event into a
fresh one.  The replay does not recover historical occurrence times, realized
future exponential marks or a stored port map; future marks are supplied by
the chosen clock law at the typed clock addresses.

### 3.2 The D34e star boundary

For a distinguished root A, the compact executable kernel state is

```text
B_A^kernel = (root row, elapsed time, neighbor rows).
```

The root row contains carrier, own-ring count, own-birth count and wire-event
count; its degree is the number of neighbor rows.  A neighbor row contains
only

```text
(actor identity, degree, own-birth count).
```

Incidence is not an additional field in the compact `Star`.  The complete
authenticated witness derives simple edges; seed and birth payloads carry two
oriented endpoint ports per edge.  Hence the root-owned B3 port map is
reconstructible in this simple-tree sector, while a complete witness counts
both endpoint incidences.

That width is deliberate.  A neighbor idle or interaction not involving A
can change the neighbor's carrier, ring and wire fields, but cannot change the
relevant A-boundary hazards.  Retaining those extra fields while thinning the
events that change them would not define a closed projection.  D38 round one
made exactly that mistake; D38b uses the accepted D34e quotient.

### 3.3 Relevant generator

Let `d=degree(A)` and let `d_x` be neighbor x's degree.  The boundary-changing
rows are

```text
A births                         rate 1/4;
A idles                          rate 1/2;
A interacts with x              rate 1/(4d);
x births                         rate 1/4;
x interacts with A              rate 1/(4d_x).
```

Their total rate is

```text
q_A(B_A) = 1 + d/4 + sum_x 1/(4d_x).
```

Every other D34b event is silent for `B_A`.  The relevant event process is
therefore a closed pure-jump projection.  If its next action is a, its embedded
probability is

```text
kappa(a|B_A) = lambda(a|B_A)/q_A(B_A).
```

The holding density for action a after relative delay t is

```text
lambda(a|B_A) exp[-q_A(B_A)t].
```

Summing over a and integrating t gives exactly

```text
sum_a lambda(a|B_A)/q_A(B_A) = 1.
```

The elapsed coordinate starts at zero at the conditioning stop.  A no-event
update advances only elapsed time; a retained event advances elapsed time and
the exact typed boundary row.

### 3.4 Positive cylinders

For incoming star boundary b and an action path
`alpha=(a_1,...,a_n)`, define

```text
gamma_(b,n)(alpha)
  = product_(i=1)^n kappa(a_i|b_(i-1)),

b_i = U(b_(i-1),a_i).
```

Every factor is positive on an admitted path.  The family is normalized by
induction.  Prefix restriction `r_(n,m)` forgets the last `n-m` actions, and

```text
(r_(n,m))_* gamma_(b,n) = gamma_(b,m).
```

The proof is repeated normalization of the successor kernel.  Consequently

```text
r_(3,1) = r_(2,1) o r_(3,2)
```

both as maps and after pushing forward the path law.  The timed cylinder has
the product of the holding densities above and obeys the same identity after
the forgotten action and delay are integrated.

### 3.5 Record restriction

A finite record region retains selected immutable source records.  For every
causal parent slot it stores

```text
INTERNAL(source_record_id)   when the parent is retained;
EXTERNAL(source_record_id)   otherwise.
```

Thus restriction is parent-total even when it is not parent-closed as a raw
record subset.  Because the source identifier never changes, direct and
staged restriction agree:

```text
r_(F,D) = r_(E,D) o r_(F,E),     D contained in E contained in F.
```

The view's external-reference census also includes every current authenticated
witness-frontier head omitted from the retained region.  Thus the typed
boundary contains omitted causal-parent references and omitted current
frontier references; both categories retain their immutable source IDs.

D38b's finite regional view additionally carries the complete authenticated
history as an explicit admission witness.  The current frontier and incidence
are re-derived from that witness.  Regional append must equal the exact next
typed event at that frontier, advances the witness and then transports the new
record into the region.  A second individually authentic event from the old
head rejects.

The complete witness is an existence construction, not a locality theorem.
A smaller localized admission certificate remains open.

### Theorem 1 — chosen-law record closure

For every finite history admitted by the chosen D34b process and every
positive finite actor-star cylinder:

1. the current discrete actor rows, wire heads and simple-edge incidence are
   determined by the typed record DAG;
2. every next admitted event emits the records needed to determine its
   successor discrete boundary state;
3. `B_A` screens the declared C/L relevant-event future and updates from its
   old value, relative elapsed increment and one typed event;
4. the kernels `gamma_(b,n)` are normalized and projective under prefix
   restriction;
5. record restriction composes on nested finite regions; and
6. admitted event transport commutes with restriction when the complete
   finite witness certifies the exact successor.

Hence the chosen D34b law admits an authenticated record-closed oriented
finite-cylinder presentation after its supplied finite seed.

### Proof

Items 1 and 2 follow by induction on the event DAG.  Seed rows and incidence
are fixed.  An accepted event names the current touched-wire heads, so adding
it preserves acyclicity and gives one new head on each touched wire.  Its exact
post-rows determine the successor discrete boundary state.  Birth's Ulam
ordinal makes the child identity fresh and supplies its incidence and clock
address.  Future exponential deadlines and marks are sampled by the supplied
clock law; they are not part of this reconstruction theorem.

Item 3 is the D34e generator partition.  The five retained row types above
exhaust every event that can change `B_A`; every omitted event preserves it.
The rates are functions of the root row and neighbor degree/birth rows.

Item 4 follows from normalization of `kappa` and repeated conditional
composition.  Exact competing-exponential integration supplies the timed
normalization.  Item 5 follows because an omitted original parent or current
frontier head receives the same external source reference whether it is
omitted in one restriction or an earlier stage.  For item 6, both paths append
the same exact witnessed successor and then apply the same immutable source-ID
classification.  ∎

## 4. What the theorem does not make small

### 4.1 Radius one is not bounded capacity

The star boundary has graph radius one.  It does not have a uniform number of
neighbor rows, ports, edges, identifiers or counter bits.  A's birth process
has unbounded support at every positive construction time and diverges almost
surely over an infinite realization.  Paper 22 therefore proves that its
constructed physical B3 carrier has unbounded width.

D38b's receipt reports the fields separately.  One specimen has radius one,
six boundary actors, seven physical history records, ten oriented endpoint-
port incidences over five edges and maximum causal parent arity two.  Its
compact root B3 has five root-owned ports.  Across the family, identifier and
total width are unbounded and the ideal elapsed coordinate is continuous.

### 4.2 Full ancestry uses a different boundary

Branch F observes every future event touching A together with its complete
persistent typed predecessor ancestry and elapsed occurrence time.  For that
query, a radius-one star is not sufficient.

Paper 22 proves that every complete fixed-radius carrier fails.  Paper 23 then
proves the exact finite-stop result: the minimal predictive quotient is the
current rooted marked component class `[K_A]_g`.  Every deterministic exact
sufficient carrier determines that class.  The statement is compatible with
distributed storage and lossless coding; it does not say one actor privately
stores a global ledger.

### Theorem 2 — local-support/predictive-width separation

For the chosen D34b law:

1. every physical event touches at most two actors;
2. one constructed physical carrier sufficient for the C/L query is
   actor-star-local and has unbounded width over growth, while its weak/timed
   minimal quotient and any different bounded carrier remain open;
3. at every licensed finite stop the exact Branch-F predictive quotient is
   `[K_A]_g`; and
4. for every M there are `2^M` reachable component classes with pairwise
   different Branch-F conditional laws.

Therefore bounded local event support coexists with necessarily unbounded
exact Branch-F predictive information width.

### Proof

Item 1 is immediate from birth, idle and interaction arities.  Item 2 is Paper
22's B3 sufficiency and growth theorem; it is not a C/L minimality theorem.
Item 3 is Paper 23's anchored tomography theorem.  Its depth-marked binary
family gives item 4 and forces at least M worst-case bits.  ∎

This theorem says nothing about a completed infinite history point.  D34f
explicitly leaves the timed or infinite predictive inverse-limit bridge open.

## 5. The anchored-prefix criterion

### 5.1 Why “a fresh anchor exists” is insufficient

The first D38 pin proposed six informal premises: persistence, growth,
returnability, a fresh anchor, an exact query and no seal.  That list was
underhypothesized.  A fresh anchor can make a return path observable without
making its prefix unique to one past.  Paper 23's failed bare-sweep proof is
the counterexample: two different cuts reproduce one three-event future trace
with leading coefficients `1/1152` and `1/576`.

The required premise is not merely anchoring.  It is anchored-prefix
separation.

### Lemma 1 — anchored-prefix separation criterion

Let K range over finite current component classes for a fixed law and query.
Assume:

1. the conditional future law factors through K;
2. K is reconstructible from the retained persistent records; and
3. for every K there are a measurable future prefix `U_K(Delta)` and integer
   `q(K)` such that, for every nonisomorphic K',

```text
liminf_(Delta downarrow 0)
  P(U_K(Delta)|K) / Delta^q  > 0,

P(U_K(Delta)|K') = 0
or
P(U_K(Delta)|K') = O(Delta^(q+1)).
```

Then K is the minimal exact predictive quotient up to equality of classes.  If
there are `2^M` pairwise separated reachable classes, every deterministic
exact carrier needs at least M worst-case bits.

### Proof

Sufficiency is premise 1.  For K different from K', the displayed small-time
orders make the two conditional measures unequal, so no predictive quotient
may identify them.  The counting lower bound follows because a deterministic
carrier with fewer than M bits has fewer than `2^M` values.  ∎

The lemma's premises are sufficient.  This paper does not prove each one
individually necessary.

### 5.2 D34b instance

D34f instantiates the lemma.  Wire ancestry is persistent and reconstructs the
current component.  A postorder collection path returns every pre-stop record
to A.  A fresh anchor is broadcast and echoed in `q=2n-1` events.  The
first-unmatched-attachment and catch-up lemmas show that a different past
cannot reproduce the target prefix at the same order: it is absent or first
appears at order at least `q+1`.

The receipt does not recompute that all-size proof.  It hash-locks the accepted
D34f source and output, checks `q=3,5,...,19`, preserves the bare and anchored
coefficients and carries the `2^M` family through `M=10` as regression data.

## 6. Ontological and operational width

It is useful to report two questions:

```text
W_ont      information identifying the persistent rooted marked history;
W_op       information needed for one alphabet, horizon and error tolerance.
```

For exact unsealed Branch F at unlimited horizon, the finite-stop theorem
identifies both with the current component quotient.  Changing the query can
make `W_op` smaller while the chosen D34b law's persistent records remain in
the history.  The broader No Silent Erasure principle is `[POSITED]` and is not
needed for this statement.

The following are distinct modifications:

- a finite future horizon can exclude a witness whose earliest return lies
  later;
- a coarser ancestry alphabet can merge record distinctions by declaration;
- an epsilon threshold can ignore sufficiently attenuated differences;
- a finite cap on complete marked histories bounds the capped model; and
- a hypothetical exact-zero read cut can preserve a record ontologically
  while forbidding later service at one interface.

D38b uses small abstract controls only to illustrate these changed questions.
It does not prove a premise-minimality theorem.  It does not identify the
exact-zero cut with V6 sealing: the corpus's sealing language is dispersal and
operational inaccessibility at declared alphabets, while Paper 23 leaves a
record-native permanently nonreturnable cut open.

Nonzero attenuation deserves special care.  At exact infinite precision,
`2^{-r}` is nonzero at every finite r, so it continues to distinguish two
conditional laws.  Attenuation bounds an operational boundary only after an
alphabet, tolerance or exact zero is declared.

## 7. What physical record birth would cost—conditionally

D38b's classical records are not automatically D26 BORN carriers.  A physical
bridge would have to identify:

1. which maintenance records are actual births rather than TOKEN activation;
2. their coherent parent line;
3. their coupling g; and
4. the record count N represented by the monitored system.

If such a bridge identifies N repeated BORN carriers on one specified parent
line, D26 gives

```text
V_N/V_0 = (1-g)^(N/2).
```

At `g=9/25`, this is `(4/5)^N`; three births give `64/125`.  A pre-existing
coherence-neutral dormant TOKEN gives factor one but presupposes the capacity.
The comparison would make record-boundary upkeep empirically constrainable.

No such bridge is constructed here.  Therefore the paper derives neither a
universal boundary-maintenance price nor a rate.

## 8. Record closure does not select the law

The construction begins with D34b's supplied seed and rates.  Record closure
forces those rates and event rows to maintain the boundary records their own
later cylinders use.  It does not prove that a different closed family cannot
exist and does not derive the numbers `1/4,1/4,1/2`.

Paper 26 already proves nonselection on supplied conflict carriers.  K3, K2
and finite marked K1 survive different locality widths.  D38b adds a closure
condition but no theorem that closure uniquely fixes coupling data.

A root-free random generated network also needs more than local row
normalization.  The mass-transport principle is the appropriate invariance
language: for every nonnegative covariant transport, expected mass sent from
a random root equals expected mass received.  D38b checks one transport on
three finite uniformly rooted graphs and a deterministic-center negative
control.  It does not prove all-transport unimodularity, construct an infinite
unimodular completion or select the rates.

The sharpened successor question is therefore:

> Which action/history principle or empirical identification map selects the
> coupling data and root-free completion phase among record-closed laws?

## 12. Candidate conclusion

Paper 26 built a boundary language on a supplied complex.  D34b built a
generator whose predictive boundaries were known but not presented as one
record-closed regional object.  The finite classical interface now exists.

The boundary is made of records in a literal, discrete sense.  Current rows,
heads and simple-edge incidence are recovered from authenticated event
ancestry.  Birth writes the new actor's incidence.  The star kernel reads
record-realized fields.  Its output is valid input for the next cylinder.
Nested record restriction preserves omitted causal parents and omitted current
frontier heads as typed external references, and witnessed regional append
cannot fork one current head.  Future clock marks remain supplied by the law.

The same construction also makes the program's central locality distinction
unavoidable.  A law can change at most two actors per event while requiring
unbounded information for exact unlimited-horizon Branch-F prediction.  For
coarse A-wire futures, one proved sufficient carrier is the growing radius-one
B3 star, but its minimality is open.  For complete ancestry forever, the
finite-stop predictive identity is necessarily the whole current component
class.

The surviving result is therefore:

```text
AUTHENTICATED RECORD-CLOSED ORIENTED FINITE-CYLINDER PRESENTATION /
CHOSEN D34b / COMPLETE FINITE ADMISSION WITNESS /
BOUNDED LOCAL EVENT SUPPORT WITH UNBOUNDED EXACT FINITE-STOP PREDICTIVE WIDTH /
NONSELECTING.
```

The next paper should not rebuild this interface.  It should attack selection:
the action bridge and root-free completion conditions that can distinguish
among record-closed laws, with D26 visibility and click tomography serving as
empirical constraints rather than architectural axioms.
