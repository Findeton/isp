**Status:** frozen before D16 executable outcomes and coefficient choices,

## 1. Question

nongravitational qubit packet.  D16 asks:

> Does exact relabeling/general covariance plus finite causal-interval
> locality select a regional action on finite causal orders, and can any

The investigation begins with the full interval-count family rather than
inserting Benincasa–Dowker coefficients by name.

## 2. Frozen source class

A history is a finite partial order `C=(X,prec)` with typed boundary
antichains/collars when composition is tested.  Two presentations related by
a bijection preserving `prec`, boundary types and ownership represent the
same history.

For each comparable pair `x prec y`, define

```math
n(x,y)=|{z : x prec z prec y}|,
qquad N_k(C)=|{(x,y):x prec y, n(x,y)=k}|.
```

The frozen scalar interval-action family is

```math
S_{alpha,beta}(C)=alpha |X| + sum_{k=0}^{K} beta_k N_k(C).
```

Benincasa–Dowker/Glaser actions may later enter as particular dimension- and
scale-dependent coefficient packets, with provenance checked against primary
sources.  D16 may not assume those coefficients are uniquely selected.

## 3. Gates

### C0 — exact partial orders

Reflexivity convention, antisymmetry, transitivity, boundary ownership and
isomorphism are explicit.  Non-posets and boundary-type violations reject.

### C1 — label gauge

`N_k`, `S` and every closed amplitude are exactly invariant under every tested
relabeling.  Canonicalization or orbit counting must not introduce label
weights.

### C2 — locality meaning

State precisely that interval-count dependence is order-intrinsic and
quasilocal/nonlocal in causal-set language; do not equate it with nearest-
neighbor locality or continuum microcausality.

### C3 — coefficient nonselection

Search the full frozen finite coefficient class needed by the witness.  If two
inequivalent coefficient packets share covariance/support/composition gates
but give different closed amplitudes, covariance and interval locality are
proved nonselecting.

### C4 — dimension provenance

For any BDG coefficient packet, record which continuum dimension,
nonlocality/discreteness scale and manifoldlike approximation selected it.
Dimension inserted into coefficients is not an emergent prediction.

### C5 — regional gluing

Define boundary data and the exact sum/quotient used to glue two finite causal
regions.  Automorphism factors and shared-boundary action terms must be owned
once.  A failed naive-additivity control is mandatory.

### C6 — quantum measure

Distinguish `exp(iS)` weights from a normalized/decoherent quantum measure over
alternative orders.  State, measure, gauge volume and convergence data must
be explicit.

### C7 — records and birth

Map at least one causal-order/matter interaction through the real D14 typed
record interface.  Pointer environment, commit, protected future, live collar
and join entitlement may not be hidden inside the order relation.

### C8 — no global clock

Relabeling invariance and whole-history summation may remove a birth-label
order, but D16 must not infer a locally computable sequential growth law.
Presentation gauge, evaluation gauge and physical support/weights remain
distinct.

### C9 — geometry predictions

Before V9 access, derive from the frozen packet:

```text
dimension estimator target and finite-size correction;
influence/propagation observable;
cone anisotropy prediction versus scale;
proper-unit bridge and role of G;
parameter-free or independently fitted uncertainty band.
```

If the packet assumes a four-dimensional sprinkling or tunes on V9, the result
is calibration, not emergence or untouched prediction.

### C10 — empirical discriminator

At least two complete candidates must make fixed differing predictions before
an untouched holdout is opened.  Otherwise the holdout remains sealed.

### C11 — hostile closure

Independent order-theory/action, ontology/locality and clean-room reviews must
reproduce the exact census, attack automorphism/gluing factors and enforce the
claim ceiling.
