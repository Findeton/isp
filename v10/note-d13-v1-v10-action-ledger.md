## 3. The apparent exceptions

### 3.1 V4 Paper 25

V4 Paper 25 says the active finite alphabet closes on a pure effective-GR
branch.  Its own formulas expose the conditioning.  The generating functional
contains `S_alpha` and `mu_alpha^0` as inputs.  The uniqueness theorem assumes
EU1–EU9, including source completeness and one calibration; the later cokernel
audit proves completeness only relative to the active `A*R*K*E*` move
alphabet.  An action outside that alphabet is classified as a future typed
extension, not excluded by nature.  Therefore Paper 25 is a conditional
normal-form/anomaly theorem, not a selection of the microscopic history law.

### 3.2 V6 Paper 4

V6 Paper 4 contains both the strongest positive and the decisive correction.
The complete closed-holonomy RN/Möbius ledger reconstructs a finite positive


reference and functional are fixed.  It selects coefficients inside that
arena; it does not select the arena, complex phase, interaction, or boundary
state.  D12's exact iSWAP-family witness confirms this distinction.

## 4. Corpus theorem

The corpus fixes four layers that a final action must respect:

1. finite sealed diamonds and durable records;
2. additive evidence and multiplicative survival for an already existing
   opportunity;
3. local collar ownership, construction-order gauge for disjoint moves, and
   whole-history/projective consistency;
4. complete RN/holonomy coordinates that identify a supplied law.

It does not fix:

```text
field and collar species;
which new interaction opportunity exists;
the complex local amplitude;
the boundary/initial quantum state;
the record/decoherence instrument;
dimensionful scales or G.
```

Accordingly no older paper contains an overlooked complete action.  The
strongest inherited object is an exact coordinate-and-gluing architecture for
a law, not the selected law itself.
