## 1. The question

Capability is settled: the grammar CAN build records of unbounded
order dimension (D45b).  Typicality is not: under the theory's OWN
law — the completed transfer / kernel candidate, not a
hand-imposed distribution — do records of growing WIDTH and
dimension carry appreciable mass, or is dimension an exotic
accident?  This is the first sampled study in the corpus; it is
therefore also a METHOD unit: declared seeds, exact per-path
weights, and an honest separation of what is enumerated (exact)
from what is sampled (estimated).
