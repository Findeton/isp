# D18 literature and rival-architecture audit

**Status:** pre-review audit, 2026-07-11.

## Result

The literature supports the D18 separation between a history container and a
physical selector.  None of the audited architectures currently derives one
unique interacting quantum-gravitational universe law with state, couplings,
records and scales fixed.

| architecture | what it supplies | selection ceiling |
|---|---|---|
| general-boundary QFT | amplitudes for arbitrary regions with state spaces on boundaries and gluing/probability axioms | needs a particular theory/action, state and well-defined measure |
| decoherent histories | class operators, decoherence functional and probabilities for decoherent alternatives | action and initial condition are physical inputs; preferred quasiclassical records are emergent and nontrivial |
| quantum measure theory | spacetime history functional beyond ordinary additive probability | full event-domain extension can fail; the functional is dynamics, not selected by the formalism |
| classical causal-set sequential growth | discrete covariance and Bell causality constrain growth to a family | free coupling sequence remains; it is classical |
| quantum sequential growth | attempts operator-valued causal growth | no general noncommutative solution is known; a 2026 analysis finds severe algebraic constraints and no two-dimensional Pauli realization |
| process tensors/quantum combs | complete operational representation/tomography of multi-time non-Markov processes | represents or learns a supplied process; does not choose nature's process |
| canonical Hamiltonian evolution | efficient evolution relative to a chosen slicing/state | the Hamiltonian, constraints, state and clock treatment are supplied; covariance must be recovered |
| stochastic/collapse modifications | a concrete click/record ontology | add rates, preferred stochastic structure or other new coefficients requiring evidence |

## Primary-source boundary

- Oeckl's [general-boundary foundations](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0509122)
  associate amplitudes to arbitrary spacetime regions and states to their
  boundaries.  This is the closest established architecture to sealed-diamond
  sewing, but it is a framework for supplied theories.
- Gell-Mann and Hartle's [quantum-cosmology history
  program](https://arxiv.org/abs/1803.04605) makes probabilities conditional on
  decoherence and treats quasiclassical domains as consequences of both the
  action and the initial condition.  That explicitly supports keeping state
  separate from action.
- Sorkin's [quantum measure proposal](https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/9507057)
  treats the history law as the basic dynamical object rather than an ordinary
  path probability.
- Dowker, Johnston and Surya show in [On extending the quantum
  measure](https://arxiv.org/abs/1007.2725) that even strongly-positive
  decoherence functionals need not extend from cylinder events to the full
  generated sigma algebra.  Hence a profinite/cylinder law must not be called a
  full quantum event measure without an extension theorem.
- Boes and Navascues show that [strong positivity is singled out by
  composability](https://arxiv.org/abs/1609.09723), motivating D18's stronger
  gate rather than diagonal nonnegativity alone.
- Rideout and Sorkin derive a [family of classical sequential-growth
  laws](https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/9904062) from discrete covariance and a
  causality condition.  “Family” is decisive: the principles constrain but do
  not uniquely select numerical dynamics.
- Srivastava and Surya's 2026 [quantum Bell-causality
  investigation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.25503) reports that natural
  operator orderings collapse to commutative algebras, while their attempted
  simplest noncommutative representation is inconsistent.  Quantum causal-set
  growth remains an active open problem, not a hidden complete rulebook that
  SHARD overlooked.

## Priority statement

The decoherence-functional/history architecture and the distinction between
law, state and records are established ideas.  D18's contribution is the
corpus-specific necessity ledger: it maps those distinctions onto sealed
diamonds, owned collars, projective records, construction-order gauge,
profinite limits, the dimension/scale bridge and the exact D13-D17
counterexamples.  No priority claim is made for the general formalism.
