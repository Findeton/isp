## 6. New literature opening

Egri, Gömöri, Gyenis, and Hofer-Szabó,
[arXiv:2602.23491](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.23491), distinguish a trajectory
of probabilities from a probability on trajectories and prove generic
nonuniqueness of stochastic implementations.  Their paper directly challenges
claims made in stochastic-quantum correspondence work.  D12 does not decide
that dispute.  Its path-measure disintegration theorem is elementary and
independent of the correspondence; the new source strengthens, rather than
weakens, D12's warning that instantaneous probability dynamics is not the
missing whole-history rulebook.
