## Abstract

The sealed-record program has a strong conditional law for the firing of an
already-supplied evidence channel,

$$
\Pr(\text{no seal}\mid\Delta I)=e^{-\Delta I},
$$

but it does not yet have the full law that generates candidate records,
chooses their parents and joint support, assigns their forward/reverse
likelihood blocks, and determines their interaction marks. This distinction
was obscured when v8–v9 fleet builders were used to grow spacetime-like webs.
Those builders supplied a live fleet, renewal/reset rules, committer and victim
choices, directional deposits, and later transfer or diffusion couplings.
Their geometry results remain measurements of those builder classes; the
builders are not derived dynamics of sealed holonomy diamonds.

A corpus-wide audit recovers a more relevant, but unfinished, route. V1–v5
developed finite whole-slab laws, exchange defects, projective naturality,
boundary centers, effective fiber laws, and same-process nonfactorization.
V6 combined these ingredients into sealed modular/holonomy diamonds, derived a
primitive binary event law within a stated arena, formulated complete-screen
composition and the no-silent-seam criterion, and proved that covariance,
positivity, locality, and projectivity alone cannot select a unique nontrivial
process law. V7 then found an exact finite forward-law witness: a positive
$h$-transform on a nonreconstructive shadow of a residual diamond-boundary
center. The witness remained dependent on hidden presentation counts, and the
intrinsic boundary-work potential producing its $h$-weight was not derived.
V8 therefore correctly retained seal placement as open. V9 subsequently
proved, at finite conditional classical scope, that construction order can be
quotiented out but that shared evidence allocation and conservative transfer
remain unselected.

The resulting v10 target is not another fleet dial. It is a marked-diamond
birth kernel:

$$
\boxed{
\text{sealed history}
\to
\text{admissible diamond extensions}
\to
\text{minimal no-silent center}
\to
\text{positive nonreconstructive shadow}
\to
\text{intrinsic }h\text{-weight}
\to
\text{new sealed record}.
}
$$

The central proposed interaction criterion is relational rather than metric:
a joint support is licensed when the current shared screen fails to screen an
above-tolerance record-visible dependence, and the smallest intrinsic center
that closes that residue must be printed. This architecture does not yet
derive the law. It identifies the exact missing physics and organizes the
decisive v10 campaigns.

## 1. The correction that starts v10

Three questions must be kept separate.

1. **Firing:** given a specified evidence channel, when does it seal?
2. **Commitment:** given a specified candidate diamond and proposition, what
   becomes a durable event?
3. **Birth:** given only the committed history, which candidate diamond,
   parent set, support, likelihood block, outcome, and transfer may occur next?

The corpus has real answers to parts of the first two questions. It does not
yet answer the third.

The one-lineage evidence law constrains a conditional survival probability:

$$
S(\Delta I)=e^{-\Delta I}.
$$

It does not construct the channel whose evidence is $\Delta I$. It does not
select the alternatives $P_{AB},P_{BA}$, the participating records, their
parentage, or the content/outcome produced at commitment.

Accordingly, the phrase "record click law" will be typed in v10:

- **clock law:** survival conditional on a supplied evidence exposure;
- **seal law:** the internal event rule of a supplied candidate diamond;
- **birth law:** the generative kernel for new candidate records;
- **interaction law:** the joint-support, outcome, and transfer part of the
  birth law;
- **history law:** the projectively compatible measure generated by repeated
  births after quotienting construction presentation.

No result about one type is promoted silently to another.

## 2. Evidence grades used in this review

This paper uses five grades.

- **[THEOREM/EXACT]:** proved in the stated finite or analytic arena.
- **[CONDITIONAL]:** proved given named premises or supplied objects.
- **[FINITE-WITNESS]:** exact in a bounded audited family but not universal.
- **[POSITED/BUILDER]:** a modeling choice used to generate or test examples.
- **[OPEN]:** the required object or theorem is not supplied.

The original papers sometimes used stronger local vocabulary. This review
grades by the conclusion that survived the later corpus and its
supersession ledger.

## 3. Three different meanings of "diamond"

The word has been used for different mathematical objects.

### 3.1 Sealed record or holonomy diamond

The SHARD primitive proposed in v6 is

$$
D=(\Omega_D,\mu_D,\text{screens},\text{collar},\text{transports}).
$$

It is a bounded finite record process with lower/upper interfaces, boundary
memory, internal comparison maps, and a whole-history law. This is the diamond
relevant to fundamental record birth.

### 3.2 Causal or Alexandrov diamond

Later geometry papers use a bounded region of Minkowski spacetime and a
sprinkling into it. These diamonds are reference populations for dimension,
tomography, and cone shape. They are not automatically sealed holonomy
diamonds.

### 3.3 Commutation or construction diamond

The paths $AB$ and $BA$ form a square used to test whether two incomparable
updates have presentation-independent physical output. This is a diagrammatic
diamond, not a record packet.

The v9 corpus mostly uses the latter two meanings. A reference-diamond or
commutation-diamond result cannot by itself supply holonomy-diamond dynamics.

## 4. V1–V5: the pre-diamond ingredients

The literal diamond ontology enters in v6, but its ingredients were developed
earlier.

### 4.1 Finite slabs and temporal indivisibility

V1 rejected an infinitesimal bare stochastic generator as the complete
physical object. Exact finite slabs retained information missed by the first
low-order generator and did not generally factor into repeated Markov steps.
This is the ancestor of the diamond as a bounded whole-process object.

### 4.2 Local comparison and exchange defect

Localized deformations were compared through finite endpoint laws. Their
order-sensitive exchange defect supplied the conceptual ancestor of the v6
closed exchange holonomy.

### 4.3 Projective naturality and boundary centers

V2 required finite endpoint laws and comparison maps to commute with
coarse-graining. It also established that boundary-flux/center data cannot be
erased by an unfibered projection without changing the physical statement.
This becomes the screen/collar and later no-silent-center discipline.

### 4.4 Effective laws and missing-center promotion

V4 developed the principle that hidden alternatives can be summed within a
visible sector, but any projected likelihood residue that affects physical
predictions must be promoted as visible center data. This is the statistical
ancestor of the no-silent seam.

### 4.5 Same-process nonfactorization

V5 treated entanglement as irreducible joint constraint inside one process,
compatible with local readouts and no signaling. Consequently, diamond
locality cannot mean product factorization or nearest-neighbor metric
locality. A boundary may carry genuinely multi-record constraints.

## 5. V6: the diamond becomes a primitive

### 5.1 Modular deletion diamond [DEFINED; universal realization OPEN]

For a candidate event $x$ inside a bounded diamond, v6 compared the retained
and eventless-repaired laws:

$$
P_x,
\qquad
P_{\setminus x}.
$$

With intrinsic shell filtration $\mathcal F_{x,k}$, the scalar deletion
profile was

$$
M_x(k)
=
D\!\left(P_x|\mathcal F_{x,k}\middle\|P_{\setminus x}|\mathcal F_{x,k}\right).
$$

The corpus correctly found that this scalar is not complete. The full event
germ must retain the two laws, shell filtration, role maps, units, and action.
Deletion is not time reversal or a physical erasure. It is a counterfactual
boundary-preserving repair used to measure the role of the event.

### 5.2 Sealed holonomy diamond [POSITED primitive]

V6 Paper 4 proposed the sealed finite record diamond as the primitive SHARD
object. For internally available transports $T_A,T_B$, the exchange defect is

$$
\Delta_{AB}=T_BT_A-T_AT_B.
$$

A complete count-symmetric event proposition $E$ obeys

$$
E^2=E,
\qquad
\operatorname{Var}_{\mu_D}(q_{\rm raw}\mid E)=0,
\qquad
E\leftrightarrow1-E.
$$

Within the imported/conditional screen calculus, this fixes a primitive binary
readout. It does not prove that nature supplies all candidate diamonds or
select which candidate commits.

### 5.3 Primitive work balance [EXACT in the binary arena]

The one-diamond balance

$$
D(P_\eta\|\mu_D)=\operatorname{Var}_{P_\eta}(q)
$$

has the unique nontrivial binary root

$$
\eta_*=1.090344354879492\ldots,
\qquad
W_*=0.364784952089976\ldots.
$$

This is a capacity/event-law result for a normalized primitive binary defect.
V6 explicitly discarded it as a universal uniqueness law on arbitrary finite
diamonds.

### 5.4 Multi-diamond composition [CONDITIONAL]

Linear retained-holonomy composition plus invariant screen weight selects the
quadratic $p=2$ norm in the two-diamond packet. Retained holonomy can also make
the visible event process non-Markovian after hidden holonomy is marginalized.
The selection is conditional on the composition premises; it is not yet a
general birth law.

### 5.5 Complete screens and the no-silent seam [KEY PRINCIPLE]

Let adjacent bounded processes have lower data $X$, shared screen $Y$, and
upper data $Z$. Exact eventless composition through $Y$ requires

$$
I(X;Z\mid Y)=0.
$$

If the residual is positive, the boundary is incomplete. The smallest
intrinsic augmentation $H$ that gives

$$
I(X;Z\mid Y,H)=0
$$

is a licensed center. If no bounded center closes the residue, the boundary
must expand or the residue must be printed as a new physical sector.

This principle is consistency plus an information-promotion rule. It does not
by itself set the probability or magnitude of the new sector.

### 5.6 Shared collars as interaction [FINITE/CONDITIONAL]

V6 joined diamonds through eventless collars. The collar network supplied an
allowed-change operator $L$, while an event supplied a centered deletion
source $\rho$:

$$
L\phi=\rho.
$$

The response then deformed future stochastic transport:

$$
K_\phi(i,j)
\propto
K_0(i,j)e^{-(\phi_j-\phi_i)/2}.
$$

This is a genuine interaction architecture: a sealed event modifies later
record probabilities through a shared intrinsic collar. It was proved only in
scoped finite/scalar refinement classes and does not supply the general collar
network or full Einstein dynamics.

### 5.7 Unique-process-law no-go [EXACT finite no-go]

V6 exhibited continuous families of positive, covariant, projective,
nontrivial whole-diamond laws with the same local receipts. Convex mixing with
the uniform projective law preserves the structural conditions. Therefore

$$
\boxed{
\text{sealed ontology}
+\text{covariance}
+\text{positivity}
+\text{projectivity}
\not\Rightarrow
\text{unique nontrivial process law}.
}
$$

A selecting action, work constraint, likelihood principle, or new physical
axiom is necessary.

### 6.1 The evidence clock [CONDITIONAL law]

The evidence-clock result must not be identified with the primitive capacity

distinct from $\eta_*$ by approximately $0.4809664914$.

### 6.2 Content supply and spacing [PARTIAL]

The odometer has coboundary form along a supplied path,

$$

$$

but the physical potential and increment magnitudes are not selected. The
one-diamond capacity bounds an admissible primitive content, while the corpus

### 6.3 Parallel/joint diamonds [UNDERDETERMINED]

Sequential refinement preserves the exponential skeleton but does not force
additivity across parallel chains. A joint interaction term $\chi_{AB}$ can
vary while marginal laws remain fixed. No-signaling constrains the outcome
family but does not select the joint likelihood content.

This is the v7 precursor of v9 Paper 9's explicit shared-evidence
underdetermination theorem.

### 6.4 Diamond-boundary center and positive shadow [FINITE-WITNESS]

Papers 30–35 developed a forward extension law on finite unlabeled record
orders. Let $C$ be the current committed record, $P$ a possible one-record
extension, and $D(P,C)$ the multiplicity of deletions of $P$ that return $C$.
Given a positive effective weight $h$, the forward law is

$$
\Pr(P\mid C)
=
\frac{D(P,C)h_{N+1}(P)}
{\sum_{P'}D(P',C)h_{N+1}(P')}.
$$

The audited $N=9$ permutation-order sector exhibited an exact held-out forward
law on a proper nonreconstructive shadow of a residual diamond center. This is
a serious finite witness because it simultaneously supplies an extension
kernel, quotients hidden presentation, and avoids full record lookup.

It is not a universal physical law. Its effective $h$-values were still
taught by hidden presentation multiplicity.

### 6.5 The missing boundary-work potential [OPEN]

=

\left[
\psi_{\mathcal P_N}(h)+\sum_j e^{-h_j}

$$

where $\mathcal P_N$ is the selected positive shadow. But commitment alone
does not determine $\psi$: for any chosen positive target, one can construct a

The central unproved object is therefore

$$
\boxed{

=
\text{record-intrinsic boundary work of the no-silent diamond shadow}.

$$

### 6.6 Bounded-history diamonds and spacetime onset [CONDITIONAL program]

Late v7 reinterpreted a diamond as a bounded experiment/history rather than
the whole universe. It correctly observed

$$
R_1\not\Rightarrow\text{spacetime onset}.
$$

A single record has no interval, overlap, loop, transport comparison, or
persistent boundary residue. A sufficiently rich pre-geometric seed plus

## 9. The surviving v10 architecture

Let $H$ be a finite canonical marked sealed history. A full birth law should
have the form

$$
\mathbb B_H(de,dS,d\pi,dm),
$$

where:

- $e$ is a candidate new diamond/event;
- $S$ is its finite participating support;
- $\pi$ is its parent/ancestry incidence;
- $m$ contains its event type, likelihood block, seal, content, and outcome
  marks.

The leading candidate construction has six stages.

### Stage A: admissible candidate extensions

From $H$, construct a covariant finite or locally finite set

$$
\operatorname{Ext}(H)
$$

of candidate sealed-diamond refinements. Candidates may extend one lineage,
branch from a recorded ancestor, or form a joint event only when licensed by
record-intrinsic boundary data.

### Stage B: no-silent center

For each candidate, compute the current shared-screen residual. In the exact
finite idealization,

$$
\epsilon(e;H)=I(X;Z\mid Y).
$$

If $\epsilon=0$, the seam is complete. If $\epsilon>0$, generate the smallest
record-intrinsic center $C_e$ that closes the target-relevant residue. A center
is admitted only because the boundary requires it, not because it improves a
later geometric score.

### Stage C: projective nonreconstructive shadow

The complete center may be too fine. Select a quotient

$$
\mathcal P_e\preceq C_e
$$

that is projectively stable, positive/reflection-compatible, deletion/insertion
controlled, target-sufficient, and explicitly below reconstruction.

### Stage D: intrinsic boundary work

Derive a potential

$$
\psi_{\mathcal P_e}
$$

from the diamond's own screen/collar response and RN likelihood ledger. This
is the first-priority missing theorem. It may not use hidden presentation
counts, external geometry, fitted future outcomes, or an arbitrary work
target.

### Stage E: positive extension weight

Generate a positive $h$-weight, for example through the commitment-shaped
variational equation

$$
\nabla\psi_{\mathcal P}(h)=e^{-h},
$$

provided the preceding stage proves that this is the correct physical
potential rather than an arbitrary convex representation.

### Stage F: normalized birth and sealing

For a discrete candidate family, the forward extension form is

$$
\Pr(e\mid H)
=
\frac{D(e,H)h(e)}{\sum_{e'}D(e',H)h(e')}.
$$

Once a candidate's typed evidence compensator is specified, the conditional
clock layer must reproduce

$$
\Pr(\text{no firing}\mid H,\Delta I_e)=e^{-\Delta I_e}.
$$

The serial simulation schedule is then quotiented from the canonical marked
history.

## 10. Interaction and the joining of records

The no-bootstrap theorem remains decisive: a rule that licenses new support
only inside existing support components can never merge disconnected
components.

The diamond audit suggests a sharper interpretation.

### 10.1 Truly factorized components

If two components have no common ancestry and their complete boundary law
factorizes, then their conditional-information residue is zero. The no-silent
rule does not join them. A bridge would require new boundary physics or a
larger hidden substrate.

### 10.2 Incompletely represented shared structure

If the visible seam gives

$$
I(X;Z\mid Y)>0,
$$

then the components were not independent at the level of the full record law.
A joint center does not create a correlation from nothing; it prints a shared
relation that the previous boundary failed to record.

This provides a candidate definition of fundamental locality:

> an interaction is local to a sealed record boundary when every influence is
> mediated by recorded ancestry, a shared screen/collar, or a newly promoted
> no-silent center.

This locality need not match emergent spatial proximity. Records far apart in
a later metric can be adjacent in the fundamental boundary/holonomy relation.

### 10.3 Root and branch law

Common-root branching can create descendants with inherited joint support,
but the root/branch rule itself is not derived. V10 must either derive a
boundary-seed law or carry the initial connected seed honestly as cosmological
boundary data.

## 11. Prioritized research avenues

### Priority 1: intrinsic no-silent center theorem

Construct the center from actual screen/collar likelihood transport rather
than hidden order presentations. Required result:

$$
\mathcal C(H,e)
=
\text{minimal covariant target-complete closure of the candidate seam}.

Required attacks include nonuniqueness of minimal centers, center growth to

### Priority 2: intrinsic boundary-work/$h$ theorem

Derive $\psi_{\mathcal P}$ and prove, or refute, that its commitment minimizer
equals the effective forward $h$-weight. This is the missing bridge already
identified in v7. A successful theorem must defeat the arbitrary-convex-
potential counterexample.

### Priority 3: marked-diamond birth functor

Upgrade the finite order extensions to marked events carrying typed lineage
ports, parentage, RN block identifiers, content, outcomes, and projected-joint
provenance. Prove covariance and finite restriction composition before any
profinite claim.

### Priority 4: support birth and initial conditions

Classify possible root laws:

1. one primitive record plus a derived branching rule;
2. one connected but relationally rich pre-geometric seed;
3. a projective boundary distribution over initial finite histories;
4. no absolute beginning, only consistent finite restrictions of an eternal
   history.

The corpus currently selects none of these.

### Priority 5: quantum joint-diamond law

Replace the finite classical common-cause outcome module with an actual
retained-holonomy joint law. The law must preserve no-signaling without
assuming Bell factorization and must distinguish signed interaction content
from nonnegative factorization evidence. The v7 transverse freedom and the
v9 classical underdetermination are mandatory controls.

### Priority 6: collar-mediated transport

Test whether event-induced future transport follows from shared diamond
collars, and whether the phenomenological v9 diffusion limit emerges from
many small collar-mediated transfers. The direction of derivation must be

$$
\text{diamond interaction}
\to
\text{effective diffusion},
$$

not the reverse.

### Priority 7: geometry after law

Only after a birth/interaction law is fixed should its grown histories face
the v9 dimension, cone-shape, manifoldlikeness, and scale-ladder instruments.
The old fleet builders remain controls and phenomenological comparators.

The smallest campaign capable of changing the ontology should avoid a new
large universe simulation.

### 12.1 Finite arena

Use small marked diamond histories with explicit lower/upper screens, collars,
forward/reverse likelihood tables, and all admissible one-record extensions.

### 12.2 Gates

1. **Center necessity:** positive seam residue is not erased.
2. **Factorized refusal:** a truly disconnected factorized pair is not joined.
3. **Minimality:** the selected center closes the residue and no proper
   retained subcenter does.
4. **Restriction:** center formation commutes with finite marked restriction.
5. **Construction gauge:** alternate scheduler presentations push forward to
   one canonical history law.
6. **Clock compatibility:** every supplied support reproduces conditional
   $e^{-\Delta I}$ renewal.
7. **Nonuniqueness attack:** if multiple centers or $h$-weights pass, report
   the remaining selector rather than choosing one.
8. **No hidden teacher:** no label multiplicity, true embedding coordinate, or
   future-held-out outcome enters the law.

### 12.3 Decision outcomes

- **Positive:** intrinsic boundary transport uniquely supplies a projective
  center and $h$-weight in the finite arena. Proceed to marked extension and
  scale.
- **Center positive, weight negative:** the joining rule advances, but the
  probability selector remains a new physical axiom.
- **Center nonunique:** no-silent promotion is necessary but not a complete
  interaction law; identify the missing minimality principle.
- **Factorized control joins:** reject the candidate as nonlocal/untyped.
- **Restriction fails:** reject the center construction before any continuum
  or profinite promotion.

All cancellation-sensitive likelihood and entropy calculations should use
exact rational/algebraic arithmetic where possible and at least 100 decimal
digits otherwise.

## 15. Conclusion

The corpus does contain more about interacting diamonds than the v9 builder
used. Its strongest unused structure is not a transfer coefficient or a new
clock. It is the combination

$$
\boxed{
\text{complete shared screen}
+
\text{no-silent center promotion}
+
\text{projective nonreconstructive shadow}
+
\text{positive forward }h\text{-transform}.
}
$$

This architecture can potentially explain which joint records are licensed,
how parentage enters a covariant forward extension, and why a simulator's
construction order need not be physical. It cannot yet fix its own positive
weight, candidate set, or initial seed. Those are not peripheral details;
they are the missing dynamics.

V10 therefore begins at the correct boundary:

> derive, or decisively refute, a record-intrinsic marked-diamond birth kernel
> whose joint supports are forced by no-silent boundary residue and whose
> positive extension weights come from the diamond's own boundary work.

Only after that law exists should the program ask what universe it grows.
