## 2. Resolved apparent conflicts

### 2.1 `exp(-I)` versus `exp(-kappa chi)`

`I` is the self-accounting RN/KL evidence used as the intrinsic hazard clock.
In that coordinate the exponential coefficient is one. `chi` is a chosen
record-content coordinate. The dense scalar representation has coefficient
`kappa`; identifying `chi` with `I` sets it to one, while converting a
different content coordinate or external parameter requires physical input.
V7's sparse regime further warns that multiplicativity is required only at
seal points, leaving the inter-seal profile free.

### 2.2 Markov collar versus non-Markov record law

An eventless collar may admit a reversible Markov presentation while the
sealed sequence remains indivisible: Chapman–Kolmogorov composition holds at
commits and may fail across unsealed intervals. A local threshold simulator is
therefore permitted only as a representation of collar survival; it cannot
replace `P_D^hist`.

### 2.3 One-diamond constants versus firing law

The KL=Fisher balance root fixes a one-diamond capacity constant. The vector
commitment fixed point selects coefficients/firing on a fixed ledger. They act
at different stages and are compatible. Neither selects the between-diamond
extension support.

### 2.4 Seal versus termination

An internal seal is a commit/refinement point. D8's rewrite retains the
instrument output on a new collar. Terminal observation is deletion of an
unobserved continuation from the description, not an internal annihilation
event. D11 conflated them.

### 2.5 Global order versus commit partial order

Commit order exists only on causally overlapping collars. Disjoint diamonds
have no physical relative order. A global linear extension can be used to
calculate only after its pushforward to the same canonical partial history is
proved presentation-independent.
