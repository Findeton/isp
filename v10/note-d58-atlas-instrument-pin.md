## 1. What a manifold needs that the corpus lacks

A manifold is local charts + large overlaps + coherent transitions +
homogeneous local structure.  The corpus has per-event skies and
nothing else: no overlap notion, no transition notion, no homogeneity
measurement.  D58 builds the FIRST TWO INSTRUMENTS and validates them
on sprinkling controls before touching grammar records.

## 2. The committed constructions

- **Chart at e:** the SKY-B(d) direction set D_e(d).
- **Overlap (the transition candidate):** for a cover pair e ⋖ e',
  the sets D_e(d) and D_e'(d−1) live at the SAME height layer; the
  transition is the identity on their intersection.  Content is in
  the **overlap fraction** ω(e, e') = |D_e(d) ∩ D_e'(d−1)| / |D_e(d)|
  — how much of one chart survives into its neighbor.  (Identity
  transitions cohere trivially; the cocycle question becomes real
  only once non-identity transitions exist — a declared later stage.)
- **Homogeneity:** the distribution of |D_e(d)| over ALL events of a
  record — a manifold-like record needs uniform width; one rich event
  in a poor record is the opposite of an atlas.
