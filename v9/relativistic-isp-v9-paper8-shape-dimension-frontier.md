## 1. The impossibility theorem and the corrected target

**Lemma (S_n in Minkowski).** For every n and every d ≥ 2, the standard example S_n (order dimension n) embeds in M^{1+d}. *Construction:* place the antichain a₁..a_n at t = 0 at spread unit spatial positions u_i (pairwise inner-product gap δ = min_{i≠j}(1 − u_i·u_j) > 0); place b_i at time T above the antipode −R·u_i. Then |−Ru_i − u_j|² = R² + 2R(u_i·u_j) + 1 is maximal exactly at j = i, so any T with √((R+1)² − 2Rδ) ≤ T < R+1 realizes a_j ≺ b_i ⟺ j ≠ i. Spread families exist on S^{d−1} for every n when d ≥ 2. ∎

Hence sprinkled M⁴ orders contain S_n for all n at positive probability: **M⁴'s order dimension is unbounded** (consistent with Meyer's theorem that Minkowski dimension and order dimension coincide only at d = 2 [Meyer 1993], and with the corpus's own tomography paper, which recorded the detachment and the celestial-clock decomposition — v8 paper 14 §1, Thm 2.1 — without connecting it to the dimension-from-channels line). (A consistency pointer to Müller, arXiv:2303.11237, is carried in the investigation note; that preprint is withdrawn.) The corpus's crown result — order dimension = C+1 exactly, S_{C+1}-witnessed (v9 paper 6; extended to a second class and second C at LEDGER #111) — is therefore the signature of a *finite clock set*, i.e., a polyhedral cone.
