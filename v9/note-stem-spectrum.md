## The objects (definitions fixed here, used verbatim in the paper)

- Omega-tilde(n) = naturally-labeled n-element causal sets (each element added above a down-set of its predecessors); end-deletion maps Omega-tilde(n+1) -> Omega-tilde(n); **Omega-tilde = lim<- Omega-tilde(n)** = the labeled history space, compact metrizable totally disconnected with clopen basis the cylinders — a light profinite set.
- A **stem** of a causet: a finite down-set, taken up to isomorphism (BDGHS). stem(b) = the event "b embeds as a stem". Countably many stems => the Boolean algebra **B** generated by {stem(b)} is countable; the distributive lattice **L** (no complements) likewise.
- **X = Stone(B)** (the stem spectrum); **X_le = Priestley(L)** (the ordered version). phi: Omega-tilde -> X by evaluation (a history answers every stem-question); phi factors through relabeling (stems are iso-invariant).

## The theorems (pinned statements; proofs in the paper)

- **T1:** Omega-tilde is a light profinite set whose clopen algebra is the cylinder algebra and whose Borel sigma-algebra is BDGHS's R-tilde. (Essentially definitional; unclaimed in any literature — the identification is the point.)
- **T2:** X is a light profinite set (B countable); phi is Borel (stem sets are countable unions of cylinders — BDGHS); phi^{-1}(Clopen(X)) = B; the sigma-algebra generated by stem sets equals phi^{-1}(Borel X).
- **T3 (rogues = non-separation):** phi(c) = phi(c') iff c, c' realize the same stems; the non-trivial fibers are exactly the rogue classes of BDGHS; for every CSG dynamics mu, the union of non-trivial fibers is mu-null (BDGHS Prop 1). The separated object is X (already Hausdorff): the defect lives in phi's injectivity, and it is named, localized, and null.
- **T4 (the measure-algebra isomorphism):** for every CSG dynamics mu, phi induces an isomorphism of measure algebras (R mod mu-null) ~ (Borel(X) mod phi*mu-null). [BDGHS Thm 1 + Cor 1, restructured; the new content is the object X and the two corollaries below.]
- **N3 (automatic extension on the spectrum):** every finitely-additive probability (more generally, bounded charge) on B extends uniquely to a Radon measure on X — on a compact zero-dimensional space, countable additivity on clopens is automatic (a countable disjoint clopen cover of a compact clopen is finite). Consequently: classical states on the stem algebra ARE Radon measures on the spectrum; the Dowker–Johnston–Surya quantum non-extension is RELOCATED (not cured): a decoherence functional is a bi-charge on B x B, automatically countably additive on clopens, and its sigma-extension question becomes classical vector-measure extension theory (Kluvanek-class) on X. Care pinned: no claim that the obstruction vanishes.
- **N4 (the ordered version):** L's Priestley dual X_le is a profinite poset (Speed 1972); at each finite truncation the Birkhoff dual of the size-<= k stem lattice is the canonical finite quotient; the covtree contact stated ONLY as computed in r5.
