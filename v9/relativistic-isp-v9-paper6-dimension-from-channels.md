## 1. The two-clock wall

**Lemma (two-clock wall, weak form — the form the builders use).** If x ≺ y iff f₀(x) < f₀(y) and f_i(x) ≤ f_i(y) for all i ∈ {1..k}, with f₀ injective, then dim(≺) ≤ k + 1. *Proof:* take L₀ = the f₀-order and, for each i, L_i = lex(f_i ascending, f₀ ascending) — all linear since f₀ is injective, and each contains ≺; conversely x below y in all of them forces f₀(x) < f₀(y) and every f_i(x) ≤ f_i(y), i.e. x ≺ y — the intersection is exactly ≺. ∎ (Elementary; stated for the record. The strict-inequalities-with-tie-breaks form previously stated here fails at ties and is not the builders' order — corrected round 35; note-3p1-dimension-ledger §Round-35 correction.) The corpus's dominance order is (b, χ)-dominance — f₀ = b injective, one weak clock — so dim ≤ 2 always: paper 13's universal realizer and the v9 census set-equality are instances. Conversely M² *is* two-clock causality (u = t − x, v = t + x), and the round cone of M^{d ≥ 3} is not two-clock: its sprinklings refuse 2-realizers at modest N. Phase 0 certified both directions instrumentally (receipt `dimwall_phase0.py`): the Golumbic implication-class tester agrees with an independent brute-force decision on 300/300 random posets and all canned cases (S₃ refuses; chains, antichains, dominance orders pass); M³ diamonds refuse 5/5; M² and grown corpus webs pass 5/5; and the Myrheim–Meyer estimator was built formula-free from measured reference sprinklings (the M² ordering fraction landing on the analytic ½ unprompted). **Re-attribution (scoped):** every order-dimension readout on corpus webs was overdetermined to refuse — the coordinate system, not the reader; the T6 seal-side refusals keep their own recorded causes.

## Related work and the novelty boundary (added round 37, after a verified prior-art audit; post-dates the round-35 assembly review)

The static skeleton of the construction — a partial order defined by dominance in k independent coordinates — is **Winkler's random k-dimensional order P_k(n)** (Order 1, 1985), canonical since **Bollobás–Brightwell** (Adv. Appl. Prob. 27, 1995); and **Bollobás–Brightwell 1991** ("Box-spaces and random partial orders", Trans. AMS 324) already unifies the dominance cube and the Minkowski causal cone in one framework, proving every box-space carries an intrinsic volume-scaling dimension exactly d with chain-based estimators (height ~ m_X·n^{1/d}, explicit chain constants) — the closest classical ancestor of this paper's volume readings. The **Erdős–Kierstead–Trotter** threshold (k ~ (t/log t)·log n, beyond which the dominance order degenerates) is the static ancestor of §4's capacity ceiling. On the physics side, **Rideout–Sorkin** sequential growth generates causal orders dynamically but with dimension as an untheoremed emergent knob and non-manifoldlike output, and **Surya's 2D causal-set quantum gravity** (with Brightwell–Henson) comes closest to the dominance mechanism by *restricting the sample space* to random 2-orders (intersections of two linear orders) with MCMC dynamics — dimension imposed kinematically, not emergent from the process. Against that boundary, the apparently-new content of this paper (verified-audit scope): (i) a **growth-with-resets** process — *not* distributionally P_k(n), since accumulate-and-reset coordinates are correlated across elements — provably achieving Dushnik–Miller order dimension exactly C+1, witness-certified; (ii) the **interior-optimum law** (channels must be distinct *and* interacting — no prior-art coverage found); (iii) the **dynamical** form of the capacity ceiling; (iv) the framing of spacetime dimension as a counting law over evidence channels. **The exactness reservation, resolved (round-37 library pass — THEOREM-ABSENT):** no located source states "dim P_k(n) = k w.h.p." as an attributed theorem for fixed k ≥ 3, and none establishes the lower bound via standard examples — Winkler 1985 treats height/width/extremal counts only (his own 1988 status survey lists all progress; exactness is not among it); El-Zahar–Sauer 1988 is two-dimensional-poset enumeration; Winkler 1991 is k = 2 model contiguity; Brightwell's 1993 survey carries no such theorem in its recovered text or reference list; and the modern literature (Brightwell–Łuczak 2015; Gao–Kumar 2025, a paper devoted to random-order dimension; 56 scanned citation contexts of Winkler 1985) states none. For the iid model the exactness is folklore and elementary (S_k has a strict realization in R^k, so 2k iid uniform points induce it with positive probability, and disjoint blocks are independent) — **we are not aware of a published statement or proof**, and this paper's novelty weight accordingly rests on the growth-with-resets dynamics (non-iid, non-exchangeable) and the constructive witness certification, not on exactness per se.
