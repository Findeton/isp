## 3. Proposition V6.1

### 3.1 Exact scope

The proposition is read in the setting of version-5 Replacement Definition
12: an independently physical resolved kernel on a frozen
\(\mathfrak O_{\rm ch}\), together with its target pushforward. It does not
say that an independently postulated primitive target-only kernel determines
a resolved bundle or a disintegration. That target-only branch remains
literal under versions 3 and 4.

### 3.2 Descriptor coherence implies pointwise branching

Let \(x\in B\), and suppose \(\ell\in\mathcal O\) and disjoint measurable
descriptor events \(D_0,D_1\) satisfy (V5.2). Since \(\ell\) is a total
\(\Xi\)-measurable descriptor,
\[
 E_i=\ell^{-1}(D_i)\in\Xi.
\]
The fiber restrictions \(A_i=E_i\cap\mathcal C_x\) belong to
\(\Xi_x\), remain disjoint, and have
\(\mu_x(A_i)>0\). They are exactly the witnesses required by (V5.1).
Thus descriptor-coherent resolved branching on \(B\) implies pointwise
resolved branching at every \(x\in B\).

The result says only that a globally meaningful physical distinction is
also locally observable. It does not assign its weights.

### 3.3 Target branching implies pointwise branching

Let \(D_0,D_1\in\Sigma\) be disjoint target events with positive mass under
the target pushforward at \(x\). Total target measurability gives
\[
 E_i=\tau^{-1}(D_i)\in\Xi,
\]
and disjointness survives preimage. For the raw resolved kernel,
\[
 \mathsf M_x(D_i)=\mu_x((E_i)_x)>0.
\]
For the normalized laws, the exact pushforward identity gives
\[
 \mathsf P_x^{\rm tgt}(D_i)
 =\mathsf P_x^{\rm res}((E_i)_x)>0.
\]
Because the character density
\(d(\tau\kappa)/(d(a)d(b))\) is everywhere strictly positive,
positive normalized resolved mass also implies positive \(\mu_x\)-mass.
Either formulation supplies the two (V5.1) witnesses.

Operationally, two positive coarse target readouts must come from two
disjoint positive resolved preimage families. The converse need not hold
because \(\tau\) can erase a physical channel distinction.

a proposed probability would be about. The same
\(\mathfrak O_{\rm ch}\) can admit many kernels, characters, whole
occurrence propensities, and cosmological states.

## 11. Fresh semantic countermodels

The following countermodels were constructed specifically for this v6
review. They are semantic tests, not implementations or proposed repairs.

### CM-O6-1 — Untethered detector-port fleet

Consider a fleet of physically separate two-port analyzers. Within each
complete input, the two output ports are literal physical landmarks, so
repeated trials can distinguish two positive local channel events. No
licensed calibration transports either landmark to a port on another
analyzer. A maintenance database nevertheless assigns every port a zero or
one.

The database makes a convenient global numbering, but changing its entries
changes no experiment. It is therefore not a physical species descriptor.
With no predeclared port descriptor in \(\mathcal O\), the system has
pointwise branching but no descriptor-coherent zero-versus-one branching
over the fleet. This is the required fresh model in which convenient
channel numbering is not a physical species.

### CM-O6-2 — One observable contract, several selector residues

Fix one \(\mathfrak O_{\rm ch}\) with two physical same-target channels and
fix one resolved conditional kernel on it. Define whole occurrence models
with composition propensities \(q_1=1/4\) and \(q_2=3/4\), and independently
allow two distinct cosmological boundary distributions. All four whole
models agree on every conditional channel probability and every descriptor.

The pre-measure object is the same in all cases, while the surviving
selector residue contains several occurrence laws. Hence
\(\mathfrak O_{\rm ch}\) cannot be \(\mathfrak R\): they differ in type,
dependency stage, and empirical content. Renaming the observable contract
does not change either model; conflating the symbols would hide the actual
underdetermination.

### CM-O6-3 — Continuous target split verifies the second implication

At one input let the resolved fiber be \([0,1]\) with Lebesgue probability.
Set
\[
 \tau(u)=
 \begin{cases}
 c_0,&0\leq u<1/3,\\
 c_1,&1/3\leq u\leq1.
 \end{cases}
\]
The target events \(\{c_0\}\) and \(\{c_1\}\) have probabilities \(1/3\)
and \(2/3\). Their resolved preimages are disjoint positive events, although
every resolved singleton has probability zero.

This model verifies target-to-pointwise branching without an atomic-channel
assumption. It also shows why the proposition uses preimages rather than a
choice of representative channel.

### CM-O6-4 — Two mechanisms, one reaction product

Suppose electron-transfer and proton-transfer mechanisms have independently
retained physical traces, and a trace-sensitive detector distinguishes them
with positive probability. Both mechanisms end in the same coarse chemical
product sector.

The resolved law branches pointwise, but the target product law is
deterministic. This refutes the converse of Proposition V6.1 item 2 without
using presentation labels. Erasing the mechanism trace would change the
observable contract; it would not prove that only one mechanism occurred.

### CM-O6-5 — Transported charge with unfixed weights

Across an input region \(B\), let a retained boundary flux define the
predeclared physical descriptor \(\ell\in\{+1,-1\}\). Both signs have
positive probability at every input. Descriptor-coherent branching
therefore holds and, by restriction, pointwise branching holds everywhere.

For every measurable \(q:B\to(0,1)\), the assignments
\(P_x(+1)=q(x)\) and \(P_x(-1)=1-q(x)\) satisfy those same branching
statements. Descriptor transport gives physical identity, not a unique
measure. Neither Proposition V6.1 nor normalization selects \(q\).

### CM-O6-6 — Uncountable cylinder channel family

Let \(I\) be uncountable and let one resolved fiber be

constructed fair product measure. A finite-coordinate cylinder and its

### CM-O6-7 — Random fusion bond versus retained staged boundary

In the simultaneous-fusion experiment, let a fresh cross-component bond be

Now compare a deliberately staged fusion whose trace contains a retained

No relative occurrence measure over the two processes follows.

### CM-O6-8 — Stable alarm bit and volatile sufficient frontier

A device writes a durable alarm bit recording whether a hidden state lies in

In a second typed process, a full intermediate state determines every

record. A normalized history law over either process selects no actual

### CM-O6-9 — Identical channel law, different actuality ontologies

Interpretation B is not obtained by replacing A with a delta measure: a
delta ensemble is still a law over alternatives and has counterfactual

They occupy different version-2 classification branches.

### CM-O6-10 — Energy scoring cannot choose the observable contract

The pre-measure freeze correctly requires the physical descriptors first,
