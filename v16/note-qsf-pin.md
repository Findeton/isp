## 2. Why the proposed binary fork is not frozen as binary

The external proposal correctly identified two important routes:

1. replace the literal CELL-HIT rule by an affine CP instrument; or
2. retain literal pure-ray dynamics and test steering/no-signalling.

The panel theorem requires two corrections.

First, the affine route is a family, not the displayed projective repair.  For
rank-one `E_c`, every affine CP outcome map is

```text
J_c(rho) = Tr(E_c rho) sigma_c,
```

with arbitrary normalized `sigma_c`.  One completion already fits the single
registered continuation while the projective completion does not.  A census
must therefore ask what is invariant across the family, whether any member
preserves the registered packet, and whether locality/recurrence/composition
select one.  One successful example never licenses “the repaired walk.”

Second, WRC's post-coin CELL-HIT cut is explicitly not a genuine division
boundary.  A Barandes-style indivisible process may require affinity and CP
only for the complete operational map between genuine record boundaries, not
for internal history labels.  That possibility is a third arm.  It is not an
interpretive exemption: QSF must construct the candidate boundary, retain the
claimed records, and prove all-input affinity, completeness, composition, and
no-signalling.  If no such boundary can be typed, the arm is unphrasable.

The three arms are the registered assay space, not a claim that every possible
future extension of quantum theory has been classified.
