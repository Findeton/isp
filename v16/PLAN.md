   dependence of normal-mode/particle candidates on the still-free law.

   If more than one survives with different predictions, stamp the architecture

   repair or terminalization.  **Completed:** two `REJECT`, one

   `ACCEPT-WITH-FIXES` and was adjudicated at #14.  Its exact bridge,
   geometry-sector, backreaction, actualization, all-arity, and Hamiltonian

   true off-tree/no-git verification are complete at #16.
