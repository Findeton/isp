First, it proves at the frozen fixture that an anonymous nonzero matrix does
not recover a kinematic rewrite grammar or an ancestry declaration. At a
zero-coupling endpoint the actual support has two arrows while the licensed

reciprocity reconstructs the inverse on the reached image and leaves the
recorded-successor weights free. Those are useful construction results.

selected before the state and independently of it; the chosen graph does not

selection, history probabilities, overlap composition, record permanence,
and actualization remain absent. The output graph can be compiled into a
three-wire typed circuit with named output effects while preserving every
registered path product and future probability. What disappears is spatial
adjacency, not any prediction. Thus the fixture establishes typed
rewrite/transport compatibility, not irreducible geometry or gravitational
backreaction.

The correct disposition is to retain the map-only and conditional reciprocal
findings, narrow the dictionary finding to the **registered declared future
family**, and demote the bundle finding from a physical weld to a local
kinematic bundle construction.

## 4. Ontology without Hilbert-space shorthand

The candidate proposes the following picture.

- A **fixed meta-catalogue** lists possible complete arrangements of matter and
  relations. It is a possibility space, not a fixed realized spacetime.
- An event is meant to replace one relational arrangement by another: two old
  sites persist under a declared ancestry choice, and one new site appears.
- Alongside that relational replacement, process information is transferred
  from the old catalogue sector to the new one.
- A record is a successor alternative that is retained rather than exactly
  erased. The fixture assigns weights to such alternatives.
- Only one realized history is intended ontologically, but Paper 4 supplies no
  rule saying which event occurs or which recorded successor actualizes.

This is compatible with a backreacting ontology in principle. A fixed set of
*possible complete relational configurations* is not a fixed spacetime any
more than a classical phase space is one fixed trajectory. But accommodation
is not construction. The fixture instantiates one declared local rewrite and
one chosen representation of it; it does not build the global catalogue or a
law that makes a realized relational geometry change in response to the
process state.

The distinction matters. In the current candidate, “the graph changes” is a
typed input to the calculation. It is not an output selected by the old
relations and old process state.

## 7. Backreaction, holonomy, couplings, and many-event structure

### Backreaction

The demanded two-way loop is absent. The rewrite grammar is identical for all
five growth angles, including endpoints where an allowed amplitude vanishes.
No input state or process observable selects a different relational rewrite.
In the other direction, the registered graph merely selects a named output
row; after compilation the same effect exists without adjacency.

There is therefore no representation-invariant nonfactorization showing

```text
process state -> changed relations -> changed later process dynamics.
```

There is a typed rewrite plus a compatible transport decoration. Calling this
“architecture for backreaction” is fair; calling it backreaction is not.

### Fourth-root phase

The phase census inserts a connection value in `{1, i, -1, -i}`. The exact
screen is

```text
p = |1 + h|^2 / 4 = 1, 1/2, 0, 1/2.
```

Boundary rephasings leave the closed product invariant, so it is legitimate
transport holonomy. The rewrite does not determine `h`; the fixture supplies
it independently. No length, duration, metric, stress-energy response,
Einstein equation, continuum limit, or curvature-to-source law exists. It is
not yet metric curvature or gravity.

### Local angles

The two angle families are exact coupling *moduli* of the registered local
maps. They do not yet qualify as physical constants. The recurring-locality
gate constructs the “second occurrence” by reusing the same map, and the
fixture's strings naming growth, recombiner, and record-choice types do not
derive cross-arena identity. Required evidence still missing is recurrence at
independently individuated sites, gauge-invariant calibration, transport of the
same constant across distinct arenas, and an observable law selecting or
measuring its value.

### Two to `n` actors

Paper 4 types one binary-input/local-growth rewrite. It gives no coherent rule
for overlapping rewrites, simultaneous events sharing a site, ordering
dependence, fusion, or `n`-event closure. A field-like limit is not typed.
Actor number is currently part of each declared rewrite span, not a derived
sector occupancy or emergent support property.
