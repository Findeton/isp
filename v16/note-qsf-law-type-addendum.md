## Binding correction

An ordinary positive transition kernel is sufficient as the visible law at a
genuine stable record/division event. QSF does not prove that the microscopic
law factors into such kernels after every elementary rewrite. Doing so would
normally assign classical alternatives before a record exists and erase the
relative information needed for interference when histories reconverge.

The forward-safe hierarchy is:

```text
one actual relational/configuration history       ontological candidate
indivisible multi-time process law                 nomological candidate
decoherence functional / Hilbert construction      quantum representation
ordinary stochastic kernel / CP instrument         division-level shadow
```

This is a type correction, not a selection result. QSF constructs neither an
indivisible Barandes law nor a global decoherence functional. Its fine
`([psi],record)` kernel exists at the displayed cuts, but QSF does not certify
those cuts as genuine divisions or that kernel as a microscopic factorization.
