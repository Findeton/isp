## 4. Why the record-mode headline fails

The candidate names classical, coherent, and hybrid modes, but it does not
generate them from one event law.

- The classical Kraus family and its coherent dilation are two presentations
  of a reset channel.
- The `w` family is a path-tag dephasing/interference construction. Its
  `w=0` endpoint is dephasing, not the reset channel: on input `|1><1|`, the
  reset outputs `|0><0|` while the MZI endpoint outputs `|1><1|`.
- The append/swap/reset/copy recovery tests act on a third abstract binary
  label grammar and are not continuations of either construction above.

Deletion and exchange controls move these three constructions independently.
Accordingly the exact real-overlap family survives only as an MZI tag family;
it is not evidence that a one-dimensional family of physical record-feedback
laws survives a common surface.

The hybrid tag is also not a durable record. At overlap `3/5` its trace
distance is `4/5` and its optimal single-shot discrimination probability is
`9/10`. No channel can turn the pair into perfectly distinguishable facts.
It is a proto-record/environment tag until a later orthogonal,

## 11. Barandes-level ontology after RFB

- A Barandes-style candidate may contain one actual configuration and one

  that law; RFB does not promote them to beables.
- Ordinary probabilities and instruments are licensed at genuine record

- An exclusive microscopic rewrite alternative is not automatically an

  division selection, durable mode-specific record, or actualization rule.
- Geometry is absent. Nothing in this unit acts as distance, causal support,

Thus Paper 10 maps a useful finite algebraic boundary around a still-missing
law. It does not discover that law or settle whether its microscopic form is
an enriched stochastic process, an indivisible multi-time process, or a
history-functional representation.
