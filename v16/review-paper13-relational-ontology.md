## 7. Analytical proofs and exact reconstructions

### 7.1 Boolean referent and support

A physical contextual Boolean element is the truth vector of a formula on the
realized nonzero cells. Quotienting the 72 nonzero ambient formula tables by
that vector gives 42 classes. For each source cell `s`, extension retains `s`
and adds `s ∪ {N}` exactly when `P(s)=1`; hence the forgetful fiber is `{s}`
or `{s,s∪{N}}`. The fibers are disjoint and exhaustive. On a satisfying
two-element fiber every old-role formula is constant while `N` flips, so `N`
cannot be any old Boolean element. This is a proper extension in the finite
Boolean subalgebra, not a role-count argument.

The finite cells are algebraic presentation coordinates for the generated
finite subalgebra. The physical candidate is the typed groupoid/orbit, not a
set of cell-points. A standalone relabeling reconstruction transported source
and target boundaries, all six states, a two-occurrence filling, its complete
operator, one bound split certificate, and the endpoint law. Identity,
inverse, and composition held exactly. Presentation covariance is therefore
supported independently of the claim that this candidate is the selected
ontology.

and branch 1 with amplitude `4/5`. The later probe queries the writer's output

00 = 9/25,  01 = 0,  10 = (16/25)(9/25) = 144/625,
11 = (16/25)(16/25) = 256/625.
