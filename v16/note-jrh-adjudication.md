## 5. New consequences found by the panel

### 5.1 Feed-forward equivalence no-go

At the delivered scope the geometry model is prediction-equivalent to a
standard measurement plus classical feed-forward.  Consequently the observed
purity loss cannot be attributed to gravity.  It is ordinary loss from
discarding a measurement record.

### 5.2 Entanglement-breaking core

The unconditioned Z-projective channel is measure-and-prepare and therefore
entanglement breaking.  Acting on one half of a Bell pair gives

```text
(1/2)|00><00| + (1/2)|11><11|.
```

If every microscopic relational rewrite used this instrument, quantum
coherence could not propagate through the rewrite network.  Therefore a CP
record instrument can only be the law **at a genuine division/record
boundary**, not automatically at every microscopic occurrence.  Between such
boundaries the theory needs a coherence-preserving whole-history object.

### 5.3 Why the fundamental candidate must move up one level

The corpus had already shown that a positive whole-history probability law
does not contain the phase information needed for interference.  Combining
that result with the entanglement-breaking finding fixes the type of the
serious refinement:

```text
ontology:
  one actual relational history, including its realized causal/transport
  geometry and durable records;

fundamental law:
  a strongly positive complex decoherence functional over complete finite
  relational histories of both matter and geometry;

division events:
  history partitions at which the off-diagonal functional vanishes (exactly,
  in the finite theorem target) and one durable complete successor is sampled;

boundary instrument:
  the conditional CP shadow induced by that functional at a genuine division
  boundary, not the microscopic ontology or universal update step.
```

This refinement is a candidate, not a result.  It avoids placing a projective
measurement at every microscopic rewrite, retains coherent alternatives before
records form, and lets geometry back-react history by history instead of via an
expectation-valued nonlinear state equation.  It still owes the complex
history weights, boundary state, record criterion, and refinement law.
