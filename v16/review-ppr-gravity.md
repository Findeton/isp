1. **Rewrite and uniform probe.** The labelled graph rewrite exists
   syntactically, and the scorer uniformly composes local edge matrices. The

   alone does not determine them.
2. **Erasure, reconstruction, relabelling, spectator, disjoint control.** The
   delivered controls reproduce. The stronger minimal-circuit eliminability
   control also reproduces, defeating graph necessity. Relabelling tests name
   covariance only. Spectator and disjoint checks are exact tensor/linearity
   identities and do not inspect the graph.
3. **Carrier dimensions.** They do no predictive work. The separate `Vgrow`
   heterogeneous fork is not physically welded to the graph rewrite.
4. **Matter rows.** They coherently reweight two predeclared alternatives. No
   event-selection map from an independently typed matter source to a new
   relation carrier is built.
5. **Nonfactorization.** The nonzero determinant is exact but is neither a
   refinement-stable discriminator as implemented nor a typed
   matter/geometry factorization test.
6. **Loop typing.** Same-fact flatness and distinct-route holonomy are coherent
   concepts. In this candidate their distinction and the nonflat phase are
   declared inputs, not consequences of the rewrite.
7. **Terminology.** `geometry` and `back-response` are overpromoted;
   `finite labelled transport network` and `source-row-dependent two-port
   response` are licensed. `Curvature-sensitive` requires the qualification
   that the connection/phase is declared. `Gravity` is correctly refused by
   most scope walls and must remain refused.
