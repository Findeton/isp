$$

## 6. Influence coordinates

For a pair with complete experiments, define total response by the existence
of a nonzero complete-reader coordinate in at least one registered complete
context. Keep these predicates separate:

- `Tot(A,B)`: some complete context and complete reader show nonzero response;
- `NoTot(A,B)`: every required complete context and complete reader has exact
  zero response;
- `Direct(A,B)`: `Tot(A,B)` survives every registered lawful mediator hold
  that blocks all declared proper mediating routes;
- `Mediated(A,B)`: total response exists, but an exhaustive separating
  mediator certificate makes the controlled residual zero;

  nonzero; and
- `Untested(A,B)`: the required intervention, context, or reader does not
  exist in the accepted experiment class.

Total response is not direct response. A common cause, cancellation, or a
mediated chain may produce total response without a primitive edge. Conversely
an XOR-like mechanism may have zero marginal response under one coarse
contrast while retaining a complete joint response.

or is explicitly labeled only total/mediated.
