## 3. Proposition V6.1

### 3.1 Descriptor-coherent implies pointwise -- proved

Let descriptor-coherent branching hold on measurable \(B\ni x\).  For the
predeclared measurable descriptor \(\ell\) and disjoint measurable descriptor

\[
 A_i=(\ell^{-1}D_i)\cap\mathcal C_x.
\]
