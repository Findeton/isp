## 2. Executive conclusion

The missing object is not one number and not even one conditional kernel.
Four logically distinct pieces must be separated.

1. **Structural support and referent:** which resolved whole-process channels
   are physically distinct before a measure is supplied.
2. **Conditional channel law:** given a complete structural opportunity,
   which resolved channel occurs.
3. **Occurrence activity:** whether a composition, replacement, creation, or
   other structural event occurs at all.
4. **Root or boundary state:** which global phase, initial complex, or
   projective extremal law describes the universe.

The accepted Paper 13D law supplies normalized histories conditional on a
fixed typed process. It supplies none of these four structural choices over
inequivalent whole-process complexes.

The strongest current strategy is therefore not to guess one global Gibbs
formula. It is to seek an **overdetermined intersection** of independent
physical routes:

1. a Gamma-native structural branching or replacement experiment;
2. exact composition and higher coherence;
3. exact spectator locality and deletion projectivity; and
4. independently observed structural transition ratios or a separately
   justified root state.

If their intersection is one occurrence law, the selector is derived. If a
finite-dimensional residue remains, its constants must be calibrated from
structural records before Paper 17 is opened. If a large residue remains,
the honest alternatives are a contingent cosmological state, one actual
complex, or selector-robust Paper 17 theorems.

## 4. Three classes of constraints

The main methodological safeguard is to label every principle by what it can
actually do.

### 4.1 Definitional constraints

These make a candidate meaningful but do not favor one physical law.

- point-free channel identity;
- a frozen measurable event algebra;
- complete typing of sources, targets, marks, interventions, and readers;
- separation of resolved channel, target sector, child history, and realized
  occurrence;
- positivity, normalization, and measurability; and
- explicit state/law/gauge/actuality classification.

### 4.2 Consistency constraints

These can eliminate candidates and relate parameters, but often leave a
family.

- composition and bracketing coherence;
- tensor-spectator locality;
- replacement-cycle integrability;
- exact deletion projectivity;
- non-explosion and trace normalizability;
- coarse-graining or cylindrical consistency; and
- extension from finite event algebras to the declared global sigma algebra.

### 4.3 Selecting constraints

Only these can supply physical numerical content.

- branching multiplicities independently generated by the same law;
- independently executable forward and reverse structural transition rates;
- a canonical physical transfer or replacement operator;
- anomaly or coherence equations that leave one positive solution;
- a separately motivated cosmological boundary/root state; or
- preregistered structural observations capable of calibrating a finite
  residual family.

A proposal that invokes only the first two classes has not selected
\(\Pi_{\rm phys}\), however elegant its formula.

## 5. Non-negotiable hard constraints

Every candidate selector must pass all of the following before its numerical
predictions are inspected.

### C1. Referent before measure

Freeze \(\mathfrak O_{\rm ch}\) before proposing weights. No failed measure
may be rescued by coarsening the observable algebra, and no successful
measure may be attacked by adding a post-hoc channel label.

### C2. Point-free covariance with occurrence multiplicity

Relabelings are gauge, but two physical occurrences exchanged by an
automorphism remain two occurrences. Representative counting, orbit
counting, labeled counting, and finite-groupoid cardinality are distinct
base measures unless a physical construction relates them.

### C3. No hidden discrete assumption

The support may be finite, countable, continuous, hybrid, contextual, or
non-standard measurable. A finite census is a bounded test, not a theorem
that reality is discrete.

### C4. Structural channels are not Gamma histories

Fresh bonds, records, or other random values inside one fixed fusion process
remain child histories. They do not create new unmarked channel odds.

### C5. Conditional Paper 13D recovery

For every supported \(\chi\), conditioning the whole law on \(\chi\) must
recover the accepted \(\mathbf\Gamma_D(\cdot\mid\chi,a)\) exactly. A
history-dependent selector term would retune Paper 13D rather than extend it.

### C6. No Markovization of indivisible cuts

A local selector specification may condition only on a complete typed
structural exterior. It may not insert a restart kernel at Paper 13D's native
nondivision or hide discarded history in a cache.

### C7. Complete experimental separability

Two proposed channels are physical alternatives only if some allowed
Gamma-derived interface can distinguish them, or if a separately justified
structural observable retains their difference. Reader names, encodings, or
program branches do not count.

### C8. Composition and path coherence

All physically identified composition paths must agree. If resolved paths
remain physical, associators and pentagon-like coherence are part of the
law. Deliberately staged and simultaneous processes may not be equated merely
to obtain a consistency equation.

### C9. Rooted spectator locality

Replacing a bounded interior while holding its complete exterior fixed must
not change local odds when an independent spectator is added. Automorphism
and wreath-product multiplicities must be handled with rooted one-hole
stabilizers; naive unrooted factorization is false for isomorphic spectators.

### C10. Exact projectivity

The same family must commute with every admitted physical deletion kernel:

\[
 P_nK_{n,m}=P_m.
\tag{4}
\]

Aggregate normalization at each size, or fitting a new coupling at each
size, is not projectivity.

### C11. Finite-word and infinite-trace control

Every finite convolution must be finite. If repeated operations produce
distinct retained traces, equal positive activity diverges; endpoint
periodicity does not erase process history. Infinite-volume existence,
uniqueness, and boundary phases are separate obligations.

### C12. No hidden construction time

Restriction depth, deletion order, birth label, tensor serialization, and
sampling iteration are not physical chronology. An auxiliary sequence is
allowed only if the final law is exactly independent of it.

### C13. No geometry in the selector dependency slice

No coupling, base measure, cutoff, phase, or boundary condition may be chosen
because it yields dimension four, manifoldlike statistics, Lorentzian
signature, a metric, gravity, or agreement with Paper 15 or Paper 16.

### C14. Quantum claims require structural interference

Complex amplitudes are warranted only if two inequivalent whole structural
alternatives can recombine in a phase-sensitive complete experiment. Writing
\(\Pi=|\sqrt\Pi e^{i\phi}|^2\) is a representation of a classical law, not
evidence for quantum structural physics.

### C15. Quantum extension and positivity

Any quantal route must construct a strongly positive decoherence functional
or equivalent process object and prove extension to the physical event
algebra. Finite-cylinder consistency alone is insufficient.

### C16. Ontological honesty

Every surviving datum must be classified as universal nomology, a law-family
constant, a contingent cosmological state, a preparation state, a gauge
convention, an empirically calibrated structural parameter, or unselected.
Calling a state a law does not derive it.

### C17. Actualization remains separate

A normalized structural law supplies propensities, not the realized complex.
Paper 17 ensemble analysis does not require an actualization mechanism, but
claims about the one actual history remain conditional on it.

## 7. Ranked physical routes

### Route A -- Gamma-native branching fusion algebra

**Question.** Can one larger typed indivisible Gamma experiment have two or
more genuinely different resolved structural outputs, rather than one fixed
fusion channel with random bond values?

If its independently derived integer support \(N_{ab}^{c}\) forms a finite
indecomposable based ring, Frobenius--Perron theory supplies a unique positive
character. The resolved conditional probabilities become

\[
 \Pr(c,\alpha\mid a,b)
 =\frac{d_c}{d_ad_b},
 \qquad 1\leq\alpha\leq N_{ab}^{c}.
\tag{5}
\]

Associativity then gives bracketing-independent target probabilities. This is
the strongest deductive route investigated because the numerical odds arise
from independently physical branching support rather than a fitted energy.

It would still leave two things open: whether fusion occurs and the global
root/size law. Current Paper 13D fusion is deterministic at the structural
level, so Route A requires new physical branching, not a reinterpretation of
its random histories.

**Priority:** highest.

### Route B -- physical structural transition ratios

Enlarge the Barandes configuration space so that whole relational structure
is part of the configuration. Derive executable structural transitions
\(\chi\leftrightarrow\chi'\) from one fixed indivisible law. If both
directions are physically available, their complete transition rates may
fix local odds:

\[
 \frac{\Pi(\chi')}{\Pi(\chi)}
 =\frac{W(\chi\to\chi')}{W(\chi'\to\chi)}.
\tag{6}
\]

All replacement-cycle products must equal one. The rates must be supplied by
the same law before \(\Pi\) is solved; otherwise detailed balance is
circular. Paper 13D's record eraser is not automatically a reverse
structural creation arrow.

This is the most Barandes-like route: one larger stochastic law on a larger
configuration space, rather than a hyperprior over laws.

**Priority:** highest, conditional on genuine structural interventions.

### Route C -- operational coherence and anomaly bootstrap

Enumerate every independently proved physical 2-cell: alternative
factorizations, refinements, duality moves, tensor interchanges, and genuine
composition equivalences. Demand that local selector amplitudes or odds have
zero holonomy around every such physical cycle.

If the resulting equations leave one positive solution, they select it. If
they leave characters or couplings, those are honest residue. The danger is
declaring physically different staged and simultaneous processes equivalent
just to obtain more equations.

A particularly promising subcase is a genuinely physical duality or
cup/cap structure. Pivotal or spherical consistency can turn categorical
dimensions into weights. Paper 13D's eraser alone does not establish such a
structural duality.

**Priority:** high.

### Route D -- local DLR/replacement specification plus deletion

For each complete typed exterior \(B\), freeze point-free local replacement
odds. Reciprocity and unit cycle product reconstruct a finite law on each
connected replacement component. A DLR-like specification asks for global
laws whose conditionals agree with these local odds.

The natural dimension-neutral baseline is

\[
 \Pi_{\theta,\eta}([\chi])
 \propto b_\eta([\chi])
 \exp\!\left[-\sum_j\theta_jO_j([\chi])\right],
\tag{7}
\]

with every observable, base, cutoff, and boundary condition frozen. Orbit
base \(b=1\) and groupoid base
\(b=|\operatorname{Aut}\chi|^{-1}\) are different physical hypotheses.

Exact deletion is then imposed, not presumed. In many dependent exponential
families projectivity fails; when it holds it often removes interactions or
relates scale-dependent couplings but leaves a family or multiple boundary
phases.

This is the best general architecture, but not yet a unique selector.

**Priority:** high as a baseline and falsification framework.

### Route E -- canonical replacement transfer operator

If the accepted physical interfaces uniquely define a positive primitive
operator \(T\) on structural classes, Perron--Frobenius or Perron--Parry
theory can select its positive eigenmeasure. This becomes explanatory only
if:

1. the move set and all matrix elements come from accepted structural
   experiments;
2. no desired stationary law is used to define \(T\);
3. finite-cutoff eigenmeasures are projectively compatible; and
4. the result is independent of auxiliary enumeration.

The likely failure mode is operator dependence: many natural-looking
replacement graphs yield different eigenmeasures.

**Priority:** medium-high.

### Route F -- covariant growth without physical birth time

Causal-set sequential growth shows that discrete general covariance and
causality can strongly constrain growth laws, but the result is a family of
dynamics rather than a unique law. Paper 18 may borrow its method only after
removing the assumption that birth order is physical chronology.

The useful translated constraints are path independence of auxiliary
construction histories, spectator independence, hereditary restriction, and
complete covariance of observables. Free growth couplings must remain
explicit.

**Priority:** medium as inspiration, not as a transplant.

### Route G -- quantum structural measure

Replace \(\Pi\) by a decoherence functional \(D(A,B)\) only if structural
alternatives possess a physical recombination experiment. Required gates are:

1. strong positivity;
2. normalization and the quantum sum rule;
3. covariance on the point-free event algebra;
4. extension from bounded cylinder events;
5. tensor and gluing coherence;
6. a phase-sensitive complete reader; and
7. a classical selector only after a separately justified decoherence or
   actualization rule.

Quantum-measure research shows that extension to the full event sigma
algebra can fail even for finite-state unitary and quantum sequential-growth
examples. A quantum lift therefore adds obligations and generally adds phase
freedom; it is not a shortcut to \(\Pi_{\rm phys}\).

**Priority:** conditional. Activate only after structural interference is
observed.

### Route H -- operational quantum axioms

Quantum combs, process matrices, purification, local tomography, and related
operational axioms are valuable for typing admissible higher-order
experiments without assuming a global causal order. They may sharply
constrain how a structural process composes.

They do not by themselves choose the state or occurrence measure. Purification
can represent a mixed structural state as part of a reversible larger state,
but the pure state and boundary condition remain data.

**Priority:** medium for admissibility, low for direct selection.

### Route I -- group-field or state-sum architecture

Group field theory supplies a serious template for summing over varying
combinatorial structures. It also demonstrates the central danger: field
rank, group data, interaction vertices, kinetic term, coupling constants,
base measure, and boundary state are normally inputs. These cannot be
imported before dimension is selected.

A dimension-neutral relational field theory may organize the selector, but
its action must be derived by Routes A--E or admitted as new nomology.

**Priority:** medium-low as architecture.

### Route J -- intrinsic coarse-graining fixed point

Construct deletion or refinement maps from the accepted relational law and
seek a unique cylindrically consistent fixed point. This may define a
universality class and eliminate cutoff-dependent couplings.

The embedding maps themselves must be selected by the dynamics. A fixed
point selected because it looks four-dimensional is forbidden. Multiple
fixed points or relevant directions remain new constants or boundary data.

**Priority:** medium after a concrete local law exists.

### Route K -- finite residue plus structural calibration

If Routes A--J reduce \(\mathfrak R\) to finitely many constants, measure
them using repeated complete structural experiments. Valid calibration
requires:

- identical complete exterior types;
- genuine structural branching rather than random child histories;
- a proved exchangeability or ergodicity theorem within one universe;
- no reuse of correlated subregions as independent universes;
- a preregistered training set; and
- chronology and dimension statistics held out until parameters are frozen.

This is honest physics rather than failure. Many fundamental laws contain
empirically determined constants.

**Priority:** high fallback.

### Route L -- contingent cosmological state

Following the Barandes distinction, the structural selector may be a
standalone cosmological distribution rather than part of the transition law.
Then the theory must say so explicitly. A boundary-state proposal can be
tested for covariance, normalizability, projectivity, and empirical adequacy,
but is not derived merely by multiplying it into \(\mathbf\Gamma_D\).

**Priority:** ontologically clean fallback.

### Route M -- selector-robust Paper 17

A unique selector may be unnecessary if a Paper 17 conclusion is constant
over the entire independently frozen residue \(\mathfrak R\). After the
residue is constructed, define a Paper 17 output functional \(F(\Pi)\) and
prove

\[
 F(\Pi)=F_*
 \quad\text{for every }\Pi\in\mathfrak R.
\tag{8}
\]

This does not choose \(\Pi\), and Paper 17 outputs may not be used to shrink
\(\mathfrak R\). It can nevertheless unblock a chronology or dimension
coordinate if the conclusion is genuinely worst-case robust.

**Priority:** high parallel theorem route.

### Route N -- one actual complex

If the universe supplies one actual point-free complex \(\chi_*\), Paper 17
can compute descriptive order and dimension diagnostics on it without a
fundamental ensemble. It cannot claim Gamma-selected typicality, sampling
probabilities, or a universal dimension law.

**Priority:** weakest honest fallback.

## 9. New angles worth testing

### 9.1 Intersection rather than preference

Do not choose the most aesthetically attractive route. Construct independent
solution sets

\[
 \mathfrak R_{\rm branch},\quad
 \mathfrak R_{\rm coherence},\quad
 \mathfrak R_{\rm proj},\quad
 \mathfrak R_{\rm rates},\quad
 \mathfrak R_{\rm boundary}
\]

and compute their intersection. A singleton intersection is substantially
more compelling than a unique solution manufactured within one flexible
formalism.

### 9.2 Extremality and tail triviality

Projective laws often form a convex mixture of extremal laws. A physical
"one phase" or tail-triviality principle can eliminate mixtures, but it does
not normally choose which extremal law. The extremal boundary should be
classified explicitly rather than hidden in an infinite-volume limit.

### 9.3 Cohomological obstruction

Treat local log-odds as a cocycle on the point-free replacement groupoid.
Cycle integrability is zero curvature. Nontrivial cohomology classifies the
remaining activities; genuine physical 2-cells can kill classes. This turns
"anomaly cancellation" into an exact finite question without assuming
spacetime geometry.

### 9.4 Structural duality

Search for physical creation/annihilation or cup/cap experiments whose
composites are operationally equivalent to identities. If they exist, a
pivotal or spherical trace may provide an independently physical dimension
functional. Do not reinterpret record erasure as structural annihilation.

### 9.5 Response-derived adjacency, not response-derived weight

Paper 13D's complete response tensors may help decide which structural
alternatives are connected by an operational replacement. They do not by
themselves specify how much probability that edge receives. Use response to
derive the transfer graph only; demand an additional theorem for its matrix
elements.

### 9.6 Structural superselection

If no cross-structure recombiner exists, inequivalent complexes may be
superselected. Decoherence would then justify a classical structural event
algebra but still leave its classical weights free. This is a useful negative
result because it tells us not to pursue arbitrary complex phases.

### 9.7 Robust partial chronology before full selection

The Gamma-relative component-to-fusion precedence already proved in Paper 17
may be evaluated within every supported complex. One can ask whether its
local directed-response relations are invariant across all selectors before
asking for ensemble frequencies. This may preserve a partial chronology
result while the dimension ensemble remains blocked.

## 10. Concrete staged program

### Stage 0 -- freeze the question

Freeze a bounded Paper 13D sector scope and the exact distinction among
support, conditional channel odds, occurrence activity, root state, and
actualization. Freeze the ban on chronology and dimension outputs.

### Stage 1 -- extract the physical structural census

For every complete input in scope:

1. enumerate inequivalent resolved structural channels;
2. retain generator occurrence multiplicity;
3. separate target equality from channel equality;
4. prove a separating physical descriptor or interface for every retained
   distinction;
5. identify all continuous or contextual fibers rather than discretizing
   them; and
6. stop if all apparent branching is one channel plus Gamma history values.

### Stage 2 -- construct the replacement and coherence groupoids

Enumerate primitive rooted replacement orbits, connected components, cycle
space, physical 2-cells, tensor spectators, and deletion maps. Distinguish
staged traces from genuine coherence equivalences.

### Stage 3 -- run three independent mathematical branches

1. **Branch algebra:** test based-ring/fusion-category/FP conditions.
2. **Local specification:** solve all positive replacement cocycles, DLR
   extensions, and deletion-projective families under both justified base
   measures.
3. **Transfer dynamics:** derive any physical structural transition operator
   and classify its positive eigenmeasures and boundary phases.

The quantum branch remains dormant unless a cross-structure recombination
experiment is supplied.

### Stage 4 -- compute the residue

Intersect the three branches and report the exact moduli object
\(\mathfrak R\): empty, singleton, finite, infinite, topological/measurable,
or unclassified. Do not assign it a vector-space dimension unless such a
structure is proved.

### Stage 5 -- close or calibrate the residual data

- If \(\mathfrak R\) is a singleton, derive the law.
- If it has finitely many physical constants, design preregistered structural
  calibration experiments.
- If it consists of boundary phases, classify the cosmological state datum.
- If it remains large, reject the claim of a unique nomological selector.

### Stage 6 -- build the joint law

Construct \(\widehat\Gamma\), rederive Paper 13D conditionals exactly, prove
normalization, projectivity, non-Markovization, and covariance, and keep
actualization separate.

### Stage 7 -- decide whether Paper 17 is unblocked

Paper 17 may reopen only through one of three gates:

1. **Unique-law gate:** one accepted \(\Pi_{\rm phys}\) and joint law.
2. **Calibrated-family gate:** all residual constants frozen from independent
   structural data before any chronology/dimension statistic is viewed.
3. **Robustness gate:** the desired Paper 17 result is proved identical for
   every member of the already frozen \(\mathfrak R\).

One supplied actual complex permits only descriptive, non-typicality claims.

## 12. Recommended priority order

The next physics investigation should proceed in this order.

1. **Derive a concrete structural branching or replacement experiment from
   an enlarged typed Gamma.** Without this, all numerical selector proposals
   float above the accepted physics.
2. **Test branching fusion/FP and operational transition-ratio routes.** They
   offer the clearest chance of deriving numbers rather than parameterizing
   them.
3. **Solve coherence, rooted spectator, and deletion equations jointly.** Do
   not merely check them after fitting a Gibbs law.
4. **Use groupoid-DLR laws as an explicit residual family, carrying both
   orbit and groupoid bases until provenance selects one.**
5. **Compute selector robustness of the already-supported partial Paper 17
   precedence.** This may preserve physics progress even if the global law is
   not unique.
6. **If a finite residue survives, calibrate it honestly.** Do not launch a
   new chain of mathematical repairs in hope that consistency will magically
   determine every constant.

- Barandes formulates quantum systems as indivisible stochastic processes in
  an ordinary configuration space. This supports enlarging the configuration
  space to include structure if structure is dynamical, but it does not make
  a standalone structural state follow from the transition law:
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.21192
- Rideout and Sorkin derive a broad causal-set growth family from causality
  and discrete covariance; the general solution remains a family, showing
  how strong principles can constrain without uniquely selecting:
  https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/9904062 and
  https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/0504066
- Shalizi and Rinaldo show that projectivity under sampling drastically
  restricts many dependent exponential families, but projectivity is a model
  constraint rather than a universal parameter selector:
  https://arxiv.org/abs/1111.3054
- Etingof, Nikshych, and Ostrik provide the fusion-category and
  Frobenius--Perron framework underlying Route A:
  https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0203060
- Sorkin's quantum-measure framework identifies the genuinely quantum
  alternative to classical additivity:
  https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/9401003
- Dowker, Johnston, and Surya show that extension of a quantum measure to the
  full event sigma algebra can fail, including in quantum sequential growth:
  https://arxiv.org/abs/1007.2725
- Surya and Zalel give a concrete covariance/extension criterion for complex
  sequential-growth models, confirming that covariant observability is an
  additional theorem rather than automatic:
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2003.11311
- Chiribella, D'Ariano, and Perinotti's quantum-comb and purification
  frameworks strongly constrain admissible compositions but do not select a
  cosmological process state:
  https://arxiv.org/abs/0904.4483 and
  https://arxiv.org/abs/0908.1583
- Oreshkov, Costa, and Brukner show how operational quantum correlations can
  be formulated without predefined global causal order, a useful firewall
  against inserting chronology into the selector:
  https://arxiv.org/abs/1105.4464
- Oriti's group-field-theory framework provides a sum-over-structures
  architecture while making clear that fields, interactions, and other model
  data must still be supplied:
  https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/0607032
- Dittrich's cylindrical-consistency program shows why coarse-graining maps
  must be selected by the dynamics rather than imposed as neutral kinematics:
  https://arxiv.org/abs/1205.6127
- Groupoidification explains why inverse-automorphism factors arise from a
  particular counting provenance; point-freeness alone does not turn that
  provenance into a physical probability:
  https://arxiv.org/abs/0812.4864 and
  https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0004133

## 14. Final diagnosis

The best present hope is not that one fashionable principle—Gibbs,
projectivity, quantum amplitude, or renormalization—will produce the law.
The best hope is that the accepted relational physics supplies a genuine
structural transition/branching algebra and that several independently
motivated consistency routes overdetermine the same positive measure.
