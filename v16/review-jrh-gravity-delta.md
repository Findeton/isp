### 3.2 General decoherence-functional framework

`PARTIAL` — On finite common boundary Hilbert spaces, the displayed formula is
mathematically coherent. Let every `V[h]:H_- -> H_+`, let `rho>=0`, and let
`K_A=sum_(h in A) a[h]V[h]`. Then

`D(A,B)=Tr(K_A rho K_B^dagger)`

is Hermitian and additive because the class operators add. For any events
`A_i` and coefficients `c_i`,

`sum_ij c_i^* D(A_i,A_j)c_j = Tr(L rho L^dagger) >= 0`,

with `L=sum_i c_i^* K_Ai`; strong positivity is automatic once the operators
are commonly typed. This is the standard class-operator/decoherence-functional
shape, compatible with quantum-measure theory
([Sorkin](https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/9401003)); strong positivity is especially
important under composition
([Dowker–Wilkes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2011.06120)).

`OPEN` — The following conditions are still required before this becomes the
changing-carrier law claimed as the research target:

1. **Changing relation carriers:** specify boundary spaces/algebras and the
   domain/codomain of every `V[h]`. Histories may be summed only after an
   independently defined identification into a common boundary space.
2. **Different output geometries:** specify whether sectors are orthogonal,
   paired by a boundary inner product, or related by a gauge/refinement map.
   A bare direct sum makes cross-sector traces vanish automatically; an
   arbitrary nonorthogonal identification can manufacture interference.
3. **Normalization:** require `D(Omega,Omega)=1` for the allowed boundary
   state. If the law is to hold for every operational input, state the operator
   identity that enforces it.
4. **Instrument completeness:** exact decoherence of one partition for one
   `rho` gives additive scalar probabilities. It does not by itself prove
   `sum_alpha K_alpha^dagger K_alpha=I` for all inputs. On fixed Hilbert spaces
   each displayed `K_alpha rho K_alpha^dagger` map is CP automatically; on
   changing boundary algebras, the amplification/ancilla types still have to be
   defined before an analogous complete-positivity claim is meaningful.
5. **Coarse graining:** define the event algebra and make
   `K_(A union B)=K_A+K_B` independent of representation for disjoint events.
6. **Gluing:** define orientation reversal, boundary pairing/contraction,
   internal-state summation, gauge-volume handling, and associative composition.
   General-boundary frameworks make these axioms substantive, not automatic
   ([Oeckl](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0509122)).
7. **Refinement:** define the push-forward maps, gauge quotient, cylindrical
   consistency, and the topology/norm in which a coarse fixed point is sought.
8. **Conditioning:** `H alpha` must be an exhaustive nested successor
   partition of `H`; exact conditional decoherence and a nonzero denominator
   must ensure that the displayed probabilities normalize.
9. **Record stability:** zero cross terms must remain zero under every licensed
   future continuation, rather than hold at one cut accidentally.
10. **Objective actualization:** the functional supplies weights/probabilities,
    not the fact that one outcome occurs. The stochastic actualization and the
    physical criterion for a division boundary remain postulates.
11. **Approximate division:** provide a refinement-stable error norm and
    empirical threshold before replacing exact zero by approximate decoherence.

`NEW-BLOCKER` — **The geometry-sector overlap dilemma.** If distinct geometry
