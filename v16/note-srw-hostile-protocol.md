## Binding outcome policy

- Preserve `SRW-MAP-ONLY-WELD-REFUTED` only if no admissible map invariant
  recovers allowed grammar and persistence in the registered discriminator.
- Preserve `SRW-DICTIONARY-CURRENT-BLIND-BUT-FUTURE-PHYSICAL` only with its
  finite declared-future quantifier unless a law-generated continuation theorem
  is supplied.
- Preserve the bundle-weld finding only if the typed pair is coherent,
  representation-safe, and nontrivially linked to the relational rewrite.
- Preserve the reciprocal finding only as conditional exact erasure on the
  reached image; it must not be promoted into a weight-selection result.
- A correct transcript does not protect an ontological overreading. Conversely,
  absence of GR, QFT, particles, Hamiltonian, or actualization is not by itself
  a rejection because Paper 4 refuses those claims.
- A failure of overlap composition, configuration individuation, or graph
  irreducibility is material and must demote the earliest dependent finding.
- No repair is authorized until all three independent reports are frozen and
  jointly adjudicated. The candidate source, fixture, paper, transcript, and
  receipt remain immutable throughout review.

## Process exit

Only after all three reports exist may an adjudication be written. Any repair
must be frozen before execution and regenerated artifacts committed before
post-repair replay. Terminal status additionally requires complete worktree and
true off-tree verification of the adjudicated artifacts. Until then SRW remains
**REPLAY-VERIFIED GREEN-UNREVIEWED**.
