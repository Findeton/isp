## 1. Governing distinction

The repeated v4 weld failures were ordinary one-law typing and dataflow
failures. They require no quantum or Barandes premise. V5 repaired the
synthetic verifier's ability to recognize one executable finite object.

The deeper scientific failure is different:

> The programme treated indivisibility as an interpretive slogan while
> continuing to construct regional laws, instruments, record writers,
> relational rewrites, and comparison maps separately and glue them afterward.

An indivisible-law programme must reverse that order. It begins with one law
on complete, law-sufficient relational configurations. Regional views,
division instruments, records, comparisons, and relational rewrites may enter
the physical theory only as derived shadows of that law.

V5 connectivity is necessary engineering hygiene. It is not evidence that the
synthetic object is indivisible, relationally complete, geometrical, selected,
or physical.

## 2. Canonical ontology and law-type sentence

For Paper 12 and future successor prose only, the following sentence replaces
the phrase “between admissible division configurations.” It does not amend any
frozen APR source, artifact, result, or historical scope statement:

> ISP's candidate ontology is one actual, law-sufficient relational
> configuration; its missing fundamental dynamics is an indivisible
> stochastic law of transition probabilities between complete
> configurations, conditioned at admissible division events/times.
> Hilbert/history machinery is representational, and APR's `AB/BC` table is

`Complete` means sufficient for the fundamental transition law. It is not a
claim that APR's prefix syntax, finite profiles, raw cospans, graphs, node
labels, or any current quotient are complete. `Law-sufficient` does not mean
Markov-sufficient at arbitrary cuts. A complete configuration is the ontic
configuration argument of the primitive law; it need not screen off earlier
boundaries or make every intermediate conditional probability lawful. Any
boundary, division, or native history context required by `Gamma` must be
explicit in its type and cannot be hidden inside derived shadows.

Completeness requires a typed-source sufficiency certificate: any two
presentations assigned the same complete `Gamma` source argument—including the
same boundary/division label and any native history context explicitly
permitted by the primitive type—must have identical future `Gamma` profiles
under every licensed interface word. Distinct boundary or history contexts may
not be identified merely because their configuration component `x_in` agrees.
A cache, history variable, clock, or target-specific context is a failure when
it changes predictions while absent from the declared source type; if declared,
it is priced and audited for covariance and hidden-tick content. The future
grammar must have finite exhaustible closure or a terminating exact
invariant/closure proof. Otherwise configuration sufficiency is `UNPROVEN`, not
assumed.

One run may realize a compatible succession of configurations. That
succession is an actual history only in this derivative sense; it does not
introduce a universal clock, global tick, ontic foliation, or simultaneous
actuality of alternative histories.

APR has not constructed the co-reference, order, or compatibility doctrine
that turns realized configurations into such an actual history. That ontology
remains a candidate rather than a result.

In Barandes, a division event is a system-relative allowed conditioning time
for first-order transition probabilities on a fixed configuration space. ISP
may seek a relational boundary criterion, but that is a separate extension,
not another definition of Barandes's term. Neither a Barandes division event
nor an ISP boundary is, by definition, a durable record. If an ISP division
doctrine is supplied rather than derived, its status is priced
nomological/kinematic data and must be printed.

## 6. Complementary division and nondivision witnesses

No indivisibility claim is licensed without both witnesses in the same law.

### 6.1 Lawful division

At one registered division the candidate must derive:

- an all-input normalized positive instrument or equivalent division map;

- delayed recovery under every word in a predeclared closed future grammar;
- equality of every lawful alternate cut through that division.

This record-bearing division is an additional ISP witness, not the definition


on the complete record algebra for every licensed `w`. The future grammar
must have finite exhaustible closure or a terminating exact invariant/closure
certificate. Alternate-cut equality likewise requires a finite complete

and reusable division remain `UNCONSTRUCTED`.

### 6.2 Nondivision cut

At one registered unrecorded intermediate boundary the candidate performs two
distinct tests.

First, work at the complete native physical configuration type `C`. If
`Gamma` natively derives a typed `L = Gamma_{M <- A}`, test exact feasibility
of a positive stochastic `K` satisfying

```text
Gamma_{C <- A} = K L.
```

If no native `L` exists, report `NATIVE-CUT-UNDEFINED`; this is a typing result,
not by itself an infeasibility theorem. A stronger indivisibility claim then
requires an exact census or infeasibility certificate over a predeclared typed
class of pairs `(K,L)`. A non-stochastic intermediate map counts only when it
is computed from `Gamma` and its uniqueness is proved. When direct inversion
is unavailable, use an exact rational/algebraic linear or convex feasibility
certificate. Merely omitting `M` from the declared `Div` list does not measure
indivisibility; it leaves the division doctrine priced.

Second, after the native test, the candidate may test a predeclared enlarged
hidden-state/adversary class. It must show that no admissible positive
intermediate factorization in that class reproduces the whole law while
preserving all calibrated outputs.

The competitor class, hidden-state allowance, precision/field, and boundary
typing must be frozen before the result. The class must have a machine-readable
parametrization and a terminating exact decision method: exhaustive finite
enumeration or a checkable algebraic/convex infeasibility certificate. Sampling
cannot establish nonfactorizability and yields only
`NONDIVISION-UNRESOLVED`. Failure in one class is
class-relative. It does not rule out every enlarged-state divisible
representation unless configuration completeness and competitor exhaustivity
are separately proved.

Interference in one representation is not by itself an absolute
indivisibility theorem. It is load-bearing only when the alternatives and
their recombination are derived shadows of the same complete law.
