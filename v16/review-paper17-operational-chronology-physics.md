## 2. Verdict and first semantic counterexample

### 2.1 First counterexample — onset orbits lose multiplicity

Take two identical singleton generator experiments in independent tensor and
choose a symmetric positive history. The tensor braiding is then an
automorphism exchanging the two generator vertices $v_1,v_2$. Their local

$$
[(H,v_1)]=[(H,v_2)]
$$

under the frozen definition “a primitive transition occurrence is its

It consequently contains one orbit element where the physical tensor trace

The complete Paper 13D history still knows that its finite diagram has two

An orbit type, an occurrence instance, and the cardinality of an incidence
structure are different objects. Adding an intervention mark or landmark to

The onset germs descend, but the proposed point-free occurrence *set* does
not yet preserve multiplicity under automorphisms as required by the pin. A
multiset, marked-occurrence cover, finite incidence groupoid, or whole

### 2.2 Second counterexample — the chronology stopping line is too strong

The construction correctly leaves the localized reverse fusion intervention

the only possible noncircular orientation certificate. The accepted trace law
already proves an independent all-suffix prefix theorem at every actual
categorical division.

Let $f:S\to D$ be a prefix, let $w:D\to T$ be any accepted normalized suffix
word, and let $A$ be any event of the complete prefix trace. Paper 13D gives

$$
\Gamma_{w\circ f}(z)(H_f\star H_w)
=\Gamma_f(z)(H_f)\Gamma_w(y)(H_w).
$$

Therefore

$$
\begin{aligned}
P_{w\circ f}(H_f\in A\mid z)
&=\sum_{H_f\in A}\Gamma_f(z)(H_f)
  \sum_{H_w}\Gamma_w(y)(H_w)\\
&=\sum_{H_f\in A}\Gamma_f(z)(H_f),
\end{aligned}
$$

because every suffix kernel is normalized. The same equation holds for every
finite suffix length by structural recursion, for source cylinders by
averaging, for tensor/fusion suffixes, and after the physical quotient because
prefix restriction is equivariant and commutes with orbit pushforward.

Thus changing among *any accepted suffix operations* after a complete
division cannot change any complete prefix-reader distribution. This is a
probability theorem, not the assertion “there is no reverse intervention

available.

## 3. Independent reconstruction

### 3.1 Accepted experiment classes

Paper 13D supplies five distinct kinds of experimental change:

1. **localized marked intervention:** a typed $X,Y,E,E'$ program entry at a
   $B_0$-source generator vertex;
2. **source context:** a nonempty cylinder on public $B_0$ fields;
3. **whole-boundary preparation:** an exact value supplied at a later atomic
   source such as $B_1$ or $B_3$;
4. **operation choice:** selection of one accepted execution arrow or syntax
   tree, including different same-typed future endomorphisms; and
5. **reader choice:** an equivariant map applied only after the reader-free

Only the first class belongs to the accepted signed localized contrast
object. A later whole-boundary preparation changes the complete source and
does not retain a shared past; it is not a localized causal break. Two

explicit same-typed operation choices. Normalized trace composition proves
that none changes the retained prefix marginal. Fusion has no alternative
localized mark or same-typed field-setting generator at its own vertex. Its
reverse localized response remains genuinely untested.
