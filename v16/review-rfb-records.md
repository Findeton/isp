## 3. The three “record modes” are not one delivered event law

### 3.1 Classical feedback and coherent dilation

The delivered classical Kraus operators are


```text

```

The delivered four-dimensional permutation and reached isometry extract the
same two Kraus operators. Thus the classical instrument and coherent dilation

If the dilation's record factor is inaccessible, their channel equivalence is

### 3.2 The hybrid family is a different channel

The mode table does not interpolate those reset implementations. It evaluates


dephasing at `w=0` and identity at `w=1`. On input `|1><1|`,

```text
Phi_reset(|1><1|) = |0><0|,
Phi_0(|1><1|)     = |1><1|.
```

Therefore the classical/dilation construction and the MZI tag construction
are not one frozen event law. A common port name and one shared input do not
weld them.
