## 5. Fresh physics/ontology countermodels

### CM-O1 — stable record with an incomplete frontier

At $B_1^r$ choose two positive histories with the same $m=r$ and relational
packet but opposite $h$. Every stable-future arrow preserves the record
sector, yet $R_c$ produces opposite mandatory $t=h$. The same record therefore
has two future profiles. This is an exact stable-but-incomplete frontier.

### CM-O2 — response without causal direction

Consider two same-stage binary mechanism slots $A,B$ with a deterministic
target relation $z=A\oplus B$. Both `set(A)` and `set(B)` have unit response
on the complete $z$ reader, but the response data supply no ordering between
$A$ and $B$ and no antisymmetric or transitive relation. The frozen joint
relational stage has the same logical lesson: response coordinates orient a
contrast from a declared manipulation to a readout, not a spacetime causal
edge among physical happenings.

### CM-O3 — identical cardinality with different graph structure

For three fixed equal endpoint colors, every bond has independent positive
probabilities $9/25$ and $16/25$. Both the empty graph and the triangle occur
at $|I|=3$ with positive mass. Cardinality alone therefore fixes neither the
relational graph nor topology, order, dimension, volume, or metric.

### CM-O4 — perfect erasure with residual statistical inference

Fix one occurrence and $q_0=0$. Before erasure,

$$
P(r=0,q_2=0)=\frac{81}{625},
\qquad
P(r=1,q_2=0)=\frac{256}{625}.
$$

Both sectors map under $E_r$ to target values carrying $q^+=0$ and no $r$.
Thus $q^+$ can remain statistically informative about the lost record, but
both input sectors have positive posterior probability and no target decoder
is perfect. “Eraser” here means loss of exact future readability, not removal
of every correlation.

### CM-O5 — same endpoint law, different fusion history

With three independent components, first fusing components one and two and
then fusing their result with component three draws the same final collection
of independent cross-pair seeds as one simultaneous three-component fusion.
The endpoint marginal can therefore agree. The staged trace nevertheless
contains an additional fused intermediate boundary. Endpoint agreement does
not turn a physical staging into gauge or make the simultaneous constructor
secretly binary.

### CM-O6 — identical coarse response with distinct complete outcomes

The two $E$ alternatives flip both $x'$ and $y'$ and hence have the same
parity distribution, while their complete pair distributions are disjoint in
a fixed source context. Reader equality after coarse-graining is not equality
of physical laws or response measures.
