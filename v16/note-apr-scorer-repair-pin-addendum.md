**Date:** 2026-08-19  
**Status:** BINDING PRE-IMPLEMENTATION ADDENDUM  
**Parent:** `v16/note-apr-scorer-repair-pin.md`

This addendum closes interpretive loopholes found while the repaired scorer was
still unfrozen and before any v2 fixture truth or artifact existed. It changes
no APR fixture, law datum, outcome word, or attempt-1 byte.

## 1. Capability census, not an absence table

No primary or orthogonal coordinate may be assigned from a static table of
expected absences. The scorer must inspect typed frozen interfaces and compute
a generic capability census with witnesses, including:

- a valid adaptive frontier absent from the boundary factory;
- identity assignments present or absent at each boundary object;
- presence or absence of an actual tensor boundary/apex/process factory;
- presence or absence of a nontrivial vertical/horizontal naturality square;
- presence or absence of a complete target-independent probe compiler;
- presence or absence of a regional-overlap global selector.

The classifier consumes those computed capabilities. Synthetic generic
classifier tests must show both directions: supplying a complete abstract
boundary/gluing package moves the classifier beyond
`APR-BLOCKED-AT-BOUNDARY-GLUING`, while deleting a genuinely earlier
normalization or raw-atomlessness capability moves it to the corresponding
earlier registered word. These are generic classifier tests, not APR results.
The attempt-1 primary is neither expected data nor a protected output.

## 2. Controls cannot create positive baselines

A `SEMANTIC-MUTATION`, `ANALYTICAL-CONTROL`, `SCOPE-CONTROL`, canonical hash,
or dependency graph is falsification/sensitivity evidence only. It cannot
supply a missing positive baseline or promote process, one-law composition,
locality, causality, contact, E-37 irreducibility, regional congruence,
physical-image atomlessness, or ontology. `UNAVAILABLE` and
`NOT-CONSTRUCTED` are never passes.

In particular, L5's constructed finite conjugation family is an analytical
model derived from frozen finite leaf/regrouping data. It is not an
instantiation of the declared infinite intrinsic replacement grammar and
cannot promote locality.

## 3. Scoped dependency roots, not one global law

The frozen fixture selects no single global active law: `ql_000` does not
select `qc_000..qc_004` or `qr_000`. The scorer must therefore build and label
separate scope-indexed semantic dependency roots, at least for:

1. the `ql_000` question/filling presentation;
2. each explicitly selected continuation catalogue;
3. the `qr_000`-compatible delayed-reader construction.

It must report `NO-GLOBAL-JOINT-ROOT`. Adding a reachable reset moves only the
selected continuation root; deleting `qr_000` or its reader/delay semantics
moves the delayed-reader root; editing unreachable `hf_008` moves none of
these active roots.

Even a connected, typed dependency root proves ancestry only. It is not proof
that its nodes form one process law or satisfy composition, tensor, naturality,
or cut equality. Keep `one_law_provenance=UNCONSTRUCTED` until a genuine
filling-to-process assignment and its equations are frozen and verified.

## 4. The overlap-selector kill remains substantive

The scorer must independently recompute that `og_000` and `og_001` have the
same uniform `AB` and `BC` marginals—each pair value `1/4`—while
`P(A=C)=1/2` versus `1`. No canonical order, row order, hash minimum,
maximum-entropy convention, sparsity preference, or first-listed completion
may choose between them. Until a physical gluing rule selects a joint
extension, regional overlap gluing is unselected law data.

## 5. Candidate-regional E-37 language

In scorer fields and rendered prose, replace:

- “physical overlap/incidence” with “represented candidate-regional Boolean
  overlap/incidence”;
- “physical-to-blind projection” with
  “candidate-regional-presentation-to-blind projection”;
- “physical outputs” with “computed mathematical signatures/controls.”

Movement of an input signature, projection, or canonical hash under M16 is
not output sensitivity, geometry load-bearingness, or E-37 irreducibility.
Only one generated uniform physical `Tau` moving a calibrated held-out
instrument could earn that claim, and no such `Tau` is frozen.

## 6. Statuses are detected, not stipulated

The scorer recomputes whether a generated contact response, causal schedule,
locality construction, congruence, or atomless physical quotient exists. It
may diagnose `PRICED`, `UNCONSTRUCTED`, or a registered block only from that
capability census; it may not assign those words merely because the pin says
they are expected.

Finite support controls cannot promote locality without generated complete
effects, total process provenance, nonconstancy, faithfulness, order
reflection, all-partition replacement closure, regional congruence, and
post-quotient atomlessness. Declared arrows, cycles, contacts, or movement of
an input relation do not constitute causal influence or generated contact.

## 7. M15/P5 non-duplication

M15 constructs and diagnoses the oracle family: syntax-copy, identifier-hash,
and finite-depth whitelist implementations, including their provenance and
presentation/refinement behavior. P5 is the concrete fresh-valid-deeper-region
behavioral failure of that same depth-bound whitelist. If the changed object
is shared, the receipt cross-references one canonical payload hash and records
two distinct predicates; it may not count them as independent mutations.

## 8. Ontology ceiling is a priori and conditional

Section 6 of the parent pin is an a priori ceiling on any repaired result, not
a statement that v2 already has a positive construction. The ontic proposal is
a **postulated candidate**: one actual partial compatible web of durable
relational facts. APR has not proved actualization or global durability. At
most it may re-establish catalogue-relative recoverability of record bits on
one fixed commutative algebra.

A physical regional referent requires, jointly, a future-complete operational
quotient, Boolean/regional congruence, support faithfulness, nonconstancy,
order reflection, post-quotient atomlessness, generated composition and
gluing, covariance, and family-level eliminability. No representation is
promoted by name, canonicalization, ancestry, or an analytical control.

## 9. Binding terminology

Every live ledger or plan phrase “result-preserving repair” is replaced by
“outcome-neutral instrumentation repair.” The repair preserves frozen inputs
and methodological scope, not a scientific result.
