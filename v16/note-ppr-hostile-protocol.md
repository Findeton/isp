Each seat must independently:

1. read the PPR pin, core-freeze correction, fixture freeze, first-run failure,
   repaired source, fixture, transcript, receipt, generated paper, and
   candidate-verification note;
2. verify the frozen hashes, candidate chronology, result-free fixture, exact
   arithmetic, independent outcome reconstruction, seal totality, and its
   lens-relevant mutants;
3. reconstruct rather than merely quote every load-bearing equation in its
   lens;
4. distinguish a theorem, a fixture identity, a declared type, a calibrated
   empirical discriminator, and an interpretive analogy;
5. search for one explicit countermodel or mutant beyond those delivered;
6. audit every claim against the strongest actually proved quantifier; and
7. return one grade: `ACCEPT`, `ACCEPT-WITH-FIXES`, or `REJECT`, plus a proposed
   adjudicated primary and a minimal repair/kill list.
