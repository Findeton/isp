## 2. Branching implication scope

### Proposition V6.1 -- exact one-way implications

For every complete input \(x\):

1. descriptor-coherent resolved branching on a region \(B\ni x\) implies
   pointwise measure-supported resolved branching at \(x\); and
2. target-sector branching at \(x\) implies pointwise measure-supported
   resolved branching at \(x\).

Neither converse holds in general.

#### Proof

For item 1, the two disjoint descriptor events in version-5 equation (V5.2)
restrict to disjoint positive events in \(\Xi_x\), which is exactly (V5.1).

For item 2, let \(D_0,D_1\in\Sigma\) be disjoint target events of positive
target probability. Their total preimages
\(\tau^{-1}D_0,\tau^{-1}D_1\in\Xi\) are disjoint, and their resolved
probabilities equal the corresponding target probabilities by the
pushforward theorem. Their fiber restrictions therefore witness (V5.1).

The finite same-target two-channel control has pointwise resolved branching
but no target branching. The countable-modification coarse-bundle control
has pointwise branching while no uniform channel-index descriptor is
physical, so pointwise branching need not imply descriptor-coherent
branching for that index. \(\square\)

No converse failure supplies a measure, occurrence propensity, or
actualization.
