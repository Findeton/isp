# Closed relational process law

## Why conditional dynamics is not yet a cosmology

### Abstract

Paper 13D constructs one exact point-free indivisible conditional law
$\mathbf\Gamma_D$. Paper 17 extracts a scoped, law-relative partial
operational precedence from that law, but proves that no selected
chronology-valued ensemble follows. This paper asks the missing question:

> Can the experimental family $\mathbf\Gamma_D$ be the marked conditional
> sector of one autonomous joint law on complete relational process complexes
> and their histories?

The answer has two parts. First, every compatible joint extension factors
uniquely into the accepted conditional law and a marginal process propensity.
Second, closure, point-free covariance, component deletion, tensor locality,
and exact recovery do not select that propensity. A covariant family with a
free fusion activity $\lambda$ changes the resulting order statistics while
leaving every Paper 13D conditional probability exact. General-boundary
gluing, process consistency, exchangeability, maximum caliber, and full
composition invariance likewise fail to select a unique law without new
physical data. Moreover, an externally randomized intervention label is not a
closed physical selector: an internal setting device and its exogeneity and
mechanism-fidelity theorems remain unconstructed.

The result is therefore a no-go for deriving an autonomous cosmology from
$\mathbf\Gamma_D$ alone, not a failure of its conditional physics. A future
theory must either supply a separately motivated process-occurrence law or
treat the realized process complex as contingent cosmological data. No
dimension, metric, or geometry calculation is opened here.

## 2. Three different physical objects

The following must not be conflated.

### 2.1 Kinematics: what may be done

$\mathsf{Exec}_D$ is a typed menu of lawful operations. It says which arrows
exist, which words compose, which boundaries they connect, and which words are
refused. Permission is not propensity: an arrow's presence does not assign a
frequency with which nature executes it autonomously.

### 2.2 Conditional dynamics: what happens if it is done

For a point-free complete experiment $[e]$,

$$
\mathbf\Gamma_D([e])([H])
$$

is the probability of complete physical history class $[H]$ conditional on
that experiment. Normalization occurs separately inside every chosen
experiment. It therefore supplies no likelihood ratio between two different
experiments.

### 2.3 Occurrence dynamics: what process exists

An autonomous theory needs relative propensities for different unmarked
process complexes. This is a probability marginal $\Pi_{\rm phys}$, an
unnormalized weight $W$, an action $S_{\rm proc}$, or equivalent data. Paper
13D contains none of these objects.

In ordinary language: $\mathsf{Exec}_D$ is the menu, $\mathbf\Gamma_D$ gives
the outcome distribution after an item is ordered, and $\Pi_{\rm phys}$ says
what nature orders. The third statement cannot be read from the first two.

## 3. Autonomous complexes and marked experiments

### Definition 1 -- unmarked process complex

For a complete experiment $e$, forget only its preparation, intervention, and
reader marks. Retain its complete typed execution diagram, boundary sorts,
generator occurrences, component incidences, and physical carrier. Denote the
result by $\chi(e)$. The accepted presentation action descends to these
objects; their groupoid of isomorphisms is denoted
$\mathsf{ProcComplex}_D$.

The forgotten marks form a typed fibre $a$ over $\chi$. They retain their
addresses, values, and transport law. The pair $(\chi,a)$ reconstructs the
accepted experiment up to the action groupoid.

This is a derived kinematic groupoid. It is not yet a probability law and does
not assert that every diagram occurs autonomously.

“Closed” here is nomological, not necessarily boundaryless. A finite complex
may retain typed external boundary sorts, but an autonomous law must assign or
condition on their complete physical values explicitly; it may not leave an
unmodeled agent choosing them in the syntax. A genuinely boundaryless universe
is a special case, not assumed by this paper.

### Definition 2 -- externally marked and internally realized protocols

If a laboratory chooses $a$, its design distribution is external sampling
data. The physical target is

$$
\widehat\Gamma([\chi],[H]\mid[a]).
$$

For a closed universe there is no external chooser. A setting device, its
record, and its physical correlations must instead occur inside $[\chi]$ and
$[H]$. Conditioning on their record may recover the marked experiment, but no
unexplained probability on laboratory labels may select the cosmology.

### Definition 3 -- exact marked recovery

A closed extension has exact marked recovery when, for every supported typed
marking,

$$
\widehat\Gamma([H]\mid[\chi],[a])
=
\mathbf\Gamma_D([H]\mid[\chi],[a]).
$$

This equation preserves Paper 13D as a conditional sector. It does not say
how frequently $[\chi]$ occurs.

### Definition 4 -- internal intervention realization

An externally indexed family is not yet an autonomous universe. To realize a
protocol choice $a$ internally, a closed complex must contain a physical
setting device with readable setting record $A$. For every supported value
$a$, it must prove:

1. **positive support:** $P(A=a)>0$;
2. **source exogeneity:** after separating the setting device's own physical
   microstate, the setting record is independent of the target-relevant source
   variables conditional on a declared common context;
3. **mechanism fidelity:** $A=a$ activates exactly the Paper 13D typed source
   program or intervention and no different mechanism;
4. **exclusion:** every path from the device to the target is contained in the
   declared intervention mechanism and carried setting record; and
5. **conditional recovery:** conditioning on the physical setting record gives

   $$
   \widehat\Gamma([H]\mid A=a,[\chi])
   =
   \mathbf\Gamma_D([H]\mid\operatorname{do}(a),[\chi]).
   $$

Paper 13D defines typed external intervention programs but does not construct
an autonomous physical device that chooses among them. Internal intervention
realization is therefore a separate unconstructed coordinate.

### Proposition 1 -- regime packaging is not physical closure

For any normalized design law $Q(a)$, the expression

$$
Q(a)\widehat\Gamma([\chi],[H]\mid[a])
$$

packages the externally indexed family into a formal joint distribution with
the same marked conditionals. Different $Q$ give different joints. Without
Definition 4, this merely randomizes a regime label and supplies no physical
setting device, causal exogeneity, or autonomous process law. ∎

## 4. Compatible extensions

### Theorem 1 -- disintegration theorem

On every positive-mass process class, a joint law has exact marked recovery if
and only if

$$
\widehat\Gamma([\chi],[H]\mid[a])
=
\Pi([\chi]\mid[a])
\mathbf\Gamma_D([H]\mid[\chi],[a])
$$

for a normalized point-free marginal $\Pi(\cdot\mid[a])$ on compatible
process complexes.

#### Proof

Marginalizing the joint law over complete history classes defines
$\Pi([\chi]\mid[a])$. Division by that positive marginal gives its conditional
history law, which exact recovery identifies with $\mathbf\Gamma_D$.
Conversely, the displayed product is normalized and has the required
conditional law. ∎

### Corollary 1 -- exogenous intervention independence

If an external protocol does not dynamically select which autonomous complex
exists, then

$$
\Pi([\chi]\mid[a])
=
\frac{
\mathbf 1_{[a]\in\mathsf A([\chi])}\Pi_{\rm phys}([\chi])
}{
\sum_{[\chi']}
\mathbf 1_{[a]\in\mathsf A([\chi'])}\Pi_{\rm phys}([\chi'])
}
$$

where $\mathsf A([\chi])$ is the typed compatible-protocol fibre. This allows
a protocol to restrict to complexes on which its addresses exist but forbids
reweighting within that support. Protocols with the same support induce the
same process marginal. A laboratory may multiply by a design law $Q([a])$,
but $Q$ is not cosmological physics and cannot be used to select chronology or
dimension.

### Corollary 2 -- process action

Every exogenous compatible extension can be written

$$
\widehat\Gamma([\chi],[H]\mid[a])
=
Z_{[a]}^{-1}
\mathbf 1_{[a]\in\mathsf A([\chi])}
e^{-S_{\rm proc}([\chi])}
\mathbf\Gamma_D([H]\mid[\chi],[a]),
$$

where

$$
Z_{[a]}
=
\sum_{[\chi]}
\mathbf 1_{[a]\in\mathsf A([\chi])}e^{-S_{\rm proc}([\chi])}.
$$

Here $S_{\rm proc}=+\infty$ on zero-mass classes. An additional
history-dependent term changes the accepted conditional probabilities rather
than merely extending them.

Thus the entire missing law is $S_{\rm proc}$ or equivalent data.

## 5. Structural principles and the fusion-activity obstruction

### 5.1 Antichain and simultaneous-star complexes

For each $n\geq2$, let $\chi_n^\otimes$ be the tensor of $n$ independent
singleton primitive components. Its onset precedence object is an
$n$-element antichain.

Let $\chi_n^\star$ contain the same components followed by one simultaneous
$n$-ary fusion. Its onset precedence object is a height-two star: each
component onset precedes the fusion onset. Both complexes use only accepted
Paper 13D objects. Component permutation is gauge, and component deletion
maps each family naturally to the corresponding smaller family.

### Theorem 2 -- exact structural nonuniqueness

For every $\lambda>0$, the fixed-size marginal

$$
\Pi_\lambda(\chi_n^\otimes\mid n)=\frac1{1+\lambda},
\qquad
\Pi_\lambda(\chi_n^\star\mid n)=\frac{\lambda}{1+\lambda}
$$

is point-free, covariant under component permutations, consistent under
component deletion, and exactly preserves every conditional Paper 13D
history law. The ratio for inserting the simultaneous fusion is $\lambda$ and
is independent of labels and remote tensor factors.

Nevertheless, if $R$ is the number of comparable onset pairs,

$$
\mathbb E_\lambda[R]=\frac{\lambda}{1+\lambda}n.
$$

The height, ordering fraction, and interval-abundance distributions also vary
with $\lambda$.

Multiplying these conditional fixed-size laws by any independently frozen
size marginal $p_n$ leaves $\lambda$ free. In particular, even if Paper 13D's
primitive occurrence-cardinality law were separately extended to this process
domain, it would not determine the relative tensor/fusion activity.

#### Proof

Both weights are positive and sum to one. They refer to isomorphism classes,
not representatives. Deleting an input component preserves the antichain or
star family and the same relative activity. Conditional recovery follows from
Theorem 1 because only the process marginal changes. The antichain has no
comparable onset pairs and the star has exactly $n$, giving the displayed
expectation. ∎

### Corollary 3 -- covariance does not select dimension

Point-free covariance, deletion, tensor separation, exact marked recovery,
and a label-independent local replacement ratio do not determine a unique
process ensemble. They therefore cannot, without another principle, determine
a universal dimension.

### 5.2 Monoidal gluing leaves generator activities

Let $N_\Phi(\chi)$ be the number of physical fusion vertices. It is additive
under disjoint tensor and staged composition and is invariant under the
accepted presentation groupoid. If $W_1$ is any admissible unnormalized
whole-complex weight, then

$$
W_\lambda(\chi)=\lambda^{N_\Phi(\chi)}W_1(\chi)
$$

obeys the same multiplicative gluing relations wherever $W_1$ does. On every
finite bounded census it is normalizable for all $\lambda>0$; on an unbounded
census it remains a distinct admissible law wherever its partition function
is finite.

Therefore monoidal gluing constrains how weights compose but does not fix the
activity of a physical generator. An independent equation must determine
$\lambda$.

## 6. Why the familiar closure proposals are insufficient

### 6.1 General-boundary or compositional closure

Associating data to boundaries and requiring consistent gluing is the correct
architecture for avoiding a preferred global slicing. But gluing axioms
specify composition laws, not the numerical amplitude or probability assigned
to every region or process complex. The fusion activity in Section 5 survives
those axioms.

### 6.2 Process consistency without predefined global order

Operational process frameworks impose positivity and normalization for all
local laboratory choices. These conditions define a generally non-singleton
space of admissible global processes. They demonstrate that consistency can
be formulated without a fixed causal order; they do not select one process
matrix or one cosmological measure.

### 6.3 Exchangeability and projective extension

Exchangeability removes auxiliary labels, and projective consistency allows
compatible finite laws to extend. Representation theorems parameterize large
families of latent kernels. The exact $\lambda$ family is already a
projectively consistent counterexample to uniqueness.

### 6.4 Maximum entropy or maximum caliber

Entropy maximization needs a sample space, base measure, and constraints.
There is no normalizable uniform measure on the unbounded execution category.
Constraining expected operation count, fusion count, depth, or record density
introduces precisely the missing process information. Maximum caliber can
infer a path distribution after these constraints are supplied, but does not
derive them from $\mathbf\Gamma_D$.

### 6.5 Classical sequential growth

Covariance and a causality condition permit a broad coupling family of
sequential-growth laws. Importing such a law would add Markov births and a
growth-stage dynamics not contained in Paper 13D. Treating birth labels as
gauge does not remove the new physical couplings.

### 6.6 Quantum measure or sum over complexes

A quantum alternative would assign a decoherence functional or relative
amplitudes across different complete process complexes. Paper 13D supplies
ordinary probabilities inside each chosen experiment, not cross-complex
phases. A decoherence functional could be new physics, but cannot be recovered
from the accepted conditionals.

### Theorem 3 -- full insertion invariance is nonnormalizable

No normalized full-support probability on finite process traces can be
invariant under insertion of every licensed staged operation.

#### Proof

Choose a licensed staged operation $F$ for which

$$
\chi,\quad F\circ\chi,\quad F^2\circ\chi,\ldots
$$

are distinct finite traces. Invariance gives every member of this countable
orbit the same mass. Positive mass makes the total infinite; zero mass removes
the entire orbit and violates full support. ∎

A stationary law therefore needs a stopping, return, or transition kernel.
That kernel is the missing dynamics, not a consequence of invariance.

## 7. What could genuinely select the occurrence law?

A successful principle must provide an independently meaningful equation

$$
\mathcal F[\Pi_{\rm phys},\mathbf\Gamma_D]=0
$$

whose solution is unique, normalizable, and fixed before any chronology or
dimension output is inspected. Presently credible sources would be:

1. an exact conserved quantity intrinsic to the accepted physical histories;
2. cancellation of a demonstrable inconsistency or anomaly;
3. an independently defined coarse-graining transformation with a unique
   attractive fixed point; or
4. an experimentally calibrated autonomous occurrence activity.

None is currently constructed. In particular:

- equal weights are not normalizable;
- $\lambda=1$ is a convention, not a symmetry consequence;
- criticality is not licensed without an intrinsic coarse-graining map and a
  theorem selecting a unique critical point;
- simplicity depends on a coding language;
- maximizing response, records, entropy, or manifoldlikeness targets the
  desired output; and
- actualization of $H$ inside a selected $\chi$ does not select $\chi$.

The absence of a selector is physical underdetermination, not an invitation to
fit one after observing a preferred dimension.

## 8. Mandatory hostile controls for any successor principle

Any proposed $\Pi_{\rm phys}$ or $S_{\rm proc}$ must be rejected if it fails
one of the following controls.

1. **Intervention randomization:** changing laboratory sampling frequencies
   must not change the autonomous process law.
2. **Label cloning:** a relabeled representative receives no extra mass.
3. **Orbit multiplicity:** orbit mass is summed correctly; representative
   mass is never substituted.
4. **Hidden stage:** a generator enumeration index cannot enter the weight.
5. **Tensor spectator:** adding a disconnected spectator cannot change a
   local replacement ratio except through the declared tensor rule.
6. **Fusion activity:** antichain and star weights must follow from the
   principle, not an unreported constant.
7. **Staged versus simultaneous:** physically distinct staged and
   simultaneous fusion traces are not silently identified.
8. **Native nondivision:** no intermediate stochastic carrier is invented to
   make process weights compose.
9. **Cutoff dependence:** changing an auxiliary maximum depth cannot change a
   claimed physical limit.
10. **Base-measure dependence:** a different enumeration or serialization
    cannot alter the result.
11. **Output targeting:** dimension, height, ordering fraction, curvature, or
    manifold scores cannot occur in the selector.
12. **Post-hoc coupling:** every activity is frozen independently before
    dimension data are opened.
13. **Actuality shortcut:** an actual-history postulate cannot be used as an
    unexplained probability on process complexes.
14. **External apparatus:** an unmodeled setting device cannot masquerade as
    autonomous dynamics.
15. **Conditioning/intervention substitution:** a setting record correlated
    with the source cannot be treated as an internally realized intervention.
16. **Mechanism selector:** changing a setting label without changing the
    physical mechanism must fail conditional recovery.
17. **Quantum/classical substitution:** ordinary conditional probabilities
    cannot be treated as unprovided cross-complex amplitudes.
18. **Nonunique solution:** multiple admissible laws with different order
    statistics must be reported as underdetermination.

## 10. Scientific decision

There are two honest continuations.

### A. Conditional theory

Treat the realized autonomous complex as contingent cosmological data.
$\mathbf\Gamma_D$ remains its exact conditional dynamics. Dimension may be
diagnosed on a sufficiently large supplied actual complex, but is not
predicted by the law.

### B. Enlarged nomological theory

Authorize one independently motivated $S_{\rm proc}$ or quantum decoherence
functional as new physics. Freeze and review it before any implementation or
dimension analysis. Paper 13D remains an exact conditional sector, but the
enlarged theory is no longer “Gamma alone.”

Until one branch is explicitly chosen, Paper 17's dimension battery remains
closed.
