## Mathematical core to construct

For every boundary object `B`, begin with a finite syntactic vector space
`Hsyn[B]`, an immediate observation map `O[B]`, and typed continuations
`C[e]:Hsyn[B] -> Hsyn[B']`. The generic instrument must compute exactly

`N0[B] = ker O[B]`

and the descending family

`N(k+1)[B] = N(k)[B] intersection (intersection over e:B->B' of C[e]^-1 N(k)[B'])`.

At finite dimension the chain must stabilize. The instrument must return the
fixed family `Ninf`, stabilization depth, bases, ranks, and a certificate that
every continuation descends to the quotient. This is multi-sorted: one
ambient matrix and one kernel are not an adequate implementation.

For histories `alpha` and `beta` reaching different intermediate carriers but
continued by `U_alpha` and `U_beta` to one common boundary, the registered
comparison candidate is the context-indexed Gram pullback

`W[alpha,beta;context] = U_alpha^dagger U_beta`

restricted to the dynamically reached quotient subspaces. No single
context-free `W` is presumed. The construction must distinguish covariance
of `W` under admissible boundary gauge from invariance of its closed-cycle and
probability content.

The record census enumerates all partitions of the finite history/outcome set.
A candidate partition is stable only if every licensed continuation preserves
its sector distinction, every advertised all-input instrument is complete,
and its record is recoverable at every registered future boundary. A present
orthogonal tag that a licensed eraser recombines is not stable.
