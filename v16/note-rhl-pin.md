## 1. The one-box question

> Can the candidate joint quantum--geometric law be stated on whole bounded
> relational regions, independently of any point set, lattice, graph mesh,
> preferred slicing, or elementary tick, while ordinary probabilities and CP
> instruments emerge only at continuation-stable record boundaries?

RHL asks for the **type and internal consistency** of such a law. It does not
ask a finite graph to reveal the fundamental dynamics, and it does not claim
that a point-free formulation uniquely selects weights, couplings, a vacuum,
or a continuum phase.

## 2. The candidate ontology, law, and representations

These three levels may not be conflated in the construction or prose.

### 2.1 Candidate ontology — what would be real

The ontic candidate is **one actual relational record history**: one compatible
web of durable facts about relations, influence, and records. Its bounded
parts are regions. A region is primitive as a part of the actual relational
whole; it is not defined as a set of spacetime points.

This candidate does **not** posit:

- a manifold of pre-existing points;
- a lattice or graph whose vertices are atoms of spacetime;
- a universal clock, update counter, or preferred foliation;
- a wave function, density matrix, path integral, Hamiltonian, or
  decoherence functional as an additional substance;
- a fully fine-grained actual trajectory between records;
- many simultaneously actual branches.

Exactly one compatible record history is actual. Between durable records, the
law may compare alternative relational fillings coherently; those alternatives
are not thereby additional actual worlds or sampled microscopic routes.
Actualization at a genuine record boundary remains an explicit postulate.

Actors, particles, clocks, and spatial locations are not primitive entries.
They must be recoverable as stable patterns, excitations, or relational roles
inside the actual history. Geometry is the invariant causal, compositional,
interval, volume, and curvature content of the same relational web if those
roles are earned. It is not a separately named field whose only job is to
decorate an instrument.

The ontology is deliberately neutral between atomic and non-atomic regional
structure. If an exact theory later selects irreducible occurrences, that is a
result. If every proposed atom can be subdivided without operational change,
atomicity is representational. Neither a graph nor a continuum is assumed now.

### 2.2 Candidate law — what would govern it

For a common recorded boundary condition `Sigma`, let
`Alt(Sigma)` denote possible bounded relational fillings, each carrying its
own matter/process and causal/geometric content. The microscopic candidate is
an **unsliced regional process law**, represented when useful by a strongly
positive, Hermitian, biadditive functional

```text
D_Sigma(A,B)
```

on pairs of alternative filling propositions `A,B`. The law is attached to a
complete bounded region, not multiplied from probabilities assigned after
elementary rewrites. It must obey normalization, coarse-graining additivity,
regional gluing, disjoint composition, boundary covariance, refinement
consistency, and dynamic locality.

At a continuation-stable record partition `{A_alpha}`, off-diagonal terms must
vanish at the licensed grain, and an all-input completeness theorem must make
the induced boundary maps a CP instrument. Only there may

```text
p_alpha = D_Sigma(A_alpha,A_alpha)
```

be read as ordinary mutually exclusive probabilities. A positive kernel after
every chart refinement is not the candidate microscopic law.

The functional is a candidate **representation** of an indivisible process,
not automatically ontology. RHL must keep open whether the most economical
nomological statement is a Barandes-style indivisible stochastic law, a
decoherence functional, or an operational regional process. It may select a
law type only by the registered gates below.

### 2.3 Representations — what may be calculational shadow

Named graphs, causal sets, triangulations, spin foams, finite matrices,
Hilbert spaces, amplitudes, class operators, process tensors, path integrals,
fields, transfer matrices, and Hamiltonians are admissible representations or
regulators. They earn physical content only through invariant maps to actual
records and relational observables.

A finite cell, vertex, or update label in the receipt is never evidence that
reality is discrete. A continuous coordinate or manifold point is likewise
not promoted to ontology. Different covers, meshes, names, and cuts that
describe the same bounded relational content must agree on every boundary
prediction.

## 3. Two changes that must never be confused

RHL freezes a two-type doctrine.

1. **Representational refinement:** subdividing, regrouping, renaming, or
   reslicing a description of the same physical region. It creates no fact.
   The law must be invariant under it.
2. **Ontic extension:** a new durable relational fact becomes actual at a
   genuine division. It changes the record history and may change future
   causal/transport possibilities.

Calling both operations “cell creation” would make a regulator look like
spacetime growth. Any construction that cannot type them separately is blocked
before a geometry claim.

## 4. Why this object is needed

The committed evidence supplies four necessities.

1. WRC/QSF show that assigning the delivered probability-times-noncollapse
   rule at each displayed cut is not an affine quantum instrument.
2. JRH shows that inserting a complete record instrument at every microscopic
   alternative kills interference that survives when the alternatives remain
   unrecorded.
3. PPR--CEL repeatedly show that named graphs and attachments can decorate an
   operator calculation without generating it.
4. RFB closes with no common record-process law across two genuine cuts.

The external mathematical precedents are components, not the answer:

- the general-boundary formulation assigns quantum data to arbitrary region
  boundaries rather than privileged time slices
  (`arXiv:hep-th/0306025`, `hep-th/0509122`);
- causal sites replace underlying points by regions with containment and
  causal precedence (`arXiv:gr-qc/0410104`);
- decoherence functionals retain interference between histories
  (`arXiv:gr-qc/9507057`);
- locally covariant QFT makes embeddings/covariance functorial but keeps the
  background spacetime as an input (`arXiv:hep-th/0403007`);
- quantum causal graph dynamics and quantum networks supply valuable graph
  representations, but their vertices and circuit layers are not adopted as
  ontology (`arXiv:1607.06700`, `arXiv:2110.10587`);
- spin-foam refinement work explicitly treats discretizations as regulators
  whose dependence must be removed (`arXiv:2211.09578`).

RHL's proposed delta is the synthesis: a point-free actual relational history,
an unsliced quantum law over alternative relational fillings that include
their own geometry, stable record divisions, and an explicit demand that all
graph/mesh descriptions descend to the same regional law.

## 5. Four-gate census for every new object

### 5.1 The causal mereology of regions

- **Referent:** bounded parts of the actual relational record history, with
  containment, overlap, disjoint composition, and causal precedence.
- **Necessity:** it supplies locality and composition without pre-existing
  points or a global time.
- **No-smuggling:** it contains no metric, dimension, manifold topology,
  particle inventory, or chosen microscopic atoms.
- **Discriminator:** arbitrary renaming and representational subdivision must
  leave boundary predictions fixed, while a calibrated change of an actual
  causal/containment relation may change them.

### 5.2 The regional process functional

- **Referent:** the law's comparison of alternative complete relational
  fillings compatible with the same recorded boundary.
- **Necessity:** unrecorded alternatives can interfere and need not possess
  physical one-step probabilities.
- **No-smuggling:** strong positivity and gluing do not choose the numerical
  weights, division partition, or desired geometric response.
- **Discriminator:** a reconvergent unrecorded pair has a measurable cross
  term; forcing an intermediate classical kernel removes it.

### 5.3 The refinement/cut system

- **Referent:** alternative descriptions of the same regional content.
- **Necessity:** no lattice, graph, or foliation may become physical by
  notation.
- **No-smuggling:** refinement maps are fixed by declared descriptive
  equivalence and boundary content, not chosen after inspecting desired
  amplitudes.
- **Discriminator:** two inequivalent subdivisions and two cuts induce the
  same boundary functional; a tampered refinement map changes it and is
  rejected.

### 5.4 The stable record division

- **Referent:** a distinguishable fact recoverable, up to lawful relabeling,
  after every licensed future regional gluing.
- **Necessity:** it is where mutually exclusive outcomes and actualization are
  meaningful.
- **No-smuggling:** a label, orthogonal ancilla, or declared partition is not a
  durable record merely because it is called one.
- **Discriminator:** an erasing continuation demotes a pseudo-record; a
  redundant recoverable record survives; the induced all-input boundary
  operation is complete.

### 5.5 Emergent geometry

- **Referent:** calibrated invariant structure governing causal reachability,
  interval/distance, volume, and loop/curvature response in the actual
  relational history.
- **Necessity:** without operational work the geometry word is eliminable.
- **No-smuggling:** no metric, adjacency matrix, or phase lookup may be supplied
  independently of the regional law and then renamed geometry.
- **Discriminator:** one uniform law must produce and use the changed
  relational structure across a held-out family under the E-37 resource-parity
  game. This is a later rung; a formal regional law does not earn it for free.

## 7. The no-point/no-tick evidence standard

The scientific primary may not be inferred from a finite row count.

- The load-bearing R1--R3 claims require proofs quantified over arbitrary
  regions/refinements or correctly scoped external theorems plus proved RHL
  adaptations.
- Finite matrices and symbolic programs are allowed only to authenticate
  algebra, exhibit counterexamples, and kill mutants.
- At least two nonisomorphic subdivisions and two non-nested cuts must be
  displayed as receipts, but agreement there is a control, not the general
  theorem.
- No graph cardinality, update number, lattice spacing, or refinement depth is
  a physical observable unless separately derived.
- A continuum of manifold points is not the default alternative. The candidate
  remains point-free unless a manifold phase is later reconstructed.
- “Emergent” requires a map, an invariant, and an error/convergence statement;
  visual resemblance is refused.

## 9. Kill conditions

Any one of the following kills the claimed rung.

1. A fundamental definition requires a chosen vertex set, lattice spacing,
   finite atom count, external clock, or preferred sequence of cuts.
2. Representational refinement and ontic extension are the same operation.
3. Two lawful descriptions of the same region give different boundary
   predictions after calibration transport.
4. A claimed regional law is only a family of unrelated matrices or weights,
   not one gluing-compatible rule.
5. A positive microscopic kernel assigns exclusive probabilities to
   unrecorded alternatives while a nonzero interference witness survives.
6. Strong positivity is checked only for one prepared state or one finite
   list, not proved at the claimed scope.
7. The division partition decoheres for one input but fails all-input operator
   completeness.
8. A claimed record can be erased by a licensed continuation with no retained
   recoverable copy.
9. A claimed geometry variable can be removed or recoded without changing any
   calibrated relational experiment in the frozen adversary class.
10. Disjoint regional interventions change a remote unconditioned record
    before causal contact, or “Bob” is never defined under the changed
    relational structure.
11. A finite regulator agreement is paraphrased as an infinite extension,
    continuum, Lorentz, GR, or QFT theorem.
12. The paper ontologizes the Hilbert/decoherence-functional representation
    without an independent beable discriminator.

## 13. Scope walls

Even the strongest positive RHL outcome would establish a candidate law type
and one internally coherent realization, not nature's unique law. RHL does not
by itself:

- select amplitudes, couplings, vacuum, species, dimension, topology, or scale;
- derive actualization;
- prove global infinite-history extension merely from cylinder consistency;
- reconstruct a Lorentzian manifold;
- derive Einstein's equations, QFT, particles, scattering, or a Hamiltonian;
- predict a deviation from established physics;
- prove that spacetime is fundamentally continuous, discrete, or atomless;
- prove an absolute metaphysical ontology from finite operational data.

The desired advance is sharper: state the candidate reality without points or
ticks, determine whether quantum probability can be consistently attached to
whole relational regions, and expose the exact first place where genuine
geometry-generating dynamics is still missing.
