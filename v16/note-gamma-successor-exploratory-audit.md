## 3. The distance/phase claim is exactly false in this fixture

```text

K_c = P_c D_n R.
```

Because `D_n` and the cell projector `P_c` are diagonal,

```text
K_c† K_c = R† D_n† P_c D_n R = R† P_c R.
```

Every outcome probability is therefore independent of `n`. After the
projective outcome, `P_c D_n R|psi>` is the basis ray `|c>` times a scalar
phase, so the phase cannot affect any later projective probability either.

The exact three-tick control replacing `gamma_phase` by `1` reproduces the
entire sorted leaf law—histories, active dimensions, counts, weights, and
normalized site probabilities—byte-for-byte. The demo's “probe moves across
record histories” test is confounded by different projective outcomes and
growth; it does not test the γ phase. Thus the proposed distance role is
epiphenomenal here.
