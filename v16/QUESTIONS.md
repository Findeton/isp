# v16 QUESTIONS

Status words: OPEN, PARTIAL, REFUSED, TERMINAL.

1. **OPEN — What is the ontic successor object?**  Can one define a complete
   finite occurrence whose outcome contains the changed relational record,
   changed geometry/collar, and changed process state without inserting a
   fictitious intermediate time slice?
2. **OPEN — Is dynamic spacetime compatible with an affine quantum law?**  Can
   geometry react to the realized local outcome while the ensemble map remains
   linear/affine and no-signalling?
3. **OPEN — What are the genuine division boundaries?**  Which cuts license
   probabilities and conditioning, and which are representational slicings?
4. **OPEN — Is two-actor dynamics viable?**  Can a binary relational fact be
   created or changed with backreaction and a coherent idle-spectator
   extension?
5. **OPEN — What, exactly, begins at three actors?**  Is it merely the first
   closed loop/curvature probe, or is any robustly irreducible three-support
   process forced?
6. **OPEN — Does one local rule extend coherently to all finite arities?**  L2,
   L3, and L4 consistency is not an L_n theorem.
7. **OPEN — What selects the complete-successor probabilities/amplitudes?**  Do
   covariance, locality, records, and gluing leave one law, a finite family, or
   an unrestricted family?
8. **OPEN — Can the existing v14/v15 walk be reconstructed?**  A successful
   reconstruction must include state, instruments, clock/cuts, observables,
   and beable map—not only a matrix with the same spectrum.
9. **OPEN — When does a Hamiltonian exist?**  Is it recoverable only after a
   fixed-geometry sector and a clock/logarithm branch are selected?  What data
   make two Hamiltonian packets empirically or ontologically equivalent?
10. **OPEN — Are particle species derived?**  Do stable excitations follow from
    a selected vacuum and law, or can the same relational ontology host
    inequivalent spectra and species inventories?
11. **OPEN — Which 'affine constant'?**  Keep separate: (a) the affine-line
    coset/event-grammar choice, (b) an affine translation in a quantum channel's
    state-space representation, and (c) the cosmological integration constant.
    Does the candidate constrain any of them?
12. **OPEN — Does the proposal predict a deviation from QFT or GR?**  A
    deviation counts only if every admissible surviving law fixes the same
    dimensionless observable difference.  Generic possibilities do not count.
13. **OPEN — What does EPR become?**  Does the joint law preserve equality of
    all operationally equivalent decompositions and compositional
    no-signalling while allowing one actual relational history?
14. **OPEN — What happens to the v6 scale wall?**  Can the dynamics generate a
    dimensionless coupling while still failing to fix an absolute unit, or
    does it merely re-declare the missing scale?
15. **OPEN — Is the history action selected or representational?**  If a
    sum-over-histories/action form is introduced, what observable or
    consistency criterion distinguishes it from alternative weightings?
