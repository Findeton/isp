## 2. Review standard

The manuscript passes internally only if:

1. every positive theorem follows from the bound objects;
2. every negative theorem attacks only principles actually tested;
3. finite controls are not promoted to global discreteness;
4. a mathematical selector family is not called a physical selector;
5. the partial Paper 17 result is restricted to common positive support; and
6. the global Paper 17 dimension gate remains closed unless a literal gate is
   proved.

This review is a consistency and scope audit. It does not substitute for
future mutually blind adjudication if terminal scientific acceptance is
required.
