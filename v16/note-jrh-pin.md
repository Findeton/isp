## 1. The question

Can the present ISP ontology be completed by one local, indivisible successor
law

`(R, G, C, S) -> {p_alpha; R'_alpha, G'_alpha, C'_alpha, S'_alpha,
record_alpha}`

that simultaneously:

- changes pair-relational facts and their durable record;
- changes effective geometry, including enough boundary/collar data to carry
  its dynamical continuation;
- changes a predictive process state without treating a Hamiltonian, wave
  function, density matrix, field, or slicing as ontic by notation;
- remains operationally affine, compositional, and no-signalling on the finite
  quantum tests;
- admits two-actor occurrences and coherent higher-arity joint occurrences;
- reconstructs a Hamiltonian only in those frozen-background/clock sectors in
  which such a representation is actually defined; and
- selects, rather than merely accommodates, any nontrivial dynamics or
  consequence?

The last bullet is decisive.  **Existence is not selection.**

## 2. The candidate, frozen before construction

### 2.1 Ontology

An actual world is one growing finite relational history.  Its atomic durable
facts are binary relations (pair records).  A complete occurrence may jointly
change any finite compatible collection of those facts.  Hence:

- **record arity** is two;
- **process arity** is the support of an indivisible complete successor and may
  be any finite `n`;
- a triangle is the smallest relational carrier of closed-loop holonomy or
  curvature, but that does not make three the minimum event arity.

`G` is not merely an intrinsic spatial snapshot.  It includes, or is paired
with, a finite collar/conjugate datum `C` sufficient to distinguish geometries
with identical instantaneous relations but different lawful continuation.

The space of possible finite relational configurations may be specified once
mathematically.  That is not a fixed physical spacetime: the realized relation
and geometry labels, their adjacency, and even the successor carrier change
with the outcome.  This is the explicit point of departure from a theory whose
spacetime stage is fixed and non-interacting.

### 2.2 Law and representation

A genuine division region carries mutually exclusive **complete** outcomes
`alpha`.  At the operational quantum level each outcome is represented by a
trace-nonincreasing completely positive map between the input sector and its
output geometry sector.  The direct sum over all outcomes is trace preserving.
Geometry reacts to the realized outcome label/flux, not to a nonlinear
function of an ensemble decomposition or to an expectation value substituted
back into a one-branch equation.

The density operator used in the finite battery begins as a process-state
representation.  The candidate does not declare `rho`, `psi`, a Kraus list, an
action, or a Hamiltonian ontic.  A history amplitude or action may later be a
compact representation of the complete-history law, but it earns no
ontological or selecting force merely by being written.

### 2.3 The non-iterative step

The physical object is the complete boundary-to-boundary instrument.  A
factorization into “choose event, update matter, update geometry, write record”
is diagnostic only.  It is physical only if the factorization is derived,
introduces no unlicensed division boundary, and every allowed ordering gives
the same complete successor up to a predeclared boundary isomorphism and
transport.
