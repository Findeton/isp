# No points, no ticks

## An unsliced regional law for quantum histories

**Status:** analytical candidate. The construction establishes a
refinement-independent quantum-law envelope and a stable division class. It
does not construct physical geometry, quantum gravity, or a unique dynamics.

### Abstract

We formulate a quantum history law without assuming a spacetime point set,
lattice, graph of fundamental vertices, preferred foliation, or microscopic
clock tick. The ontological candidate is one actual relational record history:
a compatible web of durable relational facts whose bounded parts are regions.
The law is assigned to whole bounded relational regions. Alternative
unrecorded fillings may interfere, while ordinary probabilities and completely
positive instruments appear only at stable record boundaries.

The mathematical core is a directed family of presentations of the same
region. Compatible strongly positive sesquilinear functionals descend uniquely
to the algebraic inductive limit and are unchanged by a cofinal change of
presentation. No particular mesh is therefore physical in the resulting
law. A nonzero interference term proves that no ordinary positive probability
kernel can be attached to the intermediate alternatives while preserving all
their coarse-grained probabilities. At a record partition, a completely
positive instrument exists exactly when the class operations satisfy the
all-input completeness identity; state-relative decoherence alone is
insufficient. An append-only recoverability class supplies a consistent, but
postulated, stable-record realization.

The same structural axioms do not select a dynamics. Multiplicative character
twists preserve refinement, gluing, monoidal composition, and strong
positivity, while changing interference between relational alternatives. In
addition, fixed-factor no-signalling does not define no-signalling after the
boundary algebra changes: a law-selected transport of the subsystem algebra is
required. The resulting candidate is therefore an unsliced regional quantum
law with geometry still unentered. Graphs, Hilbert spaces, path integrals, and
Hamiltonians remain representations.

## 1. The result without technical language

Imagine describing a country by maps. One map uses counties, another uses
watersheds, and a third uses a fine survey grid. If all three describe the same
country, changing maps must not cause a physical event. A new bridge being
built is different: it changes what can connect to what. This paper enforces
that distinction at the foundations.

Reality is proposed to be one history of durable relational facts, not a stage
made of points and not a sequence of universal frames. A bounded physical
occurrence is described as a whole region. We may divide that region into
smaller pieces to calculate, but those pieces are no more automatically real
than grid squares on a map.

Quantum theory adds a second discipline. Before a durable record distinguishes
two internal alternatives, their contributions may reinforce or cancel. If we
assign an ordinary probability to each alternative too early, that relation is
lost. Ordinary chance therefore belongs at a real record boundary, not at
every line in a calculation.

This paper shows that such an unsliced quantum description is mathematically
coherent and independent of its calculational subdivision. It also shows what
the construction does not accomplish. The consistency rules permit many
different laws, and they do not yet tell us how a changed relational world
defines the same distant subsystem. Most importantly, a point-free language is
not yet gravity. The law must still make its relational structure perform all
the operational work of geometry.

## 2. Ontology: what exists

<!-- CLAIM:R1-POINT-FREE-KINEMATICS -->

The candidate ontology is **one actual relational record history**. It is a
compatible web of durable facts about relation, influence, and record. Its
bounded parts are called regions. A region is not defined as a set of smaller
spacetime points; the formalism has **no underlying point set**.

Only durable relational facts are asserted to be actual. The proposal does not
assert a maximally fine actual trajectory between records. Nor does it assert
that every alternative used in the quantum law is another actual world.
Alternative fillings are counterfactual arguments of the law that determines
the chances of later records.

An actual history can be stated without a global value assignment. Each stable
record boundary carries a commutative algebra of facts that can jointly be
true. Actuality is a compatible choice on the record algebras that actually
occur, transported consistently when one record boundary contains another.
No value is assigned to an unperformed incompatible experiment.

Actors, particles, clocks, and locations are not primitive labels. They must be
recovered as stable patterns or relational roles. Geometry is not a fourth
substance beside matter, records, and law. It is the causal, interval, volume,
and curvature content of the same actual relations, if a later dynamics earns
those readings.

The proposal is neutral about atomicity. A law might eventually select
irreducible occurrences. It might instead make every finite atom a chart
artifact. Neither discreteness nor a continuum of points is assumed.

Two kinds of change are fundamental to the typing:

- **Representational refinement** subdivides, regroups, renames, or reslices a
  description of the same physical region. It creates no fact.
- **Ontic extension** adds a new durable relational fact at a genuine record
  boundary. It changes the actual history and may change future possibilities.

Conflating them would turn mesh refinement into the creation of space.

**Actualization is postulated.** At a genuine stable record boundary, one
outcome becomes the next actual fact. Nothing in strong positivity, a Hilbert
space, or a decoherence functional derives that fact.

## 3. Law: what governs

The proposed law is regional and unsliced. It assigns coherent comparison data
to possible complete relational fillings of a common recorded boundary. It is
not a product of physical probabilities attached after elementary rewrites.

Let `Reg` be a symmetric monoidal category. Its objects are relational boundary
types. A morphism

$$
M:\Sigma_0\longrightarrow\Sigma_1
$$

is a possible bounded relational filling with those boundaries, taken up to
boundary-preserving relational isomorphism. Composition is gluing; the
monoidal product is disjoint juxtaposition. The definition uses bounded
regions, containment, overlap, boundary, and causal precedence, but never
points. This is a causal mereology rather than a point-set topology.

For each recorded boundary condition `Sigma`, let `E_Sigma` be an algebra of
finite relational propositions about possible fillings. A regional quantum law
assigns a Hermitian, biadditive functional

$$
D_\Sigma(A,B)\in\mathbb C
$$

that is normalized and strongly positive. Strong positivity means that for
every finite list of propositions and coefficients,

$$
\sum_{j,k}\overline{c_j}c_kD_\Sigma(A_j,A_k)\geq 0.
$$

The law is also natural under boundary isomorphisms, monoidal on disjoint
regions, compatible with gluing, and invariant under representational
refinement. These are requirements on one family of regional functionals, not
properties inferred from a single matrix.

The quantum measure of a proposition is

$$
\mu_\Sigma(A)=D_\Sigma(A,A).
$$

It need not be additive on an unrecorded partition. Ordinary probabilities are
licensed only at a stable record partition, as developed below.

## 4. Representation: what calculates

<!-- CLAIM:R2-HILBERT-REPRESENTATION -->

A Hilbert representation follows from strong positivity but is not added to
the ontology. Form the complex vector space on formal proposition symbols and
set

$$
\left\langle[A],[B]\right\rangle=D_\Sigma(A,B).
$$

Quotient by the null space and complete when a completion is required. This is
the usual Gram or GNS construction. The resulting vector, density operator,
class operator, or amplitude is a representation of the regional functional.

Graphs, causal sets, triangulations, spin foams, path-integral histories, and
finite matrices may provide presentations of the same law. Their labels and
cells have no ontological status merely because a calculation uses them. A
Hamiltonian is still further downstream: it requires a stable sector, a clock,
and a one-parameter composition law.

The framework is Barandes-inspired in this specific sense: the physical
process and its actual facts are prior to the Hilbert description. It does not
inherit a fixed configuration space or an external time parameter. The fixed
mathematical object is only the law's domain of possible bounded relational
propositions; the realized causal and transport structure is meant to be part
of what the law decides.

## 5. Presentations and the absence of a preferred mesh

A physical region can have many calculational presentations. For one region
`M`, let `I_M` be a directed set of presentations. Presentation `i` carries a
complex vector space `V_i` of cylinder propositions. If `j` refines `i`, there
is a unit-preserving linear map

$$
J_{ji}:V_i\longrightarrow V_j,
$$

with

$$
J_{ii}=1,
\qquad
J_{ki}=J_{kj}J_{ji}.
$$

The maps say that two descriptions represent the same boundary proposition.
They do not describe physical evolution.

Suppose each presentation carries a sesquilinear functional `D_i` and the
family is compatible:

$$
D_j(J_{ji}x,J_{ji}y)=D_i(x,y).
$$

### Theorem 1 (presentation descent)

<!-- CLAIM:R2-PRESENTATION-DESCENT -->

Let `I` be directed, let the maps `J_ji` satisfy the identities above, and let
the Hermitian strongly positive functionals `D_i` be compatible. Then there is
a unique Hermitian strongly positive functional `D` on the algebraic
inductive limit

$$
V=\varinjlim_i V_i
$$

whose pullback to every `V_i` is `D_i`. Any cofinal subsystem of presentations
gives the same pair `(V,D)`.

**Proof.** Represent an element of the inductive limit by a pair `(i,x)`. Two
pairs `(i,x)` and `(j,y)` are equivalent when some common refinement `k`
satisfies

$$
J_{ki}x=J_{kj}y.
$$

For classes represented by `(i,x)` and `(j,y)`, choose a common refinement
`k` and define

$$
D([i,x],[j,y])
=
D_k(J_{ki}x,J_{kj}y).
$$

If another common refinement is chosen, directedness supplies a further common
refinement and compatibility makes the two values equal. The same argument
shows that changing either representative changes nothing. Sesquilinearity and
Hermiticity descend directly.

For a finite list of limit classes, choose one presentation refining all their
representatives. The strong-positivity quadratic form of `D` is then exactly
the strong-positivity quadratic form of that presentation and is nonnegative.
Uniqueness follows because every limit class has a representative in some
`V_i`. A cofinal subsystem reaches a common refinement of every finite list,
so it produces the same equivalence classes and functional. QED.

The theorem is algebraic. It yields a presentation-independent cylinder law.
It does not prove countable additivity, a sigma-algebra completion, or an
infinite-history extension. Those require additional hypotheses.

The class is nonempty without choosing spacetime points. For example, take a
free symmetric monoidal category of abstract boundary regions and relational
fillings, assign Hilbert spaces to its boundary types, and assign bounded maps
to its generators. The universal property extends those assignments to a
monoidal functor. Give every descriptive presentation an isometric map into
the corresponding boundary space and pull the Gram functional back along that
map. Theorem 1 then produces one functional independent of presentation. This
is an existence construction for the regional quantum-law type. The generator
maps remain physical law data; consistency has not selected them.

The same argument removes a preferred cut. If a regional representation
factors through two different intermediate boundaries, gluing compatibility
requires both factorizations to represent the same composite morphism. A cut
is then a factorization used in a calculation, not a universal instant at
which reality is sampled.

## 6. Why an ordinary kernel cannot live at every refinement

### Theorem 2 (no probability at an interfering refinement)

<!-- CLAIM:R2-NO-INTERMEDIATE-KERNEL -->

Let `{A_1,...,A_n}` be a disjoint intermediate refinement of a regional
proposition `A`, and define `mu(B)=D(B,B)`. If some union of the `A_i` has a
nonzero total off-diagonal term, then no ordinary probability measure on those
intermediate alternatives can agree with `mu` on all refined propositions and
their unions.

**Proof.** Biadditivity gives, for any subset `S`,

$$
\mu\!\left(\bigcup_{i\in S}A_i\right)
=
\sum_{i\in S}D(A_i,A_i)
+
\sum_{\substack{i,j\in S\\i\neq j}}D(A_i,A_j).
$$

An ordinary probability measure agreeing on each `A_i` must assign the union
the first sum only. If the second sum is nonzero for any `S`, finite additivity
is contradicted. QED.

The theorem is not a claim that stochastic ontology is impossible. It says
that a stochastic law capable of representing the quantum process cannot
factor physically through positive probabilities on every unrecorded
intermediate refinement. If expressed stochastically, it must be indivisible
and multi-time. A decoherence functional or Hilbert construction retains the
required relational phase information.

An exact two-route receipt makes the distinction visible. With route
amplitudes

$$
a_1=\frac{9}{25},
\qquad
a_2=-\frac{16}{25},
$$

the coherent weight is

$$
\left|a_1+a_2\right|^2=\frac{49}{625},
$$

while assigning the routes exclusive probabilities gives

$$
|a_1|^2+|a_2|^2=\frac{337}{625}.
$$

The difference is the off-diagonal term `-288/625`. The arithmetic is a finite
receipt for Theorem 2, not a claim that the world contains two lattice paths.

## 7. Record boundaries and ordinary probability

### Theorem 3 (division instrument)

<!-- CLAIM:R3-DIVISION-INSTRUMENT -->

Let a proposed record boundary have outcomes `alpha`, with fine class
operations `K_{alpha r}:H_in -> H_out,alpha`. Define

$$
\mathcal J_\alpha(\rho)
=
\sum_rK_{\alpha r}\rho K_{\alpha r}^\dagger.
$$

Then every `J_alpha` is completely positive and trace nonincreasing when its
effect is bounded by the identity. The family is an all-input complete
instrument exactly when

$$
\sum_{\alpha,r}K_{\alpha r}^\dagger K_{\alpha r}=I.
$$

**Proof.** The displayed operator-sum form is completely positive, including
after tensoring with an arbitrary ancilla. Cyclicity of trace gives

$$
\sum_\alpha\operatorname{Tr}\mathcal J_\alpha(\rho)
=
\operatorname{Tr}\!\left[
\rho\sum_{\alpha,r}K_{\alpha r}^\dagger K_{\alpha r}
\right].
$$

This equals `Tr rho` for every positive trace-class input if and only if the
operator in brackets is the identity. QED.

Where unrecorded fine alternatives inside one outcome remain coherent, their
amplitudes belong in a class operator before the completely positive boundary
operation is formed. Replacing that coherent sum by separate Kraus terms would
insert a record that the law did not supply.

State-relative decoherence and normalization are insufficient. The operator

$$
K=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&2\end{pmatrix}
$$

preserves the norm of the preparation `(1,0)` but has

$$
K^\dagger K=\operatorname{diag}(1,4),
$$

so it is not a complete operation on arbitrary inputs.

## 8. Permanence is a property of the licensed future law

Decoherence at one boundary does not make a fact permanent. A stable record
must remain recoverable under every licensed future regional continuation, up
to a lawful relabeling of its pointer values.

Let `C` be the commutative record algebra generated by the outcome
projections. For a continuation `Phi`, record recovery requires a channel or
algebra morphism `R_Phi` satisfying

$$
R_\Phi\circ\Phi\big|_C=\operatorname{id}_C.
$$

### Theorem 4 (append-only record stability)

<!-- CLAIM:R3-APPEND-ONLY-STABILITY -->

Suppose every licensed continuation embeds the record algebra as

$$
c\longmapsto\pi_\Phi(c)\otimes I
$$

inside a larger future algebra, where `pi_Phi` is an automorphism of `C`, and
suppose the discarded future factor admits a normalized state. Then the record
is recoverable after every finite composition of licensed continuations.

**Proof.** Apply a conditional expectation onto the displayed copy of `C`,
then apply `pi_Phi^{-1}`. This is a left inverse on `C`. For a composite of
continuations the embeddings tensor further factors and the automorphisms
compose; the corresponding conditional expectations and inverse
automorphisms compose in reverse order. Thus a left inverse exists after every
licensed finite continuation. QED.

This theorem constructs a stable record class, not a derivation of nature's
continuation catalogue. “Append only” is a nomological restriction and must be
priced as such. Enlarging the licensed law can demote the record.

The record result is not a neighboring mechanism glued on after the fact. Start
with one regional law on the category above, enrich each genuine division
boundary by its commutative record algebra, use Theorem 3 for the boundary
instrument, and restrict all later morphisms to the append-only class of
Theorem 4. Composition remains the same regional composition and the record
embedding composes functorially. This gives one common law with coherent
unrecorded interiors and stable recorded boundaries. It is an existence
construction; the restriction and the numerical regional maps remain
unselected.

The eraser control is exact. A controlled writer that is an involution writes
a flag on its first application and erases it on its second. With one flag the
correlation disappears. With two copied flags, erasing the first leaves the
second correlated; in the registered basis the surviving support is `{0,5}`.
The example shows why bare invariance of a record algebra is weaker than
recoverability and why redundancy can preserve a fact globally.

<!-- CLAIM:R3-ACTUALIZATION-POSTULATE -->

Even an exactly stable complete instrument yields a probability distribution,
not the occurrence of one outcome. Actualization is postulated as the extension
of the one actual record history by one compatible outcome. The postulate must
respect inclusion of stable record algebras; no refinement-stable
actualization rule is derived here.

## 9. Structural consistency does not select the dynamics

### Theorem 5 (character-twist nonselection)

<!-- CLAIM:R2-STRUCTURAL-NONSELECTION -->

Let `Z(M)` be a refinement-invariant monoidal regional amplitude assignment.
Let `ell(M)` take values in an abelian group and satisfy

$$
\ell(M_2\circ M_1)=\ell(M_2)+\ell(M_1),
$$

with the analogous law for disjoint composition, and let `ell` be unchanged by
representational refinement. For any unitary character `chi`, define

$$
Z^\chi(M)=\chi(\ell(M))Z(M).
$$

Then `Z^chi` is also refinement invariant, monoidal, and gluing compatible. Its
associated decoherence functional remains strongly positive. A coarse-grained
probability changes precisely when the relative character changes the real
part of a nonzero cross term; the exact counterfamily below realizes that
condition.

**Proof.** Additivity of `ell` and multiplicativity of `chi` give

$$
Z^\chi(M_2\circ M_1)
=
Z^\chi(M_2)Z^\chi(M_1),
$$

and the same calculation proves monoidality. Refinement invariance follows
from invariance of `ell`. On any finite alternative list the twisted
decoherence matrix is obtained from the original Gram matrix by conjugation
with a diagonal unitary matrix, so it remains positive. For two alternatives,
the cross contribution changes from

$$
2\operatorname{Re}D_{12}
$$

to

$$
2\operatorname{Re}\!\left[
\overline{\chi(\ell_1)}\chi(\ell_2)D_{12}
\right].
$$

It moves exactly when these real parts differ. QED.

The theorem is a general nonselection result. Refinement, gluing, covariance,
normalization, and positivity alone permit both the trivial character and
prediction-moving characters. The exact receipt uses four fourth-root
characters: all 196 tested composition identities hold, while one coherent
boundary weight takes the values `1,1/2,0,1/2`.

The additive label `ell` is not thereby geometry. Calling it curvature would
repeat the error of naming a phase or a graph label without showing that it
performs calibrated geometric work.

## 10. Locality when the subsystem itself changes

<!-- CLAIM:R4-FIXED-FACTOR-LOCALITY -->

At fixed factorization, the standard theorem survives unchanged. If Alice
applies any trace-preserving completely positive map locally, Bob's
unconditioned marginal is invariant. This follows by applying the dual map to
the identity on Alice, or directly by Kraus completeness. An exact Bell-state
control gives `I/2` for Bob before and after Alice's dephasing.

### Proposition 6 (dynamic-subsystem referent)

<!-- CLAIM:R4-DYNAMIC-LOCALITY-UNENTERED -->

When a regional occurrence changes the output boundary algebra, a comparison
of “Bob before” with “Bob after” is not defined until the law supplies a
transport or embedding of Bob's operational algebra into the new boundary.
If two admissible embeddings move a calibrated expectation, fixed-factor
no-signalling cannot decide between them.

**Proof.** A Bob observable `b` before the occurrence belongs to an algebra
`B`. After the occurrence the state is a functional on a possibly different
algebra `A'`. Its Bob expectation has the form

$$
\omega'(j_B(b)),
$$

which requires a physical map `j_B:B -> A'`. If `j_B` and `j'_B` are both
compatible with the bare boundary typing and

$$
\omega'(j_B(b))\neq\omega'(j'_B(b)),
$$

then no unique Bob marginal exists at that typing. A law-selected map or an
equivalence theorem is necessary before no-signalling has a truth value. QED.

The exact receipt uses a state with populations `3/4` and `1/4`; identity and
swapped boundary calibrations read those two values. The numbers prove only
that the missing transport is operationally capable of mattering. They do not
choose one transport.

This is the first block in the physical unification programme. The regional
law envelope passes fixed-boundary locality, but it does not yet define dynamic
subsystems, relational separation, or all pre-contact interventions after a
boundary change.

## 11. Why point-free is not yet geometry

<!-- CLAIM:R4-GEOMETRY-UNENTERED -->

A causal mereology is a suitable home for geometry, not a derivation of it.
For relational structure to count as physical geometry, one law must make it
perform at least four coordinated roles:

1. determine causal reachability and separation;
2. determine calibrated interval or signal-propagation data;
3. determine an operational volume or measure reading;
4. determine loop or curvature response through relational transport.

The same structure must be changed by physical content and then alter later
physical behavior. A sum over labels, a character phase, or a boundary category
supplied in advance does not meet this standard.

No such dynamics is constructed here. The character-twist theorem makes the
gap sharper: structural consistency permits prediction-moving relational
weights but does not select them or make them geometric. A decisive successor
would need a uniform law across a held-out family of boundary problems and a
resource-matched eliminability test. Neither a finite lookup table nor a
memoryless null would be an adequate adversary.

## 12. What becomes of the Hamiltonian

<!-- CLAIM:R5-HAMILTONIAN-SHADOW -->

A Hamiltonian is not part of the point-free ontology. It is recovered only
when a stable boundary sector carries a selected clock parameter and a
strongly continuous one-parameter unitary composition law

$$
U(s+t)=U(s)U(t).
$$

Stone's theorem then supplies a self-adjoint generator `H`, up to the usual
domain and representation choices. If the selected stable law is a completely
positive semigroup, the generic generator is Lindbladian rather than purely
Hamiltonian. If the regional process is indivisible and lacks a one-parameter
factorization, neither generator is fundamental.

Thus the Hamiltonian regains its classical philosophical role: an economical
representation of repeated behavior in a stable clocked sector. It is not what
the unsliced law says exists.

## 13. Relation to existing frameworks

The construction is a synthesis, not a claim of new primitive arithmetic.

**Indivisible stochastic processes.** Barandes formulates quantum theory using
a fixed configuration space and stochastic laws that need not be divisible at
arbitrary times. This supplies the closest ontology/representation inversion.
The present construction differs by refusing a fixed pointwise configuration
space and an external time parameter; it uses whole relational regions and
leaves their realized causal structure dynamical. See
[Barandes, “The Stochastic-Quantum Correspondence”](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.21192).

**Quantum measure and consistent histories.** A decoherence functional carries
the cross-history information that an ordinary probability measure loses.
The present no-kernel theorem is the regional form of that distinction. These
frameworks do not by themselves select durable records, actualization, or
dynamic geometry. See [Sorkin, “Quantum Mechanics as Quantum Measure
Theory”](https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/9507057).

**General-boundary quantum theory.** Oeckl associates state spaces and
amplitudes with arbitrary boundaries and regions, eliminating the privileged
initial/final time split. RHL inherits that compositional stance but does not
take the spacetime region or its metric as fixed input. See
[Oeckl, “A General Boundary Formulation for Quantum Mechanics and Quantum
Gravity”](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0306025) and
[“General Boundary Quantum Field Theory”](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0509122).

**Causal sites.** Christensen and Crane replace the underlying point set by
regions carrying containment and causal relations. This is the direct
kinematic precedent for the causal mereology used here. Their construction
does not supply the regional quantum dynamics developed or demanded here. See
[“Causal Sites as Quantum Geometry”](https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/0410104).

**Locally covariant quantum field theory.** Functorial covariance of local
algebras provides a model for naturality under embeddings. The background
spacetime remains an input, however, and so the framework does not solve the
dynamic-boundary referent. See [Fredenhagen, “Locally Covariant Quantum Field
Theory”](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0403007).

**Quantum causal graph dynamics.** Graph-superposition and graph-relative
causality supply valuable calculational representations and locality tools.
Here graphs are regulators that must descend under refinement, not the
ontology. The vertex-preserving structure theorem therefore does not provide
the missing dynamic regional law. See [Arrighi and Martiel, “Quantum Causal
Graph Dynamics”](https://arxiv.org/abs/1607.06700).

**Refinement in spin foams.** Spin-foam work explicitly treats discretizations
as regulators and seeks consistent boundary amplitudes in refinement limits.
This is methodologically aligned with presentation descent. It does not by
itself select a continuum phase or actualization. See [Asante, Dittrich, and
Steinhaus, “Spin Foams, Refinement Limit and Renormalization”](https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.09578).

**Quantum causal histories and process tensors.** Completely positive maps on
causal boundaries and intervention-complete multi-time processes provide
important representation machinery. Their interfaces or causal skeletons are
normally supplied. See [Hawkins, Markopoulou, and Sahlmann, “Evolution in
Quantum Causal Histories”](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0302111) and
[Pollock et al., “Operational Markov Condition for Quantum
Processes”](https://arxiv.org/abs/1512.00589).

## 14. Exact receipts and what they do not prove

The attached exact-arithmetic bundle checks twenty public and regulator
controls. It includes two nonisomorphic presentations descending to one
boundary map, a descent tamper, the interference witness, record erasure and
redundancy, character-twist composition, fixed-factor locality, and changed-
boundary calibration ambiguity.

Those calculations authenticate formulas and counterexamples. Theorem 1 is
not true because two examples agree; it is true by the directed-limit proof.
Theorem 2 is not true because `49/625` differs from `337/625`; the numbers are
one witness to its general additive contradiction. No row count or matrix
dimension is a physical scale.

The finite bundle is intentionally incapable of deciding whether reality is
discrete, continuous, or atomless. It also cannot establish an infinite quantum
measure. Extension from compatible cylinder functionals to a countably
additive history object can fail and is a separate problem; see [Dowker,
Johnston, and Surya, “On Extending the Quantum Measure”](https://arxiv.org/abs/1007.2725).

## 15. What has and has not been achieved

The paper establishes four things.

1. A point-free, no-tick regional kinematics is precise without assuming
   either a lattice or a continuum of points.
2. Strongly positive regional quantum laws descend through arbitrary directed
   representational refinements at the algebraic cylinder level.
3. Interference forbids ordinary positive probabilities at unrecorded
   refinements, while stable all-input record instruments recover ordinary
   probabilities at divisions.
4. The structural surface does not select a dynamics, and dynamic-subsystem
   locality is undefined until the law transports operational algebras across
   changed boundaries.

The paper does not establish physical geometry, a unique law, an infinite
history measure, a Lorentzian manifold, Einstein dynamics, quantum field
theory, particles, scattering, or an empirical deviation. It supplies the law
type and the ontology in which those questions can be asked without mistaking
a regulator for reality.

<!-- CLAIM:WALL-GR-QFT -->

The law-type conclusion is grain-dependent:

- at an unrecorded region, a decoherence-functional or equivalent Hilbert
  representation is required to retain interference;
- if the fundamental law is phrased stochastically, it must be indivisible and
  multi-time rather than a chain of microscopic positive kernels;
- at a stable record boundary, an ordinary stochastic kernel or CP instrument
  is sufficient;
- whether a higher-order process is required remains inconclusive until causal
  order itself is an operational variable.

The missing physics is now sharply located. It is not another finite graph
rule. It is a regulator-independent regional dynamics that selects its weights,
generates and uses its causal/geometric structure, transports subsystems under
that change, and admits a stable record and infinite-extension theory.
