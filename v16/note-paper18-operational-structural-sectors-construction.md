## Exact mathematical results

### 1. Finite bounded data do not force global discreteness

The inverse system

    S_n = {0,1}^n

has finite discrete levels but the uncountable nonatomic inverse limit
`{0,1}^N`. Therefore the finiteness of every accepted Paper 13D carrier is
insufficient to select a finite or countable global ontology.

### 2. Current Gamma has a structural/history branching fork

For a fixed component family, Paper 13D simultaneous fusion has one unmarked
structural target. Fresh cross-component bond values are random histories
inside that target.

- If bond values are treated as outcomes, their probabilities are already
  supplied by `Gamma_D`.
- If unmarked process complexes are the selector objects, current fusion is
  structurally deterministic.

Consequently the accepted fusion does not yet provide a nontrivial physical
fusion multiplicity algebra from which new occurrence odds could be derived.

### 3. General positive-character theorem

For any independently derived finite positive associative channel kernel
`M_(a,b)(dc)` and positive character `d` satisfying

    d(a)d(b) = integral d(c) M_(a,b)(dc),

the law

    P_(a,b)(dc) = d(c) M_(a,b)(dc) / (d(a)d(b))

is normalized. Associativity makes its final distribution independent of
binary bracketing. Finite Frobenius--Perron channel probabilities are one
special case, not the assumed ontology.

### 4. Measure/character gauge

Subject to finiteness, the transformation

    M^h_(a,b)(dc) = h(a)h(b) M_(a,b)(dc) / h(c)
    d^h(c) = h(c)d(c)

leaves the physical conditional channel kernel unchanged. A separate claim
for raw multiplicity or dimension therefore requires separate operational
provenance.

### 5. Representation no-go

Every associative Markov convolution can be rewritten as `M=P`, `d=1`.
Finding a normalized hypergroup or positive-character representation after
fitting probabilities therefore does not explain those probabilities. The
nonprobabilistic channel measure must be fixed first.

### 6. Discrete and continuous nonuniqueness controls

Both deterministic additive examples

    (N_0,+), d_r(n)=r^n

and

    (R_{≥0},+), d_lambda(a)=exp(lambda a)

have continuous positive-character families. Associativity and positivity
alone do not select a unique law in either the countable or continuous
branch.

### 7. Conditional channel selection is not whole-complex selection

Even a unique output law conditional on fusion leaves the fusion-occurrence
propensity free. It also leaves size/root laws, primitive-versus-staged odds,
disconnected sectors, stable trace activities, and cosmological boundary
state undetermined unless additional equations fix them.
