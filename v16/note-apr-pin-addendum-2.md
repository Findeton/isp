## 1. Reason for the addendum

An independent hostile theorem pass found that a faithful regional response
pairing can still fail to be a process. In particular,

    D(A,B) = mu(A meet B)

is a normalized, symmetric, disjoint-biadditive, strongly positive classical
Gram kernel for a strictly positive valuation mu. Its all-probe profile can
separate an atomless Boolean algebra. But A and B are region propositions, not
successive typed fillings. Biadditivity is not cut composition.

APR may use this kernel as an identity-boundary calibration or a static
positive control. It may not call it a horizontal regional process, dynamic
locality, causal structure, interference, or gravity.

## 2. Three quotient types

The construction and receipt must keep three quotients separate:

1. linear_null — a vector/history quotient V/kernel(Phi);
2. contextual_process — equality under every licensed future word, precontext,
   spectator, and calibrated effect;
3. regional_congruence — a Boolean/distributive congruence under every regional
   operation and gluing context.

The first does not imply the second. The second is a right congruence only
unless precontexts and monoidal spectators are included. Neither automatically
implies the third.

The scorer must expose all three coordinates. A positive physical-region rung
requires contextual_process=CLOSED and regional_congruence=CONGRUENCE.

## 3. Minimum typed process object

No joint-process rung is reachable without one object containing all of:

1. boundary objects Sigma with explicit boundary algebras, operator systems,
   or other typed predictive state spaces;
2. vertical passive maps J between presentations of the same boundary fact;
3. horizontal fillings M:Sigma_0 -> Sigma_1;
4. one law assignment M |-> T_M;
5. identity and exact composition

       T_(N o M) = T_N T_M;

6. disjoint composition

       T_(M tensor N) = T_M tensor T_N;

7. vertical naturality

       J_out T_M = T_M' J_in;

8. at least two composable nonidentity fillings and two registered
   factorizations of one composite;
9. generated future effects or instruments at the output, not copied expected
   numbers;
10. a declared continuation grammar whose closure gives a fixed-point stable
    null;
11. independently constructed exterior-replacement maps;
12. writer, delayed reader, eraser, spectator, overlap, and incompatible-
    boundary controls inside the same typed object.

A strongly positive D_M may be derived from T_M and the boundary pairing. It
cannot replace these maps by name.

If the candidate claims a regional quantum process, the same object must also
contain a nonzero interference witness and an all-input complete stable record
division. A classical regional existence process is allowed, but must be
graded CLASSICAL-REGIONAL and cannot select the microscopic quantum law type.

## 5. Regional-support locality versus dynamical locality

The parent pin's use of “causal exterior” is circular while causal order is an
APR output. APR therefore separates:

### 5.1 Regional-support locality

Use the Boolean/contact complement as a kinematic exterior. Construct Kin(A)
from operations supported inside A. Construct SuppDyn(A) independently as the
subobject invariant under every generated replacement supported in the
disjoint complement of A.

This may establish regional support without selecting causal direction.

### 5.2 Causal dynamical locality

Only after an operational causal relation is DERIVED or explicitly PRICED may
the exterior be restricted by causal reachability and a causal Dyn(A) be
formed. The resulting equality is a later coordinate.

New locality coordinate:

    locality =
      FAIL |
      VACUOUS-CONSTANT |
      REGIONAL-SUPPORT |
      CAUSAL-DYNAMIC

Kin and Dyn must be constructed by independent algorithms. An assignment
Dyn:=Kin is a hard-coded failure. Equality means equality or a canonical
natural identification inside one calibrated ambient law, not merely equal
dimension or an arbitrary algebra isomorphism.

The exact false-positive control uses two isomorphic but differently
calibrated subalgebras, such as span{I,Z} and span{I,X}. Abstract isomorphism
does not identify their physical records.

APR-T6 is retyped as a criterion conditional on P0, complete operational
scope, contextual congruence, dynamic/support locality, faithfulness,
nonconstancy, and physical-image atomlessness. Mathematics alone does not
promote the object to ontology.

## 6. Probe closure and the copy-oracle trap

The all-B overlap profile

    Phi(A)(B)=mu(A meet B)

is faithful only because every B is admitted as a calibrated probe. A finite
receipt cannot establish that completeness.

Mandatory control: with probes {zero, one, [0], [1]}, the regions [00] and
[01] have identical response profiles. Appending [00] separates them.

The all-B result is theorem-grade only if one predeclared uniform filling/effect
grammar generates every required probe independently of the pair being
compared. Otherwise the receipt must say COMPLETE-PROBES-POSTULATED.

A law returning the canonical serialization, label hash, prefix depth, or
target screen is a copy oracle. It can be injective and atomless while doing no
physics. Presentation/refinement invariance, one-formula held-out generation,
and relation-only changed-object tests must kill it.

New coordinate:

    probes =
      INCOMPLETE |
      COMPLETE-POSTULATED |
      COMPLETE-GENERATED

## 7. Contact is not overlap

The minimal relation

    C_min(A,B) iff A meet B is nonzero

and the maximal nonzero relation

    C_max(A,B) iff A and B are both nonzero

both satisfy the parent pin's elementary contact axioms. They differ on
disjoint [0] and [1], while the static overlap kernel is blind to the
difference.

New contact coordinate:

    contact = DERIVED | OVERLAP-ONLY | PRICED | NULL

Non-overlap contact is DERIVED only if the support of permitted joint fillings
or another generated calibrated effect distinguishes it. A declaration does
not suffice.

## 8. Correct atomic quotient control

The map sending every nonzero region to one is not a Boolean homomorphism:
disjoint nonzero regions would both map to one while their meet maps to zero.

The mandatory atomic control is an ultrafilter character chi on the prefix
algebra, for example the recursively defined character that follows one child
at each split. The quotient by its maximal ideal is the two-element Boolean
algebra. The control may use a point-like character adversarially; no positive
ontology theorem may depend on choosing one.

For an ideal I, the exact post-quotient atomlessness criterion is:

> for every A not in I, there is B≤A with B not in I and A\B not in I.

A positive symbolic proof or splitter is required. A finite leaf census is
syntax-only.

## 9. Law-family and valuation price

The dyadic valuation is not selected. For rational 0<p<1, Bernoulli valuations

    mu_p(c_w)=p^(number of 0s in w) (1-p)^(number of 1s in w)

are strictly positive, normalized, and refinement-compatible, and generally
move predictions.

Any overlap or process law built from mu must report this as physical law data,
not emergent volume. The law-selection coordinate remains UNSELECTED unless an
independent registered principle cuts the family.

## 10. Stable comparison and coherent context

Standalone probabilities do not form a complete comparison profile. Over the
complex representation, J=1 and J'=-1 have identical isolated Born weights,
but coherent recombination with a common reference gives squared norms 4 and
0.

Comparison DERIVED or NULL-QUOTIENT therefore requires every lawful coherent
reference context, all prior fed subspaces, all-input precontexts, and
spectator/ancilla extensions at the declared scope. If the RHL 1-versus-1/2
screen is not realized by a lawful generated continuation and stable record,
the result is only MATHEMATICAL-CROSS-TERM-DIFFERS, not an operational PRICED
verdict.

## 12. Ontology consequence

An algebra that can answer every question about itself is not automatically a
world. To earn physical regional content, its distinctions must be generated,
transported, composed, perturbed, and recorded by one law, and the same
distinctions must survive the contextual quotient without becoming atomic or
presentation-dependent.

APR therefore distinguishes three achievements:

1. an atomless syntax;
2. a faithful atomless response representation;
3. an atomless physical regional process.

Only the third reaches the dynamic-referent rungs. None by itself is geometry
or gravity.
