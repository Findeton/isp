## 2. Seat O findings

### O1 — When a cross-input descriptor is physical

A cross-input channel descriptor is physical here only when all of the
following are true:

1. it is declared in the observable contract before a candidate measure is
   tested;
2. it is measurable on the total bundle, not merely separately nameable in
   every fiber;
3. it is invariant under accepted presentation changes, or is transported
   together with the physical experiment under an accepted bundle
   isomorphism;
4. it is grounded in retained channel-level incidence, trace, independently
   physical topology, or another exact operational property; and
5. some admissible experiment can read the property without consulting a
   representative name, serialization slot, basis convention, or realized
   child history.

An arbitrary section or sheet numbering fails these tests. A retained
incidence parity, a transported boundary charge, or another exact
channel-level detector outcome can pass them. The difference is not whether
a mathematician can write a label; it is whether the same declared physical
operation and invariant property are being tracked across inputs.

### O2 — The pre-measure freeze blocks sigma-algebra tuning

The freeze of
\(\mathfrak R=(\mathcal C,\Xi,p,\tau,\mathcal O)\) is coherent and
noncircular. It does not derive a privileged \(\Xi\) or \(\mathcal O\);
instead, it fixes which referent is under test before
\(\boldsymbol\mu\), \(d\), or either normalized law is evaluated.

The two prohibited moves are correctly symmetric:

- deleting a predeclared physical event after its probability map is found
  nonmeasurable cannot rescue the law; and
- adjoining an external cross-fiber label after a kernel passes cannot
  retroactively defeat that law.

Either move defines a new referent proposal and requires a fresh physical
justification and law evaluation. Thus the amendment prevents selection by
sigma-algebra tuning while making no claim that the observable contract
itself has been uniquely selected.

### O3 — Local channel identity is not a universal species

The trace algebra \(\Xi_x\) can distinguish two alternatives at one fixed
input even when no single total descriptor gives them a common name over an
input region. Because every fiber event is the trace of a total event, local
probabilities remain typed. What does not follow is a transported species.

The no-forced-trivialization rule is essential. A nontrivial bundle may
have local sheet identities and pointwise branching while every convenient
global numbering is presentation dependent. Even when an auxiliary
measurable numbering happens to exist, it is not thereby an element of
\(\mathcal O\). Conversely, a genuinely physical transition-compatible
descriptor may establish a global species. The composite makes neither
promotion automatically.

### O4 — Operational meanings of the three branching predicates

The three predicates have different laboratory content.

- **Pointwise resolved branching at \(x\):** prepare the same complete input
  \(x\) repeatedly and use an admitted channel-level readout local to that
  input. Two disjoint resolved events occur with positive conditional
  probability. No calibration between different inputs is asserted.
- **Descriptor-coherent resolved branching on \(B\):** use one predeclared
  physical descriptor and its transported calibration throughout \(B\).
  Two fixed descriptor events have positive probability at every input in
  the region. This is a same-property-across-inputs claim.
- **Target-sector branching at \(x\):** ignore resolved channel detail and
  read only the target sector. Two disjoint target events have positive
  pushforward probability.

Set-level plurality precedes all three probabilistic predicates: it says
that at least two independently physical resolved classes exist, but makes
no frequency or propensity claim. Pointwise branching can occur without a
coherent species, and resolved branching can occur with a deterministic
target.

### O5 — None of the distinctions supplies a physical measure

Physical separability, a sigma algebra, local distinguishability,
descriptor coherence, topology, and target multiplicity provide event
semantics, not weights. The kernel still needs one of the declared
provenances and must satisfy the total-event measurability axiom. Infinitely
many measurable kernels can live on the same frozen observable contract.

The positive-character construction also begins only after a physical
resolved kernel and its target convolution exist. Taking an already
normalized law with \(d=1\) remains a representation of selected
probabilities, not their explanation.

### O6 — Same-target, incidence, trace, and internal quantum alternatives

The composite correctly admits two inequivalent resolved channels with one
common target. Retained generator incidence, a retained process trace, or
independently physical channel topology can distinguish them even when
\(\tau\) erases that information. Their target pushforward may therefore be
deterministic while their resolved law branches.

The distinctions are also correctly bounded:

- a realized bond value in a fixed Paper 13D generator belongs to its indexed
  history fiber, not to a second resolved channel;
- a deliberately staged physical trace is not erased merely because its
  endpoint agrees with a simultaneous construction;
- a reader label or arbitrary basis does not manufacture a channel; and
- coherent quantum alternatives are not automatically positive classical
  channels. A channel-level quantum distinction needs an independently
  physical invariant and its relevant recombination or readout experiment.
  Merely choosing a basis cannot place a descriptor in \(\mathcal O\).

This preserves both physical multiplicity and interference-sensitive
ontology without confusing either with target-sector multiplicity.

### O7 — Continuous and non-standard channels

Branching is stated using positive measurable events, not positive point
masses. A nonatomic fiber can branch through two positive sets while every
singleton has probability zero. The continuous same-target control is
therefore admitted for the right reason.

No standard-Borel, countable-generation, or regular-disintegration
assumption is hidden in the definition. On a declared non-standard branch,
a resolved kernel is admissible when its total event maps and target map are
constructed directly. A target kernel cannot be turned into a resolved
kernel by presuming a regular conditional probability. Failure of a
disintegration theorem blocks that derivation; it does not invalidate a
directly supplied kernel.

### O8 — Paper 13D structural/history fork

For a fixed simultaneous-fusion component family, Paper 13D fixes the
n-ary generator class and the unmarked target class before fresh cross-pair
bonds are sampled. Those bond values and invariant functions of them are
history outcomes of that one channel. Retaining them gives Gamma
pushforwards; forgetting them leaves the same resolved generator.

Primitive, staged, fused, and disconnected whole process complexes can be
inequivalent structural possibilities, but Paper 13D supplies neither the
frozen total observable contract over those alternatives nor an autonomous
resolved-channel kernel. They therefore witness the selector residue, not
constructed measure-supported branching. Version 5 neither suppresses a
future physical cross-input descriptor nor invents one for the current law.
The coordinate
P18-CURRENT-GAMMA-STRUCTURAL-BRANCHING-FORK-PROVED is warranted, while
P18-NONTRIVIAL-STRUCTURAL-BRANCHING-UNCONSTRUCTED remains mandatory.

### O9 — Law, cosmological state, and one actual complex

The ontology branches remain genuinely separate:

- enlarging universal nomology with a kernel is not the same as choosing
  constants within a law family;
- neither is the same as a contingent cosmological or preparation state;
- empirical calibration is not a derivation from structural mathematics;
  and
- one actual complex without a fundamental ensemble is not a delta
  probability distribution secretly added to the law.

The observable freeze only identifies what proposed probabilities would be
about. It cannot decide which of these ontological roles a surviving
selector datum occupies, and it cannot decide whether composition occurs.
The conditional resolved and target laws remain distinct from the whole
occurrence propensity \(q\).

### O10 — Stable record, complete division, normalization, actuality

A stable record can discard variables needed for future conditional
sufficiency, while a complete division can in principle be erasable.
Persistence and completeness are therefore independent predicates.
Decoherence or stable recording may make a history robust, but it does not
select the initial structural channel or whole complex.

Likewise, normalization says that the conditional propensities sum or
integrate to one. It does not cause an event, choose one history, select one
whole complex, or turn a conditional law at a null occurrence event into an
identified physical law. The coordinates for whole selection and
actualization correctly remain unconstructed.

### O11 — No forbidden physical prior selects the referent or kernel

An energy or entropy proposal would require independently declared energy
observables, a reference measure, constraints, parameters, boundary data,

normalization cannot supply those inputs from a channel catalogue. Geometry
and gravity remain downstream tests rather than selector likelihoods.

### O15 — Fresh semantic countermodels

The following countermodels were constructed for this review. They are
semantic tests, not implementations or proposed repairs.

#### CM-O5-1 — Connected double cover with sheet monodromy

Let the input region be \(B=S^1\), let the total channel space also be
\(S^1\), and let \(p(z)=z^2\). Every fiber has two points. Give each fiber
the symmetric measure and send both points to the same target. For Borel
total events, the half-count of the fiber intersection is a measurable
kernel.

Each input therefore has pointwise resolved branching. Local charts can
name the two sheets, but transport around the circle exchanges them. The
deck transformation also exchanges them. Any sheet name that changes under
that accepted presentation symmetry is not a physical descriptor, even if
an auxiliary Borel enumeration can be chosen. Thus there is no
descriptor-coherent sheet species and no target branching. A rule demanding
a global physical sheet number would incorrectly trivialize the bundle.
Version 5 gives exactly the required verdict.

#### CM-O5-2 — Frozen incidence parity with a non-Borel law

Over a Borel input space, suppose two same-target channels have an exact
retained generator-incidence parity, even or odd. Freeze that parity as
\(\ell\in\mathcal O\). Let the proposed even-channel probability be
\(1/3\) on a non-Borel set \(V\) and \(2/3\) off \(V\), with the odd
probability complementary.

The event \(\ell=\mathrm{even}\) is physically predeclared, and its
probability map is nonmeasurable. The kernel must be rejected. Erasing
parity from \(\Xi\) after seeing this failure would change the referent,
not repair the law. This remains true although the target pushforward is a
perfectly measurable point mass.

#### CM-O5-3 — Local analyzer choices with no global analyzer species

Take two local alternatives at every input and use a coarse total sigma
algebra consisting of full-fiber cylinders together with countable
modifications. Every two-point fiber has the full discrete trace algebra,
so a locally installed analyzer can distinguish the alternatives at a
fixed input. Outside a countable set, every admitted total event is either
the full fiber or the empty fiber.

Attach the displayed weights to a non-Borel external numbering. The weight
maps of all admitted events remain measurable, while the uniform
number-zero event is absent. This is a legitimate pointwise-branching
kernel for a referent with only local analyzer identities. Adding the
external numbering afterward would propose a different experiment and is
forbidden. Equal discrete fiber algebras alone do not create a global
species.

#### CM-O5-4 — Same fiber algebras, inequivalent physical contracts

Construct two two-channel bundles with identical full discrete
\(\Xi_x\) in every fiber. In contract A, a retained oriented boundary flux
defines a presentation-invariant sign descriptor that is transported across
inputs. In contract B, the two alternatives are exchanged by an accepted
presentation symmetry and no orientation apparatus is part of the
experiment.

The fibers look identical one at a time, but A has a physical coherent
sign experiment and B does not. There is no accepted bundle isomorphism
transporting all descriptors and experiments from A to B. Treating equality
of the trace algebras as physical equivalence would yield a false global
branch identity. Version 5 correctly treats these as different referent
proposals.

#### CM-O5-5 — Descriptor coherence with an infinite kernel family

Let every input \(x\in[0,1]\) admit two same-target channels distinguished
by a retained boundary charge \(\ell=\pm1\). Freeze the charge descriptor.
For every Borel function \(q:[0,1]\to(0,1)\), assign probabilities
\(q(x)\) and \(1-q(x)\).

Every such law has descriptor-coherent branching on the whole interval, and
every one has the same deterministic target law. Distinct choices of \(q\)
give distinct resolved kernels. The physical coherent descriptor therefore
supplies branch semantics but no odds. Neither coherence nor target
normalization selects a member of the family.

#### CM-O5-6 — Pointwise, coherent, and target branching separate

At each input let the resolved alternatives be
\((s,t)\in\{+,-\}\times\{0,1\}\). Let \(s\) be an independently physical
transported incidence sign and let \(t\) be only a local sheet identity.
Give all four alternatives positive weight and initially send them to one
common target.

The model has pointwise branching, descriptor-coherent branching in \(s\),
and no target branching. Removing the transported \(s\) descriptor leaves
pointwise branching but no coherent branching. Changing only the target map
so that \(+\) and \(-\) reach disjoint sectors adds target branching without
changing the resolved fiber measure. These three interventions demonstrate
that none of the predicates can be substituted for another.

#### CM-O5-7 — Nonatomic phase fibers and quantum basis gauge

Let each fiber be a circle of locally measurable phase differences with
normalized Haar measure, all with one target. Local semicircles are disjoint
positive events and all points have zero mass, so pointwise branching is
nonatomic. Suppose the local interferometer reference rotates around the
input base. Without a separately retained phase standard there is no
physical global zero-angle descriptor, even though every laboratory can
choose coordinates.

If instead the alternatives are coherent arms of a quantum experiment,
assigning positive path odds before a which-path record or a declared
recombination observable is a different error: basis vectors and amplitude
decompositions are not automatically resolved classical channels. The
frozen-contract rule handles both cases. It admits a physical transported
reference when declared and rejects a coordinate or basis masquerading as
one.

#### CM-O5-8 — Countable-cocountable non-standard fiber

Let an uncountable resolved fiber \(U\) carry the countable-cocountable
sigma algebra and the probability measure
\[
 \nu(A)=
 \begin{cases}
 0,&A\text{ countable},\\
 1,&A\text{ cocountable}.
 \end{cases}
\]
Use a directly declared constant kernel and a constant target. Every point
has zero mass. Moreover, two disjoint positive events cannot exist, because
two cocountable subsets of \(U\) cannot be disjoint.

Thus the model has abundant set-level classes but no measure-supported
branching on this observable contract. It is not to be rejected merely
because standard regular-disintegration machinery is unavailable. It also
shows that nonatomicity and plurality do not imply branching under an
arbitrary non-standard event algebra.

#### CM-O5-9 — Same conditional law, different cosmological states

Take two whole laws with the same frozen resolved contract, the same
resolved conditional kernel, the same target pushforward, and the same
Paper 13D child law on every supplied parent. Let one whole law concentrate
on parent complex \(A\), while the other assigns positive occurrence
propensity to both \(A\) and \(B\).

All conditional channel experiments agree, yet the cosmological-state and
whole-occurrence predictions differ. Neither conditional normalization nor
the observable freeze selects between them. Declaring that exactly one
complex is actual is a further ontological branch, not the assertion of a
delta ensemble.

#### CM-O5-10 — Durable checksum without complete division

Let a history carry a durable checksum \(r=h\bmod 2\) while the hidden value
\(h\) affects a later response. The record \(r\) persists indefinitely but
is not sufficient for the future, so it is not a complete division. In a
second process, let a full sufficient boundary state be erasable after its
use; it can be a complete division without being a permanent record.

A normalized law over either process does not select which history becomes
actual. This countermodel independently protects both the
record/division wall and the normalization/actuality wall.

#### CM-O5-11 — Energy and entropy do not select the kernel

On the same finite frozen channel contract, assign declared channel energies
\(E_\kappa\). The family
\[
 P_\beta(\kappa)\propto b(\kappa)e^{-\beta E_\kappa}
\]
changes with the inverse temperature \(\beta\) and with the reference weight
\(b\). A maximum-entropy answer also changes with the chosen event algebra,
base measure, and constraints. The channel catalogue and target map choose
none of these inputs.

Consequently energy weighting or entropy maximization is a new physical law
proposal with explicit provenance, not a consequence of measurable channel
structure. Filtering the family by a desired dimension or gravity
phenomenology would add the forbidden selector prior.

## 4. Firewall conclusions

The observable contract determines what a resolved-channel probability
statement means. It does not establish that the described channel objects
populate Paper 13D, that a physical measure exists, or that the measure is
unique. The target pushforward forgets resolved distinctions and cannot
recover them. A character normalizes a prior measure and cannot create its
provenance. Target finite-word associativity cannot be promoted to resolved
path coherence. A conditional composition law cannot determine composition
occurrence. None of those structures actualizes one history or one complex.

The version-5 correction is therefore conservative in the physically
important sense: it rules out opportunistic changes of observable semantics
without filling any unconstructed dynamical or ontological coordinate.
