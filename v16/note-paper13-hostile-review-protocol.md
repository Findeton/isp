## 15. Adjudication and stopping rule

Adjudication begins only after all three reports are committed and hashed.

majority rule.  A single independently reproducible type error,
counterexample, or provenance failure defeats the associated universal claim.
Positive scope is the intersection of independently supported evidence.

For every disagreement, the adjudicator reconstructs the smallest decisive
object.  The adjudication preserves all reports byte-for-byte and ends with:

- every orthogonal coordinate;
- accepted Results A through G with exact scopes;
- killed or narrowed promotions;
- paper grade and any permitted editorial changes;
- an ontology/representation ledger;
