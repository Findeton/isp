## 1. Canonical statement

> ISP's candidate ontology is one actual, law-sufficient relational
> configuration; its missing fundamental dynamics is an indivisible
> stochastic law between admissible division configurations; Hilbert/history
> machinery is representational, and APR's `AB/BC` table is only a
> simultaneous gluing diagnostic--not that law.

This is the intended interpretation of Papers 11--12. It is not a claim that
the configuration catalogue, division doctrine, physical regional quotient,
or fundamental law has been constructed.

## 2. Candidate ontology

The proposed physical state is one actually realized relational configuration,
or one compatible partial web of durable relational facts. Alternative
fillings are arguments of the law, not co-actual worlds. A wavefunction,
density matrix, amplitude family, history kernel, cospan, or Hamiltonian is not
an additional ontic substance merely because it represents the law.

`Complete` means sufficient for the sought fundamental dynamics. APR has not
shown that its raw prefix syntax, finite profile, or any quotient is complete
in this sense. It has not selected a maximally fine configuration catalogue.
Point-freedom, relationality, changing geometry, and local backreaction remain
ISP extension postulates/candidates rather than consequences inherited from
another framework.

## 3. Sought law type

The missing object is a stochastic transition law on complete configurations:
conditional chances for possible target configurations given a configuration
at an admissible division. It may be indivisible. Inserting an arbitrary
intermediate cut need not yield a positive transition kernel whose product
recovers the whole transition.

Therefore ordinary probability is not the problem. Arbitrary probabilistic
division is the problem. One run has one actual configuration/history, while
the law still assigns counterfactual chances over its admissible source and
target configurations.

Barandes's division events are admissible conditioning times. They are not,
by definition, ISP durable records. The ISP bridge

```text
stable durable record partition -> licensed ordinary conditionalization
```

is a stricter program-specific proposal. `Division record` may be written only
after division status and record stability are independently earned.

## 4. Representations

Barandes directly supports a Hilbert-space dictionary for an indivisible
stochastic law and treats familiar Hilbert objects as representation-dependent.
A differentiable unitary representative can supply a Hamiltonian, but that
does not promote the Hamiltonian into ontology.

A strongly positive decoherence functional separately admits a Gram/null-
quotient Hilbert representation. That is a mathematical representation
theorem. Neither Barandes nor Paper 11 proves that a decoherence functional is
the unique or faithful representation of ISP's still-missing relational
transition law. It remains a safe history-level candidate envelope.

## 5. What the APR `AB/BC` construction is

The APR construction supplies two simultaneous regional/configuration
marginals,

```text
P(A,B), P(B,C),
```

and exhibits two compatible complete joint extensions with identical local
data but different `P(A=C)`: `1/2` versus `1`.

This proves only that overlapping regional shadows do not select a complete
global filling. The construction has no before/after transition, division
event, actualization, source-to-target propagation, or quantum interference
claim. Its classical coupling/copula language is diagnostic only.

If `A,B,C` were unrecorded alternatives that could reconverge, treating them
as exclusive intermediate classical outcomes would be an illicit division.
The missing object would instead be the indivisible full transition law and a
lawful representation carrying their interference relations.

V4's synthetic Markov selector, classical process, quantum process, contact,
writer, rewrite, and classifier packages test generic scorer reachability.
They are not APR data and may not appear in APR's scientific receipt as its
microscopic dynamics.

## 6. Source-backed claims and limits

### 6.1 Source-backed

- Barandes separates configuration ontology, fixed transition chances, and
  contingent standalone probability information; restricts conditioning
  times to division events; and permits failure of positive intermediate
  factorization for an indivisible stochastic law.
- Barandes constructs a Hilbert representation/dictionary of the stochastic
  law and emphasizes representation/gauge dependence of familiar Hilbert
  objects, including the Hamiltonian.
- EPR motivates a completeness question under its stated criterion but does
  not determine the missing ontology or dynamics.
- Feynman's path-amplitude formulation shows that alternative amplitude
  contributions combine before probabilities in its quantum representation;
  it does not make every path ontically actual.
- Schrodinger's separated-systems analysis distinguishes joint quantum
  representatives and their entanglement from independently specified local
  representatives; it does not select ISP ontology.
- Strong positivity implies the Gram/null-quotient Hilbert construction; this
  proves representability, not ontology or unique microscopic law type.

### 6.2 ISP postulates or open constructions

- configurations are point-free relational webs rather than a fixed
  nonrelational configuration catalogue;
- one transition changes relational geometry and process state together;
- durable relational facts exhaust what is actual;
- stable records coincide with every admissible stochastic division;
- a decoherence functional faithfully/completely represents the missing
  indivisible relational law;
- actualization, law selection, changing-factorization locality, continuum,
  metric, curvature, GR/QFT, and empirical predictions.
