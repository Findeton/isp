## 1. One-box question

> Which parts of a lawful record-feedback dynamics follow from which explicit
> principles, which alternatives remain operationally distinct, and at what
> level—microscopic history, record implementation, representation, or genuine
> division—does an ordinary stochastic law become sufficient?

RFB does not ask whether the conjugate-record toy is “the law.” It constructs
an assumption-by-assumption forcing boundary over a registered family. Every
selection claim must show both directions: imposing the named assumption
removes a freedom, and dropping that assumption restores it while the remaining
surface still passes.

## 2. Binding ontology — what the nouns mean

The unit begins from a Barandes-compatible candidate ontology, without declaring
that the candidate wins:

```text
kinematic possibility space     all allowed finite named configurations
actual candidate beables        one current relational configuration and its durable facts
nomological candidate           an indivisible, possibly non-Markovian law on histories
quantum representation          Hilbert/decoherence-functional/process machinery
division-level interface        ordinary probabilities and a CP instrument at a stable record
actualization                    one record outcome occurs; postulated, not derived here
```

More explicitly, define one fixed meta-configuration catalogue

```text
C = disjoint_union over finite named G of {G} x R_G x X_G.
```

`G` is the current relational graph, `R_G` is candidate record content, and
`X_G` is whatever internal/predictive configuration the law requires. Growth
is motion between sectors of this one fixed possibility space; it is not a
background spacetime and does not require redefining the ontology when a node
is born. Names are fiducial coordinates needed to align alternatives. Physical
claims must be covariant under admissible renamings.

The representation may use `l2(C)`, a direct sum over graph sectors, a strongly
positive decoherence functional, or a higher-order process object. None is
promoted to a beable merely because it is used in a calculation. In particular:

- a wavefunction, density operator, Hamiltonian, Kraus family, or decoherence
  functional is representational unless a separate operational result makes
  an ontic promotion necessary;
- a graph is not yet physical geometry merely because it is relational;
  geometry must do calibrated work as distance, causal support, transport,
  volume/growth, or curvature and survive family-level eliminability tests;
- a value is a record only if it remains recoverable under every licensed
  continuation at the registered scope;
- assigning a positive probability to every elementary rewrite is not assumed.
  Unrecorded alternatives must retain whatever relational information permits
  later interference;
- a normalized fine-state kernel can be a useful division interface without
  being the microscopic factorization of the law.

The safe hierarchy inherited from terminal QSF is therefore:

```text
one actual relational/configuration history       ontological candidate
indivisible multi-time process law                 nomological candidate
decoherence functional / Hilbert construction      quantum representation
ordinary stochastic kernel / CP instrument         division-level shadow
```

RFB may falsify or refine this candidate hierarchy. It may not silently treat
it as a result.

## 3. The three axes that must not be conflated

The registered family is a product of three logically distinct axes.

### 3.1 Process factorization

1. `DIVISIBLE-REWRITE-KERNEL`: a physical positive kernel at each elementary
   rewrite.
2. `ENRICHED-STATE-KERNEL`: a stochastic law on a state that retains coherent
   information; intermediate rewrite labels are not themselves outcomes.
3. `INDIVISIBLE-MULTITIME`: only complete multi-time laws between legitimate
   divisions are physical.
4. `HIGHER-ORDER`: ordinary definite-order histories are insufficient.

### 3.2 Record implementation

1. lawful classical outcome-conditioned feedback;
2. coherently retained record registers;
3. hybrid or partially decohered tags;
4. inert write-only controls;
5. decoherence-only feedback (`k=0` in the clock family);
6. active steering feedback (`k!=0`).

### 3.3 Mathematical representation

1. an ordinary kernel or CP instrument at a stable division;
2. a Hilbert/direct-sum or routed-sector construction;
3. a strongly positive history/decoherence functional;
4. a comb or process tensor when interventions and memory are load-bearing;
5. a process-matrix-type representation only if indefinite causal order is
   operationally witnessed.

These are not mutually exclusive competitors. An indivisible stochastic
ontology may have a decoherence-functional/Hilbert representation and ordinary
instruments at its divisions. A coherent record register is an implementation,
not by itself an ontology. The scorer must emit one coordinate per axis.

## 5. Primary-literature guardrails

The following sources provide mathematical precedents, not ISP results:

- Arrighi and Martiel, *Quantum Causal Graph Dynamics*, arXiv:1607.06700:
  a Hilbert basis of finite labelled graphs, generalized tensor/trace notions,
  graph-relative causality, and localizability of vertex-preserving causal
  unitaries. Its structure theorem is explicitly vertex-preserving.
- Arrighi, Durbec, and Emmanuel, *Size-varying reversible causal graph
  dynamics*, arXiv:1805.10330, current v4: reversible local classical size
  change in relaxed equivalent settings.
- Arrighi, Durbec, and Wilson, *Quantum networks theory*, arXiv:2110.10587:
  the essential correction to the external survey. It already permits nodes to
  merge, split, and reconnect coherently and develops generalized partitions
  while retaining relations among unitarity, CP, trace preservation,
  no-signalling causality, locality, and localizability. RFB/Paper 11 may not
  claim that quantum size change itself is absent mathematics.
- Arrighi, Christodoulou, and Durbec, *On quantum superpositions of graphs,
  no-signalling and covariance*, arXiv:2010.13579: node names are needed for
  correct branch alignment; names are coordinate-like, with renaming
  invariance imposed on physics.
- Behr and Sobocinski, *Rule Algebras for Adhesive Categories*,
  arXiv:1807.00785: associative DPO composition along overlaps and the
  concurrency/rule-algebra kinematic precedent.
- Hawkins, Markopoulou, and Sahlmann, *Evolution in Quantum Causal Histories*,
  arXiv:hep-th/0302111: CP maps on a supplied causal skeleton, not a skeleton
  generated by the same law.
- Gudder, *Hilbert Space Representations of Decoherence Functionals and Quantum
  Measures*, arXiv:1011.1694: strong positivity and finite spanning Hilbert
  representation.
- Dowker, Johnston, and Surya, *On extending the Quantum Measure*,
  arXiv:1007.2725: finite decoherence functionals, including a quantum causal-set
  growth example, need not extend to the full event sigma algebra.
- Barandes, *Quantum Systems as Indivisible Stochastic Processes*,
  arXiv:2507.21192: indivisible non-Markovian stochastic laws can be primary and
  Hilbert structures secondary. It does not supply ISP's graph-generated,
  size-changing, record-stable dynamics.

The literature therefore removes any claim that a new foundational calculus
is already necessary. The open target is a new synthesis and new compatibility
theorems: a graph-generated, renaming-covariant, size-changing history law with
stable divisions, overlap composition, and dynamically typed subsystems.

## 6. The forcing matrix

RFB must construct an exact matrix with assumptions as rows and physical
freedoms as columns. Every nontrivial cell carries an impose test and a drop
control.

### 6.1 Assumption rows

1. process normalization and coarse-graining additivity;
2. all-input CP/trace completeness, only at a claimed operational division;
3. pointer readability;
4. full cyclic accumulation without unused pointer states;
5. renaming covariance;
6. idle-spectator naturality;
7. type universality across genuinely identified event types;
8. translation composition `U(n+m)=U(n)U(m)`, separate from universality;
9. reciprocal feedback / no inert absolute record, with the decoherence-only
   leak retained as a live counterfamily;
10. predictive-state minimality at declared resource parity;
11. disjoint-support locality and operational no-signalling;
12. continuation-stable recoverability of records;
13. refinement/gluing consistency;
14. divisibility at elementary rewrites versus indivisibility between records.

### 6.2 Freedom columns

At minimum: writer permutation and phases, winding angle `theta`, record size
`q`, reader/back-action family, charge labels, writer-reader gauge orbit,
coin/mixing law, classical/coherent/hybrid retention, factorization type,
division placement, record partition, and actualization.

Each cell is reported as exactly one of:

```text
FORCED-BY-<assumption-set>
EXCLUDED-BY-<assumption-set>
QUOTIENTED-AS-REPRESENTATION-BY-<gauge>
PRICED-POSTULATE-<dimension>
```

## 7. Mandatory finite arms

### Arm W — writer classification

For `q=2,3,4`, enumerate every pointer-faithful reversible writer. Test whether
full accumulation forces a single cycle up to renaming and phase gauge. Derive
the residual `V^q = exp(i theta) I` only if the exact census licenses it. A
winding interferometer must compare two histories that first differ by the
cycle phase and reconverge; moving an isolated marginal is not a `theta`
measurement.

### Arm R — reader/back-action classification

Separate the axioms “same event type uses the same rule” and “incremental
influences compose as a representation.” Under each assumption set, census
general readers, cyclic readers, and clock/charge forms. Quotient simultaneous
writer-power/charge relabelings and report only invariant pairings such as the
candidate `a*k mod q` if independently re-derived. Retain `k=0` as the exact
decoherence-only leak. No root of unity, charge quantization, or CRL form is
called unique outside its proven hypotheses.

### Arm M — record-mode family

Construct resource-matched classical-feedback, coherent-dilation, and hybrid
partial-tag laws with the same calibrated division interface. Parameterize the
hybrid by its operational overlap, not prose. Determine whether completeness,
locality, recoverability, and the registered overlap spectrum select endpoints
or leave a positive-dimensional semialgebraic family. Stinespring equivalence
is representational until an accessible port or future continuation separates
the implementations.

### Arm H — microscopic law type and interference

Build a minimal two-history reconvergence fixture with no intermediate durable
record and a later stable record partition. Compare:

1. a positive kernel on exclusive rewrite alternatives;
2. a stochastic kernel on an enriched state retaining relative phase;
3. an indivisible multi-time law;
4. a strongly positive decoherence functional representing the same boundary
   data; and
5. a higher-order process only if definite-order histories fail an operational
   witness.

The assay must test both interference and composition under a second cut. It
must not claim that every stochastic ontology loses interference: the target
of the classicalization test is a kernel that makes the unrecorded rewrite
alternatives themselves exclusive.

### Arm P — predictive minimality

Execute intervention-complete JS-S1a at registered finite horizons. Compare
the minimal predictive state required by classical decision-tree feedback,
coherent registers, hybrid modes, and indivisible-history representations at
equal resources and on held-out extensions. A growing classical history label
does not lose merely because it is called memory; it loses only under a frozen
resource bound that a competing family also obeys.

### Arm N — locality and no-signalling

Prove the fixed-factor disjoint-support theorem for every surviving family and
run HJW/steering controls where typed. RFB may report only fixed-factor scope.
Changing graph factorizations and the question “who is Bob after birth, merge,
or split?” are Paper 11 debts unless RFB constructs the needed named-graph
subsystem algebra explicitly.

## 10. What Paper 11 inherits—and what the literature already solved

Paper 11 must not invent graph-superposition kinematics or quantum size change
from scratch. Its strongest starting package is:

1. a fixed Hilbert/meta-space of finite **named** graphs and internal labels;
2. renaming-invariant physical operators;
3. the coherent merge/split/reconnect and logical-partition machinery of
   quantum networks theory;
4. graph-generated local events, rather than arbitrary matrices decorated by
   graph labels;
5. an indivisible or decoherence-functional history layer across unrecorded
   rewrites;
6. stable recoverable records that define divisions;
7. overlap/concurrency and disjoint-support no-signalling tests.

The genuinely open theorem package is correspondingly narrower:

1. a **graph-generated history-law theorem** joining event selection,
   transport, and weights in one renaming-covariant local rule;
2. a quantum rewrite-concurrency theorem for overlapping, size-changing,
   record-carrying histories;
3. a history-to-all-input-instrument theorem at stable divisions;
4. dynamic-factorization no-signalling after birth, merge, and split;
5. continuation-stable record and compatible-refinement theorems;
6. operational-null congruence under every rewrite, gluing, and continuation;
7. infinite-history extension; and
8. manifold/QFT/GR phase and Hamiltonian-reconstruction theorems.

This is a new synthesis and theorem program, not evidence for a new kind of
number, probability, or foundational calculus.
