## 4. The decisive comparison-map counterexample

Even granting a primitive regional category, the claimed refinement
independence is conditional on prediction-carrying comparison maps.

Use one directed span `a -> c <- b` with

```text
V_a = V_b = C^2,
V_c = C^3,
D_a = D_b = I_2,
D_c = I_3,
unit = e_0,
marked proposition p = e_1.
```

In comparison system A, both source copies of `p` map to `e_1` in `V_c`. In
comparison system B, the `a` copy maps to `e_1` and the `b` copy maps to
`e_2`. Every map in both systems is a unit-preserving isometry. Every local
positivity and pullback identity passes. Nevertheless,

```text
D_c(J_ca p, J_cb p) = 1  in system A,
D_c(J_ca p, J_cb p) = 0  in system B,
```

and the coherent-sum squared norm moves from `4` to `2`. The intersection
dimension of the two marked presentation images moves from `2` to `1`, so the
difference is not removed by a mere basis change.

The algebraic colimit theorem therefore says:

> Once a compatible comparison doctrine is supplied, the answer is
> independent of the chosen representative inside that doctrine.

It does not say:

> The law selects the doctrine, or every compatible doctrine is gauge.

The comparison maps must consequently be one of three things in a successor:

1. derived from the law's physical continuation data;
2. declared and priced as physical law data; or
3. quotiented only after proving that their residual action lies in a
   continuation-stable operational null ideal.

Paper 11 establishes none of those three. This is the exact remnant of the
cross-carrier identification debt; moving to whole regions does not erase it.

## 6. Stable divisions and the normalized nonselection witness

The quantum reviewer independently constructs the missing normalized control
inside one typed finite law. Let `P_0,P_1` be route projectors, `P_+,P_-` the
output projectors, and `U_eta=diag(1,eta)` for
`eta in {1,i,-1,-i}`. Define

```text
A_(y,r) = P_y P_r U_eta,
K_y     = sum_r A_(y,r) = P_y U_eta.
```

Then

```text
sum_y K_y^dagger K_y = I
```

for every row. On `rho=P_+`, the two-port probabilities are exactly

```text
eta=1:   (1,0)
eta=i:   (1/2,1/2)
eta=-1:  (0,1)
eta=-i:  (1/2,1/2).
```

The same construction supplies its class operations, decoherence functional,
complete division instrument, flag-append isometry, record algebra, and
recovery map. It proves that complete stable-division laws exist and that the
accepted structural surface still does not select their phase law.

This is panel evidence, not a retroactive pass for the frozen character
fixture. A successor may import it only with attribution and a newly frozen
gate.

## 7. Ontology after adjudication

The most defensible Barandes-compatible ontology is still coherent, but it is
now explicitly a candidate postulate rather than a theorem of Paper 11.

1. **What is actual:** one partial compatible web of durable relational record
   facts. “Partial” is essential: there is no commitment to a maximally fine
   actual route through every unrecorded alternative.
2. **What the alternatives are:** complete and partial relational fillings are
   arguments of the law used to compute correlations. They are not additional
   actual worlds merely because they appear in a sum.
3. **What actualization is:** a postulate selecting compatible facts on stable
   commutative record algebras. No refinement-stable global selection rule is
   derived.
4. **What the law is:** still open. An indivisible regional stochastic law is
   a serious fundamental reading; a strongly positive decoherence functional
   is a safe quantum representation; neither has been selected.
5. **What Hilbert spaces, GNS quotients, meshes, routes, matrices, and
   Hamiltonians are:** representations and computational coordinatizations,
   not spacetime atoms or substances.
6. **What an ordinary stochastic kernel is:** an emergent division-level
   interface once a stable record partition exists, not a licensed positive
   probability for every microscopic alternative.

There is no hidden point or tick in this ontology. The block is the opposite:
the proposed point-free relational facts have not yet been given enough
law-generated structure to serve as the theory's physical referents.

strongly continuous unitary one-parameter representation and a selected clock

not generally a Hamiltonian. Neither object selects the fundamental regional
law.
