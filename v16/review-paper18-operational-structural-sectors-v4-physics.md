## 2. Physics of the total event contract

The version-4 total bundle has a precise but limited physical role.

1. The point-free resolved classes in each fiber describe possible channel
   structure. The total sigma algebra records which input-varying channel
   predicates can be events of one resolved law.
2. An admitted channel observable must be represented measurably, but the
   sigma algebra does not create an observable or a physical distinction.
   Labels, reader names, bases, and child histories remain excluded.
3. Set-level inequivalence may establish a possible catalogue. If two
   classes cannot be separated by the declared total event structure, their
   split cannot receive resolved probabilities in that law. This does not
   identify their ontology; it limits the claimed probabilistic object.
4. Kernel measurability is a consistency condition across input pairs. It
   neither chooses weights nor supplies their provenance. Many distinct
   measurable kernels can inhabit the same bundle and share one target
   pushforward.
5. A target law is the operational result after forgetting resolved channel
   data through \(\tau\). It cannot reconstruct a resolved bundle, event
   algebra, kernel, or disintegration.
6. Neither resolved nor target conditional probabilities determine whether
   composition occurs, the cosmological root state, the whole-complex law,
   or actuality.

The amendment therefore repairs a mathematical typing condition without
turning operational measurability into ontology or selection.

## 3. O1–O15 findings

**Pass.** The total event algebra is generated only after the resolved

Admitted channel observables are measurable functions on that bundle;
serialization labels, chosen bases, reader names, and realized child values

removed before the class bundle is formed.

Operational equivalence still does not erase nonisomorphic literal
structure: set-level classes can remain distinct even when the current event
algebra does not separate them. The consequence is only that the purported
split is not an event of the resolved probability law at this level.

### O2 — same-target physical distinctions

**Pass.** Two channels with one target can be physically distinct when one
contains an extra accepted generator occurrence, traversed boundary, or

and a primitive construction may therefore be different resolved objects

The finite same-target control has exactly this permitted interpretation. Its

## 4. Fresh semantic countermodels

### CM4-O1 — a measurable bundle with genuinely varying finite fibers

Let the input parameter be \(x\in[0,1]\). For \(x<1/2\), let the resolved
fiber contain only \(\kappa_{x,0}\). For \(x\geq1/2\), let it contain
\(\kappa_{x,0},\kappa_{x,1},\kappa_{x,2}\), distinguished by zero, one, or
two retained occurrences of a physical structural loop. Form the Borel
subset

\[
 \mathcal C=
 \{(x,0):x\in[0,1]\}\cup
 \{(x,1),(x,2):x\in[1/2,1]\}
\]

of \([0,1]\times\{0,1,2\}\), and give all channels one target. Use
\(\delta_0\) below \(1/2\) and weights \((1/2,1/3,1/6)\) above it. Every
total event probability is Borel in \(x\). This is a valid varying-fiber
kernel whose distinctions are grounded in trace incidence rather than
labels.

### CM4-O2 — measurability leaves continuous probability freedom

On the fixed two-channel bundle
\([0,1]\times\{\kappa_0,\kappa_1\}\), let both channels share one target
and be physically distinguished by retained generator incidence. For any
Borel \(q:[0,1]\to(0,1)\), define

\[
 \mu_x(\kappa_0)=q(x),
 \qquad
 \mu_x(\kappa_1)=1-q(x).
\]

The choices \(q_1(x)=1/4+x/2\) and
\(q_2(x)=3/4-x/2\) are distinct valid kernels on the same bundle with the
same deterministic target pushforward. Thus joint measurability removes
pathological assignments but does not select a physical law.

### CM4-O3 — a continuous triangular bundle with zero singleton masses

Let \(x\in(0,1]\), fiber \(\mathcal C_x=[0,x]\), and total space

\[
 \mathcal C=\{(x,u):0<x\leq1,\ 0\leq u\leq x\}
\]

with its Borel sigma algebra. Let \(u\) be a physically readable retained
channel modulus, give every channel one target, and set

\[
 \mu_x(du)=\frac1x\mathbf1_{[0,x]}(u)\,du.
\]

For every total Borel event the fiber integral is measurable in \(x\).
Singletons have zero probability, while the total events
\(u\leq x/3\) and \(u>x/3\) have probabilities \(1/3\) and \(2/3\).
This is legitimate continuous branching without point weights or an assumed
fixed fiber.

### CM4-O4 — a direct non-standard primitive kernel

Let \(I\) be uncountable and let one resolved fiber be
\(\Omega=\{0,1\}^I\) with the cylinder sigma algebra. Interpret finite
coordinate cylinders as the admitted structural channel observables and
supply the fair product-cylinder measure directly as primitive nomology.
Let the target be constant. Finite cylinders are measurable positive events,
while singleton configurations need not be events of the cylinder algebra.
No regular disintegration or standard-Borel theorem is used. This branch is
mathematically admissible if its primitive provenance is stated; it is not
evidence that the accepted physical channel space is non-standard.

### CM4-O5 — labels and histories cannot be rescued by a sigma algebra

Duplicate one channel presentation with tags 0 and 1 and let an accepted
isomorphism exchange them. Alternatively, let one fixed channel sample a
child bond bit \(H\in\{0,1\}\). Declaring the tag event or the history-bit
event measurable does not turn either into a resolved channel distinction:
the first disappears on isomorphism classes, and the second belongs to the
indexed Gamma history fiber. The total event contract types genuine channel
events only after this ontological distinction is made.

### CM4-O6 — a measurable target law with nonphysical internal paths

Consider a recombination experiment parameterized by a Borel phase
\(\theta\). Its detector probabilities vary measurably with \(\theta\), but
two internal amplitude paths have no retained which-path record and can be
rotated to another basis. The target law is a valid measurable kernel. A
putative resolved positive split over the chosen basis paths would destroy
the interference cross term and is not a physical channel bundle. Adding a
which-path recorder defines a different experiment and may create resolved
events; it cannot retroactively reinterpret the original one.

### CM4-O7 — entropy and energy cannot select the kernel by name

Take two genuinely physical same-target channels in a measurable bundle.
Assigning weights proportional to \(e^{-\beta E_i}\) is arbitrary when
\(E_i\), \(\beta\), and a thermal preparation are absent from the accepted
objects. Likewise, maximizing entropy to obtain weights \((1/2,1/2)\)
presupposes the two-slot base and the constraint set. Both proposals produce
measurable normalized kernels, but neither has physical provenance. The
composite correctly leaves \(\boldsymbol\mu\) unconstructed.

### CM4-O8 — identical conditional bundles, different cosmological states

Let whole complexes be \(\chi_A,\chi_B,\chi_C,\chi_D\). Give two universes
the same total resolved bundle, resolved kernel, target pushforward, and
child Gamma conditionals for every supported internal composition. Choose
root states

\[

 \qquad
 \rho'=(1/10,1/5,3/10,2/5).
\]

The internal channel physics is identical while parent occurrence odds
differ. Without an enlarged transition law preparing the roots, the
difference is contingent cosmological state. A stable record of a root label
does not select its prior, and normalization does not actualize one complex.
