availability of resolved channels.

1. A **resolved channel object** records invariant physical construction

2. **Resolved plurality** is support-level structure. It assigns no odds.
   Measure-supported branching additionally requires an independently

3. The **resolved law** predicts which channel object or measurable family is

   Different resolved laws can have the same target pushforward.

   unconstructed.

   occurs, the root or size law, boundary state, whole-complex occurrence
   law, or actualization.

formal scope. With

\[
