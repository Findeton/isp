## THE THREE LEVELS (never conflated; W-REPRESENTATION binds)
WHAT EXISTS: the actual relational configuration and realized history
— actors, relations (pair-atomic), records, generated causal
geometry.  THE LAW: objective chances for complete future histories,
conditioned only where conditioning is physically meaningful.
REPRESENTATION: transition matrices, amplitudes, psi, rho, unitaries,
Hamiltonians, fields, clocks, cuts.  No unit may promote a
representation to ontology; "the kernel K is reality" is the same
error as "the Hamiltonian is reality."

## THE ARITY SPLIT (E-34-registered)
RECORD-ARITY: two actors per elementary relational fact — the atomic
beable.  PROCESS-ARITY: any finite number of actors in one
indivisible boundary-to-boundary transition.  Pairwise ontology does
not imply pairwise-divisible dynamics; destroying indivisibility by
forced pair-sequencing is the named hazard.  Triples: the smallest
CURVATURE PROBE (order defects of overlapping pair changes), not
automatically the primitive event.

## THE SIX REQUIREMENTS ON DYNAMICS-CLOSURE
1. Define the ontic configuration and complete history space
   INDEPENDENTLY of psi, H, or K.
2. Establish which cuts are GENUINE DIVISION EVENTS (where
   conditioning is physically meaningful).
3. Give a JOINT law over pair-relation changes that does not assume
   intermediate divisibility — the law assigns chances to entire
   compatible collections of local relation changes (the relational
   rewrite process; the field equivalent).
4. State how the realized record changes future relational locality
   (the interaction-geometry identity: one occurrence, two readings).
5. The HAMILTONIAN EQUIVALENCE CRITERION: two generators/Hamiltonians
   represent the same physics iff they produce the same objective
   law for every complete record-and-geometry history; local
   Hamiltonian representations relative to cuts/clocks are the
   expected general case under backreaction — a global Hamiltonian
   is not presumed.
6. Determine whether the EXISTING WALK IS RECONSTRUCTIBLE from the
   process law rather than imported alongside it.

## THE SHARP INSTRUCTION (verbatim obligation)

two-body process.  Test a theory in which configurations consist of
binary relational facts, division-to-division laws may jointly change

backreaction/curvature contexts, not automatically as primitive
