## THE THREE LEVELS (never conflated; W-REPRESENTATION binds)
WHAT EXISTS: the actual relational configuration and realized history
— actors, relations (pair-atomic), records, generated causal
geometry.  THE LAW: objective chances for complete future histories,
conditioned only where conditioning is physically meaningful.
REPRESENTATION: transition matrices, amplitudes, psi, rho, unitaries,
Hamiltonians, fields, clocks, cuts.  No unit may promote a
representation to ontology; "the kernel K is reality" is the same
error as "the Hamiltonian is reality."

## THE ARITY SPLIT (E-34-registered)
RECORD-ARITY: two actors per elementary relational fact — the atomic
beable.  PROCESS-ARITY: any finite number of actors in one
indivisible boundary-to-boundary transition.  Pairwise ontology does
not imply pairwise-divisible dynamics; destroying indivisibility by
forced pair-sequencing is the named hazard.  Triples: the smallest
CURVATURE PROBE (order defects of overlapping pair changes), not
automatically the primitive event.
