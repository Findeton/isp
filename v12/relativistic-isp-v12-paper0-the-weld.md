### T2′ — The coherent-composition defect (the core, scoped per [REV2])

Let $B(U) = |U|^{\circ 2}$ be the entrywise Born projection from amplitude evolution to its stochastic shadow. For a composable pair of amplitude propagators, define

$$
\Delta^{B}(U_2, U_1) \;:=\; B(U_2 U_1) \;-\; B(U_2)\,B(U_1).
$$

Entrywise, this is exactly the interference cross-term generated by coherent alternatives at the intermediate configuration space — [B1]'s own identification:

$$
\Delta^{B}_{ij}
\;=\;
2\,\mathrm{Re} \sum_{k<\ell}
(U_2)_{ik}(U_1)_{kj}\,
\overline{(U_2)_{i\ell}(U_1)_{\ell j}}.
$$

$\Delta^{B}$ measures the failure of the Born shadow of the coherent propagator to equal the stochastic process obtained by phase-forgetting and restarting at the intermediate cut.

**Three defects are distinguished and not conflated** ([REV2] §1; counterexample hand-verified at LOG #4):

$$
\begin{array}{ll}
\Delta^{B}(U_2,U_1) = B(U_2U_1) - B(U_2)B(U_1)
& \text{the Born-shadow defect;}\\[4pt]
D_{210} = \Gamma_{20} - \Gamma_{21}\Gamma_{10}
& \text{the residual of an actual declared stochastic law;}\\[4pt]
d_{\mathrm{div}}(\Gamma_{20},\Gamma_{10}) = \inf_{K\,\in\,\mathrm{Stoch}} \left\| \Gamma_{20} - K\,\Gamma_{10} \right\|
& \text{existential divisibility.}
\end{array}
$$

**$\Delta^{B} \neq 0$ does *not* imply stochastic indivisibility.** The exact rotation counterexample: with

$$
B(R(\theta)) = S(\cos 2\theta),
\qquad
S(c)\,S(d) = S(cd),
\qquad
S(c) = \tfrac{1}{2}\begin{pmatrix} 1+c & 1-c \\ 1-c & 1+c \end{pmatrix},
$$

one has $\Delta^{B} \neq 0$ while the stochastic matrix $K = S(c_{\mathrm{tot}}/c_1)$ factorizes the shadow whenever $|c_{\mathrm{tot}}| \le |c_1|$.

The programme hypothesis: the **family** of $\Delta^{B}$ over physically declared cuts and contexts organizes quantum interference. The claim is about **composing** (the temporal layer); whether it also governs contextual **gluing** is W4′'s open question, not asserted here. The v1 identification (quantum $\Leftrightarrow$ $[g] \neq 0 \in H^1$) stays withdrawn. $\Delta^{B}$ is the primary **interference** invariant, not a complete specification of quantum mechanics — [B1] notes interference-like discrepancies in broader non-Markovian systems; what carves the *quantum* correlation body is $\Delta^{B}$'s amplitude structure **plus** Gram positivity (§3).

## 3. The exact skeleton (the review's, adopted; convexified per [REV2] §3)

$$
P(x, y \mid a, b) \;=\; \tfrac{1}{4}\bigl(1 + xy\,E_{ab}\bigr),

$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{corr}} &\;=\; \mathrm{conv}\bigl\{\, E_{ab} = s_a t_b \;:\; s_a, t_b \in \{\pm 1\} \,\bigr\},\\[4pt]
