## The question

Given an atlas of chart-local processes, when do two local record
descriptions express THE SAME FACT, when do they denote THE SAME
PHYSICAL OCCURRENCE, and when do the resulting pairwise
identifications assemble coherently across the atlas?  Three
distinct problems, never conflated:

- **A (fact ≠ value):** two detectors may both read +1 by
  accident — equality of values is insufficient for sameness of
  fact.
- **B (fact ≠ token):** two redundant records can carry the same
  fact while being two physical tokens with different provenance.
- **C (pairwise ≠ global):** A↔B and B↔C identifications may
  disagree with the direct A↔C one; pair-level success does not
  give triple/loop coherence.  (Paper 1's own lesson, one level
  down: pair invariants complete, composite not.)

## The unit (after STEP 0 passes)

- **W6-A — fact co-reference:** two local records carry the same
  fact iff they become the same proposition in a common
  record-preserving extension, or a rigorously specified
  common-record witness identifies them.  (This weaker notion is
  what v13's regional descent needs.)
- **W6-B — event-token co-reference:** sameness of occurrence
  requires provenance: generating interaction, causal ancestry,
  local support, copying lineage, erasure history.  (This is
  where the withdrawn global (E,≺) smuggled structure.)
- **W6-C — effective descent:** given the pairwise maps, decide
  whether coherent matching families arise from (i) one
  chart-independent record object (set-level amalgam), (ii) only
  a groupoid/stack (nontrivial automorphisms), (iii) several
  inequivalent co-reference structures (underdetermined), or
  (iv) nothing (triple/loop coherence fails).  Coherence laws:
  φ_aa = id; φ_ba = φ_ab⁻¹; φ_ab∘φ_bc = φ_ac on triple overlaps,
  modulo the declared gauge only.

## Mandatory controls (each an exact model; the deliverable is the
## DESCENT TABLE — per model × per sub-problem (A/B/C) → outcome)

1. **Relabelled same record:** charts differing by a
   configuration relabelling only — co-reference MUST succeed.
2. **Redundant copies:** same fact, two tokens — A succeeds, B
   fails (by construction, gated).
3. **Two-frame final outcomes:** the committed two-ordering Bell
   structure — final-record fact descent MUST succeed exactly
   where the final laws agree.
4. **Intermediate frame content:** the two intermediate slice
   descriptions must NOT be forced identical merely for
   belonging to one experiment (frame-relativity respected).
5. **Record erasure:** erasure must not retroactively identify
   the erased token with "no event" — present availability vs
   historical occurrence distinguished in the formalism.
6. **Symmetric duplicate:** two indistinguishable tokens
   exchanged by an automorphism preserving every committed
   observable and phase invariant — if no committed datum
   distinguishes them, no invariant rule may choose; the honest
   outcome there is groupoid/underdetermination, gated.
