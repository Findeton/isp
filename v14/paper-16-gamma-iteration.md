## 3. Provenance, and the discipline it is read under

Every source is read from a path resolved from the delivery file's own
location and gated against a sha256-12 declared in the frozen pin. No
subprocess is spawned, no version-control command is invoked, and no
moving reference is read: a drifted source dies at its byte anchor
before a single measurement runs. That is what a pinned-sha read buys
without a version-control system present, and it is why the plain run
byte-reproduces off-tree and with git absent.

Both prohibition scans are structural rather than textual where they can
be, and whitespace-normalised where they cannot. The banned-name scan
reads **import names and aliases** as well as uses, so `import
subprocess as sp` is a hit; the moving-reference scan runs on a
whitespace-normalised haystack, so a reference written with a doubled
space, a tab or a newline inside it is a hit. Each has its own
falsifier, and each falsifier is the evasion itself. The needles and
the evasions are both assembled rather than typed: spelling either in
the delivery's own source would make the guard fire on itself, and it
could never be satisfied.

Every declared read path is required to be **relative**, not to escape
the root, and to resolve under the root the run computes from its own
file location — a predicate that can fail, and whose falsifier makes one
declared path absolute and another escaping.

19 verbatim-context windows bind quote fidelity — each at least 40
characters, each located exactly once in its source, each declared
against a *registered* consumer gate, and each perturbed at a
content-bearing token and re-located, so that a row counts as covered
only when its own predicate flips; 19 of 19 do. 11 path-value probes
resolve into the two pinned receipts and match declared values; an
unresolvable probe aborts the delivery rather than being swallowed.

**One source is declared and not read.** The pin cites the weld-2
re-derivation of the same carrier as a cross-check, "cited, not
imported". Its pinned sha is not the sha at the tip and its working copy
is being rewritten by a concurrent unit, so its bytes move. A file whose
bytes move cannot be a runtime input of a byte-reproducible run. The
citation is carried as a frozen declaration and this unit re-derives the
carrier itself instead.

**The gate-to-disk seal.** Every published object is digested at the
moment its gate passes, and every registry row — gate, falsifier,
anchor — at the moment it is registered. The receipt is then built
**from those sealed snapshots**, not from the live objects, and
`G-SEAL-COMPLETE` re-takes every seal against the live objects
immediately before the write: a published value mutated anywhere between
its gate and the write refuses the delivery. The artifacts are written
to temporaries, re-read, and matched **against the seal** — never
against a re-serialisation of what is in memory, which would confirm a
corruption rather than catch it — and only then moved into place with
`os.replace`. A corrupted-payload probe is written and re-read first,
and the run refuses if the comparator fails to notice it. A terminal
re-verification runs last, at process exit; if the bytes on disk have
moved it removes both artifacts and exits non-zero. **Every failure path
writes nothing, and no path leaves a corrupt file on disk.**

The delivery ships an argv-parsed command line: unknown flags exit 2
rather than being ignored; `--selftest` corrupts exactly one declared
anchor, confirms the delivery is refused, and writes nothing;
`--mutant NAME` evaluates one declared falsifier and leaves every
artifact untouched; `--list-mutants` prints the falsifier registry
before any measurement, and `--list-gates` prints the gate registry the
run evaluates, after the run has built it.

kernel is k_r(e|h) = q(e|h) G(h+e, r−1) / G(h, r), the chain's own law

at every one of the 5 cuts, and the disintegration identity

  w(h) · k₄₋|h|(e|h) = w(h+e)

identity for any price law and any carrier; it cannot fail. What is
measured is the other half: **the horizon in it is not free.** Off the

### 8.2 What carries the signature, and what does not

The natural explanation — that the whole non-Markov signature at
MENU-113 is *carried by* that quotient's multi-target labelled edges, so
that refining them away removes the signature — is refutable, and this
unit's own refinement lattice refutes it. What replaces it is five
measurements and one theorem.

| quotient | classes | multi-target edges | depth-spanning classes | CK failures |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| MENU-113 | 113 | 4 | 45 | 4 |
| refinement round 1 | 162 | 132 | 17 | 0 |
| refinement round 2 | 179 | 36 | 5 | 0 |
| refinement round 3 | 184 | 8 | 1 | 0 |
| CONG-185 | 185 | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| record quotient | 2477 | 0 | 0 | 0 |

**(i) A set identity.** The 4 multi-target labelled edges of MENU-113
sit on 4 source classes, and those 4 classes are *set-identical* to the
4 classes on which ruling property 1 fails at horizon 2. Two of the six
properties fail on exactly the same objects.

**(ii) Five sources, not four.** The Chapman–Kolmogorov failing cells at
MENU-113 trace to 5 source classes, not 4 — the multi-target sources are
a strict subset. A fifth class carries failing cells and is the source
of no multi-target edge, so even the localisation is not exact.

**(iii) Single-valuedness is sufficient and not necessary.** Split only
those 4 source classes, only by the target of their offending label,
changing nothing else: the result has 121 classes, leaves 36
multi-target edges standing — so it is not a congruence and not a
probabilistic bisimulation — and is Chapman–Kolmogorov exact at 0 of 10
triples. The signature dies without the edges being removed.

**(iv) The repair is the offending edges', not any refinement's.**
Splitting the 4 *largest other* menu classes instead — by their full
successor signature, which is a strictly finer cut than the minimal
split makes — leaves CK failing at 4 of 10. So the minimal split's
success is a fact about those edges and not about refining in
general.

**(v) The statistic is not monotone in the refinement order.**
Refinement round 1 carries 132 multi-target edges — thirty-three times
MENU's 4 — and 17 depth-spanning classes at 162 classes, and is
*already* CK-exact at 0 of 10. The multi-target count runs
4, 132, 36, 8, 0 along the lattice and rises 33-fold at exactly the
step where the signature it allegedly carries goes to zero.

**(vi) The containment the delivery asserted is computed.** The 4
multi-target source classes do lie inside the 45 of 113 menu classes
that recur across depth cuts, each at depths [1, 2, 3, 4]. It is true;
it is now gated, with a falsifier that runs the same containment against
the depth-pure classes, where it fails.

**The one general law**, and it is a theorem rather than a
correlation: a quotient with a single-valued labelled weight *and*
target is a probabilistic bisimulation, so its class process satisfies
Chapman–Kolmogorov at every triple. That is the sufficient direction.
Items (iii) and (v) are the witnesses that it is not necessary.

## 9. [B3], and the atom

### 9.1 The feasibility LP, convention-free

The register named the route: the exact rational feasibility LP, row
decomposable, at the first triple 45 independent 13-variable
non-negative feasibility problems plus one column-sum coupling. Both
halves are run here, in exact arithmetic, with **both** verdicts
certified — a feasible verdict returns a primal point verified against
the constraints, an infeasible verdict returns a Farkas vector verified
to satisfy y·A ≤ 0 and y·b > 0. A **negated** Farkas vector, which
reverses the sign of y·b, is re-checked through the same verifier and
fails it, so the certificates are instruments and not labels.

The support reduction the problems use is exact and not a relaxation: if
Γ(dd←d)[i][s] is 0 and Γ(md←d)[j][s] is positive then Ḡ[i][j] must be 0,
because every term of the sum is non-negative.

**The rows carry no obstruction.** The problem is feasible at every one
of its 772 rows over the 8 (carrier, triple) cells, at both carriers and
at all four triples with a non-degenerate first cut. Nor does the support
pattern carry one: 0 orphan columns and 0 empty rows anywhere.

**The coupling carries all of it, and it separates the two carriers.**

| MENU-113 | infeasible at 3 of 4, Farkas-certified; feasible at (1, 2, 4) | none |

### 9.2 The cell that speaks, and what it costs eq. 22

Γ-main concluded that at every depth-cut triple with a non-degenerate
first cut, no interpolant of **eq. 22's form** exists. That statement is
true of its own object — the square, padded one — and it is not the
statement this unit tests. This unit asks whether a column-stochastic
non-negative Ḡ exists with Γ(dd←d) = Ḡ · Γ(md←d): rectangular,
unpadded, no enlarged configuration space.

The two questions diverge at exactly one cell, and it is named. At
MENU-113 (1, 2, 4) the padded candidate is unique and carries 104
negative entries, and the rectangular column-stochastic problem is
feasible, in 413 variables against 258 equations, certified. At the
comparison cell (1, 2, 3) the same problem carries 121 variables against
94 equations and is infeasible with a verified Farkas vector.

The mechanism is visible. **The padding converts an underdetermined
feasibility question into a determined algebraic one.** Completing the
first transfer to a square invertible matrix makes the candidate unique,
and a unique candidate that fails positivity refutes the existence of
*that candidate* — while the unpadded feasible set can be non-empty at
the same cell, and here it is.

> **EQ-22 NEGATIVITY DOES NOT IMPLY THE NON-EXISTENCE OF A STOCHASTIC
> INTERPOLANT.**

That is a standing caution for every eq-22-based refutation in the
corpus, demonstrated at 1 of 4 cells. It is not an erratum against
Γ-main, whose claim was form-scoped and survives entirely; it is a
scope annotation, registered at
`v14/note-paper12-scope-annotation.md`. How often the two questions
diverge elsewhere is not known, and that is the successor's item.

## 10. The anchor: sedimentary, by theorem

The pin declared two honest paths and made the choice a measurement.

**Path (a), the renewal-root candidate, is measured and it fails at the
class grain.** The ruled carrier's classes are depth-pure: 0 of 185
classes occur at more than one depth cut, so the class chain is graded by
depth and no class is ever revisited. The count of histories whose class
equals the class of one of their own prefixes is 0 at CONG-185 and 1900
at MENU-113. The root's own class occurs at depth 0 alone at the ruled
carrier and at all of 0, 1, 2, 3, 4 at the predecessor carrier. The one
small set that survives the coarsening is entered at 0 transitions and
has δ\* = 0. And the first-return law the pin offers as the candidate,
g(1) = g(2) = 0 and g(n) = C(n−1,2)(3/4)^(n−3)/256 for n ≥ 3, is stated
at **delivery-free** scope by its own source, which declares in terms
that transport scope changes the picture — a quotation that is a
conjunct of the anchor gate itself, not only of its falsifier.

**No link between recurrence and non-Markovianity is claimed.** The two
carriers the pin names happen to differ in both respects, and this
unit's own refinement lattice separates them: at round 1 the root's
class still occurs at depths [0, 1, 2, 3], 204 histories return to a
prefix class, and the chain is CK-exact at 0 of 10 triples. The
co-location is coincidental and is not carried.

Projecting the holdings ladder out does not lift the obstruction,
because the carrier's own class label *determines* the depth: a second
monotone coordinate that the projection cannot remove.

**And the grading is not an artifact of this cap.**

> **Theorem (depth-grading is cap-universal).** Let the window be
> {h : |h| ≤ N} and suppose every history of depth < N has at least one
> successor. After refinement round *k*, a history's class determines
> min(N − |h|, k).
>
> *Proof.* Round 1: the in-window successor multiset is empty iff
> |h| = N, so the class determines min(N − |h|, 1). Round k+1: the
> signature contains the round-*k* classes of the successors, each of
> which determines min(N − |h| − 1, k); non-emptiness distinguishes
> |h| < N. So the class determines min(N − |h|, k+1). ∎
>
> Hence at round N the class determines |h| exactly: **the fixed point
> is depth-pure at every finite cap**, and the fixed point is reached at
> round N+1.

The hypothesis is measured rather than assumed — every history below the
cap has at least one successor — and the conclusion is measured at two
caps, because this unit builds the second one rather than pointing at
it:

that are not there. The delivery's canonical key function exists exactly

is formed, and every published path runs through that canonicalisation
or through a sorted serialisation. A `repr`-based audit of this file
will find differences the objects do not have.

## 17. The next-iteration register

**The boundary convention.** Every negative in §10 rests on the
depth-grading, and the grading is now known to be forced at every finite
cap. The successor's first question is therefore not a wider cap — this
unit has built and measured one — but a fixed point taken under a
boundary convention whose terminal stratum does not carry an empty
successor signature. Only there can the renewal-root candidate be posed
as a question about the process rather than about the window.

**The eq-22 / feasibility separation.** This unit has shown that eq-22
negativity and the non-existence of a stochastic interpolant can come
apart, at 1 of 4 cells. Nothing tells us how often. The successor should
re-run the corpus's eq-22-based refutations against the rectangular
column-stochastic feasibility question and measure the divergence rate.
What distinguishes the cell that speaks — (1, 2, 4), 413 variables
against 258 equations, where (1, 2, 3) has 121 against 94 — is plausibly
the slack of the column-sum coupling, and it is one measurement away.

**What one refinement round removes.** The non-Markov signature dies at
refinement round 1, which still carries 132 multi-target edges and 17
depth-spanning classes. What that single round of successor-closure
actually removes is unmeasured, and it is the real mechanism question
this unit leaves open.

**The R6 wall.** The depth-grading theorem bars regeneration anchors at
the class grain for every capped successor-closure fixed point of this
construction. A continuum programme that needs a renewal or regeneration
anchor cannot get one from this family, at any cap, by theorem rather
than by cost. That is a charter-level constraint, not only a paper-16
one.

**What the coupling unit inherits.** The law at its values,
(15/38, 5/19, 13/38), at a readout whose normalisation is law-native;
the gate-time seal and its disk-against-seal integrity test, which
should be the default for every successor; and the
description-property discipline for every quantum claim — that a
non-Markov statistic read at a quotient is a fact about the quotient
until something rules out the lumping. It also inherits the warning that
on every quantum-shape statistic measured here the ruled carrier is
indistinguishable from the record quotient, so those statistics cannot
be used to argue that the ruled carrier is the physically distinguished
one.

**The atom language is spent at this carrier** — empty on the block,
vacuous on the classes — and the successor should stop carrying it. What
replaces it, if anything, is the accumulation reading the anchor segment
now stamps, and that reading has no instrument yet.

**Two rows to pin.** The d ≤ 6 arena, which this unit excluded by cost
and which every full-family statement still leans on; and the weld-2
re-derivation of this carrier, once its bytes stop moving.
