## 13. Scope, and what a successor would have to change

This unit measures a relation at one arena at one declared horizon, on a
rebuild of one parent's machine. It licenses nothing about any other arena, any
other coin, any horizon it did not run, or any continuum.

What it does hand forward is a sharpened question. The relation is not absent
because the substrate identity is false; it is absent because **the decoherence
functionals available at a single time in a site-local basis are blind to the
record by construction**, and the one functional that is not blind gives a
partial dependence with a censused failure set rather than a law. A successor
that wants a gravitational-decoherence law at this substrate must therefore
change one of three things, and each is now named and sized:

1. **Make the observable read the history, not the state.** `D1` and `D2` are
   functions of the state at one time; the record enters them only through the
   walk's own trajectory, with a delay measured here as one step. A
   two-time functional — a decoherence *rate* between consecutive menus rather
   than a decoherence *value* at one — is the obvious next object, and it is
   not built here.
2. **Break the site-locality of the coin.** The blindness of `D1` is a
   consequence of the coin preserving each site's mass, which is the same
   property paper-20's law transport consumes. A walk that moved amplitude
   between sites before the menu is read would break both at once, and would
   have to re-gate the transport.
3. **Reach a record the order-blindness cannot reach.** The partial dependence
   fails exactly where two orders of the same events give different states.
   Whether some coarsening of the record is order-blind *and* still determines
   the decoherence is a question this unit poses and does not answer: the
   cell-grain record and all three coarsenings it declared fail, and their
   failure sets are censused.

The pin's `GDL-DECOUPLED` outcome was available and is not what was measured.
There is structure here — an exact identity, an exact mechanism, a forced
ladder — and none of it is a gravitational-decoherence law.
