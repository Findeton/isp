## 6. Stage 4 -- curvature of the gluing

### 6.1 The union's form against the direct sum

At a shared site the union carries six links: three of the A-chart and
three of the B-chart. I7's readout says the count on a link is the squared
length of its direction, so the union's form at that site is the solution
of an exact linear system on Sym^2 of the direct-sum chart -- ten unknowns,
six equations, solved by exact row reduction over the rationals.

| seam | n(A) | n(B) | equations | unknowns | rank | kernel | Q(v) at the witness |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ALIGNED(k=3) | [1, 1, 1] | [1, 1, 1] | 6 | 10 | 6 | 4 | -2 |
| TRIANGLE(k=3) | [1, 2, 1] | [1, 2, 1] | 6 | 10 | 6 | 4 | -1 |
| k=1 | [1, 1, 1] | [1, 1, 1] | 6 | 10 | 6 | 4 | -2 |

**the seam system has rank 6 on 10 unknowns, so its kernel is 4**.

The direct sum -- the completion that sets every cross entry to zero -- is
one point of a four-parameter affine family, and it is positive definite
by the exact Sylvester criterion, with leading minors 1, 3/4, 3/4, 9/16 at the aligned
seam and 1, 1, 1, 1 at the
triangle seam. But the family is not confined to that cone: at every seam
an exact rational completion is exhibited which reproduces all six measured
counts and is NEGATIVE on an exhibited rational vector.

So **the direct sum is a declaration, not a measurement.** The record fixes
six of the ten entries of the seam's form and says nothing whatever about
the other four.

The indefinite completion is NAMED AND NOT READ: that the seam's undetermined block admits completions of mixed sign is a statement about what the record fails to fix, it is not a signature, and no Lorentzian, causal or dimensional reading of it is taken here or licensed by anything measured here.

Note also, and read no further than the arithmetic: the triangle seam's
direct-sum minors are all 1 where the aligned seam's are 1, 3/4, 3/4, 9/16, so the
doubled link does not merely relabel the seam -- it moves its determinant.
