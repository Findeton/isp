# CORRECTION ANNOTATION — PAPER-30 (LOR), §7's UNIQUENESS CLAUSE

**Category:** correction-annotation (precedent:
note-paper23-correction.md).  **Date:** 2026-08-12.
**Source:** the PER-R effectus review
(v14/review-perr-effectus.md, 3de7cf332e70, frozen ledger
#294), adopted at the PER-R adjudication.

**The defect.**  LOR's committed §7 (paper-30-lor.md,
0a08203b7e99, terminal at ledger #285) carries the prose
clause "and no other homogeneous record."  The PER-R seat's
own R=6 census measures **28 homogeneous records, ten of
them covering** — the uniqueness clause is FALSE at R=6.

**Scope.**  Prose-only.  LOR's code-sealed budget_law never
contained the clause; no gate, no receipt key, and no
delivered measured number of paper-30 depends on it.  The
budget law itself, the live-cut law 9m − 17, the ladder
1/10/19/28/37, and every sealed quantity stand unmoved.

**The correction.**  §7 is to be read with the clause
deleted.  The uniqueness held at the R-values the LOR census
actually visited and was overwritten by prose into a
universal; the R=6 census refutes the universal.  Paper-30
remains TERMINAL; per the programme's paper discipline the
file is not edited — this annotation is the correction of
record.  Any successor unit citing §7 must cite it as
corrected here.
