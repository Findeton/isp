**Establishing measurement.** `measure_quantifiability` computes, for every
row, `q = r["verdict"] in (VERBATIM, SCOPED)` — i.e. *the two closure

> quantifiable over horizons — statable with no symbol for a size

Those are different properties, and this unit's own delivered tables show the
gap in **both** directions:

- **Sorted as exceptions, but size-free on the unit's own measurement.**
  LAW-MERGING-INDEX, LAW-PARENT-ORBITS and LAW-HOMOLOGY all have open-arena

  which the paper prints. "For every finite open patch, the merging index is
  1" contains no symbol for a size. It is exactly a HOR-law by the schema

- **Sorted as quantifiable, but not of the form the schema licenses.**
  LAW-COIN-FAMILY (640), LAW-RECTANGLE-FAMILY (16 shapes that fit) and
  LAW-CLASS-COUNT (136) are properties of the alphabet or of the whole arena.
  The unit's own `DIRECT_LIMIT_FORM` quantifies over "every observable O

  such an observable. The gate calls all three quantifiable.

Under #295 (class words gate-bound to computed predicates) the word must
follow the predicate or the predicate must be rebuilt to the word. This is
also the registered-unimplemented S-3 shape ("the gates encode the answer")

**Repair.** Rename to what is computed — `HORIZON-STATABLE-BY-THE-CLOSURE-
WITNESS-TEST` — throughout gate text, head and paper; or build the predicate

### MAJOR-3 — one of the four pre-registered outcome words is unreachable by construction, and the paper publishes it as live

**Establishing measurement.** `classify_row` returns `VERBATIM` on exactly
the branch `open_witness == closed_witness`. `measure_the_outcome` then
computes

```
offenders = [r for r in census if r["verdict"] == VERBATIM
             and r["witness_on_the_open_arena"] != r["witness_on_the_closed_arena"]]
```

which is the conjunction of a predicate with its own negation. `offenders` is
the empty list at **every possible input**, including under `MUT-CENSUS`
(which writes ARTIFACT onto a row with equal witnesses, the wrong direction).
So `HOR-GLOBAL-SENSITIVE-AT-` cannot be reached, ever. The paper nonetheless
publishes it as one of the branches that could have fired:

> The globally-sensitive word is reached by any census row the classifier
> calls surviving while its two residuals differ, and that comparison is
> computed on every row.

The sentence is true and vacuous, and it is offered as evidence that the
branch was live. Under #299-as-extended, feasibility must be argued against
the committed corpus **at the declared row list**; here the argument is
refuted by the classifier's own definition.

This matters beyond bookkeeping. The pin called this branch "a local
observable that feels the far lattice — would be a major discovery against
the formulation." The unit's most important negative result therefore has no
route to the head through the census. It does have a route elsewhere — the
contractible sweep's 0 of 10240 and 0 of 650, and the dynamical sufficiency's
0 of 64, are the real tests and they are strong — so the substance is
covered; the pre-registration is not.

**Repair.** Either re-point the offender predicate at something reachable
(the natural one: a row whose open witness is constant across the declared
open ladder yet differs from the closed witness *at every closed size* — that
is a genuine global sensitivity and it is computable from data this run
already has), or re-word the branch to say what it actually is: a
consistency check on the classifier, not a live outcome. If the latter, the
pin's four-way rule collapses to three live branches and the paper must say
so in §"What could have come out otherwise".
