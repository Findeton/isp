THE CLAIMS THIS RUN LICENSES:
  - The tick is one application of one parity stratum operator, the object the gauge parent names and never builds, and its factors are disjoint at every size where the stratification is a matching.
  - Unitarity leaves exactly 3 vanishing patterns over the 640 coins, so the reach of a tick is zero at 64 of them and one site at 576, and it exceeds one site at 0.
  - In the sum norm the emergent speed is 1 site per tick, and it is attained at 16 of 16 censused depths, so it is a maximum, not a bound.
  - Over whole sweeps the max-norm speed is 1/2 site per tick, one value at every sweep.
  - The cone is a product of an x-range and a y-range at every one of the 16 depths, its x-range is asymmetric at 16 of them, and reversing the parity order inside the schedule reflects it at 16 while moving no radius in either norm.
  - Every amplitude support this run evolves is tested against the cone and escapes it at 0, at least 576 coins realise the cone's own sum-norm radius at every one of the 8 attainment depths, and at 2 of those depths no coin fills the cone site for site because amplitudes cancel on exact interior lines.
  - The contractible observable is compared at 10240 shape-by-coin rows at the core pair over the whole coin family and at 650 rows over the whole declared ladder, and it moves at 0.
  - The dynamical observable is measured at 64 arena-by-depth rows, the horizon condition is predicted from the cone before any value is read, containing the source's cone is sufficient for arena-independence and is violated at 0 rows, and the measured slack where agreement survives without it is 4.
  - The tester finds the value unmoved at 54 of those rows and moved at 10, so it is neither blind nor unanimous.
  - The straight winding cycle exists at no open size at all, and across adjacent closed sizes its observable moves at 8 of 10 size-by-coin comparisons.
  - All 5 control arms go through one tester that is told nothing about which arm it is running: it reports moved at 4 and unmoved at 1, each on the side declared for it before the run.
  - Of the 18 sealed laws this unit uses, 9 survive the flush verbatim, 1 survive with a stated proviso and 8 were carried by the identification alone.
  - The open patch realises all 8 constant twists at every one of its 7 sizes, so its merging index is 1, which a closed arena reaches only where the phase order divides its size.
  - Closed walks of length 8 from a fixed base fall into 1 displacement class on the open arena and 13 on the closed one.
  - Every one of the parent's 9 declared axes shifts the closed arena onto itself and none shifts the open one onto itself, and the translations carrying the open link set to itself number 1.
  - The parent's closed form, fitted at the three shortest perimeters and then checked, fails at 0 of 10240 rows on an arena with no identification anywhere, and its area-blindness disagrees at 0 of 5120 equal-perimeter comparisons there.
  - Across 5 open sizes directed under inclusion the inclusions carry 400 values and fail to carry 0.
  - 10 of the 18 laws are quantifiable over horizons and 8 are not, and every one of those carries the name of the object it needs.
