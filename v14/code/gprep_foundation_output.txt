  [B1 — THE TREE OBSTRUCTION, measured and named]
  Write the backward recursion as a level operator: (M_n g)(h) = sum_e q(e|h) g(h+e), a nonnegative matrix with rows the depth-n histories and columns the depth-(n+1) histories.  Then G(., r) at level n is M_n M_(n+1) ... 1.  Birkhoff's theorem gives a contraction of the Hilbert projective metric with coefficient tanh(Delta/4), where Delta is the operator's projective diameter — FINITE only if the matrix has no zero pattern separating a pair of columns.
  MEASURED: of the 243768 columns (every non-root history of the family), 243768 have EXACTLY ONE nonzero entry — the history's unique parent — and 0 have any other number.  Unique parenthood is the defining property of a history tree.
  CONSEQUENCE, EXHIBITED: take two rows h1 != h2 at the same level and one column under each.  The 2x2 minor is [[1/4, 0], [0, 1/4]] — the off-diagonal entries are exactly 0, so the cross-ratio m11*m22/(m21*m12) is +infinity.  Hence Delta(M_n) = infinity and tanh(Delta/4) = 1 AT EVERY LEVEL: THE BIRKHOFF CONTRACTION COEFFICIENT OF THE BACKWARD RECURSION IS 1, NOT BY A CAP AND NOT BY A NUMERICAL ACCIDENT, BUT BY UNIQUE PARENTHOOD.
  The same fact kills the history-level Doeblin bound outright: for x != y at the same depth, the supports of P^N(x, .) and P^N(y, .) are disjoint sets of descendants, so the largest common minorant is the zero measure and delta = 0 for every N.  ANY minorization at transport scope must therefore live on a QUOTIENT — which is precisely where ARM A fact 5's escape bites.  This is the structural statement the predecessor's open question did not contain.
