## 3. The walk — derived where derivable, declared where not

### 3.1 The coin register is forced, by a theorem

R4b's family shape is not a coined walk at all: a generator there is a
coefficient map c on lattice offsets with M[x+v][x] = c_v, unitary exactly
when its autocorrelation is a delta. Evaluate that shape on **this** arena's

deterministic shift, carrying no interference whatever.

The theorem is checked exhaustively as well as proved — over a declared
