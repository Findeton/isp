## 1. THE QUESTION, AND WHY IT HAS AN ANSWER

The corpus works in one arena: the nine-actor plane, with four parallel
classes and three declared link directions.  Five laws were established
there.  Each of them is stated with numerals in it, and every one of those
numerals is either a 3, a 4, a 5 or a 6 -- small numbers in an arena whose own
parameters are 3, 4 and 9.  When the same small number appears in a law and in
the arena, a reader cannot tell from the law alone which of them it belongs
to.

The way to find out is to change the arena and look.  This unit builds
AG(2,2) -- four actors, the Klein-four translation group -- by the parent's
own grammar, and re-runs the five laws on it.  Where a law's numeral is 3, the
n = 4 arena separates the candidates that the nine-actor plane had collapsed:
the square root of the actor count is 2 there, and so is the field
characteristic, and so is the declared link count under the parent's
declaration.  Where those three
still coincide, a second axis is opened: the declared link count L is a free
axis of the arena, and sweeping it at FIXED n = 4 separates it from everything
that is a function of n.  Where the question survives both, the n = 16 window
answers it -- and the q = 4 plane is the first point at which the
characteristic and the square root part company.

One property of the arena points this unit can reach bounds what its words can
mean, and it is stated here rather than left for a reader to notice: at every
one of them q is the square root of the actor count exactly -- 4 and 2, 9 and
3, 16 and 4.  A formula in q is therefore also a formula in n, and no
three-point table over those arenas can separate the two.  What the three-point
table can do is compare the measurement against ONE declared n-only reading,
fixed by the rule of section 8, and that is exactly what the words below
record.  The one instrument in this unit that genuinely separates a carrier
from n is the fixed-n sweep of section 6, where n stands still and the carrier
moves.

Two slots are scored, not one, and each gets its own word.  A law's STATEMENT
is LAW-IN-N when the parent's theorem, as stated, holds at the new arena point;
it is BREAKS when it fails there.  A law's NUMERAL is LAW-IN-N when the reading
of it in which the numeral is a function of the actor count alone agrees with
the measurement at every arena point the unit can reach; it is NEEDS-3 when
that reading fails and the reading in which the numeral is a function of the
square root -- or of the declared link count, which the sweep separates --
succeeds; it is BREAKS when neither does.  Three of the five laws have no
numeral in their statements at all, which is why the two slots must be scored
separately and why the aggregate declares which slot it is aggregating.
Nothing else is asserted about the actor count anywhere in this paper, and the
reading walls of section 9 scan this text for sentences that would.

## 3. LAW 1 -- THE NAMING THEOREM

The parent's theorem says that the stabilizer of a history -- the permutations
of the actors carrying every division event to itself, setwise -- is the Young
subgroup of the participation-signature partition.  The statement contains no
numeral at all, which is already a hint; the measurement is what settles it.

The parent also PROVED it, by a Boolean-algebra argument that names no arena:
fixing every event setwise is the same as fixing every atom of the algebra the
events generate, and the atoms are exactly the signature blocks.  So the two
routes below agree for any family of subsets of any finite set whatever, and
the comparison cannot fail on a faithful implementation.  That is disclosed
here rather than scored: the leg is a REPRODUCTION of the parent's theorem and

count that the n = 4 arena actually decides.  The parent's own proof is
measured too, at every prefix: the atoms of the Boolean algebra are the
signature blocks at 53 of 53, and the stabilizer's order is the product of the
block factorials at 53 of 53.

Two routes are run and compared as sets of permutations, per object.  Route A
holds the whole of S_4 and keeps what survives the definition; route B builds
the Young subgroup from the signature blocks and knows nothing of route A.
Over the corpus there are 53 distinct prefixes, counted once per event multiset
because the stabilizer reads only which events occurred (87 counted as
sequences), and the two routes disagree at none of them.  At n = 16 the
symmetric group has 20,922,789,888,000 elements and is not filtered; route A
is taken instead on a declared permutation window of all transpositions and all

## 4. LAW 2 -- THE CRYSTALLIZATION PAIR

The parent published two objects and was careful to name both: a schedule
time, 5, and an information floor, 4, with the floor argued from a counting
bound -- k events supply at most 2^k distinct binary signatures, so no history
can force identity on fewer than ceil(log2 n) events -- and measured to be
reached at nine actors.  The offset between them is one, and one redundant
event explains it.

All three legs are re-measured here.

| n | schedule time | attained floor | counting bound | sharpened bound | offset |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4 | 3 | 2 | 2 | 2 | 1 |
| 9 | 5 | 4 | 4 | 4 | 1 |
| 16 | 7 | 6 | 4 | 6 | 1 |

The schedule time is 3 at n = 4 -- constant across C1, C1FAN and C2, exactly
as the parent's 5 was constant across its three -- and 7 at n = 16.  Those are
2q - 1: two rounds of q - 1 informative events each, plus the one that closes
the second round.  The n-only reading fails at n = 16.

The floor is where the interesting thing happens, and the licensed sentence is
narrower than "the parent's formula does not transport".  The parent's bound is
a theorem and it stands: no history forces identity on fewer than ceil(log2 n)
events, at n = 16 as at n = 9.  What the parent measured at nine actors is that
the bound is reached there, and that measurement stands too.  At nine actors
the counting bound and the bound that includes the event size q coincide at 4,
so the parent's arena could not discriminate the two forms -- the form was
UNDISCRIMINATED there, and a formula written in n was as good a label as a
formula in q.  Only sixteen actors part them.  At sixteen actors the bound
reads 4, and the smallest event subset that forces identity has size 6 at every
one of the 24 covering class tuples.  The reason is that the counting bound
ignores something the arena settles: every division event has exactly q
members, so k events distribute total incidence kq, while n distinct binary
k-signatures cost at least the total weight of the n lightest distinct
k-vectors.  Imposing both conditions gives a sharpened floor, and it reads
2, 4 and 6 -- matching the measurement at all three arena points, including the
two where the counting bound also matched.  It is the ATTAINMENT that fails to
transport, not the bound; the parent's statement was correct where it was made,
and the n-only generalisation THIS UNIT CONSTRUCTS FROM IT -- by the rule of
section 8, not by any claim of the parent's -- is not.

The sharpened formula has a closed form beyond the three points where it was
checked against a measurement: it evaluates to 2(q - 1) at q = 2 through 7,
against a schedule time of 2q - 1, so the offset is one by subtraction of two
closed forms and not only at the three arena points.  Whether the sharpened
floor is ATTAINED at every q is the open theorem of section 10; the closed form
is arithmetic and is verified here, the attainment is not.

The offset survives everywhere: one at n = 4, one at n = 9, one at n = 16.  Its
mechanism survives with it and is measured rather than inherited.  At every C1
history the last event of round one is dropped and the signature partition is
unchanged -- 2 of 2 -- and when that event is moved to the end of the sequence
the crystallization time falls to 2, which is the attained floor.  The offset
is scored twice and both scorings are published, because the uniformity claim
of section 8 is a claim about this instrument.  Under the constant-law
exemption -- the offset is not a size but a difference of two sizes already
carried, and the counting-bound rule returns a negative reading for it at four
actors -- the three candidate readings coincide and the transport procedure
stamps the row UNDISCRIMINATED, which is what a constant deserves.  Under the
declared uniform rule applied with no exemption at all, T-N reads 1, -1 and 1
against a measured 1, 1, 1, and the word is NEEDS-3, discriminated.  Neither is
allowed to stand for the other, which is why the aggregate is rendered at both.

The stratification at four actors is published rather than summarised, because

## 6. LAW 4 -- THE MOD-3 MOTIF, WHICH SPLITS

The corpus's most-repeated coincidence is a 3.  A weld is motivated exactly at
budgets divisible by 3 -- the weld ladder's rungs are the multiples of 3 -- and
the coin of the coupled walk reads the record only modulo 3.  Both are cited
from their parents, and the first is carried into the transport table as a
frozen declared constant, cross-checked against the parent arena's own link
count rather than typed.  The pin posed a dichotomy -- does the motif become
mod-q, or does it stay mod-3? -- and the measurement dissolves it, because the
two appearances do not have the same provenance.

**The ladder's modulus is derived.**  Each round contributes exactly n to the
summed link field, so R rounds contribute nR spread over nL cells; a
homogeneous record needs R/L per cell, and L must divide R.  That is an
argument, so the unit measures it instead: for each declared link count the
achievable budgets are found by exhaustive search over multisets of saturating
groupings within a declared bound of R <= 7, and the achievable set is checked
to be exactly the multiples of the first rung.

| declared links L | cells | saturating groupings | achievable budgets | modulus |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 4 | 1 | 1,2,3,4,5,6,7 | 1 |
| 2 | 8 | 2 | 2,4,6 | 2 |
| 3 | 12 | 3 | 3,6 | 3 |

Every row of that table is at n = 4.  The actor count, the square root of the
actor count and the field characteristic are held fixed throughout, and the
modulus moves anyway: it is 1, then 2, then 3.  A quantity that moves while
n, sqrt(n) and the characteristic all stand still is not a function of any of
them.  The modulus is the declared link count -- and at n = 4 with all three
parallel classes declared, mod-3 reappears at four actors, which is as direct
a refutation of the square-root reading as the arena can supply.  At n = 16 the
same sweep at L = 4 returns budgets 4 and 8, within a declared bound of R <= 8.

A fourth candidate carrier is named here because this arena cannot separate it:
the number of saturating groupings is 1, 2 and 3 at L = 1, 2 and 3, so it moves
in lockstep with the link count at every row of the sweep.  It is separated one
arena up.  At q = 3 there are 36 saturating groupings and the achievable
budgets over all of them are 3 and 6, so the first rung is the declared link
count 3 and not the grouping count 36.  That search runs under a declared prune
-- every saturating grouping's incidence vector is measured to carry mass
exactly n, so a homogeneous field of R rounds carries R/L per cell and budgets
where that is not an integer are closed by the measured mass rather than
enumerated -- and the pruned search is checked against the plain exhaustive one
at n = 4, where both are affordable, at all three link counts.

Under the parent's own declaration L = q, so the ladder does become mod-q, and
that is the pre-registered NEEDS-3 branch.  But the carrier is L, and L is a
declaration.

**The coin's modulus is declared at this unit, and this census cannot see it.**
The one-step operator's phase block is diagonal with entries a root of unity
raised to the record.  That the record enters exactly modulo m is a
DEFINITIONAL property of the coin as this unit implements it -- the operator

What is measured is the second half, and its reach is measured with it: the
census cannot see m.  Of the four legs of the criterion of section 7, only

That is blindness of one census, and blindness is not freedom.  Paper-20

The motif therefore splits into a derived half whose modulus is the link

## 7. LAW 5 -- THE DIVISION-FORCING FRACTION

parent's own verdict is stratified by grain, and the grain tested here is the

## 8. THE INSTRUMENT

Every outcome word is emitted by the real tester.  The transport procedure is

one built to force each of the three words, one whose three candidate readings

The n-only reading is fixed by rule and not fitted per law: a single datum at

## 9. SCOPE, AND WHAT IS NOT CLAIMED

The n = 4 results are exhaustive: the whole corpus, the whole actor lattice,

Two search bounds are declared rather than left silent: the ladder sweep at

separate the link count from the number of saturating groupings without the

This unit measures the transport of five published laws.  It does not measure
whether the actor count is selected by anything, and no sentence here says it
is or is not; the pin's question is answered in the only form a measurement
can answer it, which is one law at a time.  Nor is any count converted into a
likelihood: no measure over the space of arenas or the space of histories is
declared anywhere in this unit.

What the five words add up to is worth saying plainly, because it is not what
either branch of the pin's dichotomy predicted, and because the aggregate mixes
two tests and must say which.  Three of the five laws are portable at the level
of the STATEMENT -- the naming theorem, the coset-menu theorem and the
division-forcing thesis -- and all three are portable because their STATEMENTS
contain no numeral.  Two are not, and both of those are laws whose statement is
a number.  At the level of the NUMERAL the seven tested numerals are scored
separately, and none of them is reproduced by the declared n-only reading:
every one is reproduced by a formula in q or in the declared link count L.  Of

## 10. THE SUCCESSOR REGISTER

Seven questions are left open and named.

The saturation clause needs a decision at non-prime q before the grammar can be

The n = 16 window should be closed.  The saturating census at q = 4 is a large

The sharpened floor is a formula this unit supplies and verifies at three

An arena in which q is not the square root of the actor count is what would let
the words mean what section 1 says they mean, since every arena reached here
