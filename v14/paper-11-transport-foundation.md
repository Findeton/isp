## Abstract

The Γ campaign's first stage does two things. It **terminalizes the
foundation**: seven facts about the transport grammar — the grammar
declaration, the census and the finite-horizon potentials at two
scopes, the relative-horizon kernels, the renewal ports, the escape,

and gated against the committed values rather than quoted from them.
And it **attempts the one construction the predecessor named and did
not run**: operator-level minorization of the positive backward
recursion.

The attempt returns a two-sided answer, and the two sides are the same
fact seen twice. On the history tree there is no minorization at all,
and not for want of depth: every column of every level operator has
exactly one nonzero entry, so the projective diameter is infinite, the
Birkhoff contraction coefficient is $1$ at every level,
and the largest common minorant of the level-1 laws has total mass
$0$ by disjointness of supports. That is a
theorem about history trees, not about transport: it says any
minorization must live on a quotient — which is exactly where the
escape bites — and it leaves the named engine, Birkhoff contraction of
a *quotient* transfer operator, un-attempted.

`R-SIG` decomposes by the **holdings profile**
$(\lvert \mathrm{hold}(A)\rvert, \lvert \mathrm{hold}(B)\rvert)$ into
$5$ blocks, and at the matched horizon and the declared

largest common minorant has total mass exactly $1$, so the $N$-step law
is the *same* measure at every point of the block, with an explicit

degenerate end of the minorization scale rather than its strong end. At

**$0$** of the family's $243{,}768$

### 11.2 The tree obstruction

Write the backward recursion as a level operator,

$$(M_n g)(h) \;=\; \sum_e q(e \mid h)\, g(h + e),$$

a nonnegative matrix whose rows are the depth-$n$ histories and whose
columns are the depth-$(n{+}1)$ histories, so that $G(\cdot, r)$ at
level $n$ is $M_n M_{n+1} \cdots \mathbb{1}$. Birkhoff's theorem gives a
contraction of the Hilbert projective metric with coefficient
$\tanh(\Delta/4)$, finite only if the matrix has no zero pattern
separating a pair of columns.

Two clauses live here and they are separated, because only one of them
is a measurement.

**The substantive one.** Over all $1{,}998{,}856$ menu entries
of the family there are $0$ duplicated
entries. That is the only way a column census over a history tree could
have come out otherwise, and it is a real measurement of the layer's
bookkeeping.

**The forced one.** Of the $243{,}768$ columns — every
non-root history of the family — $243{,}768$ have
exactly one nonzero entry, the history's unique parent, and
$0$ have any other number. This is forced by the
representation: a history *is* its event sequence, so
$h = g + (e,)$ forces $g = h[:-1]$. Unique parenthood is the defining
property of a history tree, and the gate carrying it is a theorem-pass
with that forcing named.

**Exhibited, from a computed minor.** Two rows at the same level and
one column under each give the minor

$$\begin{pmatrix} 1/4 & 0 \\ 0 & 1/4 \end{pmatrix}$$

in which each entry is the total weight the row's own menu sends to
that exact column — so an off-diagonal zero is read off an empty sum,
not assigned by a guard. The off-diagonal entries are exactly $0$, so
the cross-ratio is $+\infty$; hence $\Delta(M_n) = \infty$, the
diameter is finite $= False$, and the derived
coefficient is $\tau = 1$ at every level.

> **The Birkhoff contraction coefficient of the backward recursion is
> $1$ — not by a cap and not by a numerical accident, but
> by unique parenthood.**

The same fact kills the history-level Doeblin bound outright: for
$x \ne y$ at the same depth, the supports of $P^N(x, \cdot)$ and
$P^N(y, \cdot)$ are disjoint sets of descendants, so the largest common
minorant is the zero measure. Measured rather than argued: the largest
common minorant of the one-step laws of the
$8$ level-1 histories, at the finest
possible grain, has total mass $0$. **Any
minorization at transport scope must live on a quotient** — which is
exactly where §8's escape bites. This is the structural statement the
predecessor's open question did not contain.

**And it is a limitation, not a result.** What dies here is the
Birkhoff / Hilbert route *on the history tree*, where it dies for every
history tree under every weight law, carrying no transport-specific

### 11.3 The quotient minorization, and what it needs

Let $\Psi$ be a declared state abstraction. For a class $C$ and a step
count $N$, the largest $\delta$ for which a common $\nu$ exists is
exactly

$$\delta^{*}(C, N, \Psi) \;=\; \sum_{s} \min_{x \in C} P^{N}\!\left(x,\, \Psi^{-1}(s)\right),$$

and $\nu$ is that common minorant, renormalized. So
$\delta^{*} > 0$ iff some $\Psi$-class is reached in exactly $N$ steps
from *every* $x$ in $C$: a measurement that can come out either way.

**Proposition (coarsening).** *If $\Psi'$ coarsens $\Psi$ then
$\delta^{*}(C, N, \Psi') \ge \delta^{*}(C, N, \Psi)$.* For a
$\Psi'$-class $s' = \bigsqcup_i s_i$,
$\min_x \sum_i P^N(x, s_i) \ge \sum_i \min_x P^N(x, s_i)$; summing over
$s'$ gives the claim. Refinement can only *decrease* $\delta^{*}$.

The proposition is what makes a two-grain measurement decisive rather
than two data points, and it is checked cell by cell on every measured
row: the event $\times$ weight grain refines the kind $\times$ weight
grain (the event determines its kind, verified at
$0$ exceptions over
$3{,}969$ histories), and both refine the
holdings-profile grain on these blocks, so every row must satisfy
$\delta^{*}(\text{event}) \le \delta^{*}(\text{kind}) \le
\delta^{*}(\text{profile})$. Violations:
$0$ over
$6$ rows.

Two consequences the unit owns. $\delta^{*} = 0$ for the full class at
the primary grain implies $\delta^{*} = 0$ at *every refinement* of it,
including all six of the abstraction ladder's committed abstractions —
so no pinned abstraction unblocks the ladder. And $\delta^{*} = 1$ on a
block at the primary grain implies $\delta^{*} = 1$ at every
*coarsening* of it.

Two grains are declared — the primary kind $\times$ weight menu shape
and the control event $\times$ weight menu shape — and two horizon
conventions are printed, because naming one silently is the defect the
