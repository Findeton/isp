## Abstract

The Γ campaign's first stage does two things. It **terminalizes the
foundation**: seven facts about the transport grammar — the grammar
declaration, the census and the finite-horizon potentials at two
scopes, the relative-horizon kernels, the renewal ports, the escape,
the reopening witness, and the root-symmetry theorem — are re-derived
from one hash-pinned committed layer, in exact arithmetic, at a family
of $243{,}769$ histories, and gated against the committed values rather
than quoted from them. And it **attempts the one construction the
predecessor named and did not run**: operator-level minorization of the
positive backward recursion.

The attempt returns a two-sided answer, and the two sides are the same
fact seen twice. On the history tree there is no minorization at all,
and not for want of depth: every column of every level operator has
exactly one nonzero entry, so the projective diameter is infinite, the
Birkhoff contraction coefficient is $1$ at every level, and the Doeblin
constant is $0$ for every $N$ by disjointness of supports. Any
minorization must therefore live on a quotient — which is exactly where
the escape bites.

On the quotient it does land, and it lands hard. The renewal class
`R-SIG` decomposes by the **holdings profile**
$(\lvert \mathrm{hold}(A)\rvert, \lvert \mathrm{hold}(B)\rvert)$, and
each block is an **exact atom**: at matched horizon and the declared
primary grain the largest common minorant has total mass exactly $1$,
so the one-step law is the *same* measure at every point of the block,
with an explicit $\nu$ on a small support. The menu-exact port `R-MENU`
is not a special class at all — it is the first block of that ladder.

And then the ladder blocks. The holdings profile decreases at
**zero** transitions of the family: it is a monotone non-decreasing
coordinate, written into the menu by the delivery budget
$1/4 \div \lvert \mathrm{hold}(a) \rvert$. So the atoms exist,
there are many of them, and the process climbs past each one. On both
of its widest windows the full class has minorization constant exactly
$0$; the hitting infimum into every block is exactly $0$; the return
infimum into their union is $0$ on a window one full depth wider than
the predecessor's; on the rows where the lookahead exceeds the
attained maximum, returning takes up to two steps and $84$ histories
never return inside the cap at all. The successor engine is therefore
**blocked at a named and measured fact — the monotone holdings
ladder** — where before it was merely open.

What is deliberately not claimed: that no uniform bound exists at any
depth. Every Arm B row is a finite-cap measurement, the caps are
printed, and the single row where a cross-profile $\delta^{*}$ turns
positive — on a window holding $105$ of the class's points — is
printed beside the rest rather than dropped.

## 6. Fact 3 — the relative-horizon kernels

$$k_r(e \mid h) \;=\; \frac{q(e \mid h)\, G(h + e,\, r - 1)}{G(h,\, r)}$$

Said before the numbers: $\sum_e k_r(e \mid h) = 1$ is an **identity of
the construction**, because $G(h, r)$ is defined as the numerator's sum
and $k_r$ divides by it. Cut-additivity of the chained measure follows
**by induction** from that identity — a chain of probability kernels
has cut mass $1$ at every cut — so it is *disclosed* here, not gated as
a measurement. What the horizon normalization buys is exactly that; the
raw path weight is not a measure at all, its cut masses being the root
potentials themselves.

The properness identity is verified as a regression tripwire on the
menu bookkeeping over $279{,}067$ (history, horizon) pairs at
$r = 1 \ldots 7$, with $0$ violations.

**The substantive properness gate is strict positivity**, since a zero
denominator is the only way the identity can break. Kernel entries
$\le 0$: $0$. Potentials $\le 0$: $0$.

The negative control fires: a deliberately mis-normalized kernel —
dividing by $G(h, 2)$ where $G(h, 3)$ belongs — breaks properness at
$69$ of $69$ tested histories. The properness gate can
fail, and this is the input that makes it.

**Window artefacts, disclosed, with the off-root prefix convention
stated.** A row at horizon $r$ is taken over the window of histories
$h$ with $\lvert h \rvert + r \le 7$; at $r = 7$ that window contains
the root and nothing else. Every claim in this unit about behaviour
across horizons is taken on the **off-root prefix** of the window
sequence — the horizons whose window still contains a history of
positive length — and a terminal value on a root-only window is
reported as a window artefact, never as contraction, shrinkage or

Two ports, because the port is not unique and reporting one would
decide the question by fiat.

- **R-SIG.** Every actor's non-superseded holdings is the same
  singleton $\{v\}$; no live proposals, no components, no merge pairs.
  The literal $\sigma$-level port: the delivery-free $\sigma$ sees the
  non-superseded token only, because the proposal enumerator skips
  superseded versions.
- **R-MENU.** R-SIG and, in addition, $\mathrm{hold}(a) = \{v\}$

  the delivery enumerator ranges over the *whole* holdings set, so

### 11.2 The tree obstruction

Write the backward recursion as a level operator,

$$(M_n g)(h) \;=\; \sum_e q(e \mid h)\, g(h + e),$$

a nonnegative matrix whose rows are the depth-$n$ histories and whose
columns are the depth-$(n{+}1)$ histories, so that $G(\cdot, r)$ at
level $n$ is $M_n M_{n+1} \cdots \mathbb{1}$. Birkhoff's theorem gives a
contraction of the Hilbert projective metric with coefficient
$\tanh(\Delta/4)$, finite only if the matrix has no zero pattern
separating a pair of columns.

**Measured:** of the $243{,}768$ columns — every non-root history of the
family — $243{,}768$ have exactly one nonzero entry, the history's unique
parent, and $0$ have any other number. Unique parenthood is the
defining property of a history tree.

**Exhibited:** two rows at the same level and one column under each
give the minor

$$\begin{pmatrix} 1/4 & 0 \\ 0 & 1/4 \end{pmatrix}$$

whose off-diagonal entries are exactly $0$, so the cross-ratio is
$+\infty$; hence $\Delta(M_n) = \infty$ and $\tanh(\Delta/4) = 1$ at
every level.

> **The Birkhoff contraction coefficient of the backward recursion is
> $1$ — not by a cap and not by a numerical accident, but by unique
> parenthood.**

The same fact kills the history-level Doeblin bound outright: for
$x \ne y$ at the same depth, the supports of $P^N(x, \cdot)$ and
$P^N(y, \cdot)$ are disjoint sets of descendants, so the largest common
minorant is the zero measure and $\delta = 0$ for every $N$. **Any
minorization at transport scope must live on a quotient** — which is
exactly where §8's escape bites. This is the structural statement the
predecessor's open question did not contain.

### 11.3 The quotient minorization, and what it needs

Let $\Psi$ be a declared state abstraction. For a class $C$ and a step
count $N$, the largest $\delta$ for which a common $\nu$ exists is
exactly

$$\delta^{*}(C, N, \Psi) \;=\; \sum_{s} \min_{x \in C} P^{N}\!\left(x,\, \Psi^{-1}(s)\right),$$

and $\nu$ is that common minorant, renormalized. So
$\delta^{*} > 0$ iff some $\Psi$-class is reached in exactly $N$ steps
from *every* $x$ in $C$: a measurement that can come out either way.

Two grains are declared — the primary $13$-class kind $\times$ weight
menu shape and the control $113$-class event $\times$ weight menu shape
— and two horizon conventions are printed, because naming one silently
is the defect the predecessor's round convicted. **(H7)** the horizon-7
chain from the root, in which a history at depth $d$ steps under
$k_{7-d}$; **(MATCHED)** the matched-horizon chain, in which every $x$
steps under the same $k_N, k_{N-1}, \ldots, k_1$ regardless of depth.

content at all. What the widest windows say is the reportable fact;
what the narrow ones say is a cap artefact, printed as one.

The obstruction is
neither the horizon nor the cap: it is the **holdings profile**. Two
R-SIG points with different profiles carry different delivery-menu
weights — the delivery budget is $1/4$ divided by
$\lvert \mathrm{hold}(a) \rvert$, so the superseded remainder is
*written into the menu* — and their $N$-step laws therefore share no
$\Psi$-class at all — until, at $N = 3$, three steps are enough to
reach one, on a window too narrow to carry the claim.

### 11.6 Why the ladder does not regenerate

**(i) The return into R-SIG itself**, on a window one full depth wider
than the predecessor's — which tested $\lvert h \rvert + N \le 5$

## 15. What the successor inherits

1. **The foundation is terminalizable and terminalized.** Seven facts,
