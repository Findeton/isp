## 1. The question, and the anchor observation

The pinned dynamics advances a FRONT. $H_a[N]$ takes a total record $(n, m)$ —
a count $n(x)$ of division events already committed at each record site, and a
matter address register $m$ — to $(n + N,\; m + w[N, n])$. The geometry record
$s = \{n_\ell(x)\}$, the count of division events in the record interval
between $x$ and $x + \ell$, is background: it enters the drag through the
weight $\Lambda(s)$ and is never written.

Refinement by accumulation is the hypothesis that a continuum could be reached
by letting the geometry record grow: more events in every interval, a finer
grain, a rescaled metric approaching a limit. That hypothesis needs a move that
writes into $s$ the way $H_a[N]$ writes into $n$. This unit asks whether the
pinned grammar contains one.

The anchor observation is that it does not, and the unit's first job is to
measure that rather than assert it.

> H_a[N] leaves the geometry record bit-identical at 2376 of 2376 (rule, record, lapse, register) instances, and the s-image of the whole pinned move monoid is a single point at depth 4, against 5 points when one geometry-writing generator is added.

The second half is the part that could have come out otherwise. The orbit
instrument expands the monoid generated by $H_a[N]$, $H_a[N]^{-1}$ and
$D_a[v]$ breadth-first to depth 4 — 341 visited nodes, no truncation — and
records the set of geometry records it reaches. It reaches one. Given one
extra generator that writes $+1$ into every interval count, the same
instrument reaches five. The singleton is a measurement of the pinned move
set, not a property of the instrument.

## 3. The atoms, and what the pinned grammar can express

A geometry-record-advancing move writes
$$n_\ell(x) \;\longmapsto\; n_\ell(x) + \Delta_\ell(x),$$
and the question is what $\Delta$ may be built from. The pinned sources answer
it twice, and both answers are quoted verbatim and gate-verified against the
files on disk. The HA paper records that GW1 section 1.2 "permits event counts
and record adjacency explicitly", and that the drag itself "has exactly two
ingredients: the front tilt $n(x+e) - n(x)$, a difference of committed
division-event counts, and the eventwise lapse value $N(x)$."

That licenses twelve atoms at a link $(x, \ell)$, in two families:

| family | atoms |
|---|---|
| LIN | $1$, $N(x)$, $N(x+\ell)$, $n(x)$, $n(x+\ell)$, $n_\ell(x)$ |
| BIL | $N(x)n(x)$, $N(x)n(x+\ell)$, $N(x)n_\ell(x)$, $N(x+\ell)n(x)$, $N(x+\ell)n(x+\ell)$, $N(x+\ell)n_\ell(x)$ |

The front tilt $n(x+\ell) - n(x)$ and the lapse tilt $N(x+\ell) - N(x)$ lie in
the LIN span and need no separate atom. A thirteenth atom is declared as the
expressibility gate's negative control: the site's index in the declared
enumeration, a held-out chart label, which is on no permitted list and is
excluded. The gate therefore admits 12 of 13 and can fail.

The census above is the design space. The remaining question is whether
anything expressible from the pinned objects narrows it. Seven selectors are

| selector | status | fiber |
|---|---|---|

| `SEL-CHART` — chart equivariance | PINNED (C-CHART) | 4096 |
| `SEL-CLOSURE` — the diagonal sector survives the move | DECLARED HERE | 4 |
| `SEL-COMMUTE-AXIS` — commutes with $H_a[N]$ for `A-axis` | DECLARED HERE | 0 |
| `SEL-COMMUTE-INSERT` — commutes with $H_a[N]$ for `A-insert` | DECLARED HERE | 0 |
| `SEL-JOINT` — closure preservation and commutation together | DECLARED HERE | 0 |

No pinned selector cuts the fiber to one. That is the third box-independent
statement, and it is what makes the verdict a blocked verdict rather than a
count: the pinned clauses constrain the design space and never determine a
member of it.

**The closure selector, and what survives it.** `A-axis` is I7's record-native
rule, whose weight $\operatorname{diag}(1/n_{e_j})$ is read from the axis
interval counts alone and which closes exactly on the five records whose
readout is diagonal. Its sector survives a move exactly when
$\Delta_{e_1+e_2} = \Delta_{e_1} + \Delta_{e_2}$ at every site, and the movers
that satisfy that are the dilations:
$$\Delta_\ell(x) = c\,n_\ell(x)\quad\text{and}\quad
\Delta_\ell(x) = c\,N(x)\,n_\ell(x),\qquad c \in \{1, 2\}.$$
Four movers, in two structurally different families. No transfer move from the
lapse or the front survives. The link-uniform ones — the constant, the source
lapse, the source front — add $\Delta$ to $n_{e_1+e_2}$ while adding $2\Delta$
to $n_{e_1} + n_{e_2}$, driving the cross term $q_{12}$ negative at a fixed
rate; the target-endpoint one would need
$N(x + e_1 + e_2) = N(x + e_1) + N(x + e_2)$ at every site, which the declared
lapse family does not satisfy.

> The strongest declared selector, closure preservation, leaves a fiber of 4 movers; commuting advancing movers number 0 at 9 of 11 drag rules.

**The theorem.** The commutation selector asks whether writing the geometry and
advancing the front can be done in either order. Writing $G$ for the move and
$H = H_a[N]$ for the pinned advance, the defect of $G H$ against $H G$ splits:

$$\delta s \;=\; \Delta[N,\, n+N,\, s] - \Delta[N,\, n,\, s], \qquad
\delta m \;=\; w\bigl[N, n; \Lambda(s)\bigr] - w\bigl[N, n; \Lambda(s+\Delta)\bigr].$$

The first vanishes for every declared lapse exactly when $\Delta$ has no front
dependence. The second vanishes exactly when the rule's weight does not see the
move — that is, when $\Delta_\ell = 0$ on every link the weight reads.

> **Theorem (static geometry).** Let $r$ be any of the 9 declared drag rules
> whose weight reads at least one interval count. Then NO admissible advancing
> mover in the declared design space commutes with $H_a[N]$ for every declared
> lapse profile; the only commuting mover is $\Delta \equiv 0$, which does not
> advance. In particular the
> fiber is empty for the record-native closing rule `A-axis` and for the
> metric-inserted rule `A-insert` alike, and the joint fiber with closure
> preservation is empty.

The 9-rule scope is a census and not an extrapolation from two samples. The
commutation requirement depends on a rule only through its READ-LINK SET — the
links whose interval count its weight consults — and the 11 declared rules
realise exactly three read-link sets: the empty set at the two count-blind
rules, the two axis links at `A-axis` and `B-axis`, and all three links at the
remaining seven. The measurement covers both nonempty sets, and both fibers are
empty. The read-link table is carried in the receipt and a declared falsifier
that hides one rule from it kills the census gate.

The mechanism is I7's own record-IS-metric fact, biting from the other side.
Because the record and the metric are one datum in two coordinate systems, and
because the rule that closes computes the count-matrix inverse, there is no
background geometry to move: any motion of $s$ is motion of the law. Requiring
the law not to notice forces the motion to zero. For `A-axis` the reason is
visible directly, and the algebra is stated here rather than measured: forcing
$\Delta_{e_1} = 0$ across the probe set already annihilates every coefficient,
because the atoms restricted to a single link are independent over the declared
records, lapses and fronts.

The reading is two-sided. The exception is exactly the two count-blind rules,
`A-chart` and `B-chart`, whose weights are constant; they commute with anything.
They also close on exactly one record, `G-FLAT`, whose counts $(1,1,2)$ are
what makes the chart identity equal the inverse metric — and any advancing move
destroys that. So the exception buys nothing.

The commutation defect is computed twice, by literal composition of the two
maps on the triple state $(s, n, m)$ and by an independently built closed form.

> The commutation defect computed by literal composition and by the independent closed form agrees at 2916 of 2916 cells.

## 7. The rescaled trajectories

The pin asks the accumulation question for every admissible mover, and the
census leaves 1232 of them. Nine are named as the census's structural classes
and carried through the trajectory measurement, along six declared schedules
drawn from the pinned lapse family, at all nine records, at two declared
normalizations, to horizon $t = 20$, in exact rationals throughout.

| mover | $\Delta_\ell(x)$ | reading |
|---|---|---|
| `M-NONE` | $0$ | the pinned forced move |
| `M-CLOSED` | $N(x) + N(x+\ell)$ | closed interval: an event at either endpoint is in it |
| `M-SOURCE` | $N(x)$ | half-open $[x, x+\ell)$ |
| `M-TARGET` | $N(x+\ell)$ | half-open $(x, x+\ell]$ |
| `M-FRONT` | $n(x)$ | the interval gains the source's whole committed count |
| `M-TILT` | $n(x+\ell) - n(x)$ | the drag's own ingredient used as a geometry writer |
| `M-DILATE` | $n_\ell(x)$ | record dilation |
| `M-LAPDIL` | $N(x)\,n_\ell(x)$ | lapse-driven dilation |
| `M-FIAT` | $1$ | the foreign control |

The first four are the four boundary conventions for "an event in the
interval": both endpoints, source only, target only, neither. Nothing pinned
chooses among them. That is the free choice at its most concrete, and it is
not a formality — the four give different geometries, and the rest of this
section together with section 9 measures how far the difference reaches.

The normalizations are the two ways of counting accumulated events:
`NORM-FRONT`, the division events committed at sites, $\sum_{\tau \le t}
\sum_x N_\tau(x)$; and `NORM-INT`, the division events written into intervals,
$\sum_{x,\ell}\bigl(n_\ell^{(t)}(x) - n_\ell^{(0)}(x)\bigr)$. The rescaled
geometry is $\hat q^{(t)} = q^{(t)}/E(t)$.

Convergence is typed by classifying the exact numerator and denominator
sequences as polynomial (certified by a vanishing finite difference, which
makes Newton reconstruction exact on the window) or as $c\,r^t + k$ (certified
by exact reproduction of every window value), and comparing. A sequence the
classifier declines is never given a limit.

> Across 972 trajectory cells at horizon t = 20, 531 converge, 117 diverge, 108 have an undefined normalization and 216 are unclassified, in 27 distinct exact limit classes.

The declinations are characterized rather than tolerated: all 216 lie either at
one of the two non-stationary schedules, where the lapse profile changes with
$t$ and the counts are quasi-polynomial rather than polynomial, or at the
lapse-modulated dilation, whose growth ratio $1 + N(x)$ is site-dependent so the
record is not a single geometric sequence. There is no unexplained residue. The
108 undefined cells are `M-NONE` and `M-TILT` at `NORM-INT`, where nothing is
written into any interval and the denominator is zero — the normalization is
undefined, not the limit. And the classification is stable under doubling the
horizon to $t = 40$: 648 recheck cells, 0 disagreements.

**Divergence, characterized.** The 117 divergent cells are of two kinds.
`M-DILATE` multiplies every count by a fixed factor per step and `M-LAPDIL` by
a site-dependent one, so $q^{(t)}$ grows geometrically while `NORM-FRONT` grows
linearly: exponential divergence, with the certified ratio carried in the
receipt. `M-FRONT` writes the front's own value and `M-TILT` writes its tilt;
both are linear in $t$ along the stationary schedules, so $q^{(t)}$ is
quadratic against a linear denominator: polynomial divergence of degree 1.

**The normalization moves the verdict.**

> The convergence type changes with the declared normalization at 216 of the 486 (mover, schedule, record) cells.

The sharpest instance is the dilation, which diverges exponentially under
`NORM-FRONT` and converges under `NORM-INT` to the initial record normalized by
its own total count. "The rescaled geometry converges" is a statement about the
declared per-event denominator, and this unit reports the denominator with the
claim.

**The limit that forgets the mover.** The dilations remember the record they
started from. `M-DILATE` writes $\Delta_\ell = n_\ell$ whatever the lapse is
doing, so its trajectory is schedule-independent, $s^{(t)} = 2^t s^{(0)}$, and
its rescaled limit at `NORM-INT` is
$$\hat q \;\longrightarrow\; \frac{q^{(0)}}{\sum_{x,\ell} n_\ell^{(0)}(x)},$$
a different value for every record whose initial data is not proportional to
another's. The additive movers do the opposite.

> The exact rescaled limit (1/27, -1/54, 1/27) is reached by M-CLOSED, M-FIAT, M-FRONT, M-SOURCE, M-TARGET -- including the foreign fiat move.

That class holds 90 of the 972 cells, at all six schedules, at `NORM-INT`, and
it is independent of the starting record. The mechanism is exact and is a
statement about the declared link set rather than about any mover: if every
interval count grows at the same rate, the limit metric is the one that makes
all three declared links the same length, and at $d = 2$ with links
$e_1, e_2, e_1+e_2$ that metric is proportional to
$$\begin{pmatrix} 2 & -1 \\ -1 & 2\end{pmatrix},$$
the hexagonal Gram matrix, normalized by $\lvert X\rvert \cdot
\lvert\mathcal{L}\rvert$ to $(1/27, -1/54, 1/27)$.

So the accumulation route, run to its limit, produces a geometry fixed by the
declaration of which links exist — and it produces the same one whether the
events arrive from the lapse at both endpoints, from one endpoint, from the
front, or from nothing at all by fiat. Convergence of the rescaled geometry is
not evidence that the mover is right. It is compatible with the mover being
foreign.

## 8. The foreign control

The pin requires a hand-declared external growth move to be flagged foreign by
the same audit that judges the expressible ones. `M-FIAT` adds $+1$ to every
interval count per step, by fiat. The coupling instrument measures, for each
mover, whether its increment varies as each pinned field varies over the
declared probe sets.

| mover | reads $N$ | reads $n$ | reads $s$ | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `M-CLOSED`, `M-SOURCE`, `M-TARGET` | yes | no | no | COUPLED |
| `M-FRONT`, `M-TILT` | no | yes | no | COUPLED |
| `M-DILATE`, `M-LAPDIL` | yes/yes | yes | yes | COUPLED |
| `M-FIAT` | no | no | no | FOREIGN |
| `M-NONE` | no | no | no | blind, but not advancing |

> The coupling instrument flags 1 of 1 declared external growth moves FOREIGN, and reports 7 coupled movers, every one of them class-(iii) unmotivated.

Two gradings are kept apart, and the control is what proves the audit can
distinguish them. FOREIGN is measured — blind to every pinned field while still
advancing. UNMOTIVATED is the choice-inventory verdict — class (iii). The fiat
move is both. Every coupled mover is the second and not the first. The identity
is blind and is excluded from the foreign class by a measured property, that it
does not advance, and never by its name.

The audit's honesty has a price, and the unit reports it. The foreign move is
not merely admitted by the coupling grading — it is the best-behaved mover in
the trajectory census (derived in text from the receipt's trajectory table:
`M-FIAT` converges at 108 of its 108 cells and diverges at none).

## 15. Opens

**THE GEOMETRY-UPDATE LAW — OPEN, and now with requirements.** A successor that
supplies one must carry, at minimum:

1. **A forcing, not a declaration.** The law must be forced by an object the
   successor's pin carries, and the forcing must be exhibited as a clause that
   cuts the fiber. This unit measures that no clause of the pinned layer does,
   and that the strongest declared selector leaves four.
2. **A resolution of the boundary convention.** Whether an event committed at
   an endpoint lies in the interval is a four-way choice the pinned semantics
   does not fix, and the four choices are not equivalent.
3. **A discriminator the limit can see.** The rescaled limit measured here is
   shared by five movers including a foreign one, and the one-step readout does
   not separate four of them. A successor claiming its law from a limit must
   exhibit a readout that separates its law from the fiat move.
4. **A stated relation to the dynamics.** Commutation forces the identity; a
   successor that wants motion must say what it puts in commutation's place and
   characterize the resulting defect.

Also open, and untouched here: whether a geometry-writing move exists in any
grammar deeper than the pinned layer (the v10/v11 renewal grammar is outside
this pin and was not read); whether a refinement that adds SITES rather than
events behaves differently; and whether the arena's own refinement — rather
than accumulation at a fixed arena — reaches a continuum.
