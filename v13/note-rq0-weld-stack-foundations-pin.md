spaces, show that every represented scalar endomorphism is eventually
periodic. If its represented value is $\lambda\in\mathbb C$, prove

$$
\lambda=0
\quad\text{or}\quad
\lambda\text{ is a root of unity}.
$$

and one cannot occur.

eigenvalue is a root of unity and that the nilpotent part at every nonzero
eigenvalue vanishes. State carefully what can remain at eigenvalue zero.

### Scope

dagger categories or category theory fail. The theorem attacks only finiteness
of the arrow set under unrestricted categorical composition.
