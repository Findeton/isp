## Overall verdict

**REJECT.**

The operator-subspace construction really is a small involutive quantaloid, both displayed residuals have the stated universal properties, and—after supplying a genuinely defined source category—the paper's variance convention for a distributor and its citation of the Shen--Zhang Isbell theorem are correct. The finite commutative central-corner result in the correspondence bicategory also survives independent reconstruction for every finite test algebra and every unitary 2-cell. Relative tensor product is correctly used only as correspondence composition, and the paper honestly declines the Weld-stack and generic derived-overlap rungs.

Those successes do not establish the provisional headline. The process-probe object class is not exactly or even type-uniformly defined; effects and general Kraus supports are not endomorphism arrows, and “canonically generated” is not a mathematical construction. Even if that defect is repaired, the Isbell layer is a formal completion of the already one-sided observability assignment: it inserts every derived observability algebra as a probe, obtains the principal concept by Yoneda closure, and then extracts a concept algebra from the record-side component alone. It therefore does not establish the claimed mutually determining physical operation/record ontology or pass the necessity and discriminator gates.

More decisively, the raw chart and record interface are not constructed. A general UCP Heisenberg transport does not carry a projector resolution to a projector resolution; the paper chooses no coherent transport when several are admitted; and projectors belonging to the individual algebras in a concept's record family need not lie in their intersection `A_c`. Pairwise commutation does not fix that containment failure. Consequently `P_c`, `Rec(c) -> Proj(A_c)`, the record-compatible subgroupoid, and the earned Weld datum are undefined at the claimed scope. The notation `[Chart_raw/G_D]` additionally does not specify an orbit, action, or homotopy quotient, does not state how gauge stabilizers are retained, and does not prove that the physical automorphism action descends through the quotient.

The selected-boundary/projector/transport no-smuggling obligation from the pin is also unmet. The paper proves only that an external support field is absent from a fixed observability formula. It does not control changes that encode the same preferred factor in the admitted boundary types, the W3 projector package, or the transport grammar. The identity-incidence test and the clean/mixed controls do not test that mutation. On the binding cumulative ladder, only `RQ0-L0-REPRESENTABLE-W3-SKETCH` survives this review. Narrower formal results survive above it, especially the quantaloid theorem and the central-corner theorem, but they do not independently constitute a registered rung.

## Ranked findings

### F1 — FATAL — `Proc_D` is not a defined enriched category at the paper's exact scope

Definition 8.1 requires every probe object to be a pair `p=(y,U)` with

`U in Q_D(y,y)`, an endomorphism subspace.

It then says that `U` is “canonically generated by a finite family of represented admitted endomorphism/effect/Kraus supports,” or is a derived observability algebra. This is not a type-correct generation rule:

1. An effect on `y` lies in `S_D(y,1)`, not `S_D(y,y)`.
2. The Kraus support of a channel `y -> x` lies in `S_D(y,x)`, not generally in `S_D(y,y)`.
3. No operation such as `f^dagger f`, `K^dagger K`, typed closure under composites, or generated endomorphism algebra is selected.
4. “Canonically generated” is not defined as a predicate or a function, so it does not determine the promised object class.

The discrete hom formula would define a valid `Q_D`-category for any actual typed set of probes, but the paper has not supplied that set. Therefore Proposition 8.3 does not have a defined source category, and the hypotheses of the imported Isbell theorem are not established for the delivered construction.

This finding is fatal to `RQ0-L0-ADDRESSABLE-W3-CONCEPTS` and every later cumulative rung. It does not damage Theorem 7.2 itself.

### F2 — MAJOR — the fixed points are a relabelled one-sided observability completion, not an independently grounded operation/record mutuality result

Grant a charitable repair of F1 by retaining only well-typed endomorphism probes. The formal Isbell construction then works, but its physical interpretation does not.

For each W3 diagram `W`, Definition 8.1 expressly inserts

`p_W=(y,A_W(y))`

as a probe object. Definition 8.2 sets incidence to the residual

`A_W'(y) \swarrow U`,

whose identity test is exactly `U subseteq A_W'(y)`. Proposition 8.5 applies Yoneda closure to the already inserted `p_W`. Its conclusion is therefore the standard principal-element consequence of that insertion: the relevant W3 diagrams are precisely those whose one-sided observability algebras contain `A_W(y)`, and their intersection returns `A_W(y)` because `W` itself is in the family.

The later extraction confirms the one-sidedness. `A_c` is computed solely by intersecting `A_W(y)` over the record-side predicate `1_y <= lambda(W,y)`; the process-side presheaf `mu` contributes no independent executable operation semantics. The paper itself says the probes are mathematical support subspaces and are not separately executable interventions. Thus the Isbell pair is mathematically mutually closed, but the claimed physical “operation/record concept” adds no discriminator beyond the original observability assignment.

The four gates fail as follows:

- **Referent:** arbitrary support probes have, at most, a mathematical-span referent, not an admitted intervention referent.
- **Necessity:** the addressable object `(W,y,A_W,E)` and all symmetric copies can be retained without the Isbell completion; no theorem needs the new primitive.
- **No-smuggling:** the W-specific answer is inserted as the probe `p_W`, while the admitted probe class remains undefined and selectable.
- **Discriminator:** the positive “principal concept” control reduces to `U subseteq A_W`; there is no example in which the enriched fixed point changes a physical classification relative to the one-sided algebra and split.

The fixed-point category is a legitimate formal concept completion once F1 is repaired. What is not earned is the paper's ontology of genuinely mutually determining physical operation/record concepts.

### F3 — FATAL — `P_c` and the partial record functor are not defined by the preceding constructions

Definitions 9.1 and 9.2 add to a concept a “family of transported W3 projector resolutions” and assert

`Bool<P_c> -> Proj(A_c)`.

Three independent obstructions defeat that assertion.

1. **UCP transport does not preserve projectors.** The observability construction deliberately uses general UCP Heisenberg maps. For a projection `P`, `Phi^*(P)` is generally a positive effect, not an idempotent. A depolarizing channel already sends a rank-one projection to a nonprojective scalar effect. Therefore the transports used to define `A_W(y)` do not supply transported projector resolutions.
2. **There is no selected or coherent transport.** `Tr_D(y,W)` is a family, possibly with several channels. The algebra uses all their images, but the chart definition never chooses a transport for a record resolution or proves independence/coherence among the choices.
3. **Individual projectors need not lie in the concept intersection.** By Definition 8.4,

   `A_c = intersection_W A_W(y)`.

   A projector transported from one `W` is known, at best, to lie in that one `A_W(y)`. It need not lie in every other member's algebra and hence need not lie in `A_c`. Even if all such projectors happen to commute, commutation does not imply membership in the intersection. For example, in a common diagonal finite algebra, one may have `A_1=C*(P,Q)` and `A_2=C*(Q,R)` with commuting `P,Q,R`; their intersection can be `C*(Q)`, while `P` and `R` remain outside it.

The commuting-projector condition is sensible only after actual projectors in `A_c` have been obtained. It cannot create them. The claimed Boolean algebra, its inclusion in `Proj(A_c)`, and hence the domain and object action of `Rec` are missing.

Conditional audit: if one starts instead with a subgroupoid whose objects already carry a commuting projector family inside `A_c` and whose arrows are `*`-isomorphisms pulling those projectors back, the stated identity and composition equations do define a contravariant Boolean functor, and handle renaming gives a natural isomorphism. That conditional lemma is all that survives. It does not repair the delivered chart object.

F3 is fatal to the raw record-bearing chart groupoid, `Rec_partial`, the Weld datum of Definition 11.1, record-bearing overlap classifications, and `RQ0-L0-WELD-QUANTALOID-CHARTS` as delivered.

### F4 — MAJOR — the presentation-gauge quotient and residual physical-symmetry action are not constructed

Definition 9.3 writes

`[Chart_raw/G_D]`

without defining its objects, arrows, composition, or universal property. For a group acting on a groupoid, at least three inequivalent readings are available: a strict orbit groupoid, an action/Grothendieck groupoid, and a stack or homotopy quotient. They treat stabilizers differently. The paper does not choose one.

The ambiguity is load-bearing here:

1. Raw arrows are already ambient process-sketch isomorphisms, a class that can contain presentation changes and physical automorphisms. The paper does not specify the subgroup/factorization that prevents double counting when `G_D` is imposed again.

The elementary no-selection theorem is correct: a transitive action on at least two candidates has no invariant member. It justifies retaining the physical orbit and symmetry arrows. It does not fill the missing quotient definition.

## Independent positive audit

### 1. Operator-support quantaloid and residuals

Let `S_D(x,y)` be the finite-dimensional complex hom-space of the least represented linear dagger subcategory defined in Section 7.

For each pair `(x,y)`, `Sub_C(S_D(x,y))` is a complete lattice. The join of any family is the span of its union, the meet is intersection, and the bottom is the zero subspace. Because `S_D` is closed under composition, for `U:x->y` and `V:y->z`,

`V odot U = span{vu}`

is a subspace of `S_D(x,z)`. Bilinearity gives arbitrary-join preservation separately in both variables. Associativity follows by expanding both sides to the span of all triple products; `span{I_x}` is a two-sided unit. Adjoint is an order-preserving bijection between opposite homs, reverses composition, preserves arbitrary spans, fixes identities and squares to the identity. Finitely many boundary objects and set-sized hom-lattices make the quantaloid small relative to the fixed universe.

For `U:x->y`, `V:y->z` and `H:x->z`, define

`H \swarrow U = {v in S_D(y,z) : vU subseteq H}`

and

`V \searrow H = {u in S_D(x,y) : Vu subseteq H}`.

Each is a linear subspace. Directly,

`V odot U subseteq H`

if and only if every `v in V` satisfies `vU subseteq H`, if and only if

`V subseteq H \swarrow U`.

The analogous elementwise argument gives

`V odot U subseteq H` if and only if `U subseteq V \searrow H`.

Thus both residual formulas and their universal properties are correct. No variance reversal occurs here.

The dormant-exclusion proposition has only least-closure force: raw matrices absent from every generator and its closure are absent. Linear combinations inside an admitted support can still be non-executable, as the paper correctly acknowledges. The theorem is about operator support, not physical execution.

### 2. Distributor types, action inequalities and Isbell theorem

Under the paper's convention, a component

`Phi(w,p): |p| -> |w|`

has the Shen--Zhang type `Phi(p,w)` for a distributor `Proc_D -> W3_D`. With homs typed `A(a,a'):|a|->|a'|`, the combined distributor action is

`W3(w,w') odot Phi(w,p) odot Proc(p',p) <= Phi(w',p')`.

The source is `|p'|` and the target is `|w'|`, so the displayed orientation is correct. In discrete enriched categories the left side is nonbottom only for `w=w'` and `p=p'`, reducing to the unit laws. Unequal extents are assigned the correctly typed bottom arrow. There is no residual or action-law variance error.

For a genuinely defined small distributor `phi:A->B`, Shen--Zhang define

`phi_up(mu)=phi \swarrow mu`,

`phi_down(lambda)=lambda \searrow phi`,

prove `phi_up` left adjoint to `phi_down`, and prove that the fixed points of `phi_down phi_up` form a skeletal complete `Q`-category. The paper imports exactly that theorem. Its formulas for the closure monad and fixed-point pair have the correct order.

For a Yoneda presheaf `Yp_W`, the component formula reduces, in the discrete source, to

`(phi_up Yp_W)(W',y)=A_W'(y) \swarrow A_W(y)`.

All other probe terms are residuals by bottom and therefore top, so they do not change the meet. Hence

`1_y <= (phi_up Yp_W)(W',y)`

if and only if

`A_W(y) subseteq A_W'(y)`.

The identity-incidence test and the algebraic intersection argument in Proposition 8.5 are therefore correct after F1 is repaired. The adjunction identities also ensure that pairing `C_DYp_W` with `phi_up Yp_W` is fixed. The defect is not this algebra; it is the missing source definition and the physical overinterpretation identified in F1 and F2.

### 3. Finite correspondence bicategory and central-corner theorem

Define `Corr_fd` as follows:

- objects: finite-dimensional `C*`-algebras, including zero;
- 1-cells `A->B`: finite right Hilbert `B`-modules with a nondegenerate left `*`-representation of `A` by adjointable operators;
- 2-cells: unitary bimodule intertwiners;
- horizontal composition: interior tensor product;
- identity: the standard `A-A` correspondence.

Associators and unitors are the canonical unitaries. Restricting the usual adjointable 2-cells to unitaries gives a bicategory locally enriched in groupoids.

For `D=C^n`, a finite right Hilbert `D`-module is an `n`-tuple of finite-dimensional Hilbert spaces. An `X-D` correspondence is therefore an `n`-tuple of finite-dimensional nondegenerate representations of `X`; a unitary 2-cell is a coordinatewise unitary intertwiner.

Let `A=pD`, `B=qD`, `P=pqD`, and use the corner correspondences `pD:A->D` and `qD:B->D`. Extension along `pD` inserts zero coordinate fibers outside the support of `p`; extension along `qD` does the same outside `q`. For every finite test algebra `X`, a unitary

`alpha: u tensor_A pD -> v tensor_B qD`

forces both extended tuples to vanish outside `supp(p) intersection supp(q)` and identifies their `X`-representations on that intersection. The common tuple is exactly an `X-P` correspondence.

Conversely every `X-P` correspondence extends to such a cone. An arbitrary comparison unitary `alpha` does not add an unrepresented modulus: it can be absorbed into one leg, making the cone isomorphic to the canonical diagonal cone. Automorphism pairs satisfying the compatibility square reduce to the same unitary automorphism of the underlying `X-P` correspondence. This gives an equivalence of groupoids, not merely a bijection of isomorphism classes. Tensor precomposition preserves the coordinate restriction and the construction up to the canonical associators, so the equivalence is pseudonatural in every `X`.

Therefore Theorem 10.4 really represents the homotopy cone for all finite test algebras and unitary 2-cells. When `pq=0`, the zero algebra represents the unique zero cone; calling that the empty overlap is the paper's algebra/space convention. Relative tensor product is used only for composing 1-cells and is never falsely identified with the representing object.
