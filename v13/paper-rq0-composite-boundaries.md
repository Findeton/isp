## Abstract

The predecessor cycle proved that de-smuggling cannot be a one-boundary test.
A record manufactured by choosing a task to match a preselected measure is the
greatest element of the very record lattice any closure operator would act on,
and every closure fixes its own top; the block is law-independent. The
predecessor named the escape without opening it: compare several
independently declared tasks and the boundaries they induce.

This paper opens that scope in two halves, and reports exactly how far each
half reaches.

The first half declares how two earned boundaries compose. Under the product
law — local admitted operations on each factor, and for the composite effect
system the linear span of the simple tensors — the composite minimal boundary
factorizes and the operational classical core distributes exactly. The proof
is a support-space identity together with two hypotheses that are stated and
measured: each effect system contains the unit, and each core block indicator
is itself an admitted split. Neither is decorative; without the second the
theorem's "exactly" is false, and a three-dimensional effect system on four
atoms shows it. The conclusion is verified by exact subspace intersection on
every ordered pair of the nine committed fixtures with composite carrier
dimension at most twenty, sixty-five pairs, with the gap zero throughout; the
operator system whose core is trivial is not promoted by composition with any
readable factor — though that trivial core is itself relative to the declared
span reading, and under the ∗-algebra reading of the same declaration it is
not trivial at all.

Under a declared joint law the distribution fails, and the failure is
measured on ten declared joint tasks. For the four-outcome task built from
the committed maximally entangled objects the composite classical core has
four atoms while nothing at all is readable on either factor alone, so the
composite boundary does not factorize and the two partitions of the same atom
set differ maximally. The pre-registered reading of that gap is corrected by
its own controls: a classically correlated task with separable rank-two atoms
opens the gap, and so does a task all of whose atoms are rank-one product
projections. The gap alone is therefore a **joint-readability** witness. The
entanglement witness is the gap together with the atom classification, and
that classification is exact at every rank — every atom of every declared
task is decided by the exact partial transpose, which at this composite
dimension decides separability. A classifier reading only rank-one atoms
returns a false negative on an admitted task at the same dimension whose two
rank-two atoms are both entangled, and that task is run here.

The second half builds the arena, and the honest description of what it
tests is narrower than the one the pre-registration invited. Two task
families are declared with one overlap declared before either of them, and a
record is certified when it transports to the overlap without collision, when

## 2. The composite operational structure, declared

A **declared boundary** is a pair \((E,A)\): a finite direct sum \(A\) of
matrix blocks with centre \(Z(A)\), and a self-adjoint spanning set for the
effect system \(E\subseteq A\), with \(A\) its envelope. Its **admitted
splits** are the projections of \(E\cap Z(A)\), and its **core** is the
partition of the central atoms of \(A\) cut by them. For \(E=A\) this returns
the minimal central projections; for the hostile operator system, whose
intersection with the envelope centre has computed dimension one, it returns
the trivial core.

### Definition 2.1 — the composite `[FIN]`

Let \((E_A,A)\) and \((E_B,B)\) be declared boundaries. The **composite
carrier** is \(A\otimes B\), whose central atoms are the products
\(z_k\otimes z_l\). Two composite laws are declared.

- **The product law.** The admitted joint operations are the local ones
  together with the linear combinations of their simple tensors; the composite
  effect system is the **linear span** of the simple tensors
  \(E_A\otimes E_B\) — *not* the ∗-algebra they generate, which is a different
  and strictly larger declaration (§2.1). The admitted states are the product
  states.
- **A joint law.** A joint task family is declared; the composite effect
  system is the range \(A_P\) of the matched dephasing
  \(\mathcal D_P(\rho)=\sum_rP_r\rho P_r\) of its measure, obtained by exact
  elimination; where the committed fixtures supply them, entangled admitted
  states are declared with it.

### Definition 2.2 — the two sides of the distribution question `[FIN]`

\(\operatorname{Core}(A\otimes B)\) is the partition of the composite
boundary's central atoms cut by its admitted splits.
\(\operatorname{Core}(A)\otimes\operatorname{Core}(B)\) is the partition of
**the same** atoms cut by those admitted splits that are readable on one
factor alone, that is, of the form \(Q\otimes1\) or \(1\otimes Q\). Both are
partitions of one set, so the comparison is well typed and the **gap** is
their inequality, not a difference of two counts.

The definition is justified by that well-typedness alone. It is not justified
by an exhibit: measured across the declared family, no task has equal block
counts on the two sides with different partitions — every declared task that
opens the gap also has unequal counts. The definition is nonetheless the
right one, because a count-based comparison would be comparing two numbers
where the objects are two partitions of one set.

## 3. The product law: factorization and distribution

### Lemma 3.1 — minimal supports `[FIN]`

For subspaces \(V\subseteq A\), \(W\subseteq B\), \(X\subseteq A\),
\(Y\subseteq B\) of finite-dimensional spaces,

\[
(V\otimes W)\cap(X\otimes Y)=(V\cap X)\otimes(W\cap Y).
\]

**Proof.** Write \(t\) in a representation \(\sum_ia_i\otimes b_i\) of minimal
length; then \(\{a_i\}\) and \(\{b_i\}\) are each linearly independent, and
the spaces \(L(t)=\operatorname{span}\{a_i\}\) and
\(R(t)=\operatorname{span}\{b_i\}\) do not depend on the representation. For
any subspaces \(U,U'\) one has \(t\in U\otimes U'\) iff \(L(t)\subseteq U\)
and \(R(t)\subseteq U'\). Membership in both sides of the display therefore
reads \(L(t)\subseteq V\cap X\) and \(R(t)\subseteq W\cap Y\). \(\square\)

### Theorem 3.2 — product-law factorization `[FIN]`, `[PAIR-20]`

Assume each effect system contains the unit, and that each core block
indicator of each factor is itself an admitted split. Then under the product
law the composite boundary's admitted split algebra is exactly the one
generated by the local admitted splits, so

\[
\operatorname{Core}(A\otimes B)
=\operatorname{Core}(A)\otimes\operatorname{Core}(B),
\]

and the composite minimal boundary factorizes.

**Proof.** The centre of a finite direct sum tensor is the tensor of the
centres, \(Z(A\otimes B)=Z(A)\otimes Z(B)\), spanned by the products of
central atoms. By Lemma 3.1,

\[
(E_A\otimes E_B)\cap(Z(A)\otimes Z(B))
=(E_A\cap Z(A))\otimes(E_B\cap Z(B)),
\]

so every admitted composite split is a combination of products of admitted
local splits, and the composite cut is coarser than the product cut.
Conversely, since each effect system contains the unit, \(P\otimes1\) and
\(1\otimes Q\) are admitted whenever \(P\) and \(Q\) are, so the composite cut
refines the product cut; the two cuts therefore coincide. Under the second
hypothesis the block indicators of that common cut are themselves admitted,
so the projections among the admitted splits are exactly the unions of
products of local core blocks. \(\square\)

**Both hypotheses are measured, and the second is not vacuous.** Every
committed fixture is unital and has all its core block indicators admitted,
and so does every composite in the declared sweep. That the second hypothesis
is needed at all is shown by an effect system that violates it: on the
diagonal algebra of four atoms take the self-adjoint unital span of
\((1,1,1,1)\), \((1,1,0,0)\) and \((1,0,1,0)\), of computed dimension three.
Its projections cut the **discrete** partition, yet no singleton indicator
lies in it — all four are computed non-admitted. Without the hypothesis the
core blocks need not be admitted splits at all, and the theorem's "exactly"
fails. (The counterexample is the operator lens's, Cycle B′ panel.)

The identity of the display is verified by exact subspace intersection —
each side computed by its own construction and compared by dimension and
containment — on all thirty fixture pairs of composite carrier dimension at
most nine. The theorem's conclusion is then measured directly on **all
sixty-five ordered pairs** of the nine committed fixtures with composite
carrier dimension at most twenty, comparing the composite core with the
product of the two factor cores: the gap is zero on every pair, with no
exception. The remaining sixteen ordered pairs of the eighty-one exceed the
declared carrier cap and are carried by the theorem; six of those sixteen
were additionally measured directly at carrier twenty-five, and all six
distribute exactly (operator lens, Cycle B′ panel).

### 3.1 The hostile wing is not promoted

The operator system whose operational core is trivial under the declared span
reading — its intersection with its envelope's centre has computed dimension
one, only the scalars — must not acquire a nontrivial core by composition
with a readable factor. Ten declared composites are run, in both orders,
against the classical bit, the full matrix factor, the mixed control, the

What this control establishes is therefore relative to the declared reading:
it shows that composition does not promote the wing, not that the wing is
trivial in some reading-independent sense. Under the ∗-algebra reading the
wing's core is two before any composition occurs (§2.1).

## 4. The joint law: the gap, and what it witnesses

Ten joint tasks are declared on the two-factor carrier of dimension four,
each supplying a measure whose matched dephasing's range is the composite
boundary. The entangled members are built from the two **committed**
entangled objects: the Choi projection of the identity channel, whose

remaining two maximally entangled projections are obtained from the first by
the admitted **local** unitaries \(Z\otimes1\) and \(X\otimes1\); the four are
verified to be mutually orthogonal projections summing to the unit, each of
exact Schmidt rank two, each with maximally mixed local restriction.

### Definition 4.1 — the atom classification `[FIN]`

An atom of any rank is **entangled** when its partial transpose fails to be
positive semidefinite, decided exactly: the partial transpose is formed over
\(\mathbb Q(i)\) and its positivity is decided by the signs of all fifteen
principal minors, each an exact determinant. Failure of positivity implies
entanglement in every dimension; at this composite dimension positivity of
the partial transpose is equivalent to separability, so the verdict is exact
in both directions here. Where a rank-two atom is separable a **constructive**
certificate is also produced: a decomposition into two orthogonal rank-one
product projections, found by solving the binary quadratic form
\(\det(\alpha M_1+\beta M_2)\) on a basis of the atom's range. An atom of any
rank is a **product projection** when it equals the tensor of the supports of
its two partial traces, both computed exactly.

Across the ten declared tasks all thirty-two composite-core atoms are
classified: twenty-five of rank one and seven of higher rank. No verdict is a
default. On rank-one atoms the partial-transpose verdict and the exact
Schmidt rank agree without exception.

declared task families with their earned boundaries and cores, and a declared

\[
\iota(k)=\{\,j:\operatorname{tr}(w_j\,z_k)\neq0\,\},

## 6. The selector, the guard, and the discriminator

### Definition 6.1 — descent `[FIN]`

A record \(\pi\) of a context, a partition of its core atoms, **descends**
across the arena when all three clauses hold.

- **(D1) Transport.** The images \(\iota(B)=\bigcup_{k\in B}\iota(k)\),
  \(B\in\pi\), are pairwise disjoint. This is the predecessor's availability
  criterion, posed at the declared overlap instead of at a later boundary of
  the same context. Under (D1) the images form a partition of the overlap
  atoms, the record's **transported fact content** \(\operatorname{fact}(\pi)\).
- **(D2) Co-reference.** Some record \(\pi'\) of the declared second context
  also satisfies (D1) and has
  \(\operatorname{fact}(\pi')=\operatorname{fact}(\pi)\).
- **(D3) Non-vacuity.** \(\operatorname{fact}(\pi)\) is not the one-block
  partition of the overlap.

At the arena run here the second context coincides with the overlap and
realizes every partition of it, so **(D2) excludes no record that (D1) and
(D3) admit** (§5.3, measured on every context). (D2) is binding only against
the coarser tomographic context, where the positive certificate is withdrawn.

Fact content is a partition of the overlap atoms and carries no block name.
Clause (D2) is therefore an equality of facts, never an identification of
tokens: the split between what a record *says* and which outcome *occurred*
is preserved by construction, and Section 7.3 measures that it is.

### Theorem 6.2 — the selector is not a closure on the candidate's lattice `[FIN]`, `[ARENA]`

For each of the three constructed manufactured contexts the certified set
omits that context's greatest record. Every closure operator on the same
record lattice fixes that greatest record. Hence the certified set is **not**
the fixed-point set of any closure operator on the candidate's own record
lattice.

**Proof.** A closure's fixed-point set contains \(\top\) by extensivity; the
certified set does not contain \(\top\); the two sets are therefore distinct
for every closure. \(\square\)

The universal half of that argument is extensivity and needs no sampling.
What is measured, because it is the predecessor's premise rather than a
generality, is that the **availability** closure fixes the greatest record:
sweeping every left-total relation at atom counts two, three and four — nine,
three hundred and forty-three, and fifty thousand six hundred and twenty-five
relations — not one fails to fix it, so the availability closure fixes it for
every family over those relations rather than for a sample. One hundred and
sixty seeded families at atom counts two to five are retained as a
cross-check at the atom count the arena uses. (The exhaustive form is the
effectus lens's, Cycle B′ panel.)

The carrier is exhibited rather than asserted. Ordering pairs of records — one
per context, agreeing in transported fact content and non-vacuous — by
refinement in each component, the carrier for the manufactured
\(2{+}1{+}1\) context against the declared address context has exactly one
element, whose components are the record \(\{\{0,1,2\},\{3\}\}\) of the
manufactured context and the record \(\{\{0,1,2,3\},\{4\}\}\) of the address
context. The candidate manufactured record occurs in **no** pair of that
carrier: it is not merely non-maximal there, it is absent.

### Theorem 6.3 — why the one-boundary covariance obstruction does not transfer `[FIN]`, `[ARENA]`

No reversible map carrying the legitimate five-atom boundary onto the
constructed manufactured five-outcome one preserves the declared overlap.

**Proof.** The legitimate context's atoms are the declared overlap's atoms
(§5.3). A map carrying the legitimate boundary onto the manufactured one
carries the former's core atoms onto the latter's, hence carries the overlap
atoms onto the manufactured atoms; and four of those five are computed not to
be address projections. \(\square\)

The predecessor's own witness is recomputed as an instance, with the same
committed atom map \((4,0,1,2,3)\): it sends four of the five declared address
projections to non-diagonal elements. A criterion covariant for one context
alone is therefore not a criterion covariant for the arena, and the
predecessor's covariance theorem, which concerns triples of one boundary, has
no purchase on a selector whose carrier includes an overlap. (The

### Theorem 6.4 — THE DISCRIMINATOR, both directions `[FIN]`, `[ARENA]`

**Direction one — the manufactured record fails descent.** For all three

`RQ0-L1-SMUGGLING-SURVIVES-DESCENT` therefore does not occur, and the arc does
not halt.

### Theorem 6.5 — what the negative direction separates `[FIN]`, `[ARENA]`

At the declared overlap, a context's greatest record transports without
collision **if and only if** every atom of its measure is an address
projection.

**Proof.** Sufficiency is immediate: address projections with disjoint
supports have disjoint incidences. For necessity, suppose the atoms'
incidences \(S_r=\iota(P_r)\) are pairwise disjoint. Since
\(\sum_r(P_r)_{jj}=1\) at every address \(j\), each \(j\) lies in some
\(S_r\), so the \(S_r\) partition the addresses. For \(j\notin S_r\) we have
\((P_r)_{jj}=0\), and \(P_r\succeq0\) then forces row \(j\) and column \(j\)
of \(P_r\) to vanish. Hence \(P_r\preceq Q_r\), the coordinate projection onto
\(S_r\). Since \(\sum_rP_r=\sum_rQ_r=I\) and every \(Q_r-P_r\succeq0\), each
\(Q_r-P_r=0\). \(\square\)

The biconditional is verified on all nine declared contexts, without
exception. The constructed manufactured measures were built by rotating the
coordinate basis, and the overlap was declared to be the coordinate algebra;
their rejection therefore separates **rotated** from **address-aligned**. It does not separate
manufactured from earned — §7.6 exhibits the same manufacturing construction
with the rotation deleted, and it descends. (The theorem is the effectus
lens's, Cycle B′ panel; the instrument and operator lenses converge on it.)
