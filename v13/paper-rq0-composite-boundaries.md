## Abstract

The predecessor cycle proved that de-smuggling cannot be a one-boundary test.
A record manufactured by choosing a task to match a preselected measure is the
greatest element of the very record lattice any closure operator would act on,
and every closure fixes its own top; the block is law-independent. The
predecessor named the escape without opening it: compare several
independently declared tasks and the boundaries they induce.

This paper opens that scope in two halves.

The first half declares how two earned boundaries compose. Under the product
law — local admitted operations on each factor and the joint class generated
by them — the composite minimal boundary factorizes and the operational
classical core distributes exactly. The proof is a support-space identity:
the intersection of the composite effect system with the composite centre is
the tensor of the two factor intersections. It is verified by exact subspace
intersection on every ordered pair of the nine committed fixtures with
composite carrier dimension at most twenty, sixty-five pairs, with the gap
zero throughout; the operator system whose core is trivial is not promoted by
composition with any readable factor.

Under a declared joint law the distribution fails, and the failure is
measured on eight declared joint tasks. For the four-outcome task built from
the committed maximally entangled objects the composite classical core has
four atoms while nothing at all is readable on either factor alone, so the
composite boundary does not factorize and the gap is four against one. The
pre-registered reading of that gap is corrected by its own control: a
classically correlated task with separable rank-two atoms opens the gap too.
The gap alone is therefore a **joint-readability** witness. The entanglement
witness is the gap together with the exact atom classification, and in that
form it is gated in both directions across the declared family — including a
product task in a rotated basis, which does not open the gap and so separates
"built in a rotated basis" from "entangled".

The second half builds the arena. Two independently declared task families
and one overlap declared before either of them; independence measured, not
asserted, by searching all one hundred and twenty admitted relabellings; a
record certified when it transports to the overlap without collision, when
that transported fact content is realized in the second context, and when it
is non-vacuous. Co-reference is decided on transported fact content and never
on outcome tokens: a four-block record of a four-atom context co-refers with a
four-block record of a five-atom context, where no token bijection exists.

The selector escapes the predecessor's obstruction, provably rather than by
renaming. Its carrier is the pair-plus-overlap structure; the certified set of
each constructed manufactured context omits that context's greatest record,
while every closure operator on the same lattice fixes it, so the certified
set is not the fixed-point set of any closure operator there. The reversible
map that carries the legitimate five-atom boundary onto the constructed
manufactured one — the predecessor's own witness, with the same committed atom
map — does not preserve the declared overlap, which is why the one-boundary
covariance obstruction does not transfer.

The discriminator then fires in both directions. All three constructed
manufactured records fail descent, and they fail at the first clause: their
blocks collide at the overlap. They fail for exactly the feature that
manufactured them — the measure was chosen in a rotated basis so that no block
structure could be read off the coordinates, and that is what leaves it with
no fact content at an independently declared overlap. The legitimate eraser
record survives, with a co-referring record exhibited. The same construction
with the rotation deleted does descend, so descent certifies non-vacuous fact
content at an independently declared overlap and not absence of manufacture;
and a strictly coarser second context withdraws the legitimate certificate
while leaving the manufactured rejection untouched.

The registered outcome is `RQ0-L1-COMPOSITE-BOUNDARY` together with
`RQ0-L1-ENTANGLEMENT-WITNESS` in its corrected form and
`RQ0-L1-DESCENT-SELECTOR` at the declared arena.
`RQ0-L1-SMUGGLING-SURVIVES-DESCENT` does not occur.

## 2. The composite operational structure, declared

A **declared boundary** is a pair \((E,A)\): a finite direct sum \(A\) of
matrix blocks with centre \(Z(A)\), and a self-adjoint spanning set for the
effect system \(E\subseteq A\), with \(A\) its envelope. Its **admitted
splits** are the projections of \(E\cap Z(A)\), and its **core** is the
partition of the central atoms of \(A\) cut by them. For \(E=A\) this returns
the minimal central projections; for the hostile operator system, whose
intersection with the envelope centre has computed dimension one, it returns
the trivial core.

### Definition 2.1 — the composite `[FIN]`

Let \((E_A,A)\) and \((E_B,B)\) be declared boundaries. The **composite
carrier** is \(A\otimes B\), whose central atoms are the products
\(z_k\otimes z_l\). Two composite laws are declared.

- **The product law.** The admitted joint operations are those generated by
  the local ones; the composite effect system is the algebraic span
  \(E_A\otimes E_B\); the admitted states are the product states.
- **A joint law.** A joint task family is declared; the composite effect
  system is the range \(A_P\) of the matched dephasing
  \(\mathcal D_P(\rho)=\sum_rP_r\rho P_r\) of its measure, obtained by exact
  elimination; where the committed fixtures supply them, entangled admitted
  states are declared with it.

### Definition 2.2 — the two sides of the distribution question `[FIN]`

\(\operatorname{Core}(A\otimes B)\) is the partition of the composite
boundary's central atoms cut by its admitted splits.
\(\operatorname{Core}(A)\otimes\operatorname{Core}(B)\) is the partition of
**the same** atoms cut by those admitted splits that are readable on one
factor alone, that is, of the form \(Q\otimes1\) or \(1\otimes Q\). Both are
partitions of one set, so the comparison is well typed and the **gap** is
their inequality, not a difference of two counts.

The distinction matters: Section 4 exhibits a declared task where the two
partitions have the same number of blocks and are nevertheless different.

## 3. The product law: factorization and distribution

### Lemma 3.1 — minimal supports `[FIN]`

For subspaces \(V\subseteq A\), \(W\subseteq B\), \(X\subseteq A\),
\(Y\subseteq B\) of finite-dimensional spaces,

\[
(V\otimes W)\cap(X\otimes Y)=(V\cap X)\otimes(W\cap Y).
\]

**Proof.** Write \(t\) in a representation \(\sum_ia_i\otimes b_i\) of minimal
length; then \(\{a_i\}\) and \(\{b_i\}\) are each linearly independent, and
the spaces \(L(t)=\operatorname{span}\{a_i\}\) and
\(R(t)=\operatorname{span}\{b_i\}\) do not depend on the representation. For
any subspaces \(U,U'\) one has \(t\in U\otimes U'\) iff \(L(t)\subseteq U\)
and \(R(t)\subseteq U'\). Membership in both sides of the display therefore
reads \(L(t)\subseteq V\cap X\) and \(R(t)\subseteq W\cap Y\). \(\square\)

### Theorem 3.2 — product-law factorization `[FIN]`, `[PAIR-20]`

Under the product law the composite boundary's admitted split algebra is
exactly the one generated by the local admitted splits, so

\[
\operatorname{Core}(A\otimes B)
=\operatorname{Core}(A)\otimes\operatorname{Core}(B),
\]

and the composite minimal boundary factorizes.

**Proof.** The centre of a finite direct sum tensor is the tensor of the
centres, \(Z(A\otimes B)=Z(A)\otimes Z(B)\), spanned by the products of
central atoms. By Lemma 3.1,

\[
(E_A\otimes E_B)\cap(Z(A)\otimes Z(B))
=(E_A\cap Z(A))\otimes(E_B\cap Z(B)),
\]

so every admitted composite split is a combination of products of admitted
local splits, and the projections among them are exactly the unions of
products of local core blocks. \(\square\)

The identity of the display is verified by exact subspace intersection —
computed on both sides by independent routes — on all thirty fixture pairs of
composite carrier dimension at most nine. The theorem's conclusion is then
measured directly on **all sixty-five ordered pairs** of the nine committed
fixtures with composite carrier dimension at most twenty, comparing the
composite core with the product of the two factor cores: the gap is zero on
every pair, with no exception. The remaining sixteen ordered pairs of the
eighty-one exceed the declared carrier cap and are carried by the theorem.

### 3.1 The hostile wing is not promoted

The operator system whose operational core is trivial — its intersection with
its envelope's centre has computed dimension one, only the scalars — must not
acquire a nontrivial core by composition with a readable factor. Ten declared
composites are run, in both orders, against the classical bit, the full
matrix factor, the mixed control, the trivial boundary and itself. In every
one the composite core equals the product of the two factor cores exactly: one
against one gives one, one against two gives two. A promotion would have
appeared as a composite core strictly finer than the product; none appears.

Eight joint tasks are declared on the two-factor carrier of dimension four,

maximally entangled projections are obtained from the first by the admitted

be mutually orthogonal projections summing to the unit, each of exact Schmidt
rank two, each with maximally mixed local restriction.

### Definition 4.1 — the atom classification `[FIN]`

A rank-one atom is **entangled** when the exact rank of its amplitude matrix,
reshaped from any nonzero column of the projection, exceeds one. An atom of
any rank is a **product projection** when it equals the tensor of the supports
of its two partial traces, both computed exactly. The entanglement
classification is claimed only on rank-one atoms, where the reshaped rank
decides it.

Consequently the gap alone is a **joint-readability** witness — it says that
the composite carries a classical record no factor can read — and the

## 6. The descent selector, the guard, and the discriminator

### Definition 6.1 — descent `[FIN]`

A record \(\pi\) of a context, a partition of its core atoms, **descends**
across the arena when all three clauses hold.

- **(D1) Transport.** The images \(\iota(B)=\bigcup_{k\in B}\iota(k)\),
  \(B\in\pi\), are pairwise disjoint. This is the predecessor's availability
  criterion, posed at the declared overlap instead of at a later boundary of
  the same context. Under (D1) the images form a partition of the overlap
  atoms, the record's **transported fact content** \(\operatorname{fact}(\pi)\).
- **(D2) Co-reference.** Some record \(\pi'\) of the independently declared
  second context also satisfies (D1) and has
  \(\operatorname{fact}(\pi')=\operatorname{fact}(\pi)\).
- **(D3) Non-vacuity.** \(\operatorname{fact}(\pi)\) is not the one-block
  partition of the overlap.

Fact content is a partition of the overlap atoms and carries no block name.
Clause (D2) is therefore an equality of facts, never an identification of
tokens: the split between what a record *says* and which outcome *occurred*
is preserved by construction, and Section 7.3 measures that it is.

### Theorem 6.2 — the selector is not a closure on the candidate's lattice `[FIN]`, `[ARENA]`

For each of the three constructed manufactured contexts the certified set
omits that context's greatest record. Every closure operator on the same
record lattice fixes that greatest record — re-verified natively here on one
hundred and sixty declared admitted families at atom counts two to five,
without exception. Hence the certified set is **not** the fixed-point set of
any closure operator on the candidate's own record lattice.

**Proof.** A closure's fixed-point set contains \(\top\) by extensivity; the
certified set does not contain \(\top\); the two sets are therefore distinct
for every closure. \(\square\)

The carrier is exhibited rather than asserted. Ordering pairs of records — one
per context, agreeing in transported fact content and non-vacuous — by
refinement in each component, the carrier for the manufactured
\(2{+}1{+}1\) context against the declared address context has exactly one
element, whose components are the record \(\{\{0,1,2\},\{3\}\}\) of the
manufactured context and the record \(\{\{0,1,2,3\},\{4\}\}\) of the address
context. The candidate manufactured record occurs in **no** pair of that
carrier: it is not merely non-maximal there, it is absent.

### Theorem 6.3 — why the one-boundary covariance obstruction does not transfer `[FIN]`, `[ARENA]`

The reversible map carrying the legitimate five-atom boundary onto the
constructed manufactured five-outcome one — the predecessor's own witness,
recomputed here with the same committed atom map \((4,0,1,2,3)\) — does not
preserve the declared overlap: four of the five declared address projections
are sent to non-diagonal elements. A criterion covariant for one context alone
is therefore not a criterion covariant for the arena, and the predecessor's
covariance theorem, which concerns triples of one boundary, has no purchase on
a selector whose carrier includes a second context and an overlap.

### Theorem 6.4 — THE DISCRIMINATOR, both directions `[FIN]`, `[ARENA]`

**Direction one — the manufactured record fails descent.** For all three
constructed manufactured boundaries the manufactured record fails **(D1)**:
distinct blocks reach a common overlap atom. It is certified against neither
declared second context. Each manufactured context certifies exactly one
record out of its fifteen, five and fifty-two, namely the split of the
retained sink from everything else — which is precisely the part of its
boundary that was not manufactured.

**Direction two — the legitimate eraser record survives descent.** The
corrected eraser minimum's record transports without collision, its fact
content is the discrete partition of the five overlap atoms, it is
non-vacuous, and the independently declared second context realizes exactly
that fact content; the co-referring record is exhibited. Fifty-one of the
fifty-two records of that context are certified, the excluded one being the
trivial record, which fails non-vacuity.

**The manufactured record fails for exactly the feature that manufactured
it.** The measure was chosen in a rotated basis so that no block structure
could be read off the coordinates. That is what leaves it with no fact content
at an independently declared overlap.

`RQ0-L1-SMUGGLING-SURVIVES-DESCENT` therefore does not occur, and the arc does
not halt.

### 7.6 What descent does **not** certify

Two limits are measured and are reported rather than suppressed.

**The aligned manufactured measure descends.** The same manufacturing
construction with the rotation deleted — a chosen measure aligned with the
declared addresses — satisfies all three clauses. Descent therefore does not
certify "not chosen to match a measure"; it certifies "has non-vacuous fact
content at an independently declared overlap".

**Certification is arena-relative.** Replacing the second context by the
strictly coarser committed tomographic context leaves the manufactured
rejection untouched — none of the three manufactured records is certified
there either — but withdraws the legitimate record's certificate: a two-atom

context's certified count falls from fifty-one to one. The **negative**
direction of the discriminator is robust across both declared second contexts;
the **positive** direction is not, and is claimed only at the declared arena.

## Appendix A. Declared deviations from the frozen pin

Shipped as part of the delivery, per the standing rule.

1. **The entanglement witness is corrected, not merely earned.** The pin
   pre-registers "the core gap opens exactly on the entangled fixtures and
   vanishes on products". Measured, the first half is false as stated: the
   classically correlated parity task opens the gap with separable atoms. The
   outcome is earned in the corrected form of Theorem 4.3, and the naive form
   is recorded as refuted by its own control.

2. **The gap is defined as a partition inequality, not a count difference.**
   Both sides are partitions of the same atom set. One declared task has equal
   block counts on the two sides and different partitions; a count-based gap
   would have missed it.

3. **`Core(A) ⊗ Core(B)` is instantiated as the splits readable on one factor
   alone.** The pin does not fix the instantiation. This one keeps both sides
   partitions of the same set and makes the comparison well typed; the
   alternative of comparing abstract factor cores would have required a
   further identification the pin does not supply.

4. **The predecessor record work's committed model numbers are cited as
   antecedent but not reused,** so no anchor claims them. The pin conditions
   their anchoring on reuse; there is none.

5. **The arena is instantiated with one candidate-context slot and one fixed
   independently declared second context,** rather than with both candidates
   inside the same pair. With both candidates in one pair neither can be
   tested, since neither context can co-refer with the other's finest record;
   the pin's discriminator sentence — "across an independently declared second
   context" — is read as licensing the slot form.

6. **The overlap is declared before both contexts** (overlap-first), which the
   pin permits but does not require. Declaring it after a context would have
   made the no-smuggling gate unmeetable.

7. **A sharpness control not requested by the pin is added and reported:** the
   aligned manufactured measure descends, and the coarser second context
   withdraws the legitimate certificate. Both narrow what the earned rung
   says, and both are in the verdict.

8. **The predecessor's top-of-lattice theorem is re-verified natively** on one
   hundred and sixty declared families rather than cited, so that the guard's
   premise is measured inside this unit.

9. **The product-law sweep is capped at composite carrier dimension twenty**
   (sixty-five of eighty-one ordered pairs); the remaining sixteen are carried
   by the proved lemma and theorem, and the cap is printed in the receipt and
   stated at the claim.

10. **The hostile-wing control is run in both orders** and against five
    partners rather than the single composite the pin names, which strengthens
    it and costs nothing.
