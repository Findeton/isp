## Abstract

Two committed bases and four terminal units have measured a single
declaration→structure law: a declared symmetry's failure to centralise a
declared preparation leg *is* the transport curvature, the defect is the
commutator $[P,u]$, the based holonomy group is dihedral of order
$2\,\mathrm{ord}[P,u]$, and the connection's gauge class is a function of that
one order. Every instance in which that law was measured had **two wings**,
and therefore a wing symmetry group of order two.

This unit builds the first **purpose-built** base — three wings, each a system
of dimension two and a pointer of dimension two, carrier $2^3\times2^3 = 64$ —
whose wing symmetry group is $S_3$, non-abelian **by construction**, whose
frames are the $3! = 6$ leg-orders so that triple overlaps are native rather
than declared, and whose preparations can be GHZ-class or W-class, a
distinction no bipartite state has. Five axes are run on it.

**A1.** The completion census is exhaustive at 5,040 completions; the maximum
defect order the carrier admits is measured to be **6**. Completions are then
selected by rule at orders 1, 2, 3 and 6, and the based holonomy group is
computed at each by two independent routes: the orders are **1, 1008, 72,
15120**. The dihedral law $|\mathrm{Hol}| = 2\,\mathrm{ord}[P,u]$ holds at
**0 of 4** targets. On a declared sample of five completions *sharing* the
same defect order at the declared symmetry, the holonomy order takes **two**
values, 72 and 2160 — so the group is not a function of that order at all.

**A2.** The law's *form* was an involution artifact and the unit says exactly
how. The measured transport defect — read off the conjugated leg matrices, not
off any formula — is reproduced by the group commutator $[P^{-1},u]$ at
**54 of 54** (member, wing-symmetry) cells, and by the committed four-factor
product $P u^{-1} P u$ at **36**, precisely the involution cells; at the
three-cycles the four-factor product does not even split off the pointer
factor (36 of 54 against 54 of 54). The **cocycle survives verbatim**:
$\delta_P(XY) = \delta_P(Y)\cdot Y^{-1}\delta_P(X)Y$ holds at every cell and
on 200 randomised triples, and its instantiation at the declared
factorisation reproduces the measured defect with **0** deviations. The
geometry is generated by the commutators **together with** the wing
symmetries and not by the commutators alone: the commutator subgroup has
order 360, is normal, and the holonomy group has order $2160 = 6\times360$.

**A3.** On the native six-frame atlas — 36 charts over **six** seeds, against
COC's two — the triangle census runs to **1,226,304** admissible triangles at
the reference instance by two independent routes, and **0** of them escape the
level-1 holonomy group, while the chart-generating group centralises it at
only **1 of 6** elements. The committed criterion's *necessity* direction is
therefore measured **false** where the triangles are not engineered. A
computed refinement — *normalises* in place of *centralises* — agrees with the
measured closure at **5 of 5** instances, and the census has teeth in both
directions: two instances escape, at 62,784 triangles each.

**A4.** The state factor $E_P(\psi)$ is the identity **exactly** on the
locus where $\psi$ is invariant under that wing symmetry, 0 deviations over 54
cells. At identical declarations the GHZ-class members carry a holonomy group
of order 2160 and the W-class members 6, 4, 1 and a group that is not a
permutation group at all. The exhaustive 36-member pair census finds a
**fixed-Born-shadow witness pair**: `psi-W1` and `psi-W4` share a Born shadow,
agree cell for cell on their admission table, carry the same 90-link graph —
and differ on the gauge-invariant Born holonomy of common named loops.

**A5.** The committed graph triple $(8, 13, 6)$ becomes $(30, 150, 121)$. Of
eight survival candidates declared before measurement, **three survive** — the
structural *form* of the graph, the per-coordinate admission pattern, and the
alignment profile — and **five do not**, including both the dihedral order law
and the forcing of the profile by one commutator's order.

Everything is stated at the declared finite scope and nothing is claimed about
nature.

## 1. The question, and what it is asked of

The transport geometry of this programme has been measured on two bases. Base
1 has two qubit wings and a 36-configuration carrier; base G has two qutrit
wings and an 81-configuration carrier. They share no rotation, no
preparation, no completion and no carrier dimension, and they were built years
of ledger apart. What they share is a wing count.

That is not a small thing to share, because the wing count *is* the symmetry
group. With two wings the declared symmetry is a single involution $W$;
$\langle W\rangle$ has order two, is abelian, and every commutator
$[W, u]$ satisfies $W [W,u] W = [W,u]^{-1}$, so $\langle W, [W,u]\rangle$ is
dihedral *by algebra*. XBA derived that relation symbolically and measured it
on eight instances. Its corollaries — the order law, the forcing of the
class-count profile — are corollaries of a **two-element group**.

So the natural question is not whether the law is *true*. It is what the law
is a law **of**. This unit answers by building the first base designed to
break the answer if it is going to break:

- **three wings**, so the wing symmetry group is $S_3$: non-abelian, with
  elements of order three, and containing the two-wing case as a subgroup;
- **the six leg-orders as frames**, so triple overlaps are what the base *has*
  rather than what an extension declares — COC had to declare a 216-element
  extension scope to obtain a non-abelian chart-generating group, and here the
  $j_0$ filter admits $S_3$ natively;
- **multipartite preparations**, so GHZ-class and W-class states — which
  differ in exchange structure in ways no bipartite $\psi$ can — can be
  compared at identical declarations.

Three things make the comparison meaningful and each is a declaration of this
unit. The machinery is written **generic in the wing count**, so the positive
control is the same code at two wings and a defect in the three-wing
measurement is a defect in the two-wing anchor. Every link carries a
$\psi$-independent name, so "the same loop at two preparations" is a statement
about names. And every census is read by two genuinely independent routes.

## 4. A2 — the one law at $S_3$

### 4.1 What is tested, and against what

The committed law is written as a **four-factor product**,
$D = P\,u^{-1}\,P\,u$, and is called the group commutator $[P,u]$ *because $P$
was measured to be an involution*. At three wings $S_3$ contains elements of
order three, so the two readings come apart, and which — if either — is the
law becomes a measurement.

The comparator is the defect the **chart rule actually produces**. Relabelling
a frame conjugates each of its legs; the preparation leg is the one the wing
symmetry fails to intertwine, and
$\sigma.u\,\sigma^{-1} = u\,D_\sigma^{-1}$ *defines* $D_\sigma$ off the
conjugated leg matrices. No commutator formula enters the comparator. Three
candidate forms are then evaluated blind at every one of the
$9\times6 = 54$ (member, wing-symmetry) cells:

| form | reproduces the measured defect | of which at the 4 involution cells | splits off the pointer identity |
|---|---|---|---|
| $F_1 = P u^{-1} P u$ — **the committed writing** | **36 / 54** | 36 / 36 | **36 / 54** |
| $F_2 = [P, u] = P^{-1}u^{-1}Pu$ | 36 / 54 | 36 / 36 | 54 / 54 |
| $F_3 = [P^{-1}, u] = P u^{-1} P^{-1} u$ | **54 / 54** | 36 / 36 | 54 / 54 |

Each of the three forms is additionally gated against an **independent
construction** from the $8\times8$ system algebra — $F_2 = \delta_\pi(V)\otimes
I$, $F_3 = \delta_{\pi^{-1}}(V)\otimes I$, and
$F_1 = (\Sigma V^{\mathsf T}\Sigma V)\otimes\Sigma^2$, the last of which is
where the pointer factor $\Sigma^2$ becomes visible — at all
$3\times54 = 162$ cells, so a form that is not what its name says kills the
run before the verdict is read.

### 4.2 The verdict, and exactly what it corrects

$$\boxed{\;\texttt{TB3-ONE-LAW-GENERALIZES}\;}$$

with the qualifier carrying the measured counts: the group commutator
reproduces the measured defect at 54 of 54 cells; the committed four-factor
product at 36, and only at the involutions.

The correction is precise and it is small. **The law is the group
commutator.** The four-factor *writing* was an involution artifact: with
$P^2 = \mathbf 1$ the four-factor product *is* the group commutator, which is
why two bases and four units could not tell them apart. At a three-cycle they
differ, and the difference is visible in a second, independent place — the
four-factor product fails to split off the pointer factor at exactly the 18
non-involution cells (36 of 54 against 54 of 54), because its pointer factor
is $\Sigma_\pi^2$ rather than the identity. A form that does not even leave
the pointer pair alone is not the defect the preparation leg carries.

### 4.3 The cocycle survives verbatim

$$\delta_P(XY) \;=\; \delta_P(Y)\cdot Y^{-1}\delta_P(X)\,Y,
\qquad \delta_P(X) := P^{-1}X^{-1}PX$$

holds for **any** invertible $P$ — no involution needed — and it is entered as
a **disclosure**, not advertised as a discovery, because it is forced by
algebra in one line. Measured at all 54 cells: **0** deviations. Measured on
**200** randomised triples drawn from a generator seeded by the SHA-256 of the
declared data alone: 200 of 200 exactly orthogonal, **0** deviations, and
**166** triples where the mirror-ordered expansion gives a *different* answer
— the order is not forced, and the sweep is not vacuous.

Instantiating at the declared factorisation $V = H(\psi)\,Q$ gives the whole
$\psi$-law in one step,
$\delta_P(HQ) = \delta_P(Q)\cdot Q^{\mathsf T}\delta_P(H)\,Q$, and the
reconstruction reproduces the **measured** defect with **0** deviations at all
54 cells.

### 4.4 What generates the geometry

| measured at the reference member and the declared completion | value |
|---|---|
| the commutator subgroup $\langle [P,u] : P\in S_3\rangle$ | order **360** |
| it is normal under the wing symmetries | **yes** |
| the level-1 holonomy group | order **2160** |
| generated by the commutators **alone** | **no** |
| generated by the commutators **and** the wing symmetries | **yes** |

$2160 = 6\times 360$. The pin asks whether the geometry is generated by the
commutator subgroup; the measured answer is that the commutator subgroup is
the *normal* part and the wing symmetries are the quotient. At two wings the
same statement reads $4 = 2\times 2$ and is invisible.

## 5. A3 — level 2, native

### 5.1 The atlas, and why it is harder than the committed one

For $\sigma$ in the admitted group and $X$ a committed frame, $\sigma.X$ is
the chart whose $k$-th leg is $\sigma L_k\sigma^{-1}$ and whose law datum at
every checkpoint is the $\sigma$-pushforward of $X$'s; the atlas is the orbit
of the committed frames, deduplicated by chart identity. COC's atlases had
**two** seeds, and its telescoping corollary used that fact: any three
pairwise-distinct charts repeat a seed, the repeated pair's seed-level map is
the identity, and the defect collapses to a conjugate of one level-1 object.

Here there are **six** seeds. Three pairwise-distinct charts need repeat none
of them, so the two-seed reduction is unavailable and the census must be run.

| instance | charts | seeds | level-1 $\lvert\mathrm{Hol}\rvert$ | admissible triangles | escapes | centralising | normalising | closes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **the declared base, fully symmetric setting** | **36** | 6 | **2160** | **1,226,304** | **0** | **1 / 6** | **6 / 6** | yes |
| the equivariant-completion control | 6 | 6 | 1 | 600 | 0 | 6 / 6 | 6 / 6 | yes |
| a **partially symmetric** setting | 36 | 6 | 6 | 343,296 | **62,784** | 1 / 6 | **2 / 6** | no |
| an asymmetric setting | 36 | 6 | 1 | 216,720 | 0 | 6 / 6 | 6 / 6 | yes |
| a W-class preparation, fully symmetric | 36 | 6 | 6 | 343,296 | **62,784** | 1 / 6 | **2 / 6** | no |

Both censuses are taken by two genuinely independent routes — adjacency lists
walking drawn edges, and a direct triple loop over every ordered triple of
distinct charts and every rule assignment with dictionary lookups — and the
triangle and escape counts are gated equal at every instance.

### 5.2 The criterion, re-tested where the triangles are not engineered

COC's criterion:

> The cocycle closes at an atlas **if and only if** the chart-generating group
> centralises the level-1 holonomy.

COC was explicit that only the *sufficiency* direction is forced, and that
necessity was a measurement it made at two constructed atlases. This is the
first test on a base that was not designed around it.

**Sufficiency survives**: both instances whose group centralises are measured
to close. **Necessity is measured false.** At the declared base the
chart-generating group centralises the level-1 holonomy at **1 of 6**
elements, and **0 of 1,226,304** triangles escape.

The mechanism is measured, not conjectured. At that instance the level-1
holonomy group **contains all six wing symmetries** — the FULL/REALIZED bigon
at a checkpoint where both rules draw has holonomy exactly the drawn
symmetry — so the group is closed under conjugation by the chart-generating
group. The escape route is shut not by centralisation but by **containment**,
and the correct condition is the weaker one:

> **(computed)** The cocycle closes at an atlas **iff** the chart-generating
> group **normalises** the level-1 holonomy.

Measured across the five instances: the committed centraliser form agrees with
the measured closure at **4 of 5**; the normaliser form at **5 of 5**.
Centralising implies normalising, so the sufficiency direction COC proved is
untouched; what the refinement replaces is the direction COC declined to
claim.

The census has teeth: two instances escape, at 62,784 triangles each, so a
zero escape count is a measurement about those instances and not a property of
the instrument. And the two escaping instances are the two whose level-1
group is small — the **partially symmetric** setting and the W-class
preparation, both of which have exactly two of the six symmetries available.

## 9. The gauge layer and the symmetry self-test

The declared switching group assigns a sign to each link, so a closed loop's
matrix is defined only up to a sign. Both of this unit's holonomy readings are
invariant under that action, and RUNBOOK §14 requires the instrument to
measure the invariance under the symmetry's **own** action rather than under a
wholesale replacement.

Swept over all **150** single-link switchings against all **121**
cycle-basis generators — 18,150 cells:

| measured | value |
|---|---|
| Born-shadow readings that moved | **0** |
| permutation parts that moved | **0** |
| generators whose **sign** the switching flips | **non-zero** — the sweep is not vacuous |
| generators **rebuilt from their own directed link path** and matching the spanning-tree construction | **121 / 121** |
| with every link switched: rebuilt holonomy = unswitched × the parity read independently off the path | **121 / 121**, of which **84** have negative parity — the sign path is measured to be exercised |
| fresh-mode cache hits | **0** |
| cache entries primed / reads that returned a stored value | both **non-zero** |
| fresh requests for a key already in the cache | **non-zero** |

The two rebuild clauses are the RUNBOOK §14 addendum applied to this
instrument's own comparator: each generator is reconstructed by the
walk-holonomy routine from its directed link path — code that shares nothing
with the spanning-tree construction that produced it — and the sign path is
then *exercised* rather than merely declared, by switching every link and
gating the result against a parity read independently off the path length.

The self-test evaluates **fresh**: the cache is primed with the very keys it
will request, the priming phase's own re-read is counted so the read path is
measured to work, the fresh-mode hit count is gated at zero, and the count of
fresh requests for a key that *is* in the cache is gated **non-zero** — zero
hits of zero lookups would be vacuous.

## 11. Scope and non-claims

1. Everything is measured **inside one declared finite model**, at the
   declared scope printed with every verdict. Nothing is claimed about nature,
   about quantum theory, or about any physical system.
2. The preparation family is a **declaration**, tagged `[SAMP]`. No property
   of it is extrapolated to preparations outside it. The one census that is
   exhaustive over completions is the 5,040-member label census of §3.1, and
   it is exhaustive only at the declared symmetry $P^*$ and the declared
   reference preparation.
3. The transport analysis of §3, §4, §6 runs at the **fully symmetric setting
   `TB-000`**; the graph census of §7 runs at all 27; the triangle census of
   §5 runs at the five declared instances. Each scope is printed with its
   table.
4. The 576 completion anchors are **self-anchors**, labelled as such. They
   establish that no constructor drifts from the printed declaration; they do
   not establish provenance, because no committed receipt carries a
   three-wing completion.
5. The refined criterion of §5.2 is measured at **five instances**. It is
   offered as a computed refinement whose sufficiency direction is forced by
   the same argument COC gave, and whose necessity direction is a measurement
   at five instances and not a theorem.
6. §6.2's reading of the GHZ/W difference — that the class enters through the
   Born shadow's symmetry — is a statement **about this base and this
   admission predicate**. The absence of a fully $S_3$-invariant rational W
   state means the comparison is not at matched stabiliser, and that is
   disclosed rather than corrected for; §6.3's witness pair, which *is* at
   matched Born shadow and matched admission table, is the claim made at
   matched coordinates.
7. `psi-W4`'s holonomy group is **not computed**: its generators leave the
   permutation class, and the unit reports the readable fraction and uses the
   always-defined Born shadow rather than filtering the unreadable loops away,
   which would have hidden the finding.
8. Two independent routes are gated on every census — the completion order
   census, the holonomy group, the triangle census, the walk census. "Two
   routes" here means two computations that share no intermediate value, not
   two readings related by an algebraic identity.

## Appendix: deviations

1. **The pin's A1 target "the maximum the carrier admits" is a computed
   quantity, and it is 6 rather than the naive 7.** The maximum order of any
   element of $S_7$ reachable as $[\Sigma_{P^*}, Q]$ is measured over the
   exhaustive 5,040-member census; 7 occurs at the three-cycles of $S_3$ but
   not at the declared transposition $P^*$. The sweep is run at the maximum
   *at $P^*$*, as the pin's wording ("for at least one wing-pair symmetry
   $P$") requires, and both numbers are printed.

2. **A1's group family is computed at the fully symmetric setting only.** The
   pin does not require otherwise, but the scope is stated rather than
   implied: the graph and its holonomy depend on the setting, and §7's census
   over all 27 settings is where the setting dependence is measured.

3. **The two-wing positive control cannot realise defect order 2.** The reason
   is computed and printed in §8.1: at that species the defect lies in the
   alternating group on three labels. XBA's order-2 profile
   $82/86/90/106$ is therefore *not* anchored, and the anchor set is the
   order-3 profile and the order-1 refusal row. No order-2 claim is made.

4. **A3's census is at five declared instances, not at all 27 settings.** The
   census costs $\sim10^6$ triangle evaluations per instance; five instances
   were declared to span the three setting symmetry types, the equivariant
   control and a W-class preparation. The scope is printed with the table and
   the refinement of §5.2 is scoped to it.

5. **S7 is measured on a declared sample** `[SAMP]` **of five completions, not
   exhaustively.** The exhaustive alternative is 1,728 completions at the
   modal order, each requiring a full graph and group computation. The sample
   is the lexicographically first five, the size is declared in the source and
   gated, and the measured answer is a **refutation** — two distinct holonomy
   orders — which a larger sample can only strengthen.

6. **The cocycle is read at $\pi^{-1}$.** The measured transport defect is
   $D_\pi = [P^{-1}, u]$ (§4.1), so the cocycle expansion that reconstructs it
   is $\delta_{\pi^{-1}}$. The convention is fixed by the *measurement* and is
   printed in the source at the point of use; it is not chosen to make an
   equality come out, and the reconstruction's 0 deviations are measured
   against the defect the chart rule produces.

7. **The second holonomy route uses the same base node.** An earlier route
   design took the second base at a different node; a based holonomy group at
   another node is a *conjugate matrix group*, whose elements need not be
   permutations even when the first group's are, so equality of the two is not
   the right gate. The delivered routes differ in spanning tree (breadth-first
   in declared link order against depth-first in reversed order, measured to
   have different cotrees, hence different generating loops) and in closure
   direction, and are gated equal **as sets**.

8. **`psi-W4`'s holonomy order is reported as absent**, not as a number, and
   the class comparison in §6.2 carries that absence rather than substituting
   a readable subgroup for it.

9. **Three of the predicate's four clauses are measured inert at this base**
   (§2.8). The predicate is carried verbatim from the committed units and is
   not trimmed — the inert clauses are part of what is being generalised — but
   the clause-dropping mutant had to be re-targeted from the exact-law clause
   to the leg-key clause, because a mutant that drops an inert clause cannot
   die and would have inflated the falsification census with a falsifier that
   falsifies nothing.

10. **The graph can be disconnected under a sufficiently destructive mutation.**
    The spanning-tree route was made robust to that (cotree links outside the
    base node's component are skipped) and the node coverage is reported, so
    such a mutant dies at a named anchor rather than crashing. No delivered
    instance is disconnected: every graph in every table reaches all 30 nodes
    from the base node.
