### F3 — [MODERATE] The stated mechanism for the 576 flat-setting zeros is a **non-sequitur**; the real mechanism is a coboundary and is much stronger

**The claim under attack.** §5.4: *"What makes the identity come out
there is measured in §6: those coordinates carry **no multiplicity at
all, so the three edges' maps cannot be mixed**."* §6 closes with the
same reading.

**Why it does not follow.** Absence of multiplicity means each ordered
pair draws *one* map, so all eight rule assignments give the same
triangle. It does **not** make that map the identity. And the flat
settings do **not** draw only identities: I measure **44 wing-exchange
draws and 12 identity draws per flat setting** (per cell at t∈{0,1,3}:
12 W, 4 identity; at t2: 8 W, 0 identity). A triangle there has
W-edges. Counterexample to the inference: a rule-independent
(multiplicity-one) sign table on four charts with
`ε(F1,F2)=ε(F2,W.F1)=ε(W.F1,F1)=1` gives defect `W` on **24 of 24**
ordered triples with no multiplicity anywhere.

**The real mechanism, measured.** Write `ε_r(X,Y) ∈ Z/2` for the drawn
map (0 = identity, 1 = W). I solved for a potential at every one of the
24 cells and both rules:

- `ε_r(X,Y) = f_r(X) + f_r(Y) mod 2` — **an exact coboundary at all 48
  (cell, rule) pairs; 0 exceptions.**
- `f_FULL(σ.F_i) = [σ = W]`, at every setting and checkpoint.
- `f_REAL = f_FULL` at the **four flat settings**;
  `f_REAL = f_FULL + [i = 2]` at the **two symmetric settings**.
- Defect parity law: `δ = W^(number of W-edges mod 2)` — **0 violations
  of 1,824**; and the coboundary prediction
  `δ = Σ_edges (f_{r_e}(head) + f_{r_e}(tail))` — **0 mismatches of
  1,824**.

Every headline number falls out of this in one line:

- **flat settings:** the two potentials *coincide*, so the sum
  telescopes and the defect is identically the identity. That — not
  "no multiplicity" — is why the 576 are zero.
- **symmetric settings:** the potentials differ by `g = [frame = F2]`,
  so `δ` is the parity of `Σ_{REAL edges}(g(head)+g(tail))` — nonzero
  for exactly **96 of 192** per cell.
- **single-rule triangles:** 624, **all** identity, for either rule
  alone (each rule's table is separately pure gauge).
- **the four empty cells:** at GP-A…D/`t2` the only drawn pairs are
  `F1↔W.F1` and `F2↔W.F2` — a graph of two disjoint edges. A triangle
  needs an odd cycle in the `f`-bipartition; there is none. That is a
  structural reason, where the paper only names the cells.

This is a **strengthening**, not just a correction: "the pair table is
pure gauge, per rule" is the sharpest possible form of *"the connection
is the whole story"*, and it makes §6's if-and-only-if a corollary
rather than a coincidence.

**Repair.** Replace the "no multiplicity" explanation in §5.4 and §6
with the measured coboundary statement and its two potentials, and gate
the coboundary solve (it is a 3-bit search per cell and it passed
48/48).

### F4 — [MODERATE] "D is never a triangle defect" is **analytically forced** and is presented in measurement language

**K1(b), answered: artifact of which maps can be drawn — not a theorem
about triangle geometry.**

The paper states the proper-subgroup result three times in measurement
voice: the abstract (*"the completion's own defect is measured never to
be a triangle defect"*), §5.2 (*"`D` and `W·D` … are measured never to
be triangle defects"*), and §10 (*"measured to be **invisible** to the
triple structure"*).

**The derivation.** Every drawn identification lies in the search scope
(measured, all 400). `D` is **not in any declared search scope on this
base** — I measured `D ∉ S` (order 2), `D ∉ S_ext` (order 8),
`D ∉` the 162-element relabelling scope, `D ∉` the 216-element
extension; and `W·D ∉ S_ext`. Both scopes are groups (measured closed),
so no composite of three drawn maps can be `D` or `W·D` either. The
exclusion is therefore decided **before any chart is built and before
any triangle is drawn**, at both declared scopes. It carries zero
information about triangle geometry.

Structurally: `D = P_W U_prep^{-1} P_W U_prep` is manufactured by the
*preparation leg*, which no relabelling in the declared scopes
intertwines; the drawn identifications are relabellings, and
relabellings cannot generate a leg-conjugation defect. `D` is visible
to bigons only because a bigon may **traverse a leg**; a triangle at a
fixed coordinate never does. That is the honest statement, and it is a
statement about the *shapes of loops the two levels admit*, not about
closure.

The paper *does* disclose the forced confinement (§5.4 "Forced", D4),
but it never connects that disclosure to the D-exclusion headline, and
§5.2 precedes §5.4. Under RUNBOOK §14's addendum (analytically forced
clauses are disclosures, not results), this claim should carry the
forced tag **at every place it appears**.

**Repair.** Tag the proper-subgroup statement as forced in the
abstract, §5.2 and §10, with the one-line reason (`D` is in no declared
search scope; the scopes are groups). Replace "invisible to the triple
structure" with the loop-shape statement: a triangle at a fixed
coordinate traverses no leg, so it cannot see a leg-conjugation defect.
Note also that `D`'s absence would remain forced even at the wider
`S_ext` scope — which is exactly why F1's escaping defect is a
*different* element (`W·τ_A·τ_B`, not `D`).
