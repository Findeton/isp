# RQ0-L0 overlap-first localization — design only

**Date:** 2026-07-31
**Status:** DESIGN — NOT CONSTRUCTED SCIENTIFICALLY
**Authority:** architecture-reset pin `c0fab11`
**Claim ceiling:** no RQ0-L0 outcome

## 1. Why the architecture is being reset

The preceding computational cycles treated recovery of global normal direct
factors as the main route to localization.  That is now demoted to a narrow
finite calibration lemma.  A direct product answers a special question:

> Can the whole finite process be written as mutually independent global
> pieces?

It does not answer the intended locality question:

> Which proper operational processes overlap physically, and which stable
> records live on those same overlaps?

A manifold-like atlas is not generally one global Cartesian product.  Its
patches overlap, different patches need not extend to one global factorization,
and intersections are part of the structure.  The scientific L0 target must
therefore be overlap-first.

## 2. Primitive input

The proposed input remains one finite amplitude instrument

\[
D=(X,\mathrm{Amp},\mathrm{Comp},\mathrm{Prep},\mathrm{Int},
\mathrm{Probe},\mathrm{Readout},\mathrm{Gauge}),
\]

presented through exact raw operational data.  It contains no coordinate,
metric, circuit-wire locality, qubit name, causal arrow, field propagator or
global event set.

The access family is provisional and explicit.  It states what may be
prepared, selected, composed and read.  Permanently inaccessible raw matrix
structure is not permitted to manufacture a region.

## 3. First reconstruction: `OpSub(D)`

`OpSub(D)` is to be recovered using composition and operational access only.
It must not read any W3 verdict or record-projector support.

An object is a proper operational subinstrument `A -> D` satisfying a frozen
finite analogue of:

1. its admitted operation classes close under every implemented internal row;
2. every retained operation is accessible in the restricted context;
3. its preparations, interventions, probes, readouts and gauges restrict
   consistently;
4. the inclusion is an executable, field-complete amplitude-instrument map;
5. presentation relabellings and boundary gauge produce isomorphic objects;
6. no direct-product decomposition is required.

Arrows are executable full-field restrictions, embeddings or isomorphisms.
They map operation classes, complete rows and exact laws, access declarations,
preparations, contexts, probes, readouts and gauges.  A mere inclusion between
sets of handles is not an arrow.

The output may be a category, a groupoid of ambiguous categories, or a finite
negative.  It is not yet a spatial category.

## 4. Independent reconstruction: `RecSub(D)`

`RecSub(D)` is recovered independently from the record candidates frozen in
the raw operational interface.  It must not read the objects returned by
`OpSub(D)`.

For each candidate `r`, the reconstruction re-evaluates:

\[
\mathrm{Occ}_D(r),\qquad
\mathrm{Avail}_D(r;V),
\]

from its exact write, preserve, erase and no-write dynamics.  A record-bearing
subinstrument is then generated from the composition-closed operational
support on which that W3 record is accessible.  Its arrows carry:

- the complete record dynamics;
- exact projector resolutions;
- Boolean-algebra pullbacks;
- access-operation support;
- continuation-relative availability.

Record handles are presentation data.  Renaming a handle while preserving its
dynamics, projector algebra and operational maps must leave `RecSub(D)`
isomorphic.

## 5. Agreement gate

The architecture succeeds on a public calibration object only when there is
an explicit functor or equivalence

\[
F_D:\operatorname{OpSub}(D)\simeq\operatorname{RecSub}(D)
\]

at the declared finite scope.  The comparison must preserve objects, arrows,
amplitude laws, record projector pullbacks and composition.

The following never suffice:

- equal record probabilities;
- equal record handles;
- equal numbers of objects;
- a shared hidden atom label;
- membership in one global direct-product truth;
- an atlas attached by the proposer.

The trusted verifier must reconstruct both sides from raw data and verify the
claimed comparison.

## 6. Physical pair and triple overlap

For operational regions `A` and `B`, a pair overlap is an amplitude instrument
`O_AB` with executable maps making a pullback square in the declared finite
cover category.  The verifier checks the universal property against every
available test object and arrow in that category.  It does not infer overlap
from a nonempty intersection of planted labels.

For three regions, `O_ABC` must carry executable maps to all three pair/region
objects and make every direct-versus-composite diagram commute.  Three
pairwise overlaps do not imply a triple overlap.

A twisted control contains three valid pair maps but fails only the loop law

\[
\phi_{12}\phi_{23}\ne\phi_{13}.
\]

It is rejected at triple descent.  A control with one invalid pair map is not
a twisted triple.

## 7. Public non-product calibration

The opened quaternion model `Q8` is the first architecture calibration:

- `Q8` has no nontrivial decomposition into proper normal direct factors;
- its three cyclic order-four subinstruments overlap in the central order-two
  subinstrument;
- the operational reconstruction uses closed admitted contexts;
- the record reconstruction uses independently re-evaluated W3 supports;
- the two reconstructions agree on the public fixture;
- the pair and triple intersections are executable instrument pullbacks.

This is not evidence that nature or a generic quantum law selects those
regions.  The model was opened and designed as a calibration.  It proves only
that the overlap-first architecture is not logically restricted to globally
factorized systems.

## 8. Ontological reading

The proposed ontology is not “the universe is a tensor product of places.”
It is:

> A local place is a stable, independently accessible quantum process fragment
> whose operational boundary and factual boundary agree, and whose overlaps
> with other fragments are themselves physical quantum processes.

The full amplitude instrument remains quantum.  The stable record interface
is a derived commutative seam within it.  Locality would be earned only where
these two independently reconstructed organizations agree.

Even a successful future L0 result would not yet provide distance, direction,
causal order or spacetime.  It would provide candidate *local pieces* and
physical overlap maps.  Topology and influence remain separately prohibited
successors until explicitly authorized.

## 9. Open obligations for a future held-out cycle

This design still requires a future, separately authorized cycle to show that:

1. a frozen generic estimator recovers the overlap category on unseen data;
2. the category is not constructor truth in disguise;
3. ambiguous recoveries are retained as a groupoid;
4. pair/triple universal properties scale beyond the public finite model;
5. access postulates can eventually be weakened or derived;
6. complex boundary gauge is handled at the intended scope.

No such result is claimed here.
