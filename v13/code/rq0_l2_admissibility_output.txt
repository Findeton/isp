THE AXIOM.  A patch is a QUADRUPLE (boundary, declared task family,
declared preparation, admitted law) on the committed carrier.  It is
ADMISSIBLE iff
  (i-a)  A(B) = ker(F)          the boundary is the #103-minimal
                               sufficient boundary of its own family
                               -- ker, NOT Cycle B's Core (L2-22)
  (i-b)  F   = Pres_L(A(B))     the family is the boundary's Cycle B
                               closure (availability criterion)
  (ii-a) the family is non-empty and comp(F) = A(B) for all F in it
                               -- the realized legs write exactly
                               what the boundary asserts
  (ii-b) the reachable subprocess occupies every atom, and every
                               asserted identification is realized
