THE AXIOM.  A patch (boundary, declared task family, admitted law) on
the committed carrier is ADMISSIBLE iff
  (i-a)  A(B) = ker(F)          the boundary is the #103-minimal
                               sufficient boundary of its own family
  (i-b)  F   = Pres_L(A(B))     the family is the boundary's Cycle B
                               closure (availability criterion)
  (ii-a) comp(F) = A(B) for all F in the family   -- the realized legs
                               write exactly what the boundary asserts
  (ii-b) the reachable subprocess occupies every atom, and every
                               asserted identification is realized
