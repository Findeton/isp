### 2.4 The encoding layer, and the four cells

**The transport side's encoding** is GEN's defect law itself,
$\delta(Q) = \Sigma Q^{\mathsf T}\Sigma Q$. Written with $\sigma = $ conjugation
by $\Sigma$ it is the $\sigma$-twisted cocycle map

$$\delta(Q) \;=\; \sigma(Q)^{-1}\,Q ,\qquad
\delta(QQ') = \sigma(Q')^{-1}\delta(Q)\,Q' ,\qquad
\delta(\sigma(Q)) = \delta(Q)^{-1} .$$

The last identity is the parity that matters: **the transport side's encoding is
label-exchange ANTI-equivariant.**

**The deformation side's encoding** is HA's readout, rebuilt exactly as
`ha_successor_exact.py` builds it — rows the declared links in HA's own sorted
order, columns HA's own `sym_index` — with determinant **2** exactly (HA G28).
That matrix is the coefficient matrix of the linear system $q_{ij}e^i e^j = n_\ell$,
so it runs $q\to$counts (X03). And $q$ reproduces every declared link count at
every one of the 11 declared records (9 admissible, 2 negative controls, all
determinants reproduced against HA's table).

$$q_{11} = n_{e_1},\qquad q_{22} = n_{e_2},\qquad
q_{12} = \tfrac12\bigl(n_{e_1+e_2} - n_{e_1} - n_{e_2}\bigr).$$

Under the chart involution $\tau$ (the axis swap $e_1\leftrightarrow e_2$) this
map is **equivariant**: $E\circ\tau = \tau\circ E$, measured. **The two encodings
carry the same involution with opposite signs**, and that is where the unit ends
up.

**Why four cells, declared.** HA's datum is *one datum in two coordinate
systems*, so the readout is an endomorphism of one space only after (i) an
**identification** of the two coordinate triples and (ii) a **direction**. Both
are declared and both are swept, because HA's prose and HA's code place the

## 4. S1: the commuting square, censused

### 4.1 The census, and its two routes

A candidate is a map $\alpha : V \to G_C$ from the record datum space to the
completion group, and the square

$$\begin{array}{ccc}
V & \xrightarrow{\;E\;} & V\\[2pt]
\downarrow{\scriptstyle\alpha} & & \downarrow{\scriptstyle\alpha}\\[2pt]
G_C & \xrightarrow{\;\delta\;} & G_C
\end{array}
\qquad\qquad \delta\circ\alpha \;=\; \alpha\circ E$$

is evaluated **as measured matrices**, entry by entry, at every one of the
$p^3 = 125$ record cells. The candidate space is enumerated, never solved for by
one expression, and the count is computed by two genuinely independent
computations:

- **Route B (exhaustive enumeration, no group theory).** Every homomorphism
  $(V,+)\to G_C$ is enumerated by pairwise-commuting basis images — the
  candidate set for each slot is the full set of completions of order dividing
  $p$, and commutation is tested, not assumed. **41,665** homomorphisms are
  enumerated, and their image-size spectrum is measured
  $\{1{:}1,\ 5{:}41{,}664\}$. Each is then run through the matrix square at every
  record cell.
- **Route A (linear algebra).** The kernel of $E^{\mathsf T} - 2I$ over
  $\mathbb F_p$ is solved by Gaussian elimination, and its nonzero covectors are
  multiplied by the measured count of label-exchange-anti-invariant elements of
  order $p$, divided by the declared redundancy.

Neither route reads the other: the taint counter is measured **0** and both
invocation counters are nonzero. The enumeration's **own size** is checked
against an independent count built from the measured order-$p$ element count
alone — the cyclic subgroups of order $p$ number (elements)$/(p-1) = 336$, each
carrying $p^3 - 1 = 124$ surjections, plus the one trivial map, giving
$\mathbf{41{,}665}$ — so a dropped basis image cannot pass silently (G11).
`route-alias`, `route-a-lax`, `square-lax`, `matrix-lax`, `enum-drop` and
`hom-lax` all die at G10, G11 or G24.

### 4.3 What the square forces — measured, not cited

Every step below is a measurement in the run, not a citation:

1. **The image is cyclic of order 1 or $p$.** The completion group's order is
   $8! = 40{,}320$ and its $p$-part is computed by exact division to be $p^1$;
   independently, route B's own enumeration measures the image-size spectrum of
   **all 41,665** homomorphisms to be $\{1, 5\}$.
2. **The sign is forced to $-1$.** The square forces $\Sigma g\Sigma = g^{c}$
   with $c^2 \equiv 1$; the branch $c = +1$ requires an order-$p$ element
   commuting with the label exchange, and the count of those is measured **0**
   (against **48** anti-invariant ones).
3. **So the completion$\to$commutator map acts as SQUARING.** On the
   anti-invariant locus $\delta(Q) = \sigma(Q)^{-1}Q = Q^2$, verified element by
   element at **40 declared cells** — the first 8 of the 48 anti-invariant
   generators against all 5 of their powers, a named subset, not a draw.
4. **Hence the square reads $\lambda\circ E = 2\lambda$.**

> **The intertwining demands that 2 lie in the spectrum of the deformation
> side's record$\leftrightarrow$metric re-encoding.**

The re-encoding's spectrum is $\{1,1,\tfrac12\}$ in the registered direction
(and $\{1,\tfrac12,-1\}$ in the index identification). $2$ joins it only when
$2 \equiv \tfrac12$ or $2\equiv -1$, i.e. **only at $p = 3$** — swept over every
prime below 60 and measured — and $p = 3$ is one of the two primes at which HA's
residual $\rho = (1/6,1/6)$ does not reduce. `theorem-lax`, which asserts the
sign step instead of measuring it, dies at G12.
