## Overall verdict

**REJECT**

The note contains several correct formal observations, but its declared finite amplitude-instrument language does not contain its load-bearing W3 seed, the counterfactual-completion theorem is not a construction in the stated category, and the fixed-ambient meet theorem is not the physical pullback claimed by Definition 7.1. The provisional headline RQ0-L0-INTRINSIC-CHART-GROUPOID is therefore not earned.

Mathematically, a forgetful-fibre non-identifiability lemma, a conditional fixed-diagram closure lemma, the elementary no-selector lemma, the M2(C)-to-C no-retraction argument, and a conditional projector-compression Boolean map survive. Physically, these yield only a formal closure system relative to selected boundary objects, selected projector resolutions, a postulated complete counterfactual law, and a stipulated map class. They do not establish an independently addressable quantum process chart or physical localization. No pre-registered positive or negative L0 outcome is secured by this note.

## Ranked findings

### F1 — FATAL — The finite exact amplitude-instrument language excludes the note’s own W3 seed

Definition 2.1 asks for a finite category and an exact functor into finite-dimensional complex Hilbert spaces. In any finite category, the powers of every endomorphism eventually repeat: for an endomorphism c there are m less than n with c^m = c^n. Functoriality then forces rho(c)^m = rho(c)^n.

The seed in Section 10 has a closed endomorphism of the distinguished unit boundary with exact amplitude 1/2. With basis preparation eta = |00>, Q the |00><00| alternative projector, V = H2 tensor I, and probe f = <00|,

f V Q U eta = (1/sqrt(2))(1/sqrt(2)) = 1/2.

Calling this a closed scalar, as the note does, requires rho(1) = C. Its powers are 2^(-k), which never repeat. Hence the seed cannot be represented by any finite category satisfying Definition 2.1. The same problem is generic for any admitted closed amplitude strictly between zero and one in modulus.

The class in Definition 2.1 is not literally empty: a trivial category or a finite groupoid represented by roots of unity is an example. It is, however, not the advertised class of finite quantum amplitude instruments, and it does not contain the only nontrivial W3 construction offered in the note. The pin allowed declared partial composition; the delivery instead chose a category, whose endomorphism composition creates the contradiction.

Definition 2.1 also omits rho(1) = C, additive or dagger structure in the source, zero arrows, and a statement that the equations P = sum Q and projector orthogonality are equations of represented maps. The operational congruence is required to preserve only W3 zero/nonzero questions, not exact projector relations or the projector pullbacks later used by Rec. Thus those later objects need not be well defined on operational classes.

**Consequence:** C1, C2 and Theorem 10.1 are not instances of the declared ambient language. The general headline cannot rely on them.

**Required replacement sentence:** “All W3, envelope and completion claims in this note are conditional on an as-yet-unspecified exact process-presentation category; Definition 2.1 does not presently furnish a category containing the stated branch-memory seed.”

### F2 — FATAL — The counterfactual-completion theorem is not constructed, even apart from F1

The matrix identity for synchronized realized amplitudes is correct:

(f direct-sum f)/sqrt(2) composed with (a direct-sum a) and (eta direct-sum eta)/sqrt(2) equals f a eta.

The W3 seed also checks at the matrix level. For basis input |b,m>, U produces the two alternatives |0,m> and |1,m xor 1>; their record bits differ, so correlation holds. N produces |0,m> and |1,m>; their record bits agree, so correlation fails. V lets a basis probe see only alternatives with one fixed memory bit, so availability holds. E lets a fixed probe see alternatives from different memory sectors, and the corresponding cross term is nonzero, so it is an eraser. Occurrence and continuation-relative availability are correctly distinct in this calculation.

Those identities do not define the two claimed instruments. The note gives no object set, hom-sets, composition table, congruence, distinguished-family closure, or gauge action for either completion. Further:

1. The expression a direct-sum I is ill typed for an arrow between distinct boundary cuts unless an independently supplied identification provides an identity arrow between those cuts.
2. A block projector family P_r direct-sum 0 does not sum to the identity of H direct-sum H. Mapping the seed identity to a block identity therefore conflicts with a strong functor preserving identities and projector resolutions. Adding separate block boundary objects could repair the typing only by supplying the block chart in the ambient ontology.
3. Definition 3.1 requires complete admitted preparation/probe families and every admitted continuation of the selected type. The added synchronized and block operations are never retested against that quantifier, so the claimed block W3 diagrams have not been shown to be internal W3 diagrams of the completion.
4. The fact that one diagram W generates A does not imply that every W3-positive diagram in the synchronized completion generates the whole completion. The claimed absence of every proper W3 envelope is not proved.
5. Two block charts exchanged by a swap do not by object count alone give a groupoid non-equivalent to a one-object action groupoid. A transitive action groupoid can be equivalent to the stabilizer groupoid of one object. “Embedded-chart structure” and its equivalences are not defined, so the asserted non-equivalence has no proved invariant.

The general logical lemma survives only conditionally: if two well-defined operational completions have isomorphic realized restrictions and a specified chart invariant differs, that invariant is not determined by the realized restriction. The note has not supplied the required pair in its stated category. The block objects, block projectors and block embeddings also insert precisely the independent-address structure whose appearance is then reported.

**Consequence:** Theorem 10.1, Corollary 10.2, the grounding trilemma as an exhaustive result, and the boxed claim that realized amplitudes and record laws fail to determine the displayed groupoid are unproved at the stated scope.

**Required replacement sentence:** “The direct-sum matrices exhibit an exact realized-amplitude identity and motivate a conditional completion-sensitivity lemma, but no pair of finite exact amplitude instruments with non-equivalent intrinsic chart groupoids has been constructed.”

### F3 — MAJOR — The internal W3 construction removes access_operations but still begins with selected support-bearing structure

Definitions 3.1–3.3 correctly derive preserve and erase classes by testing admitted continuations, keep Other continuations, use an internal no-write candidate, type the cross-sector eraser expression, and distinguish occurrence from availability. Those are real improvements over the public access_operations schema.

They do not derive the three boundary objects, the write candidate, the no-write candidate, either projector resolution, or the refinement map. These are selected before W3 is evaluated. Boundary typing and the admitted projector list can already encode a decomposition of the process. Enumerating all positive diagrams avoids choosing one representative, but it does not show that the primitive boundary and projector ontology is support-neutral.

The completeness sentence is also stronger than the formulas. Preparations are used only at x0 and probes only at x2; admitted preparations or effects at x1 and the other selected boundaries are not tested. Availability tests f V Q as a nonzero effect without composing the admitted preparation family. The label “no-write” is not derived: failure of H-corr only says that some fine alternatives in one coarse record sector occur, not that N physically leaves a pre-existing record unwritten.

Most importantly, the generated envelope includes every continuation from x1 to x2. In a genuine product instrument this normally includes operations on both factors and their composites. The tensor and identical-copy controls simply assert that a local W3 diagram generates a proper factor; they do not show this after applying their own all-continuations rule. Restricting the arrow type so that only one factor appears would make the factor boundary typing load-bearing and must itself pass no-smuggling.

**Consequence:** For a chosen typed W3 experiment, the note defines a formal closure. It does not show that a stable record determines a proper chart, nor that the selected typed boundaries are innocent.

**Required replacement sentence:** “For each selected typed W3 diagram, the construction forms its formal operational closure; the note does not derive the selected boundaries, projector refinement, no-write interpretation, or independent addressability from the record.”

### F4 — MAJOR — Envelope existence is only a conditional closure-system result; gauge covariance is not proved

If closed subinstruments are literal saturated subsets of one already fixed ambient structure and all closure conditions are universal, their intersection is closed. Under those assumptions, Theorem 5.3 is the standard closure-operator argument and uniqueness for one fixed W is correct.

The assumptions are not supplied at the instrument level. A closed subinstrument is not assigned its own gauge group or a gauge homomorphism making its inclusion a strong map. The note explicitly declines to saturate one chart by all gauge images, but Definition 2.1 makes gauge data part of every instrument. “The family is equivariant” does not by itself turn each family member into an object of QIns_fin^str. Operational-equivalence saturation also does not guarantee that exact represented projector relations descend.

Theorem 5.4 is sound for a separately defined isomorphism of closure systems. Its gauge specialization does not follow: G_D was described as natural unitary changes of boundary presentation, not as an action by strong automorphisms on category arrows and all distinguished families. Consequently gW and the claimed equality of envelopes are not typed by the definitions.

Uniqueness stops exactly where the note says it stops: it is uniqueness for a fixed selected diagram only. Nothing selects a diagram, makes its envelope proper, or supplies independent addressability.

**Consequence:** A formal fixed-diagram closure lemma survives. The asserted object in the stated strong-map/gauge category does not.

**Required replacement sentence:** “Given a separately specified gauge-equivariant closure system of strict ambient subinstruments, intersection yields the unique closure of a fixed diagram; the present definitions do not establish that hypothesis for QIns_fin^str.”

### F5 — MAJOR — Theorem 7.3 is not the physical pullback of Definition 7.1

Definition 7.1 states the ordinary universal property and correctly quantifies over every test object and compatible cone in the chosen category. Uniqueness up to isomorphism is standard.

Theorem 7.3 changes categories. It proves only that literal intersection is the meet in the poset Sub_cl(D), whose arrows are inclusions. That is a tautological poset pullback. It does not prove a pullback in QIns_fin^str, the category named by Definition 7.1 and postulate P3.

General strong embeddings have boundary isometries into target Hilbert spaces; their images need not be literal boundary objects or literal subinstruments of D. The note does not define image factorization, prove that every compatible strong-map cone factors through the literal intersection, or show that the inclusion of Sub_cl(D) into QIns_fin^str creates pullbacks. “Transport to the images” therefore does not handle independently presented embeddings. At most, already strictified common-ambient inclusions have a meet after the common presentation and its handles have been supplied.

The note correctly concedes that even this meet may carry no W3 record. Terminal RQ0-A is a valid narrow positive control for declared maps and projector pullbacks in its finite thin cover category, but it does not upgrade the poset meet to the general physical pullback.

**Consequence:** Process-level fixed-presentation meet survives; physical overlap of independently presented charts is not established.

**Required replacement sentence:** “Theorem 7.3 is a meet theorem in the fixed-ambient poset of strict closed subinstruments; it is not a pullback theorem in QIns_fin^str and does not construct overlap for independently presented embeddings.”

### F6 — MAJOR — The no-selection lemma is correct, but the action groupoid is neither forced nor correctly identified with gauge

Theorem 9.1 is the elementary fixed-point argument: a transitive action on more than one element has no globally fixed member, so no invariant selector exists. That theorem survives.

Aut(D), however, was not identified with the boundary-gauge group G_D. An ambient physical symmetry can exchange two distinct physical subsystems without making them gauge copies. In that case the theorem forbids a selector from symmetry-invariant data; it does not license calling a named subsystem a gauge fixing. Conversely, a mere boundary re-presentation should be quotiented or represented according to the separately declared gauge semantics.

The action groupoid is an available bookkeeping object, not a consequence of no-selection. The invariant family or orbit is also natural. Moreover, the chosen action groupoid contains only pointed isomorphisms extending to ambient automorphisms. Pointed chart isomorphisms that do not extend ambiently are omitted, so it is not the full groupoid of pointed chart presentations claimed by the prose.

**Consequence:** The abstract no-selector result survives, but it does not earn the intrinsic-chart-groupoid headline or the statement that the groupoid is the uniquely correct physical output.

**Required replacement sentence:** “Theorem 9.1 proves only the absence of an invariant member selector for the stated action; whether physical copies, an invariant orbit, or an action groupoid is appropriate requires a separate distinction between gauge re-presentation and physical symmetry.”

### F7 — MAJOR — Rec is a correct compression calculation inside a category defined to make it true, not a derived general record functor

With J an isometry whose range reduces every target record projector, compression is a Boolean homomorphism. If e = J J-dagger, reduction gives eP = Pe, and for Boolean projectors P and Q,

J-dagger P Q J = J-dagger P e Q J

equals the product of their two compressions. Identity, complement, and the direct-versus-composite equation follow. The variance A to B inducing Rec(B) to Rec(A) is correctly contravariant.

Definition 8.1, however, assumes that the compressed target resolution is exactly the source W3-certified resolution and that the full write/preserve/erase/no-write diagram is mapped. W3 stability and record compatibility are therefore hypotheses, not consequences. The category of such maps is not shown to be independently physically populated, and the operational congruence does not make exact projectors representative-independent. Gauge covariance inherits the missing gauge action from F4.

Restricting the arrows this severely is legitimate, but it makes Theorem 8.2 a conditional restriction functor, not evidence that arbitrary physical chart maps possess record descent. The note’s own Section 8.1 essentially acknowledges this limitation.

**Consequence:** The projector-compression algebra survives conditionally; a substantive post-chart record functor on physical chart maps is not established.

**Required replacement sentence:** “Projector compression defines a contravariant Boolean homomorphism on maps assumed to be reducing and exactly W3-compatible; the note does not derive record compatibility for the physical chart maps.”

The theorem does not independently prove that arbitrary proposed support labels are physically admissible, nor does it establish a general obstruction to internal support. These are useful foundations, but they support only a theorem about a formal closure system. They do not establish the provisional intrinsic-chart-groupoid headline, independent addressability, physical localization, or any later-physics statement.
