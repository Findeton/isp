## Abstract

Six terminal units asked, in six different ways, what makes a coarse patch of
a process a legitimate one. Each answered negatively, and the negatives are
now complete enough to be a positive statement about where legitimacy lives.
It is not intrinsic to the patch, not certifiable by a family of contexts, not
a structural property of the law at coarse grain, not carried by the declared
state, not remembered by the law at a full-group configuration, and not
carried by an attached generation history even when that history is carried at
amplitude level. What is left is that legitimacy is relative to a **declared
arena**.

This paper states that thesis with the six-unit theorem record, and then does
the one thing the thesis makes both possible and necessary: it runs the first
**invariance census**. An admissible arena is a declared tuple drawn exactly
from the terminal fixtures — a patch declaration, a committed law, a named
state, an admitted relabelling, a checkpoint-phase switching. The family is
enumerated; its size is $1{,}648{,}128$, computed. Eight quantities the tower
built, plus one imported from the composition-defect work, are censused.

**Every verdict here is derived from measured motion, coordinate by
coordinate.** Three quantities are fixed under every arena coordinate: the
one-step closed holonomy (constant at $\zeta_8^4 = -1$, exhaustively over all
$512$ admitted lifts under all $512$ switchings — $262{,}144$ instances, none
moving), the rigidity classifier, and the occupancy defect $\omega$, which is
fixed **at its constructed neutral value** and is therefore recorded
`ARENA-INERT` rather than invariant. The other five move, each in coordinates
its own definition consumes: the fine-grained transition data with the law,
the generation profile and the legitimacy certificate with the patch and the
law, $\varepsilon$ with the patch and the state, the seam datum with the patch.

The result the unit carries is an **asymmetry between two quantities that face
the identical action**. The legitimacy certificate and the cross-arena seam
datum are declared alike, are swept alike over the same $3{,}219$ instances of
the same fibration, and read the same coordinate. At a fixed patch
declaration and with the naming quotiented out, the certificate takes $5$
distinct values as the law ranges over the five committed ones; the seam takes
$1$. Whether a declared patch is legitimate moves with the arena's law; what
two declared patches share on intersection does not. And the two halves of
that second clause are not of the same kind, which is the discipline this
census exists to enforce: the seam's fixity under the law and the state is
**definitional** — a source scan shows its definition names neither — while
its invariance under the admitted relabellings is **measured**, with a control
that has teeth: an admitted relabelling leaves it fixed at all $3{,}219$
instances, and a mis-conventioned one moves it at $2{,}772$ of $3{,}024$.

The composition defect $\Delta^{B}$ transports exactly, and the
arena-variation question then cannot be posed at all: $\Delta^{B}$ vanishes
identically on monomial unitaries by a theorem, and no committed law admits a
non-monomial operation. That is `BLOCKED-AT-THE-NON-MONOMIAL-ADMITTED-OPERATION`.

## 1. The thesis

**ADOPTED-ARENA-RELATIVITY.** The legitimacy of a coarse patch is not
intrinsic; not certifiable by families of contexts; not law-structural at
coarse grain; not state-carried; not law-remembered at a full-group
configuration; not provenance-carriable even at amplitude level. It is
relative to a **declared arena**.

Each clause of that sentence is a terminal theorem of a distinct unit, and the
six together are the reason the sentence is a residue rather than a
preference: every alternative locus for legitimacy that the programme could
name was built, instrumented, and measured to fail. The fifth clause carries
its unit's own scope and is stated with it: branch C's impossibility is proved
at a **full-group configuration**, a hypothesis carried by $25$ of the $4845$
declared states, with $1200$ of them carrying no symmetry at all.

The thesis is prose. What is gated in this paper is the census that follows
from it — which quantities survive arena change, and in which coordinates.

## 3. The admissible arena

Everything in this section is fixed before any fixture value is computed. The
receipt's gate order proves it within the run: `SYN-FREEZE` records the
declarations with the census evaluation counter still at zero, and the source
scans of `SYN-SWITCH-SCOPE` decide, by reading the source rather than by
assertion, which quantities can see which coordinates at all.

### 3.1 The family

An **admissible arena** is a declared tuple

$$A = (d,\; L,\; \rho,\; \sigma,\; \varphi)$$

with each coordinate drawn exactly from the terminal fixtures — no new object
is introduced:

- $d$, a **patch declaration**: the legitimate corrected tomographic minimum,
  the forged aligned manufactured $2{+}1{+}1$, or the forged aligned
  manufactured $2{+}2$ — the three committed declarations at the committed
  carrier of five configurations. Count $3$.
- $L$, a **committed law**: `DET`, `FUNNEL`, `REV`, `FUNNEL-CLOSURE` or the
  counter-law, of sizes $3125$, $21$, $120$, $3006$, $120$. Count $5$.
- $\rho$, a **named state**: the thirteen named declared states of stage 5's
  state-map table, read out of that unit's own source rather than retyped.
  Count $13$.
- $\sigma$, an **admitted relabelling**: an admitted isomorphism of the
  declared data in branch C's sense — a carrier relabelling fixing the law
  setwise, the declared state, and the declared preparation.
- $\varphi$, a **checkpoint-phase switching**: one of the vertex switchings of
  the committed one-step diagram, an eighth-root phase at each vertex of the
  loop component with one vertex fixed as the global phase. The diagram has
  $3$ free vertices, hence $512$ switchings, computed from the diagram.

The admitted-isomorphism group is a function of the declared law and state
(branch C, Thm 7.1), so the family is a **fibered** product over the $65$
law-state pairs rather than a flat one: a flat product would count
relabellings that no arena admits. Summed over the fibers there are $1073$
admitted relabellings, and

$$\lvert \mathcal{A}\rvert \;=\; 3 \times 1073 \times 512 \;=\; 1{,}648{,}128 .$$

Every factor is measured and the product is gated (`SYN-FAMILY`); no count in
this paper is typed.

**What the fibering buys, and what it costs, both disclosed.** Across the five
committed laws the fibration yields only **two** distinct fiber-size patterns:
`DET`, `FUNNEL`, `REV` and `FUNNEL-CLOSURE` have the identical vector of
thirteen fiber sizes and the counter-law is trivial at all thirteen states, so
the dependence on the law is real but degenerate. In the other direction the
fibering does real work on a reported number: the admitted relabellings
realize $30$ distinct patches, and over a flat product of those with the
thirteen named states $\varepsilon$ takes $17$ values, where over the fibered
family it takes $14$ — the number this census reports. The fibration
suppresses three $\varepsilon$ values a flat reading would report. It changes
no verdict, and it is disclosed because it is the fibration doing real work on
a reported quantity.

### 3.2 The action, and how much of it there is

An arena change acts on the **whole configuration**, never on one argument
alone. The realized data of $A$ is the triple
$(\sigma \cdot d, \sigma \cdot L, \sigma \cdot \rho)$ together with the
$\varphi$-switched amplitude diagram. But how much that moves is a
measurement, not a claim, and the self-test measures it before it uses it:
over all $1073$ admitted relabellings in all $65$ fibers, **none moves the
law** and **none moves the state** — the stabilizer fixes the law setwise and
the state pointwise by construction. So what an admitted relabelling actually
moves is the naming of the patch, which is exactly the coordinate a
name-reading statistic would betray itself on, and it is also the reason a
mis-conventioned control has to be built outside the admitted group before it
can bite a law-reading quantity at all (§7.3).

### 3.4 The verdicts

Only the pin's four names are used, and each is decided by measurement.

- `ARENA-INVARIANT-⟨Qk⟩` — the value does not move under **any** arena
  coordinate, and it is nondegenerate: its single family value is not the
  constructed neutral object, and a declared **ambient witness** outside the
  family exhibits the quantity taking another value.
- `ARENA-INERT-⟨Qk⟩` — does not move, and its single family value **is** the
  constructed neutral.
- `ARENA-ARTIFACT-⟨Qk⟩` — moves under some arena coordinate; the orbit is
  counted, both raw and with the naming quotiented out.
- `BLOCKED-AT-⟨object⟩` — the census cannot be run for want of a named object,
  or the arena-variation question cannot be posed at the committed fixtures.

The trichotomy is deliberately coarse, and it is coarse because it is
declaration-free: since every quantity here is a function of the arena tuple,
the only reading of "invariant" that does not smuggle in a stipulation about
which coordinates are "the arena" is *constant across the family*. All the
discriminating content therefore lives in the per-coordinate table, which is
measured cell by cell, and that is where §5 and §9 do their work. Two further
pre-registered outcomes govern the census as a whole:
`RQ0-SYNTH-CENSUS-COMPLETE` — which is unreachable while any must-pass gate or
anchor has failed, so no mutant of this instrument can flip a verdict and
still be reported complete — and `ACTION-TOO-WEAK`, a kill, if the arena
action moves nothing at all.

### 5.3 What does not

**Q2, the one-step closed holonomy — `ARENA-INVARIANT` (positive control).**

**Q7, $\omega$ — `ARENA-INERT`.** One value over the whole family, and that

other declared patch — and a neutral built without that skip rule would not be
a value the quantity could ever take, so the predicate that had to recognise
it could never fire.

## 8. Q-OPT: the composition defect

The pin admits $\Delta^{B}(U_2,U_1) = B(U_2U_1) - B(U_2)B(U_1)$ only behind an
explicit exact-transport gate, with `BLOCKED-AT-IMPORT` recorded plainly if it
does not transport.

**The arithmetic transports (lemma, verified).** $\Delta^{B}$ is rebuilt here
in exact $\mathbb{Q}(\zeta_8)$, as $4$-tuples of Fractions reduced mod
$x^4+1$, and reproduces the committed value at the Hadamard exactly.
`BLOCKED-AT-IMPORT` was available and is not used.

**The defect vanishes on monomials (theorem).** $B$ of a monomial unitary —
a permutation matrix times a diagonal of phases — is its underlying
permutation matrix, and $B$ is multiplicative on monomials; hence
$\Delta^{B} \equiv 0$ on every monomial pair, for every phase. This is a
theorem, and it is confirmed rather than discovered: exhaustively over **all
$14{,}400$** composable pairs of admitted reversible operations at the
committed carrier, and over a declared **phased** monomial sweep of $960$
further pairs, since the admitted reversible lifts are monomial and not merely
permutation matrices — a sweep built from $0/1$ entries alone would test only
the trivial-phase representatives and would leave the arena's own phase
coordinate silently fixed at zero. Nothing here is sampled: a "sample" of $24$ of
the $120$ permutations taken in enumeration order is not a sample at all but
the point stabilizer of one configuration, a subgroup, and its square is a
subgroup's square.

**The object transports, and the question then cannot be posed.**
$\Delta^{B}$ is a function of admitted amplitude operations. Measured: the
only declared amplitude family at the committed carrier lifts a support step —
the two-configuration merge — that **no committed law admits**, at all five
laws; and the only admitted operations carrying unitary lifts at all are the
reversible ones, of which `DET` and `REV` have $120$ and the other three laws
one apiece, counted by two independent routes. So there is no admissible arena
at which $\Delta^{B}$ could take a second value: not because it fails to
transport, and not because it is measured constant across arenas, but because
the arena-variation question has no admitted operation to be asked about.

**Verdict: `BLOCKED-AT-THE-NON-MONOMIAL-ADMITTED-OPERATION`.** That is a
`BLOCKED-AT-⟨object⟩`, one of the pin's four names, and it is chosen from all
of them by measured conditions: the selector is exercised over declared
condition tuples and every pre-registered name is reached, which is gated
(`SYN-QOPT-NAMES`); a mutant that admits a non-monomial operation makes the
live selection come out as `ARENA-ARTIFACT-Q-OPT` instead. Interference, as
the composition defect measures it, is not *invisible* in every arena the RQ0
tower declares — it is *unaskable* there.

**The positive control, and what actually separates its two halves.** The same
instrument returns a non-zero defect on $384$ of the $512$ members of the
declared non-monomial lift family, so the zero above is a fact about the
admitted operations and not about the instrument. What separates the
remaining $128$ is not that their square is again non-monomial — $256$ of the
$384$ non-zero members have non-monomial squares too, so that property does
not single them out. The criterion is two-sided and is gated:
$\Delta^{B}(U,U) = 0$ exactly when $U^2$ is again **fully unbiased**, every
entry of the carried block of modulus $2^{-1/2}$ — $128$ of $128$ on one side
and $0$ of $384$ on the other. It is a one-line consequence of $B(U)$ being
the flat matrix for every member of this family, so that $B(U)B(U)$ is flat
too and the defect is $B(U^2)$ minus the flat matrix.

## 9. The operational consequence: the declared-arena discipline

The census is what makes RUNBOOK §15 operational rather than hortatory.

1. **Every unit's pin declares its arena as data** — boundary, family, law,
   state, arena, and provenance if carried — not as prose.
2. **Claims of physical significance are entered only where a quantity is
   MEASURED fixed under a coordinate, with a control that discriminates.**
   Not where a label says invariant, and not where a signature says blind. In
   this corpus that licenses: the closed holonomy, under every arena
   coordinate including the gauge, exhaustively; the rigidity classifier; and
   the seam datum **in the relabelling coordinate only**, where it is fixed at
   $3{,}219$ instances with $2{,}688$ of them discriminating and a
   mis-conventioned action moving it at $2{,}772$ of $3{,}024$. It does not
   license the seam's law-fixity or state-fixity, which are facts about its
   signature; and it does not license the generation profile as against the
   certificate, since the two have identical measured law-dependence and
   receive the same verdict.
3. **Arena artifacts may serve as instruments but never as conclusions.** That
   is the standing status of the legitimacy certificate and of $\varepsilon$:
   both are good instruments — the certificate decides admissibility, and
   $\varepsilon$ is exactly the residual Bayes error — and neither may head a
   physical claim.
4. **Inert is not invariant.** A quantity constant at its neutral value across
   the family has been measured to say nothing there. $\omega$ is the type
   specimen and the reason the distinction is first-class — and the
   distinction has to stay reachable for every quantity, gated by feeding each
   constructed neutral through the decision path.
5. **Where an instrument enforces the arena action, §14 applies**: it must be
   self-tested under that action, freshly evaluated, over a tested set fixed
   by declaration, with a mis-conventioned control that moves — and, where the
   admitted group cannot move a coordinate, with a control built outside it.
