## Abstract

Six terminal units asked, in six different ways, what makes a coarse patch of
a process a legitimate one. Each answered negatively, and the negatives are
now complete enough to be a positive statement about where legitimacy lives.
It is not intrinsic to the patch, not certifiable by a family of contexts, not
a structural property of the law at coarse grain, not carried by the declared
state, not remembered by the law, and not carried by an attached generation
history even when that history is carried at amplitude level. What is left is
that legitimacy is relative to a **declared arena**.

This paper states that thesis with the six-unit theorem record, and then does
the one thing the thesis makes both possible and necessary: it runs the first
**invariance census**. An admissible arena is a declared tuple drawn exactly
from the terminal fixtures — a patch declaration, a committed law, a named
state, an admitted relabelling, a checkpoint-phase switching. The family is
enumerated; its size is $1{,}648{,}128$, computed. Eight quantities the tower
built, plus one imported from the composition-defect work, are censused: for
each, the arena action on its arguments is declared before any fixture value
is computed, and the verdict is then gated.

Two quantities move. The legitimacy certificate of a **fixed** patch
declaration takes $123$ distinct values across the family and moves with the
arena's law: the same declared patch is certified differently in different
admissible arenas, which is the thesis itself, measured. The concordance
defect $\varepsilon$ takes $14$ values and moves with the declared state, up
to $8$ distinct values at one patch as the state varies alone.

Six do not move. The fine-grained transition data, the one-step closed
holonomy (constant at $\zeta_8^4 = -1$ over the whole admitted lift family and
under all $512$ switchings), the rigidity classifier, the name-blind
generation profile, the occupancy defect $\omega$ and the cross-arena seam
datum are all fixed under the coordinates declared to act on them, with zero
equivariance failures in $3{,}219$ instances per quantity of which $2{,}144$
actually move the configuration. Of the six, $\omega$ is fixed **at its
neutral value** and is recorded `ARENA-INERT`; the inert/invariant distinction
is decided against a constructed neutral object, not a pattern.

The seam is the finding worth carrying forward. What two declared patches
share on intersection does not move under any arena coordinate declared to act
on it, while the legitimacy of either patch separately does. The composition
defect $\Delta^{B}$ transports exactly and is then identically zero on every
admissible arena, for a reason the census can name.

## 3. The admissible arena

Everything in this section is fixed before any fixture value is computed. The
receipt's gate order proves it: `SYN-FREEZE` records the declarations with the
census evaluation counter still at zero, and `SYN-SWITCH-SCOPE` decides by
source scan which quantities can see amplitude data at all.

### 3.1 The family

An **admissible arena** is a declared tuple

$$A = (d,\; L,\; \rho,\; \sigma,\; \varphi)$$

with each coordinate drawn exactly from the terminal fixtures — no new object
is introduced:

- $d$, a **patch declaration**: the legitimate corrected tomographic minimum,
  the forged aligned manufactured $2{+}1{+}1$, or the forged aligned
  manufactured $2{+}2$ — the three committed declarations at the committed
  carrier of five configurations. Count $3$.
- $L$, a **committed law**: `DET`, `FUNNEL`, `REV`, `FUNNEL-CLOSURE` or the
  counter-law, of sizes $3125$, $21$, $120$, $3006$, $120$. Count $5$.
- $\rho$, a **named state**: the thirteen named declared states of stage 5's
  state-map table, read out of that unit's own source rather than retyped.
  Count $13$.
- $\sigma$, an **admitted relabelling**: an admitted isomorphism of the
  declared data in branch C's sense — a carrier relabelling fixing the law
  setwise, the declared state, and the declared preparation.
- $\varphi$, a **checkpoint-phase switching**: one of the vertex switchings of
  the committed one-step diagram, an eighth-root phase at each vertex of the
  loop component with one vertex fixed as the global phase. The diagram has
  $3$ free vertices, hence $512$ switchings, computed from the diagram.

The admitted-isomorphism group is a function of the declared law and state
(branch C, Thm 7.1), so the family is a **fibered** product over the $65$
law-state pairs rather than a flat one: a flat product would count
relabellings that no arena admits. Summed over the fibers there are $1073$
admitted relabellings, and

$$\lvert \mathcal{A}\rvert \;=\; 3 \times 1073 \times 512 \;=\; 1{,}648{,}128 .$$

Every factor is measured and the product is gated (`SYN-FAMILY`); no count in
this paper is typed.

### 3.2 The action

An arena change acts on the **whole configuration**, never on one argument
alone. The realized data of $A$ is the triple
$(\sigma \cdot d, \sigma \cdot L, \sigma \cdot \rho)$
together with the $\varphi$-switched amplitude diagram.
Because $\sigma$ is admitted, $\sigma\!\cdot\!L = L$ and
$\sigma\!\cdot\!\rho = \rho$ setwise, so what an admitted relabelling actually
moves is the naming of the patch — which is exactly the coordinate a
name-reading statistic would betray itself on.

### 3.4 The verdicts

Only the pin's four names are used.

- `ARENA-INVARIANT-⟨Qk⟩` — the value is fixed under every coordinate declared
  to act on it, and it is nondegenerate: its single family value is not the
  constructed neutral object, and a declared **ambient witness** outside the

`RQ0-SYNTH-CENSUS-COMPLETE` when every verdict is entered with both controls

## 8. Q-OPT: the composition defect

The pin admits $\Delta^{B}(U_2,U_1) = B(U_2U_1) - B(U_2)B(U_1)$ only behind an
explicit exact-transport gate, with `BLOCKED-AT-IMPORT` recorded plainly if it
does not transport. It transports, in both senses, and the interesting fact is
what happens next.

**The arithmetic transports.** $\Delta^{B}$ is rebuilt here in exact
$\mathbb{Q}(\zeta_8)$, as $4$-tuples of Fractions reduced mod $x^4+1$, and
reproduces the committed value at the Hadamard exactly.

**The object transports, and is then empty.** $\Delta^{B}$ is a function of
admitted amplitude operations. Measured: the only declared amplitude family at
the committed carrier lifts a support step — the two-configuration merge —
that **no committed law admits**, at all five laws; and the only admitted
operations carrying unitary lifts at all are the reversible ones, of which
`DET` and `REV` have $120$ and the other three laws one apiece, counted by two
independent routes. Over the admitted reversible pairs the defect is
identically zero, exactly and without exception. The positive control fires:
the same instrument returns a non-zero defect on $384$ of the $512$ members of
the declared non-monomial lift family, so the zero is a fact about the admitted
operations and not about the instrument.

**Verdict: `ARENA-INERT-Q-OPT`**, at its neutral value on every admissible
arena, with the obstruction to content named:
`BLOCKED-AT-THE-NON-MONOMIAL-ADMITTED-OPERATION`. Interference, as the
composition defect measures it, is invisible in every arena the RQ0 tower
declares — not because it fails to transport, but because no committed law
admits an operation on which it could be non-zero.

## 9. The operational consequence: the declared-arena discipline

The census is what makes RUNBOOK §15 operational rather than hortatory.

1. **Every unit's pin declares its arena as data** — boundary, family, law,
   state, arena, and provenance if carried — not as prose.
2. **Claims of physical significance are entered only for quantities gated
   invariant across the unit's admissible arenas.** In this corpus that
   licenses the fine-grained transition data, the closed holonomy, the
   rigidity classifier, the name-blind generation profile and the seam datum.
3. **Arena artifacts may serve as instruments but never as conclusions.** That
   is the standing status of the legitimacy certificate and of $\varepsilon$:
   both are good instruments — the certificate decides admissibility, and
   $\varepsilon$ is exactly the residual Bayes error — and neither may head a
   physical claim.
4. **Inert is not invariant.** A quantity constant at its neutral value across
   the family has been measured to say nothing there. $\omega$ is the type
   specimen and the reason the distinction is first-class.
5. **Where an instrument enforces the arena action, §14 applies**: it must be
   self-tested under that action, with a mis-conventioned control that moves.

Finite; one committed carrier of five configurations; the five committed law

This paper constructs no locality, topology, causality, spacetime, field, QFT

declared tuple of committed fixtures; no temporal, causal or spatial reading is
made of "path", "step" or "checkpoint". The census is a census of the

not claim that the invariant five exhaust the invariants, nor that a larger
arena family would leave them invariant. It closes the RQ0 line; it opens no
arena-relativity research line.

## Appendix A. Deviations

1. **The family is fibered, not a flat product.** The pin writes the family
   with a product sign. The admitted-isomorphism group is a function of the
   declared law and state (branch C, Thm 7.1), so the relabelling coordinate is
   fibered over the $65$ law-state pairs; a flat product would count
   relabellings no arena admits. Size computed as
   $3 \times 1073 \times 512 = 1{,}648{,}128$ and gated.
2. **The thirteen named states are extracted from stage 5's source.** They are
   a list local to that unit's separation routine, with no importable symbol.
   Rather than retype thirteen exact states, the census parses the literal out
   of the terminal module and evaluates it in a namespace containing only the
   fixtures it names; the count and every $\varepsilon$ triple are then anchored
   against stage 5's own committed receipt table.
3. **READS versus ACTS is a declaration the pin does not fix.** The pin
   requires the arena action to be declared per quantity before fixture truth;
   it does not say which coordinate is a quantity's argument. The split used
   here is stated in §3.3 and justified there. To keep the declaration from
   doing hidden work, the census measures and prints the full dependence
   profile of every quantity over every coordinate.
4. **Q-OPT was not blocked.** The pre-registered `BLOCKED-AT-IMPORT` was
   available and is not used: the transport succeeded in both senses. The
   verdict is `ARENA-INERT-Q-OPT` with the obstruction to content recorded as
   `BLOCKED-AT-THE-NON-MONOMIAL-ADMITTED-OPERATION`.
5. **The non-monomial control is not universal.** $\Delta^{B}$ is non-zero on
   $384$ of the $512$ lift-family members, not on all: the remaining $128$ are
   the phase choices whose square is again non-monomial, where the Born map
   happens to compose. The scope gate says so.
6. **The $687$-law census was not needed.** The pin admits it "only where a
   candidate demands it". No censused quantity demanded it; the identity-free
   side enters only as Q3's declared ambient nondegeneracy witness, at three
   configurations, where it supplies $745$ admissible proper charts.
