## 5. Independently addressable complement

A candidate local address object \(A\subset\mathcal M_D\) is positive only if
there is a nontrivial complement \(B\) and every following test passes:

1. **Independent selection:** each primitive class used to generate \(A\) or
   \(B\) is separately admitted at the relevant boundary type.
2. **Mixed joint implementation:** every required pair \((a,b)\in A\times B\)
   has an `IMPLEMENTED` row in both declared orders.  Missing rows are not
   synthesized from matrices.
3. **Law commutation:** the two orders have the same exact operational class
   and amplitude law modulo the declared boundary gauge.
4. **Faithfulness:** the multiplication map

   \[
   A\times B\longrightarrow AB
   \]

   is injective after the declared central/gauge quotient.  Any untyped or
   unexplained collapse fails addressability.
5. **Closure:** \(A\), \(B\), and the implemented product object \(AB\) close
   under the admitted typed composition table and involution.
6. **Trivial typed intersection:** their intersection is only the declared
   identity/central record structure.
7. **Represented algebra test:** their exact accessible star-algebras commute,
   have the declared scalar/central intersection, generate the joint algebra,
   and satisfy the finite product-dimension theorem.
8. **Restriction stability:** the same statements hold after every claimed
   regional restriction and overlap.

These tests define operational co-addressability.  They do not define
spacelike separation.

### 11.5 Localization groupoid
