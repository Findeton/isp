## 2. The quintuple, and the axiom in both variants

### Definition 2.1 — the quintuple `[FIN]`

A **patch** is the quintuple

$$
P=(B,\ \mathfrak F,\ X_0,\ L,\ H)
$$

with the first four components exactly B″'s, and $H=(g_1,\dots,g_m)$ a
**declared generation history**: a finite list of admitted operations. Its
**checkpoints** are the intermediate composites $C_t=g_t\circ\cdots\circ g_1$,
and the **carried certificate** at checkpoint $t$ is the B″-certificate at
$C_t$ — the record $\operatorname{comp}(C_t)$ the composite writes, the closure
the law assigns it, the four clause bits, and the reachable subprocess.

**The pin calls this certificate *fine-grained*, and it is worth recording at
once that it is not fine-grained about the operation.** Its first component is
the record the checkpoint writes, and every later component is computed from
that record together with the law and the preparation; nothing else about $C_t$
enters. So a chain of carried certificates carries exactly the sequence of
written records. That is Theorem 6.1, and it is why the lossy kill against V-CL
is unconditional rather than exhibited.

### Definition 2.2 — the axiom `[FIN]`

$P$ is **L5-admissible** iff the quadruple $(B,\mathfrak F,X_0,L)$ is
B″-admissible and

- **(P1)** every $g_t$ lies in $L$, the composites are formed, and
  $\operatorname{comp}(C_m)=A(B)$: the carried path is admitted and its
  endpoint is the declared patch;
- **(P2)** each carried certificate equals the certificate recomputed against
  the law at its own checkpoint.

Two readings of (P2) are carried, both computed, because the choice is
load-bearing and the pin does not fix it. **(P2)-weak** is the reading above:
the certificate must be *honest*. **(P2)-strong** additionally requires each
checkpoint to be B″-*admissible*. Section 4 measures both.

**(P2)-weak is an identity, not a measurement, and this is worth stating at the
definition because it is half the finding.** The carried certificate is
recomputed by the same route that produced it, so an honestly declared carry
verifies *by construction*; the predicate can return false only on a carry the
declarer did not compute, which no adversary would make. It is nonetheless
wired and can fail — a dishonest carry, another boundary's certificate carried
against a path that does not produce it, is rejected (gate `L5-P2-VACUOUS`).
The consequence is that the discriminating weight of (P2) rests entirely on the
strong reading, and Section 4.1 measures that reading collapsing onto rigidity.

### Definition 2.3 — the two variants `[FIN]`, `[AMP]`

**V-CL** is Definition 2.2 with the carried certificates classical, as just
stated — the pin's original form.

**V-AMP** carries, in addition, the amplitude-level description of every step:
each $g_t$ is carried with a declared amplitude operation $U_t$ whose sector
support is $g_t$, and the checkpoint certificate is computed at the amplitude
level. It is the certificate of Definition 2.1 extended by three data: the
moduli profile; **the record the amplitude composite writes**, which may be
strictly finer than the support one; and **the gauge-invariant loop
holonomies** of the carried diagram.

**Why carrying is legal, and what may be carried.** No-cloning constrains

switching on the layered path graph, so a raw per-edge phase is an artifact of
the lift and carrying it would smuggle. Section 3 fixes the invariant content

## 3. The amplitude layer, and where its content is

### Definition 3.1 — the carried diagram and its gauge `[AMP]`

The carried path's **layered diagram** has vertices $(t,j)$ for $t=0,\dots,m$
and one edge per realized leg of each step, carrying that step's amplitude. The
**gauge** is vertex switching: a phase at each vertex, every edge amplitude
multiplied by the phase difference of its endpoints. A quantity is
gauge-invariant iff it is a product of edge amplitudes around a **closed loop**,
conjugated on reversed edges; a cycle basis therefore carries all of its
**phase** content. It does not carry all gauge-invariant content: the moduli are
gauge-invariant too and are not loop products, so they are not trivialized by
switching, and they travel separately in the certificate's moduli profile. Nor
is the *reported* family a complete invariant of the gauge class — it is a
sorted multiset of fundamental-cycle phases, which forgets which basis element
carries which phase, and at the two-step diagram it identifies the
cross-checkpoint invariant $X$ with $X+4$ ($4$ reported families over $8$
values of $X$; §6.3). This is the W7 form transported unchanged, and it is why
the holonomy family — and not the raw phases — is what V-AMP carries.

Two things are checked rather than assumed, because a criterion that is only
stated is a criterion an instrument can quietly fail.

**Closure, leg by leg.** Every fundamental cycle carried is verified to be a
closed walk: each leg's tail is the previous leg's head, and the walk returns
to its start (gate `L5-HOL-CLOSED`). Two reconstructed mis-conventions — one
traversing the spanning-tree return path in the *same* direction as the extra
edge, one reversing the extra edge — fail that check, so it is a measurement
with a negative control and not a formality.

**Gauge covariance, under the gauge's own action.** The carried invariant is
recomputed under an exhaustive sweep of Definition 3.1's own symmetry: all
$512$ assignments of eighth-root phases to the committed one-step diagram's
loop component (one vertex fixed as the global phase), every switched lift
verified to remain unitary and inside the declared family. The invariant moves
under **none** of them. The same sweep at the two-step diagram, $32{,}768$
switchings, likewise fixes both the fundamental-cycle family and the
cross-checkpoint invariant $X$ of §6.3. The negative control is the unclosed
convention, reconstructed inside the test: it moves under $384$ of the $512$
and $29{,}696$ of the $32{,}768$ (gates `L5-HOL-GAUGE`, `L5-HOL-GAUGE-2`).

**What the column reports is a phase, not a product.** The fundamental-cycle
products are not of unit modulus: the single loop of the declared Hadamard step
has loop product exactly $-\tfrac14$, reported as $\zeta_8^4$ with the modulus
travelling in the moduli profile (gate `L5-AMP-MODULUS`).

### Theorem 3.2 — the acyclicity theorem `[FIN]`

Let every step of a carried path be **single-valued**. Then the layered diagram
has cycle rank $0$, so the carried gauge-invariant **loop** content is **empty**
and V-AMP's certificate reduces to V-CL's on the loop content and on the
amplitude record.

**Proof.** Each vertex of a layer below the top has exactly one upward edge, so
$E=mn$ while $V=(m+1)n$. Following upward edges from any vertex reaches the top
layer, so every connected component meets it and $C\leq n$. Hence
$\operatorname{rank}=E-V+C=C-n\leq 0$; ranks are non-negative, so
$\operatorname{rank}=0$ and $C=n$ exactly. A connected acyclic graph admits a
vertex switching sending every edge amplitude to its modulus, so no phase
survives as an invariant. $\square$

**What the theorem does not cover, stated at the theorem.** It empties the loop
content and nothing else. The moduli profile is gauge-invariant and is not a
loop product, and a single-valued *non-injective* step has no unitary lift at
all, so neither (P1) nor (P2) pins its amplitude description. Measured: three
declared lifts of one committed single-valued step give the same rank $0$ and
the same empty holonomy family with **two** distinct moduli profiles (gate
`L5-ACYC-MODULI`). At the committed scope the profile is therefore fixed by a
**declared choice** — the all-ones canonical lift — not by the axiom. The
verdict is unaffected, since a varying profile would be one more free
declaration and Section 5 eats those; but the reduction is identical only on
the subclass the theorem covers.

Measured before it was stated, at a declared sample of $60$ operations per law
at DET, REV, the counter-law and the funnel closure — $2{,}880$ path
evaluations over $1{,}680$ distinct carried paths `[SAMP]` — cycle rank $0$
and empty holonomy family in **every** case, with $E=mn$ measured and $C=n$
**counted** by union-find on the full layered vertex set, isolated upper-layer
vertices included, rather than inferred from Euler (gate `L5-ACYC-M`). And
**every committed law is single-valued** (anchor `A11`, stage-5's own gate). So:

> **At the entire committed scope, V-AMP carries no loop content V-CL does not
> — not because amplitudes were withheld, but because a deterministic step has
> no gauge-invariant phase content at all.**

That is the first half of the delta, and it is a theorem rather than a
measurement of the fixture; the sweep is confirmatory, which is why its sample
scope costs the theorem nothing.

### Theorem 5.4 — the constancy theorem, and the regress at the amplitude scope `[AMP]`, `[LIFT]`

The amendment's whole point is that the amplitude bits might anchor what the

> **Constancy.** Let a declared step have a full-support $2\times2$ block with
> a unitary lift $\left(\begin{smallmatrix}\alpha&\beta\\
> \gamma&\delta\end{smallmatrix}\right)$, all entries non-zero. Then its single
> fundamental cycle has loop product
> $\alpha\bar\beta\,\delta\bar\gamma=-\lvert\beta\rvert^2\lvert\delta\rvert^2$
> — or the conjugate of that, according to the spanning tree — a **negative
> real** either way. Its phase is $\zeta_8^4=-1$, whatever the lift.

**Proof.** Row orthogonality gives $\alpha\bar\gamma+\beta\bar\delta=0$, so
$\alpha\bar\gamma=-\beta\bar\delta$. Multiply both sides by $\bar\beta\delta$:
$\alpha\bar\beta\,\delta\bar\gamma=-\beta\bar\beta\,\delta\bar\delta
=-\lvert\beta\rvert^2\lvert\delta\rvert^2<0$. $\square$

Gated exhaustively over the **complete** family of admitted unitary lifts of one
declared support step — all $512$ of the form
$2^{-1/2}\left(\begin{smallmatrix}\zeta^a&\zeta^b\\ \zeta^c&\zeta^d\end{smallmatrix}\right)$

family: a second declared lift family with **unequal** moduli $(3/5,4/5)$ is
again constant at $\zeta_8^4$ on all $512$ of its members, while dropping
unitarity and keeping the same full-support block shape lets the loop holonomy
sweep **all eight** values (gate `L5-AMP-CONSTANCY-CTRL`). The gate has teeth
in both directions.

**This is the blindness result, and it is what fires REGRESS at the amplitude
