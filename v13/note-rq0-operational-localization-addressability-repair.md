## 1. Question and answer

The unit asked whether localized quantum subinstruments and a nontrivial
overlap nerve can be reconstructed from operational amplitude and composition
data without reading qubit names, circuit wires, a planted tensor
factorization, coordinates, causal arrows, fields or geometry.

At one finite exact scope, the answer is positive.  The estimator receives a
flat typed family of admitted amplitude operations and their complete
composition table.  It first quotients operationally indistinguishable
amplitudes, then searches the whole resulting composition object for faithful
independently addressable direct factors.  Only afterward are the hidden
construction labels opened for scoring.

The returned local objects are operational address objects.  They are not
subsets of a global event set and are not called spatial regions.  Each is
specified by a proper product of recovered address factors, its represented
accessible star-algebra, and the independently W3-certified records that live
in that algebra.

## 6. Composition, gauge and phase controls

The positive and negative addressability worlds have identical individual
matrices, preparations, probes, records and gauge data.  They differ only in
two mixed `Comp_D` rows:

* `D+`: both rows are implemented and the address factorization exists;
* `D-`: both rows are unavailable and addressability is blocked;
* collapse control: both rows collapse to one result and faithfulness fails.

The complete estimator was also run on the fully renamed/generator-changed
dataset and on the full configuration-relabelled, finite-boundary-phase gauge
dataset.  Both return the same structural signature as the main fixture.
Every operational field is checked under gauge conjugacy; mutating an omitted
record field makes that check fail.

An uncompensated complex unitary changes 180 of 216 exact operational
signatures.  Its whole dataset—including all record preparations—is an exact
conjugate of the base dataset.  All three W3 decisions remain positive, so
phase changes operational amplitudes but is not used as a fact-identity
criterion.

## 9. Classification of claims

**Definitions:** operational equivalence, quotient composition object,
addressable direct factor, `RegAddr`, `FactIface`, and contravariant record
pullback.

**Postulates:** finite exact tomography of the declared accessible support;
access to the flat typed composition table; and operational availability of
the declared preparations, controls, probes and candidate readouts.

**Exact finite theorems/verified consequences:** composition congruence;
faithful direct-factor reconstruction; represented-algebra product tests;
W3 record decisions; category/functor closure; gauge/presentation invariance;
and the finite bridge/no-bridge classifications.

**Measurements:** all class, factor, algebra, region, map, overlap, bridge,
candidate and check counts quoted above.

**Conjectures/open questions:** stability under larger carriers and changing
operational access; uniqueness beyond the declared finite gauge; natural
emergence of these access structures; and any relation to spatial or causal
locality.
