## 2. The ε-form

### Definition 2.1 — the per-task concordance defect `[FIN]`

For a declared boundary with atom partition $\pi$, an admitted future $F$ and a
declared state $\rho$,

$$
d(F,\pi,\rho)\ =\ \sum_{r\in\pi}\Bigl[\Pr(r)-\max_s\Pr(r,s)\Bigr],
\qquad \Pr(r,s)=\sum_{j\in r,\ s\in\operatorname{sup}_F(j)}
\frac{\rho_j}{\lvert\operatorname{sup}_F(j)\rvert},
$$

the state mass on which the admitted later readout resolves a distinction
inside a declared atom, for one declared task. This is precisely the summand of
the inherited refinement statistic, whose value is the maximum of $d$ over the
declared family. **$d$ is intrinsic to $(F,\pi,\rho)$**: nothing else in the
declared family and nothing else in the law can change it. That is what makes
Section 6's graded bound run, and it is the same intrinsic-ness the inherited
cost lower bound rests on.

### Definition 2.2 — ε-admissibility `[FIN]`

Fix the declared state $\rho$. A carrier-typed patch
$P=(B,\mathfrak F,X_0,L)$ is **ε-admissible at tolerance
$(\tau_\varepsilon,\tau_\omega)$** iff

- **(a)** $\mathfrak F=\operatorname{Pres}_L(A(B))$ and $\mathfrak F\neq
  \varnothing$ — clause (i-b) and non-emptiness, kept **exact**;
- **(b)** $\varepsilon(P)=\max_{F\in\mathfrak F}d(F,A(B),\rho)\ \leq\
  \tau_\varepsilon$ — the **concordance defect**, which relaxes (i-a) and
  (ii-a) jointly;
- **(c)** $\omega(P)\leq\tau_\omega$ — the **occupancy defect** of
  Definition 9.1, which relaxes the first clause of (ii-b);
- **(d)** no asserted identification is unrealized between reachable
  configurations — the residue of (ii-b), kept **exact**.

$\varepsilon(P)$ *is* the inherited refinement statistic $\delta(A(B),
\mathfrak F,\rho)$, recomputed here by the per-task route and gated equal to it.

Two design decisions are stated rather than buried. First, **clause (a) is not
relativized**: the ε-form reads the whole admitted law, exactly as the terminal
axiom does, because relativizing it is negative control 1 (Section 7.1).
Second, **(i-a) and (ii-a) are relaxed together because they are one
condition.**

### Lemma 2.3 — under (i-b), (i-a) and (ii-a) are the same clause `[FIN]`, `[EXH-5]`, `[CEN]`

Let $\mathfrak F=\operatorname{Pres}_L(\pi)$ be non-empty. Then (i-a) holds iff
(ii-a) holds.

**Proof.** ($\Rightarrow$) is the inherited entailment theorem. ($\Leftarrow$)
Suppose (i-a) fails. By (i-b) every $F\in\mathfrak F$ has
$\operatorname{comp}(F)$ refining $\pi$, and $\ker(\mathfrak F)$ refines
$\operatorname{comp}(F)$ because equal supports collide; so $\ker(\mathfrak F)$
refines $\pi$ always, and (i-a)'s failure means some $F$ separates a pair
inside a block. Then $\operatorname{comp}(F)$ is strictly finer than $\pi$, so
$\operatorname{comp}(F)\neq\pi$ and (ii-a) fails. $\square$

So the pair {(i-a), (ii-a)} is a single condition — "no declared task resolves
inside a declared atom" — and $\varepsilon$ is its state-weighted defect. The
ε-form relaxes one condition, not two.

## 8. The mixed-law arena

### Definition 8.1 — cross-law admissibility comparison `[FIN]`

A **mixed-law arena** is an ordered pair of patches at one committed carrier
whose declared laws differ. Its verdict has three components, each decided on
committed structure alone:

1. per-patch admissibility, each patch at its **own** declared law;
2. **comparability** of the two declared boundaries — what the inherited joint
   unforgeability theorem quantifies over;
3. the **shared refinement** $L^\ast=\langle L_1\cup L_2\rangle$, the least
   composition-closed set containing both declared laws, together with both
   patches re-read at $L^\ast$.

The arena **escapes** when (1) certifies both while the boundaries are
comparable — the configuration a shared law forbids.

### Proposition 8.2 — the anchored escape, reproduced `[ARENA]`, `[FIN]`

Member one at $L_1=\{(0,0,2,3,4)\}$ and member two at DET are both admissible,
and their boundaries are strictly comparable (gate `L3-16`). The escape is real
and is this section's object.

### Theorem 8.3 — route (i), shared refinement, closes it and is empty as a condition `[FIN]`

The least composition-closed law containing both declared laws is **DET
itself**, all $3125$ operations, because the escape generator is a
deterministic map and so already an admitted operation of DET. At that law
member one is inadmissible by rigidity, and the escape closes (gate `L3-17`).

The condition is nevertheless empty. At a finite carrier the closure of the
union of two admitted laws is always composition-closed, so **a shared
refinement always exists**: route (i) is not a hypothesis that can fail, it is
the deletion of the cycle's declared scope — one law family per context.

### 8.1 Route (i)'s price, exactly `[FIN]`

Shared refinement is the exact negation of negative control 1. The sub-law
relativization says *read the patch at the law it runs*; shared refinement says
*read it at the law both patches inhabit*. The price is measured on both sides
(gate `L3-18`).

First, **member one's declared family does not survive the move** — one task at
its own law against $240$ at the shared one — so route (i) does not adjudicate
the declared patch; it adjudicates a different one.

Second, and decisively, **the shared law is nobody's declaration.**
Constructing $L^\ast$ and calling it *admitted* infers admission from algebraic
existence, which the terminal cycle forbids in terms: *admission is measured
per law and never inferred from algebraic existence.* Route (i) closes the
escape under a new hypothesis — call it the amalgamation hypothesis, that the
union-closure of two admitted laws is itself admitted — and that hypothesis is
not committed and is not measured here.

### Theorem 8.4 — the escape delivers the attack `[ARENA]`, `[FIN]`

The terminal cycle stated that whether the predecessor's record-descent attack
still runs at the escape law was a separate question it had **not** measured.
It runs, at every law, and this is proved rather than sampled: **the terminal
descent selector takes no law argument at all.** Certification, realized facts
and collision-free transport are functions of the two contexts and the declared
overlap; no declared law appears in any of them. So the forged context's
certification is invariant under every declaration of law — $14$ of its $15$
records certified, with the forged greatest record descending (gate `L3-19`).

**The adversary who declares a law per context keeps both admissibility
verdicts and the attack.** The escape is not a bookkeeping artefact.

### Theorem 8.5 — route (ii), inter-law descent, closes it and certifies almost nothing `[ARENA]`, `[FIN]`

Because the terminal selector reads no law, route (ii) requires a construction.
Define **law-relative descent** by restricting the partner's realized facts to
those the partner is **admissible to assert** at its own declared law: the
transported fact content — a partition of the carrier, since the declared
overlap *is* the carrier's address algebra — must be an admissible boundary
under that law.

Measured (gate `L3-20`):

| descent | forged context certified | greatest record descends | legitimate eraser certified |
|---|---|---|---|
| law-free (the terminal selector) | **14** of 15 | **yes** | **51** of 52 |
| law-relative, partner law DET | **0** | no | **1** |
| law-relative, partner law $L_1$ | 1 | — | — |

The closure is real: the forged context's descent is killed outright. Its price
is measured with it: **the same restriction cuts the legitimate eraser context
from 51 certified records to 1**, because rigidity leaves the partner exactly
one admissible boundary with which to assert facts. Route (ii) closes the
escape by importing the blockade it was invoked to escape.

### 8.2 The price of pluralism, stated

Both routes close the escape; neither is free.

- **Keep nomological pluralism** — which is the *default*, since the declared
  scope is one law family per context — and the adversary holds two admissible
  patches and, by Theorem 8.4, the attack as well.
- **Take route (i)** and pay the per-context law, plus an uncommitted
  amalgamation hypothesis that contradicts the standing admission doctrine.
- **Take route (ii)** and pay 50 of the legitimate eraser's 51 certified
  records.

That is `RQ0-L3-NOMOLOGICAL-PLURALISM-PRICED`, registered alongside
`RQ0-L3-MIXED-LAW-CLOSED` because at the measured scope the two are not
exclusive: the escape closes, and what closure costs is the finding.

## 9. The occupancy statistic

### Theorem 9.1 — the terminal statistics' blindness is structural `[FIN]`, `[FIX]`

σ and δ are functions of (boundary, declared family, state) alone; the declared
preparation is not an argument of either. The terminal minimal witness W1 and
W3 agree on all three arguments, differ **only** in the declared preparation,
and receive opposite verdicts. Hence no function of those three arguments can
ever see a failure of the occupancy clause (gate `L3-21`). The proof is one
line and the witness is inherited.

### Definition 9.2 — the occupancy defect ω `[FIN]`

Run the boundary's record instrument twice: on the committed state $\rho$,
giving outcome probabilities $p_r$; and on the patch's **own realized state**,
the uniform preparation over $\operatorname{Reach}(P)$, giving $q_r$. Then

$$
\omega(P)\ =\ \sum_{r\in A(B)}p_r\cdot\mathbb 1[q_r=0],
$$

the committed state's probability of a record outcome the patch's own realized
process never produces. It is exact-rational, it is a functional of two
admitted experiments' outcome distributions, and — unlike σ and δ — **it reads
the declared preparation**. With full support, $\omega(P)=0$ exactly when every
declared atom meets the reachable subprocess.

### Theorem 9.3 — the gate the terminal cycle left open is positive `[FIN]`, `[FIX]`

| patch | admissible | σ | δ | **ω** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| W1 (three configurations, preparation the whole carrier) | **yes** | 1 | 0 | **0** |
| W3 (three configurations, preparation $\{0\}$) | no | 1 | 0 | **2/3** |
| the committed carrier under the counter-law, preparation the whole carrier | **yes** | 1 | 0 | **0** |
| the committed carrier under the counter-law, preparation $\{0\}$ | no | 1 | 0 | **15/16** |

ω changes a number exactly where the terminal statistics cannot: at three
configurations, exactly $0$ against exactly $2/3$; at the committed carrier,
exactly $0$ against exactly $15/16$ — in both cases with σ and δ identical
(gate `L3-22`). `RQ0-L3-OCCUPANCY-STATISTIC` is earned.

### 9.1 ω's honest halves

Two, stated with the result rather than after it (gate `L3-23`).

**ω does not read provenance.** It reads the declared preparation, which is
progress; it is still a function of the quadruple, and by Theorem 5.3 nothing

state moves ε.

**ω does not grade the second clause of (ii-b).** Asserted identifications
unrealized between reachable configurations are kept exact in Definition 2.2
and are not graded here.
