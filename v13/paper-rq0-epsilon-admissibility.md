## 2. The ε-form

### Definition 2.1 — the per-task concordance defect `[FIN]`

For a declared boundary with atom partition $\pi$, an admitted future $F$ and a
declared state $\rho$,

$$
d(F,\pi,\rho)\ =\ \sum_{r\in\pi}\Bigl[\Pr(r)-\max_s\Pr(r,s)\Bigr],
\qquad \Pr(r,s)=\sum_{j\in r,\ s\in\operatorname{sup}_F(j)}
\frac{\rho_j}{\lvert\operatorname{sup}_F(j)\rvert},
$$

the state mass on which the admitted later readout resolves a distinction
inside a declared atom, for one declared task. This is precisely the summand of
the inherited refinement statistic, whose value is the maximum of $d$ over the
declared family. **$d$ is intrinsic to $(F,\pi,\rho)$**: nothing else in the
declared family and nothing else in the law can change it. That is what makes
Section 6's graded bound run, and it is the same intrinsic-ness the inherited
cost lower bound rests on.

### Definition 2.2 — ε-admissibility `[FIN]`

Fix the declared state $\rho$. A carrier-typed patch
$P=(B,\mathfrak F,X_0,L)$ is **ε-admissible at tolerance
$(\tau_\varepsilon,\tau_\omega)$** iff

- **(a)** $\mathfrak F=\operatorname{Pres}_L(A(B))$ and $\mathfrak F\neq
  \varnothing$ — clause (i-b) and non-emptiness, kept **exact**;
- **(b)** $\varepsilon(P)=\max_{F\in\mathfrak F}d(F,A(B),\rho)\ \leq\
  \tau_\varepsilon$ — the **concordance defect**, which relaxes (i-a) and
  (ii-a) jointly;
- **(c)** $\omega(P)\leq\tau_\omega$ — the **occupancy defect** of
  Definition 9.1, which relaxes the first clause of (ii-b);
- **(d)** no asserted identification is unrealized between reachable
  configurations — the residue of (ii-b), kept **exact**.

$\varepsilon(P)$ *is* the inherited refinement statistic $\delta(A(B),
\mathfrak F,\rho)$, recomputed here by the per-task route and gated equal to it.

Two design decisions are stated rather than buried. First, **clause (a) is not
relativized**: the ε-form reads the whole admitted law, exactly as the terminal
axiom does, because relativizing it is negative control 1 (Section 7.1).
Second, **at a law of single-valued operations (i-a) and (ii-a) are relaxed
together because they are one condition** — which is Lemma 2.3, and the
hypothesis in it is not decorative.

### Lemma 2.3 — under (i-b), (i-a) and (ii-a) are the same clause `[FIN]`, `[EXH-5]`, `[CEN]`

Let every admitted operation of $L$ be **single-valued** and let
$\mathfrak F=\operatorname{Pres}_L(\pi)$ be non-empty. Then (i-a) holds iff
(ii-a) holds. The forward direction needs neither hypothesis.

**Proof.** ($\Rightarrow$) is the inherited entailment theorem, and holds at
every law. ($\Leftarrow$) Suppose (i-a) fails. By (i-b) every
$F\in\mathfrak F$ has $\operatorname{comp}(F)$ refining $\pi$, and
$\ker(\mathfrak F)$ refines $\operatorname{comp}(F)$ because equal supports
collide; so $\ker(\mathfrak F)$ refines $\pi$ always, and (i-a)'s failure means
some $F$ takes two configurations of one block to *different* values. Because
$F$ is single-valued those values are distinct singletons, which do not
intersect, so $\operatorname{comp}(F)$ is strictly finer than $\pi$ and (ii-a)
fails. $\square$

**Single-valuedness is what carries the last step.** At a support-valued law two
configurations in one atom can have different but *overlapping* supports, and
$\operatorname{comp}(F)$ merges configurations whose supports intersect — so
the record stays equal to $\pi$ while the kernel splits it. Minimal
countermodel, exact: the one-operation law
$\operatorname{sup}(0)=\{0\}$, $\operatorname{sup}(1)=\{0,1\}$ at two
configurations, which is composition-closed, at the one-atom boundary. There
$\operatorname{Pres}_L(\pi)=\{F\}$ is non-empty and
$\operatorname{comp}(F)=\pi$, so (ii-a) holds, while
$\ker(\mathfrak F)=\{0\mid1\}\neq\pi$, so (i-a) fails. Censused over every
composition-closed law generated by at most two left-total relations: **20**
such countermodels at two configurations and **45,810** at three, of which
**zero** at a single-valued law, and **zero** failures of the forward direction
anywhere (gate `L3-26`; the census is R2's, the effectus lens of the frozen
stage-5 panel, re-run here natively).

So at a law of single-valued operations — which every committed law is
(gate `L3-27`) — the pair {(i-a), (ii-a)} is a single condition, "no declared
task resolves inside a declared atom", and $\varepsilon$ is its state-weighted
defect. The ε-form relaxes one condition, not two.

**The non-emptiness hypothesis is weaker than it looks.**
$\operatorname{Pres}_L$ of the one-atom boundary is $L$ itself at every one of
the 687 censused laws, so an empty preservation family already forces
$\pi$ to be a proper boundary, whence $\ker(\varnothing)$ is the one-atom
boundary and (i-a) fails alongside (ii-a): the biconditional holds at all
**974** empty-family census instances. What non-emptiness is load-bearing
against is the **empty law**, which is what §3.1's control declares.

## 3. The reduction

### Theorem 3.1 — ε = 0 is the terminal axiom `[FIN]`, `[EXH-5]`, `[CEN]`

Let $\rho$ have full support and let every admitted operation of $L$ be
**single-valued**. A patch is $(0,0)$-admissible in the sense of
Definition 2.2 if and only if it is admissible under the terminal axiom.

**Proof.** ($\Leftarrow$) If admissible then (i-a) holds, so no declared task
separates a pair inside a block; since the admitted operations are
single-valued, each atom's mass therefore lands entirely on one later
configuration and every $d(F,\pi,\rho)=0$, so $\varepsilon=0$; (ii-b) gives
$\omega=0$ and clause (d); (i-b) and non-emptiness are clause (a).
($\Rightarrow$) If $\varepsilon=0$ then for every declared task and every atom
the whole of that atom's mass lands on one later configuration; full support
makes that "no declared task separates a pair inside a block", so (i-a) holds,
and Lemma 2.3 gives (ii-a). Clause (a) is (i-b) and non-emptiness; $\omega=0$
with full support says every atom meets the reachable subprocess, which with
clause (d) is (ii-b). $\square$

**Single-valuedness is what carries ($\Leftarrow$).** A genuinely multi-valued
admitted operation separates nothing inside an atom — so the terminal axiom
admits — and still leaks the atom's mass across two later configurations.
Minimal countermodel, exact: one operation at two configurations with
$\operatorname{sup}(0)=\operatorname{sup}(1)=\{0,1\}$, composition-closed and
left-total, at the one-atom boundary, with $\rho$ uniform and the declared
preparation the whole carrier. Every clause of the
terminal axiom holds — the patch is **admissible** — while
$\varepsilon=\mathbf{1/2}$ and $\omega=0$, so it is not $(0,0)$-admissible. At
support-valued laws the ε-form at tolerance zero is therefore *strictly
stronger* than the terminal axiom rather than equal to it. Swept: **9**
disagreements over the 38 composition-closed laws generated by at most two
left-total relations at two configurations, and **124** over the 342 closures
of a single left-total relation at three, across every non-empty declared
preparation in both cases (gate `L3-25`; the countermodels are R2's,
re-derived here). The general characterization, which needs no hypothesis on
$L$, is: with full support, $(0,0)$-admissible **iff** terminal-admissible and
every $F\in\operatorname{Pres}_L(A(B))$ has singleton supports.

Measured before it was proved, and in both directions: **368 instances** over
every record at two, three, four and five configurations against every
committed law, and **3435 instances** over the whole 687-law census at three
configurations — **zero disagreements** in either sweep (gate `L3-01`).

Those two sweeps hold the declared preparation at the whole carrier, where
$\operatorname{Reach}(P)$ contains every configuration and $\omega$ vanishes
identically, so clause (c) is never exercised in any of the 3803 instances.
It is exercised separately, and the theorem survives: over the whole census at
every one of the seven non-empty declared preparations, and over every record
at the committed carrier at every one of the thirty-one, **25,657 further
instances with zero disagreements**, of which **9837** carry $\omega\neq0$
(gate `L3-29`; the repair is R2's, adopted natively). Clause (c) is not
decorative either — in **3117** of those instances $\omega>0$ is the *only*
thing standing between the ε-form and a patch the terminal axiom rejects, so
dropping it produces exactly 3117 disagreements, while clause (d) never blocks
alone.

*Scope of the gating.* Every gated instance declares
$\mathfrak F=\operatorname{Pres}_L(A(B))$, which clause (a) forces in any case;
the theorem is stated for arbitrary carrier-typed patches and gated on
closure-family ones.

### Theorem 4.2 — ε is law-blind inside the identity-containing class `[FIN]`, `[EXH-5]`

Let $L$ contain the identity and let every admitted operation of $L$ be
**single-valued**. Then for every carrier-typed boundary,

$$
\varepsilon(\pi)\ =\ \sum_{r\in\pi}\Bigl[\Pr(r)-\max_{j\in r}\rho_j\Bigr],
$$

a quantity of the boundary and the state alone. The law drops out entirely.

**Proof.** The identity's collision partition is discrete, which refines every
boundary, so $\mathrm{id}\in\operatorname{Pres}_L(\pi)$ for every law and every
boundary; and $d(\mathrm{id},\pi,\rho)$ is the stated sum. For any admitted
**single-valued** future, $\max_s\Pr(r,s)\geq\max_{j\in r}\rho_j$ — the mass of
a single configuration is a lower bound on the mass reaching whichever later
configuration it reaches — so $d(F,\pi,\rho)\leq d(\mathrm{id},\pi,\rho)$ and
the identity attains the maximum. $\square$

The inequality fails as soon as an admitted operation has a multi-valued
support, which Definition 2.1's own $\rho_j/\lvert\operatorname{sup}_F(j)\rvert$
construction accommodates and which this corpus's `ALL` family instantiates.
Witness, inside the corpus's own law list: at `ALL(2)` — nine relations,

## 6. Monotonicity, perturbation, and the graded cost tower

### Proposition 6.1 — ε is monotone under refinement `[FIN]`, `[EXH-5]`, `[CEN]`

Refining a boundary never raises its defect, at every law and every state.

**Proof.** Let $\pi\preceq\pi'$. Then
$\operatorname{Pres}_L(\pi)\subseteq\operatorname{Pres}_L(\pi')$, since images
of distinct blocks of $\pi'$ are unions of images of distinct blocks of $\pi$.
And for each $F$ in the smaller set, $d(F,\pi,\rho)\le d(F,\pi',\rho)$, because
each block of $\pi'$ is a union of blocks of $\pi$ and the maximum over a union
is at most the sum of the maxima. Both facts are law-free and state-free, so
the maximum over the family cannot fall. $\square$

Corroborated exhaustively over every strictly comparable pair of records at
three, four and five configurations under every committed law — **358 distinct
comparable pairs** ($7+45+306$), swept against the five committed families at
each arity for **1790 (pair, law) instances, zero violations** (gate `L3-10`).

Inside the identity-containing class this is a *corollary* of Theorem 4.2
rather than an independent measurement — splitting a block cannot decrease
$\sum_r\max_{j\in r}\rho_j$ — and all 1790 of those instances lie there. The
identity-**free** side, where proper charts live and the closed form does not
apply, is swept separately: **4809** comparable pairs over the whole 687-law
census including its 428 identity-free laws, and **306** pairs at the
identity-free escape law $\{(0,0,2,3,4)\}$ at five configurations, **zero
violations** in both (gate `L3-32`). The closed form is measured to *fail* off
the identity class — at 1712 of the 3435 census instances and at 46 of the
escape law's 52 records — which is exactly what makes Theorem 4.2's scope tag
necessary rather than decorative.

This is what makes the form usable as a grading. It is also, restated, exactly
why it cannot discriminate: a grading monotone in coarseness is a measure of
coarseness — and, since the forgeries sit *below* the legitimate chart in the
refinement order, it is why **no refinement-monotone statistic whatever** can
separate them by a threshold (§10).

## 8. The mixed-law arena

### Definition 8.1 — cross-law admissibility comparison `[FIN]`

A **mixed-law arena** is an ordered pair of patches at one committed carrier
whose declared laws differ. Its verdict has three components, each decided on
committed structure alone:

1. per-patch admissibility, each patch at its **own** declared law;
2. **comparability** of the two declared boundaries — what the inherited joint
   unforgeability theorem quantifies over;
3. the **shared refinement** $L^\ast=\langle L_1\cup L_2\rangle$, the least
   composition-closed set containing both declared laws, together with both
   patches re-read at $L^\ast$.

The arena **escapes** when (1) certifies both while the boundaries are
comparable — the configuration a shared law forbids.

### Proposition 8.2 — the anchored escape, reproduced `[ARENA]`, `[FIN]`

Member one at $L_1=\{(0,0,2,3,4)\}$ and member two at DET are both admissible,
and their boundaries are strictly comparable (gate `L3-16`). The escape is real
and is this section's object.

### Theorem 8.3 — route (i), shared refinement, closes it and is empty as a condition `[FIN]`

The least composition-closed law containing both declared laws is **DET
itself**, all $3125$ operations, because the escape generator is a
deterministic map and so already an admitted operation of DET. At that law

The condition is nevertheless empty. At a finite carrier the closure of the

refinement always exists**: route (i) is not a hypothesis that can fail, it is

### 8.1 Route (i)'s price, exactly `[FIN]`

Shared refinement is the exact negation of negative control 1. The sub-law

### 9.1 ω's honest halves

Three, stated with the result rather than after it (gates `L3-23`, `L3-34`).

**ω is a function of the quadruple, and this is proved and measured.** By
Definition 9.2, $\omega(P)=\sum_{r\in A(B)}p_r\mathbb 1[q_r=0]$ where $p_r$
depends on $(A(B),\rho)$ and $q_r$ on $(A(B),\operatorname{Reach}(P))$; and
$\operatorname{Reach}(P)$ is the declared preparation closed under the realized
legs of the declared family, hence a function of $(X_0,\mathfrak F)$, while
$\mathfrak F=\operatorname{Pres}_L(A(B))$ is a function of $(A(B),L)$ by clause
(a). So $\omega=f\bigl(A(B),\operatorname{Pres}_L(A(B)),X_0,\rho\bigr)$.
Measured as a *factoring test* rather than asserted: over the whole census
there are **6118** distinct (boundary, family, preparation) keys and **zero**
of them carries two different $(\varepsilon,\omega)$ values. Varying the
preparation at fixed (boundary, family, law) moves ω at 2316 of the 3435
triples and moves ε at **none** — ω's argument list is strictly larger than
ε's and strictly inside the quadruple. It reads the declared preparation,
which is progress; it does not read provenance, and by Theorem 5.3 nothing on
the quadruple can separate a forged declaration from a legitimate declaration
of the same patch. An adversary who declares the preparation moves ω exactly as
one who declares the state moves ε.

The block is in fact one step earlier than that. Both statistics touch the
admitted law **only** through $\operatorname{Pres}_L(A(B))$: of the **874**
distinct (boundary, family) keys in the census, **175** are reachable from more
than one declared law, and the worst is reachable from **428** — every one of
them indistinguishable to ε and to ω at that boundary. So the instruments are
blind not only to how a declaration came to be made but to *which law was
declared*, wherever two laws share a preservation family. This forecloses the
repair a reader reaches for next, and it also means the declared data is not
saturated by $(\varepsilon,\omega)$: a statistic reading $L$ directly would see
strictly more — and by Theorem 5.3's witness still not see provenance.

**ω does not separate the comparison ε failed.** Run on it: at the committed
state, the committed law and the committed declared preparation the two forged
coarse patches and the legitimate one carry $\omega=0$, $0$ and $0$. The zero
is *structural*, not a coincidence of the fixture — ω sums the state over atoms
disjoint from $\operatorname{Reach}(P)$, and $\operatorname{Reach}(P)$ contains
the declared preparation by construction, so at the whole carrier no atom of
any boundary is disjoint from it: **260 of 260** instances over every committed
law and every record. Under DET the forged $2{+}1{+}1$ attains $\omega=0$ at
**all 31** non-empty declared preparations; the forger does not even need to
choose well.

One honest consolation, and it is a genuine structural difference from ε: **ω
is anti-monotone under refinement** — coarsening never raises it — with zero
violations over 4590 comparable-pair instances. So wherever ω is non-zero it
ranks the legitimate, coarsest patch as the *most* admissible, the exact
reverse of ε's ordering. That grading exists at 15 of the 155 (law,
preparation) rows — 12 at FUNNEL, 3 at the counter-law, and none at all under
DET, REV or the funnel closure — and admits the legitimate patch at tolerance
zero at 6 of them — all six at FUNNEL, which this corpus carries as a declared task family
and not as a law, so clause (a) of Definition 2.2 is not well posed there. At
the only *genuine law* whose rows order the legitimate patch best, the
counter-law, not one of them admits it at tolerance zero: admitting it there
takes a positive $\tau_\omega$, which is the amnesty structure of Theorem 5.2
transplanted onto ω. And by Theorem 5.3 the ordering is not a provenance
separation in any case: a forgery of the same shape would win identically. The
grading exists; it is a grading of the same wrong thing, with the sign flipped.
(The whole measurement is R3's, adopted natively.)

**ω does not grade the second clause of (ii-b).** Asserted identifications

  separate two declarations of the *same* patch (§9.1).

  the forger attains ω = 0 at every declared preparation under DET (§9.1).
