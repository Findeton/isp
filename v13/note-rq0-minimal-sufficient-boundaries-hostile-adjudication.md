## 3. The future experiment rung

### 3.1 What is constructed

For a fixed typed cut and a fixed admitted future-task class, each complete
past/source instrument branch \(\theta\) induces a conditional future comb

\[
F_\theta^{(c)}.
\]

The parameterized family

\[
\mathsf E_c=\{F_\theta^{(c)}:\theta\in\Theta_c\}
\]

is a quantum statistical experiment in the admitted process category. Its
members retain branch weights, output systems, disturbance, flags and all
tester-visible continuation data. The parameter \(\theta\) is not supplied
to the simulator as a classical side channel.

Exact comparison uses one parameter-independent admitted deterministic
superchannel:

\[
\mathsf E\succeq\mathsf F
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\exists\,\Gamma\ \text{admitted such that}\
\Gamma(E_\theta)=F_\theta\quad\forall\theta.
\]

Operational equivalence is mutual exact simulation. Approximate comparison
is measured only by complete admitted decision tests on one common process
type.

### 3.2 Binding typing qualification

The rung is earned only when the family has a predeclared common
flag/output profile, or when a genuinely neutral, \(\theta\)-independent
padding is physically admitted. A direct sum whose tag reveals \(\theta\)
is forbidden parameter leakage.

For subnormalized deterministic branches with scalar success probability,
the common success/sink construction is valid. A general probabilistic comb
whose success probability depends on an input must be completed by its
actual complementary CP branch; it cannot be completed by a synthetic
scalar sink.

Similarly, several future tasks can be bundled only when a
parameter-independent controlled selector or normalized tagged
randomization is an admitted process. An unweighted formal direct sum is not
automatically deterministic.

These restrictions narrow the general notation but do not invalidate the

## 4. The minimal-sufficiency theorem

### 4.1 Deficiency and exact simulation

At finite \(\Theta\), the experiment error lives in a finite
\(\ell^\infty\)-sum of tester-quotiented process spaces. Its norm has the
dual form

\[
\max_{\theta}\|z_\theta\|
=
\max_{\sum_\theta\|g_\theta\|_*\leq1}
\sum_\theta g_\theta(z_\theta).
\]

The admitted superchannel hom-set and the dual ball are compact convex
sets, and the pairing is continuous affine in each argument. Finite
minimax therefore yields the paper's exact deficiency formula. Compactness
also ensures that zero deficiency is attained by one exact simulator.

The dual witness is a signed operational decision functional. It is a
literal laboratory decision test only when the declared tester family
generates the corresponding dual ball.

The direction of W3 recovery deficiency is fixed here. If a proposed center
is to simulate the complete future experiment, the relevant quantity is

\[

not its reverse.

### 4.2 Minimum-rank stabilizer construction

Let \(\operatorname{Stab}(\mathsf E)\) be the admitted deterministic
endomorphisms that fix every \(E_\theta\). The identity supplies a
stabilizing idempotent. Choose a stabilizing idempotent \(e\) of minimum
linear rank.

For any endomorphism \(f=efe\) of the Karoubi object \((X,e)\) fixing the
experiment, compactness makes the powers \(f^n\) bounded. Their Cesàro
averages converge in finite dimension to the projection \(p\) onto
\(\operatorname{Fix}(f)\). Closedness and convexity keep \(p\) admitted,
and \(p=epe\) still fixes every experiment point.

If \(f\neq e\), then \(f\) is not the identity on \(\operatorname{ran}e\),
so its fixed space is proper and

\[
\operatorname{rank}p<\operatorname{rank}e,
\]

contradicting minimum rank. Hence the minimum is rigid.

If \(\Gamma\) is any sufficient coarse-graining with recovery \(R\), then
with

\[
a=\Gamma e,
\qquad
b=eR,
\]

the composite \(ba\) fixes the minimal experiment. Rigidity gives

\[

\]

Thus \((X,e)\) retracts from every sufficient representative. Applying the
same argument in both directions makes any two rigid minima isomorphic.
Reversible presentation maps conjugate the stabilizer semigroup and preserve
rank, so the minimal isomorphism class is presentation covariant.

## 5. Why the classical center does not follow

- Archimedean matrix order;

Therefore the expression

\[

The Choi--Effros product also cannot be invoked automatically. It requires a

### 5.2 Exact operator-system obstruction

The paper's valid counterexample is

\[

\{(I,I),(Z,Z),(X,2X+Z)\}
\subset M_2\oplus M_2.
\]

Writing the generators as \(s_0,s_1,s_2\), one has

\[
s_2^2=(I,5I),
\]

so the generated algebra contains

\[
z_1=\frac{5s_0-s_2^2}{4}=(I,0),
\qquad
z_2=\frac{s_2^2-s_0}{4}=(0,I).
\]

The generated and enveloping algebra is \(M_2\oplus M_2\), but neither
central projector belongs to \(S\).

More strongly, the states

\[
\varphi_\lambda(a,b)
=
\lambda\operatorname{tr}_2(a)
+(1-\lambda)\operatorname{tr}_2(b)
\]

all restrict identically to \(S\), while

\[
\varphi_\lambda(z_1)=\lambda.
\]

Thus the operational states on \(S\) do not determine the central block
weight. The algebraic envelope center is canonical abstractly, but it is not
an effect or instrument available in the original experiment.
