## Abstract

The transport geometry measured so far is **two-chart**: every loop runs
between a pair of frames. Real atlases have many charts glued pairwise, and the
next structural floor is **triple** consistency. For charts $A$, $B$, $C$ with
admitted pairwise identifications the composite

$$\delta \;=\; g_{CA}\circ g_{BC}\circ g_{AB}$$

is the **cocycle defect**. The question with content beyond the two-chart
measurements is whether every triangle defect is generated by the bigon
holonomies — the connection is the whole story — or whether some triangle
carries structure **outside** the bigon-generated group.

Base G carries two frames per setting, so a third chart had to be built from
the base's own admitted material. The declared route is a **relabelled frame
via a declared admitted isomorphism**, taken in its principled form: the chart
family is the **orbit of the two committed frames under the declared admitted
group**, four members, measured pairwise distinct at every setting. The
relabelled first frame is measured **different** from the second committed
frame, and the mechanism is measured: the wing exchange does not intertwine the
preparation leg, so **the third chart exists exactly because the completion is
non-equivariant**. At the exchange-equivariant completion the atlas collapses
to two charts at precisely the two settings that bear the geometry and no
triangle survives there.

The census is exhaustive: **1,824** admissible triangles over **20** of the 24
declared coordinate cells, the size computed twice by independent routes. The
**defect set has two members** — the identity (1,248 triangles) and the wing
exchange (576) — and the non-identity defect occurs only at the two symmetric
settings, at exactly half the triangles of each cell that carries an
ordered-pair multiplicity.

**Every defect lies in the bigon-generated holonomy group at its own setting:
0 outside, of 1,824.** The reading is stronger than membership and is gated as
an if-and-only-if at the level of coordinates: every non-identity defect is
measured **equal** to the multiplicity bigon's holonomy at its own coordinate,
and every coordinate carrying no such bigon carries only identity defects. The
defect set generates a group of order **2**, a **proper** subgroup of the
order-4 group at the symmetric settings — the completion's own defect is
measured never to be a triangle defect.

What is forced and what is free is separated and disclosed. Every admissible
identification is an element of the declared admitted set, which is measured
closed under composition, so the *confinement* of the defect to that set is
analytically forced and is reported as a disclosure. Membership is forced only
where that set is contained in the holonomy group — the two symmetric settings.
At the four settings whose connection is measured **flat** the admitted set is
measured **not** contained in the trivial group, so each of the **576**
triangles there could have carried a defect that no bigon generates — a pure
level-2 obstruction at a flat setting — and the gate measures that none does.

**Unit verdict:** `COC-COCYCLE-CLOSES-AT-THE-DECLARED-TRIPLE-STRUCTURE`, at the
committed finite scope, at the declared admission scope, at the declared triple
structure, at the pinned completion, per coordinate.

## 1. The question, and what it is asked of

Two chart-level facts are inherited. Lawful transport between declared contexts
is path-dependent; and the group the loops generate at a symmetric setting is
the Klein four-group, with one generator the base's own wing exchange and the
other manufactured by the declared completion.

Both are readings on an atlas with **two** charts. In a two-chart atlas every
loop is a bigon, and the cocycle condition — that the identifications of three
charts compose to the identity on their common overlap — cannot even be stated.
It is the first condition that is not a restatement of the connection: a
triangle defect that no bigon holonomy generates would be structure at a level
the connection does not reach, which is what the pin calls the higher
obstruction.

This unit states it on base G and measures it. Three things had to be
constructed before any defect could be computed, and all three are declarations
recorded in §2 before any fixture value appears: a third chart, an admission
rule for each pair, and a coordinate at which the three identifications are all
read. The instrument's own gate order is the proof of that ordering, and it is
gated: every declaration gate is measured to sit strictly before the first
fixture-truth gate, with the defect counter measured at zero when the freeze is
recorded.

## 7. Controls

### 7.1 The positive control: the degenerate triangle

The pin's third candidate route — the two gluing rules as parallel atlases,
(frame1-FULL, frame2, frame1-REAL) — *is* a triangle whose third chart is the
first one again, so its third identification is that chart's own self-map. Two
clauses, both measured over **152** degenerate triangles:

- the self-map is measured to be **uniquely the identity** at every chart,
  every coordinate and both rules — without which the degenerate triangle would
  not be degenerate;
- every degenerate triangle's defect is measured **equal** to the holonomy of
  the corresponding two-chart loop, computed independently from the committed
  path graph as a product of exact matrices.

This is the control that says the cocycle machinery reduces to the level-1
object it must reduce to.

### 7.2 The negative controls, with teeth

Two triangles are deliberately mis-glued at a coordinate where the base itself
draws an admitted map:

| injected map | fixed configurations | in the admitted set | defect in the defect set | defect in the bigon group |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| the completion's defect $D$ | 45 | no | **no** | **yes** |
| a pointer transposition of the declared extension | 27 | no | **no** | **no** |

The first shows the census's defect set is a **proper** part of what the group
contains, so a defect-set reading could have failed. The second shows the
**membership predicate can fire**: the zero it reports on the census is not the
only answer it can give. Had this gate failed, the membership gate would be
dead, and the run would say so.

### 7.3 The pin's wrong-order control is measured inert, and is reported as such

Every triangle of the census was recomposed in the reversed order. The number
of composites that **move** is **0 of 1,824**. The reason is measured beside
it: the drawn maps generate an **abelian** group in which every element is an
**involution**, so no reordering and no inversion of the three factors can
change their product for any input. A mis-composed triangle therefore *cannot*
land outside the expected set here. This control has **no teeth on this base**
and is entered as a disclosure rather than as a must-pass gate.

The teeth are put where they can bite. The composition and inversion routines
are gated on a **declared non-abelian witness** drawn from base G's own
relabelling scope — a cyclic system relabelling, the wing exchange, a cyclic
pointer relabelling — against a comparator built by **direct index
expression**: the composition is measured to return what the index expression
returns, the reversed order is measured to return something **different**, the
witness is measured to have order greater than two so inversion is observable,
and a link variable's reverse traversal is measured to be the inverse matrix
and not the matrix.

## 10. The verdict

The decision rule is pre-registered and the verdict is derived from the gate
results and from nothing else: `COC-TRIANGLES-ABSENT` if the census is empty;
`COC-HIGHER-OBSTRUCTION` if any admissible triangle's defect lies outside the
bigon-generated holonomy group at its own setting; `COC-COCYCLE-CLOSES`
otherwise; `COC-BLOCKED-AT-⟨object⟩` where a census cannot be posed. The
verdict is re-derived inside its own gate from the recorded results and
measured to be the string the rule selects.

> **`COC-COCYCLE-CLOSES-AT-THE-DECLARED-TRIPLE-STRUCTURE`**, at the committed
> finite scope, at the declared admission scope, at the declared triple
> structure, at the pinned completion, per coordinate.

**What that means, at the level the evidence carries.** On base G, at the
declared triple structure, the connection is the whole story at level two.
There is no gerbe-like residue: not only does every triangle defect lie in the
bigon-generated group, every one of them **is** a bigon holonomy at its own
coordinate, and the defects generate a proper subgroup of that group. The
completion-manufactured elements $D$ and $W\!D$ — the part of the geometry that
escapes every declared relabelling scope — are measured to be invisible to the
triple structure.

**And what it does not mean.** The closure has a measured mechanism, and the
mechanism is narrow. Every identification this atlas admits is drawn from a
scope that is measured to be a group of order two, so the defect is confined
before any geometry is consulted. The question that survives is therefore not
"does base G's atlas close" but "what would an atlas have to look like for it
not to" — and this unit measures one necessary ingredient: an admitted set
whose composites leave the holonomy group, at a coordinate where an admissible
triangle can realise them. The declared extension supplies the first half and
is measured not to supply the second.

## 11. Scope and non-claims

1. **No claim about nature.** Every result is a statement about declared finite
   models, a declared gauge and declared finite search scopes.
2. **The triple structure is a declaration.** The chart family is the orbit of
   the committed frames under the admitted set; another declared third chart
   could give another census. What the unit measures is that this family is
   well-posed, that its members are distinct, that its gluing rule is the
   base's own, and what its triangles do.
3. **The third chart is not independent of the first two.** It is a
   relabelling of a committed frame by a map the base admits, so the atlas is
   generated by the two-chart atlas together with an admitted isomorphism.
   That is exactly why §6's if-and-only-if is available, and it bounds the
   result: a base with genuinely independent charts is not measured here. The
   one candidate for such a chart the pin names, the sub-leg split, is measured

## Appendix: deviations

**D1 — the pin's phrase "three pairwise-identifiable charts" is delivered as a
four-chart family, and the census runs over its ordered triples.** The declared
route generates the orbit of **both** committed frames under the admitted set,
which has four members rather than three. Taking the orbit rather than
hand-picking three charts is what makes the family a declaration with a
computed size instead of a choice, and every ordered triple of distinct members
is censused, so the pin's triple is contained in the delivery rather than
replaced by it.

**D2 — the pin's wrong-order negative control is measured to have no teeth on
this base, and a second negative control with teeth is added.** The drawn maps
are measured to generate an abelian group of exponent two, so a reordering
cannot move any defect: 0 of 1,824. The pin's own instruction for that case is
followed — it is reported plainly rather than presented as a passing control —
and the membership gate's teeth are supplied instead by two **injected**
triangles, one landing inside the group but outside the defect set and one
landing outside the group. The order and inversion conventions are gated
separately on a declared non-abelian witness.

**D3 — the membership comparator is a per-setting object, and the
per-checkpoint match is a separate gate.** The bigon-generated holonomy group
is a group of loops that traverse all checkpoints, so the finest coordinate at
which it exists is the setting. The per-checkpoint comparison the
matched-coordinate discipline asks for is made in §6 against the multiplicity
bigon **at the triangle's own coordinate**, and that gate is an
if-and-only-if in both directions.

**D4 — the confinement of the defect is analytically forced, and it is the
unit's central disclosure rather than a hidden assumption.** Because every
admissible identification is drawn from the declared admitted set and that set
is measured closed, no triangle defect can leave it whatever the geometry does.
The unit does not present its membership zero as if that were not so: the
forced settings and the free settings are separated, counted and named, and the
free half is what the verdict rests on.

**D5 — the switching sweep covers this unit's own loops, not the inherited
base's.** The declared swept set is every triangle loop of the census and every
declared two-chart loop of this unit (the multiplicity bigons and the defect
loop). The inherited canonical loop's own switching sweep belongs to the
inherited unit and is not repeated here.

**D6 — the sub-leg split is measured at one declared setting.** Route R1's
absence is measured at GP-E, over all four charts, both rules, both matchable

**D7 — the completion measurement is about existence only.** The unit rebuilds

elsewhere.
