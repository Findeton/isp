## Abstract

The O4 terminal unit bequeathed a question: transportability on this base is
decided by the two frames' declared **leg prefix**, not by the divisibility
residual. This unit asks the next question. Not whether *facts* descend — whether
the **law's own local data** can be carried between declared contexts along the
base's own structure, and whether the carrying is **path-dependent**. Path-dependence
of lawful transport is holonomy, and holonomy is the first geometric structure this
programme could earn rather than assume.

The pin's first clause is discharged first: **PREFIX-DECIDES is re-derived here
by an independent route** — by multiset matching on canonical leg keys rather than
the O4 script's permutation search, by this unit's own four-clause admissibility
predicate, and by its own defect matrix — and reproduces the O4 terminal profile
exactly: the leg-prefix profile agrees with the transport profile at **18 of 18**
cells, the residual profile at **12 of 18**, and all six equal-residual /
opposite-transport witnesses reappear. Seven anchors pin it cell by cell against
the committed O4 receipt.

The path space is then built and enumerated: nodes are (frame, checkpoint)
coordinates with the read time a declared coordinate of every node; moves are leg
applications in both directions and the co-reference identifications the
O4-terminal instrument admits uniquely, at the declared **narrow** admission
scope. **34,024** reduced paths and **4,972,096** path pairs sharing both
endpoints are enumerated, none typed.

**The pin's canonical loop is exactly flat.** Frame 1's leg order forward against
frame 2's backward, closed by the identifications at the two declared division

## 7. The gauge layer, separated from its orbit (§14)

The L5 disease is exhibiting a gauge orbit as physics. The pin makes the sweep
mandatory. What the sweep can and cannot establish is stated here before its
numbers, because one of its clauses is forced by algebra and is reported as a
disclosure rather than as a measurement.

**The declared switching group** assigns one sign to each link of a setting's own
graph. Its order is computed, never typed: $2^{9} = 512$ at SP-A through SP-D
(six leg links and three identifications) and $2^{13} = 8192$ at SP-E and SP-F
(the four extra realized-rule identifications). **The sweep is complete at every
setting**, over every declared loop that carries an edge list — the canonical loop
everywhere, and at SP-E and SP-F the three aligned-prefix bigons and the
prefix-crossing loop as well. **The checkpoint subgroup** — the switchings induced
by a sign at each *node*, which is the base's own checkpoint-phase redundancy —
has order $2^{8-1} = \mathbf{128}$ at every setting, the graph being connected,
and is swept complete as well.

**What is measured.** With every holonomy in the sweep **rebuilt from the link
variables**, caches bypassed:

- **The switching acts by a global scalar.** Each switched holonomy is compared,
  exactly, against $\bigl(\prod_{\text{traversals}}\varepsilon\bigr)\cdot H_0$,
  where $H_0$ is the unswitched holonomy built by the base's own product routine.
  Deviations: **0 of 85,760** exact matrix comparisons, of which **81,920** are the
  complete 8,192-element sweeps of all five declared loops at SP-E and SP-F.
- **Every swept holonomy is a signed permutation at all** — which for the
  eight-link canonical loop, a product of matrices that are not permutations, is a
  real fact and not a bookkeeping one.
- **The loop's sign is fixed under the whole 128-element checkpoint subgroup.**
  That is a telescoping property — a closed walk visits each node an even number of
  times — and a wrong sign convention breaks it. The `gauge-sign` mutant, which
  drops the switching on a reversed traversal, dies here and at the scalar-action
  clause.
- **The mis-conventioned control moves.** A quantity reading the raw sign moves
  under the full link-sign group, which is what makes it an orbit datum and not a
  result; the `gauge-subsample` mutant, which collapses the sweep, dies at the
  group-order clause.

**What is forced, and is therefore disclosed and not claimed.** Because the
switching acts by a global $\pm 1$, and a signed permutation matrix and its
negative have the *same* permutation part, **the invariance of the permutation
part is an algebraic identity**: no switching and no mutant could make that clause
fail, whatever else were wrong with this instrument. The sweep confirms it —
exactly one permutation part at every declared loop, at every setting, over the
complete group — and that confirmation is reported as a disclosure. It is not
evidence that the permutation part is the *right* content to report.

**The sign content that is invariant is the relative one.** Under $s \mapsto
\varepsilon s$ the raw set of signs is not invariant (a uniform-sign loop flips
between $[+1]$ and $[-1]$), but the relative class $s_j s_0$ is. That class is
what this paper reports as sign content, and two of the four holonomy-group
elements of §8.2 are measured to carry **mixed** relative signs — gauge-invariant
content that a report of the raw sign set discards.

**The self-test evaluates fresh (§14 addendum).** Its cache-hit count is gated at
**zero** and its miss count gated positive, so a self-test reading the instrument's
cache would be testing the cache and not the quantity; the `memo-lax` mutant lets
it read the cache and dies.

## Appendix: deviations

**D1 — the canonical loop does not return T1, and that is the finding, not a
control failure.** As first written, the positive control required the canonical
loop to return *both* the amplitude-layer holonomy (identity) and T1's own value.
It fails the second clause at every setting, because the declared Born-level
backward action — the transposed one-step transition — is measured not to invert
the forward one. The control is therefore scoped to the amplitude layer, where the
"must agree" claim is the one the commuting-legs anchor licenses, and T1's
behaviour is reported as the measurement it is (§8.1). The two are stated as a
layer contrast, not reconciled away.

**D2 — the switching group is 512 at four settings and 8192 at two, and it is
swept complete at both.** The pin names "the 512 checkpoint-phase switchings".
Measured on this unit's arena, one sign per link of a setting's own graph gives
$2^9 = 512$ where only the full-leg rule supplies identifications and $2^{13} =
8192$ where the realized rule supplies four more. Every element of both groups is
swept, at every declared loop that carries an edge list, and the checkpoint
subgroup (128) is swept complete as well. No sampling is used anywhere in this
unit, and there is no `[SAMP]` disclosure to make.

**D3 — the admission criterion is FORCED, not CERT, and FORCED is
scope-dependent.** The pin says "identifications where the O4-terminal instrument
certifies them, i.e. at aligned-prefix/division coordinates". The instrument has
two readings of "certifies": uniqueness of the admitted transport (the
discriminator's FORCED) and the ROUTE-EXT certificate. Only the first agrees with
the pin's own gloss — the O4 certificate is degenerate at $t=1$, refuses the pair
at $t=2$, and is **VACUOUS at SP-E at $t=3$**, so the second would admit links at
the final division event alone and, at SP-E, not even there: that setting's loop
space would be empty outright. The first is declared, the second disclosed with its
measured consequence (§8.5), and every verdict is licensed at the declared
criterion. Uniqueness is also relative to the scope it is taken over: at the
declared 96-element extension four of the drawn links are no longer unique and are
refused (§8.6).

**D4 — T2's leg action carries the matrix unchanged.** The defect is a datum of the
cut; the declared leg action therefore measures the defect's stability under moving
the cut rather than implementing paper 1's affine chain law between consecutive
cuts. That law is not used and nothing is claimed about it. Because no leg move
transports T2, the last column of §8.1's table cannot be read as a transport
statement for it: SP-C and SP-D enter that column for the **base-point** reason —
closed paths based at different nodes carry different cuts — and at those two
settings no pair of same-endpoint paths disagrees about T2 at all.

**D5 — the holonomy value set is counted as permutations, and it is measured
already closed.** The value set is enumerated over the closed paths of the
committed path space, which is bounded at $L_{\max}=8$, so it *need* not be closed
under composition; here it is measured to be, and the group computed separately by
closure is the same four-element group. As first written, this instrument counted
the value set as a set of **name strings** drawn from a table that knew two
permutations, so every unnamed permutation collapsed onto one label and the count
came out **3** where the number of distinct permutations is **4** — a count of the
wrong object, and one the receipt contradicted on its own adjacent line, where the
group's four elements were printed with two identical names. Worse, the earlier
text explained the resulting 3-versus-4 gap by the *possibility* that a value set
need not be closed at a length bound: a real mathematical caution, narrating a
labelling bug as though it were structure. The count is now of permutation tuples,
the closure is measured rather than hypothesised, and the `label-collapse` mutant
restores the two-name table and dies at the corrected gate. Both numbers are
carried in the receipt, and the paper never calls the value-set size a group order.

**D6 — the fixtures are held, and the field's own arithmetic is memoised.**
Rebuilding the committed model's operators inside the §14 sweep dominated the run
time. The legs, the propagators built from them, the node laws and the defect
matrices are **fixtures**, not transported values: each is a function of (setting,
frame, checkpoint) and of nothing else, and each is built once from the committed
model and held, exactly as the base itself holds its legs. Separately, the sweep's
matrix product memoises the *field's* own products and sums — a few hundred
distinct exact values for the whole run, in $\mathbb{Q}(\cos\pi/8)$ where tuple
equality is field equality — so that the sweep spends its time on structure rather
than on recomputing identical exact products. Nothing is approximated and nothing
is cached at the level of a transported value. The two product routines are
measured against each other on **every** swept instance: the §7 gate compares each
switched holonomy, built by the memoising routine, against the unswitched holonomy
built by the base's own product, so an all-positive switching is an exact equality
test between them. Every quantity the self-test measures — the loop holonomies —
is rebuilt from the fixtures on every switching, with the transported-value cache
bypassed, and the fresh-eval gate is stated over those quantities.

**D7 — the path length bound is a declaration.** $L_{\max} = 2\cdot\text{NLEGS}+2$
is computed from the model as the canonical loop's own length, so the pin's loop is
inside the enumerated space; every count in §3.2 is a count at that bound and no
claim is entered about longer paths.

**D8 — a waiver mutant must exercise its predicate in the failing direction.**
As first written, the three waiver mutants set their gate's computed predicate to
`True`. That cannot fail a gate that already passes, and all three survived. The
defect is the waiver *convention*, not the gates: a waiver's purpose is to measure
that the predicate carries the exit code, which is shown by overwriting it to
`False`. All three are rewritten that way and all three now die. The episode is
recorded because it is exactly the failure the falsification census exists to
catch, and the census caught it before delivery.

**D9 — no Lean.** As the pin states.

**D10 — the checkpoint set extends O4's read times by one.** O4's instrument
evaluates $t \in \{1,2,3\}$; this unit adds $t=0$, because the pin's canonical loop
closes there and the graph needs the node. So §3.1's table is an O4 re-derivation at
three of its four checkpoints and this unit's own construction at the fourth, and
$t=0$ is a coordinate at which the two "contexts" are one context (§3.1). Two of
the six twisted-corridor witnesses live there. The verdict's gate measures that the
refutation survives on the witnesses at O4's own read times alone, so nothing rests
on the added coordinate.

**D11 — one must-pass gate is falsified by a waiver alone.** `NT-VOCABULARY` has
no computational falsifier in this suite: every branch that emits a verdict builds
it from a pre-registered template, so the only declared mutant that breaks it is
the waiver that overwrites the emitted string. The census therefore prints two
denominators — the must-pass gates falsified by *some* mutant, and the smaller
count falsified by one that perturbs a *computation* — and names the gate that sits
between them (§10). Six further gates are declared **disclosures** rather than
must-pass, either because their content is forced by algebra (§7) or because no
mutation of this instrument could perturb them without becoming a different model
(T1's Born-level cause); none of them carries a verdict.
