## 1. Executive disposition

The paper successfully repairs the finite-arrow inconsistency and gives a
correct exact branch-memory W3 representation. Its Heisenberg observability
algebra, product law, seed and mixed-control calculations, UCP group-average
expectation, and abstract all-subspace quantaloid theorem are mathematically
sound at their explicit finite-dimensional scopes.

The advertised highest rung nevertheless fails for three independent reasons.

First, Definitions 9.1–9.2 treat UCP Heisenberg images of record projectors as
projectors. They are generally only positive effects. Pairwise commutativity
does not restore idempotence. Moreover, a projector/effect belonging to one
algebra in the family defining a concept need not belong to their intersection
`A_c`. Consequently the displayed inclusion

`Bool<P_c> -> Proj(A_c)`

is not defined for the chart class declared by the paper. This invalidates the
partial `Rec` component of the claimed finite Weld datum and the stated
chart-level scope.

Second, the addressability split is not typed in the one physical channel
category that the paper actually declares. The declared channel envelope has
full boundary algebras as its objects; the proper algebra `A` is not such an
object. The endomorphism `e=iE` can be admitted and its operator-algebraic
properties are correct, but the claimed admitted channel `E:B_y -> A` and
split pair do not live in the specified channel envelope.

Third, the operator-support quantaloid is a valid involutive quantaloid, but it
is not the least support quantaloid generated by admitted supports. After
forming a least linear dagger category, the paper takes *every* subspace of
each hom-space. This introduces arbitrary support lines that need not be the
support of an admitted amplitude, effect, channel, or any closure production
declared by the experimental grammar. The dormant-exclusion result only
excludes matrices outside the linear category and does not establish the
stronger least-admitted-support claim required by the pin.

These are not spatial or continuum objections. They occur wholly inside the
paper's finite operator/process layer and defeat its pre-registered headline.

## 3. Ranked findings

### FATAL O1 — the chart record interface is not defined for general UCP transports

Definitions 5.1 and 9.1 allow general admitted UCP Heisenberg transports.
Definition 9.2 requires only that the transported members of `P_c` commute and
then places the Boolean algebra they generate inside `Proj(A_c)`. Both steps
can fail.

Take `B_y=M_2(C)`, `P_0=|0><0|`, `P_1=|1><1|`, and the random-unitary
Heisenberg channel

`Phi*(X) = (3/4) X + (1/4) X_flip X X_flip`.

Then

`Phi*(P_0)=diag(3/4,1/4)` and
`Phi*(P_1)=diag(1/4,3/4)`.

These effects commute, are unital as a resolution, and generate the proper
diagonal algebra, onto which an admitted dephasing expectation may split.
Neither effect is idempotent, so neither lies in `Proj(A_c)`. This object passes
the paper's stated commutativity test while the displayed Boolean inclusion is
undefined. Commutativity of effects yields a commutative effect algebra, not a
Boolean algebra of projections.

There is also an independent intersection failure. For a principal concept
anchored at a coarse diagonal algebra `A_2`, Proposition 8.5 makes every W3
algebra containing `A_2` relevant. A relevant finer diagonal algebra `A_1`
may have fine record projections not contained in `A_2`. All these projections
commute, while

`A_c = intersection A_W = A_2`.

Thus membership of each transported projector in its own `A_W` does not imply
membership in `A_c`. Definition 9.2 proves neither sharpness nor intersection
membership. The claimed functor `Rec`, the third component of `X_D`, and the
claim-register entry for the partial record interface therefore fail at the
declared chart scope. This finding is fatal to the advertised highest rung but
does not erase the earlier W3 and observability theorems.

## 4. Required independent audit

### 4.1 Finite-arrow scalar and spectral obstruction

The obstruction is correct. In a finite category, the powers of every
endomorphism repeat: `c^m=c^n` for some `0<=m<n`. For a closed scalar with
represented value `lambda`, this gives

`lambda^m(lambda^(n-m)-1)=0`.

Thus `lambda=0` or it is a root of unity. A nonzero scalar of modulus strictly
between zero and one is impossible.

For a represented endomorphism `T`, its minimal polynomial divides

`t^m(t^(n-m)-1)`.

Over characteristic zero, `t^(n-m)-1` has simple roots and is coprime to `t`.
Every nonzero eigenvalue is therefore a root of unity and every nonzero
generalized eigenspace is semisimple. At eigenvalue zero a nilpotent block may
remain, with block size at most `m`; if `m=0`, zero is absent. The paper states
this scope correctly: the contradiction concerns a finite arrow set closed
under unrestricted composition, not finite-dimensional Hilbert spaces,
finite presentations, dagger categories, or category theory.

### 4.2 Dagger-linear source, quotient, representation, and grammars

The source

`K<B,G>_dagger / <R>`

is a well-defined finitely presented `K`-linear dagger category when `<R>` is
the two-sided dagger ideal. Identities, zero arrows, addition, scalar
multiplication, and dagger are present and typed. The unit boundary represents
`C`; the scalar unit and the exact `1/2` are therefore meaningful. The finite
presentation is not a finite category, so distinct represented powers
`2^(-k)` avoid the earlier obstruction.

Exact equality of represented maps is a composition-, linear-, and
dagger-compatible congruence. Quotienting by `ker rho_D` makes the
representation faithful, although that operational quotient need not retain a
finite presentation; the paper does not need it to. The amplitude layer and
the separately declared CPTP/UCP channel layer are correctly distinguished.
Unitary amplitude arrows induce unitary channels, while expectations and
discard maps require separate admission.

The qualification is O6: the concrete base grammar is described by intended
closure rather than by a fully printed production list.

### 4.3 Independent W3 reconstruction

Use the ordered basis `00,01,10,11` and

`U=CNOT_(b->m)(H_b tensor I_m)`,
`N=H_b tensor I_m`,
`V=H_b tensor I_m`,
`E=(H_b tensor I_m)CNOT_(b->m)`.

For each basis input,

`U|b,m>=(|0,m>+(-1)^b|1,m xor 1>)/sqrt(2)`.

The two nonzero fine alternatives lie in distinct memory sectors, so the
paper's universally quantified correlation implication holds. In contrast,

`N|b,m>=(|0,m>+(-1)^b|1,m>)/sqrt(2)`

has two distinct nonzero alternatives in the same memory sector and fails it.
Because `V` changes only the branch bit, a fixed final basis effect can see
only fine alternatives with its memory value, proving availability.

For the eraser witness with input and output `00`, the `Q_00` and `Q_11`
branch amplitudes are each exactly `1/2`; their cross term is `1/4`. They lie
in different memory sectors, so availability fails with the required
nonzero cross-sector witness. The closed preserving amplitude

`f_00 V Q_00 U eta_00`

is exactly `1/2`. All sources and targets match the printed boundary types.
The term "no-write" is correctly declared an operational role rather than
inferred solely from its failed predicate.

### 4.4 Heisenberg observability theorem

The definition

`A_W(y)=C*(Phi_a*(P): a admitted, P in R_W)`

is correctly typed for channels whose Schrödinger direction is `y -> x_W`.
It does not confuse a nonunital amplitude sandwich with a UCP Heisenberg map.
The claimed properties follow directly:

- generated-C*-algebra minimality;
- monotonicity under adding transports;
- covariance under exact process isomorphism and gauge conjugation;
- independence from operation handles, tensor-factor names, and external

For a product channel, the spectator contribution is
`Psi_B*(I_B)=I_B`; the clean-product proof therefore uses precisely the
unitality condition demanded by the pin. Without it, a nonunital amplitude

### 4.5 Seed and mixed observable algebras

For the seed,

`N^dag Z_m N = Z_m`,
`U^dag Z_m U = X_b Z_m`.

Hence

`A_0=C*(Z_m,X_b Z_m)=span{I,Z_m,X_b,X_b Z_m}`,

a four-dimensional proper MASA in `M_4(C)`. This independently confirms the

Multiplication by `Z_m` yields `Z_b` and `X_b`; together with `X_m,Z_m` these
generate the Pauli algebra `M_4(C)`, dimension 16. No support label is used in
this calculation. The completion dependence is real: it proves that one

`{I,X_b,Z_m,X_b Z_m}`

### 4.7 Accessible supports and quantaloid axioms

For a finite-dimensional CP map, any two minimal Kraus families differ by a
unitary mixing, so their linear spans coincide. The canonical Kraus support is
well defined and gauge covariant. Closing the admitted generators, effects,
projectors, and Kraus supports under typed composition, complex linear span,
adjoint, and identities gives a least complex-linear dagger category `S_D`.

For the full subspace lattices of its hom-spaces:

- arbitrary joins are spans of unions;
- bottom is the zero subspace;
- composition is the span of pointwise matrix products;
- bilinearity gives join preservation in each variable;
- associativity and two-sided identity lines follow from matrix composition;
- dagger reverses composition, preserves joins, and is involutive;
- boundary conjugation gives a gauge-covariant quantaloid isomorphism.

Thus Theorem 7.2 is correct. The caveat is O3: all-subspace completion is not
the least family of supports with operational referents, so the strong
accessible/dormant interpretation is not proved.
