## 3. The decisive obstruction in one calculation

The chart layer assumes that record propositions transported by the allowed
Heisenberg channels remain sharp projectors from which a Boolean record
algebra can be formed. General UCP transport does not have that property.

Let

$$
P_0=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&0\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
P_1=\begin{pmatrix}0&0\\0&1\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
X=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix},
$$

and define the unital completely positive Heisenberg channel

$$
\Phi^*(A)=\frac34 A+\frac14 XAX.
$$

Then

$$
\Phi^*(P_0)=
\begin{pmatrix}3/4&0\\0&1/4\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
\Phi^*(P_1)=
\begin{pmatrix}1/4&0\\0&3/4\end{pmatrix}.
$$

These two effects commute and sum to the identity, but

$$
\Phi^*(P_0)^2\ne\Phi^*(P_0),
\qquad
\Phi^*(P_1)^2\ne\Phi^*(P_1).
$$

They are therefore not projections. Commuting effects form a commutative
effect interface, not automatically a Boolean algebra of sharp propositions.
The paper's displayed inclusion

$$
\operatorname{Bool}\langle\mathsf P_c\rangle
\hookrightarrow
\operatorname{Proj}(\mathcal A_c)
$$

is consequently undefined for channels explicitly allowed by its own
observability construction.

There is a second independent failure. If a concept algebra is defined by

$$
\mathcal A_c=\bigcap_W\mathcal A_W,
$$

a projector belonging to one \(\mathcal A_W\) need not belong to every other
one and therefore need not lie in \(\mathcal A_c\). Pairwise commutativity of
the collected projectors does not imply intersection membership.

Thus the problem is not missing topology. It occurs earlier: the proposed
record-bearing chart object is not produced by the allowed quantum maps.

## 6. Ontological interpretation

The cycle has established a coherent exact language in which a genuinely
quantum process writes a stable record under one continuation and can erase
that availability and recover coherence under another. That is an important
ontological base: records are features of the same quantum dynamics, not
classical labels attached afterward.

It has also established a meaningful law-relative question:

> Given a specified record experiment and specified admissible
> counterfactual transports, which operator algebra can that record make
> observable at another boundary?

What it has not established is that those boundaries, projector resolutions
and transports are intrinsically selected by the quantum process, nor that
the resulting observability algebras form physical local charts. The present
objects therefore remain quantum-process and record structures, not regions
of space.

The failure of Boolean `Rec` is physically informative. A noisy quantum
channel naturally carries sharp propositions to unsharp effects. A future
theory must choose honestly among at least three possibilities:

1. restrict chart morphisms to sharpness-preserving maps;
2. replace Boolean record interfaces by an effect-algebra/POVM interface; or
3. derive a sharp subinterface by an additional dynamical theorem.

This adjudication chooses none of them. Any choice changes the theory and
requires a new pin.
