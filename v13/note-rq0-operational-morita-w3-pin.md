## 3. Nonnegotiable type corrections

### 3.1 Complete means complete relative to the admitted law

For every boundary $x$, the operational marking must contain the complete
admitted families of:

- preparation instruments and all their outcome branches;
- effect/readout instruments and all their outcomes and coarse-grainings;
- channels and outcome-indexed instruments;
- admissible counterfactual comparisons; and
- scalar experiment contexts generated by these data.

“Complete” does **not** silently mean every mathematical state or effect of
the ambient matrix algebra. Full tomography is an additional physical
postulate when used. It means that no candidate seam may select a favorable
proper subfamily from the effects or preparations that the declared law
already admits.

A W3 experiment may use one complete admitted source or readout instrument.
It must include every branch of that instrument. The theory-level seam object
must range over all eligible complete admitted instruments; it may not range
over arbitrary subsets of their outcomes.

### 3.2 Effects are not scalar probes

The complete admitted output effects at $y$ generate a unital self-adjoint
operator system

$$
\mathcal E_D(y)\subseteq A_y.
$$

Preservation is tested on this whole operator system. A singleton effect
chosen after a candidate record is seen is not an eligible test object.

### 3.3 Block preservation is not by itself readable availability

Let $C\cong\mathbb C^\Omega$ be a finite classical record object acting
sharply at a cut, with sector idempotents $p_r$. Let

$$
T:A_y\longrightarrow A_x
$$

be the UCP Heisenberg map of a continuation. Define the dephasing conditional
expectation

$$
\mathcal D_C(a)=\sum_r p_r a p_r.
$$

The universal block condition is

$$
\boxed{
\mathcal D_C T(a)=T(a)
\quad\text{for every }a\in\mathcal E_D(y).
}
$$

Equivalently,

$$
p_rT(a)p_s=0
\quad(r\ne s)
$$

for every admitted output effect. In a concrete faithful representation this
is equivalent to

$$
T(\mathcal E_D(y))\subseteq C'.
$$

The paper must prove these equivalences at its declared scope.

This condition says that no admitted output effect sees cross-sector
coherence. It is weaker than exact readable availability. A preserving W3
continuation must additionally contain a complete admitted outcome
instrument and an outcome coarse-graining whose sharp Heisenberg pullback is
the record algebra, or the equivalent internal classical-module/comodule
factorization. The identity effect, which necessarily spans all record
sectors, must never be forced into one sector.

If $\mathcal E_D(y)=A_y=B(H_y)$ and $T$ is a unitary isomorphism, the block
condition permits no nontrivial $C$. This is a legitimate negative result,
not a reason to shrink the effect system after seeing the candidate.

### 3.4 Erasure is the failure of the universal block law

An admitted erasing continuation must provide an admitted effect
$a\in\mathcal E_D(y)$ and distinct sectors with

$$
p_rE(a)p_s\ne0.
$$

The paper must retain the original W3 requirement that this coherence is
connected to the written alternatives and to a complete admitted experiment,
not merely to an arbitrary matrix element.

## 4. Marked finite operational theory

The paper must define one exact finite-dimensional object. A permitted
architecture is a marked operational correspondence theory

$$
\mathbb D=(\mathsf{Corr}_D,\mathsf{Op}_D,\mathcal M_D),
$$

with the following separately typed layers.

### 4.1 Horizontal Morita layer

$\mathsf{Corr}_D$ is a bicategory whose:

- objects are finite-dimensional C*-algebra boundary objects;
- 1-morphisms are declared C*-correspondences/Hilbert bimodules; and
- 2-morphisms are adjointable bimodule intertwiners.

Invertible 1-morphisms are imprimitivity bimodules and implement strong
Morita equivalences.

### 4.2 Operational CP layer

$\mathsf{Op}_D$ contains the admitted CP maps, UCP Heisenberg channels and
outcome-indexed instruments with their exact source, target, outcome and
composition types. CP maps must not be silently identified with arbitrary
Hilbert bimodules. Any use of a GNS/Paschke correspondence must state its
hypotheses and what process information it preserves.

### 4.3 Complete marking

$\mathcal M_D$ assigns to each boundary its complete admitted state, effect,
instrument, comparison and scalar-context semantics. It includes closure
under the declared physical operations and contains no candidate-dependent
support label.

Operationally indistinguishable raw arrows are identified before seam
construction by equality in every complete marked scalar context. The paper
must show that this is a congruence for every constructor actually used.

### 4.4 Marked operational Morita equivalence

An operational Morita equivalence is not bare strong Morita equivalence of
the C*-algebras. It consists of an imprimitivity bimodule together with a
coherent equivalence of the complete markings, including all outcome
instruments, counterfactual comparisons and scalar pairings.

Bare Morita equivalence may erase physically accessible matrix size or
control structure and therefore does not count. Conversely, two marked laws
with the same ambient C*-algebra but different admitted instruments need not
be equivalent.

The paper must state the coherence needed for identities, composition,
dagger/adjoint where used, instrument outcome maps and scalar experiment
values. It must not define equivalence by saying only that W3 seams agree.

## 5. Classical record objects after localization

### 5.1 Primary object

A sharp finite record is represented internally by a finite commutative
classical algebra object

$$
C\cong\mathbb C^\Omega,
$$

equivalently at the declared categorical scope by a commutative special
symmetric dagger-Frobenius algebra, together with its admitted action on an
operational module/correspondence.

The classical object and its action are primary. An embedded PVM

$$
\iota:C\to A_x
$$

is a concrete representative when such a representative is available. It is
not the invariant definition.

### 5.2 Fine/coarse structure

Fine alternatives and the coarse record must be typed as a morphism of finite
classical objects or an equivalent inclusion/action datum. The refinement map
must be part of the internal categorical structure, not an independently
supplied support field.

### 5.3 Morita transport obligation

For an $A$--$B$ imprimitivity bimodule $X$, a classical $C$-action on an
$A$-module must transport by interior tensor product to a classical action on
the corresponding $B$-module. The paper must prove exactly which Frobenius,
sharpness and W3 structures survive this transport.

Ordinary Morita equivalence does not automatically turn a selected
commutative subalgebra of $A$ into a selected commutative subalgebra of $B$.
The paper must therefore work with the transported module/action object or
register a block if it cannot make this step type-correct.

## 8. Effective isotropy and finite rigidification

At the finite groupoid scope, let $\mathfrak S$ be the marked Morita W3
groupoid before rigidification. For every object $s$, define

$$
K_s=
\{g\in\operatorname{Aut}_{\mathfrak S}(s):
g\text{ acts trivially on every marked state, effect, instrument,}
\text{ scalar context, classical action and addressability map}\}.
$$

The paper must prove that the $K_s$ form a conjugation-stable normal system.
It must then construct the finite quotient groupoid explicitly, including
its hom-sets and composition:

$$
\mathfrak S_{\mathrm{eff}}=\mathfrak S/\!/K.

Only ineffective isotropy is removed. An automorphism that permutes W3
alternatives, changes an admitted control, or acts nontrivially on any marked
operational module remains as physical symmetry.

No general algebraic-stack rigidification theorem may be invoked without its
hypotheses. The required result is the explicit finite groupoid theorem.
