## 4. Declared finite comb scope

The paper must use one explicit finite-dimensional convention for quantum
combs or process tensors.

### 4.1 Typed wire profile

A process type $\tau$ is a finite ordered list of input and output Hilbert
spaces. A deterministic $N$-comb is represented by a positive Choi operator
obeying the complete causal normalization recursion. A probabilistic comb is
a positive subcomb dominated by a deterministic comb. A finite quantum
instrument is a finite family of probabilistic combs whose sum is
deterministic.

The paper must print:

- the Choi convention;
- the deterministic-comb normalization equations;
- the type of every source, write, continuation, record and output wire;
- the link product or equivalent composition operation; and
- the generalized Born pairing with testers.

The ordered wire profile is a declared laboratory composition order. It is
not an emergent causal order, temporal metric, global time or spacetime
foliation. No result may use it to claim `RQ0-C1` or later causality.

### 4.2 Complete instruments are primary

Every source, continuation and readout instrument is retained as its complete
family of CP branches. Branch probabilities, conditional quantum outputs,
classical outcomes and later output types are part of the object. Replacing
an instrument by its normalized states or POVM effects is a shadow map, not
an identity.

### 4.3 Admitted testers

For every process type $\tau$, the declared law supplies a candidate-
independent family $\mathsf{Test}_D(\tau)$ of complete generalized testers.
It may contain ancillas, quantum memory, adaptive controls and terminal
readouts when those operations are admitted by the law.

The family must contain the deterministic trivial tester and be closed under
the classical postprocessings, refinements and admitted pre/post-compositions
actually used in the paper. “All testers” always means all testers admitted
at this declared law-relative scope, not every mathematically possible
tester unless full process tomography is explicitly postulated.

## 5. Operational quotient before W3

For a comb $C$ and a complete tester
$\mathbf T=(T_t)_{t\in\Lambda}$, let

$$
p_{\mathbf T}(C)
=
\bigl(\langle T_t,C\rangle\bigr)_{t\in\Lambda}
$$

be the generalized Born probability vector.

For two Hermitian process operators of the same type, define

$$
\|C-C'\|_{\mathsf{Test}_D}
=
\sup_{\mathbf T\in\mathsf{Test}_D(\tau)}
\|p_{\mathbf T}(C)-p_{\mathbf T}(C')\|_1.
$$

This is an admitted-tester seminorm; the conventional factor $1/2$ may be
introduced only if stated consistently. Define

$$
C\simeq_{\mathrm{op}}C'
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\|C-C'\|_{\mathsf{Test}_D}=0.
$$

The paper must prove at the declared finite scope:

1. nonnegativity, symmetry, homogeneity and the triangle inequality on the
   real span of same-type combs;
2. equivalence with equality of every admitted tester probability;
3. invariance under outcome-handle renaming and Choi/basis presentation
   changes;
4. congruence under every admitted link composition used later; and
5. reduction to the established comb/strategy distinguishability norm when
   the tester family is unrestricted.

No dormant matrix coefficient survives the quotient merely because it is
nonzero.

## 7. Operational W3 conditions

Let $W$ denote the written branch-instrument comb before the continuation
slot, with every source outcome retained. Let $\mathcal D_R\star W$ denote
insertion of the record dephasing channel at the cut.

### 7.1 Write correlation

The complete source outcome and conditional process are retained. For every
nonzero source branch, the admitted fine/coarse record interrogation must
satisfy the exact W3 support/correlation rule. The paper must state whether
the fine alternatives are an admitted cut instrument, a classical action, or
another explicitly typed object.

No source branch may be divided by its total weight and then forgotten.

### 7.2 No-write control

The no-write process is another complete comb of the same type. At least one
retained source branch must fail the matched branch-record correlation test.
The failure must be a complete probability statement, not one selected
matrix column.

### 7.3 Preservation

For a complete continuation instrument $V$, including its accessible
classical outcome wire, define preservation by

$$
\boxed{
\|V\star W-V\star\mathcal D_R\star W\|_{\mathsf{Test}_D}=0.
}
$$

Every admitted downstream tester must give the same outcome probabilities
for the coherent and dephased processes. Because the continuation's outcome
wire remains available, this compares all branches rather than only their
unconditional sum.

### 7.4 Coherent erasure or recovery

For a complete candidate eraser instrument $E$, define

$$
\boxed{
\|E\star W-E\star\mathcal D_R\star W\|_{\mathsf{Test}_D}>0.
}
$$

Equivalently, an admitted complete tester and one of its outcomes must have a
different probability on the two processes. A nonzero complex matrix
coefficient with no probability contrast is not operational erasure.

### 7.5 Recoverable process coherence

Define the law-relative quantity

$$
\mathcal C_R(W;E)
=
\|E\star(W-\mathcal D_R\star W)\|_{\mathsf{Test}_D}.
$$

The paper must prove its presentation invariance, monotonicity under
restriction of the tester family, and contraction under admitted downstream
supermaps whose pullback preserves the tester family. It must remain distinct
from the Born composition defect and the record multiplicativity defect.
