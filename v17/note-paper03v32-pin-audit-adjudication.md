## 1. Question and answer

The question is whether the unchanged v2 relativistic quantum process can be
represented by one honest predictive object at every licensed laboratory
frontier, without omitting memory, assuming the desired stochastic/Heisenberg
compatibility, or confusing prediction with ontology.

The answer is:

> **Yes, on the explicitly restricted packet domain, and only as a
> representation theorem.**

The audits and direct reconstruction show that the theorem domain is nonempty
and the categorical construction is not circular. They do not show that all
physical instruments lie in the domain, that the predictive algebra is the
ontology of nature, or that any frontier is a native physical division.

## 2. Root reconstruction A — the complete-boundary repair is exact

The v3.1 counterexample used two laws with identical represented quantum and
record data but different future-readable external memory. v3.2 removes the
open third factor from the type.

For each frontier $D$, the construction must freeze the complete licensed
future and reader interface first. One represented algebra
$\mathcal B_D$ then contains every licensed future-readable nonrecord
observable. The hybrid reader algebra is

$$
\mathcal O_D
=\mathcal B_D\,\overline\otimes L^\infty(R_D,\nu_D),
$$

with the finite/countable atomic direct sum as the exact atomic regime.

This is a constant-fiber theorem. It neither assumes that
$\mathcal B_D$ factors internally nor permits one to pad nonisomorphic
outcome fibers with operationally absent observables. If the true packet has
nonisomorphic record-dependent fibers, it is outside this theorem and must be
refused or separately typed.

The repair is domain-level, not verbal. If two exact samples have the same
displayed record and complete normal state but some licensed future produces
different reader statistics, the object fails admission. The construction
may represent the missing variable before testing or refuse the packet; it
may not add it after seeing the result.

An exact diffuse classical variable cannot be hidden in an $L^\infty$ factor
and then read by generic point evaluation. The finite/atomic versus diffuse
point-state firewall remains binding even inside $\mathcal B_D$.

## 3. Root reconstruction B — `Ens` and closure

For an admitted law $\Lambda\in\mathfrak E_D$, let

$$

\qquad
h=\frac{d\eta}{d\nu_D},
$$

and let $r\mapsto\bar\beta_r$ be the strongly measurable conditional
normal-state barycentre. Then

$$
g(r)=h(r)\bar\beta_r
\in L^1(R_D,\nu_D;(\mathcal B_D)_*)
$$

defines the normal state

$$
\operatorname{Ens}_D(\Lambda)(F)
=\int\langle g(r),F(r)\rangle\,\nu_D(dr).
$$

Dominance makes this representative independent; the predual field gives
positivity, normalization, and normality. No generic diffuse point state is
constructed.

The v3.1 singleton-class counterexample is now excluded independently of the
normal-state proof. Every admitted class must close under:

1. identity;
2. every licensed primitive;
3. every licensed finite path;
4. every registered finite preparation mixture; and

For a positive event $\Delta$,

$$

## 4. Root reconstruction C — one paired category

A physical arrow is the exact five-field object

$$

$$

not a UCP map with an external or implicit stochastic witness. Admission

stochastic null-ideal preservation, target landing, lineage, and

$$
\operatorname{Ens}_E(\Lambda K_a)
=\operatorname{Ens}_D(\Lambda)\circ\Phi_a

for every admitted source law and target reader.

The equality defines the certified hom-set, but it may not be used as the
constructor proof. Each physical constructor must derive $K_a$, $\Phi_a$,
and their equality from one common frozen primitive.

For valid $a:D\to E$ and $b:E\to F$,

$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Ens}_F(\Lambda K_aK_b)
&=\operatorname{Ens}_E(\Lambda K_a)\circ\Phi_b\\
&=\operatorname{Ens}_D(\Lambda)\circ\Phi_a\circ\Phi_b.

An explicit skip may have identity semantic projections while retaining a

## 5. Root reconstruction D — the continuous domain is nonempty

The quantum audit gives a genuine continuous, record-dependent witness. Let

$$
R_D=\{0,1\},\qquad S=[0,1],\qquad
R_E=R_D\times S,
$$

with $j(r,s)=(r,s)$, counting/Lebesgue measure classes, and
$\mathcal B_D=\mathcal B_E=M_2(\mathbb C)$. For
$0<\epsilon<1$, define

$$
E_{r,s}=I+(-1)^r\epsilon\cos(2\pi s)Z,
\qquad M_{r,s}=E_{r,s}^{1/2}.
$$

Every $E_{r,s}$ is strictly positive and
$\int_0^1E_{r,s}\,ds=I$. Hence

$$
\mathcal I_r(B,\Delta)
=\int_\Delta M_{r,s}^*BM_{r,s}\,ds
$$

is a normal complete instrument and

$$
\widehat{\mathcal I}_r(G)
=\int_0^1M_{r,s}^*G(s)M_{r,s}\,ds
$$

is a normal UCP extension. The density

$$
p_{\rho,r}(s)=\rho(E_{r,s})
$$

is continuous and bounded below by $1-\epsilon>0$, and

$$
\rho_{r,s}(B)
=\frac{\rho(M_{r,s}^*BM_{r,s})}{p_{\rho,r}(s)}
$$

is jointly Borel. The target retains the old record exactly. Thus the
positive domain contains more than finite or uninformative controls.

This witness proves nonvacuity only. It is not new physical input and cannot
replace the general constructor theorem.

## 6. Root reconstruction E — the continuous proof is derived

For a current record $r$, input state $\rho$, and rectangle reader,

$$
\int_\Delta\rho_{r,s}(B)\,\mu_{\rho,r}(ds)
=\rho(\mathcal I_r(B,\Delta))
=\rho(\widehat{\mathcal I}_r(B\otimes\chi_\Delta)).
$$

For a simple target field and record update $j$, linearity gives the target
reader expectation. Joint measurability and a separately proved decomposable

Integration over every admitted input law gives the primitive `Ens` identity;
paired-category composition gives every admitted finite path.

Construction must additionally prove the exact sample-level intertwining:
the conditional barycentre of the frozen jointly Borel kernel $K_a$ at
$(x,r,s)$ must equal the posterior generated by the same

This requirement is already contained in the pin's common-primitive,
posterior-kernel, and all-reader clauses. This adjudication makes its required
proof form explicit; it adds no new object.

## 7. Binding construction conditions

The following are theorem hypotheses or proof obligations, never automatic
consequences of fiberwise NEP.

1. **Constant fibers.** One common $\mathcal B_D$ represents the boundary.
   Nonisomorphic outcome-dependent fibers are outside the pin.
2. **Complete interface.** The entire licensed future reader algebra freezes
   before completeness is tested, including infinite registered reader
   algebras—not merely a finite test list.
3. **No fake padding.** Enlarging a common fiber may not introduce
   operational states/readers absent from the physical packet.
4. **Common continuous class.** One sigma-finite technical outcome/null class
   supports the declared continuous arrow. Uncountably many mutually singular
   fiber laws may force schema splitting or refusal.
5. **Joint measurability.** Extension, posterior, and sample-kernel families
   are jointly measurable in all retained records, states, and outcomes.
6. **Assembled normality.** Normality of every fiber map does not suffice;
   the decomposable hybrid arrow needs a predual or monotone-net proof.
7. **Joint null transport.** The record update must satisfy

   $$
   j_*(\nu_D\otimes\mu)\ll\nu_E
   $$

   or an explicitly equivalent instrument-weighted condition strong enough
   to make the full pullback representative independent.
8. **Target landing.** Ambient-algebra extensions do not define arrows unless
   their range lands in the exact target packet.
9. **Posterior/kernel identity.** Both semantic routes arise from the same
   instrument, with exact sample-state barycentre equality.
10. **Ensemble closure.** Paths, mixtures, and every registered positive
    central conditioning remain in the declared class.
11. **Old-record retention.** A measurement appends or preserves the existing
    record unless an explicit licensed discard or coarse-graining changes the
    interface.
12. **Exact/a.e. split.** Integrated predictions ignore the common null ideal;
    exact concurrency compares frozen kernel representatives and full paired
    arrows in every reachable context.
13. **Finite histories.** Every history law is indexed by an initial sample or
    admitted law. Finite induction supplies no infinite-stream measure.
14. **Refusal.** Non-NEP, approximate-NEP, ambient-only, singular,
    nonmeasurable, hidden-memory, late-reader, and varying-fiber cases are
    refused at the exact failed gate, not repaired by retuning physics.

## 8. Exact scope relative to Barandes and ontology

The accepted construction attempt is compatible with a Barandes-style
separation of configuration, law, conditioning, division, and actual
trajectory, but it does not complete that ontology.

The complete predictive algebra is a sufficient statistic for one frozen
licensed experiment family. It may be global, contextual, entangled, and
memory-bearing. It is not thereby:

- a local beable algebra;
- the configuration space of nature;
- an ontic Markov checkpoint;
- a universal division event;
- a spacetime slice;
- an internal clock; or
- an actuality rule.

Representational Markovization is allowed because it makes the memory debt
explicit. It does not turn the original indivisible stochastic process into
a Markov process at every physical cut.
