## 1. Verdict and binding construction scope

The mathematical boundary is coherent and jointly constructible in a finite
or standard-Borel comparator. It correctly distinguishes:

1. a clock correlated with an external parameter;
2. clock-relative operational adequacy;
3. a clock-neutral parent representation; and
4. operational redundancy of the external parameter.

No implication among these four is automatic. A constrained history-state
wrapper can earn formal parametrization without earning independent physical
explanation, ontology, or timelessness.

Acceptance is conditional on all of the following construction rules. They
are necessary to prevent an in-scope false positive and are not optional
wording advice.

1. **Nonvacuous temporal target.** The registered held-out comparator must
   contain at least one complete system reader whose law differs between two
   laboratory parameter values, and at least one genuinely sequential or
   adaptive context whose joint law is not fixed by one-time marginals.
   A stationary one-dimensional system or identity-only program can test
   clock correlation but cannot earn clock-relative adequacy or parameter
   redundancy.
2. **Separating calibration.** Fitting the two affine parameters $a,b$
   requires calibration data distinct from at least one positive-support
   reading region used only for validation. Two deterministic reading values
   alone cannot test affine form: any ordered pair fits one affine map.
3. **Nonvacuous error gate.** The error functional, weighting of complete
   readers, tolerance, training/test split, and a no-clock or deliberately
   mistuned baseline freeze before clock/parent selection. The tolerance must
   distinguish the claimed task; total-variation tolerance one or an
   equivalent always-pass criterion earns nothing.
4. **One common complete instrument.** Reading probabilities, posterior
   states, retained records, disturbance, adaptive guards, and later clock
   behavior come from the same clock instrument. Equal POVM effects with
   different instruments are not interchangeable.
5. **Diffuse version discipline.** A continuous reading conditions only
   almost everywhere. A feedback policy must be measurable and invariant
   under allowed null-version changes; a selected singleton posterior cannot
   seed exact future physics.
6. **Frame-map sufficiency.** A common joint law does not alone imply

   $$
   P_A(R\mid\tau_A)=
   \int P_B(R\mid\tau_B)M_{B\mid A}(d\tau_B\mid\tau_A).
   $$

   This classical form requires a separately proved conditional-sufficiency
   or Blackwell-garbling relation for the complete reader family. Otherwise
   the construction must use the full parent reduction map, retain the other
   clock/residual memory, or report clock-choice-dependent/lossy dynamics.
7. **Complete temporal-frame transport.** A quantum frame change transforms
   states, observables, records, instruments, entanglement, and adaptive
   contexts through the common parent. A scalar label map or equality of one
   marginal is insufficient.
8. **Operational backreaction.** “Nonzero interaction” means that removing
   the frozen interaction changes predeclared clock and system reader laws in
   both intervention directions on admitted states. A multiple of the
   identity, a global phase, or an inert eigen-sector does not pass T19.
9. **Finite-resource honesty.** Dimension, energy origin and bandwidth,
   coherence, resolution, recurrence window, success/failure probability,
   readout cost, and running interval are fixed at the level used. Rescaling
   a finite Hamiltonian without paying energy is not a finite-clock result.
10. **Parent-source lineage.** The parent state, constraint/law,
    factorization, reduction maps, controller, and clock preparations freeze
    before held-out target dynamics are inspected. Encoding the external
    parameter in $\sigma_C(s)$, a controller program, boundary condition, or
    adaptively chosen factorization counts as consuming it.
11. **Physical normalization.** A Page--Wootters or Dirac route must construct
    the physical solution space and physical inner product or rigging/group-
    averaging map. A kinematic norm or formal distributional constraint
    vector is not a probability model.
12. **Local/sector scope.** Covariant POVMs, clock reductions, round trips,
    and temporal-frame changes are claimed only on their common positive-
    support sector/window. Periodic, relativistic frequency-sector, stopped,
    and Gribov-type domain boundaries remain printed.
13. **Periodic-clock enlargement.** An auxiliary cycle record creates a
    larger composite reference system; it does not make the original
    periodic reading globally injective or automatically gauge invariant.
14. **Outcome-neutral classification.** If any gate fails, the corresponding
    product coordinate remains failed, lossy, dependent, formal, or
    unconstructed. No neighboring success compensates for it.

## 2. Independent quantum-clock reconstruction

### 2.1 Clock instruments and complete records

A physical clock must be a subsystem in the complete Paper-03 packet, not a
function of slot rank. Its reading operation is a complete finite/countable
or standard-Borel instrument. For a discrete result $t$, the branch maps
$\mathcal J_t$ are CP and generally nonunital, while the complete retained-
record arrow is normal UCP on the direct-sum target. The nonselective map
$\sum_t\mathcal J_t$ forgets $t$ and cannot drive a later clock-controlled
guard.

Two clock instruments with the same POVM effects can have different state
updates. For example, a Lüders clock readout and a measure-and-reset clock
can have identical one-reading statistics but different second-reading and
system-continuation laws. Therefore T4 and T8 must bind the joint sample
kernel, posterior state, retained record, and Heisenberg map to one common
instrument as Paper 03 requires.

For a standard-Borel reading, positive-event conditioning is

$$
P(Y\in A\mid T_C\in\Delta,u)
=\frac{P(Y\in A,T_C\in\Delta\mid u)}
       {P(T_C\in\Delta\mid u)}.
$$

It is normalized only when the denominator is positive. Point conditioning
is a regular conditional kernel defined clock-marginal almost everywhere;
changing it on a null set changes no integrated complete-reader prediction.
Any adaptive policy must be a frozen measurable function of the retained
record class, not of an after-the-fact point version.

### 2.2 Sequential and adaptive adequacy

One-time conditional Born probabilities do not characterize a clock-relative
process. A correct construction must reproduce for every registered finite
sequence the joint law

$$
P(dt_1,dr_1,\ldots,dt_n,dr_n\mid u)
$$

generated by the complete clock and system instruments, including readout
disturbance, retained memories, and branch-dependent later mechanisms.
Equality of all one-time marginals does not imply equality of this law. A
simple counterexample is two binary histories with identical fair marginals
but respectively perfect correlation and perfect anticorrelation between
the two records.

Paper 03 already types finite histories and complete adaptive contexts. Paper
04 may condition or reduce those histories, but may not replace two
same-reading histories by one Markov checkpoint unless their complete future
profiles agree. Finite-path results do not create an infinite stream.

### 2.3 Two clocks and the frame-map obstruction

Distinct subsystem identity plus an independently variable preparation,
generator, or readout is a coherent independence criterion. It correctly
allows interaction and entanglement while excluding a renamed clock,
deterministic copy, or affine postprocessing of one result.

One joint law is necessary but not sufficient for a scalar stochastic
change-of-clock equation. Let $A,B,R$ be binary, $A$ and $B$ independent and
fair, and $R=A$. Then

$$
P_A(R=r\mid A=a)=\delta_{r,a},
\qquad
P_B(R=r\mid B=b)=\tfrac12.
$$

The actual kernel $M_{B\mid A}$ is fair, so every mixture of the
$B$-conditioned reader laws remains fair and cannot equal the deterministic
$A$-conditioned law. This packet has one perfectly valid joint law and
positive support everywhere. The missing premise is conditional sufficiency,
equivalently the Markov/garbling relation needed for the stated direction of
reconstruction.

The pin survives because the equation is explicitly a theorem target, not an
admission axiom, and because complete parent reduction maps may carry more
than the scalar $B$ reading. Construction must either prove the sufficiency
relation for every complete reader, transform the full residual system
including clock A, quantify exact information loss, or return
`P04-CLOCK-CHOICE-DEPENDENT-DYNAMICS`.

### 2.4 Clock-neutral parent and Page--Wootters route

A coherent constrained route begins with

$$
(\mathcal H_{\rm kin},\widehat C,
 \mathcal H_{\rm phys},\eta_{\rm phys},E_A,E_B,
 \mathcal O_{\rm rel},\mathcal R_A,\mathcal R_B),
$$

defines the kernel/solution space of the constraint with its physical inner
product, and derives reductions to each clock perspective. Temporal-frame
changes must pass from one reduction back through the clock-neutral physical
space and into the other reduction. This is stronger than refactoring a
Hilbert space or writing a stationary vector.

The Page--Wootters/Dirac/relational-Heisenberg trinity is available only under
the exact constraint, covariant-POVM, sector, physical-inner-product, and
reduction-map hypotheses. Its primary-source construction transforms both
states and observables and is generally local in clock perspective. It does
not prove that every interaction, every finite periodic clock, or every
factorization lies in one global trinity domain.

An arbitrary parameterized unitary can be encoded in a history state after
the fact. Such a wrapper may exactly reproduce the comparator, but when the
constraint, parent state, or factorization was reverse-engineered from the
full target dynamics, its proper outcome is
`P04-FORMAL-PARAMETRIZATION-REPRESENTATION`. It cannot earn independent
parent selection or fundamental timelessness.

### 2.5 Finite clocks, recurrence, and backreaction

A finite clock has finite information capacity and normally recurrent
dynamics. It can support a local temporal frame on an injective or
low-ambiguity window. When a reading recurs with different complete future
profiles, conditioning on that reading produces a mixture rather than a
global time coordinate. An additional physical cycle memory changes the
reference system and must pay its own resources; hidden path position cannot
serve as the cycle count.

Clock-system interaction can yield a time-nonlocal relational equation. This
is physically admissible. What matters operationally is that the same frozen
interaction changes registered system responses under clock interventions
and changes registered clock responses under system interventions. Removing
only one direction, retuning the system Hamiltonian, or discarding readout
disturbance changes the model.

Finite dimension alone does not bound precision. Scaling the Hamiltonian can
make phase advance arbitrarily fast unless energy bandwidth, mean/variance,
coherence, and running interval are included. Postselecting rare accurate
ticks also requires the failure probability and record to remain in the
joint law.

### 2.6 Reparameterization and hidden-time audit

Pushing a clock POVM and record forward under an admitted
orientation-preserving reading-coordinate bijection is coordinate covariance,
provided density and calibration measures transform too. It does not prove
that the parent law lacks an external evolution parameter.

The held-out dependency graph must start at the fixed parent state/law,
physical preparations and controllers, and exposed context $u$. It must end
at complete reader probabilities without a path through comparator $s$,
frontier rank, source order, run counter, random seed, target label, hidden
winding number, or an externally timed switch. A physical controller is
allowed, but its memory, preparation, energy, and source lineage are then
part of the clock/reference packet.

## 6. Fresh Seat-Q semantic countermodels FQ1--FQ18

These countermodels are additional to C1--C42 and attacks 1--68.

### FQ1 — two-point affine tautology

Let clock A have deterministic supported readings $0,1$ and clock B have
deterministic readings $7,11$ in the corresponding two contexts. The unique
positive affine map $B=4A+7$ fits exactly. Repeating those same two values on
a test set checks stability/noise but cannot test affine functional form;
every ordered two-point relation passes. T9 therefore needs at least one
positive-support validation region not used to determine $a,b$, or another
explicit overidentifying statistic. **Affected:** T7, T9, products 7 and 9.

### FQ2 — always-pass accuracy threshold

Take total-variation loss and predeclare tolerance $1$, its maximum. Every
clock model, including an independent random reading, passes held-out
adequacy. Preregistration alone does not make the criterion informative. A
claimed adequacy gate must beat a frozen null/mistuned comparator or use a
strict separating threshold. **Affected:** T7--T9, products 7--9.

### FQ3 — temporally empty target

Let the system algebra be one dimensional, all system operations identities,
and all readers constant, while two physical clock subsystems tick. Every
system prediction is external-parameter independent even if the clock is
uncorrelated. This can establish clock physics but not replacement of an
external evolution parameter. A time-sensitive held-out system reader and a
nontrivial sequential context are mandatory. **Affected:** T7, T8, T13,
product 22.

### FQ4 — joint law without a stochastic frame garbling

Use the binary $A,B,R$ law of Section 2.3 with $R=A$ and independent B. All
conditionals exist and the joint law is normalized, but no mixture of
$B$-relative $R$ laws reconstructs the A-relative law. Common parent does
not imply conditional sufficiency. **Affected:** T10, T11, products 10--11.

### FQ5 — equal clock POVM, unequal clock instrument

Use the same projective clock effects for a Lüders instrument and a reset-to-
$|0\rangle$ instrument. First-reading distributions agree for every state,
but repeated readings and clock-controlled system operations differ. A POVM-
only calibration falsely passes. **Affected:** T4, T8, T18.

### FQ6 — one-time equality, different process comb

Let two two-step binary laws have uniform marginals at each step, with
$t_2=t_1$ in one and $t_2=1-t_1$ in the other. One-time clock tomography
cannot distinguish them, while a retained-record equality reader does.
**Affected:** T8 and the complete-context portion of T21.

### FQ7 — null posterior used as a control bit

For a diffuse clock, choose two regular conditional versions differing at one
zero-marginal reading and let an alleged feedback map branch on that exact
value. The branch has no integrated physical support; a singular preparation
would change the packet. **Affected:** T6, T8, T17.

### FQ8 — shared hidden oscillator called two independent clocks

Let A and B be two readout heads measuring deterministic functions of one
unrepresented oscillator/counter. Their subsystem labels differ, but neither
has an independently variable preparation/generator/readout once the hidden
source is exposed. This fails Definition 3.3 rather than proving agreement.
**Affected:** T2--T4.

### FQ9 — dummy interaction

Choose $V=cI$ or choose every admitted state in a common eigenspace on which
$V$ contributes only a global phase. The interaction coefficient is nonzero,
yet removing $V$ changes no registered reader. It cannot earn backreaction.
**Affected:** T19/product 19.

### FQ10 — apparent one-way interaction on a restricted source class

A controlled unitary can change the system for the one prepared clock state
while every admitted system preparation leaves the clock marginal fixed. A
matrix interaction is not evidence of reciprocal response. Interventions on
both clock and system source classes must separate the two directions.
**Affected:** T19/product 19.

### FQ11 — Hamiltonian-rescaling free lunch

Keep clock dimension fixed but replace $H_C$ by $\lambda H_C$ and claim
arbitrarily fine ticks in a fixed laboratory interval as
$\lambda\to\infty$. The energy bandwidth/variance diverges. Dimension alone
cannot define the resource comparison. **Affected:** T18/product 18.

### FQ12 — rare-tick postselection

A filter yields near-perfect readings with success probability $10^{-12}$;
failed runs are discarded. Conditional accuracy is excellent but the
complete clock is not. The success record, failures, energy, and repetition
cost must stay in the joint law. **Affected:** T4, T7, T18.

### FQ13 — external time hidden in clock preparation

The held-out predictor does not accept $s$ explicitly, but each run prepares
$\sigma_C(s)=|s\rangle\langle s|$ using the comparator timer. The source
already contains the answer. This is an externally written time record, not
autonomous elimination, unless a physical upstream controller and its own
clock resources are included. **Affected:** T12, T17, product 22.

### FQ14 — distributional constraint with no physical norm

Let $\widehat C$ have continuous spectrum and write a formal
$\delta(\widehat C)|\psi\rangle$. The kinematic norm diverges and conditional
Born expressions can be arbitrarily renormalized. A rigging map/group average
and physical inner product are load bearing. **Affected:** T12--T14.

### FQ15 — target-adapted factorization

Given the full target history state, choose a tensor factor called “clock”
whose basis labels the desired target evolution. Exact PW conditionals follow
by construction, but another factorization gives different dynamics. This is
formal parametrization/multiple choice, not independent clock physics.
**Affected:** T3, T21, T25.

### FQ16 — entanglement lost in temporal-frame change

Start with entangled A--B--system state. Replace the full parent reduction by
a classical kernel on $(\tau_A,\tau_B)$ and trace out clock coherences. Scalar
reading statistics may agree while a later interference reader fails. A
complete quantum frame transformation must act on states and observables,
not only probabilities. **Affected:** T10--T11.

### FQ17 — periodic clock plus an illicit winding label

Condition a periodic clock on phase $\tau$ and append the mathematical orbit
index as $n$. The pair $(n,\tau)$ is injective, but $n$ was not a physical
observable and need not define a gauge-invariant relational observable. A
physical auxiliary counter creates a different composite clock and pays its
resources. **Affected:** T18, T20.

### FQ18 — postselected Bell/steering calibration

Choose “good clock” runs using a retained outcome correlated with system
settings or source. The selected clock-conditioned data can appear to match a
target while changing measurement-independence/source premises. Complete
contexts must retain the selection record and recompute all premise ledgers.
**Affected:** T4, T7, T8, and inherited Paper-03 Bell scope.

FQ1--FQ4 are false-positive controls that the construction must make
explicit. FQ9--FQ12 make backreaction and finite-resource claims operational.
FQ13--FQ16 separate a genuinely predictive parent from a target-fitted
representation.
