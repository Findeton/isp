## 2. Independent reconstruction of the frozen law

### 2.1 Attempted runs and causal identification

The primitive issued attempt is `(Xi,A,E,Y)`, not a successful-run record.
`Xi=(j,s,rho,ell)` is immutable context; `A=(h,i,f,z)` is assignment; `E` is
literal execution; and `Y=(b,r,n,q)` is physical observation. The comparison
projection removes literal treatment syntax while retaining common execution
coordinates and every future-accessible material trace. Typed sentinels put
every assignment, timeout, noncompliance event, and failure on one measurable
space. The normalized parent law precedes all conditioned secondary analyses.

The primary target is the intention-to-treat law

`Q_{xi,a}=P(Y* in . | Xi=xi, do(A=a))`.

For `ID-R`, physical assignment-source exogeneity, cellwise positivity,
consistency on all assigned attempts, and bounded or blocked carryover are
joint premises. A staged policy is fixed before the attempt and uses
sequential exogeneity and positivity conditional on the complete registered
past at each decision. For `ID-T`, the causal graph/transport model,
intervention map, identification theorem, consistency, interference rule,
overlap or bounded extrapolation, preassignment adjustment, frozen functional,
uncertainty law, and rejectable validation are all explicit premises. Source
closure alone supplies neither route.

The ordered `ID-*` map makes a broken object procedural-invalid, a refuted
claimed assignment invalid, an uncertified route unidentified, and only then
permits randomized or transported identification. Subject to its printed
premises, this part repairs the terminal v2 source-to-do error.

### 2.2 Presentation, matching, screening, and decisions

Exact presentation is a joint pushforward of the whole serial-labelled
experiment. Boundary matching is a TV confidence statement on validation
copies. Screening is a separate TV confidence statement on complete causal
outcomes. The earlier/interior record must also certify positive separation;
duplicate labels do not qualify.

Numerically, both matching and screening put their margins strictly inside the
TV diameter and put positive gaps on each side. Error and power targets lie in
`(0,1/2)`. Matching additionally requires an above-margin calibration anchor.
Local screening uses `U<=epsilon` for pass, `L>epsilon` for fail, and the
straddling case is underdetermined. Candidate pass is the conjunction of all
mandatory local passes, while one established local fail refutes that
conjunction. The simultaneous family includes selection, candidates, duties,
regimes, bins, bracketings, adaptive reporting, and stopping.

The numerical gap intervals are nonempty. Finding M3-01 shows that the
corresponding statistical alternatives need not be.

### M3-03 — Major — an empty seam confidence set makes theorem branches vacuous

- Severity: **Major**.
- Affected claims: `V3-C6` and `V3-C13`.
- Category: mathematical/statistical and semantic.
- Exact evidence: contract §10.2, lines 1125--1176; contract §6.4.

model/confidence set, but the law neither requires that set to be nonempty nor
routes an empty realization to invalid or unresolved status. A confidence

`theta` with probability `1-alpha` and equals a special symbol `bot` with
probability `alpha`. Define the confidence set to be `{theta}` on the first
event and the empty set on `bot`. Its coverage is exactly `1-alpha` for both
parameter values. No procedural identity or separate model-validation test
need fail on the `bot` event.

With at least one mandatory seam input and an empty realized set, all of the
following universal statements are vacuously true: uniform nonexistence,
uniform existence, uniform exterior ambiguity, uniform exterior uniqueness,
uniform internal nonuniqueness, uniform internal uniqueness, uniform budget
exceedance, and uniform budget satisfaction. The priority list returns
`SEAM-NO-SOLUTION`, although the empty set certifies no physical or
mathematical no-solution proposition. `SEAM-UNRESOLVED` is written for a set
that spans cases; the empty set spans none. The frozen rule therefore needs a
semantic premise not present in its bytes and is not finite-resolution safe.
