## 1. Independent core reconstruction

### 1.1 What the gate actually asks

The pin does not postulate a clock law. It asks whether a single operational
packet `P`, frozen after model-neutral family comparison and before any
held-out prediction, can support two derivations of one complete transcript
law:

$$
\mu_e^{\rm con}=F_{\rm con}(P,e),
\qquad
\mu_e^{\rm aut}=F_{\rm aut}(P,e).
$$

The first derivation is constrained and clock neutral; the second uses one
fixed autonomous realization. Equality of these measures is an internal
consistency test. It is not evidence for `P`. Evidence can enter only through
finite, sequestered laboratory data under the independently frozen observation
and decision model.

The root/preparation law is part of the complete measure. Equal conditional
kernels with unequal root laws are unequal physical experiments. Every
adaptive posterior, classical record, controller memory, null result, stopped
sector, leakage event, and resource observation used by a later reader must
therefore be in the common transcript.

The external scalar test is also task relative. It asks whether the complete
predictor factors through an operational quotient that does not consume the
named scalar. It does not remove the supplied experiment identity,
preparation/readout arrangement, adaptive policy, or causal-slot order, and
does not imply chronology or timeless ontology.

### 1.2 Exact reconstruction of the Paper-04 classical-record failure

The accepted Paper-03 classical record at boundary A has hybrid algebra

$$
\mathcal O_A
=\mathcal B_A\,\overline\otimes\ell^\infty(\mathbb Z_7),
\qquad
z_\alpha=I_{\mathcal B_A}\otimes|\alpha\rangle\langle\alpha|.
$$

The `z_alpha` are central. A reversible covariance action on this retained
classical record must preserve the center; in the finite case it acts there by
a state-independent permutation of record sectors.

The rejected fixture instead used

$$
T_g^{R_A}
=\sum_q|q\rangle\langle q|_Q\otimes T_{(1+q)g}^{R_A}.
$$

Hence

$$
T_g^{R_A}z_\alpha(T_g^{R_A})^\dagger
=\sum_q|q\rangle\langle q|_Q\otimes
z_{\alpha+(1+q)g}.
$$

Because the `Q` projectors are not central in the admitted nonrecord quantum
algebra, this is not central when different coherent charge sectors induce
different shifts. On UCOH, the transformation preserves cross-sector
coherence only by producing a coherently controlled quantum pointer. Calling
that pointer a classical record is a type error. Dephasing it restores a
commutative output but destroys the same coherence used by the proposed
complete reader. The three demands—classical A record, coherently
Q-dependent shift, and UCOH coherence—cannot all hold.

This is not repaired by renaming `pointer`, `phase`, `clock memory`, or `POVM
outcome` as `record`. The new pin blocks the evasion in four independent ways:

1. its term firewall binds a clock record to a retained normalized instrument
   output on the accepted Paper-03 boundary;
2. T2 requires symmetry and constraint actions to preserve that output type;
3. A11--A13 explicitly separate a phase POVM, a controller-dependent pointer
   action, and dephasing from a valid retained record; and
4. T17 requires the record and controller, not just the state or scalar label,
   to transform.

The binding finite-record criterion is therefore:

> the covariance action must restrict to a star-automorphism of the admitted
> classical center, equivalently a state-independent permutation of its
> sectors (or of the joint tuple when another retained classical record is a
> legitimate controller). A coherently controlled pointer may be retained as quantum memory

> separately normalized classical instrument is applied and its disturbance
> is retained.

This criterion is already inherited from the accepted boundary; printing it
in the model-comparison pin is required scope, not a change of physics.

### QF1 — central-on-states but not central-as-an-algebra
