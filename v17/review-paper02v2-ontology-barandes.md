## 1. First decisive semantic counterexample

**None.** The v1 equal-arrow defect is absent: physically distinct procedures
are arguments in `P`, and only their operational images are identified in
`Q`. The positive model is not a procedure-tag lookup, and none of its
positive claims constructs or selects a complete ontology.

One bounded logical qualification is required when reading Theorem 5. Mere
physical nonidentity of two channel decompositions does not force their

forgotten randomization between two identity-channel branches are distinct

$$
\delta_{\lambda}(d\lambda').
$$

## 2. Seat O claim-by-claim reconstruction

### O1. Apparatus and system remain distinct

The candidate uses three levels:

```text
presentation of a laboratory protocol
  -> reproducible physical procedure in P
  -> complete operational class in Q.
```

A procedure is a type of repeatable preparation, transformation, instrument,
reader, randomizer, or wiring. It is not an individual execution token. The
presentation groupoid removes internal bound names, harmless drawings, and
serialization of independent vertices. It does not remove calibration,
incidence, retained records, randomizer correlations, boundary type, or a
physical landmark. A physical exchange is an arrow, not presentation gauge.

This construction retains precisely the apparatus context needed to state
generalized contextuality. It does not demand that a complete apparatus
microstate be part of the system ontic state. A scratch on a sealed housing,
an unrecorded thermal microstate, and the serial identity of the run are not
automatically arrows or system beables. If an internal degree is exposed by a
calibrated port or reader, it changes the procedure interface and may refine
the quotient. That is new operational access, not retrospective inspection.

Future choices are likewise not smuggled into a procedure prefix. The
complete procedure may contain the setting actually selected for the run,
but shared physical prefixes must have literally equal marginal laws under
all normalized later suffixes.

### O2. Contextuality applicability is correctly partial

A general adequate packet supplies complete-history laws and readers. It need
not supply a sufficient state at each intermediate boundary. Preparation,
transformation, measurement, and instrument contextuality require common
typed spaces `Lambda_A` plus measures, kernels, and responses. Without that
interface, the factorization equations are not propositions. The paper's
`NOT-APPLICABLE-UNTIL-INTERFACE` disposition is therefore exact, not a way to
evade a failed noncontextuality test.

Adding the standard interface is a substantive ontological assertion: it says
that a boundary variable is sufficient for the registered future and that
procedures act on it through the printed stochastic kernels. Calling a
complete-history label a “state” without these composition rules would not
meet the definition.

Noncontextuality is imposed only on the declared system-facing assignment:

$$
\mu_p=\bar\mu_{q(p)},\qquad
\tau_t=\bar\tau_{q(t)},\qquad
\xi_m=\bar\xi_{q(m)}.
$$

It is not imposed on every fact about two apparatuses whose system behavior
is equal.

### O3. The positive model is genuine, but nonexplanatory

At an ordinary boundary, the candidate ontic space is the set of normalized
quantum predictive states. A direct preparation uses `delta_rho`; a trusted
mixture uses the affine mixture of its component measures; channels push
states forward; measurement responses are Born responses; and instruments
use Born-weighted substochastic update kernels. All quantities are ordinary
nonnegative measures.

The decisive non-tag witness is

$$
\frac12\delta_{|0\rangle\langle0|}
+\frac12\delta_{|1\rangle\langle1|}
\ne
\frac12\delta_{|+\rangle\langle+|}
+\frac12\delta_{|-\rangle\langle-|},
$$

although both barycenters are `I/2`. The supports remain different after all
procedure names are deleted. Born affinity then makes every registered
future probability agree. This is a real positive preparation-contextual
ontological model in the standard interface, not a diagnostic tag.

At a valid finite-memory cut, the corresponding candidate state is the
complete normalized conditional predictive comb, including every required
memory port. CP-instrument contraction and normalization provide the same
lineage. This establishes an existence control only on cuts for which that
complete predictive object is typed; it does not Markovize an arbitrary
whole-history representation.

The physical price is not hidden. The model reifies the quantum predictive
object used by the accepted theory. It may be global, nonlocal, and as
memory-bearing as the complete process. It explains neither why the quantum
calculus is correct nor why this realization rather than another exists. It
is evidence against “positivity is impossible,” not evidence that density
operators are nature's hidden furniture.

The narrow channel qualification in Section 1 must be retained: one obtains
transformation contextuality only where operationally equivalent direct and
randomized procedures induce different stochastic kernels. The displayed
six-rotation construction supplies such a case.

### O4. Structural contextuality is not an idle bit

Contextuality is failure of a family of assignments over `P` to descend
through `q`. It is not a coordinate attached pointwise to one operational
state. The proposed map that sends every decomposition measure to a point
mass at its barycenter cannot be one positive affine Markov kernel while also
fixing each direct point mass:

$$
D_*\delta_\rho=\delta_\rho
\Longrightarrow
D_*\sum_i\alpha_i\delta_{\rho_i}
=\sum_i\alpha_i\delta_{\rho_i},
$$

not `delta_{sum alpha_i rho_i}`. Hence context erasure leaves the admitted
positive affine representation class.

An idle fiber is different. It is extra hidden structure equipped with a
normalized prefix-coherent law and a positive probability-preserving
projection. The projection is generally many-to-one, so it is not an
invertible coordinate gauge. The candidate correctly calls the binary
inflation a nonisomorphic adequate packet only **over the predictive-object
projection**. It does not infer that the bit is real, unreal, or equiprobable
among all possible completions.

Theorem 8 consequently proves only that invariants natural under an actually
admitted idle projection ignore that fiber. It does not manufacture a
universal projection from every ontology to the operational quotient.

### O5. Records, divisions, and actuality remain separated

A record is operational when different typed values are separated by a
calibrated future reader. Such values descend to distinct operational
classes. Persistence of that reader distinction is weaker than predictive
sufficiency. A boundary is a division only if any two histories with the same
complete boundary argument have identical probabilities for every licensed
future.

The candidate therefore permits a durable local record while refusing to
factorize through it when phase, environment, memory, or another law-relevant
degree is missing. It does not repair failed sufficiency by adding a hidden
history identifier after the test.

One quotient-level record history may be declared actual. Multiple
microscopic histories can project to it, and idle-fiber inflation gives an
exact family of such completions. Normalization, conditionalization, and
decoherence determine or stabilize probabilities; none chooses one member of
the microscopic fiber. No collapse or actualization mechanism is hidden in
Theorem 12.

### O6. The Barandes boundary is exact

The candidate does not confuse a positive stochastic representation of
laboratory records with a Barandes ontology. A complete comparison requires
at least a fixed configuration referent (or fully typed configuration
family), contingent standalone probabilities, a complete indivisible
transition-law family across admitted experiments and legitimate divisions,
and one actual configuration trajectory.

The predictive-object model supplies none of those as one complete packet.
It is compatible with such a completion but does not select one. Conversely,
the POM, Peres--Mermin, transformation, and CHSH no-gos exclude only their
printed noncontextual, sharp, measurement-independent, or Bell-factorizable
premise packages. They do not refute indivisible stochastic processes in
general.

### O7. The discriminator ledger is physically honest

Every row distinguishes three things: equality of registered predictions, a
different ontological claim, and the additional access or principle that
could separate the claims. A bit-sensitive reader, fiber preparation,
two-boundary latent access, environment access, matched source/composition
contract, microhistory reader, configuration-sensitive physics, or new
actualization law would enlarge the physics. None is inferred from the old
data.

The paper does not assign a prior over completions. Entropy, description
length, smallest Hilbert carrier, elegance, and usefulness for later gravity
are not accepted selectors. The absence of a discriminator means
underdetermination, not equal plausibility and not impossibility.

### O8. The downstream firewall holds

Sequential notation is declared laboratory control order, not an emergent
time coordinate. Tensor factors are system types, not spatial regions.
Neither record values nor idle ontic coordinates are called spacetime atoms.
Contextuality supplies no metric, and phase completeness does not select
complex amplitude as fundamental substance. The candidate constructs no
clock, indefinite causal order, QFT, dimension, topology, geometry,
curvature, gravity, or cosmology. Paper 03 remains closed pending terminal
adjudication.

## 7. Fresh Seat O attacks

### O-F1. Laboratory token mistaken for a procedure type

Take two executions of one calibrated instrument, with different exact
thermal microstates and different spacetime locations. The candidate assigns
one reproducible procedure type, not two arrows solely because two tokens
occurred. It therefore does not count runs or microdifferences as ontology.
If location or microstate is made an exposed calibrated input, the procedure
type changes prospectively. **Disposition: survives.**

### O-F2. Apparatus microdifference imposed on system noncontextuality

Let two sealed devices differ only by an inaccessible casing scratch while
having identical typed system interfaces. System noncontextuality asks their
system measures/kernels to descend through `q`; it does not assert identical
complete apparatus worlds. **Disposition: survives.**

### O-F3. Whole-history model mislabeled noncontextual

Give a packet only a normalized joint law on complete histories, with no
common intermediate `Lambda_A`, preparation measures, or transformation
kernels. The descent equations are undefined. Theorem 4 returns
`NOT-APPLICABLE-UNTIL-INTERFACE`. **Disposition: survives.**

### O-F4. Predictive object advertised as explanatory hidden reality

The density/comb state already contains the operational predictive content
and the Born rule is used in every response/update. Thus the model cannot
explain the quantum formalism from a smaller physical mechanism. The paper
states exactly this cost and uses it only as an existence control.
**Disposition: survives with the printed nonexplanatory scope.**

### O-F5. Noninvertible projection called coordinate gauge

Project a two-point fiber over each predictive state to its singleton base.
No inverse commuting with the base exists. The candidate calls this an
admitted positive reduction, not an invertible presentation change.
**Disposition: survives.**

### O-F6. Stable record without complete future sufficiency

Choose two histories with the same readable record but different unexposed
environment/phase data, and a licensed coherent continuation that separates
their future distributions. The record is stable yet the displayed
future-sufficiency implication fails; the boundary is not a division. The
paper refuses factorization and does not append a history cache.
**Disposition: survives.**

### O-F7. Positive law without a Barandes referent or trajectory

Theorem 5 gives positive measures and kernels on operational predictive
objects but no single fixed configuration ontology, universal transition-law
family, contingent universal state, or actual configuration trajectory. The
candidate classifies it as admissible evidence only, not Barandes
reconstruction. **Disposition: survives.**

### O-F8. Entropy or minimal dimension as ontology selector

The direct predictive model, an idle-bit inflation, and a continuous idle
fiber have different entropies and carrier sizes while sharing every
registered prediction. No physical measure over models or law penalizing the
extra structure is supplied. The paper refuses both entropy and minimum
dimension as selectors. **Disposition: survives.**

### O-F9. Decomposition nonidentity without kernel nonidentity

Compare a direct identity channel with a forgotten randomization whose two
branches are both the identity channel. The procedures can be distinct in
`P`, but the model kernel is identical. Therefore physical decomposition
nonidentity alone does not entail transformation contextuality. The
candidate's proof contains the correct additional condition—unequal induced
kernels—and the six-rotation example satisfies it. **Disposition: bounded
scope qualification; no product demotion.**
