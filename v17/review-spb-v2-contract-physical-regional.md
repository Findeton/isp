## 3. Findings

### P-R-V2-01 - Farooq gives exact ideal one-contact equality and approximate separate-network agreement

- Severity: none for the v2 ceiling; high if promoted to literal laboratory
  equality or composition.
- Category: physical-source and apparatus.
- Affected claims: `V2-C9`, `V2-C10`, and the `SPB-S6` ceiling.
- Exact evidence: exact `SPB-S6`, Eq. (6), defines general isoscattering by
  conjugacy. Page 3 states that one contact makes the scattering matrix scalar,
  so conjugacy reduces to `S_Gamma2(k)=S_Gamma1(k)`. Eqs. (9)-(11) prove
  `M_Gamma1(k)=M_Gamma2(k)=2k tan(3kl)` for the ideal graphs. Figures 1 and 3
  show different networks connected to the VNA through distinct leads `L1`
  and `L2`, not one transplanted common connector. Page 6 calls the measured
  amplitudes and phases over 0.01-1 GHz "almost perfect agreement". Page 8
  models absorption by a complex wave number and makes data request-only.
- Finding: the ideal scalar functions are literally equal; the physical
  records are close, finite-resolution measurements of separately connected
  networks. This is a valid fixed-one-channel control, not common-hardware
  multiport screening, a complete stochastic-law result, or reconnection.
  V2 Section 9.5 prints this exact distinction.

### P-R-V2-02 - Lawniczak establishes multiport transplantation conjugacy, not fixed-port equality

- Severity: high source-scope boundary; no v2 defect.
- Category: physical-source, apparatus, and semantic.
- Affected claims: `V2-C9`, `V2-C10`, and the `SPB-S4` ceiling.
- Exact evidence: exact `SPB-S4`, Eqs. (5) and (11), states
  `S^(I)=T_4^-1 S^(II) T_4`, not equality. The n=2 experiment uses four-lead
  networks but an Agilent E8364B two-port VNA. Page 5 says six port-pair
  connection configurations were used, with the other two ports terminated by
  50-ohm loads, to obtain full matrices. Figures 3-4 display trace agreement
  and one transformed modulus, not equality of all same-labelled responses.
  The paper reports small discrepancies above 0.55 GHz in the transformed
  component and at 1-1.3 GHz in the trace, and the matrices are request-only.
- Finding: the measured relation is approximate conjugacy under a mathematical
  channel map. Trace, determinant, spectra, pole sets, and transformed `S11`
  do not establish equality for every same-labelled physical excitation and
  phase-sensitive reader. No adapter or direct gluing was built. V2 correctly
  keeps conjugacy outside fixed-port screening unless a physical adapter is
  independently constructed and charged.

### P-R-V2-03 - The printed `T4` cannot be a scalar-normalized passive lossless mixer

- Severity: high apparatus restriction; decisive against the passive-adapter
  mutant.
- Category: physical-source and apparatus.
- Affected claims: `V2-C10` and contract Sections 9.4, 9.5, and 14.3.
- Exact evidence: exact `SPB-S4`, Eq. (15), prints
  `T4=[[1,-1,0,0],[1,0,-1,0],[0,1,0,-1],[0,0,1,1]]`. Independent exact
  multiplication gives
  `T4^dagger T4=[[2,-1,-1,0],[-1,2,0,-1],[-1,0,2,1],[0,-1,1,2]]`,
  with eigenvalues `2-sqrt(2)` twice and `2+sqrt(2)` twice.
- Finding: no nonzero scalar makes `T4` unitary, so software conjugation or a
  scalar-normalized four-channel lossless adapter is impossible. A broader
  implementation may use attenuation, active gain, separate input/output
  networks, ancillary dump ports, or a unitary dilation, but then loss, gain,
  extra ports and loads, inverse-map realization, noise, phase, dynamic range,
  common-plane calibration, and comparator resources are new physical data.
  V2 treats exactly that object as an instrument or enlarged collar.

### P-R-V2-05 - Complete-law claims require noise, reference, saturation, memory, and failure kernels

- Severity: high evaluation and codomain duty; no v2 semantic defect.
- Category: apparatus, stochastic response, and source closure.
- Affected claims: `V2-C9`, `V2-C11`, `V2-C12`, `V2-C14`, and contract
  Sections 7, 9.2, 9.5, 10.3, and 11.
- Exact evidence: both microwave sources measure coherent scattering on lossy
  coaxial networks. Neither publishes noise-wave statistics, receiver state,
  power/intermodulation sweeps, saturation, hysteresis, time-stationarity
  blocks, mode-conversion tests, dropped acquisitions, or failed-calibration
  attempts. Two modules can have identical coherent `S=0` while one emits no
  registered noise and the other emits `+sigma/-sigma` or times out with
  positive probability; their complete attempted-run laws differ.
- Finding: `R-B0` is correctly only a deterministic control. `R-B1` or higher
  needs incident/reflected wave records, emitted/thermal noise-wave laws,
  reader and reference records, drift/calibration covariance, invalid/missing/

  frozen bandwidth and resolution using synchronized receivers, noise-wave
  calibration, controlled terminations/temperatures/powers, and complete
  attempt logging. It is operational completeness, not microscopic
  omniscience. Detection of unmodelled conversion, nonstationarity, saturation,
  hysteresis, or interface memory routes to model-invalid, a higher frozen
  candidate, or underdetermination; it cannot be fitted away after target
  opening.

- Category: apparatus, stochastic compatibility, and target leakage.
- Affected claims: `V2-C11`, `V2-C12`, and contract Sections 7 and 10.3.
- Exact evidence: noise emitted into a seam is reincident on other modules;
  connector mismatch, shared source/receiver clocks, common thermal state, and
  feedback can correlate nominally separate module records. Normalized local
  conditionals alone can be incompatible, as in binary constraints `X=Y` and
  `Y=1-X`, or nonunique, as in `X=Y` and `Y=X`.
- Finding: adding independent output-error bars after a deterministic star
  product is insufficient. The finite-bandwidth state/noise variables,
  connector kernels, shared reference law, cross-seam correlations, boundary

  calibrated without the direct `ABC` target. If they cannot be, full

  be reported. V2's `COMP-*` classifier has exactly these routes and forbids
  target data from selecting or normalizing a joint law.

### P-R-V2-07 - The generalized star-product theorem is a lossless graph control, not lossy-apparatus evidence

- Severity: high source-scope boundary; no defect in the v2 seam calculus.
- Category: physical-source and apparatus.
- Affected claims: `V2-C11`, `V2-C12`, and the `SPB-S5` ceiling.
- Exact evidence: exact `SPB-S5`, Section 2, constructs self-adjoint Laplacians
  on finite graphs and unitary on-shell scattering matrices. Definition 3.1
  states the compatibility condition. Theorem 3.8 extends the generalized
  product to arbitrary unitary inputs and proves unitarity; the immediately
  following equation gives associativity under its port-dimension conditions;
  Theorem 3.9 gives continuity. Theorem 4.1 gives the glued-graph scattering
  matrix at positive energies, including exceptional noncompatible cases.
  The source contains no lossy coax, VNA calibration, additive/emitted noise,
  replugging, failure transcript, or direct laboratory composite.
- Finding: the theorem is an exact conformance control inside its self-adjoint
  unitary domain. Real lossy linear modules may still be composed by their
  actual seam equations where a stable exterior solution exists, but that is
  not evidence supplied by the theorem. V2 makes the deterministic solution a
  nested coordinate, separately classifies stochastic well-posedness, and
  retains the directly measured composite.

### P-R-V2-08 - Global and sequential seam statuses are physically realizable and logically distinct

- Severity: high conditioning and routing duty; no contract defect.
- Category: apparatus and deterministic seam analysis.
- Affected claims: `V2-C11` and contract Sections 10.1-10.3.
- Exact evidence: with `K=I-VS_ss`, a seam exists only when the forcing is in
  `Range(K)`. Internal amplitudes are unique only if `Ker(K)=0`; the exterior
  can nevertheless be unique if `S_es Ker(K)=0`. Taking `K=0`, `S_se=0`, and
  `S_es=0` gives arbitrary internal amplitudes but one exterior response. A
  global block system can be invertible while an intermediate sequential
  Schur pivot is singular, and a nearly singular invertible system can exceed
  a physical uncertainty/dynamic-range budget.
- Finding: each case can occur near resonant feedback networks. The v2 ordered
  classifier separates model validity, existence, exterior uniqueness,
  internal nonuniqueness, conditioning, and regularity; the bracketing ledger
  separately records pivot failures. Undefined intermediate algebra is not a
  second physical network and cannot be called empirical nonassociativity.
  Connector electrical length, mismatch, repeatability, orientation, and
  covariance must enter `V` and the uncertainty propagation.
