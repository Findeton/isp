## 2. Joint finding A — the `Ens` bridge is mathematically sound

Fix an admitted integrated boundary

$$
\mathcal O_D
=\mathcal A_D\,\overline\otimes L^\infty(R_D,\nu_D).
$$

Let $\Lambda_D$ be an admitted ensemble, with record marginal
$\eta_D\ll\nu_D$ and density

$$
h_D=\frac{d\eta_D}{d\nu_D}.
$$

Disintegrate the law over the record and let $\bar\rho_r$ be the strongly
measurable conditional barycenter in $(\mathcal A_D)_*$. The field

$$
g_D(r)=h_D(r)\bar\rho_r
$$

is positive and Bochner integrable, with

$$
\int\lVert g_D(r)\rVert\,\nu_D(dr)=1.
$$

It therefore defines the normal state

$$
\operatorname{Ens}_D(\Lambda_D)(F)
=\int\langle g_D(r),F(r)\rangle\,\nu_D(dr).
$$

If $F_1=F_2$ as $L^\infty$ classes, they agree $\nu_D$-almost everywhere
and hence $\eta_D$-almost everywhere. `Ens` is therefore representative
independent. Positivity and normalization follow pointwise; normality follows
because $g_D$ is a predual $L^1$ field.

This is the construction's mandatory proof route. A symbolic integral, a
reader-by-reader scalar family, or point evaluation followed by integration
does not establish it.

## 3. Joint finding B — sample, state, conditioning, and division separate

The accepted semantics has two layers:

1. `Ev` retains exact standard-Borel sample values and a frozen posterior
   kernel version; and
2. `Heis` acts on hybrid observable algebras paired with normal ensemble
   states through `Ens`.

For a finite or declared atomic record $r$, the central singleton projection
exists and

$$

boundary is a probabilistic division still requires future sufficiency.

For $\nu_D(\{r\})=0$, evaluation at $r$ is not representative independent on
$L^\infty(R_D,\nu_D)$ and is not normal. The exact sample can occur in `Ev`,
but it is not thereby a normal restart state, a division, or an actuality

## 4. Joint finding C — null-ideal preservation is an arrow law

A deterministic record map $f:R_D\to R_E$ induces

$$
f^*:L^\infty(R_E,\nu_E)\to L^\infty(R_D,\nu_D)
$$

only when

$$
f_*\nu_D\ll\nu_E.
$$

For a stochastic record kernel $K$, the corresponding requirement is

$$
\nu_E(N)=0
\Longrightarrow
K(r,N)=0
\quad\text{for }\nu_D\text{-almost every }r.
$$

The second audit makes this stochastic form explicit. It does not amend the
pin: it is already required by normal target landing, measure-class
compatibility, and ensemble closure. Construction must verify it directly
for every continuous instrument rather than checking deterministic maps only.

One exact integrated boundary has one frozen sigma-finite measure class that
dominates every admitted incoming record marginal. Instrument-specific null
ideals do not form one category object. An arbitrary uncountable mutually
singular family is refused rather than fitted after the fact.

The reference class is technical typing data. It is not a prior, state,
selector, cosmological measure, or actuality law.

## 5. Joint finding D — duality begins at ensemble level

For every primitive $p:D\to E$, construction must prove the state identity

$$
\operatorname{Ens}_E(\Lambda_D\operatorname{Ev}(p))
=\operatorname{Ens}_D(\Lambda_D)
\circ\operatorname{Heis}(p)
$$

for every admitted ensemble, equivalently for every complete hybrid reader.
The left side is the fully evaluated iterated integral over input and output
predictive values. It is not an operator-valued shorthand.

The proof may use representatives inside a version-independent integral. It
may not first assign a normal state to each nonatomic input point. Doing so
would recreate the exact v3 counterexample that v3.1 repairs.

If every primitive preserves the admitted ensemble class, the tower property
and contravariant functor law give, by induction,

$$
\operatorname{Ens}_F(\Lambda_D\operatorname{Ev}(p)
\operatorname{Ev}(q))
=\operatorname{Ens}_D(\Lambda_D)
\circ\operatorname{Heis}(p)
\circ\operatorname{Heis}(q),
$$

and similarly for every finite path. No theorem about an infinite record
stream follows without a separate projective-limit or convergence result.

## 6. Joint finding E — continuous feedback survives without point restart

A continuous policy is an almost-everywhere measurable field
$r\mapsto\Phi_r$ whose decomposable action is one normal UCP arrow on the
declared tagged hybrid objects. Construction owes measurability, exact
endpoints, target landing, normality, and class closure.

Two versions agreeing $\nu_D$-almost everywhere yield the same predictions
for every admitted dominated ensemble and every admitted finite continuation.
This is sufficient for exact full-program operational probabilities.

It is not a canonical posterior or restart law for each zero-mass point. A
post-hoc choice of posterior version after observing a desired value is
forbidden. A newly admitted singular preparation changes the boundary class
or schema; it cannot be smuggled into the old theorem.

## 7. Joint finding F — complete memory is the sufficiency firewall

`Ens` is generally many-to-one. That is harmless only when every licensed
future factors through the tagged hybrid boundary.

If two ensembles have the same `Ens` state but a later operation reads an
unexposed decomposition label, preparation name, source correlation,
posterior cache, or process-memory bit, no common Heisenberg arrow can predict
both. The boundary is incomplete.

Construction must expose every future-relevant quantum memory in the quantum
algebra or an exact typed predictive extension, and every future-relevant
classical value in a retained coordinate. Otherwise it must refuse the future.
The test quantifies over every finite continuation, including delayed reads.

This is operational future sufficiency. It is not an actuality theorem and
does not make every complete predictive boundary a Barandes division event.

## 8. Joint finding G — exact and integrated equalities must not be mixed

The frozen `Ev` layer uses exact kernel representatives. The integrated
`Heis`/`Ens` layer identifies allowed almost-everywhere posterior and control
versions in prediction.

An exchange certificate in the pin requires:

1. equality of the complete Heisenberg maps;
2. equality of the complete frozen `Ev` kernels;
3. exact tagged source and target schemas;
4. output-record permutation;
5. source, apparatus, and memory lineage; and
6. measure-class compatibility.

Integrated equality on all admitted ensembles does not silently become exact
kernel equality. A future construction may define an a.e. kernel quotient
only by separately proving composition congruence; v3.1 does not assume one.

All-linearization equality is available only when every reachable co-enabled
incomparable pair has a full certificate in every reachable record context.
This is laboratory schedule independence, not a derived microscopic clock or
spacetime order.
