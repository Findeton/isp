## 8. Cross-phase firewalls

### F1 — no discreteness assumption

Finite models are theorem controls. The physical support may be continuous,
discrete, or hybrid. Any discreteness claim needs an operational or dynamical
selection principle.

### F2 — no selector category error

A universal law need not select the contingent state or actual initial
condition. A separate `Pi_phys` is introduced only if the theory treats
alternative structures as stochastic outcomes and supplies an experiment or
cosmological-law reason for doing so.

### F3 — no hidden global time

Program order, list position, growth index, and source/target syntax are not
physical time. Time claims require clocks, response, and consistency under
reparametrization or alternative scheduling.

### F4 — no geometry by naming

Graph distance, event count, chain length, spectral dimension, and order
dimension are diagnostics. A metric claim requires a declared observation
map, dimension/signature, scale calibration, clocks/radar, continuum control,
and dynamic response.

### F5 — Bell premise ledger

Any proposed completion states parameter independence, outcome independence,
measurement independence, causal ordering, and operational no-signalling
separately. “Local” is forbidden without the ledger.

### F6 — actualization

One actual history may remain a primitive ontological assertion. It is not a
random selector over laws. If an actualization dynamics is proposed, it must
have novel testable content or be classified as empirically equivalent.

### F7 — amplitudes

Complex amplitudes are neither banned nor presumed fundamental. If the
stochastic ontology reconstructs them as a representation, the translation
must be compositional and phase complete. If positive history probability
cannot reproduce quantum composition without an amplitude/decoherence object,
that is a scientific result, not a failure of the programme.

### F8 — implementation ceiling

Rust, Python, symbolic algebra, and proof assistants may check a frozen
mathematical object. Runtime, serialization, mutability, and ownership bugs are
code defects. A change to a law, carrier, probability, experiment, quotient,
or physical interpretation is semantic and requires a new freeze.

  available;

- a proposed successor merely reruns a closed no-go on a renamed fixture.
