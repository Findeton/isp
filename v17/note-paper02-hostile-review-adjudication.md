## 2. Independent rebuild of the first counterexample

### 2.1 The candidate's two incompatible levels

Candidate Section 2.1 defines $\mathcal Q$ to be operationally separated:
two same-typed procedures are equal arrows exactly when every compatible
ancillary continuation and reader gives the same outcome distribution.

Candidate Section 2.2 then declares a representation map

$$
J_R:\mathcal Q\longrightarrow\mathcal S_R.
$$

Because $J_R$ is a function or functor, equality in its source implies equality
of its images.

Candidate Section 4.2 nevertheless asks an inflated representation to store
which of two operationally equivalent preparation procedures was used. That
requires unequal ontic images over one equal arrow of $\mathcal Q$.

### 2.2 Exact parity-preparation witness

Let

$$
P_{\mathrm e}=\frac12(P_{00}+P_{11}),
\qquad
P_{\mathrm o}=\frac12(P_{01}+P_{10}).
$$

Both prepare $I/2$ in the parity-oblivious multiplexing control. Complete
operational equivalence therefore makes

$$
P_{\mathrm e}=P_{\mathrm o}
\quad\text{in }\mathcal Q.
$$

Consequently every well-defined $J_R$ obeys

$$
J_R(P_{\mathrm e})=J_R(P_{\mathrm o}).
$$

But a preparation-contextual completion needs different ontic distributions,
for example

$$
\nu(dz\mid P_{\mathrm e})=\delta_{\mathrm e}(dz),
\qquad
\nu(dz\mid P_{\mathrm o})=\delta_{\mathrm o}(dz).
$$

The two requirements cannot hold in one function on $\mathcal Q$.

The identical defect appears for transformation contextuality: the five
laboratory decompositions are equal as channels in $\mathcal Q$, while a
transformation-contextual ontology must be permitted to represent those
procedures differently.

### 2.3 Why the candidate's qualification does not repair it

The phrase “under a declared preparation interface that forgets the
implementation label” correctly describes the intended physics but does not

procedures are not equal there. If it is erased before $J_R$, the functor
cannot recover it. If $\Gamma_R$ receives it through a second undeclared
argument, the adequate packet is incomplete.

This is the precise difference between:

1. a category $\mathcal P$ of concrete laboratory procedures;
2. an operational quotient functor

   $$

3. an ontological representation defined on $\mathcal P$; and

Paper 02 starts at item 2 and therefore cannot express failure of item 4.

## 3. What survives unchanged

profiles is a typed congruence under every registered continuation. The

The result is not a global inverse on extra native states and not a
microscopic equivalence between two representations. A diagram

### 3.2 Static and dynamical idle fibers

The static bit, explicit countable or standard-Borel product fiber, hold/flip

causal-prefix coherent, and ignored by the fixed reader domain.

### 3.3 Phase-complete predictive residue

must therefore retain phase-complete predictive structure. Exact
realification preserves the encoded experiment while retaining a global
complex structure or an equivalent tensor/source constraint. The experiment
does not select complex numbers as fundamental substance.

### 3.4 Positivity, contextuality, and Bell scope

The parity-oblivious, six-rotation, Peres--Mermin, and CHSH contradictions are
valid standalone no-go witnesses with their printed premises. The sharp
Peres--Mermin route additionally requires the preparation-noncontextuality,
perfect-prediction, and common maximally mixed decomposition bridge, or sharp

These results exclude specified noncontextual or Bell-local completions. They
do not exclude ordinary positive contextual probabilities on registered
records. What fails in the submitted packet is the bridge placing contextual
