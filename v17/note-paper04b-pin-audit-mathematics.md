## 1. Independent mathematical reconstruction

### 1.1 Typed probability object

For every registered experiment `e`, let `X_e` be the complete endpoint
transcript space. On the inherited Paper-03 boundary, `X_e` is finite or
standard Borel. The two routes must return probability measures on the same
typed space:

$$
\mu^{\rm con}_e,\mu^{\rm aut}_e\in\operatorname{Prob}(X_e).
$$

The transcript includes the preparation and experiment tags, physical clock
records, comparator records, controller memory readable later, null/leakage/
failure/stopped/recurrent flags, and registered resource observations. A
coherent quantum memory may occur in the represented noncommutative part of
the boundary; it is not thereby a classical transcript field. A classical
record is the outcome of one complete normalized instrument whose branches
need not individually be trace preserving.

For adaptive experiments, a joint law has the schematic form

$$
\mu_e(dx_0,dx_1,\ldots,dx_n)
=\pi_e(dx_0)\prod_{k=1}^{n}
 K_{e,k}(x_{<k},dx_k),
$$

where `pi_e` is the root/preparation law and the frozen policy is part of `e`.
Equality of all conditional kernels does not imply equality of the joint law
unless the root law also agrees. Kernels are compared on common positive
support, or as versions relative to an explicitly common dominating law.
Null histories receive a frozen refusal or invalid-clock rule; arbitrary
regular-conditional versions at null points cannot seed later predictions.

### 1.2 Complete-law equality

A reader family `R_e` is measure determining only when

$$
\left[\int f\,d\mu=\int f\,d\nu
\quad\text{for every }f\in\mathcal R_e\right]
\Longrightarrow \mu=\nu.
$$

For finite `X_e`, all singleton indicators suffice. For standard-Borel
`X_e`, a countable determining class can be used after a compatible Polish
presentation is frozen. A few moments, selected marginals, or observed
branches are not determining. Exact equality and tolerance agreement are
different claims: tolerance must be a frozen metric or family of reader
seminorms over the complete law.

Thus T7 is a valid consistency gate. It is not independent evidence: if both
routes are faithful derivations from the same parent, equality is a required
commuting diagram.

### 1.3 One-parent criterion

The phrase “one parent” is semantic, not a count of top-level filenames. A
valid parent contains a common root law, physical carrier/domain, primitive
dynamics, instruments, interactions, transcript typing, resources, and
calibration map from which both routes are derived. There must be a
route-independent primitive diagram whose two pushforwards give the route
laws.

A tagged direct sum

$$
P=P_{\rm con}\oplus P_{\rm aut}
$$

with one route reading each summand is still two fitted laws. It fails T1 and
C1 even though both maps are formally restrictions of a single ordered pair.
Shared output tables, parameter names, or likelihood maxima do not repair the
failure.

### 1.4 Physical instrument schema

For a finite classical clock outcome set `K`, a Schrödinger-picture
instrument is a family of completely positive maps
`{I_k}` satisfying

$$
\sum_{k\in K}\operatorname{tr}\mathcal I_k(\rho)
=\operatorname{tr}\rho.
$$

The complete retained-record channel writes `k` into an orthogonal classical
register. Each branch is generally trace decreasing. A coherent fork instead
retains a quantum branch memory and may support off-diagonal readers. The two
output types may be compared only through actual CP maps and a common typed
reader interface. Dephasing a memory needed by a coherent reader changes the
experiment; calling a coherent pointer a classical record repeats the exact
Paper-04 failure.

In infinite dimension, normality, domains, measurability, and a named
instrument theorem are additionally required. The pin's finite coherent/
diagonal schema is therefore a discriminator template, not a general theorem.

### 1.5 Statistical experiment

Let `theta` denote all parameters learned from training/calibration data and
let `D_hold` denote sequestered observations. Before any held-out prediction,
Stage C must freeze `theta`, the joint observation likelihood, trial
dependence, covariance, nuisance treatment, missing/null mechanism, stopping
rule, multiplicity family, equivalence margin, decision rule, and validity
domain.

An empirical L4 pass can mean only that the common prediction survives the
registered finite-data adequacy or equivalence criterion at its declared
power and error rates. It cannot prove exact equality with an unknown physical
law. Failure to reject is not confirmation unless the procedure has a frozen
equivalence region and adequate power. “Overidentified” means the rank of the
independent held-out constraints exceeds the number of parameters estimated
before holdout; raw observable count is insufficient when covariance is rank
deficient.

### 1.6 External-scalar factorization

Let `U` be the raw task specification containing supplied context and the
candidate external scalar `lambda`. Before held-out predictions, freeze a
measurable operational equivalence relation `~op` and quotient

$$
q:\mathsf U\longrightarrow\mathsf Z=\mathsf U/{\sim_{\rm op}}.
$$

The quotient may use physically specified preparations, policies, slots,
clock-record protocols, and registered resource semantics. It may not be
defined from the candidate's desired output distribution, held-out data, a
lookup table, or a hidden schedule. It must be nontrivial: at least one pair
of specifications with distinct `lambda` values must map to one operational

Task-relative redundancy is the existence of a measurable predictor

$$
\mathcal M_P=\overline{\mathcal M}_P\circ q

on the frozen domain, with the root law, controller, random source,

`q(u)=M_P(u)` would make every predictor factor through its image and is

### 1.7 Universal controls

For standard-Borel experiment and transcript spaces, a jointly measurable
Markov kernel admits a randomization representation

$$
X=f(e,U),\qquad U\sim\operatorname{Unif}[0,1].
$$

For a finite registered family this is just inverse-transform sampling of the

Likewise, finite-dimensional channels, instruments, and finite combs admit

predictions with certified approximation error, resource accounting, and a
legitimate sequestered-data route. If no family meets those conditions, the
correct result is a feasibility obstruction or lower-rung salvage, not a
