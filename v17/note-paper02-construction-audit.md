## 5. Key anti-overclaim checks

### 5.1 Quotient scope

The natural isomorphism is proved only on the reachable operational image.
Extra native states and operations of an adequate representation need not be
globally inverted. Distinct boundary types are never merged by scalar
coincidence.

### 5.2 Fiber maps are not all gauge

Invertible packet relabellings, minimal dilation freedom, noninvertible
forgetting, contingent unobserved structure, and future-readable physical
differences are separate cases. Preparation-correlated latent data are used
only under an interface that forgets the implementation label.

### 5.3 No-selection is not an ontology theorem

The factorization result quantifies over invariants natural under a declared
representation category containing the reductions. It proves
nonidentifiability in that category, not that nature contains only the
quotient or that every completion is equally plausible.

### 5.4 Complex versus real scope

The realification matrix, normalization, trace equality, global complex
structure $J$, carrier doubling, and tensor mismatch are printed. Natural
real theory with product-state independence is not conflated with real
simulation under operational independence.

### 5.5 Positivity versus noncontextuality

Preparation contextuality is reconstructed by the two-bit parity-oblivious
$3/4$ bound. Transformation contextuality is reconstructed by five convex
decompositions of one qubit channel and a pointwise even-integer
contradiction. Peres--Mermin and CHSH retain all their extra premises.
Positive contextual probabilities on actual records therefore survive.

### 5.6 Resource minima

An early draft incorrectly grouped operational Choi rank with
representation-variant realized micro-dimension. The delivered manuscript
corrects this before freeze: decoded minima are quotient invariants inside
their class, while the claim that every adequate ontology literally has that
size is defeated by fiber inflation.

### 5.7 Actuality and downstream scope

An actual record is not a complete microscopic trajectory. Normalization and
decoherence are not actualization. Laboratory slot order is not emergent time,
a tensor factor is not space, and no discrete fiber is used as a spacetime
seed.
