## 2. Independent reconstruction of the category counterexample

The candidate's boundary type is

$$
(M,\mathcal A,\mathfrak S,\text{open probe ports},
 \text{classical records},\text{reader domain}).
$$

It contains no executed causal down-set, causal frontier, support cut, or
other datum distinguishing an earlier from a later boundary on the same
spacetime.

Choose one admitted comparator and one boundary value $B$ with no open probe
port and no retained outcome. Let $K_-$ and $K_+$ be compact timelike-related
coupling regions with $K_-\prec K_+$. Perform the localized probe coupling in
$K_-$, complete its instrument, discard its outcome, and expose no new port or
record. This is typed by the candidate as

$$
F_-:B\longrightarrow B.
$$

The analogous forgotten-outcome coupling in $K_+$ is also typed as

$$
F_+:B\longrightarrow B.
$$

In an ordinary category, every pair with matching codomain and domain has a
composite. Therefore both

$$
F_+\circ F_-
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
F_-\circ F_+
$$

must exist as category morphisms. The candidate instead says that composition
also requires an external causal-wiring predicate and that the reversed
timelike order may be inadmissible. The composition law is therefore partial
beyond the printed object typing.

This is not cured by calling the forbidden word physically impossible. That
physical fact must appear in the source and target types, or the referent must
be declared to be a partial category, causal operad, event structure, or other
nonordinary process object. As printed, $\mathcal P_{\rm rel}$ is not the
claimed category.

Consequently the following do not follow as written:

1. the ordinary quotient category $\mathcal Q_{\rm rel}$;
2. categorical congruence under every constructor;
3. the ordinary universal factorization property; and
4. every result coordinate whose proof requires that quotient category.

The defect is semantic because it changes the mathematical referent and the
theorem package. It does not change the AQFT instrument maps themselves.

## 3. Independent reconstruction of the frame counterexample

Local covariance compares jointly transported physical data. If $\beta_v$ is
the automorphism induced by a Lorentz boost, covariance gives

$$
(\beta_v^*\omega)(A)=\omega(\beta_v(A)).
$$

It does not give

$$
\omega(A)=\omega(\beta_v(A))
$$

for one fixed state $\omega$.

The admitted packet does not require every contingent state to be boost
invariant. For a noninvariant locally normal state—physically, a thermal/KMS
state with a thermal four-velocity is the standard example—there is some
registered local observable or probe effect $A$ for which the two expectation
values differ. The state or material apparatus can therefore define a
detectable rest frame.

What the construction actually proves is narrower and useful:

1. coordinate changes with all physical data transported do not change the
   prediction;
2. causally disjoint operations have no dependence on an arbitrary linear
   serialization when their full maps factorize; and
3. no undeclared scheduling foliation enters that registered law.

It does not prove Lorentz invariance of every state/apparatus and does not
exclude empirically idle preferred microscopic structure.

## 4. Measurement and probability core

The central quantum-operational mathematics survives direct reconstruction.
For one localized system--probe scheme,

$$
\mathcal J_{s,B}(A)
=(\operatorname{id}\otimes\sigma)\Theta(A\otimes B)
$$

is CP for positive $B$ under the packet's fixed $C^*$ tensor convention.
In a represented $W^*$ packet, normality of the scattering map, slice, and
registered operations must be an admission hypothesis.

For a complete finite POVM,

$$
\sum_r\mathcal J_{s,B_r}=\mathcal J_{s,1},
\qquad
\mathcal J_{s,1}(1)=1.
$$

Thus probabilities normalize, zero-support branches remain zero, and no
posterior is formed at zero support. Standard-Borel outcomes require a
countably additive measurable instrument/history kernel and only
almost-everywhere regular conditioning.

For causally factorizing localized schemes, physical chronological order is
represented by the corresponding Heisenberg composition. Causally disjoint
complete maps agree in either order; adjacent swaps then prove equality for
all finite linear extensions of the same causal partial order.

The remote nonselective marginal uses operation locality:

$$
\sum_a p(a,b\mid\omega)
=\omega(\mathcal J_{A,1}(D_b))
=\omega(D_b).
$$

This is stronger than a commutator slogan and weaker than Bell factorization.
Selective steering may change a conditional subensemble, but requires the
retained outcome record for comparison and supplies no controllable
superluminal signal.

The Summers--Werner existence theorem and exact split-qubit CHSH arithmetic
remain separate. The result is Bell compatibility, not universal construction
of an exact localized probe for every ideal Bell observable.

## 6. Covariance and localization qualifications

The elementary variance statement is correct:

$$
(\psi^*\nu)(A)=\nu(\mathcal A(\psi)A).
$$

constructed. A successor must not use one implicitly.

probe state, effects, record/control tree, readers, and history maps all need

is transported. Claims over proper embeddings require separately typed packet
morphisms and cannot be inferred from one scalar naturality identity.

The localization theorem also owes the exact Haag/local-comparison hypothesis
or a named substitute. Independent multi-probe causal factorization owes the
product-source premise. These are theorem hypotheses, not optional prose.

## 9. Verdict

Paper 03 as delivered is **REVISE** and terminal at rung 4. The failure is not
that relativistic quantum measurement is incoherent. The failure is that the
paper placed a genuine causal admissibility condition outside the source and
target types while still claiming an ordinary category and quotient.

The surviving physical result is substantial but conditional: on a declared
AQFT comparator with admitted localized measurement schemes, the theory
supports positive normalized instruments, causal factorization, equality of
spacelike schedules, nonselective no-signalling, selective steering with
record cost, Bell compatibility, and positive finite-program histories.

It does not yet supply a correctly typed relativistic procedure quotient, a
full covariant family of experiment packets, an operationally frame-free
state sector, a local microontology, actualization, emergent spacetime, or
gravity.
