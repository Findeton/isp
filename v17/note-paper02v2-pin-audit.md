## 4. The repaired architecture is well typed

### 4.1 Three levels, not two disguised as one

The construction has three distinct levels:

```text
physical-procedure presentations
        -> physical procedures P
        -> operational equivalence classes Q.
```

The first arrow removes harmless names and serializations through a
presentation groupoid. The second arrow is the quotient functor

$$
q:\mathcal P\longrightarrow\mathcal Q.
$$

This is the essential repair. A system-facing ontological assignment is
defined on $\mathcal P$. It is noncontextual precisely when it descends
through $q$. It may therefore distinguish $p$ and $p'$ when
$q(p)=q(p')$ without asking a function on $\mathcal Q$ to distinguish one
argument from itself.

### 4.2 Physical procedures are not microscopic states

The pin defines a procedure as a reproducible typed laboratory instruction,
not an individual token event and not the complete microstate of the device.
It also refuses source strings, function names, evaluator flags, and
unperformed future choices. Consequently $\mathcal P$ is neither software
syntax nor a hidden ontology smuggled in under the word apparatus.

The construction still owes an explicit finite registered presentation of
$\mathcal P$. The safe route is a small colored symmetric-monoidal procedure
category generated by the declared preparations, transformations,
instruments, measurements, ancillas, classical records, discard maps, and
adaptive wirings, modulo only typed category equations and the separately
declared presentation groupoid.

### 4.3 Complete operational equivalence is a congruence

For same-typed arrows, $p\sim_{\rm op}p'$ means that every compatible
prefix, ancilla, adaptive continuation, and complete reader yields equal
outcome laws. Because the tester family contains the closure of each
registered context under composition, substitution of equivalent arrows into
any larger registered context preserves every scalar probability. This is
the standard contextual-equivalence argument and makes $\sim_{\rm op}$ a
typed monoidal congruence.

The construction must prove each closure separately, including retained
classical records, forgotten outcomes, entangled ancillas, and discard. It
may not assert congruence merely because the word quotient was used.

The quotient's universal property is feasible: any typed prediction functor
from $\mathcal P$ that is constant on complete operational-equivalence
classes factors uniquely through $q$ on the reachable image. Measurable
versions remain restricted to packets with measurable decoders and complete
