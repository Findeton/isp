public packet and let $R_e$ be the complete registered record interface. An
entrant $i$ may carry a native prediction object $X_i$ of a different type

$$
\mathcal O_i(b,c)
=
\kappa_i\!\left(\lambda_i(X_i;b,c)\right)
\in\Delta(R_e)
\tag{3}
$$

for each licensed control context $(b,c)$.

Equation (3) is a family of contextual registered distributions. It is not a
single global joint distribution over incompatible interventions.

### 3.1 Ordinary-positive entrant

An ordinary-positive law with carrier $\Omega_i$, measure $P_i$, and physical
record map $\rho_i$ supplies the pushforward

$$
p_i(r\mid b,c)
=P_i\!\left(\rho_i^{-1}(r)\mid b,c\right).
\tag{4}

No Hilbert or pair-history ontology is imposed.
