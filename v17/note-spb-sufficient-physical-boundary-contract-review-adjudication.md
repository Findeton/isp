two selected architectures are feasible at bounded effective scope, its
source ceilings are honest, and its direct memory and gluing controls would

   five-duty vector calls logically separate.

The terminal decision is therefore

$$
\boxed{\text{SPB-D2 — REVISE BEFORE PHYSICAL PIN}.}
\tag{1}
$$

estimand or a codomain mismatch. One independently reproduced semantic

question deserves a corrected contract, not that nature lacks or possesses a

## 2. Root reconstruction of load-bearing mathematics

### 2.1 A screening estimand needs separate assignment and outcome types

The contract defines the complete attempted-run record as

$$
\omega=(j,p,c,b,f,r,n,q),
\tag{2}
$$

where `p` is the history/interior preparation, `c` contains commands and
seeds, and `f` is the future policy. It then compares laws

$$
P_{h,i,f}(d\omega_F),\qquad P_{h',i,f}(d\omega_F),
\tag{3}
$$

without defining the projection from the complete attempt to `omega_F`.

If the complete transcript in the total-variation comparison includes `p`,
then for distinct `h` and `h'` the event

$$
A_h=\{\omega:p=h\}
$$

has probabilities one and zero. Hence

$$
d_{\rm TV}(P_{h,i,f},P_{h',i,f})=1,
\tag{4}
$$

even when every post-boundary physical record is identical. Every nontrivial
screening pair fails by construction.

If `p,c,f` are instead projected out, the frozen bytes do not say which
boundary outcomes, pre-cut records, nuisance coordinates, and failure states
remain. Projecting out `b`, for example, can falsely screen two histories whose
instrument-outcome laws differ but whose later reader marginals agree.

The same ambiguity affects causal meaning. Recording an assignment does not
turn an observational conditional into an intervention. A valid successor
must type at least:

1. an assignment object `A` containing apparatus, history, instrument,
   future policy, commands, and assigned seeds;
2. a common measurable outcome `Y` containing the retained boundary outcome,
   future reader transcript, standardized nuisance record, and every quality
   or failure state relevant to the claim; and
3. the implemented kernel
   $Q(dY\mid\operatorname{do}(A=a))$, including noncompliance and missing
   attempts.

This reproduces M-1 and is blocking.

### 2.2 Equivalence needs pass, fail, and indeterminate regions

The positive rule

$$
U_{1-\alpha}[d]\leq\epsilon
\tag{5}
$$

is a valid sufficient condition for equivalence. It does not classify every
nonpass. If a simultaneous confidence interval is

$$
[L,U]=[0.02,0.20],\qquad\epsilon=0.10,
$$

the data establish neither equivalence nor non-equivalence. Routing this case
to no-boundary outcomes manufactures a negative; routing it to equivalence
commits failure-to-reject promotion.

The contract names underdetermination outcomes but contains no binding routing
rule. A successor must separate:

```text
PASS             U <= epsilon_science
FAIL             L >  epsilon_science
UNDERDETERMINED  otherwise
```

or another predeclared three-way rule with equivalent coverage. The
scientifically negligible effect scale must be distinct from uncertainty in
preparation, readout, and drift; a poor reader may not enlarge the acceptable
physical effect. Multiplicity must include validation-based pair selection,
the candidate lattice, all pairs/instruments/policies, composition targets,
frequency bins, and bracketings claimed jointly.

This reproduces M-2 and is blocking.

### 2.3 Pairwise agreement cannot replace direct triple composition

The direct `ABC` requirement is necessary. Let `P_iid` be the uniform law on
all eight triples of bits and `P_even` the uniform law on the four even-parity
triples. Their one- and two-bit marginals coincide, but

$$
d_{\rm TV}(P_{\rm iid},P_{\rm even})=\frac12.
\tag{6}
$$

Thus all pairwise segment checks can pass while the triple law fails. The
contract's direct three-part run and pairwise-good/triple-bad mutant survive
review.

### 2.4 A deterministic scattering relation is not a complete-attempt law

For a regional module, a matrix `S(nu)` predicts a deterministic coherent
input--output relation inside the validated linear regime. The common contract
instead requires

$$
\widehat P_{ABC}=\mathsf{Comp}(B_A,B_B,B_C)
\tag{7}
$$

normalized over the complete attempt space, including reader noise, nuisance
records, aborts, dropped attempts, invalid states, and hardware failures.

Take two modules with the same coherent relation `S=0`. In one, the registered
noise wave is always zero. In the other, it is `+sigma` or `-sigma` with equal
probability. Their `S` matrices and every matrix star product agree, but their
complete reader laws have positive total-variation distance. The same example
can use equal `S` and different timeout probabilities.

The contract says the module matrices and connector calibration are the only
regional prediction inputs while elsewhere requiring a stochastic nuisance
parent. Both statements cannot supply equation (7). Either:

1. the scored regional claim is explicitly restricted to a deterministic
   coherent-response estimand with a separately typed measurement kernel; or
2. the boundary and composition objects include stochastic noise-wave,
   reader, nuisance, failure, and seam-correlation kernels.

This reproduces M-3 and the physical seat's P-R-05/P-J-03 scope warning. It is
blocking.

### 2.5 Singular internal seams have three distinct cases

Writing the regional seam equations as

$$
b_e=S_{ee}a_e+S_{es}a_s,\qquad
b_s=S_{se}a_e+S_{ss}a_s,\qquad a_s=Vb_s,
$$

gives

$$
K a_s=V S_{se}a_e,\qquad K=I-VS_{ss}.
\tag{8}
$$

For every exterior input:

1. a seam solution exists only when
   $\operatorname{Ran}(VS_{se})\subseteq\operatorname{Ran}(K)$;
2. the internal seam amplitude is unique only when
   $\ker K=\{0\}$; and
3. the exterior response may remain unique despite an internal ambiguity when
   $S_{es}\ker K=\{0\}$.

For example, `K=0`, `S_se=0`, and `S_es=0` permit arbitrary internal seam
amplitude but a unique exterior response. Conversely, a singular system can be
inconsistent, while a near-singular invertible system can amplify uncertainty
beyond usefulness.

A global three-module seam matrix can also be invertible while one sequential
Schur pivot is singular. A successor must distinguish existence, internal
uniqueness, exterior uniqueness, conditioning, global solve, and each
sequential bracketing. This reproduces M-4 and is major.

### 2.6 Presentation symmetry and empirical closeness are different relations

The contract uses one symbol `sim_B` both for a presentation quotient and for
empirically certified boundary equivalence. Exact passive relabelling may be
an equivalence relation. Threshold closeness usually is not: for boundary
coordinates

$$
0,\quad 0.75\delta,\quad 1.5\delta,
$$

the first is close to the second and the second to the third, but the first is
not close to the third.

A successor must type separately:

1. a predeclared exact presentation groupoid/quotient;
2. a statistical matching relation with its own distance, tolerance,
   calibration uncertainty, and simultaneous selection coverage; and
3. the held-out screening relation on the common outcome space.

This reproduces M-5 and is major.
