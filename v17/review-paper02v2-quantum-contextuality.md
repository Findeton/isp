### 3.2 Channels and randomized channels

A deterministic channel has kernel

$$
\tau_{\mathcal E}(d\lambda'\mid\lambda)
=\delta_{\mathcal E(\lambda)}(d\lambda').
$$

A trusted mixture uses the convex mixture of the component kernels. Both
decode to the averaged channel because every later quantum probability is
affine in the propagated density operator. The kernels need not be equal.

$$

$$

### 3.3 Instruments, zero support, and telescoping

$$
