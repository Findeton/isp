## 2. Independent reconstruction of the ontology argument

### 2.1 The four objects that must remain distinct

My reconstruction of the package is the following typed chain:

$$
B_e
\xrightarrow{\mathcal N_{\rm eff}}
\mathfrak R_e
\xrightarrow{\mathsf{Read}_e}
P_{{\rm rec},e}
\xrightarrow{\mathsf{Act}}
\text{an actuality claim}.
$$

The four coordinates do different work:

1. $B_e$ contains contingent physical boundary, preparation, material,
   reference, apparatus, and control facts.
2. $\mathcal N_{\rm eff}$ is the supplied fixed-background predictive
   nomology. In R1 it includes Maxwell propagation, macroscopic QED, quantum
   composition, a boundary quantum state, and the Born/instrument rule.
3. $\mathfrak R_e$ is a predictive relational carrier. Depending on
   presentation, it may be Hilbert/operator, process-tensor, Gram,
   pair-history, real-$J$, or equivalent quantum data.
4. $P_{{\rm rec},e}$ is an ordinary-positive law on licensed complete retained
   records after a physical reader and record partition have been supplied.
5. $\mathsf{Act}$ is an additional statement about what exists or happens in
   one run. A normalized record law does not logically provide it.

The lead prints all three nonimplications needed here at lines 143--150:
$P_{\rm rec}\not\Rightarrow\mathfrak R$,
$\mathfrak R\not\Rightarrow$ material ontology, and
$P_{\rm rec}\not\Rightarrow\mathsf{Act}$. This hierarchy is not decorative;
it is respected by the direct dependencies.

### 2.2 Record-chain factorization is not a Barandes configuration law

For any finite prefix-autonomous record distribution, conditionalization on
positive-probability stable prefixes produces normalized positive kernels.
That is a probability identity on an operational record quotient. It neither
identifies microscopic physical configurations nor supplies a physical
transition map among them. The complete prefix can carry the entire relevant
past and need not be a reusable material state.

Barandes's object is different. In the exact sources it begins with a
configuration space and first-order transition probabilities at licensed
conditioning/division events. The configuration space is proposed as
kinematical/ontological content; the standalone probability distribution is
contingent; and the transition map is nomological. The frozen package calls
the record-chain kernel only a “Barandes-style” stable-boundary shadow and
then explicitly denies that the shadow generates a Barandes physical law.

Thus the existence of a positive stable-record $\Gamma$ does not select
ordinary-positive microscopic ontology. Equally, it does not refute such an
ontology.

### 2.3 Barandes representation does not reverse the physical source arrow

The exact Barandes sources support a strong representation statement and an
ontology proposal, but not the source-completion statement sought by R1:

- `2302.10778v3` starts with a time-dependent stochastic matrix and gives a
  Hilbert-space dictionary. It explicitly leaves higher-order and
  whole-trajectory probabilities unspecified and asks whether a further
  selection principle exists.
- `2309.03085v2`, theorem 69, starts with a **given** finite indivisible
  stochastic process and embeds it as a subsystem of a unistochastic process.
  Its proof begins from the supplied transition probabilities and constructs
  a Hilbert representation.
- `2608.04354v1`, the Born Representation and Unistochastic Theorems, starts
  with an arbitrary supplied stochastic matrix and constructs POVM/PVM or
  unistochastic dilations.
- `2507.21192v1` develops the proposed configuration ontology and demotes
  Hilbert variables to secondary, gauge-dependent tools. It still chooses a
  configuration space, transition law, and contingent standalone state as
  axiomatic model ingredients. It does not map independently described
  materials, boundaries, controls, and readers to the unique physical
  cross-context $\Gamma$ family.

Consequently, the stochastic--quantum theorem establishes representation
capacity after $\Gamma$ is present. It does not establish

$$
\text{physical descriptors}\longrightarrow \Gamma.
$$

The frozen package states exactly this limitation and does not misdescribe it
as a refutation of Barandes.

### 2.4 The Barandes actuality nuance

Barandes uses strong ontological language: configurations are what systems
physically are, and `2507.21192v1` says that from a more fundamental
perspective a system ultimately takes one trajectory. At the same time, the
published first-order indivisible law intentionally does not specify a unique
Kolmogorov tower or probability measure on complete trajectories; compatible
non-Markovian realizers are left underdetermined and potentially contingent.

Those are not contradictory claims, but they are different claims:

1. an **ontic assertion** that one configuration/trajectory is actual; and
2. a **complete predictive stochastic law** for multi-time or whole-trajectory
   propositions.

The bundle correctly says that the first-order $\Gamma$ does not supply the
second. It also correctly avoids turning that incompleteness into a theorem
that Barandes lacks an ontology. This distinction is essential to C10 and C11.

### 2.5 Hilbert, real-$J$, Gram, Gaussian, and pair-history status

The package uses several equivalent or enlarged predictive carriers, but
does not infer ontology from their syntax:

- A complex Hilbert representation and a real representation with preserved
  complex structure $J$ carry the same relational orientation. Realification
  does not delete the information or make it ontic.
- A Gram/pair-history kernel can represent finite quantum instruments when
  the process vectors are supplied. Its diagonal gives licensed record
  probabilities, while its off-diagonal orientation controls composition.
- A positive Gaussian lift can reproduce the same record marginal, but only
  by receiving the target kernel as covariance and retaining a norm-square
  response. This relocates rather than derives the relational payload.
- A process tensor or Schwinger--Keldysh doubled history is predictive
  nomology, not an ordinary probability on one actual fine history.

The strongest positive statement earned is representation/compositional
adequacy. No minimality, material-source, actuality, or fundamental-ontology
theorem follows. The lead explicitly keeps the ordinary-positive ontology,
relational-predictive-law, and quantum-plus-actualization hypotheses open.

### 2.6 What the pair-history witness proves

The $D_+$/$D_-$ witness holds the isolated diagonal event law fixed and changes
one relational orientation. Under a fixed admitted reference, the composed
event values differ, $3/8$ versus $5/8$. Therefore no composition map whose
only input is the isolated diagonal record law can recover both outputs.

This establishes **nonidentifiability from the diagonal shadow**. It does not
establish:

- that complex numbers are material substance;
- that pair histories are actual;
- that the particular Gram presentation is unique or minimal;
- that an ordinary-positive contextual source law is impossible; or
- that Hilbert space is fundamental.

The frozen lead states the result at precisely this level.

### 2.7 Why the positive-ontology question remains open

The fixed-route monotonicity result defeats only a restricted construction:
opening a route by adding disjoint alternatives while retaining all old
nonnegative weights and record memberships cannot reduce the old record's
odds against an unchanged comparator. The package prints its escape premises:
contextual reweighting from one physical parent, changed history identity,
nonexclusive fine histories, or relational structure before record sampling.

The later positive circle/cosine construction is also used correctly. It
shows that a contextual positive configuration plus a supplied reader can
produce a scalar fringe, defeating any blanket claim that positive ontology
cannot interfere. Because the response law is supplied and does not complete
mixed, adaptive, Bell, or material-source cases, it is a comparator rather
than a native derivation.

Hence C11 is an open constructive burden, not a universal no-go.

### 2.8 Operational reconstruction is conditional reconstruction

The two exact Chiribella--D'Ariano--Perinotti sources support the bundle's
classification:

- `0904.4483v2` supplies a framework of quantum states, measurements,
  channels, quantum networks, input/output Hilbert spaces, Choi operators, and
  link-product composition. It analyzes admissible transformations **within
  quantum mechanics**; it is not a material source of that framework.
- `1011.6451v3` begins with the operational-probabilistic circuit framework
  and adopts causality, perfect distinguishability, ideal compression, local
  distinguishability, pure conditioning, and purification. It then derives
  finite-dimensional complex quantum theory within that substrate.

Those are substantive axioms/postulates about systems, tests, circuit order,
composition, distinguishability, compression, purity, and reversible
completion. A latent positive refinement with the same operational quotient
inherits every principle stated only on that quotient. Therefore the
reconstruction selects an operational predictive theory conditional on its
premises; it does not select a microscopic ontology or generate its own
physical intervention/reader bridge.

The frozen package classifies these premises as imported/postulated rather
than outputs of a passive configuration law.

### 2.9 Bell, contextuality, and preparation premises are charged

The package does not use “non-Markovian” or “indivisible” as a substitute for
Bell analysis. It states that a positive model reproducing Bell-violating
records must explicitly locate the changed premise: nonfactorizable
common-parent dependence, retrodependent/two-boundary structure, measurement
dependence with a source account, incompleteness of the screening variable,
or another precise alternative. Operational no-signalling is kept distinct
from Bell-local factorization and relativistic microcausality.

Likewise:

- preparation, transformation, and measurement noncontextuality are charged
  under the Spekkens premises;
- PBR is restricted to models satisfying preparation independence;
- failure of preparation independence is not treated as a free hidden common
  source;
- the PR counterfamily is openly contextual/non-Bell-local and is not called
  a local hidden-variable model; and
- a context-indexed answer table is not accepted as a physical contextual
  law without a compact source map.

This satisfies the seventh Seat O duty.

### 2.10 Calculation labels, records, and actual referents

The direct dependencies carefully distinguish:

1. path, Kraus, absorption, quasiparticle, hotspot, waveform, and other
   calculation labels;
2. fine histories used to resolve a predictive functional;
3. physically licensed stable records supported by amplification,
   persistence, readout, and a declared future grammar; and
4. an actual microscopic referent or one-run actuality law.

Naming or Markovizing an intermediate coordinate does not make it an actual
happening or division. Decoherence and redundancy support record stability
and operational objectivity but do not select one outcome. The R1 member
explicitly lists “one stable record ... is what actually happens” among its
inputs and later calls it an actuality postulate.

The only wording that needs disciplined scope is discussed in finding O-01.

### 2.11 Background time, spacetime, QFT, and gravity debt

R1 is a fixed-background effective construction. It receives laboratory
causal order, geometry, localization, time/frequency, subsystem boundaries,
quantum/QFT composition, a boundary state, and detector interfaces. Circuit
order is not derived chronology. Optical modes are not fundamental
discreteness. A finite history control is not emergent spacetime.

No frozen object supplies a matter--geometry parent, identifies what gravity
couples to before records form, derives the GR limit, or adjudicates classical,
quantum, or hybrid gravity. The lead's declared-versus-derived table and
gravity wall keep every one of these debts visible. C12 is correctly negative.
