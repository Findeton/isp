## 4. Frozen claim vector

### C1 — SPB asks for a physical interface, not a predictive representation

A process tensor, wavefunction, density matrix, scattering matrix, complete
history, or target-trained predictive quotient can represent future records.
It receives no physical-boundary credit unless its carrier, preparation,
interventions, readers, equivalence relation, and resource costs descend
independently of the held-out target.

### C2 — five duties are distinct

Every candidate is tested separately for target-independent descent,
complete-record screening, target-blind composition, implemented
intervention/readout, and no-refit transfer. No one duty implies the others.

### C3 — temporal and regional sufficiency are not interchangeable

`SPB-T` concerns a temporal causal break and memory carrier. `SPB-R` concerns
regional substitution and gluing. Each receives its own rung vector; a result
cannot be averaged or transferred between them.

### C4 — the sample space contains every attempted run

The scored parent includes apparatus identity, preparation, controller
commands and seeds, boundary operation and outcome, held-out policy, reader
record, nuisance/environment record, and failure/quality state. Successful
postselection is never the sole normalized law.

### C5 — screening requires interventional equivalence

Failure to reject a difference is not a pass. A positive screening claim needs
predeclared, physically justified equivalence margins and simultaneous
one-sided upper uncertainty bounds on complete-record distances under the
registered interventions.

### C6 — SPB-T uses a frozen boundary-candidate lattice

The temporal candidates are freshly prepared `S`; `S+M`; `S+M` plus a printed
controller/reference carrier; and a bounded finite history. A reconstructed
full process or whole past remains an anti-control. An unregistered latent
memory cannot be appended after held-out opening.

### C7 — the temporal memory grammar is physical and typed

The named memory branches include identity, read, toggle/orthogonalization,
seed-retained and future-inaccessible randomization, reset, isolation, and
reset-plus-isolation. Readout backaction, causal-break backaction on memory,
reset destinations, retained seeds, controller records, leakage, residual
coupling, and failures remain in the parent rather than being called erasure.

### C8 — temporal composition is directly held out

Separately characterized segments and the candidate boundary must predict a
directly executed three-segment continuation. Per-program process tables,
tomography of the connected target, or a segment family growing like the
target receive no explanatory credit.

### C9 — SPB-R requires one fixed physical interface

Literal response equality at common labelled ports is distinct from equality
of trace, spectrum, poles, determinant, or similarity class. A port-mixing
transplantation must be either an already-declared presentation symmetry or an
independently calibrated physical adapter whose extra channels, loss, gain,
noise, phases, and costs are included. The printed four-port `T_4` is not
unitary up to scalar normalization.

### C10 — regional composition is direct and regime-scoped

The simple `S(nu)` boundary applies only in a validated linear,
time-stationary, single-mode regime. Independently measured module and
connector relations predict a directly assembled composite through the seam
equations. Singular bins are registered outcomes. Both three-module
bracketings must agree where defined and predict the same directly measured
physical network.

### C11 — resources and no-boundary outcomes remain visible

Tomography, precision, reset energy and entropy, latency, history depth,
storage, calibration, adapters, reference systems, discarded records, and
data volume are charged. A target-sized table is a compression failure.
Larger, history-like, resource-heavy, underdetermined, and no-registered-
proper-boundary outcomes remain admissible.

### C12 — the maximum award is bounded experimental validity

An accepted contract establishes only that SPB is a well-posed physical test.
A later positive experiment could establish only task- and regime-relative
screening/composition. Neither establishes a universal state, Markovianity,
tensor-factor ontology, Hilbert ontology, Barandes ontology, actuality,
chronology, spacetime, gravity, or unification.
