## 3. Conceptual checks

### 3.1 Quantum target is operational

`QProc_fd` is defined by finite-dimensional systems, CP instruments, and
finite-slot positive normalized multilinear process functionals. Choi, comb,
dilation, and circuit forms are proof representations rather than ontology.
Continuous-variable QFT and quantum gravity are outside the target.

### 3.2 Contextuality is not precluded

The stochastic law is a compatible contextual family indexed by typed
physical experiments. The pin does not require a joint distribution over
outcomes of incompatible experiments. It does require common construction,
composition, and naturality, so contextual typing cannot collapse into an
answer lookup.

### 3.3 “One architecture” is not “one Hamiltonian”

The target is one uniform translation/construction rule across systems,
processes, dimensions, and finite slot counts. It does not assert that every
laboratory Hamiltonian or controlled operation is the same numerical law.
Controls enter typed experiments; preparations enter contingent state; the
construction must state which process data are nomological. A later universal
theory-selection question is not pre-awarded.

### 3.4 Bell cost is result-neutral

The pin requires exact CHSH/no-signalling reproduction and a premise ledger.
It permits global/contextual histories, measurement dependence if openly
postulated, and all-at-once/retrocausal candidates if fully typed. It does not
permit the word locality to replace the premise analysis.

### 3.5 Positive probability is a hypothesis under test

The candidate starts with ordinary probability on typed histories because
that is the Barandes-inspired ontology being tested. If compositional quantum
processes require irreducible amplitude/decoherence-functional structure, the
registered `HILBERT-IRREDUCIBLE` or failure outcomes can be earned. The pin
does not force a stochastic success.

### 3.6 State, law, and actuality are separated

The state map carries preparations/contingent data. `Gamma-bold` carries the
fixed conditional rule schema. `Act` is separately postulated, derived, or
unconstructed. The pin contains attacks against all three pairwise
conflations.

### 3.7 No spacetime input leaks

Slot order is explicitly laboratory control order. Discreteness, geometry,
dimension, signature, selector `Pi_phys`, cosmological state, and gravity are
outside the live unit. Paper 13D is a control only.

## 4. Feasibility balance

A positive construction is not logically ruled out: the stochastic–quantum
correspondence motivates single-system realizers, and contextual/global
history laws can evade naive Bell and contextuality contradictions. A strong
positive result is nevertheless nontrivial because sequential and tensor
naturality, general instruments, process consistency, and record uncomputation
must hold uniformly.

A negative or scoped result is also feasible: the correspondence may require
a hidden Hilbert wrapper, fail natural composition, require unbounded memory,
or stop at single-system dynamics. The product coordinates prevent such an
outcome from being converted into a total rejection or acceptance.
