## 2. Independent test of empirical content versus circular definition

Four parts of the proposed distinction are empirically nonvacuous at the
printed operational scope.

1. Descent can fail while prediction succeeds: the full process, full history,
   and target quotient are explicit anti-controls (contract lines 19–23,
   213–231).
2. Screening can pass or fail independently of mere fit because distinct
   intervened histories/interiors must prepare a prevalidated boundary class
   and are compared on held-out complete attempted-run laws by an equivalence
   upper bound (contract lines 128–159, 233–255, 304–320, 400–446).
3. Composition can pass or fail independently of pairwise screening because
   separately characterized pieces predict a directly assembled three-part
   experiment, including both bracketings and registered seam failures
   (contract lines 163–188, 322–337, 448–488).
4. Implemented carrier operations can fail even when a predictive object fits:
   temporal read/toggle/randomize/reset/isolate branches and regional physical
   substitution/reconnection retain their failures and costs (contract lines
   257–288, 448–505).

Those comparisons admit real positive, negative, invalid, and underdetermined
records. They are not made true by the word “sufficient.” The fifth advertised
coordinate, however, is not independent under the frozen definitions; Finding
O-01 is load-bearing.

## 3. Numbered findings

### O-01 — Major / semantic — no-refit transfer is already a condition of composition

Affected claims: C2; the five-coordinate definition in contract Section 3;
the claim that every coordinate is separately reportable; interpretation of
the integrated rung vector.

Exact evidence:

- Pin C2 says the five duties are tested separately and that “[n]o one duty
  implies the others” (pin lines 201–205).
- The contract defines `C` as “target-blind held-out composition” and `X` as
  “no-refit held-out transfer” (contract lines 110–119).
- The binding composition rule says composition passes **only if** “the same
  rule transfers to a held-out policy/band without retuning” (contract lines
  178–185).
- P0 defines D5 by freezing the law, interface rule, reader map, and nuisance
  family before the held-out future, exterior, frequency band, or gluing is
  opened (P0 lines 90–94), which is also a precondition of the held-out
  composition test.

Therefore `C => X` under the printed pass rule. There is no admissible scored
case in which composition passes but this version of no-refit transfer fails.
The empirical anti-leakage requirement is sound, but the asserted five-way
logical independence is false. This is not an editorial synonym: it changes
the meaning of the frozen duty vector and directly defeats C2 as printed.

### O-02 — Major / semantic — the regional candidate does not instantiate the physical-carrier tuple it invokes

Affected claims: C1; C2’s descent/intervention coordinates for SPB-R; C9;
regional `SPB-L2` eligibility; R1–R4 as *physical-boundary* outcomes.

Exact evidence:

- C1 withholds physical-boundary credit from a scattering matrix unless its
  carrier, preparation, interventions, readers, equivalence relation, and
  resource costs independently descend (pin lines 193–199).
- The generic boundary candidate is a six-coordinate tuple containing `B`,
  `Prep_B`, `Instr_B`, `Read_B`, `~_B`, and `Cost_B` (contract lines 96–108).
- The regional candidate is instead the five-coordinate tuple
  `(S_B(nu), P, R, Q_port, Cost_R)` (contract lines 378–392). Its first entry is
  the calibrated complex response relation; no regional carrier entry and no
  `Instr_B` entry are present.
- The contract itself forbids calling a scattering matrix physical merely
  because it predicts records (contract lines 30–36), while P0 calls the
  calibrated complex multiport response the boundary candidate (P0 lines
  353–363).
- Physical ports, modules, adapters, connectors, sources, and readers are named
  in the apparatus and gluing sections (contract lines 359–376, 448–488), but
  the frozen text never types which of them is the carrier whose physical
  state/property is `B`, nor how the regional substitution/reconnection
  operations occupy the omitted instrument coordinate.

The regional experiment can noncircularly establish that an independently
measured response relation predicts held-out exterior and composite wave
records. The frozen type does not establish the further C1 statement that the
response object has earned physical-carrier descent. Calling the operational
disposition itself the carrier would be a substantive referent choice; calling
the ports or collar the carrier would be a different one. The frozen bytes do
not choose or type either. Thus a positive R result is presently decisive for
effective response-model composability, but not for C1 physical-boundary
credit as printed.

### O-03 — Moderate / semantic — the regional quotient does not fully separate passive relabelling from active port permutation

Affected claims: C1, C5, C9; fixed-port screening; R1–R4.

Exact evidence:

- C9 requires literal equality at common labelled physical ports and permits
  transplantation only as an already declared presentation symmetry or a
  calibrated physical adapter (pin lines 249–256).
- The regional candidate nevertheless declares `Q_port` as a quotient by
  “port relabellings and reference phase conventions” without printing the
  action or the fixed physical-port identification (contract lines 389–392).
- The later cases distinguish a coordinate relabelling from a physical
  port-mixing adapter (contract lines 413–429), and the hostile list includes a
  port permutation represented correctly and incorrectly (contract lines
  490–505).

A simultaneous passive renaming of already identified ports changes no
experiment. An active permutation of which exterior cable meets which
physical port can change the experiment and can make two matrices equal only
after consulting their relation. The prose recognizes the distinction, but
the frozen quotient does not type it. Predeclaration prevents held-out
retuning; it does not by itself prevent the candidate pair and quotient from
being co-chosen from a known answer. C9’s principle is correct, but the
regional instantiation is not yet semantically closed enough to guarantee it.

## 5. Outcome neutrality and ceiling

The contract does not force a scientific answer.

- Temporal positives remain possible through `T1`–`T4`; screening without
  composition, target-built-only prediction, registered-boundary failure, and
  underdetermination remain possible through `T5`–`T8`.
- Regional effective-response positives remain possible through `R1`–`R4`;
  composition/coherence failure, simple-response failure, registered-boundary
  failure, and underdetermination remain possible through `R5`–`R9`.
- `T0` and `R0` preserve packet invalidity rather than coercing missing data
  into a physical result.
- Complete-attempt normalization, equivalence rather than nonrejection, direct
  held-out composition, resource charging, and the underdetermined outcomes
  leave both positive and negative empirical records possible.

O-01 prevents the five coordinates from being interpreted as logically
independent. O-02 and O-03 prevent a positive regional response-model result
from receiving the stronger physical-carrier/fixed-interface meaning claimed
by C1 and C9. These defects affect what the acquisition would be permitted to
award, not merely how a later result would be described.

The pin ceiling itself is sound. This review has no authority to award an
empirical boundary, and the maximum possible review award is `SPB-L0`.

## 6. Disposition

negative operational outcomes, but the frozen package contains load-bearing

of C1, C2, C9, or the regional award. This report proposes no in-pin repair,
successor, apparatus action, empirical run, or repository change.
