## 3. Shared questions all seats must answer

Each report must answer `Q1`--`Q12` independently.

### Q1 — neutrality

Do the objects avoid assuming a metric, continuum, dimension, topology,
foliation, tensor-product matter/gravity split, classical gravity, quantized
gravity, history ontology, or actuality rule?

### Q2 — common interface

Can ordinary-positive, non-Kolmogorov/pair-history, and quantum-plus-
actualization laws map to the registered output without being forced into one
another's microscopic probability semantics?

### Q3 — role typing

Are `S`, `G`, `P`, apparatus, interventions, readers, records, law, state,
control, and actuality separated without secretly imposing a microscopic
factorization?

### Q4 — same-parent reciprocity

Does the protocol reject two independently fitted directions while permitting
controlled one-way limits? Is reciprocity typed without deriving a
fundamental chronology from the laboratory schedule?

### Q5 — complete experiment

Is “the same complete source--geometry--probe experiment” sufficiently exact
to prevent candidates from using different apparatus, nuisance channels,
record algebras, hidden data, or comparison maps?

### Q6 — entrant completeness

Can completeness be adjudicated at a narrow finite-mode weak-field scope
without requiring a solution to all QFT, nonlinear GR, cosmology, or
actuality? Does the gate still exclude sketches, compilers, separately solved
metrics, and incomplete master equations?

### Q7 — genuine distinctness

Does the test reject gauge, basis, relabelling, parameter, presentation, and
answer-table duplicates while permitting an objective unravelling only when
its referent and reciprocal source law are physical?

### Q8 — fixed-background control

Is R1RR-L4 used faithfully? Does it prevent degradation of quantum physics
without being counted as an entrant or ontology?

### Q9 — massive-apparatus completeness

Does the frozen public-input schema adequately require source preparation,
traps, supports, coherence witnesses, retained/erased records, probe readout,
electromagnetic/Casimir/thermal/seismic/vibrational confounds, failures, and
uncertainty propagation? If not, does the defect require contract revision or
a separate pre-entrant baseline?

### Q10 — discriminator validity

Do PG, CP, ME, and DD distinguish source functionals and reciprocal laws
without making Page--Geilker, coherence sensitivity, mediator entanglement, or
decoherence--diffusion into universal ontology theorems?

### Q11 — nonselection and outcome scope

Is agreement between distinct entrants correctly treated as nonselection? Are
null, infeasible, confounded, excluded, and nonidentifying outcomes preserved?

### Q12 — authority

Do the frozen objects and pin clearly bar candidate work, comparison,
ontology selection, spacetime claims, gravity verdicts, and automatic
successors inside this review?
