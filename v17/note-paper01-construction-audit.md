### 5.1 Modulus-square nonfunctoriality

The paper explicitly computes $|H|^2|H|^2\ne|H^2|^2$. Sequential naturality
therefore means physical program wiring followed by one whole-process
evaluation, not multiplication of positive kernels at every slot.

### 5.2 Coherence is not silently deleted

Equal-diagonal $|+\rangle$ and $|-\rangle$ preparations are distinguished by
one common continuation. The paper says phase-complete continuation structure
is required; it does not claim complex amplitudes have been explanatorily
eliminated.

### 5.3 Bell scope

The paper earns operational no-signalling and prints the exact global,
contextual-history cost. It neither claims parameter/outcome independence nor
infers relativistic locality.

### 5.4 Contextuality scope

The chosen measurement context is a physical program component. Unperformed
contexts receive no simultaneous outcomes. No hidden context-independent
Peres--Mermin values are asserted.

### 5.5 Memory scope

Operational process memory, CP divisibility, enlarged-state memory,
stochastic division, and interference nondivision are separated with exact
counterexamples. Program order is not promoted to time.

### 5.6 Record scope

Sector transport is a non-kill, coherent global uncomputation restores the
fringe, and a leaked environment record blocks local restoration. Record
stability is not called actualization.

### 5.7 Trajectory scope

The microscopic realizer no-go uses two positive Kolmogorov measures with
identical licensed first-order marginals and opposite two-time correlation.
No absent variable is assigned a uniform law.
