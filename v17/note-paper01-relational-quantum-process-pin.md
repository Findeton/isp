## 4. Quantum target category `QProc_fd`

### 4.1 Systems

Objects are finite-dimensional complex Hilbert systems, including the tensor
unit. A composite is represented by the standard tensor product. The target
domain contains every finite dimension and every finite number of slots; a
construction may prove the general result algebraically rather than enumerate
them.

### 4.2 Operations and instruments

An event at a slot is a completely positive trace-nonincreasing map. An
instrument is a finite or standard-Borel outcome-indexed family whose sum or
integral is trace preserving. Classical randomization, conditioning, adaptive
control, ancilla extension, and discard are part of the interface.

### 4.3 Processes

A finite-slot quantum process is defined operationally as a multilinear
functional on compatible local CP events that:

1. returns nonnegative probabilities even under arbitrary finite ancillary
   extensions;
2. normalizes to one for every sequence of deterministic instruments;
3. is consistent when a slot is ignored via its deterministic operation;
4. respects classical randomization in each slot;
5. composes with admissible pre/post-processes and parallel processes.

This representation-independent definition is binding. A Choi operator,
comb, circuit, dilation, or process tensor may be used in proofs after its
equivalence to this definition is shown under one explicit convention.

### 4.4 Outcome law

For process `W`, experiment/instrument family `e`, and complete outcome `o`,
write the standard probability

$$
p_Q(o\mid e,W).
$$

This is a comparator value, not an ISP measured result. Construction must
derive the stochastic value independently before comparison.
