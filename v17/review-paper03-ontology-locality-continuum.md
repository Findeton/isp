## 1. First decisive semantic counterexample

### 1.1 Covariant law does not imply frame-symmetric state

The candidate universally claims:

> No preferred frame is operationally visible in the registered comparator
> domain.

But an admitted packet includes a contingent initial state `omega`, and the
state class is not restricted to Lorentz-invariant states. Let `M` be
Minkowski spacetime and let `beta_v` be the algebra automorphism induced by a
Lorentz boost. Choose an admissible locally normal state `omega` that is not
boost invariant. A thermal/KMS state with a definite thermal four-velocity is
the standard physical example. By noninvariance, there exists a local
observable or localized reader effect `A` such that

$$
\omega(A)\ne\omega(\beta_v(A)).
$$

The two probe orientations/velocities can therefore produce different
registered probabilities while the background, apparatus type, and state are
held fixed. The state's rest frame is operationally detectable.

Nothing in local covariance forbids this. Covariance instead gives the
different statement

$$
(\beta_v^*\omega)(A)=\omega(\beta_v(A)),
$$

which compares an observable and a **jointly transported state**. It does not
imply

$$
\omega(A)=\omega(\beta_v(A))
$$

for one fixed `omega`. Spacelike branch-map commutativity likewise removes a
serialization convention, not a physical rest frame selected by contingent
matter or state data.

Thus Theorem 11.1 item 17, Corollary 7.5, the title's unqualified frame
language, and the `preferred-frame` product row conflate four distinct
claims:

1. coordinate covariance;
2. independence from a program's arbitrary linear serialization;
3. Lorentz symmetry of the contingent state and apparatus; and
4. absence of hidden preferred microscopic structure.

The construction proves 1 and 2. It explicitly refuses 4. It neither assumes
nor proves 3, and its packet family admits counterexamples to 3.

### 1.2 Scientific effect

This is semantic, not editorial, because it changes a named theorem target,
the result product, and the physical interpretation. It does **not** defeat
the localized instruments, causal factorization, no-signalling, steering,
Bell compatibility, quotient, or positive-history construction. It lowers
the frozen candidate's presently supported ceiling to rung 6. A forward
repair may restore rung 7 by replacing the claim with:

> No undeclared foliation or loop serialization affects the registered law;
> state- or apparatus-selected rest frames remain physical packet data and
> may be operationally detectable.

No comparator, parameter, or physical law needs retuning for that repair.

## 2. Ontology/locality/gauge/continuum reconstruction

### 2.1 Positive history: real probability, predictive reification

For each performed finite program, the branch kernel has ordinary positive
mass

$$
K_i(r,d\lambda'\mid\lambda)
=p_i(r\mid\lambda)\,
\delta_{U_i(\lambda,r)}(d\lambda').
$$

Iteration yields normalized probabilities for complete record histories.
Zero-support branches remain zero and do not acquire a posterior. This is a
genuine positive stochastic representation of the registered predictions.

Its hidden object is nevertheless the complete algebraic predictive state or
conditional process object already required by AQFT. It may encode global
entanglement and all process memory. The update kernel is instrument
dependent, and the family is indexed by declared experiments. The paper
correctly prices it as predictive reification rather than explanatory local
microphysics. It is not a local Bell variable, classical field ontology, or
universal law.

### 2.2 Operational locality is not Bell locality

The candidate keeps separate:

```text
Einstein causality of spacelike observable algebras
locality of an admitted nonselective operation
unchanged remote marginal
selective remote steering
Bell factorization of a hidden-variable model.
```

The nonselective theorem depends on the complete localized system--probe map,
not merely commutators. Selective conditioning can change a remote
conditional state, but the uncontrolled outcome and retained classical record
are required to form the subensemble. The existential QFT Bell result is
kept distinct from the split-qubit arithmetic control. No Bell-local hidden
variable is claimed.

### 2.3 Preferred structure has three statuses

Map-level equality under adjacent exchanges proves that an arbitrary loop
order for causally disjoint couplings is presentation-idle. An additional
hidden variable ignored by all transitions/readers is empirically idle under
the frozen interface. A variable that changes registered probabilities is
physical and cannot be gauged away.

Definition 7.3 rigorously appends a standard probability-space label. It does
not by itself construct a geometrically coherent foliation across all
spacetimes, embeddings, tensor compositions, and history restrictions.
Calling `Z` a “foliation label” is therefore an interpretation of an idle
coordinate, not a construction of a preferred-foliation ontology. This is
enough to block inference from operational covariance to microscopic absence,
but it supplies no positive preferred-frame model.

Separately, the initial state or apparatus can select an operational rest
frame. That case is neither gauge nor hidden microstructure and is the exact
counterexample in Section 1.

### 2.4 Records, divisions, conditioning, decoherence, actuality

A retained probe outcome is a typed classical record. Forgetting it applies
the full nonselective channel and changes the boundary. Conditioning is
defined only at positive support or through a declared regular conditional.
It does not cause a global hypersurface collapse.

Prefix coherence follows by summing complete suffix instruments. It does not
license a restart kernel at every geometric boundary. A cut is a division
only when its retained predictive object and records are sufficient for every
licensed future. A stable record can fail that condition.

The construction supplies no actuality map. One recorded branch in an
observed run is not a theorem selecting one microscopic history. Neither
conditioning, decoherence, superselection, nor a classical record provides
the missing selection law.

### 2.5 Barandes compatibility is not completion

Paper 03 shows that positive whole-program probabilities can coexist with
relativistic quantum operational structure without forcing a Markov
checkpoint at every cut. That is compatible with the indivisible-process
motivation.

It does not supply a fixed fundamental configuration referent, one
experiment-independent law family for possible histories, a contingent
cosmological state separated from that law, exact physical division rules,
or one actual trajectory. Each packet begins with a spacetime, algebra,
state, couplings, and readers. The correct Barandes coordinate is therefore
admissible but incomplete, never reconstructed or refuted.

### 2.6 Type-III local meaning

The main construction uses algebraic positive functionals and positive/normal
CP maps, so it does not require an intrinsic regional trace-class density
matrix. In a type-III local algebra a normal state remains physically usable
through its expectation functional even though no intrinsic regional density
operator or finite Kraus list is available. The paper correctly keeps any
positive type-III assertion model specific rather than deriving it from the
AQFT axioms.

The global predictive state must not be confused with a local beable merely
because it restricts to local algebras. Its global correlations and future
predictive role remain explicit.

### 2.7 Split property

A split inclusion with a collar and the required nuclearity/transport
hypotheses can supply a type-I intermediary and the matrix-subalgebra controls
used for steering and preparation contextuality. This is conditional
operational independence. It does not tensor-factor touching regions, every
gauge theory, or the continuum globally, and it does not create lattice
cells.

The split-qubit control and the Summers--Werner Bell existence theorem are
different comparator uses. The candidate does not multiply them into one
universal QFT construction.

### 2.8 Reeh--Schlieder

Dense local action on a cyclic vector does not give a bounded deterministic
remote-control channel. Approximating a target can require growing operator
norm, and implementing the rescaled contraction can have very small success
probability. Selecting successful outcomes requires a classical record. The
complete nonselective operation remains no-signalling. The candidate retains
all of these costs.

### 2.9 Gauge, charge, sectors, and edges

The paper constructs a type firewall, not a gauge theory. Observable and
charged field algebras, gauge actions, DHR sectors, Wilson/flux operators,
regional centers/edge data, and probe records remain distinct. Gauss-law
constraints can obstruct regional tensor products. No gauge group, charge
spectrum, sector statistics, or actualization mechanism is inferred from
covariance.

### 2.10 Identical particles and field scope

No run or particle label is promoted to a beable. Local field/observable
algebras and sector structure replace labeled-particle tensor factors.
Particle number, Fock representation, asymptotic particles, and
spin/statistics retain their representation, spectrum, localization, and
sector hypotheses.

### 2.11 Continuum and ultraviolet scope

The main result is conditional abstract-net mathematics plus separately named
controls. It does not construct an interacting four-dimensional theory, the
Standard Model, or a continuum limit of a cutoff model. A mode truncation or
lattice is only a control. Cutoff removal needs independent convergence and
renormalization.

Regional density matrices and entanglement entropy are not treated as
universal AQFT objects. Bell correlations and algebraic instruments do not
require them. No discrete substrate or spacetime atom is inferred.

### 2.12 Declared geometry is not emergent geometry

`Loc`, dimension, Lorentzian metric, orientation, time orientation, causal
convexity, and global hyperbolicity are comparator inputs. Local covariance
and relative Cauchy evolution describe a field theory across declared
backgrounds; they do not place a probability law on metrics, impose quantum
constraints, or yield Einstein dynamics. The paper correctly refuses
background independence, internal time, geometry emergence, and gravity.

## 7. Mandatory fresh Seat O attacks

### O1. Empirically idle preferred-foliation inflation

Append a standard-Borel label ignored by every transition and reader. The
projection preserves registered predictions, so operational data cannot
select the label or a prior. But the label alone is not yet a foliation: a
physical foliation requires compatible geometric assignments under
embeddings, restrictions, and composition. The candidate's nonselection
lesson survives; a positive hidden-foliation construction is unearned.

### O2. Two predictive-history realizers with identical profiles

Compare the base predictive history with its independent binary inflation.
Every complete reader profile and record law agrees after projection, yet the
history spaces are not isomorphic over the base. No registered invariant
natural under that projection selects the bit. This confirms microscopic
nonselection rather than a unique ontology.

### O3. Type-III region with no intrinsic density matrix

Choose a named comparator whose local von Neumann algebra is type III. The
restriction of a global normal state is still a normal positive functional,
but no intrinsic trace-class regional density operator exists. The
instrument and reader formulae remain algebraic. The candidate survives and
correctly refuses density-matrix ontology.

### O4. Gauss-law nonfactorization

In a gauge model with Gauss constraints, regional observable algebras can
share center/flux constraints and fail a naive Hilbert tensor split. The main
proof never uses that split; the gauge row only types the obstruction. No
edge mode, charge algebra, or tensor factor is invented. The candidate
survives.

### O5. Reeh--Schlieder approximation with diverging cost

Take a sequence of local operators whose normalized action approaches a
remote target while their norms grow. Rescaling to valid contractions drives
the successful selective probability down. Without the success record the
complete channel does not prepare the target. The candidate retains this
cost and survives.

### O6. Free-field result generalized to interacting 3+1 QFT

The localized-probe construction has named comparator examples, while the
main theorem is conditional on an admitted net and causal factorization. It
does not prove existence of an interacting four-dimensional model, cutoff
removal, the Standard Model, or gravity. The cross-model extrapolation is
explicitly refused.

### O7. Global predictive state called a local beable

The complete `lambda` can encode spacelike entanglement and future process
memory. Its restriction to local observables does not make the global object
a local hidden variable. Cost theorem 6.6 says exactly this. The candidate
survives.

### O8. Recorded branch with no selection rule

The stochastic kernel assigns probabilities and the record tree labels
branches, but no field chooses one `H`. Conditioning assumes a record value;
it does not cause that value. The `actuality` coordinate remains
unconstructed, as required.

### O9. Detectable frame in a contingent state

Hold a non-boost-invariant admitted state fixed and compare a localized probe
with its boosted physical counterpart. Some response differs by definition
of noninvariance. Joint transport covariance remains exact. This refutes the
literal preferred-frame theorem while leaving scheduling independence and
law covariance intact. **This is the first decisive semantic
counterexample.**
