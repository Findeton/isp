## 2. Verdict and exact scope

The pin is internally coherent and implementable as a **conditional
representation theorem**.  It correctly repairs the two category-level
failures that mattered in v3.1:

1. the boundary object now contains every variable that a frozen licensed
   continuation may read, rather than retaining an external third factor; and
2. a physical arrow is one typed pair containing both the unchanged sample
   kernel and the normal Heisenberg map, with exact all-reader compatibility
   derived for each constructor and preserved by composition.

The acceptance is bindingly narrower than a universal theorem about arbitrary
continuous instruments or arbitrary hybrid fields.  It covers only packets
that satisfy all of the following before the theorem is invoked:

- one represented sigma-finite von Neumann algebra with separable predual is
  common to the admitted record fibers of a boundary;
- the licensed future and reader interfaces are frozen first;
- every licensed future-readable nonrecord variable is represented in that
  algebra;
- the admitted ensemble class is closed under identity, every primitive,
  every finite path, finite registered mixtures, and every registered
  positive central conditioning event;
- continuous families possess one common technical outcome class, jointly
  measurable normal extensions, jointly measurable posteriors and sample
  kernels, target landing, and joint record-class nonsingularity; and
- exact paths and exact concurrency retain frozen representatives and all
  presentation tags rather than quotienting by integrated or almost-everywhere
  equality.

These are restrictions of theorem domain, not consequences of the frozen v2
physics.  A construction must refuse or split a packet when any one fails.
It may not enlarge the domain by adding a hidden state, choosing posterior
versions after an outcome, or replacing exact compatibility by approximation.

## 3. Independent reconstruction of the mathematical object

### 3.1 Complete tagged boundary

For each frozen frontier `D`, the pin first fixes the port schema, retained
record schema, complete licensed continuation family, every memory variable
read by that family, and every final reader algebra.  Only then may it choose
a represented sigma-finite von Neumann algebra `B_D` with separable predual.
The hybrid reader algebra is

```text
finite/atomic:  O_D = B_D bar-tensor l-infinity(R_D),
integrated:     O_D = B_D bar-tensor L-infinity(R_D, nu_D).
```

The tagged object also retains the exact v2 sample space `X_D`, admitted law
class `E_D`, record map `r_D`, complete normal-state field `b_D`, regime,
measure class, frozen futures, frozen readers, and physical boundary tag.
Thus algebra isomorphism alone cannot identify two objects with different
memory interfaces or different null ideals.

This is a **constant-fiber** direct-integral representation.  It says that
one `B_D` is used across the admitted record fibers.  It neither asserts a
product state nor licenses outcome-dependent nonisomorphic algebras.  A
family `r -> B_{D,r}` outside this hypothesis needs a genuinely measurable
field/direct-integral schema and is not silently covered by this pin.

### 3.2 Predual ensemble semantics

For an admitted law `Lambda`, let `eta=(r_D)_* Lambda << nu_D`, let
`h=d eta/d nu_D`, and let `bar beta_r` be its strongly measurable conditional
normal-state barycentre.  The normal functional is represented by the
Bochner-integrable predual field `h(r) bar beta_r`:

```text
Ens_D(Lambda)(F)
  = integral b_D(x)(F-sharp(r_D(x))) Lambda(dx).
```

The pointwise notation is harmless only because the record marginal is
dominated and the predual-field formula is primary.  If two representatives
of `F` differ on a `nu_D`-null set, they differ only on an `eta`-null set for
that ensemble, so the functional is representative independent.  Positivity
and normalization follow from the state field.  Normality follows because
`h bar beta` lies in the canonical `L1(R_D,nu_D;(B_D)_*)` predual.  Nothing in
this argument defines a generic point state on a nonatomic `L-infinity`
fiber.

Closure under positive central conditioning is essential.  Mere domination
does not make `E_D` a state space stable under the experiments the packet
claims to support.  The pin now demands closure under all primitive and finite
path kernels, finite registered mixtures, and normalized restrictions to
registered positive central events.  This removes the exact v3.1
counterexample in which a singleton ensemble class was dominated but not
closed after conditioning.

### 3.3 Paired category

An arrow is the exact five-field tuple

```text
(g, D, E, K_a, Phi_a),
```

where `K_a` is the unchanged normalized v2 kernel and `Phi_a:O_E -> O_D` is
normal UCP.  Admission is not granted by either component in isolation.  It
also requires tags, ensemble closure, interface preservation, deterministic
and stochastic null-ideal preservation, target landing, exact lineage, and
the all-reader identity

```text
Ens_E(Lambda K_a) = Ens_D(Lambda) o Phi_a
```

for every admitted source ensemble.  The identity is an extensional
hom-set predicate, but the construction is forbidden to assume it for a
physical primitive.  Each primitive constructor must derive both components
from one frozen physical datum and prove the identity.

If `a:D->E` and `b:E->F`, composition is

```text
(b o a, D, F, K_a K_b, Phi_a o Phi_b).
```

The variance is correct.  For an admitted `Lambda`,

```text
Ens_F(Lambda K_a K_b)
 = Ens_E(Lambda K_a) o Phi_b
 = Ens_D(Lambda) o Phi_a o Phi_b.
```

Normalized kernels and normal UCP maps are closed under the displayed
composition.  Exact boundary tags make composition total on matched hom-sets
and undefined on mismatches.  Kernel associativity, function-composition
associativity, and concatenated lineage give associativity of the pair.
The identity uses the Dirac identity kernel and the algebra identity.  The
physical path label remains part of the tuple, so a slot-consuming explicit
skip need not collapse to the empty categorical identity even when its two
semantic projections happen to coincide.

`Ev` and contravariant `Heis` are therefore genuine projections of the paired
category.  Neither projection is the physical arrow by itself.

### 3.4 Continuous assembly

For an already-retained record `r`, one frozen instrument family `I_r`, one
record update `j(r,s)`, and one family of normal UCP extensions

```text
Ihat_r : B_E bar-tensor L-infinity(S,mu) -> B_D
```

define the candidate hybrid arrow by

```text
[Phi(F)](r) = Ihat_r(s |-> F-sharp(j(r,s))).
```

This formula is meaningful on equivalence classes only if the pullback by
`j` preserves the relevant null ideals.  A sufficient exact condition is

```text
j_*(nu_D tensor mu) << nu_E,
```

or an explicitly equivalent instrument-weighted condition strong enough to
make `F -> F o j` well defined for `nu_D`-almost every fiber.  Checking only
each named deterministic slice is not enough for a stochastic family.

Fiberwise NEP is also not enough.  The construction owes:

- one common sigma-finite technical outcome class `mu` for the displayed
  domain;
- weak-star/predual measurability of `r -> Ihat_r` sufficient for the output
  field to be measurable;
- a decomposable normal preadjoint or an equivalent proof of normality of the
  assembled `Phi`;
- a jointly measurable posterior-state family and one jointly Borel sample
  kernel;
- target landing in the exact `B_E`/`O_E` packet; and
- old-record retention through `j`, absent an already licensed
  coarse-graining or discard.

On generators `B tensor chi_Delta`, the common instrument gives

```text
integral_Delta rho_{r,s}(B) mu_{rho,r}(ds)
 = rho(I_r(B,Delta))
 = rho(Ihat_r(B tensor chi_Delta)).
```

Linearity gives finite simple fields.  Ultraweak density plus normality gives
the complete hybrid algebra.  Positivity and monotone convergence are useful
inside that argument, but a construction should not rely on an unqualified
claim that every arbitrary operator-valued field has a pointwise increasing
simple approximation.  Integration over admitted ensembles and the paired
category theorem then yield finite-path duality.  This is a derivation of the
all-reader identity, not its insertion as a premise.

### 3.5 Exactness and covariance

Integrated predictions ignore changes on the common null ideal.  Exact sample
kernels and exact concurrency do not.  The pin correctly freezes one jointly
measurable representative for `Ev` and requires equality of those frozen
representatives for an exchange certificate.

A packet isomorphism must jointly transport comparator, algebras, sample and
ensemble spaces, measure classes, state fields, futures/readers, both semantic
layers, sources, apparatus, records, multiplicities, and certificates.  A
proper `Loc` embedding still induces observable transport and state pullback;
it does not canonically push a contingent state forward.  These variance
rules are category-correct and preserve the inherited BFV/Fewster--Verch
scope without upgrading an input spacetime to emergent geometry.
