## 3. Conceptual audit

### 3.1 The quantifier domain is not one preferred representation

The class of adequate representations is defined by the terminal Paper 01
interface: probability equality, complete continuations, sequential/tensor
naturality, instruments, and declared microscopic data. Paper 01's Hilbert
dilation is one member and cannot serve as the definition of every member.

The construction must fix ordinary set-theoretic size conventions or state
the universal result as a schema over small typed representation categories.
This is a formal hygiene requirement, not a physical input.

### 3.2 The quotient is typed

Operational equivalence is defined separately at each compatible boundary
type and uses every ancillary extension, continuation, and reader. The
quotient claim is therefore about reachable boundary/process classes, not a
set of terminal scalar probabilities. The construction still owes proof that
the equivalence is a congruence for every accepted composition.

### 3.3 Fiber freedom is not automatically gauge

The pin explicitly distinguishes invertible redescription, inaccessible
dilation coordinates, deletable empirical redundancy, contingent but
unobserved physical data, and experimentally distinguishable structure.
Fiber inflation proves nonidentifiability under the registered interface. It
does not prove that every added variable is unreal.

The universal factorization theorem is intentionally conditional on
invariance under admitted fiber refinements and reductions. The construction
must not present that hypothesis as an independently discovered law of
nature.

### 3.4 Positivity and noncontextuality remain separate

Paper 01 already supplies positive probabilities on actual records. The
contextuality/negativity theorems concern stronger fixed-sample-space,
affinity, and noncontextuality premises. The pin requires a premise ledger and
therefore cannot turn those theorems into a no-go against all positive
contextual history models.

### 3.5 Phase completeness does not pre-award complex ontology

The full phase circle and common continuations force more than a diagonal
probability vector. They do not alone prove that complex coordinates are
fundamental. The real-coordinate controls require every global carrier,
tensor, local-tomography, and source-independence cost to be exposed.
Conversely, writing complex amplitudes as larger real matrices does not remove
the equivalent complex structure.

### 3.6 Resource minima are class-relative

Minimal Stinespring dimension, Choi rank, statistical sufficiency, process
memory, and Markovian ontic dimension are mathematically meaningful only
under their stated representation classes. The pin contains direct attacks
against promoting those minima to universal ontology.

### 3.7 Exact equivalence cannot self-discriminate

The discriminator ledger prevents an ontology from being selected by
reanalyzing probabilities on which it is exactly equivalent to a competitor.
An enlarged experiment or new physical principle is a new input and must be
frozen later. Simplicity and implementation convenience are not substitutes.

### 3.8 Actuality and time remain closed

One actual physical record is inherited only as a postulate. Microscopic
actuality, outcome selection, internal time, spacetime, and gravity remain
unconstructed. Laboratory program order cannot be used as a hidden bridge to
later chronology.
