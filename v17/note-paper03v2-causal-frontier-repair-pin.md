## 4. Finite causal laboratory slots

### 4.1 Slot structure

The slot structure is

$$
\mathsf L=(V,\prec,\operatorname{supp},\operatorname{port},
           \operatorname{guard}),
$$

with:

- a finite set $V$ of laboratory operation opportunities;
- a strict partial order $\prec$;
- compact coupling/support data $\operatorname{supp}(v)\subset M$;
- typed quantum/probe/classical input-output ports; and
- optional guards controlled only by earlier retained records.

Slots are not microscopic events. They are repeatable laboratory protocol
positions. Slot names and one chosen linear display order are presentation
only; support, causal incidence, ports, guards, and physical mechanism choices
are not.

### 4.2 Admission conditions

An admitted slot structure must satisfy:

1. $\prec$ is irreflexive and transitive;
2. every guard depends only on records produced at predecessor slots;
3. every port input is produced at a predecessor or is an exposed source;
4. whenever the support of one slot lies entirely to the causal past of
   another, the slots are comparable in that direction; overlapping or
   causally mixed supports are not exchangeable without a separate theorem;
5. incomparable slots have no hidden record, port, or source dependency;
6. a pair called exchangeable must also satisfy the exact map-level causal-
   factorization theorem for its mechanisms; and
7. no integer rank, loop index, or serialization token is physical data.

The partial order is declared comparator/laboratory structure. Paper 03 v2
does not derive it from a deeper ontology.

### 4.3 Lower-set frontiers

A completed frontier is a lower set $D\subseteq V$:

$$
v\in D,\ u\prec v\quad\Longrightarrow\quad u\in D.
$$

The enabled slots are the minimal elements of $V\setminus D$ whose guards are
typed by records already available at $D$.

Two linear serializations that complete the same incomparable slots have the
same lower-set frontier. This is the exact no-hidden-global-clock control.

## 5. Boundary objects

For one packet $\Xi$, a boundary object is

$$
B_{\Xi,D}=(\Xi,D,\mathsf P_D,\mathsf R_D,\mathsf X_D),

Different mechanisms filling the same enabled slot have the same source and
target frontier types when their physical interfaces match. This preserves the

The categorical identity at $B_{\Xi,D}$ is the empty path. An explicit idle

$$

B_{\Xi,D\cup\{v\}},

not the categorical identity. This prevents the v16-style mistake of silently
identifying “an opportunity passed” with “nothing happened,” while making no
microscopic ontology claim.

## 6. Primitive arrows and the ordinary category

### 6.1 Primitive arrows

For each enabled $v\notin D$ and each admitted mechanism $m$ at that slot,
there is a generator

$$
g_{v,m,D}:B_{\Xi,D}\longrightarrow B_{\Xi,D\cup\{v\}}.

and explicit skip, with exact port and record typing.

No generator may add a nonenabled slot. In particular, after a later timelike
slot has been completed, there is no generator that adds one of its missing
predecessors as a new maximal slot.

### 6.2 Free path category

Let $G_\Xi$ be the directed multigraph of all admitted boundary objects and
primitive arrows. Define

$$
\mathcal P_\Xi=\operatorname{Path}(G_\Xi).
$$

Then:

1. identities are empty paths;
2. composition is path concatenation;
3. every pair with exactly matching target/source objects composes;
4. composition is associative; and
5. causal inadmissibility occurs only in the absence of an ill-typed
   generator, never as a second predicate on composition.

They are not two endomorphisms of one boundary. The reversed word is absent

### 6.3 Adaptive programs

A finite adaptive program is represented by typed classical control on
retained records. Each branch mechanism fills the same slot and lands at the
same frontier type, possibly with different record values. A false guard uses
the explicitly typed skip mechanism. No future record can enable a past slot.

## 7. Causal factorization and schedule congruence

proved for the complete registered mechanisms. Only then may the relation

$$
g_{w,D\cup\{v\}}\circ g_{v,D}
\sim_{\rm ex}
g_{v,D\cup\{w\}}\circ g_{w,D}

The relation $\sim_{\rm ex}$ must be closed as a congruence, not used as a
partial composition rule. Adjacent exchanges of incomparable slots then show

Timelike order, classical dependence, correlated sources, and retained record
flow are never exchange generators.
