## 2. Root reconstruction of the load-bearing mathematics

### 2.1 Source closure is not causal identification

The contract defines

$$
Q_{c,a}(dy)
=
P\!\left(Y^\star\in dy\mid C=c,\operatorname{do}(A=a)\right)
\tag{2}
$$

and says it is identified by a randomized **or otherwise source-closed**
assignment mechanism. Randomization can identify the intended
intention-to-treat law under the printed complete-attempt accounting. Merely
knowing a nonrandom assignment source cannot.

Let

$$
U\sim\operatorname{Bernoulli}(1/2),\qquad A=U,\qquad Y=U,
\tag{3}
$$

with no causal arrow from `A` to `Y`. The source rule is completely known and
may be logged exactly. Observationally,

$$
P(Y=1\mid A=1)=1,
\qquad
P(Y=1\mid A=0)=0.
\tag{4}
$$

Under an intervention that sets `A` while leaving the preassignment variable
`U` and its effect on `Y` intact,

$$
P(Y=1\mid\operatorname{do}(A=a))=1/2
\quad\text{for both }a.
\tag{5}
$$

Registering `U` does not restore within-`U` overlap: only `A=U` is observed.
A causal transport model could identify the intervention, but that is an
additional assumption with its own validation duty. “Source-closed” alone
does not supply counterfactual support.

Section 2.4's option to return underdetermined is sound, but it does not repair
the universal identification sentence in Section 2.3. This independently
defeats `V2-C3` and the proposed causal firewall.

### 2.2 The margins permit equivalence by definition

For total variation,

$$
0\le d_{\rm TV}(P,Q)\le 1.
\tag{6}
$$

The contract imposes no binding upper restriction below the diameter on the
boundary-matching radius `delta_B` or the screening margin `epsilon_sci`.
Choose

$$
\delta_B=1,
\qquad
\epsilon^{\rm sci}=1.
\tag{7}
$$

Take two genuinely different histories that pass the earlier-history
separation test, whose boundary laws are at TV distance one and whose future
laws are also at TV distance one. With perfect readers and arbitrarily many
attempts, every valid upper confidence bound can be clipped at one. Thus

$$
U_B[d_B]\le\delta_B,
\qquad
U[d_{\rm TV}]\le\epsilon^{\rm sci},
\tag{8}
$$

so maximal physical disagreement is declared both matched and screened.

The phrase “maximum downstream record change compatible with the claimed
predictive use” does not make the use nontrivial, and the uninformative-reader
clause does not apply because the readers in this counterexample are perfect.
The history-separation certificate prevents duplicate labels but does not
prevent a vacuous matching/screening threshold.

A physical pin cannot be allowed to cure this after apparatus selection. A
valid contract must bind nontrivial margins relative to the distance diameter,
define the smallest scientifically meaningful alternative, and require power
against that alternative. This independently defeats the nonvacuity and
statistical-decidability claims.

### 2.3 Kernels do not supply their own initial law

Equation (7.3) passes piece kernels, seam kernels, and the shared
nuisance/reference law `Lambda` to `Comp`, but every piece is conditional on
an input boundary coordinate. No initial boundary or preparation probability
is an argument.

Let the first boundary bit be `X_0 in {0,1}`. Let every piece and seam copy its
input deterministically, let the final reader output that bit, and let
`Lambda` be trivial. All printed kernels are normalized. Nevertheless both

$$
P(X_0=X_1=\cdots=Y=0)=1
\tag{9}
$$

and

$$
P(X_0=X_1=\cdots=Y=1)=1
\tag{10}
$$

obey the same supplied kernels. Integrating the first input against counting
measure instead gives total mass two.

The Ionescu--Tulcea theorem extends an **initial probability measure plus** a
sequence of kernels. It does not create the initial measure. The generic
candidate type contains `Prep_B`, but `Prep_B` is absent from equation (7.3).
Reading `K_A` as preparation or reading `Lambda` as the initial carrier law
changes their printed types.

Therefore the asserted `Q_hat_ABC` is only an incompletely typed family, not
the one normalized complete-attempt prediction claimed. This independently
defeats the current composition contract.

### 2.4 Feedback and deterministic seam classifiers are not exhaustive and disjoint

let a valid simultaneous confidence set for `theta` include zero and small

the existence premise required by `ILL-CONDITIONED`. No

### 2.5 Minimal in a poset is not individually necessary

The contract calls a passing carrier “required” when every registered strict
predecessor fails, while also allowing incomparable passing candidates.
Consider the frozen poset

$$
p\prec x,
\qquad
p\prec y,
\qquad
x\parallel y.
\tag{13}
$$

Let `p` fail and both `x` and `y` pass the same duty bundle. Both are minimal
passing elements. Neither is individually required: `y` is a passing
alternative that need not contain `x`, and conversely.

The evidence supports the minimal passing antichain `{x,y}` and the set-level
claim that some augmentation beyond `p` is needed inside the registered
lattice. It does not support an individual necessity claim for either member.
The “no unique-selection” sentence does not make both uses of “required” true.

There is a second typing problem. `T-B1` is a physical `S+M` carrier, whereas
`T-B3` is only a retained response history. No physical inclusion map or
monotone resource order justifies the printed `T-B1 prec T-B3` relation.

This is a physics-facing stop, not a software stop. The apparatus is buildable;
