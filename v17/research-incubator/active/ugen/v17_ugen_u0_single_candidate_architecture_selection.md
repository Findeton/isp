## 3. Why the other live routes were not selected

### 3.1 Bare first-order $\Gamma$

A supplied Barandes law can be represented quantum-mechanically and may be
indivisible. The audited sources do not provide the uniform source map that
selects the complete law of a new source, interaction, reader, and control
family. Stable-record $\Gamma$ is also generic. Bare $\Gamma$ is therefore the
question CRP must answer, not a construction rule.

### 3.2 Per-setting unistochasticity or transition-probability geometry

Every finite stochastic matrix has broad Born/unistochastic representation
capacity, and the exact v17 PR control is globally unistochastic per setting.
Only one common product-local representation excludes that control. Supplying
that common representation would import the quantum composition rule.

Transition-probability geometry remains a useful secondary description, but
it was not selected as the parent because its physical member and composite
law are still free.

### 3.3 Purification and local tomography as axioms

These can conditionally reconstruct a quantum operational framework. They do
not specify the actual referent or source a new material member. If the
purifying environment is declared a complete restartable actual carrier, the
law becomes divisible on that enlarged carrier. If it is not complete, the
whole-process law is still missing.

### 3.4 Nelson, diffusion, and local fluctuation laws

N1/N1A are bounded prior-art controls. They inherit external time, continuous
trajectories, Euclidean configuration space, Brownian noise, and strong
global phase-sector debt. FRP additionally shows that detailed balance and
linear fluctuation-response structure do not source coherent quantum
composition.

### 3.5 Action, phase, holonomy, Hamiltonian, or pair kernel

These are excellent predictive compressors when one universal physical law
generates them. A target-specific version is information-equivalent to the
answer and remains a compiler. G1/G2 and the Gram/Gaussian controls already
establish that boundary.

### 3.6 Primitive pair-history or decoherence-functional law

This remains the leading complete predictive rival. It is not selected for
the one positive-parent slot because it is not an ordinary additive
probability law over fine actual histories. If CRP fails, the predictive
ranking may move toward this rival without proving it ontic.

### 3.7 Arbitrary contextual whole-program probability

An unrestricted map from program labels to record tables fits everything and
explains nothing. CRP must obtain context dependence from physical boundary,
interaction, reference, and reader relations through one law.
