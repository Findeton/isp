## 0. Executive verdict

Relativistic objective-collapse models remain the strongest published route in
the present v17 census for combining standard quantum predictive structure with
an explicit one-run stochastic actuality. They are therefore important physical
controls. They are not, at their audited scope, a completed matter law or a
Barandes-native derivation of quantum theory.

The exact source audit separates seven objects that must not be assembled into
one fictitious model:

1. Bedingham's 2010 **pointer-field QFT architecture** supplies a formal
   Lorentz-covariant Tomonaga stochastic law, a physical probability measure,
   a nonstandard pointer field, and two candidate local-beable prescriptions;
2. the 2011 **Poisson-hit outline** gives a simpler discrete-hit presentation of
   the same broad mechanism but leaves its smearing functions open;
3. the 2013 **past-light-cone matter-density prescription** supplies a
   primitive-ontology map only after an independently complete collapse law is
   given;
4. Pearle's 2014 **standard-scalar-field non-Markovian proposal** was withdrawn
   because its kernel was required to satisfy incompatible properties;
5. the 2019 **local scalar-field CSL model** has explicit relativistic collapse
   behavior but produces infinite energy and particles and is explicitly not an
   experimentally viable physical theory;
6. Bedingham's 2016 **collapse-outcome ontology** makes the stochastic outcomes
   primary and the wavefunction informational, but it is an interpretive and
   architectural synthesis rather than one source-complete QFT law; and
7. the 2025 **quantized-time finite-particle model** is Poincare covariant in
   $1+1$ dimensions and conserves energy in stochastic expectation, but it uses
   a supplied auxiliary evolution parameter, distinguishable fixed-number
   particles, and separately analyzed noncommuting collapse sectors rather than
   an interacting QFT process.

The family is constrained by a genuine physical theorem. Under the assumptions
of Markovian stochastic state evolution, relativistic causality, a stable vacuum,
and only standard QFT degrees of freedom, Myrvold proves that the stochastic
evolution must be deterministic. The theorem does **not** rule out genuinely
non-Markovian or whole-process laws, nonstandard degrees of freedom, or other
changes to its assumptions. It explains why the pointer field and non-Markovian
routes are serious responses rather than dispensable decoration. It does not
select either response as reality.

The source-bounded result is

## 4. RCF-PTR — Bedingham's pointer-field architecture

### 4.1 Supplied physical and mathematical structure

RCF-S1 assumes:

1. Minkowski spacetime $(M,\eta)$ and its spacelike hypersurfaces;
2. a conventional matter/gauge Hilbert space $\mathcal H_{\rm qft}$;
3. a Lorentz-scalar matter current $J(x)$;
4. a nonstandard pointer-field Fock space generated by $a(x)$ and $a^\dagger(x)$;
5. an initial quantum state, including pointer vacuum;
6. a classical spacetime Brownian field;
7. a collapse coupling $\lambda$; and
8. causal smearing functions $f$ and $g$.

The nonstandard field obeys

$$
[a(x),a^\dagger(y)]=\delta^4(x-y),
\qquad
[a(x),a(y)]=0.
\tag{4}
$$

Its degrees of freedom are indexed by all spacetime points rather than by a
configuration on one spacelike surface. Define

$$
n(y)=a^\dagger(y)a(y),
\tag{5}
$$

$$
N(x)=\int_M d^4y\,f(x,y)n(y),
\tag{6}
$$

and

$$
A(x)=\int_M d^4y\,g(x,y)
\bigl(a(y)+a^\dagger(y)\bigr).
\tag{7}
$$

The support choices are directed:

$$
f(x,y)\ne0\Longrightarrow y\in J^-(x),
\qquad
g(x,y)\ne0\Longrightarrow y\in J^+(x).
\tag{8}
$$

They make $N(x)$ record pointer excitations in the causal past of $x$, while
$J(x)A(x)$ deposits an excitation into the future-indexed pointer degrees of
freedom. The construction is unusual, but its causal roles are intelligible.

### 4.2 Ordinary-positive stochastic state law

Let $(\Omega,\mathcal F,Q)$ carry spacetime Brownian increments $dW_x$ with

$$
\mathbb E_Q[dW_x]=0,
\qquad
dW_xdW_y=\delta_{xy}\,d\omega_x.
\tag{9}
$$

For an advancing hypersurface, the unnormalized state satisfies

$$
d_x|\Phi(\sigma)\rangle=
\left[
-iJ(x)A(x)d\omega_x
-\frac{\lambda^2}{2}N(x)^2d\omega_x
+\lambda N(x)dW_x
\right]|\Phi(\sigma)\rangle.
\tag{10}
$$

The physical measure $P$ is defined by norm weighting. In conditional form,

$$
\mathbb E_P[X\mid\mathcal F_\sigma]
=
\frac{
\mathbb E_Q[X\langle\Phi(\sigma_f)|\Phi(\sigma_f)\rangle
\mid\mathcal F_\sigma]
}{
\mathbb E_Q[\langle\Phi(\sigma_f)|\Phi(\sigma_f)\rangle
\mid\mathcal F_\sigma]
}.
\tag{11}
$$

The state norm is asserted and formally shown to be a $Q$-martingale, making
the physical probabilities independent of how far the final surface is placed
beyond the region of interest. With the spacelike commutators imposed by (8),
two foliations sharing a leaf give the same state on that leaf for one fixed
initial state and one complete physical noise realization.

This is genuine ordinary probability. It is also a stochastic modification of
a supplied quantum-field law; it does not generate the quantum predictive
structure from a narrower ordinary-positive ontology.

### 4.3 Actuality is present but not uniquely typed

RCF-S1 offers two local-beable directions.

The first uses past-light-cone quantum expectations, for example

$$
\overline T_{\mu\nu}(x)
=
\langle T_{\mu\nu}(x)\rangle_{\operatorname{plc}(x)},
\tag{12}
$$

assuming that the light-cone limit and renormalized expectation exist.

The second uses the physical stochastic field integrated over bounded regions,

$$
W_R=\int_R dW_x.
\tag{13}
$$

The source calls the noise the physical stochastic information and describes
it as the more natural beable option, but it does not bind one unique ontology.
Equation (12) and equation (13) are not automatically two descriptions of one
identical material referent. An official control would have to freeze exactly
which object is actual and how detector records are functions of it.

### 4.4 The smearing law is an exhibited family, not selected physics

RCF-S1 proposes, illustratively,

$$
g(x,y)=C_g(x)
\exp\left[-k\overline T^{\mu\nu}(x)(y-x)_\mu(y-x)_\nu\right]
\mathbf 1_{J^+(x)}(y),
\tag{14}
$$

and an analogous past-supported $f$. It immediately states that many other
forms are possible. Since $\overline T^{\mu\nu}$ depends on the past-light-cone
state, the resulting model is non-Markovian relative to a state on an arbitrary
surface and exact calculation is difficult.

No source principle selects:

1. these functions rather than another causal pair;
2. the scale $k$;
3. a unique matter current $J$;
4. the collapse strength $\lambda$; or
5. one beable map.

Free physical constants are not themselves a defect. The issue is that the
functional law and referent are part of the model definition and are not yet
one frozen source object.

### 4.5 Process and existence scope

Equations (10)--(11) give a serious formal surface-indexed stochastic process.
The source does not prove a global existence-and-uniqueness theorem for the
state-dependent infinite-dimensional stochastic evolution with the exhibited
smearing functions. “The equations completely specify the dynamics” at the
formal physics level is not a mathematical well-posedness theorem.

This distinction matters because:

1. the coefficients contain unbounded QFT and pointer-field operators;
2. the state-dependent past-light-cone smearing is implicit;
3. renormalized stress-energy expectations are assumed;
4. realistic interacting QFTs are not constructed in the model; and
5. the physical-measure change relies on the norm martingale over the relevant
   domain.

The correct status is **formal stochastic process / global theorem absent**, not
“no process” and not “rigorously complete process.”

### 4.6 Interaction and QFT reach

The term $J(x)A(x)$ is a genuine interaction between conventional matter and
the pointer field. RCF-S1 says that interactions among conventional quantum
fields can be added and that scalar currents exist for bosons and fermions,
including in the presence of gauge interactions. Those statements establish
architectural compatibility, not a constructed Standard Model member.

The printed equation omits conventional field interactions. It supplies no
renormalized interacting gauge model, no source-to-reader calculation, and no
variable-number detector member. The QFT coordinate is therefore partial.

### 4.7 Energy statement

The use of smeared pointer operators avoids the immediate $\delta^4(0)$ energy
divergence of point collapse. This is a real success. The stronger total-energy
claim is conditional. The pointer Hamiltonian generates the required
translation of $A(x)$ only under the approximation

$$
\partial_{x^0}g(x,y)\simeq-\partial_{y^0}g(x,y),
\tag{15}
$$

which is motivated when the local stress-energy changes slowly on the smearing
scale. Under that approximation the matter--pointer interaction conserves a
total energy, and the collapse part conserves total energy in expectation. In
the point-smearing limit the divergence returns.

Therefore the source establishes finite formal rates for the smeared model and
an approximate total-energy balance, not a general exact conservation theorem
for a realistic interacting matter law.

### 4.8 Reader and empirical status

The source demonstrates collapse for an idealized two-branch matter-density
example and gives a simplified $1+1$ numerical illustration. It does not derive:

1. a fabricated detector's complete event distribution;
2. stable record and eraser maps;
3. a no-refit parameter transfer;
4. a model-specific present experimental exclusion region; or
5. a prediction that differs from R1 after independently measured inputs.

The collapse timescale and energy-transfer ideas are prospective observables.
They are not yet an empirical transfer result.

### 4.9 RCF-PTR verdict

RCF-PTR is the strongest audited member because it puts QFT-facing prediction,
ordinary probability, Lorentz-covariant stochastic actuality, and finite
smearing in one architecture. It does not yet meet the full same-object core
(3). A later bounded pin could choose one source-exhibited current, smearing
pair, parameter domain, and noise-beable interpretation and then ask whether
that exact mathematical object is coherent. Such a pin would test an
architecture, not certify a reality-grade matter law.

m(x)=

## 9. RCF-OUT — outcomes primary, wavefunction informational

RCF-S5 develops the ontological insight most relevant to ISP. It proposes that
collapse outcomes, not the hypersurface-dependent state, constitute matter in
spacetime. The state is then a conditional information object used to predict
future outcomes from past ones. On complete outcome histories, the underlying
probability law can be time-reversal symmetric even though forward state
updating appears asymmetric.

This provides a clean separation:

$$
\boxed{
\begin{array}{ll}
\text{actual layer} & =\text{collapse outcomes in spacetime},\\
\text{predictive layer} & =\text{quantum state conditioned on past outcomes}.
\end{array}}
\tag{20}
$$

The separation resembles the current v17 distinction between predictive
nomology and actuality. It is not a Barandes-native derivation:

1. the quantum state and collapse operators still generate the outcome law;
2. the examples use discrete times, Poisson events, or discretized spacetime;
3. discreteness is argued for physically but not derived from a form-neutral

4. the complete realistic QFT process is not constructed; and
5. the gravity discussion proposes possible classical sourcing but prints no
   constraint-compatible reciprocal dynamics.

The user-mandated form-neutrality wall is therefore binding. RCF-S5 may inspire
an outcome-first ontology, but it cannot make a discrete web, lattice, flash
sprinkling, or field of events fundamental without an independent physical
reason.

## 10. RCF-QTIME — quantized time and relational interval collapse

### 10.1 Model type

RCF-S9 considers $N$ distinguishable particles in $1+1$ dimensions. Each
particle has operators

$$
(\hat x_i,\hat t_i,\hat p_i,\hat E_i),
\tag{21}
$$

with

$$
[\hat x_i,\hat p_j]=i\delta_{ij},
\qquad
[\hat t_i,\hat E_j]=-i\delta_{ij}.
\tag{22}
$$

The state evolves in an auxiliary parameter $s$. For one collapse operator,

$$
d|\psi,s\rangle=
\left[
-i\hat Hds
-\frac{\lambda}{2}
(\hat A-\langle\hat A\rangle)^2ds
+(\hat A-\langle\hat A\rangle)dB_s
\right]|\psi,s\rangle.
\tag{23}
$$

The construction assigns equal probability weight to $s$ in a finite interval
and defines a spacetime density

$$
P(x,t)=\frac{1}{S}
\int_{-S/2}^{S/2}ds\,
|\langle x,t|\psi,s\rangle|^2.
\tag{24}
$$

This is a supplied auxiliary evolution parameter, not a derivation of time from
timeless physical facts.

### 10.2 Mass and relational configuration sectors

The model uses mass-sector operators

$$
\hat A_i=\hat p_i^2-\hat E_i^2
\tag{25}
$$

and relational interval operators

$$
\hat A_{ij}
=(\hat x_i-\hat x_j)^2-(\hat t_i-\hat t_j)^2.
\tag{26}
$$

The mass and interval families do not all commute. The paper explicitly calls
them competing. Its clean collapse calculations then isolate sectors and often
set $H=0$. Consequently, separate demonstrations of definite mass and definite
relational intervals are not a proof that the full noncommuting model converges
to a simultaneous exact eigenstate of both sets.

### 10.3 Relational rather than absolute localization theorem

Let $q_i=(x_i,t_i)$ and apply one global Poincare transformation

$$
q_i\longmapsto\Lambda q_i+a
\qquad\text{for every }i.
\tag{27}
$$

Every interval (26) is unchanged. Therefore any collapse law whose
configuration readers depend only on the set $\{A_{ij}\}$ is constant on global
Poincare orbits. It cannot select an absolute embedding or preferred frame.

This is not a failure if the intended ontology is relational. The maximum
legitimate claim is localization of a relational configuration class, with
possible discrete reflection and degeneracy ambiguities, not selection of one
absolute spacetime configuration.

The counting observation

$$
\binom N2\ge 2(N-1)
\tag{28}
$$

for $N\ge4$ is not by itself a rigidity theorem: it does not prove independence
of the constraints, generic uniqueness, or treatment of degenerate distance
data. A frozen successor would need an actual Minkowski-distance rigidity
statement rather than constraint counting alone.

### 10.4 Actuality, process, and physics scope

The stochastic state path and Brownian noise are explicit. Equation (24) is
interpreted as a density for where a particle is, but the source does not define
one realized worldline sampled from that density. Thus its one-run referent is
most clearly the collapsing state/noise pair, not an independent particle
trajectory.

The model has real positive features:

1. Poincare covariance at its $1+1$ scope;
2. norm-preserving stochastic state dynamics;
3. Born-rule collapse in analyzed sectors;
4. relational rather than absolute position collapse; and
5. conservation of energy in stochastic expectation.

Its binding limits are:

1. fixed $N$ distinguishable particles;
2. no creation, annihilation, indistinguishable fields, or gauge interactions;
3. particle interactions only suggested through a potential;
4. use of $H=0$ in the principal interval-collapse calculation;
5. possible negative mass-squared outcomes and stochastic backward motion in
   particle time relative to $s$;
6. no global $S\to\infty$ probability construction or mathematical existence
   theorem printed;
7. no detector/readout descent or empirical parameter transfer; and
8. no reciprocal gravity law.

RCF-QTIME is a high-information finite-particle construction, not a stronger
QFT matter-law candidate than RCF-PTR.

## 11. The Myrvold boundary and what it actually forces

RCF-S6 assumes a relativistic Markovian stochastic state evolution represented
by local completely positive maps, relativistic causal commutation, a unique
stable vacuum, and standard QFT degrees of freedom satisfying the spectrum
condition. Reeh--Schlieder reasoning then implies that local stochastic
alternatives acting on the vacuum are proportional on the standard Hilbert
space. The resulting state evolution is deterministic.

At the theorem's exact scope,

$$
\boxed{
\begin{gathered}
\text{MARKOVIAN RELATIVISTIC STOCHASTIC COLLAPSE}\
+\text{CAUSALITY}+\text{STABLE VACUUM}
+\text{STANDARD QFT DEGREES}\
\Longrightarrow\text{NO NONTRIVIAL STOCHASTIC STATE REDUCTION.}
\end{gathered}}
\tag{29}
$$

The theorem does not say that objective actuality is impossible. It identifies
where new physics must enter. At least one audited assumption must change, for
example:

1. add nonstandard degrees of freedom, as RCF-PTR does;
