## 6. Result-neutral outcome ladder

The finite-mathematics coordinate may end in exactly one of:

1. **THEOREM VERIFIED:** all stated finite claims survive;
2. **COUNTEREXAMPLE:** an explicit admitted matrix/category defeats a claim;
3. **ILL-TYPED:** the support, composition, gauge, or normalization contract
   is inconsistent; or
4. **SOURCE/SCOPE FAILURE:** mathematics survives but a physical
   interpretation exceeds its sources.

The generator coordinate may end in exactly one of:

1. **NATIVE MEMBER CONSTRUCTED:** one physical law reduces a registered input
   and passes held-out tests;
2. **ZERO-GAIN RECONSTRUCTION:** standard quantum data are re-encoded;
3. **CLASS-RELATIVE OBSTRUCTION:** one printed generator class fails with
   named escapes;
4. **EMPIRICAL DEVIATION:** a candidate makes and survives a new prediction;
5. **UNDERDETERMINED:** several members survive and no selector is supplied;
   or
6. **REFERENT INCOMPLETE:** the physical input contract cannot support the
   requested inference.

No result in the first coordinate automatically upgrades the second.
