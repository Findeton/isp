## 6. Frozen held-out families

These families are registered before any native candidate is written.

### H1 — exact algebraic continuations

The complete process must satisfy

$$
H^2=I,\qquad
T^2=S,\qquad
T^8=I,\qquad
\mathrm{CNOT}^2=I.
$$

The equality is at complete instrument scope, not merely on computational
endpoints.

### H2 — sign-sensitive phase continuation

Prepare $|+\rangle=H|0\rangle$. Compare $T$ with $T^\dagger$, then apply
$S^\dagger H$ and read $Z$. The quantum targets are

$$
p_0(T)=\frac{2+\sqrt2}{4},
\qquad
p_0(T^\dagger)=\frac{2-\sqrt2}{4}.
$$

This catches global conjugation only when a relative phase reference is held
fixed. Conjugating the complete world is recorded separately as a possible
presentation gauge.

### H3 — adaptive magic-state injection

Let

$$
|A\rangle=T|+\rangle.
$$

For an arbitrary data input $|\psi\rangle$, apply CNOT from data to the
ancilla, measure the ancilla in $Z$ with outcome $m$, and apply $S^m$ to the
data. Each branch has probability $1/2$, and after the correction the
record-erased channel equals direct application of $T$.

The candidate must reproduce the retained branches, the feedforward
instrument, and the erased-record channel from the same law.

### H4 — scalable GHZ phase parity

Prepare

$$
|\mathrm{GHZ}_n\rangle
=\frac{|0^n\rangle+|1^n\rangle}{\sqrt2},
$$

apply $T$ on every qubit, and read the product of $X$ outcomes. The even-parity
probability is

$$
p_{\rm even}(n)
=\frac{1+\cos(n\pi/4)}2.
$$

This tests uniform scaling and phase transport. A compact phase accumulator
is an admitted control, so this family alone cannot prove exponential memory.

### H5 — record, erase, and uncompute

A which-alternative record must suppress the corresponding interference when
it remains physically distinguishable. Coherent uncomputation before readout
must restore the interference. Operational discard without access to the
record must give the appropriate mixed channel.

The candidate may not identify coherent uncomputation with mere deletion of a
classical label.

### H6 — adaptive and tensor consistency
