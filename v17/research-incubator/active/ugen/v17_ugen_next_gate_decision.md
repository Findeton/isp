## 2. Why not broaden PC2 numerically

Moving immediately to a catalogue of larger complex Hadamard matrices would
classify more mathematical ambiguity without addressing physical selection.
The decisive new ingredient must be an independently meaningful composition
principle and a held-out prediction, not a larger fiber census.

Higher-dimensional and tensor fibers remain necessary controls, but they
should enter only after the PC3 selection contract has made clear what is
being selected and from which physical inputs.

---

## 3. Why not declare reconstruction complete

Operational reconstructions can derive the quantum framework from explicit
axioms. They do not by themselves supply:

1. the actual Hamiltonian or action;
2. interaction strengths and particle content;
3. contingent state or cosmological boundary data;
4. a stochastic-native generator of interacting parent laws;
5. internal time or causal geometry; or
6. a reason to identify one empirically equivalent representation as the
   ontology.

ISP should use reconstruction axioms as candidates in a selector tournament,
not treat the word “reconstruction” as completion.

## 5. Minimal PC3 grammar

The smallest useful grammar needs:

1. two reversible generators \(a,b\);
2. at least two operationally distinct paths with common typed endpoints;
3. one complete reader that separates some candidate extensions;
4. one actual record-insertion control;
5. a finite set of training relations;
6. at least one held-out closed word;
7. an endpoint-only positive source packet;
8. one quantum composition control;
9. one positive Markov control;
10. one alternative coherent control; and
11. one unrestricted direct-whole-law control.

The training relations must be motivated by independently calibrated physical
symmetry or interaction data. A purely abstract group presentation can
classify lifts but cannot claim to explain nature.
