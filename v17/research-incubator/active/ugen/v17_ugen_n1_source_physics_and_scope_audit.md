## 1. Primary source S1 — Nelson 1966

Edward Nelson,
[*Derivation of the Schrödinger Equation from Newtonian Mechanics*](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRev.150.1079),
*Physical Review* **150**, 1079--1085 (1966).

S1 explicitly postulates:

1. Brownian motion for a particle of mass $m$;
2. diffusion coefficient $\hbar/(2m)$;
3. no friction;
4. a Newtonian force law expressed through stochastic mean acceleration; and
5. continuous particle trajectories.

S1 derives the Schrödinger equation within its stated nonrelativistic scope
and describes its interpretation as classical in the sense of actual
trajectories and ordinary probability. It claims only a limited measurement
equivalence.

N1 does not claim novelty for this construction.

v17 process interface.

N1 therefore treats the variational route as a candidate physical motivation,
not as an empirical selection theorem.
