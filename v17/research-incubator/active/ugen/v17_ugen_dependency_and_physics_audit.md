## 3. The representation-invariance correction

A complete quantum evaluator can be written as:

- complex matrix multiplication followed by the Born rule;
- real matrix multiplication on a doubled carrier;
- tensor-network contraction;
- path summation;
- a direct recurrence for positive completed-record probabilities; or
- a lookup oracle.

The spelling does not determine the ontology or explanatory content. In
particular,

```text
NO EXPLICIT HILBERT SYMBOLS
```

is not a physical success criterion. Conversely, a Hilbert representation does
not prove that amplitudes are ontic.

The invariant accounting object is a dependency map from independently stated
physical inputs to the complete operational profile:

$$
\mathcal D_u
\longmapsto
\Phi_u:\mathcal E_{\rm reg}\to\mathsf{Instr}_{\rm op}.
$$

For each candidate, the audit must ask:

1. Which data are fixed nomology?
2. Which are contingent preparations, settings, and calibration records?
3. Which process-level quantities can be recovered from those inputs?
4. Which supposedly removed inputs have reappeared in a compiler, oracle,
   boundary condition, enlarged carrier, or response function?
5. Which changes alter registered predictions, and which are presentation
   gauge?

A candidate earns explanatory reduction only when a comparator input is
derived from older independently motivated premises without an equally strong
surrogate elsewhere.

## 4. Why the fixed quantum control is necessary but insufficient

The standard finite gate/Born evaluator is one uniform law for every finite
program in the grammar. Its completed transcript distribution is ordinary and
positive. Therefore it correctly belongs to the admissible class.

This proves:

$$
\text{uniform positive complete-record law exists}.
$$

It does not prove:

$$
\text{positive stochastic principles explain quantum composition}.
$$

The evaluator takes as nomological input precisely the structure that needs
explanation: complex gate action, tensor/sequential composition, instrument
semantics, and the Born valuation. Calling the final transcript law
“stochastic” changes the output representation, not the dependency graph.

This control is therefore the zero of explanatory gain. Any proposed ISP law
must be compared against it, not merely shown to reproduce it.

## 5. The calibration firewall

Gate names such as $H$, $T$, and CNOT are ambiguous unless their physical
calibration is printed.

### Endpoint-only calibration

If a primitive is specified only by basis-input/basis-output endpoint
probabilities, coherent continuations are not determined. E-Comp's candidate
example gives

$$
q(H)=q(V)=G,
\qquad
q(H^2)=I_2\ne X=q(V^2).
$$

The endpoint quotient is not a congruence for composition.

### Complete process calibration

If the primitive is specified by complete process tomography, phase-sensitive
continuations already distinguish $H$ from $V$. The missing composition datum
has then been supplied empirically.

Neither antecedent is wrong. They answer different questions. A U-Gen pin must
choose one and charge every datum. It may not use endpoint-only calibration
when claiming a derivation and silently switch to complete calibration when a
countermodel appears.

## 9. Current monolithic-pin defects

### D1 — set-sizedness dominates the wrong layer

Coding all Borel laws by reals makes quantifiers legal but does not constrain
nature. Keep the construction as appendix infrastructure.

### D2 — effective syntax is not physical explanation

One finite algorithm can contain the complete quantum nomology. Uniformity is
necessary to exclude per-instance fitting, but it does not derive the law.

### D3 — “non-Hilbert” is presentation dependent

Complex, realified, tensor-network, path-sum, and direct-positive evaluators
can represent the same complete profile. Classify independent inputs and
empirical invariants instead.

### D4 — gate labels can smuggle calibration

The primitive packet must say whether it contains endpoint statistics or full
process tomography.

### D5 — adequacy can make nonuniqueness tautological

Operationally separated extensions must be sought before imposing quantum
adequacy. Microscopic nonuniqueness inside the adequate subset needs a
different discriminator.

### D6 — code length is not a physical resource

Finite description length may establish anti-table uniformity. It cannot rank
ontologies or establish memory, energy, precision, or likelihood without an
independent bridge.

### D7 — supplied wiring is not endogenous causal structure

The current grammar's precedence and ports are laboratory inputs. They cannot
be promoted to chronology, locality, or spacetime.

### D8 — source absence is not a no-go

The audited Barandes papers supply fixed endpoint/parent laws at their stated
scope. Not locating a universal interacting-parent generator in those papers
does not prove that no such generator exists.

### D9 — one pin combines incompatible theorem burdens

Measurable class formation, source exegesis, a finite composition theorem,
scalable information bounds, generator construction, and locality/Tsirelson
selection require different inputs and hostile controls. One failure must not
trigger a repair chain across all of them.
