## 2. Quantum measure and histories sources

Supports:

## 3. Consistent/decoherent histories sources

### 3.1 Griffiths 1984

Source:

- Robert B. Griffiths, [*Consistent histories and the interpretation of quantum mechanics*](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01015734), *J. Stat. Phys.* **36**, 219–272 (1984).

The publisher abstract says that a consistency criterion permits classical
probability rules for selected multi-time event sequences and that the scheme
does not require wavefunction collapse or a fundamental measurement process.

Supports:

- the partition/family dependence of ordinary history probabilities;
- treating consistency as a probability-licensing condition.

Does not support:

- one preferred global history algebra;
- the claim that every version of consistent histories demands a dynamically
  selected realm;
- any C4 verdict against the approach.

### 3.2 Gell-Mann and Hartle

Sources:

- Murray Gell-Mann and James B. Hartle, [*Classical Equations for Quantum Systems*](https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/9210010), arXiv:gr-qc/9210010;
- Murray Gell-Mann and James B. Hartle, [*Quantum Mechanics in the Light of Quantum Cosmology*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1803.04605), arXiv:1803.04605v1.

The sources discuss decoherent sets, quasiclassical domains, coarse graining,
and probabilities for alternative histories. The 2018 abstract explicitly
states that probabilities can be assigned only to sets that approximately
decohere and identifies the possible inequivalence of quasiclassical domains
as an important question.

Supports:

- the approximate/coarse-grained character of physical history probabilities;
- printing a domain/family coordinate rather than presuming one unique fine
  algebra.

Does not support:

- exact decoherence in every laboratory model;
- a selected actuality ontology;
- a derived cosmological initial condition or action.

### Terminology warning

The literature uses several consistency/decoherence conditions. C4 fixes its
own exact vocabulary:

$$
\operatorname{Re}D(R_i,R_j)=0
$$

for weak probability consistency and

$$
D(R_i,R_j)=0
$$

for medium decoherence. Future reviewers must compare definitions, not labels.

### 5.1 Current source state

- Jacob Barandes, [*Quantum Systems as Indivisible Stochastic Processes*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.21192);
- Jacob Barandes, [*The Stochastic-Quantum Theorem*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.03085), with the v2 HTML used for equation-level inspection.

The first arXiv document is still active and its current HTML reports a later
manuscript date than its original submission. C4 does not freeze the moving

### 5.2 Exact supported source claims

The current indivisible-process source states that:

1. the ontology uses ordinary probability and configurations;
2. the minimalist law contains first-order transition probabilities

   $$
   \Gamma_{ij}(t\leftarrow t_0)=p(i,t\mid j,t_0);
   $$

3. a generic minimalist process admits a large or infinite family of complete
   Kolmogorov towers, called non-Markovian realizers;
4. the process therefore encompasses an equivalence class of realizers;
5. only the minimalist ingredients are connected to the quantum empirical
   predictions in the presented correspondence; and
6. the Hilbert/potential representation is nonunique and gauge-dependent.

The stochastic-quantum theorem source also states that an apparatus ends in
one measurement-reading configuration with Born-rule probability and
describes the system as following a highly non-Markovian configuration-space
trajectory.

### 5.3 Fair interpretation wall

The sources do not require that one empirically identify a privileged
realizer. They explicitly entertain its being unknowable or meaningless.
Therefore C4 must not conclude:

> “Barandes is inconsistent because he does not select a realizer.”

The exact open question is conditional:

- if only licensed target configurations are physically asserted, the theory
  has thin event actuality and no complete hidden path law is owed;
- if a complete fine trajectory with multi-time truth values is asserted, the
  minimalist first-order law does not generally determine its Kolmogorov
  tower.

This is a semantic fork to be clarified by source review, not a refutation.

### 5.4 What the sources do not currently establish for v17

The inspected sources do not supply, as a general theorem:

1. a uniform physical generator of all interacting process laws from fields,
   actions, and interaction primitives;
2. a unique microscopic configuration algebra selected by experiment;
3. a unique non-Markovian realizer;
4. internal operational time;
5. relativistic causal locality derived from indivisibility;
6. spacetime or gravity.

“Not supplied in the inspected sources” is not an impossibility theorem.
