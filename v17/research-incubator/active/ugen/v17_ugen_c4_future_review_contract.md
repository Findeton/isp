## 7. Finding classification

Every review finding must be classified as one of:

| Class | Meaning |
|---|---|
| mathematical-fatal | a proposition or proof is false at its printed scope |
| type-fatal | event algebra, partition, law, or actuality referent is undefined or inconsistent |
| source-fatal | a load-bearing named-source attribution reverses or invents the source claim |
| scope-fatal | conclusion exceeds finite, partition-relative, or conditional premises |
| empirical-fatal | an advertised discriminator is not operationally different or lacks the frozen model/data it claims |
| semantic-repair | wording can be narrowed without changing object, theorem, or branch classification |
| editorial | citation, notation, grammar, or formatting only |
| nonfinding | proposed issue is refuted by the frozen object |

Changing $\Omega$, $D$'s axioms, the decoherence condition, the actuality
referent, the parity control, or the maximum claim is semantic. It cannot be
passed off as editorial repair.
