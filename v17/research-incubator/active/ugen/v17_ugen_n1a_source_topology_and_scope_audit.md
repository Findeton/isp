## 0. Purpose

This audit asks whether N1A has found a physical origin for quantum phase
topology or only redescribed the Wallstrom condition.

It separates:

1. established stochastic-mechanics and quantum-topology results;
2. proposed repairs in the literature;
3. exact author-side reconstructions;
4. a contemporaneous unreviewed preprint with overlapping mathematics;
5. the new bounded locality theorem; and
6. claims that remain prohibited.

The controlling conclusion is:

> Local positive diffusion fields do not determine global circulation.
> Smooth value-space completion can motivate the $U(1)$ phase period, but a
> fixed global descent/character law remains independent. Within repairs that
> preserve the exact local N1 physics and add no new field, the surviving route
> must therefore be global or whole-process. Modified local physics remains a
> separately charged possibility.

## 1. Primary source S1 — Wallstrom 1989

Timothy C. Wallstrom,
[*On the derivation of the Schrödinger equation from stochastic
mechanics*](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF00696108),
*Foundations of Physics Letters* **2**, 113--126 (1989).

Wallstrom identifies the exact foundational standard used by N1A:

1. stochastic mechanics intends density and drift to be fundamental;
2. deriving a diffusion after solving the Schrödinger equation is not the
   deeper claim;
3. treating the phase potential as globally single-valued is an additional
   assumption; and
4. local stochastic equations are not automatically equivalent to the
   Schrödinger theory.

This source also stresses that the wave-like superposition structure needs
an independent physical justification. N1A does not claim that fixing
circulation alone supplies superposition.

## 2. Primary source S2 — Wallstrom 1994

Timothy C. Wallstrom,
[*Inequivalence between the Schrödinger equation and the Madelung

N1A narrows rather than enlarges that claim. It proves a bounded obstruction

## 3. Source S3 — modern mathematical audit of Madelung theory

Maik Reddiger and Bill Poirier,
[*Towards a mathematical theory of the Madelung equations: Takabayasi's
quantization condition, quantum quasi-irrotationality, weak formulations,
and the Wallstrom phenomenon*](https://doi.org/10.1088/1751-8121/acc7db),
*Journal of Physics A* **56**, 193001 (2023),
[arXiv:2207.11367](https://arxiv.org/abs/2207.11367).

The source is important for four reasons.

1. It proves Takabayasi's circulation condition for appropriate wavefunction
   regularity while exposing limits of extending that condition to weak or
   distributional solutions.
2. It constructs explicit non-quantized strong Madelung solutions.
3. It distinguishes local equations from the global state-space problem.
4. It concludes that a satisfactory mathematical theory may still be
   possible but requires more work.

N1A's circle witness is simpler and logically independent of nodal-vortex
regularity: its density and current are smooth and strictly positive. Thus no
claim about singular vortex cores can silently dispose of it.

N1A does not adjudicate the full proposal. It establishes one exact scope
limit:

$$
\rho=1/L>0

on the circle has no zeros, so every zero-only postulate is vacuous on that
member. Such a proposal may address nodal circulation on another carrier; it
does not generate a fixed character for every multiply connected node-free
carrier.

## 5. Sources S5--S7 — internal-periodic repair

Maaneli Derakhshani,
[*A Suggested Answer To Wallstrom's Criticism: Zitterbewegung Stochastic
Mechanics I*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1510.06391),
arXiv:1510.06391v6;
[*A Suggested Answer To Wallstrom's Criticism: Zitterbewegung Stochastic
Mechanics II*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1607.08838),
arXiv:1607.08838; and
[*Stochastic Mechanics Without Ad Hoc Quantization: Theory And Applications
To Semiclassical Gravity*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1804.01394),
doctoral thesis (2018).

The proposal adds a physical internal periodic motion with rest-frame
frequency

$$
\omega_c=mc^2/\hbar.
$$

Its claimed role is to make the stochastic velocity potential the phase of a
physical oscillator and thereby motivate the quantization condition. Part II
extends the construction to many particles with external and classical
interaction potentials.

N1A classifies this route fairly:

1. it genuinely escapes the strictly local theorem by adding an $S^1$
   referent;
2. it does not derive the action scale because $\hbar$ appears in the
   oscillator frequency;
3. the physical synchronization, joint-configuration phase, gauge, complete
   apparatus, relativistic, and empirical ledgers remain open; and
4. semiclassical Newtonian gravity is not reciprocal quantum gravity or a
   derivation of spacetime.

The proposal is a live candidate, not an accepted N1A result.

## 6. Source S8 — multiply connected path integrals

Michael G. G. Laidlaw and Cécile Morette DeWitt,
[*Feynman Functional Integrals for Systems of Indistinguishable
Particles*](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.3.1375),
*Physical Review D* **3**, 1375 (1971).

The source shows that multiply connected configuration spaces support
distinct propagators associated, in its scalar scope, with representations
of the fundamental group. It also relates the topology of indistinguishable-
particle configuration space to statistics.

This supports N1A's classification claim and simultaneously blocks a false
selection claim: topology supplies admissible sectors; it does not by itself
choose the physical representation in every system.

## 7. Source S9 — character quantization principle

Detlef Dürr, Sheldon Goldstein, James Taylor, Roderich Tumulka, and Nino
Zanghì,
[*Quantum Mechanics in Multiply-Connected
Spaces*](https://doi.org/10.1088/1751-8113/40/12/S08),
*Journal of Physics A* **40**, 2997--3031 (2007),
[arXiv:quant-ph/0506173](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0506173).

The source develops Bohmian dynamics on general Riemannian configuration
manifolds and classifies important topological theories using characters of
the fundamental group. It explicitly treats different characters as
different possible quantum theories.

For the N1A circle,

$$
\operatorname{Hom}(\mathbb Z,U(1))\cong U(1).
$$

Therefore topology alone leaves a continuous family. A fixed apparatus or
species needs one stable character, not a character chosen separately for
each state after its circulation is known.

## 8. Source S10 — geometry of the Madelung transform

Boris Khesin, Gerard Misiołek, and Klas Modin,
[*Geometry of the Madelung
transform*](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00205-019-01397-2),
*Archive for Rational Mechanics and Analysis* **234**, 549--573 (2019),
[arXiv:1807.07172](https://arxiv.org/abs/1807.07172).

This paper proves that the Madelung transform has symplectic, metric, and
Kähler properties and relates the density/phase description to projective
complex wavefunctions with Fisher--Rao/Fubini--Study geometry.

It is strong prior art for the statement that density and phase possess a
natural geometry closely related to complex quantum state space. It does not
by itself derive the stochastic ontology, the global phase sector, the
Hamiltonian member, or the actual path law.

N1A's finite-dimensional polar-target calculation is elementary and narrower.
No novelty is claimed for the general geometry of the Madelung transform.

## 13. Audit of the locality theorem

The theorem is intentionally conditional.

### Required premises

1. admissibility is determined on a good cover;
2. the rule uses only existing N1 fields;
3. every locally quantum-valid exact-current germ is preserved;
4. no global winding, phase, bundle, or boundary character is supplied; and
5. the region is node-free for the main corollary.

### Valid conclusion

The rule cannot impose a proper restriction on global periods.

### Invalid promotions

The theorem does not prove:

1. all stochastic mechanics fails;
2. all local differential equations fail;
3. nodes can never generate topology;
4. an internal phase is impossible;
5. a nonlocal positive law is impossible;
6. complex amplitudes are ontic; or
7. quantum theory uniquely follows from smoothness.

## 14. Audit of the smooth-target theorem

The exact result holds only in the printed category:

1. two local value coordinates $(r,\theta)$;
2. phase-translation homogeneity;
3. quotient by a closed subgroup of $\mathbb R$;
4. one phase-blind zero;
5. the N1 kinetic metric; and
6. smooth Riemannian, not orbifold or singular, completion.

Under those premises the orbit-length ratio fixes the angular period to
$2\pi$ and hence the action period to $2\pi\kappa$.

The result does not establish why nature chooses this target category. In
particular, it excludes by premise:

1. extra internal dimensions;
2. multiple zero states;
3. conical defects;
4. singular sources at zero density;
5. non-Riemannian target geometry; and
6. a noncompact phase line with a singular completion.

These exclusions are physically plausible only if independently defended.

## 15. Ontology audit

Three ontologies must remain distinct.

### O1 — density/current only

The actual referent is the stochastic path; $(\rho,v)$ are nomological or
ensemble fields. This ontology has no global phase map and fails the fixed-
sector circle gate.

### O2 — global complex state plus actual path

The state includes

$$
Z=\sqrt\rho e^{iS/\kappa}
$$

or a line-bundle section. This repairs global state space if its sector is
fixed. It is a pilot-wave/stochastic hybrid with a phase-complete field on
configuration space, not a reduction to bare ordinary probability.

### O3 — indivisible positive whole-process law

No global complex field is declared fundamental. Instead, one complete
positive law for the parent experiment encodes all loop, reference, boundary,
and record relations. This is closest to the Barandes/ISP ambition and lies
outside the local no-go. No such generator currently exists.

N1A selects none of O1--O3 empirically.

## 16. Physical interpretation

The deepest result is not “quantum mechanics needs complex numbers.” It is:

> Global phase is gluing information. A locally exact current specifies how
> neighboring patches relate, but its circulation records how those relations
> close around a loop. Patchwise dynamics cannot decide that closure unless a
> global object or whole-process law is present.

This is why all successful-looking repairs pay in one of three currencies:

1. **global field geometry** — phase maps, bundles, characters;
2. **new local physics** — node regularity, internal oscillators, preferred
   structures; or
3. **holistic nomology** — an indivisible law on the complete experiment.

The ledger is a physical distinction, not terminological bookkeeping.

## 17. QFT and gravity scope

The character classification becomes more consequential, not less, in QFT:

1. identical-particle configuration spaces carry statistics information;
2. gauge theories carry bundle topology and constraints;
3. particle creation changes fixed-$N$ configuration spaces;
4. anomalies can obstruct simultaneous symmetries; and
5. interacting continuum measures require renormalization.

N1A contains none of the required QFT construction.

Gravity further makes geometry and possibly topology dynamical. A fixed
configuration manifold and its chosen character cannot be promoted to
spacetime ontology. A credible gravity successor would need a covariant sum or
law over global structures, constraint closure, internal time, and reciprocal
matter--geometry response.

The smooth-cone calculation has no direct gravitational consequence.
