unit-modulus factor and leaves $\Gamma$ invariant.

### 8.2 Coherent gluing

Multiplicative magnitude and character data make path concatenation equal
matrix multiplication of $K$. Modulus squaring does not commute with that

### 8.3 Two-port member

The real rotation $K(\theta)$ is orthogonal and produces the exact
$\cos^2/\sin^2$ transition law. The example is finite, normalized, positive,
and explicitly nondivisible.

### 8.4 Normalization obstruction

for every boundary vector requires the full identity

$$
K^\dagger K=I.

kernels are not closed under coherent composition.

1. no fine path is asserted;

non-Markovian realizers; it shows that their complete contextual joint law is
