## 4. The six principles and their exact physical content

### 4.1 QP1 — causality

The probability of an outcome in a preparation test is independent of which
observation test is chosen later. Equivalently, every system has one unique
deterministic effect $u_A$.

For every normalized state $\omega$,

$$
u_A(\omega)=1.
\tag{6}
$$

**v17 interpretation.** This is the operational prefix condition for an
already ordered program: once a retained earlier record exists, later freely
chosen extensions do not change its marginal.

**It does not establish.** A fundamental arrow of time, a global clock, a
spacetime causal order, or the absence of boundary-conditioned physics.

**Executable falsifier.** Randomize a later reader or extension only after an
earlier record is irreversibly stored. A statistically reproducible change in
the earlier complete-record marginal, after source correlations and leakage
are excluded, violates QP1 for that intervention grammar.

### 4.2 QP2 — perfect distinguishability

Every state that is not completely mixed can be perfectly distinguished from
at least one other state.

**v17 interpretation.** For any operational preparation class outside the
interior/full-support class, some independently executable preparation and
reader separate the two with disjoint complete-record support.

**Physical debt.** This is an exact universal availability statement. A
finite experiment can falsify a proposed discriminator but cannot verify the
principle over an unbounded state space. It is weaker than a general
no-restriction hypothesis, but it still asserts the existence of suitable
physical readers.

### 4.3 QP3 — ideal compression

Every information source admits an encoding and decoding that are both:

1. lossless on the entire face generated by the source; and
2. maximally efficient, so every state of the encoding system represents a
   source-face state.

If $\mathcal E:A\to C$ and $\mathcal D:C\to A$, then

$$
\mathcal D\mathcal E\sigma=\sigma
\quad\text{for all }\sigma\in F_\rho,
\qquad
\mathcal E( F_\rho)=\mathsf{St}_1(C).
\tag{7}
$$

**v17 interpretation.** Prepare every registered refinement in a bounded
source face, encode, decode, and compare the full raw-record law with the
direct control; separately test that the code system contains no unused
operational states.

**Physical debt.** Exact losslessness and minimality are ideal-law claims.
They require a supplied family of encoders, decoders, and code systems and
carry nontrivial implementation and resource costs.

### 4.4 QP4 — local distinguishability

Any two different bipartite states are distinguished by at least one product
experiment. Equivalently, local statistics tomographically determine the
joint state:

$$
D_{AB}=D_A D_B.
\tag{8}
$$

**v17 interpretation.** Once a physical subsystem factorization and local
reader grammar have independently been established, no joint operational
degree of freedom is forever invisible to all products of local readers.

**Why it matters.** In the `CDP` proof this is the premise that, together with
the other principles, selects complex rather than real quantum state spaces.
It is therefore not harmless bookkeeping. Real quantum theory violates local
tomography in its usual composite construction.

**Physical debt.** The subsystem split, product-reader equivalence, and
absence of permanently global-only observables are physical hypotheses. A
pregeometric theory may not possess them fundamentally.

### 4.5 QP5 — pure conditioning

Conditioning one side of a pure bipartite preparation on an atomic outcome
leaves a pure conditional state on the other side.

**v17 interpretation.** For a preparation certified operationally extremal
and a reader outcome with no nontrivial refinement, tomography of the
conditional remote preparation admits no nontrivial convex refinement.

**Physical debt.** Purity and atomicity are defined relative to the complete
licensed test category. A hidden restriction of readers can make a mixed
physical situation appear operationally pure. The claim therefore depends on
experiment completeness.

### 4.6 QP6 — purification

Every state is the marginal of a pure state of a larger system, and for
a fixed purifying system any two purifications are connected by a reversible
transformation acting only on that system:

$$
\operatorname{marg}_B\Psi_\rho=\rho,
\qquad
\Phi_\rho=(\mathcal I_A\otimes\mathcal U_B)\Psi_\rho.
\tag{9}
$$

Purification is the specifically quantum postulate in the `CDP` route.

**v17 interpretation.** For each operational preparation, construct
an extremal common-parent preparation with the given marginal. For any two
such parent preparations on the same declared purifying system, construct a
reversible environment-side procedure that maps one to the other while
preserving all joint complete-record statistics.

**Physical content.** This is a conservation-of-information principle: every
operationally irreversible process admits an essentially unique reversible
system--environment realization. It aligns with v17's information-placement
theorem, but does not identify the physical environment or where the retained
distinction resides.

**Empirical ceiling.** One counterexample can refute exact purification.
Finite noisy data cannot establish its universal exact form. Approximate
purification and stability of the reconstruction require separate theorems;
they are not inherited here.
