## 7. Repair classification

### Editorial repairs

Spelling, rendering, cross-reference, and algebraically inert notation defects
may be repaired if the frozen object and every result value remain unchanged.
Every repair must be enumerated and the bytes rehashed.

### Semantic repairs

The following require a new pin and fresh authority:

1. changing a calibration track;
2. changing the programme grammar;
3. changing a held-out programme or target;
4. changing the candidate law or candidate class;
5. adding a resource premise;
6. altering the product or composition rule;
7. adding an actuality rule;
8. weakening a fatal control; or
9. changing a claim ceiling.

There is no automatic repair chain.
