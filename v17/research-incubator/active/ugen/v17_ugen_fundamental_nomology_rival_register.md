## 1. Rival A — ordinary-positive indivisible whole-history law

### Fundamental object

An ordinary probability law on complete physical histories, configurations,
or records, with no requirement that unresolved intermediate cuts admit a
positive stochastic restart.

### Required achievement

One uniform law must generate complete quantum operational profiles across
system size, programme depth, adaptation, composition, retained/erased
records, and held-out continuations without receiving the target quantum
process or an equivalent answer table.

### Current evidence

Positive record-history representations and Barandes-style first-order laws
are mathematically coherent. No native scalable interacting generator has been
constructed in v17.

### Kill condition

If the candidate merely encodes the complete quantum process in its state,
context, response functions, parent law, nonuniform advice, or oracle, it earns
representation but no explanatory reduction.

---

## 2. Rival B — strongly positive pair-history nomology

### Fundamental object

A normalized strongly positive decoherence functional

$$
D(A,B)
$$

or an equivalent non-Kolmogorov quantum measure structure. Ordinary record
probabilities arise on decoherent/consistent subalgebras.

### Required achievement

The programme must generate the history carrier, $D$, composition, physical
record algebra, and actuality/truth account from independently calibrated
inputs. Supplying standard amplitudes and Born pairing is a comparator, not a
derivation.

### Current evidence

C1--C3 show that diagonal event data are not compositionally complete, that
$D$ contains weights plus relational correlations, and that additive scalar
action with $U(1)$ transport yields an exponential character. These are
diagnostic lemmas. They do not generate nature's $D$.

### Kill condition

If $D$ is simply the standard quantum process rewritten on histories, the
programme is a coherent formulation of quantum mechanics but not yet a deeper
law or selected ontology.

---

## 3. Rival C — modified actualization

### Fundamental object

Standard quantum predictive structure plus a new collapse, trajectory,
coevent, noise, or other actualization law.

### Required achievement

The added law must specify its actual referent, stochastic dynamics,
parameters, conservation/covariance behavior, composition, and at least one
frozen empirical wedge.

### Current evidence

Objective-collapse theories show that this kind of programme is logically and
experimentally meaningful. v17 has not derived or selected such a law.

The Bell-current comparator supplies a positive actual path without modifying
ordinary quantum predictions at equilibrium, but imports the quantum current,
configuration observable, external time, and least-traffic rule. It is a
control, not a native Rival C discovery.

### Kill condition

An empirically equivalent interpretation cannot be promoted to new physics
merely because its ontology is vivid. A modification fitted after observing
the target data is also not a prediction.

## 5. What can change the ranking

A rival can gain standing only by at least one of:

1. shrinking an empirical-equivalence class;
2. deriving a previously independent input from narrower physical premises;
3. proving a class-wide invariant or no-go;
4. predicting a held-out operational profile without target import;
5. improving a fully charged scaling/resource bound; or
6. producing a frozen empirical deviation.

Simplicity, familiarity, classical visualizability, complex notation,
mathematical elegance, or philosophical preference do not decide the contest
without a fixed and physically justified scoring rule.
