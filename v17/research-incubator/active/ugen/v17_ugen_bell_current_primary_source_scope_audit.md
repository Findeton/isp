## 0. Purpose

This audit binds the Bell-current comparator to its published sources and
separates four kinds of statement:

1. a published theorem or formula;
2. an elementary finite specialization reconstructed in v17;
3. an interpretive comparison;
4. an open physical claim.

The comparator may not claim novelty for the minimal jump-rate formula, its
standard-current minimality, Bell-type QFT, Bohmian configuration ontology, or
Nelson-type stochastic alternatives.

## 1. Load-bearing source S1

Detlef Dürr, Sheldon Goldstein, Roderich Tumulka, and Nino Zanghì,
[*Quantum Hamiltonians and Stochastic Jumps*](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0303056v3),
arXiv:quant-ph/0303056v3, revised 15 July 2004; published in
*Communications in Mathematical Physics* **254**, 129--166 (2005),
[DOI 10.1007/s00220-004-1242-0](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00220-004-1242-0).

### S1 supplies

S1 equation (1) gives the measure-theoretic minimal rate formula

$$
\sigma(dq\mid q')=
\frac{
\left[(2/\hbar)\operatorname{Im}
\langle\Psi|P(dq)HP(dq')|\Psi\rangle\right]^+}
{\langle\Psi|P(dq')|\Psi\rangle}.
$$

S1 equations (12)--(13) define equivariance through the forward generator:

$$
\mathcal L_t|\Psi_t|^2
=\frac{d|\Psi_t|^2}{dt}.
$$

S1 equations (87)--(90) state that the standard-current property is stronger
than equivariance: equivariance equates the relevant marginals/divergences,
whereas standard-current matching equates the full antisymmetric current.

S1 equations (91)--(96) introduce the nonnegative traffic measure

$$
C(dq\times dq')=\sigma(dq\mid q')\rho(dq')
$$

and give the general standard-current solution

$$
C=\mathbb J^+ + S,
\qquad
S\ge0,
\qquad
S=S^{\rm tr}.
$$

It then proves that the minimal rates are the least-jump member of this class.
The source explicitly notes that one direction on each unordered pair is
forbidden for the minimal member.

S1 also states rather than hides the model inputs:

1. a well-defined Hamiltonian is assumed, often after cutoffs;
2. a position/configuration observable is assumed chosen when alternatives
   exist;
3. the state vector and external time evolution are supplied; and
4. global process existence is nontrivial.

### S1 does not supply

S1 does not derive the Hamiltonian, configuration observable, Born law, or
external time from ordinary-positive principles. It does not claim that
equivariance alone selects its standard current. It does not supply an ISP- or
Barandes-native indivisible generator, quantum gravity, or a passive
experimental discriminator among all equivariant path laws.

## 2. Global-existence source S2

Hans-Otto Georgii and Roderich Tumulka,
[*Global Existence of Bell's Time-Inhomogeneous Jump Process for Lattice Quantum Field Theory*](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0312294v2),
arXiv:math/0312294v2, revised 31 March 2004; published in
*Markov Processes and Related Fields* **11**, 1--18 (2005).

S2 proves existence and uniqueness for Bell's lattice-QFT jump process under
suitable assumptions on the Hamiltonian or initial state. Its non-explosion
proof uses the special $|\Psi_t|^2$ distribution.

S2 is important because it blocks two opposite mistakes:

1. singular denominators do not automatically make every Bell process
   ill-defined; and
2. an instantaneous finite-rate formula does not automatically establish
   global existence for every Hamiltonian and state.

The v17 finite theorem remains support-relative and does not import S2's
global conclusion outside S2's hypotheses.

## 3. QFT construction source S3

Detlef Dürr, Sheldon Goldstein, Roderich Tumulka, and Nino Zanghì,
[*Bell-Type Quantum Field Theories*](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0407116v1),
arXiv:quant-ph/0407116v1; published in *Journal of Physics A* **38**, R1
(2005),
[DOI 10.1088/0305-4470/38/4/R01](https://doi.org/10.1088/0305-4470/38/4/R01).

S3 describes explicit Bell-type processes for broad regularized QFTs,
including composition of free and interaction generators and particle-number
changing configurations.

It supports the limited statement that the comparator is not confined to one
finite toy. It does not support unregularized universal QFT, manifest Lorentz
covariance, a derived configuration observable, or gravity.

## 5. Bohm and Nelson comparison sources

### S5 — Bohm

David Bohm,
[*A Suggested Interpretation of the Quantum Theory in Terms of “Hidden” Variables. I*](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRev.85.166),
*Physical Review* **85**, 166--179 (1952), and
[*Part II*](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRev.85.180), **85**, 180--193 (1952).

S5 is the primary comparator for an actual configuration guided by a
phase-complete quantum law in nonrelativistic mechanics.

### S6 — Nelson

Edward Nelson,
[*Derivation of the Schrödinger Equation from Newtonian Mechanics*](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRev.150.1079),
*Physical Review* **150**, 1079--1085 (1966).

S6 postulates Brownian trajectories with diffusion coefficient
$\hbar/(2m)$ and gives a stochastic-mechanics route to Schrödinger dynamics
within its stated scope. It is relevant as a concrete alternative equivariant
trajectory construction. It prevents v17 from presenting stochastic
configuration paths as unique to Bell minimal rates.

The comparator does not adjudicate the full empirical or mathematical status
of Nelson stochastic mechanics.

## 6. Barandes scope sources

### S7

Jacob A. Barandes,
[*The Stochastic-Quantum Theorem*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.03085v2),
arXiv:2309.03085v2, revised 5 February 2026.

### S8

Jacob A. Barandes,
[*Quantum Systems as Indivisible Stochastic Processes*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.21192),
arXiv:2507.21192. The existing E-Comp source reconstruction freezes its
equation-level audit to the retrieved v1 bytes; the public source is moving and
must be version-frozen again before any official comparison.

These sources support the following scope statements:

1. Barandes assigns fundamental importance to ordinary probability,
   configuration trajectories, and first-order laws;
2. indivisible laws need not factor through arbitrary intermediate times;
3. Hilbert-space structure can be used as secondary representation; and
4. supplied quantum dynamics can be translated into stochastic language.

They do not license identifying a Bell Markov generator with the generic
indivisible law class. They also do not remove the v17 question of how a single
physical member, its interactions, and its complete parent laws are selected
without supplying the target quantum process.

## 10. Novelty and claim ceiling

The strongest permissible source-conditioned claim is:

> The published Bell-type current construction gives a complete positive
> trajectory comparator conditional on standard quantum inputs. Its finite
> specialization makes explicit that Born equivariance fixes only current
> divergence, standard-current matching fixes edgewise net current but leaves
> symmetric counter-traffic, and the minimal Bell law adds a least-traffic
> selection. This diagnoses the input burden of an actuality law; it does not
> derive a native ISP generator or select an ontology.

The package may not claim:

1. discovery of Bell's rate law or minimality;
2. uniqueness from Born probabilities;
3. empirical observation of the hidden path;
4. derivation of Barandes indivisibility;
5. unregularized or manifestly covariant QFT;
6. internal time; or
7. quantum gravity.
