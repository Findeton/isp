## 2. Independent algebra rebuild

### 2.1 Flat fiber

For a flat $2\times2$ unitary, orthogonality gives

$$
a+d-b-c\equiv\pi\pmod{2\pi},
$$

which is exactly the condition required for $U=LHR$. No classification
theorem from the literature is needed.

### 2.2 Chain product

Expanding

$$
(L_mHR_m)\cdots(L_1HR_1)
$$

places $S_j=R_{j+1}L_j$ at each internal boundary. Left and right diagonal
unitaries disappear after entrywise modulus squares. Conversely, taking
$U_1=H$ and $U_{j+1}=HS_j$ realizes any seam tuple. The normal form is both
necessary and sufficient inside the registered flat-unitary class.

### 2.3 Gauge

The decomposition redundancy

$$
(L_j,R_j)\mapsto(z_jL_j,z_j^{-1}R_j)
$$

changes only the scalar part of adjacent seams. A consistent internal basis
change inserts $D^{-1}D$ and leaves the seam itself unchanged. The candidate
therefore does not mistake the phase convention of one isolated matrix for
the relational composition datum.

### 2.4 Homogeneous classifier

The two-step law contains

$$
x=\cos^2(\phi/2).
$$

Equal $x$ means $\psi\equiv\pm\phi$ modulo $2\pi$. The minus choice complex
conjugates a product built from real $H$, so all positive endpoints agree.
This proves that $x$ classifies the complete positive sequence only inside
the homogeneous balanced-unitary subclass.

### 2.5 Delayed control

At $\phi=\pi/2$, Cayley--Hamilton yields

$$
(S_{\pi/2}H)^2=iS_{\pi/2}H+I_2.
$$

Multiplication by $H$ produces a diagonal unitary, hence
$\Gamma_3=I_2$, while $\Gamma_1=\Gamma_2=G$. This is an exact control against
the inference that matching one additional depth proves Markov composition.

### 2.6 Rank wall

If $x\ne1/2$, $\Gamma_2$ has rank two. If $x=1/2$, $\Gamma_3=I_2$ has rank
two. Since every $KG$ has rank at most one, every homogeneous member is
nondivisible at depth two or three through the printed carrier. The statement
does not survive an unprinted-carrier change without a new proof.

## 3. Why this is not yet an explanation of quantum theory

The candidate starts with

$$
U_j\in U(2),
\qquad q(U_j)=G.
$$

It then classifies what survives when endpoint phases are quotiented out.
That is a valid inverse/completion theorem. It does not answer why the
universe should be described by unitary composition in the first place.

The explanatory debt is therefore visible rather than hidden:

1. **law-class debt:** why coherent unitary lifts rather than another
   ordinary-positive whole-law class;
2. **seam-selection debt:** why one relational seam rule rather than another;
3. **scaling debt:** how the rule extends across dimension, system number,
   and depth;
4. **interaction debt:** how entangling parent laws arise;
5. **calibration debt:** which seam data are inferred from laboratory
   continuations and which are derived nomologically; and
6. **resource debt:** what physical carrier, precision, or communication is
   required by a stochastic-native realization.

Calling $\phi$ a connection, holonomy, memory, or phase does not pay these
debts. Those names become physical only after their transformation law,
composition rule, operational readers, and empirical consequences are fixed.
