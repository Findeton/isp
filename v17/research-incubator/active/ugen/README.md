embedding $\iota_0$. Classify the uniform complete extensions

$$
\mathsf{Ext}_{\rm src}(\iota_0)

$$
\mathsf{Ext}_{Q}(\iota_0).
$$

printed: endpoint-only calibration and full process calibration are different
antecedents.

For a scalable family, require one law across system number, circuit depth,
context, adaptive intervention, and held-out continuation. Charge information

$$
(R_{\rm pred},R_{\rm mem},R_{\rm ctx},R_{\rm comm},

theory”:

1. stabilizer/Clifford processes;
2. one controlled non-Clifford resource;
3. universal finite-dimensional processes;
4. finite-memory non-Markovian instruments/process tensors; and
5. only later, relativistic local-net families.

### U-Gen C — constructive contest

Give the positive indivisible ontology its strongest fair test: construct one
uniform native generator for the complete registered process interface.
Success must show where the phase-sensitive and adaptive composition capacity
resides without merely renaming the Hilbert process table.

Failure must identify an unavoidable input rather than announce that
“positivity failed”:

- noncommutative predictive state;
- whole-program context or global boundary data;
- communication or future-setting dependence;
- nonuniform law advice;
- enlarged physical carrier with charged resources; or
- empirical deviation.

5. A Hilbert/process representation receives no explanatory credit unless its
   physical principle and additional information are independently stated.
6. No clock, causal order, QFT, spacetime, or gravity conclusion follows from
   a composition classifier.
