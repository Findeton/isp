## 1. What the primary sources establish

### 1.1 Barandes: configurations and law precede Hilbert representation

Barandes explicitly separates a model's fixed configuration space, fixed
indivisible transition laws, and contingent standalone probabilities. He also
argues that Hilbert-space objects can be secondary representations of the
stochastic dynamics, and that dynamical symmetries may be imposed as
constraints on construction of an indivisible process.

This supports three U0 requirements:

1. distinguish kinematics, contingent state, and nomology;
2. formulate active physical transformations at configuration level; and
3. test symmetry before optional Hilbert dilation.

It does **not** supply the missing map from target-blind physical source
descriptors to a uniform complete stochastic law. Nor does it show that the
configuration carrier should be discrete, continuous, spatial, a trajectory
space, or relational in the specific sense developed here.

### 1.2 Bartlett--Rudolph--Spekkens: reference information is physical

Their reference-frame analysis makes a physically important distinction
between an abstract bit string and information such as a direction or clock
relation that can be carried only by suitable physical degrees of freedom.
They emphasize that directions and phases are defined relative to other
systems, not to absolute Newtonian space or time; that reference frames have
finite precision, can degrade, and cost resources; and that losing a shared
reference produces an averaging or twirling restriction.

The U0 lesson is not quantum formalism. It is:

> A reference is a physical subsystem with a preparation, transformation
> rule, coupling, reader, precision, provenance, and cost. It is not a free
> coordinate convention.

The source's Hilbert spaces, unitary group representations, $U(1)$ phase
references, $SO(3)$ frames, superselection rules, and quantum-resource
monotones are not inherited.

### 1.3 Gour--Spekkens: asymmetry can be a resource rather than a law

The resource theory of quantum reference frames studies states that overcome
restrictions caused by lacking a reference. Its durable U0 lesson is that a
prepared asymmetric reference state can be a contingent, consumable resource
while the allowed laws remain covariant.

That distinction prevents two opposite mistakes:

1. treating every observed asymmetry as a fundamental asymmetric law; and
2. averaging away a physically prepared reference because the underlying law
   is symmetric.

The paper begins inside quantum theory and specified symmetry groups. It does
not derive the U0 configuration object or native law.

### 1.4 Giacomini--Castro-Ruiz--Brukner: the frame belongs inside physics

Their quantum-reference-frame construction starts from the observation that
laboratory frames are idealizations of physical systems and studies changes
between such frames without an external absolute frame. This reinforces the
constituent/reference distinction below.

Its quantum state space, canonical variables, background spacetime examples,
and unitary transformations are controls, not U0 premises. In particular, it
does not authorize gravity to select the matter ontology.

## 2. Four distinctions that a native search must preserve

### 2.1 Presentation label versus physical reference

A label is changed without changing any physical preparation, control,
reader, or record. It belongs to the neutral groupoid of source descent.

A physical reference can instead be:

1. prepared in distinguishable conditions;
2. transformed or compared by licensed interventions;
3. coupled to a system or reader;
4. recorded with finite resolution and noise; and
5. depleted, drifted, erased, isolated, or replaced.

If none of those operations exists, a purported reference token is an opaque
identifier and receives no physical credit.

### 2.2 Absolute background versus physical constituent

An absolute background is supplied without dynamics, provenance, response,
or resource cost and is not acted on by the same presentation changes as the
rest of the experiment.

A physical constituent is included in the joint configuration and experiment
interface. Its state is contingent, its transformations are typed, and its
records are part of the complete transcript. A law can be invariant under a
joint change of presentation while predictions depend on the constituent's
relation to another system.

### 2.3 Covariant law versus asymmetric state

Let $g$ denote a verified simultaneous transformation of system and
reference. The law may obey

$$
\mathcal N(g\cdot x,g\cdot r)=g_*\mathcal N(x,r)
$$

while a prepared reference distribution $\sigma_R$ is not invariant. The
former is nomological covariance; the latter is contingent asymmetry. They
must occupy different ledger coordinates.

### 2.4 Relative variable versus quantum phase

A relative variable is any experimentally grounded invariant of a joint
system--reference configuration. It need not be an angle, group element,
complex phase, coordinate difference, or holonomy.

Recovering a relative variable therefore supplies no license to set its value
in $U(1)$, exponentiate an action, introduce a complex amplitude, or identify
it with the quantum phase sector. G1 remains a compiler/control.

For a system type $S$, augment a target-blind physical source packet only when
the following fields are available:

$$
\mathfrak R_S=
(R,\Sigma_R,\mathsf{Prep}_R,\mathsf{Ctrl}_R,
 \mathsf{Compare},\mathsf{Read}_R,\mathsf{Prov}_R,
 \mathsf{Cost}_R).
$$

Here:

1. $(R,\Sigma_R)$ is an abstract measurable reference-configuration object;
2. $\mathsf{Prep}_R$ states which reference conditions can be prepared;
3. $\mathsf{Ctrl}_R$ gives active interventions on the reference;
4. $\mathsf{Compare}$ gives physical system--reference or reference--reference
   comparison procedures;
5. $\mathsf{Read}_R$ gives retained reference records and resolution;
6. $\mathsf{Prov}_R$ states how the object and calibrations were obtained; and
7. $\mathsf{Cost}_R$ records size, precision, stability, degradation,
   communication, shared preparation, and refresh costs.

The joint source object is not assumed to be a Cartesian product
fundamentally. For a bounded fixture, a declared measurable joint object

$$

$$

and measurable projections or reader maps may be used. The candidate must
state whether $C_{SR}=C_S\times R$ is physics, a benchmark convenience, or an
effective factorization.

No external time is hidden in the word `Prep` or in a transcript index. The
laboratory schedule remains an intervention descriptor until an internal
clock law is separately earned.

## 4. Reference-admission tests

A proposed reference field passes U0-T3 only if all of the following hold.

### R1 — physical contrast

At least two reference conditions are distinguished by a target-blind
preparation, comparison, or reader procedure.

### R2 — joint descent

Verified presentation changes act on the system, reference, controls, and
records together. The complete prediction descends under that joint action.

### R3 — intervention covariance

Active transformations and passive redescriptions are not conflated. The
candidate prints how each licensed physical intervention changes the joint
configuration and transcript.

### R4 — non-equivalent input

The reference packet does not determine the held-out process before the
proposed nomological rule acts. It must pass U0-T2's advice-recovery,
tomography, opaque-code, and calibration-fiber attacks.

### R5 — provenance and transfer

The same reference definition is fixed before the no-refit implementation or
member is opened. A serial number, laboratory identity, or target-specific
calibration table cannot choose the output law.

### R6 — resource accounting

Precision, stability, size, drift, degradation, refresh, communication, and
shared-source costs are measured or bounded. An ideal infinite reference is a
declared control, not a free primitive.

### R7 — reference-removal control

The fixture includes a version in which the reference is randomized,
unshared, unread, erased, or replaced. The candidate must predict the change
without being told the target answer.

### R8 — common-source control

Correlations caused by a shared reference are separated from interaction,
communication, and indivisible joint dynamics.

### R9 — ontology neutrality

Passing the reference tests does not promote the reference carrier, group,
topology, dimension, or apparatus architecture to fundamental ontology.
