## 7. Theorem SCR-C — record laws do not select microscopic happenings

### Statement

Let $P$ be any probability law on a finite stable-record alphabet
$\mathcal R$. For every finite latent alphabet $\mathcal Z$ and every family
of conditional laws $Q(z\mid r)$,

$$
P_Q(r,z)=P(r)Q(z\mid r)
\tag{15}
$$

is a normalized positive refinement with the same record marginal $P$.
Distinct choices of $Q$ can give inequivalent latent histories while leaving
all present records unchanged.

### Proof

Positivity is immediate and

$$
\sum_zP_Q(r,z)
=P(r)\sum_zQ(z\mid r)

\tag{16}

positive-probability record fiber. $\square$

### Scope

distinguish them. That reader and its coupling are precisely the additional
