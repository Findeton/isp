## 4. Audit of the six principles

### QP1 — causality

Every system has one deterministic effect, and a retained earlier outcome is
independent of which later freely chosen test is appended.

**Physical support:** randomized delayed-choice extensions after a stable
earlier record can falsify violations at bounded scope.

**Selection role:** gives prefix autonomy and rules out operational
future-to-past signalling inside the declared circuit order.

**Native debt:** it does not derive the order or a fundamental time arrow. A
two-boundary/retrodependent theory can satisfy QP1 at the operational quotient
while correlating hidden boundaries, or reject exogenous-setting semantics and
leave the CDP comparison class.

### QP2 — perfect distinguishability

Every non-completely-mixed state has another state from which some physical
test distinguishes it perfectly.

**Physical support:** concrete source/reader pairs can test individual cases.

**Selection role:** supports spectral structure and faces.

**Native debt:** universal reader availability and a complete test category
are idealizations. A hidden restriction can make distinct physical states
operationally identical.

### QP3 — ideal compression

Every source face has a lossless maximally efficient encoding system, with no
unused code states.

**Physical support:** bounded encoders/decoders and full raw records can test
loss and unused-state mutants.

**Selection role:** supplies recursive face/system structure needed by the
reconstruction.

**Native debt:** exact minimality is a universal resource postulate. It assumes
availability of code systems and transformations and has no automatic QFT or
pregeometric extension.

### QP4 — local distinguishability

Product observations distinguish all bipartite operational states, so

$$
D_{AB}=D_AD_B.
\tag{5}
$$

**Physical support:** once independent subsystem and product-reader claims are
established, tomography can search for permanently global-only coordinates.

**Selection role:** it is load-bearing in selecting complex rather than real
quantum composites.

**Native debt:** the factorization and local-reader grammar enter first. A
pregeometric whole-process ontology need not possess a fundamental $A\otimes
B$ split, and gravity/gauge constraints may complicate local factorization.

### QP5 — pure conditioning

An atomic outcome on one part of a pure bipartite state leaves a pure
conditional state on the other.

**Physical support:** extremal preparations, maximally refined readers, and
conditional tomography can test bounded cases.

**Selection role:** links atomic transformations and pure-state structure.

**Native debt:** “pure” and “atomic” are defined on the operational quotient.
Unseen physical refinements can turn operational purity into microscopic
mixture.

### QP6 — purification

Every operational mixed state is the marginal of a pure state, unique up to a
reversible transformation on a fixed purifying system:

$$
\operatorname{marg}_E\Psi_\rho=\rho,
\qquad
\Phi_\rho=(I\otimes U_E)\Psi_\rho.
\tag{6}
$$

**Physical support:** bounded common-parent preparations and environment-side
reversible transformations can test examples and counterexamples.

**Selection role:** this is the specifically quantum conservation-of-
information postulate in the package and excludes nontrivial classical theory
when combined with the other premises.

**Native debt:** the purifying system, its physical accessibility, and the
meaning of reversible completion are supplied operationally. Exact universal
purification cannot be certified from finite noisy data, and approximate
reconstruction needs a stability theorem.

$$
\mathsf O(\widetilde L)=\mathsf O(L).
\tag{7}
$$

Then

$$
Q(\mathsf O(\widetilde L))
=Q(\mathsf O(L)).
\tag{8}
$$

In particular, QP1--QP6 cannot distinguish latent refinements with the same
complete operational quotient.

### Proof

$Q$ is a predicate only of the quotient object and its operations. Equality
(7) identifies every argument on which $Q$ depends. Substitution gives (8).
$\square$

latent variable $k\in\{1,\ldots,m\}$ with a fixed positive distribution

$$

=P_L(r\mid e)q_m(k).

$$

Readers forget $k$. Every $m$ has the same complete operational law and hence

## 8. Theorem QPB-B — purification/indivisibility carrier trilemma

Consider a proposed Barandes-facing theory whose operational quotient obeys
QP6. Let $E$ be a purifying carrier for an operational state of $A$.

### Statement

At least one of the following must hold.

1. **Predictive purification:** $E$ and $\Psi_{AE}$ are operational/Hilbert
   coordinates only and are not asserted to be the actual configuration.
2. **Actual complete purification:** the actual $AE$ carrier is
   future-sufficient and supports the licensed restart/composition grammar; the
   law is divisible through that enlarged carrier at the cut.
3. **Actual incomplete purification:** $E$ is asserted actual but the displayed
   $AE$ state is not future sufficient or restartable; an additional
   whole-process/source law is required, and purification alone is not native
   completion.

### Proof

First decide whether the purifying coordinates refer to the actual carrier. If
not, case 1 holds. If they do, decide whether the carrier contains all
future-relevant distinctions and supports the licensed restart kernel. If so,
ordinary conditional probability factors future records through the current
$AE$ carrier, giving case 2. If not, the carrier omits future-relevant or
whole-boundary information and case 3 holds. The cases exhaust the typing
choices. $\square$

### Consequence

Purification and indivisibility are not contradictory. They live at different
carriers unless a theory identifies them. But one may not simultaneously call
the purifying environment the complete actual state and claim fundamental
nondivision through that same restartable state.

This is the exact Barandes-facing question hidden by the phrase “reversible
dilation.”
