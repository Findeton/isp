## 5. Mathematical reconstruction ledger

### 5.1 Stable-boundary factorization

For a finite prefix-autonomous complete record family and every
positive-probability prefix $h$, the conditional kernel

$$
K(r\mid h,c)
=
\frac{P(hr\mid h,c)}{P(h)}
\tag{RR-3}
$$

is positive, normalized, unique on that prefix, and gives the usual chain
factorization. Values on null prefixes remain arbitrary.

**Rebuild verdict:** exact. The theorem is a chain-rule statement plus prefix
autonomy. It establishes a generic positive representation of stable records,
not a microscopic material state and not an ontological selection theorem.

### 5.2 Pair-history composition witness

The two kernels

$$
D_+
=
\begin{pmatrix}
1/2&i/4\\
-i/4&1/2
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
D_-
=
\begin{pmatrix}
1/2&-i/4\\
i/4&1/2
\end{pmatrix}
\tag{RR-4}
$$

are Hermitian positive semidefinite, have eigenvalues $3/4$ and $1/4$, and
give the same diagonal value on every event in a two-atom space. Holding a
second $D_+$ system fixed and evaluating
$F=\{(0,0),(1,1)\}$ gives

$$
(D_+\otimes D_+)(F,F)=\frac38,
\qquad
(D_-\otimes D_+)(F,F)=\frac58.
\tag{RR-5}
$$

**Rebuild verdict:** exact. The result refutes composition from the isolated
event-diagonal law alone. It does not show that nature is ontically described
by a complex pair history.

For Bochner-integrable vectors $V(h)$ and a record partition $A_r$,

$$
P(r)
=
\frac{\left\|\int_{A_r}V(h)\,d\mu(h)\right\|^2}
{\sum_s\left\|\int_{A_s}V(h)\,d\mu(h)\right\|^2}

$$
