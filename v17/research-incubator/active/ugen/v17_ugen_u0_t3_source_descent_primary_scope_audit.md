## 2. What Barandes establishes at this boundary

The audited Barandes construction treats the configuration space as a fixed
ingredient of a model, the standalone distribution as contingent, and the
transition probabilities as nomological. It also insists that Hilbert-space
potentials related by the printed gauge transformations have no distinct
physical significance.

These are three legitimate lessons:

1. configuration labels are not automatically physical structure;
2. contingent probability and fixed transition law must not be conflated;
3. a secondary Hilbert representation must not create physical distinctions
   absent from the positive stochastic law.

The source does not supply a uniform rule selecting the configuration object
or transition law for a previously unseen apparatus. Source descent therefore
sits upstream of the stochastic--quantum representation theorem.

---

## 3. The functorial lesson and its limit

Brunetti--Fredenhagen--Verch formulate a locally covariant QFT as a covariant
functor from a category of globally hyperbolic spacetimes and embeddings to a
category of algebras. Fewster--Verch use the same style to ask when a theory
represents the same physics on different spacetimes and prove, under their
printed hypotheses, a no-go for a single covariantly preferred state.

U0 inherits none of their spacetime or algebraic ontology. It retains only the
methodological separation:

1. a theory can be covariant across presentations or backgrounds;
2. a state or dynamical member is additional to that covariance;
3. covariance can constrain a selection without uniquely making it.

This supports an abstract experiment-packet groupoid and a natural assignment
of record laws. It does not justify calling a laboratory program a spacetime,
an algebra, or a QFT.

## 4. The contextuality firewall

Spekkens defines noncontextuality for preparations, transformations, and
measurements using operational equivalence classes and proves quantum
violations of the resulting ontological assumptions.

Therefore U0 must not identify two physically different procedures merely
because the present calibration cannot distinguish them. That would impose a
strong generalized-noncontextuality postulate and could reject the target
physics by construction.

The mandatory quotient is narrower:

1. renaming configuration or record labels;
2. changing coordinates or units with an explicit inverse;
3. gauge transformations already proved to preserve every admitted physical
   quantity;
4. lossless public recodings;
5. version-preserving apparatus descriptions known to be the same physical
   implementation.

Operational equivalence outside this verified neutral class is evidence, not
an ontological identity theorem. A candidate may use a contextual physical
difference only when that difference is independently described, measurable
or procedurally controlled, target-blind, and transferable.

## 5. What reconstruction programmes do and do not select

Hardy and Chiribella--D'Ariano--Perinotti show how strong operational
principles can recover large parts of quantum probabilistic structure without
starting from the textbook Hilbert axioms. In the cited works, continuity,
composition, purification, and reversible realization are substantive
premises.

For U0 they are candidate constraints, not facts that may be silently
inherited. More importantly, framework reconstruction is not yet
source-completion for a particular material system. It does not by itself
provide:

1. the actual interaction strengths;
2. the contingent preparation;
3. the physical control-to-law map;
4. the reader coupling;
5. the complete held-out process; or
6. a positive indivisible actuality law.

The same distinction applies to categorical probability. Fritz demonstrates
that stochastic kernels and statistical notions can be treated across
discrete, measure-theoretic, Gaussian, and other settings. U0 may use that
form-neutral language at genuine divisions. It does not inherit Markov
factorization at unrecorded seams.

## 6. Configuration neutrality is not structurelessness

The phrase “abstract physical configurations” means that the programme does
not choose in advance whether configurations are discrete, continuous,
spatial, field-like, graph-like, or trajectory-like. It cannot mean that the
input has no physically distinguishing structure.

If the source packet contains only an anonymous carrier, an invariant law can
use only invariants of that carrier. It cannot know which laboratory control
was rotated, which reader was connected, which component was heated, or which
record was retained.

The needed middle is a **structured but form-neutral experiment object**. Its
structure must be supplied by target-blind physical facts, not by quantum gate
names or held-out response tables.

## 8. Three kinds of transformation

Every proposed transformation of an input packet must receive exactly one
primary type.

### Type N — neutral presentation transformation

It changes no physical preparation, control, reader, composition interface,
or record event. Predictions must descend or transform equivariantly.

### Type P — physical intervention or contingent change

It changes a system, boundary condition, control, reader, coupling, or
record. A response is expected and must be predicted.

### Type U — unresolved

The present evidence does not establish whether the change is neutral or
physical. U changes cannot be used either to demand invariance or to award a
novel prediction. They require an independently designed discriminator.

Calling every difficult difference N erases contextual physics. Calling every
renaming P creates fake dynamics.

## 9. Symmetry is a constraint, not generally a selector

A verified symmetry can require the output law to lie in a fixed-point set.
It selects a unique law only when that fixed-point set contains exactly one
admissible member after normalization, composition, state, and resource
conditions are imposed.

If two distinct complete-process laws obey the same symmetry, symmetry alone
does not decide between them. This is the same structural warning illustrated
by the no-natural-state results in their much more specific locally covariant
QFT setting; U0's finite theorem is proved independently in the companion
mathematical gate.

Spontaneous symmetry breaking creates no loophole. A symmetric law may have
asymmetric individual actual histories. A sector-specific ensemble prediction
still needs a contingent sector/boundary record, a law-level asymmetry, or an
explicit mixture over sectors.
