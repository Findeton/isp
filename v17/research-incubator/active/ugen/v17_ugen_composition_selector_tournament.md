> Which independently motivated principles constrain or select a composition

The investigation separates three targets that are often conflated:

Quantum-reconstruction axioms can address the first target without choosing
the second. Exact process tomography can address the second without deciding

## 1. Selector accounting

Let

$$

$$

For a candidate physical premise \(P\), define its PC2 feasible set

$$
\mathsf F(P)
=
\{x\in[0,1]:
\text{some representative of }\mathcal L_x\text{ satisfies }P\}.
$$

The predicate \(P\) must be invariant under complete-experiment gauge. If it
depends on an arbitrary phase convention for one isolated lift, then
\(\mathsf F(P)\) is not physically defined.

### 1.1 Four levels of success

**Constraint.** \(\mathsf F(P)\subsetneq[0,1]\).

**Mathematical selection.** \(\mathsf F(P)=\{x_*\}\).

**Explanatory selection.** Mathematical selection plus all of:

1. \(P\) is stated without naming \(x_*\) or the target quantum answer;
2. its inputs are independently measured or physically motivated;
3. it applies uniformly beyond the calibration fixtures;
4. it predicts registered held-out continuations; and
5. no equivalent process law is hidden in a representation, compiler,
   boundary condition, response table, or enlarged carrier.

**Ontological selection.** Explanatory selection plus an operational or
independent theoretical reason to prefer one empirically equivalent
microscopic account over another.

A singleton feasible set is therefore not automatically an explanation.

### 2.1 Reciprocity proof

For every \(2\times2\) unitary \(W\), its modulus-square matrix has the form

$$
q(W)
=
\begin{pmatrix}
p&1-p\\
1-p&p
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

It is symmetric. Hence every PC2 completed block satisfies positive
input-output reciprocity. A probability-level time-reversal or reciprocity
condition cannot select \(x\) at this scope.

### 2.2 The false-checkpoint control

The premise \(\Gamma_2=G\) is a singleton selector inside PC2, but it selects
\(x=1/2\), whose depth-three law is

$$
\Gamma_3=I_2.
$$

It does not select the all-depth Markov law. This is an exact warning against
mistaking one local checkpoint for a complete composition principle.

### 2.3 Relation selection is not yet explanation

The relations

$$

\qquad\text{or}\qquad
b^2=f
$$

can select \(x=1\) or \(x=0\) operationally. But if the relation is inferred
from the same two-step experiment it predicts, it is calibration. It becomes
explanatory only when it follows from a broader independently established
symmetry or interaction law and predicts other words that were not used to
choose the relation.

## 3. Primary-source route audit

### 3.1 Feynman sum/product reconstruction

Goyal, Knuth, and Skilling associate a pair of real numbers with a sequence of
measurement outcomes and, using symmetry and combination conditions, recover
complex arithmetic, Feynman's sum and product rules, and modulus-square
probabilities.

This route can explain why a composition calculus has the algebraic form of
complex amplitudes, conditional on its operational premises. It does not
choose the amplitude assigned to a particular physical operation. On PC2 it
can justify the ambient coherent calculus while leaving every
\(x\in[0,1]\) available.

The route must therefore be credited at **framework** level, not as a selector
of the universe's dynamical member.

### 3.2 Hardy and Masanes--Müller

Hardy's axioms use continuous reversible transformations, subspaces,
composite-system structure, and a simplicity condition. Masanes and Müller
derive finite-dimensional quantum theory from requirements on information
capacity, local tomography, equivalence of subspaces, symmetry, and all
measurements.

These programmes show that physically interpretable principles can strongly
constrain state spaces and transformations. Their continuity and symmetry
requirements do not, by themselves, choose a Hamiltonian, coupling constant,
initial state, or PC2 seam. They select a theory class in which such dynamical
members live.

### 3.3 Chiribella--D'Ariano--Perinotti

The informational reconstruction uses causality, perfect distinguishability,
ideal compression, local distinguishability, pure conditioning, and the
purification postulate. The published result derives the finite-dimensional
quantum operational framework from that package.

Purification is powerful: every mixed process/state is represented by a pure
one on a larger system, essentially uniquely up to reversible action on the
purifier. This is a structural physical postulate, not a measured value of
the PC2 parameter. It can select quantum process architecture while leaving
the actual reversible transformation \(U\) free.

For ISP, purification is a serious candidate principle only if translated
into native stochastic language without silently taking the quantum
purification object or tensor product as primitive.

### 3.4 Local tomography

Local tomography constrains how composite states are identified from local
measurement statistics and helps distinguish complex quantum theory from
some real or Jordan alternatives in reconstruction programmes.

It is relevant to the future tensor gate. It has no content for the isolated
one-system PC2 seam, and it presupposes a typed composite-system interface.
It cannot be used to claim that composition was derived if the tensor product
and local effects were already supplied.

### 3.5 Gate-set tomography

Gate-set tomography characterizes preparations, gates, and measurements
self-consistently, relative to one another, while explicitly retaining gauge
freedom. Long sequences reveal coherent errors and relational gate data that
isolated endpoint tomography misses.

This is the cleanest empirical analogue of the PC2 seam:

1. isolated endpoint probabilities leave a fiber;
2. common continuations and long words shrink the fiber;
3. only gauge-invariant predictions are reportable; and
4. held-out sequences test the reconstructed gate set.

GST supplies empirical composition data. It does not explain why the
universe's law has that value or select an ontology.

### 3.6 Barandes

The current Barandes single-system framework supplies fixed transition
probabilities for arbitrary targets from admitted division events. A complete
model can therefore contain every PC2 block law directly.

Its Schur--Hadamard transformations make isolated potential phases gauge.
Accordingly, the PC2 seam must be translated back into differing native
whole-process continuation probabilities, not treated as an ontic phase.

Barandes' published dynamical symmetries are transformations that leave an
already specified transition matrix invariant, up to the printed gauge. They
classify symmetries of a law; they do not generate or select the law itself.

This leaves the exact U-Gen question intact:

> Can one native stochastic principle generate the complete family of target
> and interacting-parent laws from a bounded source-faithful packet, or must
> those laws be supplied independently?

### 3.7 Hamiltonian or action input

Known quantum physics predicts long sequences from a fixed Hamiltonian,
interaction, duration, and composition rule. In path language, additivity of
the action supports multiplicative phase transport.

This is an excellent compression of predictions. It is not yet an origin
theorem. The Hamiltonian/action, its degrees of freedom, couplings, time
parameter, and the quantum phase/Born rules remain inputs unless derived from
older physical principles.

For gravity, a generally covariant action could eventually constrain the law,
but only after the dynamical referents and operational spacetime content have
been earned. Importing a spacetime action now would reverse the v17 inference
direction.

## 4. The most promising middle route

The tournament leaves a gap between two honest but incomplete endpoints:

1. **tomography**, which measures the composition law; and
2. **full reconstruction**, which can select the quantum framework but not the
   actual dynamical member.

The most promising intermediate route is a uniform law of
**gauge-covariant relational composition** generated by physical symmetries,
interactions, or action data.

### 4.1 Typed procedure groupoid

Let \(\mathcal P\) be a finite category or groupoid whose:

- objects are operational boundary types;
- arrows are physically executable reversible procedures;
- composition is actual laboratory composition; and
- identities and inverses have complete operational meanings.

This structure is not chronology or spacetime. The arrow direction records
typed procedure composition supplied by the experiment.

Let \(T_g\) be a candidate lift or native transport assigned to an arrow
\(g:A\to B\). Boundary gauge acts as

$$
T_g\longmapsto D_B T_g D_A^{-1}.
$$

For a closed executable word

$$
w=g_k\cdots g_1:A\to A,
$$

the complete prediction of \(T_w\), modulo conjugation at \(A\), can contain
gauge-invariant relational information invisible at every isolated endpoint.

### 4.2 Why closed words matter

A separately chosen phase on one arrow is gauge. A closed sequence compares
multiple composition paths within one complete experiment. Its observable
return probabilities can expose relative transport without assigning reality
to an arbitrary matrix phase.

This is the proper generalization of the PC2 delayed separator.

### 4.3 Cocycle caution

A projective rule can have the form

$$
T_gT_h=\omega(g,h)T_{gh},
$$

with associativity imposing a cocycle equation on \(\omega\). But a purely
scalar phase multiplying an entire resolved path may cancel from every
modulus-square probability. Observable content requires relative holonomy
between alternatives, noncentral transport, or a common continuation that
converts it into a recorded probability.

Therefore “the missing datum is a cocycle” is a hypothesis, not a result.
The future gate must exhibit the reader that detects it.

### 4.4 What would count as explanation

A composition-transport law would earn explanatory credit only if:

1. its symmetry group, charges, or interaction data are independently
   calibrated;
2. one fixed rule applies to all registered words;
3. the training packet omits at least one complete closed word;
4. the rule predicts that word without refitting;
5. positive Markov, alternative coherent, real/quaternionic, and
   direct-whole-law controls are tested; and
6. the input ledger proves that no complete target response table was
   supplied.

Elegance, finite code length, or resemblance to a gauge connection is not
enough.
