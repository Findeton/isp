## 0. Executive verdict

The post-CRP and A1 results leave a precise matter-law deficit. The provisional
quantum/QFT predictive layer is broad enough to calculate complete laboratory
record laws, but it does not say what is actual in one run. The positive-first
CRP control supplied ordinary probability upstream of a secondary Hilbert
construction, but its noncommuting readers were not records of that same
positive process. A1 supplied a primitive relativistic flash referent for each
fixed finite flash-count vector, but not one proved indefinitely continuing
interacting process, variable-number QFT, complete reader descent, empirical
transfer, conservation, or reciprocal gravity.

The correct next question is therefore not which ontology sounds most
attractive. It is:

$$
\boxed{
\text{Does any existing matter law jointly possess predictive completeness,}
\text{ one-run actuality, and process completion at QFT scope?}}
\tag{1}
$$

The answer at the audited source versions is **no**.

1. Standard QFT and the R1 macroscopic-QED member remain the strongest
   predictive baseline, but actuality stops at stable records and the quantum
   relational rule is input.
2. Bell-type QFT is the strongest orthogonal prior-art control. It supplies an
   ordinary-positive configuration process with creation and annihilation for
   broad **regularized** QFT classes, but consumes the wavefunction,
   Hamiltonian, position observable, external time, and cutoffs; general global
   existence, cutoff removal, Lorentz completion, source transfer, and gravity
   remain open.
3. Relativistic collapse/pointer-field constructions provide covariant
   stochastic actuality candidates, but the audited model is an idealized
   field-theory architecture with supplied quantum fields, pointer field,
   smearing functions, noise, and collapse coupling rather than a complete
   transferred matter law.
4. Quantum-measure/coevent, Newtonian collapse-gravity, and
   classical--quantum gravity programmes each illuminate a different missing
   coordinate, but none closes the complete post-A1 vector.
5. The Barandes-native branch remains a serious guiding hypothesis, not a
   completed QFT matter law. Stable-record positivity alone is generic and
   cannot select that ontology.

The maximum justified routing result is:

$$
\boxed{
\text{POST-A1-NO-COMPLETE-MATTER-LAW;}
\quad
\text{BELL-TYPE-QFT IS THE SINGLE HIGHEST-VALUE NEXT PRIOR-ART CONTROL.}}
\tag{2}
$$

Equation (2) does **not** authorize that control. If explicitly authorized, it
should be one bounded source reconstruction and hostile comparison only. It
must not become a Bohmian parent ontology by default and must not start an
automatic repair or successor chain.

## 1. Why the newer external review changes emphasis but not the frontier

The review of repository checkpoint `bfd158c` correctly identifies four
facts.

1. Effective material descent is now real at conventional-QED scope.
2. Ordinary positivity on stable records is generic and therefore not evidence
   by itself for a microscopic stochastic ontology.
3. Fixed context-independent nonnegative route weights cannot produce
   destructive darkening, while Gram/pair-history data carry the missing
   compositional information conditionally.
4. The weak source/product/reversibility/record/resource conjunction admits a
   PR member and therefore does not select the quantum boundary.

Three qualifications are binding.

1. The result does not prove that every ordinary-positive indivisible
   whole-process law is predictively insufficient. It defeats the positive
   record shadow, fixed additive route weights, covariance relabeling, and the
   one tested CRP model. A genuinely contextual source-complete positive law
   remains logically possible.
2. A pair-history or decoherence functional is presently the strongest
   **predictive architecture**, not a selected material ontology. Its source
   law, boundary state, actuality, and gravity coupling remain open.
3. The review's proposed S3 purification/local-tomography investigation has
   already been performed. The Q-source audits show conditional reconstruction
   of the complex finite operational layer while leaving the physical
   operational substrate, material member, and microscopic ontology
   unselected. Reopening S3 would duplicate work.

Likewise, Q-Cut is mathematically review-ready but no longer the highest-value
unification gate. It applies to a positive future-sufficient cut, whereas the
live Barandes-facing possibility is a genuinely indivisible whole-process law
with no such cut.

## 2. Admission vector

Every candidate or control is scored on the same nine coordinates:

$$
\mathfrak M=(Q,A,P,V,R,T,G,N,B).
\tag{3}
$$

| coordinate | question |
|---|---|
| $Q$ predictive completeness | Does one fixed law generate complete cross-context probabilities in its declared domain? |
| $A$ actuality | What is actual in one run, beyond a predictive state or final record table? |
| $P$ process completion | Is there one indefinitely extensible, normalized and consistent process rather than unrelated finite laws? |
| $V$ variable-number QFT | Are interacting creation/annihilation and indistinguishable sectors included at controlled scope? |
| $R$ interventions/readers | Are interventions, readers, stable records, erasers, and divisions physically typed? |
| $T$ transfer | Has one fixed law made no-refit predictions for independently characterized systems? |
| $G$ reciprocal gravity | Do matter and geometry respond through one constraint- and conservation-compatible parent? |
| $N$ native depth | Is the target wavefunction, process, action, covariance, current, or equivalent quantum answer absent from the inputs? |
| $B$ background debt | Are external time, fixed geometry, foliation, or a privileged configuration observable derived, absent, or explicitly charged? |

The statuses used below are:

- **yes** — established at the printed source scope;
- **conditional** — established only after supplying decisive physics;
- **partial** — a real subcase or architecture exists but the full coordinate
  is unproved;
- **no** — absent or explicitly fails;
- **open** — not decided by the source.

No sum of these statuses is used. A candidate failing a load-bearing coordinate
cannot compensate by accumulating easy passes elsewhere.

### 3.1 Bell-type QFT source boundary

The sources establish continuum configuration processes, including stochastic
jumps for particle creation and annihilation, for broad regularized QFT
classes. They assume a well-defined Hamiltonian, introduce cutoffs where
needed, and choose a generalized position observable. Equivariance of the
configuration distribution under the supplied quantum evolution is the bridge
to empirical predictions.

The same sources explicitly leave nontrivial global-existence work, cutoff
removal, and Lorentz invariance open in general. The finite-rate existence
conditions and worked global-existence examples do not prove one regulator-free
relativistic process for all interacting QFTs.

### 3.2 Relativistic pointer-field collapse source boundary

The audited source gives a covariant Tomonaga-style stochastic state-reduction
architecture with a new pointer field, classical noise, local-beable options,
smearing functions, and a collapse coupling. It develops an idealized example
and discusses energy transfer. It does not supply a source-complete Standard
Model matter law, a unique beable choice, a transferred detector interface, or
reciprocal gravity.

### 3.3 Gravity-control source boundary

Tilloy--Diósi gives a concrete Newtonian $N$-particle monitoring-feedback law,
Newtonian pair interaction, and extra decoherence with testable dependence on
the collapse kernel. It explicitly stops short of a consistent general-
relativistic theory.

Oppenheim gives a broad completely-positive classical--quantum framework and
candidate stochastic gravity dynamics with a classical-GR limit and possible
experimental signatures. The audited version also prints open questions about
regularization, constraint compatibility, anomalous diffusion/heating,
conservation, and whether the description is fundamental or effective.

Sorkin's quantum-measure programme directly pursues a single-history realist
spacetime interpretation, but the audited source identifies conditional
preclusion and the operational identification of spacetime regions as
unfinished. Its quantum measure is not an ordinary first-order probability
measure on fine histories.

## 5. Why Bell-type QFT is the highest-value single next control

The selection criterion is not closeness to the preferred ontology. It is
orthogonal information gain relative to A1 and R1.

R1 already tests complete quantum prediction without microscopic actuality.
A1 already tests relativistic primitive actuality but fails the indefinitely
continuing interacting-process gate and lacks variable-number QFT. Bell-type
QFT occupies the missing corner:

$$
\text{supplied quantum QFT law}
\longrightarrow
\text{ordinary-positive configuration process with creation/annihilation}.
\tag{4}
$$

It can therefore answer five concrete questions without asking us to invent a
new law.

1. Exactly which quantum inputs determine the process: Hamiltonian,
   wavefunction, configuration observable, cutoff, initial distribution, and
   time parameter?
2. At what scope is the jump process globally defined rather than only
   formally equivariant?
3. Does variable particle number genuinely improve one-run ontology, or does
   it simply choose one preferred quantum observable as beable?
4. Can interventions and stable records be typed from the same configuration
   process without silently importing a measurement cut?
5. Which barriers are specifically relativistic/QFT barriers, and which are
   consequences of importing the quantum predictive law?

Bell-type QFT is therefore selected only as:

$$
\boxed{\text{B1 — REGULARIZED VARIABLE-NUMBER ACTUALITY CONTROL}.}
\tag{5}
$$

It is **not** selected as the parent ontology of v17. Equation (5) is a routing
recommendation requiring explicit authorization before any candidate pin,
construction, independent review, or successor exists.

## 6. Why the alternatives are not selected next

### 6.1 Bedingham pointer-field collapse

This is the closest runner-up because it attacks Lorentz-covariant stochastic
actuality. It is not selected first because its audited source is less complete
as a variable-number interacting matter law and introduces more model-specific
objects at once: pointer field, noise, smearing maps, collapse strength, and a
choice among local-beable readings. It should be a hostile comparator inside a
future B1 review contract, not a parallel model construction.

### 6.2 Sorkin/coevent

This direction is conceptually close to whole-process actuality and avoids a
fundamental Markov cut. Its conditioning, operational-region, and source-law
gaps are precisely the gates v17 must not fill by verbal interpretation. It is
retained as a whole-history comparator.

### 6.3 Tilloy--Diósi and Oppenheim

These are gravity discriminators, not admissible replacements for the missing
matter law. Promoting either now would let gravity choose between incompletely
specified matter ontologies. MG0 correctly requires at least two fully typed
matter laws with distinct gravity-sensitive predictions before such selection.

### 6.4 Another native positive formula

CRP exhausted the one authorized architecture attempt. No new physical
mechanism has yet earned a second positive-parent construction. Writing another
contextual weight, covariance, action, or reader would repeat the source-payload
problem.

### 6.5 Q-Cut

Q-Cut remains valuable as an optional theorem and later hostile control. It is
not the next matter-law control because its future-sufficient-cut premise does
not apply to every genuinely indivisible whole-process candidate.

## 8. Implication for the ontology register

The current ontology should be stated in two layers rather than collapsed into
one.

### Predictive layer

At fixed-background laboratory scope, the best complete structure remains
standard quantum/QFT, equivalently expressed through instruments, Gram/pair-
history data, process tensors, or appropriate decoherence functionals. This is
provisional predictive nomology, not a declaration that Hilbert space is
material substance.

### Actuality layer

Stable macroscopic records are empirically actual. Their ordinary-positive
prefix law is generic and therefore ontologically nonselecting. Microscopic
actuality remains unresolved. Configuration, flash, collapse-noise,
single-history, and record-only options remain controls with different debts.

The live research problem is the bridge:

$$
\boxed{
\text{one fixed physical law}
\quad\stackrel{?}{\longrightarrow}\quad
\begin{cases}
\text{complete cross-context prediction},\\
\text{one-run actuality},\\
\text{indefinitely extensible QFT process},\\
\text{reciprocal matter--geometry coupling}.
\end{cases}}
\tag{6}
$$

No audited candidate supplies (6). That is the honest frontier.
