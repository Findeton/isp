## 0. Result-neutral purpose

The v17 programme already requires matter and geometry to arise inside one
theory. This document makes that requirement precise enough to constrain a
future construction without deciding in advance what the universe is made of.

The central question is:

> Can one immutable relational law generate quantum operational structure,
> physical records or actual events, and reciprocal gravitational response,
> without receiving either the complete quantum process or the gravitational
> solution as an answer input?

MG0 is not an equation of motion. It is an admission contract for rival laws.
It may end by showing that the present categories are inadequate. Passing it
would not establish a final theory.

---

## 1. Physical intuition and the gravity-selector hypothesis

Fixed-background quantum experiments may leave several ontologies
operationally equivalent. Gravity can make their hidden differences physical
because a gravitational theory must say what produces gravitational response.
Candidate sources include:

1. an ensemble expectation;
2. one actual configuration or trajectory;
3. a branch- or record-conditioned configuration;
4. a stochastic collapse or signal field;
5. a pair-history or decoherence object;
6. an operator-valued or fully quantum gravitational source; or
7. a more primitive joint law in which “source” and “response” are only
   effective descriptions.

The **gravity-selector hypothesis** is that reciprocal coupling plus existing
constraints shrinks the empirical or explanatory equivalence class among these
options.

It is a hypothesis, not a premise. MG0 must permit:

$$
\text{gravity distinguishes none of the surviving nomologies}
$$

as an honest outcome.

This hypothesis is not actionable until genuinely different, fully specified
matter laws make distinct gravity-sensitive predictions. MG0 may not compare
an incomplete native-law sketch with a complete quantum control, use gravity
to fill U0's missing stochastic law, or treat N1/N1A as the default matter
ontology.

## 2. Assumptions forbidden at the contract level

The following may be admitted later as derived structures, declared controls,
or candidate-specific postulates. None is part of MG0 merely by notation.

1. A smooth manifold or continuum.
2. A fixed dimension or Lorentzian signature.
3. A metric field $g_{\mu\nu}$ as fundamental.
4. A lattice, causal set, spin network, graph, or discrete web.
5. A global time, time step, foliation, or Hamiltonian slicing.
6. Einstein's equation or the Einstein--Hilbert action as microscopic law.
7. Quantization of the gravitational variables.
8. Classicality of the gravitational variables.
9. A tensor-product factorization into matter and gravity.
10. A Born rule or ordinary-positive fine-history law.
11. A unique actual history, branch, collapse, or Everettian multiplicity.
12. Regional composition as fundamental rather than emergent.
13. Locality defined using an already supplied spacetime metric.
14. A cosmological state, vacuum, no-boundary condition, or equilibrium measure
    selected by the law.

Every future use of one of these structures must be entered in the input ledger
as derived, independently calibrated, contingent, declared control, or imported
target structure.

## 3. Comparison interface, not asserted ontology

For a bounded registered experiment $e$, introduce a raw comparison space

$$
\widetilde\Omega_e
$$

and an empirically silent presentation/gauge groupoid $\mathcal G_e$. The
physical comparison classes, when this quotient exists, are

$$
\Omega_e=\widetilde\Omega_e/\mathcal G_e.
$$

This notation does **not** declare that nature fundamentally consists of
histories. It supplies a neutral interface on which rival history, process,
algebraic, or actualization descriptions can be compared.

Each experiment must type at least the following maps or objects:

$$
\mathfrak X_e=
(B_e,C_e,M_e,G_e,R_e,A_e,\mathcal O_e).
$$

Here:

- $B_e$ is admissible boundary or preparation data;
- $C_e$ is the registered control/intervention grammar;
- $M_e$ is the matter-sensitive operational projection;
- $G_e$ is the gravity-sensitive operational projection;
- $R_e$ is the complete registered record algebra;
- $A_e$ is the candidate's actual referent, if it has one; and
- $\mathcal O_e$ is the complete operational prediction object.

$M_e$ and $G_e$ are operational labels, not necessarily fundamental factors.
A genuinely unified candidate may deny that its microscopic carrier splits as

$$
\Omega_e\cong\Omega^M_e\times\Omega^G_e.
$$

It must nevertheless explain how matter-sensitive and gravity-sensitive
records arise and how their interventions are distinguished.

### 3.1 Law, state, control, record, actuality

Every candidate must keep five roles distinct:

$$
\boxed{
\text{law }\mathcal N
\ne
\text{contingent state }\sigma
\ne
\text{control }c
\ne
\text{record }r
\ne
\text{actual sample/referent }a.}
$$

The complete prediction map has the schematic type

$$
\Phi_e[\mathcal N;\sigma,b,c]
\longmapsto
\mathcal O_e.
$$

Changing $\sigma$, $b$, or $c$ between experiments is permitted when declared.
Changing $\mathcal N$ after seeing a held-out result is a different theory.

### 3.2 Actuality is optional but cannot be ambiguous

A record-only theory may leave $A_e$ empty. A theory with particles,
trajectories, flashes, branches, fields, or coevents must state exactly which
object is actual and how it relates to $R_e$.

A fundamentally stochastic law need not explain a random draw by a deeper seed.
It must specify the law of the object said to be drawn. Record probabilities do
not silently become a probability law on unrecorded fine histories.

## 4. Rival nomological forms remain live

MG0 admits at least three mathematically distinct families.

### 4.1 Rival A — ordinary-positive joint law

A candidate may assign an ordinary probability measure

$$
P^{\mathcal N}_e(d\omega\mid\sigma,b,c)
$$

to complete physical comparison histories or actual configurations. The theory
must say which event algebra is physical and how unresolved interference is
represented without smuggling in a separate target amplitude table.

### 4.2 Rival B — pair-history or decoherence law

A candidate may assign

$$
D^{\mathcal N}_e(A,B\mid\sigma,b,c)
$$

with ordinary record probabilities only on licensed decoherent or consistent
event algebras. It must generate the history carrier, pairing, composition, and
record algebra; rewriting standard quantum amplitudes earns adequacy, not
explanatory reduction.

### 4.3 Rival C — predictive law plus actualization

A candidate may supply

$$
(\mathfrak Q^{\mathcal N}_e,\mathcal A^{\mathcal N}_e),
$$

where $\mathfrak Q$ is the predictive quantum structure and $\mathcal A$ is a
collapse, trajectory, flash, field, coevent, or other actualization law. Both
parts, their coupling, and their parameters are charged.

### 4.4 No premature common denominator

The rivals need agree only on complete registered operational predictions. MG0
must not force a decoherence functional to masquerade as a positive fine-path
measure or force a positive theory to declare a Hilbert object ontic.

## 5. Jointness is stronger than juxtaposition

A matter model and a gravity model written on the same page do not form a joint
law. A candidate is jointly closed only if the same $\mathcal N$ determines:

1. which combined configurations or operational algebras are admissible;
2. the matter-sensitive response;
3. the gravity-sensitive response;
4. their mutual coupling;
5. composition or regional gluing;
6. record formation and, when claimed, actuality;
7. normalization or probability interpretation; and
8. the relevant gauge/constraint relations.

The disallowed construction is

$$
\text{matter law}
+
\text{externally solved gravity table}
+
\text{fitted feedback table}.
$$

The desired type is

$$
(\sigma,b,c)
\xrightarrow{\quad\mathcal N\quad}
\mathcal O^{M+G}_e
$$

for held-out combined experiments.

## 6. Operational reciprocity gate

Reciprocity must be demonstrated through interventions and records, not merely
through symmetric-looking notation.

For a declared low-energy laboratory control with a supplied causal scaffold,
let $x,x'$ be two matter-source interventions and $Y_G$ a gravity-sensitive
record. Define

$$
\Delta_{M\to G}(y;x,x')
=
p_{\mathcal N}(Y_G=y\mid \operatorname{do}x,b,c)
-p_{\mathcal N}(Y_G=y\mid \operatorname{do}x',b,c).
$$

Let $z,z'$ be two independently calibrated gravitational interventions or
source configurations and $Y_M$ a matter-sensitive record. Define

$$
\Delta_{G\to M}(r;z,z')
=
p_{\mathcal N}(Y_M=r\mid \operatorname{do}z,b,c)
-p_{\mathcal N}(Y_M=r\mid \operatorname{do}z',b,c).
$$

A reciprocal benchmark requires registered members with

$$
\Delta_{M\to G}\ne0,
\qquad
\Delta_{G\to M}\ne0,
$$

after nongravitational channels, common causes, direct control leakage, and
postselection artifacts are represented or bounded.

### 6.1 Same-parent requirement

The two responses must be marginals or conditionals of one admissible joint
parent law. Independently specifying two channels does not prove their joint
consistency.

### 6.2 No fundamental chronology inferred

The `do` notation above belongs only to a declared laboratory causal control.
It does not derive time, chronology, spacetime, or causal orientation from
$\mathcal N$. A candidate without external time must reconstruct an equivalent
relational instrument using physical clocks and records.

### 6.3 One-way approximations

Test matter on a fixed background and semiclassical matter sourcing without
feedback may be valid approximations. They do not pass the common-law
reciprocity gate as fundamental descriptions.

## 8. Gauge, covariance, and regional composition

### 8.1 Empirical quotient

For every declared gauge or presentation arrow

$$
g:\widetilde\omega\to\widetilde\omega',
$$

complete registered predictions must agree. Conversely, transformations that
change relational boundary records, holonomy, curvature, or physical reference
systems may not be discarded as gauge merely because a coordinate changed.

### 8.2 No metric-dependent locality at the root

Before geometry is derived, locality can only be stated through operational
factorization, intervention independence, or a candidate's declared relational
adjacency. A spacetime light cone may be used as a low-energy target, not as an
unpaid microscopic definition.

### 8.3 Regional gluing is a gate

When a candidate admits regions $R_1,R_2$ with compatible boundary data, it
must define or derive

$$
\mathcal N_{R_1\cup R_2}
=
\operatorname{Glue}
(\mathcal N_{R_1},\mathcal N_{R_2};\partial),
$$

including integration/summation over shared boundary data, gauge quotienting,
and any boundary or edge degrees of freedom. Held-out triple gluings test
associativity or the printed higher coherence.

This requirement is conditional: MG0 does not assume that fundamental reality
comes pre-partitioned into regions. A candidate may instead derive regional
composition as an effective structure or prove why it is inapplicable.

## 9. Conservation and constraint closure

In a classical metric regime, geometric identities constrain admissible
sources. A future candidate must reproduce the appropriate conservation law,
not merely fit a Newtonian force curve.

MG0 therefore requires a typed closure statement of one of the following
forms:

1. exact constraint preservation;
2. stochastic preservation with specified martingale/noise balance;
3. boundary-charge balance under gluing;
4. anomaly cancellation;
5. controlled effective violation with a prospective bound; or
6. proof that the candidate's nonmetric variables yield the GR conservation
   identity in the declared limit.

For a metric GR limit this includes, at minimum,

$$
\nabla_\mu G^{\mu\nu}=0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\nabla_\mu T^{\mu\nu}_{\mathrm{eff}}=0
$$

at the relevant approximation order. A collapse, actual-trajectory, or noisy
source cannot ignore the compensating degrees of freedom needed for this
identity.

No classical constraint algebra is imposed before the arena is chosen. Once a
candidate uses it, closure and propagation become load-bearing tests.

## 10. Causality and no-signalling

A candidate must provide complete operational predictions for entangled
preparations, local interventions, retained records, erased records, and
postselection. It may not establish causality from one unconditioned marginal.

Required controls include:

1. ensemble-decomposition independence of operational predictions unless a new
   preparation variable is physically measurable;
2. no controllable remote signalling outside the candidate's derived or
   declared causal relation;
3. no future control hidden in an earlier source packet;
4. no preferred foliation hidden in simulation order;
5. complete inclusion of noise or actualization records that restore linear
   operational evolution; and
6. distinction between ontological nonlocality and operational signalling.

Deterministic nonlinear state evolution is not rejected by label. It must
survive the complete entangled-ensemble signalling control.

## 11. Two mandatory limits from the same law

### 11.1 Fixed-geometry quantum limit

For a regime $L_Q$ in which gravity is effectively fixed or negligible,

$$
\Phi_e[\mathcal N;L_Q]
\longrightarrow
\mathcal O^{\mathrm{QFT/QM}}_e
$$

with controlled error and complete process/composition predictions. N1 may be
used as a nonrelativistic path-law control and G1 as a phase/holonomy record-law
control. Neither alone supplies this limit for general QFT.

### 11.2 Classical-gravity limit

For a regime $L_G$ of sufficiently classical geometry and matter records,

$$
\Phi_e[\mathcal N;L_G]
\longrightarrow
\mathcal O^{\mathrm{GR}}_e
$$

including reciprocal backreaction, universal coupling or its explicit
violation, conservation, and the held-out weak-field/relativistic controls
appropriate to the claimed scope.

### 11.3 Immutable-law rule

$L_Q$ and $L_G$ may use different states, boundary conditions, and
coarse-grainings. They may not use separately fitted fundamental laws. Shared
parameters must be calibrated once and propagated forward.

## 12. Time and physical reference systems

N1 and ordinary laboratory quantum mechanics use external time. General
covariance makes permanent reliance on such a parameter scientifically
insufficient for a fundamental gravity theory.

MG0 does not reopen Paper 04B or award a clock. It requires each candidate to
declare one of:

1. external time is fundamental physical structure, with its empirical cost;
2. time is a gauge parameter and registered predictions are invariant;
3. dynamics is expressed through correlations between physical subsystems;
4. a finite-region law requires no global evolution parameter; or
5. time is emergent only in a controlled limit.

Any relational-clock route must test at least two clock species and show when
their conditional predictions agree. “The code evaluated this variable first”
is never a physical orientation.

diffusion after choosing an experiment is post-hoc theory construction.

not a universal proof that every possible gravity theory is quantized.

parameters independently fixed and all confounds typed.

This authorizes no claim of a full theory without separate review, scaling,
relativistic, cosmological, and empirical gates.
