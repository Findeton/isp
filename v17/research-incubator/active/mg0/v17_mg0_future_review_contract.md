## 2. Questions the review must answer

### Q1 — neutrality

Does MG0 avoid assuming continuum, metric, dimension, topology, foliation,
Einstein dynamics, quantized gravity, classical gravity, or a preferred
actuality ontology?

### Q2 — comparison type

Does the comparison-history language remain an interface rather than a hidden
assertion that histories are fundamental? Can algebraic, process, and
actualization rivals enter without changing their probability semantics?

### Q3 — law/state/control/record/actuality split

Are the five roles mutually distinguishable and sufficient to expose target
import, cosmological inputs, and actual-source ambiguity?

### Q4 — jointness and reciprocity

Is the same-parent requirement stronger than two fitted response channels? Do
the operational response matrices test both directions without pretending to
derive fundamental chronology?

### Q5 — source functional

Does the contract distinguish expectation, actual configuration, branch,
stochastic signal, pair-history, noncommutative gravity, and primitive-joint
sourcing without claiming the list is exhaustive?

### Q6 — gauge and gluing

Does the empirical quotient preserve relational boundary data? Is regional
gluing treated as a candidate gate rather than a universal premise? Are edge
and shared-boundary data charged?

### Q7 — conservation and constraints

Does the contract impose GR conservation only in the appropriate limit while
still preventing collapse/noise/actual-source candidates from ignoring
constraint balance?

### Q8 — causality

Does the hostile battery test entangled preparations, ensemble decomposition,
future-setting leakage, preferred updates, and operational signalling without
mistaking ontological nonlocality for controllable signalling?

### Q9 — two limits

Are fixed-geometry quantum/QFT and classical-gravity/GR limits required from
one immutable law? Does the contract avoid counting imported baseline equations
as derivations?

### Q10 — empirical protocol

Does the reciprocal diamond contain enough records and controls to distinguish
source classes? Are Page–Geilker, mediator-entanglement, and
decoherence–diffusion conclusions correctly scoped?

### Q11 — sources and novelty

Are Barandes, hybrid classical–quantum, histories, general-boundary, nonlinear
signalling, relational-time, and low-energy-gravity sources represented
faithfully? Is v17 synthesis separated from prior art?

### Q12 — authority

Does every file state that there is no model, data evaluation, pin, review,
scientific result, or successor authority?
