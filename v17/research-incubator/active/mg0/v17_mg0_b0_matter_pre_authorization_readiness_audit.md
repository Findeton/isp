### 4.1 Demonstrated fixed-background gravity-sensitive matter waves

- Overstreet *et al.*, [“Observation of a gravitational Aharonov--Bohm
  effect”](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.abl7152), measured the phase of a
  rubidium interferometer near a kilogram-scale classical source and measured
  the arm deflections independently. This is a strong B0 external-potential
  calibration. It is not a superposed source or reciprocal quantum-gravity
  experiment.
- Fixler *et al.*, [“Atom interferometer measurement of the Newtonian
  constant of gravity”](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1135459), and Rosi
  *et al.*, [“Precision measurement of the Newtonian gravitational constant
  using cold atoms”](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature13433), establish real
  source-mass geometry, positioning, gradiometry, and systematic-control
  obligations. They do not provide a quantum source mass.
- Westphal *et al.*, [“Measurement of gravitational coupling between
  millimetre-sized masses”](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-021-03250-7),
  measured gravity from roughly 90-milligram source masses with a torsion
  balance and held nongravitational contributions below the reported signal
  scale. This establishes a valuable classical mechanical gravity control,
  not coherent source preparation.

### 4.2 Demonstrated massive coherence

- Kovachy *et al.*, [“Quantum superposition at the half-metre
  scale”](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature16155), demonstrated long-baseline
  atom-interferometric separation and recombination. Its exact macroscopicity
  interpretation was subsequently debated; B0 uses it only as evidence for
  large-separation atom control, not as a unique ontology claim.
- Schumm *et al.*, [“Matter-wave interferometry in a double well on an atom

  [“Integrated Mach--Zehnder interferometer for Bose--Einstein
  condensates”](https://doi.org/10.1038/ncomms3077), demonstrate split,
  phase-controlled, recombined condensate matter waves with explicit trap and
  interaction effects.
- Fein *et al.*, [“Quantum superposition of molecules beyond 25
  kDa”](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41567-019-0663-9), demonstrate interference
  for molecules above 25,000 Da. The source is not trapped and does not form a
  reciprocal gravity pair.
- Pedalino *et al.*, [“Probing quantum mechanics with nanoparticle
  matter-wave interferometry”](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-025-09917-9),
  demonstrate quantum interference of sodium clusters above `170 kDa` with

  claim that nanoparticle COM interference is absent. The multipath

  near-field propagation.

  oscillator”](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.adf7553), demonstrate Wigner
  interference for two coherent states of an acoustic phonon mode. The
  effective mode mass must not be conflated with a 16-microgram

### 4.3 Levitated-microsolid component status

- Tebbenjohanns *et al.*, [“Quantum control of a nanoparticle optically

## 12. Temporary marker, coherent eraser, and stable record

Let a reversible marker start in `|0⟩_M` and let

### `E_retain`

### Proposition B0-C — local erasure cannot reverse an inaccessible record

Let `A` contain every accessible source and visible-marker coordinate. Suppose
retained alternatives correlate with orthogonal inaccessible environment
states:

$$

=
\frac{|A_L\rangle|E_L\rangle
+e^{i\varphi}|A_R\rangle|E_R\rangle}{\sqrt2},
\qquad
\langle E_L|E_R\rangle=0.
$$

No channel acting only on `A` can restore the original source coherence for
every phase. More directly, after the orthogonal environment is traced out,
the accessible reduced state is

$$
\rho_A
=
\frac12
\left(
|A_L\rangle\langle A_L|
+|A_R\rangle\langle A_R|

which is the same for every `varphi`. One fixed local channel therefore has
the same output for every `varphi` and cannot reproduce the distinct family
`{|psi_varphi><psi_varphi|}`. A channel supplied with `varphi` could prepare a
new coherent state, but that would be repreparation from an extra phase input,
not erasure of the retained record.

Thus deleting a displayed file, resetting a bit, or postselecting an eraser
outcome is not coherent erasure of a stable record. The benchmark's

`E_retain` is a different typed future.
