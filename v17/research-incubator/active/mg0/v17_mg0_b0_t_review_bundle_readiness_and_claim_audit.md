record boundary, and the finite `R` carrier is only a retained-register proxy.
No amplified material record or hardware-stable division is claimed.

the inaccessible environment holding phase information. It is not a universal
no-recovery theorem with arbitrary side information.

profile are distinct typed objects. Standard quantum nomology is charged as

outside the nuisance-score span. Zero profiled information proves first-order
blindness, not automatically global nonidentifiability.

reversal separates an odd response from even common drift. A nuisance with the
same complete score restores first-order blindness; exact global degeneracy
