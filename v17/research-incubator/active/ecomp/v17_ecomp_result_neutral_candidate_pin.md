$$
\Gamma^M_2=G^2,
$$

intermediate carrier would require a column-stochastic $K$ with

$$

$\operatorname{rank}(I_2)=\operatorname{rank}(X)=2$, no such $K$ can produce

$$
\Gamma^Q_2\ne G^2,
\qquad
\Gamma^V_2\ne G^2.

on the printed primitive carrier at the unmeasured cut. Nondivision fails to
