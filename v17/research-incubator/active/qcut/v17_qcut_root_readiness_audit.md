decision**, but it is not an accepted theorem and must not enter the numbered

## 2. Independent root reconstruction of the core inequality

### 2.1 External lower bound

For \(n\in4\mathbb N\), the natural \(\alpha=1/4\) Partial Matching ensemble
obeys, for some source constant \(c_0>0\) and all sufficiently large \(n\),

$$
D^{\pi_n^{\rm nat}}_{1/3}(f_n)
\ge
c_0\sqrt n.
$$

The same distribution is fixed for every candidate realizer.

### 2.2 Finite message induced by a continuous cut

For an admitted realizer, form the finite vector of all response probabilities

$$
r(e)=\bigl(\xi_y(e)\bigr)_{y\in\mathcal Y_n}.
$$

The reader set is finite and every coordinate is Borel. Quantizing each
coordinate on a \(1/40\) grid gives a finite Borel label \(Q=q(\Lambda)\).
It changes every binary reader law by at most \(1/40\).

### 2.3 Induced protocol error

The exact pin requires:

$$
\delta_{\rm act}=\frac1{10},
\qquad
\varepsilon_{\rm fac}=\frac1{40},
\qquad
\eta=\frac1{40}.
$$

The private-coin protocol induced by \(Q\) therefore has error

$$
e
\le
\frac1{10}+\frac1{40}+\frac1{40}
=
\frac3{20}.
$$

### 2.4 Correlated-input identity

Although the promise correlates \(X\) and \(Y\), the preparation of
\((S,\Lambda,Q)\) depends on \(X\) and Alice-side randomness only. Hence

$$
Q\perp Y\mid X
$$

and therefore

$$
I(X,Y:Q)
=
I(X:Q).
$$

HJMR Definition V.1 explicitly uses the external transcript information
\(I(XY;Q)\). The displayed Markov identity, rather than a substitution of an
internal information notion, is what turns that source quantity into the
preparation-information coordinate.

### 2.5 Message compression

Specialize HJMR Lemma V.3 to one round with

$$
\Delta=\frac1{20}.
$$

Writing the universal additive term as \(C_{\rm H}\ge0\), the induced protocol
gives

$$
D^{\pi_n^{\rm nat}}_{1/5}(f_n)
\le
\frac{2I(X:Q)+C_{\rm H}}{1/20}
=
40I(X:Q)+20C_{\rm H}.
$$

Because \(1/5<1/3\), allowing only error \(1/5\) cannot require less
communication than allowing error \(1/3\):

$$
D^{\pi_n^{\rm nat}}_{1/5}(f_n)
\ge
D^{\pi_n^{\rm nat}}_{1/3}(f_n)
\ge
c_0\sqrt n.
$$

Thus

$$
I(X:Q)
\ge
\frac{c_0}{40}\sqrt n-\frac{C_{\rm H}}{2}.
$$

For sufficiently large \(n\), for example after increasing \(n_0\) so the
additive term is at most half the leading term,

$$
I(X:Q)
\ge
\frac{c_0}{80}\sqrt n.
$$

### 2.6 Transfer to the cut

Since \(Q=q(\Lambda)\), \(p(\Lambda)=S\), and \(S\perp X\),

$$
I(X:Q)
\le
I(X:S,\Lambda)
=
I(X:\Lambda\mid S).
$$

Therefore the fixed core theorem follows, conditional on the exact external
source lemmas and the printed measurability premises.

This reconstruction was performed without using a finite ontic cardinality,
coordinate dimension, computational-efficiency assumption, or physical-memory
interpretation.

## 3. Quantum comparator reconstruction

At \(\alpha=1/4\), one copy of

$$
|\psi_x\rangle
=
\frac1{\sqrt n}\sum_i(-1)^{x_i}|i\rangle
$$

reveals an input matching-edge parity with probability \(2\alpha=1/2\) and
heralds failure otherwise. Three independent copies fail simultaneously with
probability \(1/8\). Guessing fairly only in that branch produces error

$$
\frac12\cdot\frac18
=
\frac1{16}
<
\frac1{10}.
$$

Their joint Hilbert dimension is \(n^3\), so the log-dimension and cq mutual
information are at most \(3\log_2n\). This verifies the matched asymptotic
information separation at the operational-cut coordinate.

It does not establish a separation in total physical resources.

## 4. Countermodel audit

### A. One exact real

An injective real encoding of \(X\) remains admissible but carries the
corresponding mutual information. Coordinate count cannot violate the bound.

**Verdict:** no counterexample.

### B. Huge independent seed

Input-independent \(S\) may have arbitrarily large entropy. The finite response
label can inherit that randomness, but HJMR compression charges only its
correlation with \(X\).

**Verdict:** no counterexample; law-description and computation remain outside
the theorem.

### C. Seed correlated with the setting

If \(S\) helps determine \(Y\), the frozen independence premise fails.

**Verdict:** genuine outside branch, not a counterexample.

### D. Future-dependent cut

If \(\mu\) depends on \(y\), then \(Q\perp Y\mid X\) can fail.

**Verdict:** genuine retrocausal/whole-program outside branch.

### E. Indivisible whole-history law

A law may directly assign \(P(\hat b\mid x,y)\) without a positive sufficient
cut variable.

**Verdict:** the central live outside branch. Q-Cut must not be advertised as
ruling it out.

### F. Signed, quasiprobability, operator, or amplitude boundary

Such a boundary is not an ordinary positive random variable.

**Verdict:** outside branch; potentially explains compact phase-complete
response structure, but ontology remains unselected.

### G. Nonmeasurable carrier

Nonmeasurable response functions evade quantization only by ceasing to define
ordinary operational probabilities.

**Verdict:** ill-typed, not an admitted counterexample.

### H. Vanishing error gap

A sequence whose success margin approaches zero can evade the fixed-constant
asymptotic.

**Verdict:** outside the fixed pin; varying-gap theorems must retain explicit
dependence and cannot demote the core.

## 5. Scientific selection accounting

If proved, Q-Cut would earn:

- **invariant-structure gain:** mutual information survives measurable
  relabeling, one-real encodings, and idle-noise inflation;
- **broad scoped no-go gain:** all standard-Borel positive sufficient cuts for
  one scalable task obey the same lower bound; and
- **scaling gain:** the burden grows as \(\sqrt n\) against an
  \(O(\log n)\) quantum comparator.

It would not earn:

- empirical partition refinement;
- a new deviation from quantum theory;
- selection of a microscopic ontology;
- a physical memory, energy, or precision bound;
- a uniform law-description theorem;
- a result about genuinely indivisible histories; or
- progress on clocks, chronology, spacetime, or gravity.

This is a worthwhile theorem because it locates where an explanatory
ordinary-positive **sufficient-cut** description cannot claim low retained
preparation information. A positive whole-history theory need not possess
such a cut. Q-Cut is not yet the explanatory law itself.
