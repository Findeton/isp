## 0. Executive finding

This audit tested the thirteen assumptions N5, N6, and N18--N28 from the
foundational assumption-deletion charter. It did not try to replace familiar
physics by verbal novelty. For each assumption it asked four narrower
questions:

1. What is actually a theorem or successful physical approximation?
2. What stronger ontological claim is often smuggled into it?
3. What is the smallest weakening that preserves the known evidence?
4. What new information or experiment would make the weakening physical
   rather than merely permissive mathematics?

The main result is not that the familiar assumptions are all false. It is that
they divide into four sharply different classes.

1. **Two identifications are false as logic.** No-signalling is not the same
   predicate as causal locality (N6), and continuum versus discrete lattice
   is not an exhaustive classification of mathematical carriers (N22).
2. **Four familiar structures are powerful effective inputs but not necessity
   theorems.** A fixed global order (N5), quantization of classical GR
   variables (N19), an expectation-value source (N20), and a supplied action
   or Hamiltonian (N26) each define serious calculational programmes. None is
   thereby forced as the microscopic ontology.
3. **Four claims are presently open emergence or descent questions.** Full
   background independence (N18), microscopic dimension/signature (N23), the
   joint priority of geometry and matter (N24), and inference from a
   gravity-mediated entanglement record (N21) require typed experiment-class
   premises rather than slogans.
4. **Three proposed selectors do not select without extra physical input.** A
   symmetry, stationary state, naturalness judgment, maximum-entropy rule, or
   simplicity ranking (N25 and N27) constrains or ranks a family only after a
   carrier, admissible family, constraints, reference measure, language, or
   prior is supplied. Low-energy success (N28) establishes adequacy in a
   domain, not uniqueness of the ultraviolet referent.

The deepest recurring issue is not “discrete or continuous?” and not
“classical or quantum?” It is whether a theory has silently supplied the
interfaces that make those words meaningful:

$$
\boxed{
\text{events, interventions, boundaries, subsystems, source, reader,
and comparison class}.}
\tag{1}
$$

Deleting an assumption before these interfaces are typed only enlarges the
formal model class. It becomes physics only when the weakened theory recovers
the same complete experiment, removes an independently countable input, or
forces a held-out difference.

The most defensible forward consequence is therefore conservative:

$$
\boxed{
\begin{gathered}
\text{do not quantize, discretize, causal-order, or maximize by default;}\\
\text{do not forbid those structures either;}\\
\text{make each one descend from a complete parent law or print it as input.}
\end{gathered}}
\tag{2}
$$

## 1. Binding scope and method

This is one strand of a parallel author-side investigation. Its conclusions
have the following ceilings.

1. A process-matrix counterexample to fixed order is not a derivation of
   chronology or a claim that every logically consistent process is physical.
2. A quantum-switch implementation demonstrates causally nonseparable order
   of operations at its operational scope; it does not establish absence of a
   background spacetime causal order.
3. A hybrid classical--quantum construction shows that “quantize the metric”
   is not a theorem of logical necessity. It does not show that any existing
   hybrid is empirically or gravitationally complete.
4. Page--Geilker constrains the simplest expectation-sourced semiclassical
   scheme at its recorded macroscopic scope. It is not a no-go against every
   classical or stochastic gravity theory.
5. Gravity-mediated entanglement conclusions are conditional on the admitted
   mediator, locality, matter, nuisance, and independence class. A currently
   disputed QFT mechanism is recorded as a live scope dispute, not adjudicated
   here by citation count.
6. Scale-dependent spectral dimension in a candidate approach does not prove
   that nature's dimension runs. It proves that fixed microscopic integer
   dimension is not a theorem of all quantum-gravity formalisms.
7. An exact finite counterexample to selection establishes underdetermination
   under the named premises. It does not prove that no deeper physical

8. No result below selects Barandes ontology, Hilbert ontology, a pair-history
   ontology, a continuum, a graph, a unique actuality law, or a gravity law.
9. The terminal R1 fixed-background baseline and the MG0 common-law contract
   remain recovery obligations. This audit does not weaken them.
10. No official pin or review cycle follows from this document.

## 2. N5 — causality is not definitionally one pre-existing global order

### 2.1 Necessity record

| field | audit entry |
|---|---|
| $S$ | Every complete physical experiment is embedded in one supplied acyclic global order, and causal influence is exactly reachability in that order. |
| $D$ | Ordinary circuits, globally hyperbolic background spacetime models, Markov or process factorizations with typed input/output slots. |
| $T$ | Extremely successful modelling structure; not a theorem from probabilities alone. |
| $R$ | Which localized intervention can alter which later registered record. |
| $\Delta$ | Do not supply one global order. Define a directed influence predicate only from differences under licensed interventions, then test acyclicity, transitivity, locality, and scale stability. |
| $K$ | Recover ordinary circuit order, relativistic causal behavior, no future-setting leakage, stable records, and consistent composition in the laboratory regime. |
| $C$ | A typed intervention grammar, setting independence, complete readers, comparison across interventions, and an account of fine tuning or faithfulness. |
| $O$ | Derived partial order; cyclic/indefinite operational structure; fixed-order recovery only in a limit; or underdetermination. |

For registered controls $x$ in region or role $A$ and complete records $y$ in
role $B$, the weakest operational influence relation is

$$
A\rightsquigarrow B
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\exists x,x',b:
p(y\mid\operatorname{do}x,b)
\ne
p(y\mid\operatorname{do}x',b).
\tag{4}
$$

Equation (4) is not yet spacetime causality. It depends on which interventions
are physical, which background conditions $b$ are held fixed, and which
records are complete. It is nevertheless better typed than reading an arrow
from program execution, a matrix index, or the order in which a category was
written.

### 2.2 What known physics actually establishes

Oreshkov, Costa, and Brukner formulate correlations among local laboratories
without assuming a definite global causal order and exhibit process matrices
that violate a causal inequality. Chiribella, D'Ariano, Perinotti, and Valiron
construct the quantum switch, in which the order in which two black boxes are
queried is coherently controlled. Causal-witness work and experiments certify
causal nonseparability of the quantum switch while also emphasizing that the
switch need not violate a causal inequality.

These results defeat the logical equivalence

$$
\text{consistent operational probabilities}
\Longleftrightarrow
\text{one definite order of all local operations}.
\tag{5}
$$

They do not prove that arbitrary noncausal process matrices occur in nature,
nor that a quantum-switch apparatus lacks a perfectly ordinary embedding in
laboratory spacetime. The correct lesson is narrower: **the order of operations
on a target system and a fundamental global causal order are not the same
object.**

### 2.3 The smallest serious deletion

The useful deletion is not “assume acausality.” It is:

> Remove the global order from the microscopic input while retaining a typed
> preparation/intervention/readout interface; ask whether a stable directed
> relation descends from the complete response family.

A candidate must then distinguish at least:

1. signalling influence, Equation (4);
2. common-cause dependence;
3. selective steering versus nonselective signalling;
4. controllable versus merely ontological dependence;
5. laboratory spacetime order versus order of black-box action; and
6. exact microscopic relation versus coarse-grained partial order.

The candidate fails if its “emergent arrow” is just the direction of input and
output types that the author supplied.

### 2.4 Proposed future gate

For one complete parent law and a frozen family of localized instruments:

1. compute every intervention-response equivalence class;
2. construct $\rightsquigarrow$ without using source-code or slot order;
3. test whether its quotient is acyclic and locally finite;
4. test prefix independence/no-backwards influence where a prefix is
   independently defined;
5. compare two independently built clock/readout species;
6. verify recovery of the declared relativistic light-cone control; and
7. retain “no unique order” as an allowed result.

This is a chronology-descent gate, not yet an authorized chronology paper.

## 3. N6 — no-signalling is not causal locality

### 3.1 Necessity record

| field | audit entry |
|---|---|
| $S$ | If observed marginals are no-signalling, the underlying process is causally local; if a process is ontologically nonlocal, it enables signalling. |
| $D$ | Bell experiments under explicit measurement independence and spacelike separation; operational quantum theory; causal models with stated latent variables. |
| $T$ | False as a general identification. The two directions require extra premises. |
| $R$ | Ability to transmit a controllable message versus mechanism by which physical influence or correlation is carried. |
| $\Delta$ | Keep no-signalling as an operational constraint and type causal locality independently. |

| $C$ | Setting freedom, composition, complete instruments, nuisance channels, latent/common causes, and fine-tuning assumptions. |
| $O$ | Local and no-signalling; nonlocal but no-signalling; signalling; or experimentally indistinguishable under the registered interface. |

The marginal constraint

$$
\sum_b p(a,b\mid x,y)
=p(a\mid x)
\quad\text{independent of }y
\tag{6}
$$

is a statement about a table. It does not print a mediator, a causal graph, a
factorization, a spacetime, or an ontology. A PR-box table is the clean finite
control: it is no-signalling while lying outside the Bell-local polytope. A
common-cause correlation is another: statistical dependence need not be a
controllable channel. Conversely, a causal influence can be hidden in a
particular equilibrium or by fine-tuned parameter cancellation.

Therefore v17 should require a four-column ledger rather than the single word
“local”:

| predicate | question |
|---|---|
| operational no-signalling | can a setting change a remote unconditioned record distribution? |
| causal influence | does a licensed intervention change the other record family? |
| factorization/local causality | does the complete law factor through admitted common causes or boundaries? |
| geometric locality | is the carrier confined to the derived or declared spacetime relation? |

No one column may be inferred from another without the missing theorem
premises.

## 6. N20 — the gravitational source is not settled by writing
## $\langle T_{\mu\nu}\rangle$

### 6.1 Necessity record

| field | audit entry |
|---|---|
| $S$ | The local stress tensor, or its quantum expectation value, is the fundamental object that sources one gravitational field. |
| $D$ | Classical GR, semiclassical Einstein equations, perturbative QFT on curved spacetime, and weak-field approximations. |
| $T$ | Classical/effective success; universal microscopic actuality claim unearned. |
| $R$ | Which actual, stochastic, branch-conditioned, operator, or joint-law quantity covaries with gravity-sensitive records in one run. |
| $\Delta$ | Freeze complete source preparations sharing selected marginals but differing in actuality/coherence/record status; let the parent law determine the response. |

| $C$ | Source ontology, regularization, noise/collapse records if used, compensating carriers, and complete reader law. |
| $O$ | Expectation, actual configuration, conditional branch, stochastic signal, operator-valued gravity, pair-history source, or primitive joint law survives. |

Page and Geilker found results inconsistent with the simplest semiclassical
Einstein equation in which one macroscopic classical field follows the
expectation of macroscopically different recorded source outcomes. Their own
claim was evidence supporting, not proof compelling, quantized gravity.

The failure matters because an ensemble mean and one registered happening are
different referents:

$$
\langle T_{\mu\nu}\rangle_{\rho}
\ne
T_{\mu\nu}[a]
\ne
\mathbb E[T_{\mu\nu}[A]\mid r]
\tag{9}
$$

in general. Here $a$ is an actual configuration if the ontology has one, and
$r$ is a complete record. A theory must choose or derive which quantity
appears in its response law.

Stochastic collapse-signal and actual-configuration models show that other
source referents can be written. They also expose the price: extra noise,
regularization, decoherence, empty-wave or conservation questions, and a
complete backreaction law. Merely replacing brackets by an “actual” symbol
does not solve those debts.

The correct physical gate is the MG0 reciprocal diamond:

$$
\text{source alternatives}
\longrightarrow
\text{gravity-sensitive response}
\longrightarrow
\text{matter records},
\tag{10}
$$

with both arrows generated by one normalized parent, not separately fitted
conditional channels.

$$
\text{entangled matter record}
\Longrightarrow

That conclusion additionally needs the channel to be genuinely gravitational,

> The observed complete record excludes the frozen local-classical-mediator

It should not state “gravity is quantum” without printing what “gravity,”
“mediates,” “local,” and “quantum” mean in that theorem.

### 8.1 Necessity record

“Not a smooth manifold” does not imply “a graph of little objects.” An

This is only a logical opening. It earns no physical credit unless the carrier

1. a finite computation is not evidence that reality is discrete;
2. an index set is not a set of physical atoms;
3. a graph of dependencies is not automatically spacetime adjacency;

5. a point-free formulation may still import continuum information through

The correct default is carrier neutrality plus an explicit input ledger, not
a prior preference for either pole.

## 9. N23 — microscopic dimension and signature need not be fixed inputs

### 9.1 Necessity record

| field | audit entry |
|---|---|
| $S$ | Spacetime has one fixed integer dimension and signature at every physical scale, supplied before the microscopic law. |
| $D$ | Smooth manifolds, conventional QFT, GR, and many discrete regulators with preselected dimension/signature. |
| $T$ | Empirically excellent macroscopic structure; microscopic necessity open. |
| $R$ | Scaling of volumes, diffusion/propagation, causal intervals, response, and clock/radar relations measured by physical probes. |
| $\Delta$ | Do not supply a microscopic dimension. Apply several estimators only after a candidate generates an ensemble and its operational probes. |
| $K$ | Stable Lorentzian $3+1$ behavior across the observed range, with local Lorentz symmetry and QFT/GR propagation. |
| $C$ | Generated ensemble, scale definition, probe law, estimator bias, finite-size control, and distinction among spectral/Hausdorff/order dimensions. |
| $O$ | Stable four; scale-dependent; estimator-dependent; nonmanifoldlike; or no dimension. |

Ambjørn, Jurkiewicz, and Loll measured a spectral dimension near four at large
scales and near two at short scales in their causal-dynamical-triangulation
ensemble. Whether that model is nature is not assumed. The result suffices to
show that “dimension” can be an operational estimator with scale dependence
rather than one universal microscopic integer.

Different estimators answer different questions:

| estimator family | operational content |
|---|---|
| volume/Hausdorff | how counted or measured volume scales with radius |
| spectral | return probability of a supplied diffusion process |
| causal-order/Myrheim--Meyer type | interval ordering fractions under manifoldlike assumptions |
| propagation | scaling of Green functions or dispersion |
| thermodynamic | density-of-states scaling |

Agreement among held-out estimators is evidence of an effective dimension;
one fitted estimator is not. Signature is still stronger: a Lorentzian
$3+1$ claim requires causal and propagation structure, not merely total
dimension $D=4$.

Thus Paper 17's older dimension-selection intuition remains valid only after a
law generates a varying-size ensemble and operational chronology. It cannot
be used to choose the law that was missing those objects.

## 11. N25 — symmetry, stationarity, naturalness, and maximum entropy
## constrain families; they do not generally select the law

### 11.1 Necessity record

| field | audit entry |
|---|---|
| $S$ | A sufficiently symmetric, stationary, natural, or maximum-entropy principle uniquely determines the physical law and state. |
| $D$ | Named carrier, transformation group, admissible family, constraints, reference measure, and dynamical class. |
| $T$ | Sometimes a uniqueness theorem inside a supplied class; not a source-free selector. |
| $R$ | Which law and contingent state nature instantiates. |
| $\Delta$ | Require a uniqueness theorem over the complete admitted family and charge every defining structure. |
| $K$ | Recover dynamics, transients, response, conservation, records, and held-out processes—not only an equilibrium marginal. |
| $C$ | Carrier, group, constraints, base measure, coarse graining, prior, and allowed generator family. |
| $O$ | Unique member under stated inputs; residual free family; or selection delegated to contingent cosmological data. |

Four exact controls expose different underdeterminations.

### 11.2 Symmetry control

Let a group $G$ act on a finite set $X$ with orbits $O_1,\ldots,O_m$. Every
invariant probability has the form

$$
p(x)=\frac{w_j}{|O_j|}
\quad(x\in O_j),
\qquad
w_j\ge0,
\quad
\sum_jw_j=1.
\tag{13}
$$

Symmetry fixes uniformity inside each orbit and leaves the orbit weights free.
Only a transitive action plus a declared finite normalized carrier produces
the uniform law, and even then the carrier and action were supplied.

### 11.3 Stationarity control

For the identity transition kernel $K=I$, every probability distribution is
stationary. An irreducible finite Markov generator may have a unique
stationary distribution, but the generator and its rates remain inputs. The
v17 closed-carrier audit sharpened this: detailed balance fixes rate ratios
while leaving symmetric conductances and absolute rates free.

### 11.4 Maximum-entropy control

Maximizing

$$
H(p)=-\sum_xp_x\log p_x
\tag{14}
$$

requires $X$, the admissible constraints, and the coarse graining. In a
continuous space it also requires a reference measure or coordinate-aware
relative entropy. Changing any of these can change the maximizer. Maximum
entropy is a disciplined inference rule once that inferential problem is
specified; it does not generate the physical carrier, dynamics, reader, or
constraint values.

### 11.5 Naturalness control

Technical naturalness can identify parameters protected by enhanced symmetry
or radiative stability. It does not assign a normalized probability over all
possible laws, nor prove that nature must choose the aesthetically simplest
protected member. A naturalness criterion is valuable only after its measure,
cutoff, comparison family, and empirical consequences are stated.

The correct v17 rule is:

$$
\boxed{
\text{constraint} + \text{uniqueness theorem}
\ne
\text{physical origin of the constraint set}.}
\tag{15}
$$

$$

1. derive the interaction form or coefficient from independently physical

| $T$ | Useful heuristic or explicit inference prior; not a generally calibrated law of nature. |
| $R$ | Relative frequency or credence assigned to rival nomologies. |
| $\Delta$ | Print the language/prior and demand empirical calibration or a new prediction. |
| $K$ | Preserve known parameter precision, symmetry, universality, and cross-domain transfer. |
| $C$ | Coding language, universal machine or representation, constants, precision cost, and model class. |
| $O$ | Heuristic ranking; explicit Bayesian prior; predictive success; or no physical selector. |

Description length is representation dependent up to a language-dependent
constant, and “simple” may refer to equations, initial state, boundary rule,
or generated data. A short law with an astronomically special state can move
complexity rather than remove it. A physical simplicity principle therefore
needs a complete two-part ledger:

$$
L(\text{law})+L(\text{contingent state}\mid\text{law}),
\tag{17}
$$

plus a reason the chosen coding induces physical probabilities. Without that,
simplicity can guide research but cannot adjudicate ontology.

## 14. N28 — low-energy success does not uniquely identify the fundamental
## ontology

### 14.1 Necessity record

| field | audit entry |
|---|---|
| $S$ | If a representation reproduces low-energy QM/QFT and GR, its variables are the fundamental constituents of reality. |
| $D$ | Effective field theory and universality regimes below a stated scale and precision. |
| $T$ | False as a uniqueness inference; low-energy adequacy remains mandatory. |
| $R$ | Which microscopic distinctions survive or become measurable outside the shared effective regime. |
| $\Delta$ | Demand no-refit transfer to a new scale, composition, gravitational source, or actuality-sensitive experiment. |
| $K$ | Preserve every successful low-energy observable and error estimate. |
| $C$ | Matching map, coarse graining, renormalization, parameter transfer, and domain of validity. |
| $O$ | Empirical equivalence persists; one candidate transfers; or all current candidates fail. |

Effective field theory is a strength of physics precisely because very
different ultraviolet completions can share the same infrared degrees of
freedom and symmetries. Donoghue's low-energy quantum-gravity treatment shows
that robust long-distance quantum corrections can be extracted without
knowing the ultraviolet completion. The converse is therefore invalid:

$$
\text{same low-energy observables}
\centernot\Longrightarrow
\text{same microscopic referent}.
\tag{18}
$$

For ISP this cuts both ways. A Barandes-style positive law that merely compiles
the same QFT predictions has not yet displaced Hilbert ontology. A Hilbert or
field representation that reproduces all present experiments has not thereby
proved itself fundamental. Gravity becomes useful only if complete rival
matter laws make distinct, predeclared predictions under one common
source--geometry--probe experiment.

## 15. Cross-assumption synthesis

### 15.1 The common hidden move

Most failed identifications share this form:

$$
\boxed{
\text{a representation makes an interface explicit}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\text{the interface is fundamental}.}
\tag{19}
$$

Examples are:

- a circuit order treated as cosmic chronology;
- a no-signalling table treated as a local mechanism;
- a manifold treated as the necessary carrier of covariance;
- a matter/gravity tensor split treated as prior ontology;
- a stress-tensor expectation treated as one-run source;
- an action treated as its own physical origin; and
- a stationary or simple member treated as selected.

The correction is not to erase every interface. Physics requires interfaces
to define experiments. The correction is to classify them as **derived,
effective, contingent, control, or nomological**, and then demand the missing
descent map.

### 15.2 Three levels that must not be collapsed

| level | object | question |
|---|---|---|
| logical possibility | admissible probability/process/carrier class | is the structure mathematically consistent? |
| physical law | one immutable parent with state, controls, readers, composition, actuality | does it generate complete experiments? |
| empirical selection | held-out records and cross-regime transfer | does nature distinguish it from rivals? |

Process matrices, alternate carriers, and hybrid gravity expand level one.
Actions, CP constraints, symmetries, and low-energy limits constrain level two.
Only complete common experiments can reach level three.

### 15.3 The strongest surviving physical principles

The audit did not find a single hidden-assumption deletion that by itself
generates the missing law. It did identify principles that survive the
deletion tests because they are operational or conservation obligations rather
than preferred ontologies:

1. normalization or exactly typed non-Kolmogorov probability semantics;
2. complete intervention and reader specification;
3. no future-setting leakage;
4. operational no-signalling in the recovered relativistic regime;
5. stable records and explicit actuality semantics;
6. reciprocal matter--geometry response;
7. conservation with every carrier of exchanged charge represented;
8. recovery of complete QFT/QM and GR limits from the same law;
9. no answer-equivalent action, process, metric, or reader table as hidden
   input; and
10. held-out transfer without retuning.

These do not create a theory. They sharply limit what can count as one.
