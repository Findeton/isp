## 3. The exact common hypothesis: sufficient physical boundaries

Let a complete experiment $E$ be divided across a candidate interface
$\Sigma$. Let $E_-$ and $E_+$ denote the two sides without assuming that the
division is temporal, spatial, or fundamental. Let $B_\Sigma$ be the proposed
boundary object.

A serious sufficient-boundary claim has five independent predicates.

### 3.1 Descent

The boundary type and values must be identified without consuming the
held-out complete target process:

$$
B_\Sigma
=
\mathsf D_\Sigma[	ext{public preparation, controls, records, and law}],
\tag{2}
$$

where $\mathsf D_\Sigma$ may not contain the target wavefunction, process
tensor, solved geometry, complete future table, or equivalent advice.

### 3.2 Screening

If two interiors agree on $B_\Sigma$, every compatible exterior complete
record must agree:

$$
B_\Sigma(x)=B_\Sigma(x')
\Longrightarrow
P(F_+\mid\operatorname{do}c_+,x)
=
P(F_+\mid\operatorname{do}c_+,x')
\tag{3}
$$

for all frozen exterior controls and measure-determining readers. Equation
(3) may hold exactly, approximately, inside a charge sector, on a code
subspace, or to a perturbative order. Those scopes may not be merged.

### 3.3 Composition

The same boundary must support one target-blind gluing rule:

$$
\mathfrak P_E
=
\mathsf G_\Sigma
(\mathfrak P_{E_-},\mathfrak P_{E_+};B_\Sigma).
\tag{4}
$$

Screening alone does not imply (4). Information invisible to current exterior
readers can become relevant after reconnection. Triple gluing must test
associativity or expose an associator/anomaly.

### 3.4 Physical intervention and readout

If the boundary is claimed as material rather than predictive, it needs a
typed physical interface:

1. preparation or occurrence conditions;
2. readout or stable record;
3. interventions such as toggle, erase, isolate, reconnect, or reprepare;
4. persistence under its licensed future; and
5. charge, energy, information, precision, and stability accounting.

A mathematically minimal sufficient statistic need not satisfy any of these.

### 3.5 Transfer

The boundary type and gluing law must predict a held-out family without
retuning:

$$
(B_\Sigma,\mathsf G_\Sigma)_{\rm calibration}
\longrightarrow
\mathcal O_{\rm held\ out}.
\tag{5}
$$

Otherwise one exact boundary object can simply encode one target answer.

The conjunction of (2)--(5) plus physical interface descent is what v17 had
often called a “complete boundary” in separate contexts. The synthesis makes
clear that completeness is graded and experiment-relative before it can be
fundamental.

## 5. Exact countercontrols that survived integration

The following controls are compact enough to become standard v17 tests.

### 5.1 Parity process — one-step kernels do not determine a process

Independent fair bits and a three-bit even-parity law have the same initial
marginal and adjacent one-step kernels, but different complete triples. This
proves that endpoint/first-order data do not determine the complete process.
Full-history factorization remains possible only after consuming the target
law.

### 5.2 Evidenced memory — some non-Markovianity is omitted apparatus

A fair memory copied to an early and late record survives a causal break on
the nominal system. Reading, toggling, erasing, and isolating that memory
change the late statistics exactly as predicted. This is a genuine physical
hidden-Markov enlargement. Appending the transcript without such controls is
only representation.

### 5.3 Rebit pair — positivity and local readers do not imply local tomography

The real two-rebit states

$$
\rho_\pm
=\frac14(I\otimes I\pm\epsilon Y\otimes Y)
\tag{7}
$$

agree on every product of locally allowed real-symmetric effects while a
joint $Y\otimes Y$ reader distinguishes them. This is a theorem-domain
counterexample, not evidence that nature uses real quantum theory.

### 5.4 Common cause — conditioning is not intervention

For fair $C$ with $X=C$ and $Y=C$,

$$
P(Y=y\mid X=x)=\delta_{yx},
\qquad
P(Y=y\mid\operatorname{do}X=x)=\frac12.
\tag{8}
$$

The type distinction exists before quantum mechanics.

### 5.5 Equal effects, different instruments

Two instruments can have the same immediate POVM probabilities and different
post-measurement states. A later continuation distinguishes them. Therefore a
self-adjoint operator or effect is not a complete physical reader.

### 5.6 Decoherent global state — stable record is not selector

For

$$
|\Psi\rangle
=\alpha|0,R_0,E_0\rangle
+\beta|1,R_1,E_1\rangle,
\qquad
\langle E_1|E_0\rangle\simeq0,
\tag{9}
$$

the reduced record algebra becomes approximately diagonal while the global
state acquires no stochastic term choosing one component.

### 5.7 Idle dilation — existence does not select a real environment

Appending an unused factor to any exact Stinespring dilation preserves the
system channel. Minimality is a mathematical compression convention until an
environment is independently sourced and probed.

### 5.8 Symmetry orbits — invariance does not choose orbit weights

For group orbits $O_j$, every invariant distribution has

$$
p(x)=\frac{w_j}{|O_j|},
\qquad x\in O_j,
\tag{10}
$$

with free $w_j$. Symmetry fixes within-orbit equality, not a source-free law.

### 5.9 Identity dynamics — stationarity need not select a state

For $K=I$, every state is stationary. Conversely, a fixed irreducible kernel
may select one stationary state while leaving the kernel unexplained.

### 5.10 PR table — no-signalling does not imply Bell-local factorization

A PR-box table has perfectly no-signalling marginals and lies outside the
Bell-local polytope. It is a logical control, not a proposed physical device.

Together these controls reveal one pattern:

$$
\boxed{
\text{successful quotient, marginal, dilation, or invariance}
\ne
\text{physical source and referent}.}
\tag{11}
$$

## 11. What would count as a genuine breakthrough

An assumption deletion earns more than formal generality only if it does at
least one of the following.

1. **Input reduction:** removes a target process, action, metric, state, or
   reader table without importing equivalent information elsewhere.
2. **Physical carrier discovery:** identifies an independently manipulable or
   readable boundary/memory/reference that predicts held-out behavior.
3. **Unified descent:** generates two previously separate structures—such as
   quantum composition and gravity response—from one immutable law.
4. **Empirical wedge:** forces a predeclared complete-record difference from
   every retained rival.
5. **Resource theorem:** proves that a familiar representation hides an
   unavoidable information, precision, memory, energy, or boundary cost.
6. **No-go/underdetermination:** proves, within a physically named class, that
   the desired structure cannot be selected from the admitted data.

Merely allowing more mathematical models is not a breakthrough. Merely
rewriting standard quantum theory in positive variables is not a source law.
Merely failing one positive model is not evidence for Hilbert ontology.
