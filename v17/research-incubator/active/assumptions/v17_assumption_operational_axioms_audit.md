## 0. Disposition

This audit investigates N10--N17 of the v17 foundational
assumption-deletion programme. It asks whether eight identifications that are
often convenient inside quantum operational practice are physically
necessary:

1. local data determine the global state;
2. every mixed state is the discarded part of a fundamentally pure reversible
   whole;
3. an observed input--output conditional is a causal transformation;
4. complex Hilbert objects are material states;
5. a self-adjoint observable is an intrinsic possessed property;
6. decoherence and a stable record imply one actual outcome;
7. complete tomography identifies the source law; and
8. a mathematical dilation identifies a real environment and its
   microdynamics.

The audit finds no license to delete quantum operational structure wholesale.
It finds a sharper pattern:

$$
\boxed{
\begin{array}{c}
\text{operational theorem or exact representation}\\
\not\Rightarrow\\[-1mm]
\text{physical source, material carrier, or actuality law}.
\end{array}}
\tag{0.1}
$$

Local tomography and purification are substantive postulates inside a typed
operational-probabilistic framework. They are not consequences of positivity
alone and are not automatically universal properties of a closed universe.
An intervention-defined subsystem evolution and an observed conditional are
different mathematical objects. Realification shows that complex coordinates
need not be material substances, while independent-source network results show
that the *natural local-tensor real theory* is empirically too small. Thus one
cannot infer either “complex numbers are literal matter” or “complex structure
is dispensable.” Contextuality blocks a naive possessed-value reading of all
operators under its printed premises, but does not block every realist
ontology. Decoherence creates stable classical record algebras but supplies no
single-outcome selector. Tomography identifies an operational equivalence
class relative to a declared experiment grammar, and dilation proves
existence of a representation; neither operation selects microscopic physics.

The most promising shared pressure point is not an alternative equation. It
is the unproved completeness of the operational boundary itself:

> Are the declared systems, local readers, interventions, purifying
> complements, and process boundaries independently physical and sufficient,
> or are they only effective interfaces of a more indivisible whole?

No answer is awarded here.

## 3. N10 — local tomography is global completeness

### 3.1 Exact necessity record

| field | entry |
|---|---|
| $S_{10}$ | If every product of local readers has the same statistics on two composite preparations, the preparations are physically identical. |
| $D_{10}$ | A declared bipartite system $AB$; independently fixed local systems $A,B$; a composition rule; a complete local effect set; closure under product experiments; an operational equivalence relation; usually finite informational dimension. |
| $T_{10}$ | A substantive composition/tomography axiom. It is a theorem inside standard finite complex quantum theory, not a theorem of probability theory. |
| $R_{10}$ | Physical local preparation and reader procedures on independently identified subsystems, plus joint preparations against which product readers can be applied. |
| $\Delta_{10}$ | Retain systems, positive probabilities, composition, and local readers, but allow a joint coordinate invisible to every product reader. |
| $K_{10}$ | Recover ordinary complex-quantum tomography where observed; no-signalling; consistent joint readers; associative composition; stable records; QFT limits at later scope. |
| $C_{10}$ | Additional joint/gluing coordinates, bilocal or global readers, boundary charges, reference resources, or a proof that the hidden coordinate is empirically idle. |
| $O_{10}$ | O1 within complex quantum operational theory; O2 by exact broader countermodel; O3 if local tomography emerges; O7 only if a joint-only coordinate has a held-out consequence. |

### 3.2 The theorem premise is not innocuous

In a locally tomographic operational theory, the product-effect statistics
separate joint states. In dimension notation this gives the familiar
parameter multiplication

$$
D_{AB}=D_A D_B.
\tag{3.1}
$$

The Chiribella--D'Ariano--Perinotti reconstruction uses local
distinguishability together with its other principles to obtain the complex
finite-dimensional quantum framework. Removing it does not leave the same
theorem with one line missing; real and other globally richer composites
become possible.

### 3.3 Exact smallest-deletion witness

Real-vector-space quantum theory gives a compact exact witness. Let

$$
Y=
\begin{pmatrix}
0&-i\\ i&0
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
\rho_{\pm}
=\frac14\left(I\otimes I\pm\epsilon\,Y\otimes Y\right),
\qquad 0<\epsilon\le1.
\tag{3.2}
$$

$Y\otimes Y$ is a real symmetric matrix with eigenvalues $\pm1$, so
$\rho_\pm$ are valid real density matrices. Every allowed one-rebit effect is
real symmetric and is spanned by $I,X,Z$. Hence, for every local real-symmetric
$A,B$,

$$
\operatorname{tr}[(Y\otimes Y)(A\otimes B)]
=\operatorname{tr}(YA)\operatorname{tr}(YB)=0.
\tag{3.3}
$$

All product readers therefore assign the same probabilities to $\rho_+$ and
$\rho_-$. A joint measurement of the allowed real observable $Y\otimes Y$
distinguishes them, with expectations $+\epsilon$ and $-\epsilon$.

This proves only:

$$
\boxed{
\text{positivity + composites + local readers}
\not\Rightarrow
\text{local tomography}.}
\tag{3.4}
$$

It does not show that nature is described by real quantum theory. Hardy and
Wootters showed that real-vector-space quantum theory is bilocally rather than
locally tomographic. The witness is a model-class counterexample to necessity.

### 3.4 Known-physics recovery and empirical pressure

Any deletion must still explain why ordinary laboratory quantum mechanics is
locally tomographic at its accepted finite complex scope. Three admissible
recovery forms are:

1. the deeper theory is exactly locally tomographic on all physical
   boundaries;
2. global-only coordinates exist but are superselected or inaccessible in
   the tested regime; or
3. local tomography is approximate and emerges after a boundary/reference
   resource is fixed.

There is already an important nontrivial test of scope. Independent-source
quantum-network results separate complex quantum theory from the *natural
real-Hilbert theory with its standard independent-source tensor product*.
That does not prove that complex numbers are material or rule out every real
reformulation; it shows that the source-independence and composition contract
can make the real/complex distinction empirical.

### 3.5 Held-out test

Freeze before data access:

1. two independently prepared source interfaces;
2. all local readers and their calibration;
3. one joint/gluing reader not generated by product effects;
4. the independence and shared-reference contract; and
5. the predicted product and joint statistics under each candidate.

The decisive outcomes are:

- product data determine every joint continuation: support for O1 at that
  interface;
- one joint reader separates product-indistinguishable preparations: O2/O7;
- the distinction disappears once an omitted shared reference is included:
  the reference, not “holism,” carries the missing resource; or
- no registered reader accesses the coordinate: O6, not ontology selection.

### 3.6 Current author-side inference

Local tomography is not a necessity of positive or probabilistic physics. It
is a powerful, testable composition principle. Its physical content cannot be
assessed until the subsystem split and local-reader completeness are
independently earned.

### 4.1 Exact necessity record

| field | entry |
|---|---|
| $S_{11}$ | Every mixed state is the marginal of an essentially unique pure state of a larger system, and this representation identifies the actual universe as pure and its microdynamics as reversible. |
| $D_{11}$ | A causal operational-probabilistic theory with typed composites, pure/extremal states, reversible transformations, discard, and the purification existence-and-uniqueness postulate. Finite-dimensional quantum purification is a special case. |
| $T_{11}$ | Purification is an explicit operational principle; reversible dilation is a theorem inside that framework. The claim that the purifying whole is physically actual is an additional ontology postulate. |
| $R_{11}$ | A mixed preparation, a declared purifying system, a joint pure preparation, discard, and physically executable reversible transformations on the purifying side. |
| $\Delta_{11}$ | Keep the mixed operational state and its predictions while dropping universal purification, uniqueness, or the identification of a mathematical purifier with a real environment. |
| $K_{11}$ | Recover observed quantum state/channel statistics, reversible controls where available, information-flow accounting, stable records, and thermodynamic limits. |
| $C_{11}$ | Fundamental irreversible events, superselection sectors, inaccessible boundary data, multiple inequivalent purifiers, or an independently sourced physical complement. |
| $O_{11}$ | O1 inside a theory postulating purification; O2 for broader probabilistic theories; O4 for ordinary quantum dilation without environment evidence; O7 only with independent complement-side predictions. |

### 4.2 Exact theorem scope

Chiribella, D'Ariano, and Perinotti study operational-probabilistic theories
in which every mixed state has a purification unique up to reversible
transformations on the purifying system. Within that framework, purification
is equivalent to reversible realization of physical processes and supports a
state--transformation duality. This is a strong theorem with a strong premise.

The theorem does **not** have the form

$$
\text{observed mixed state}
\Longrightarrow
\text{the universe is ontically pure}.
\tag{4.1}
$$

The distinction is visible in classical probability. In the ordinary
classical tensor product, pure states are point distributions. A pure joint
point distribution has pure marginals, so a nontrivial classical mixture is
not the marginal of a pure classical state. Classical theory remains a
consistent probabilistic theory; it simply lacks the purification principle.

### 4.3 The smallest honest deletion

For a quantum density operator

$$
\rho_A=\sum_k p_k|k\rangle\langle k|,
\tag{4.2}
$$

one may construct

$$
|\Psi\rangle_{AE}=\sum_k\sqrt{p_k}|k\rangle_A|k\rangle_E,
\qquad
\operatorname{tr}_E|\Psi\rangle\langle\Psi|=\rho_A.
\tag{4.3}
$$

Equation (4.3) proves representability. The smallest deletion is to retain
the predictive equality while refusing to assert that this particular $E$,
basis, pure vector, or unitary completion is the material cause of the mixed
preparation. Operationally equivalent preparations can have different
laboratory lineages, and inaccessible purifiers are not selected by subsystem
data.

### 4.4 Recovery obligations and costs

A theory without fundamental purification must still account for:

1. the remarkable empirical success of unitary dilation and error correction;
2. reversible interference whenever the alleged environment is controlled;
3. conservation of information in closed quantum models at their tested
   scope;
4. irreversible thermodynamic and record behavior without confusing coarse
   graining with fundamental loss; and
5. why the CDP purification principle works so well if it is only effective.

A theory retaining physical purification must expose:

1. the actual purifying carrier;
2. its initial state and interaction law;
3. the reversible transformation connecting alternative purifications;
4. the physical discard operation;
5. environment-side records, energy, and resource costs; and
6. why the enlargement is not merely target-built Markovization.

### 4.5 Held-out test

Prepare the same tomographic mixed state through two physically distinct,
pre-registered source lineages. Candidate purification laws must predict,
without target tomography:

1. the complement associated with each lineage;
2. joint correlations with independently calibrated complement readers;
3. whether a complement-only reversible maps one purification to the other;
4. recoherence under a common joint continuation; and
5. energy/information flow during discard and recovery.

Subsystem tomography alone cannot decide the question. If no complement is
accessible, the proper result is O4/O6, not universal purity.

### 4.6 Current author-side inference

Purification is one of the most physically informative reconstruction
principles, precisely because it is not innocent. It may encode a deep
information-conservation law. It does not by itself identify the universe's
ontology or prove that all effective irreversibility is discard from one pure
whole.

## 5. N12 — an observed conditional is a causal completely positive map

### 5.1 Exact necessity record

| field | entry |
|---|---|
| $S_{12}$ | The conditional relation inferred between observed input and output states is the physical transformation caused by setting the input. |
| $D_{12}$ | A typed input system, output system, environment, preparation procedure, and intervention grammar. Equality holds only when the conditioning procedure implements the relevant intervention or the required screening/independence premises are proved. |
| $T_{12}$ | Often a type error: an inference conditional is substituted for an intervention map. Complete positivity belongs to the intervention-defined quantum map. |
| $R_{12}$ | A physically executable re-preparation that severs incoming causes, not passive sorting on an observed correlated variable. |
| $\Delta_{12}$ | Keep the observed joint distribution but represent passive inference and active re-preparation as distinct arrows. |
| $K_{12}$ | Recover ordinary CPTP dynamics under independent preparation, correlated open-system behavior, adaptive interventions, and process-tensor statistics. |
| $C_{12}$ | Explicit causal structure, re-preparation apparatus, environment marginal, process memory, and assumptions needed to identify the intervention experimentally. |
| $O_{12}$ | O5 if conditional and intervention are conflated; O1 when an intervention theorem establishes equality; O6 where interventions are unavailable; O7 if the two protocols differ experimentally. |

### 5.2 Exact classical counterexample

Let a fair common-cause bit $C$ determine two observed bits:

$$
X=C,
\qquad
Y=C.
\tag{5.1}
$$

Passive observation gives

$$
P(Y=y\mid X=x)=\delta_{y,x}.
\tag{5.2}
$$

Now intervene by replacing $X$ with an independently set value while leaving
the mechanism for $Y$ unchanged. Since $Y=C$ ignores $X$,

$$
P(Y=y\mid\operatorname{do}X=x)=\frac12.
\tag{5.3}
$$

The observational conditional is an identity channel; the causal map is a
randomizer. Both are ordinary positive and normalized. No quantum subtlety is
needed to expose the type error.

### 5.3 Quantum scope

For an initially correlated system and environment, attempting to fit passive
input--output pairs to one subsystem map can produce nonlinear, nonpositive,
or undefined objects. Schmid, Ried, and Spekkens show that the appropriate
quantum evolution map is defined by a counterfactual re-preparation of the
system, independent of its causal past, while retaining the environment
marginal and joint dynamics. That map is completely positive. The passive
inference conditional may be a different object.

This does not prove that every laboratory possesses the required
re-preparation. It states what must be physically implemented before the CP
map is called causal.

### 5.4 Recovery and held-out test

For the same source and later reader compare:

1. passive sorting on the naturally occurring input record;
2. randomized discard-and-reprepare of the input;
3. an informationally complete causal break;
4. interventions retaining versus erasing the earlier record; and
5. several later controls probing process memory.

If the passive conditional and re-preparation map agree under screened
conditions, that earns O1 for the tested interface. If they differ, the
difference localizes common-cause or memory information; it does not imply
that quantum evolution is fundamentally non-CP. If the input cannot be
reprepared without changing the parent experiment, only a process/regional
object is licensed.

### 5.5 Current author-side inference

N12 is the clearest hidden-assumption deletion in this packet. It is not a
new ontology proposal. It is a mandatory typing rule:

$$
\boxed{
P(\text{output}\mid\text{observed input})
\ne
P(\text{output}\mid\operatorname{do}(\text{input}))
\quad\text{without a bridge theorem}.}
\tag{5.4}
$$

### 6.2 Exact realification and its limit

For a complex matrix $M=A+iB$, define

$$
\mathfrak R(M)=
\begin{pmatrix}
A&-B\\ B&A
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
J=
\begin{pmatrix}
0&-I\\ I&0
\end{pmatrix}.
\tag{6.1}
$$

Then

$$
\mathfrak R(MN)=\mathfrak R(M)\mathfrak R(N),
\qquad
\mathfrak R(M^\dagger)=\mathfrak R(M)^T,
\qquad
[\mathfrak R(M),J]=0.
\tag{6.2}
$$

With

$$
\rho_{\mathbb R}=\frac12\mathfrak R(\rho),
\qquad
E_{\mathbb R}=\mathfrak R(E),
\tag{6.3}
$$

one has

$$
\operatorname{tr}_{\mathbb R}(\rho_{\mathbb R}E_{\mathbb R})
=\operatorname{tr}_{\mathbb C}(\rho E).
\tag{6.4}
$$

This is an exact predictive recoding. McKague, Mosca, and Gisin construct real
simulations of multipartite quantum evolution and measurement as well.

But (6.1) does not make the structure noncomplex in the algebraic sense. The
global $J$ selects the real two-plane rotation that plays multiplication by
$i$. Composition must transport one coherent $J$ across sources and
interventions. A per-experiment choice of $J$ is a compiler, not a source law.

### 6.3 What network experiments do and do not show

Renou and collaborators proved that complex quantum theory and the natural
real-Hilbert theory make different predictions in networks with independent
sources and the standard local tensor/composition premises. Subsequent
experiments report violations of that real-theory bound under stated source,
locality, measurement-independence, and sampling assumptions.

The legitimate inference is:

$$
\boxed{
\begin{gathered}
\text{real local Hilbert spaces}\\
+\ \text{standard independent-source tensor composition}\\
\text{are empirically inadequate}.
\end{gathered}}
\tag{6.5}
$$

It is not:

$$
\text{the universe is made of complex numbers}.
\tag{6.6}
$$

Real simulations that reproduce complex theory must violate or enlarge at
least one premise of the natural real theory—for example by carrying a shared
global complex-orientation resource or changing composition. That resource
must be charged rather than hidden.

### 6.4 Recovery and held-out test

The discriminating family must include:

1. independent sources with no hidden shared orientation unless declared;
2. composition and interchange of independently calibrated subsystems;
3. continuous reversible phase controls;
4. adaptive continuations and erasure/uncomputation;
5. network readers sensitive to relative rather than absolute phase; and
6. transfer to new systems without choosing a new $J$ after target access.

A real formulation that passes by carrying one universal, source-independent
$J$ has not eliminated complex predictive structure; it has shown that
complex numbers need not be material coordinates. A formulation that requires
shared per-target advice fails source completion. A genuinely different
phase-complete structure is admissible only if it predicts the same held-out
processes and exposes its resources.

### 6.5 Current author-side inference

The present evidence supports a distinction:

```text
complex coordinates as material substance:       unselected
phase-complete relational predictive structure:  required by continuations
one globally coherent complex orientation:        serious theorem target
Hilbert ontology from predictive success:         invalid converse
```

This leaves Barandes's Hilbert-secondary hypothesis open but does not let an
ordinary-positive endpoint law discard cross-context phase information.

## 7. N14 — a self-adjoint observable is an intrinsic possessed property

### 7.1 Exact necessity record

| field | entry |
|---|---|
| $S_{14}$ | A self-adjoint operator represents a context-independent property with a possessed spectral value that a measurement merely reveals. |
| $D_{14}$ | Spectral/Born quantum prediction plus an added value map. Kochen--Specker applies in dimension at least three under value definiteness, noncontextuality, and preservation of the required functional/orthogonality relations. |
| $T_{14}$ | The operator is a predictive object. The possessed-value reading is an ontology postulate and is obstructed in its naive universal noncontextual form. |
| $R_{14}$ | A physical measurement coupling, complete instrument, apparatus context, outcome record, and admitted common continuations. |
| $\Delta_{14}$ | Treat effects/operators as operational equivalence classes of outcome statistics; treat the physical reader as an instrument/coupling; do not assign every operator a context-independent pre-existing value. |
| $K_{14}$ | Recover Born statistics, compatibility, repeatability where observed, sequential disturbance, contextuality witnesses, and stable macroscopic properties. |
| $C_{14}$ | Apparatus context, transformation kernels, contextual response, preferred beable subset, or an explicit nonlocal/global ontology. |
| $O_{14}$ | O5 for the universal naive identity; O1 only for a restricted commuting/beable algebra with an explicit theorem; O6 among empirically equivalent contextual ontologies; O7 for new reader-sensitive predictions. |

### 7.2 Exact no-go scope

Kochen and Specker exhibit a finite set of directions in three-dimensional
Hilbert space for which no map to $\{0,1\}$ assigns exactly one value $1$ in
every orthonormal triple while giving the same projector the same value across
contexts. The theorem rejects the printed conjunction. It does not prove:

- that no physical property is ever possessed;
- that all observables are created from nothing at measurement;
- that contextual stochastic or global ontologies are impossible; or
- that one particular interpretation is true.

### 7.3 Effects are not instruments

Even before ontology, an observable/effect does not specify the physical
measurement transformation. Let $P_a=|a\rangle\langle a|$. The two
instruments

$$
\mathcal I_a^{\rm L}(\rho)=P_a\rho P_a,
\qquad
\mathcal I_a^{\rm R}(\rho)=
\operatorname{tr}(P_a\rho)|0\rangle\langle0|
\tag{7.1}
$$

have the same immediate probabilities

$$
\operatorname{tr}\mathcal I_a^{\rm L}(\rho)
=\operatorname{tr}\mathcal I_a^{\rm R}(\rho)
=\operatorname{tr}(P_a\rho),
\tag{7.2}
$$

but different post-measurement states. A later $Z$ reader separates them after
outcome $a=1$. Thus “measure $P_a$” is incomplete as a causal physical
description unless the instrument is typed.

### 7.4 Smallest deletion and recovery

The smallest deletion does not abolish observables. It replaces

```text
self-adjoint operator = intrinsic possessed value
```

with

```text
self-adjoint operator/effect = predictive equivalence class;
physical measurement = sourced coupling + instrument + record;
possessed-property claim = separately typed ontology statement.
```

Stable records may support an approximately commuting property algebra.
Position, matter density, flashes, or another restricted beable set may be
actual in a candidate ontology. Such a candidate must show how all other
operator statistics arise and where contextual dependence resides.

### 7.5 Held-out test

Implement several apparatuses with the same immediate POVM/effect but
different independently calibrated transformations. Apply common adaptive
continuations, including entangled-ancilla readers and attempted
uncomputation. The test distinguishes:

1. immediate statistic equivalence;
2. transformation/instrument inequivalence;
3. stable record formation;
4. any claimed context-independent property assignment; and
5. apparatus-dependent backaction.

The outcome can refute a possessed-value package or a reader model. It cannot
select an ontology solely from standard quantum agreement.

### 7.6 Current author-side inference

The naive identification is untenable at universal scope. The physically
serious replacement is not “nothing has properties”; it is that property,
reader, and intervention must be typed separately, with context dependence
charged rather than hidden.

### 8.2 Exact algebraic boundary

After measurement and environmental correlation, a typical pure global state
has the form

$$
|\Psi\rangle
=\alpha|0\rangle|R_0\rangle|E_0\rangle
+\beta|1\rangle|R_1\rangle|E_1\rangle.
\tag{8.1}
$$

Tracing the environment yields

$$
\rho_{SR}=
|\alpha|^2|0R_0\rangle\langle0R_0|
+|\beta|^2|1R_1\rangle\langle1R_1|
+\alpha\bar\beta\langle E_1|E_0\rangle
|0R_0\rangle\langle1R_1|+\text{h.c.}
\tag{8.2}
$$

When $\langle E_1|E_0\rangle\simeq0$, local interference is suppressed and
the record algebra is approximately classical. The global state (8.1) has not
acquired a stochastic selector term. A coherent inverse acting on all copied
degrees can in principle restore interference; local erasure while an
environment copy remains cannot.

This establishes the exact distinction:

$$
\text{decoherent record probabilities}
\not\Rightarrow
\text{one selected fine actuality}.
\tag{8.3}
$$

Zurek's decoherence programme explains pointer stability and environment
selection. Adler's measurement-problem analysis makes explicit that the
diagonal reduced state does not, by itself, derive one outcome. The v17 C4
audit reaches the same typing boundary across record-only, Barandes,
consistent-histories, coevent, Bohm, collapse, and Everett branches.

### 8.3 Recovery obligations and costs

Every candidate must print which of the following it claims:

1. only stable records are ontologically asserted;
2. one configuration/fine history is actual and a law selects it;
3. objective collapse selects a record with new parameters and deviations;
4. all relative-state branches persist and no single global selector exists;
5. truth is assigned by a non-Kolmogorov event/coevent rule; or
6. actuality is deliberately left open.

Calling decoherence “collapse” without changing (8.1) is not an additional
law. Adding a random seed is not a selector unless its distribution,
coupling, and empirical content are fixed. Treating the record-only stance as

### 8.4 Held-out tests

2. record-copy and erasure controls test stability and information placement;
3. mass, separation, time, heating, radiation, and noise test objective

### 9.2 Exact operational gauge witness

In a linear gate-set representation, a circuit probability has the form

$$
p(i_1,\ldots,i_n)
=\langle E|G_{i_n}\cdots G_{i_1}|\rho\rangle.
\tag{9.1}
$$

For any invertible representation change $B$,

$$
|\rho\rangle\mapsto B|\rho\rangle,
\qquad
\langle E|\mapsto\langle E|B^{-1},
\qquad
G_i\mapsto BG_iB^{-1},
\tag{9.2}
$$

and every circuit probability is unchanged. Physicality constraints and
canonical conventions restrict useful gauges, but data do not make the
coordinate choice material. Gate-set tomography explicitly characterizes
operations self-consistently and scores them by held-out circuit prediction;
it does not turn a gauge-fixed matrix entry into a unique microscopic cause.

There is a second, non-gauge underdetermination. Distinct Hamiltonians,
environments, noise decompositions, and Stinespring realizations can induce
the same reduced channel on the registered system. System-only process
tomography cannot distinguish them.

Using (9.4) as the input to a claimed derivation of the same target process is

5. history-growing sufficient interfaces; or

### 11.3 What survives all eight deletions

1. ordinary registered outcomes have nonnegative normalized probabilities;

   diagonal probability vector;

5. stable records and decoherence are real dynamical phenomena;
6. complex Hilbert quantum theory is the successful fixed-background
   predictive framework;

The deletions challenge their promotion to unique fundamental ontology, not

### 11.4 Where genuinely new physics would have to enter

1. **boundary selection:** a law determines which cuts and subsystem roles are

3. **intervention descent:** apparatus physics determines which counterfactual

4. **source completion:** material descriptors predict held-out process laws
   without target tomography; and
5. **actuality:** a law or explicit interpretation states what occurs in one
   run and, if empirical, predicts a discriminator.

Renaming a density matrix, dilation state, process tensor, or target quantum
law as a “positive configuration” does not fill any of these entries.
