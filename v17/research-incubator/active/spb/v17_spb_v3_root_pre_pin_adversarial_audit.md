## 2. Causal-identification attacks

### A1 — known nonrandom source is not a do-law

Let

$$
U\sim\operatorname{Bernoulli}(1/2),\qquad A=U,\qquad Y=U,
\tag{2.1}
$$

with no causal arrow `A -> Y`. Then

$$
P(Y=1\mid A=1)=1,
\qquad
P(Y=1\mid A=0)=0,
\tag{2.2}
$$

while

$$
P(Y=1\mid\operatorname{do}(A=a))=1/2.
\tag{2.3}
$$

The source can be perfectly logged and still provide no within-`U` overlap.
V3 assigns neither `ID-IDENTIFIED-R` nor `ID-IDENTIFIED-T` unless a genuine
randomizer or complete causal bridge is supplied. A source log alone routes to
`ID-UNIDENTIFIED`. The v2 counterexample is caught.

### A2 — a nominal random bit shares a hidden cause with the device

Let physical noise `U` influence both a pseudo-random controller bit and the
device outcome. Balanced treatment counts do not establish exogeneity. V3
requires the randomizer not to read the device/memory/outcome source and
requires a physical common-cause audit plus the explicit assignment-source
exogeneity premise. If the channel is detected, the assignment is invalid; if
it cannot be certified, the law is unidentified. This new attack forced the
pre-pin exogeneity sentence.

### A3 — positivity failure hidden inside a covariate cell

Let `A=0` whenever preassignment register `W=0` and `A=1` whenever `W=1`.
Marginally both assignments occur, but neither counterfactual occurs inside a
`W` cell. The strict per-cell `p_min` or density condition fails. Standardizing
over `W` cannot create support. V3 routes the primary comparison invalid or
unidentified rather than extrapolating silently.

### A4 — noncompliance is postselected

Suppose ten per cent of assigned resets time out and those attempts retain
memory. Conditioning on successful reset changes the estimand. V3 defines `A`
as assignment, retains execution and failure in `Y_star`, and states
consistency without conditioning on successful execution. The primary law is
intention-to-treat; successful-run analysis is secondary.

### A5 — carryover between nominal attempts

Let the reservoir after attempt `t` influence attempt `t+1`. IID shot analysis
is false even under a good per-shot randomizer. V3 requires a physical washout
bound or a registered block/cluster state and randomization. Unregistered
carryover makes the causal status unidentified or model-invalid.

### A6 — descendant adjustment deletes the effect

Let reset heat `H` be caused by the assigned memory operation and also predict
the future reader. Regressing on `H` can remove part of the physical effect.
V3 permits only preassignment/exogenous standardization and requires
future-accessible heat to remain scored. The mutant cannot pass as drift
correction.

### A7 — copied syntax versus material trace

If literal `h` is scored, two otherwise identical branches have TV one. V3
maps correct executions to the same `COMPLIANT` state. If a material controller
bit retains `h` and the licensed future can read it, that bit remains in `b` or
`n_star`, restoring a genuine TV difference. Syntax is removed and matter is
not.

Root result: the causal/record type survives all seven attacks. This is an
author-side result about the contract, not evidence that any physical
randomizer or transport bridge exists in a future packet.

## 3. Nonvacuity, margin, and power attacks

### B1 — maximal matching radius

Total variation has diameter one. V2 permitted `delta_B=1`, making every pair
matched. V3 requires

$$
0<\delta_B<1,
\qquad
\delta_B-\Delta_B^->0,
\qquad
\delta_B+\Delta_B^+<1,
\tag{3.1}
$$

with both separations positive, family error and power errors strictly between
zero and one-half, two-sided power, and an above-margin physical anchor.
`delta_B=1` is procedural invalidity.

### B2 — maximal screening margin

V2 permitted `epsilon_sci=1`. V3 requires

$$
0<\epsilon_c^{\rm sci}<1,
\quad
\epsilon_c^{\rm sci}-\Delta_c^->0,
\quad
\epsilon_c^{\rm sci}+\Delta_c^+<1,
\tag{3.2}
$$

with positive separations, nonvacuous familywise coverage, and power on both
sides. Maximal physical
disagreement can no longer pass by threshold choice.

### B3 — a formally submaximal but meaningless margin

Choose `epsilon=0.999` for a task whose physical loss changes materially at
TV `0.01`. The strict-diameter condition alone is not enough. V3 additionally
requires the margin to descend from a predeclared downstream physical
loss/decision functional and independent resolution. A reviewer can reject a
margin whose physical derivation is missing. The above-margin power condition
prevents an untestable outside region.

### B4 — perfect coverage with no power

A confidence interval `[0,1]` has coverage but decides nothing. V3 requires
power for true distances inside `epsilon-Delta_minus` and outside
`epsilon+Delta_plus`; unavailable power routes to underdetermined. Coverage
alone earns no pass.

### B5 — interval straddles the margin

At margin `0.10`, `[0.02,0.20]` satisfies neither pass nor fail. V3 routes it
only to `LOCAL-UNDERDETERMINED`. Candidate failure requires an established
local fail and no invalid item; missing branches never pass.

### B6 — threshold matching is chained into a quotient

At radius `delta`, the points `0`, `0.75 delta`, and `1.5 delta` show
nontransitivity. V3 keeps matching statistical and separate from exact
presentation. No chain of close pairs forms a carrier equivalence class.

### B7 — pair selection consumes the same data

Selecting the closest validation pair and reporting an unadjusted held-out
interval can under-cover. V3 places validation selection, all pairs,
candidates, duties, bins, regimes, bracketings, and stopping in one frozen
simultaneous family. Reusing held-out residuals is invalid.

Root result: the false-equivalence route is closed author-side without
selecting any numerical threshold. A future evaluation pin must still provide
the physical loss, margins, power calculations, and anchors; this review
cannot invent them.

## 4. Initial-law and stochastic-composition attacks

### C1 — conditional kernels without an initial law

Let the initial bit `X_0` be copied deterministically through every normalized
piece and seam. With no initial probability, both the all-zero and all-one
laws obey the kernels; counting measure has total mass two. V3 supplies

$$
\Lambda(d\lambda)\Pi_0(dx_0,dy_0\mid u_0,\lambda)
\tag{4.1}
$$

before the first piece. The deterministic special case is an explicit point
mass. Ionescu--Tulcea now has its required initial probability.

### C2 — preparation failure leaves the kernel domain

If `Pi_0` emits `PREP-FAIL` but `K_A` is defined only on successful `x_0`, the
composition is partial. V3 requires every downstream kernel to accept typed
failure/sentinel states or one absorbing attempt state. The complete law stays
normalized.

### C3 — incompatible cyclic kernels

For bits `X,Y`, the local conditions `X=Y` and `Y=1-X` admit no joint. They
route to `COMP-NONEXISTENT` only after model validity and exact certification.
No product-and-renormalize escape is allowed.

### C4 — nonunique cyclic kernels

The conditions `X=Y` in both directions admit every mixture

$$
p\,\delta_{(0,0)}+(1-p)\,\delta_{(1,1)}.
\tag{4.2}
$$

The target may not select `p`. Without a target-independent selector, V3
prints `COMP-NONUNIQUE` and no `C` prediction.

### C5 — model invalid and mathematical nonexistence both appear true

Take C3 and also fail stationarity. V3's priority selects
`COMP-MODEL-INVALID`; exact nonexistence may be a subordinate fact but is not a
second routing code. If model validity is uncertain, `COMP-UNRESOLVED`
controls.

### C6 — pairwise-good/direct-triple-bad

The iid fair-bit law and uniform even-parity law have identical one- and
two-bit marginals but

$$
d_{\rm TV}(P_{\rm iid},P_{\rm even})=1/2.
\tag{4.3}
$$

V3 requires direct pair targets and one direct `ABC` target. Pairwise success
cannot award direct composition.

### C7 — target-sized exact predictor

Store the exact target law for every connected control word. Prediction is
perfect, but the object fails target-independent descent and the sub-target
resource criterion. Connected data cannot choose a feedback joint,
normalization, `Pi_0`, seam correlation, adapter, or margin.

Root result: the complete composition type is normalized and target-blind
when its explicit premises are supplied. No premise is claimed to exist in the
current repository.

## 5. Deterministic seam and bracketing attacks

### D1 — nonunique interior, unique exterior

Take `K=0`, `S_se=0`, and `S_es=0`. Every internal seam amplitude solves the
equation while the exterior is unique. V3 routes the uniformly certified case
to `SEAM-INTERNAL-NONUNIQUE-EXTERIOR-UNIQUE`, not exterior failure.

### D2 — finite uncertainty spans no-solution and regular cases

For scalar `theta a=1`, let the simultaneous admissible set contain zero and
small nonzero `theta`. It certifies neither uniform nonexistence nor uniform
existence. V3 prints `SEAM-UNRESOLVED`; it does not force `NO-SOLUTION` or
`ILL-CONDITIONED`.

### D3 — both sequential pivots singular, global solve regular

For

$$
G=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix},
\qquad\det G=-1,
\tag{5.1}
$$

both scalar diagonal pivots are singular while the global solution is unique.
V3 prints

```text
(GLOBAL-DEFINED,PIVOT-SINGULAR,PIVOT-SINGULAR)
```

without discarding the exterior prediction.

### D4 — deterministic mean and stochastic law disagree in status

A coherent seam can have a unique mean while its shared noise conditionals
admit multiple joint laws. Conversely a stochastic regulator can yield one
measured distribution while invalidating the claimed linear model. V3 prints
one `SEAM-*` and one `COMP-*` status; neither fills the other.

Root result: the status products cover the predecessor counterexamples and
preserve the physical/mathematical distinction.

## 6. Carrier, coordinate, and ontology attacks

### E1 — finite response history renamed as matter

A vector of the last `k` outcomes may improve prediction on `S+M`. It is not

### E2 — higher regional kernel renamed as a collar extension

A target-independent Volterra or nonstationary kernel can describe the
existing collar without enlarging it. V3 permits `R-COORD-HR` but awards no
material extension. `R-B3` requires an additional material device not already
counted in `Sigma_R0`.

### E3 — experimenter-added history bank is called preexisting ontology

Actively attaching and writing `H_T,k` or `H_R,k` changes the experiment. V3
retains the write/coupling channel, loading, disturbance, records, and costs
and restricts the result to the augmented apparatus. It cannot show that the
unaugmented world possessed that bank.

### E4 — discrete record slots promoted to fundamental discreteness

The laboratory can have finite registered slots without reality being a
discrete web. V3 allows continuous material modes under frozen resolution and
explicitly denies a fundamental discreteness inference.

### E5 — density operator or `S` matrix promoted to carrier

The same response coordinate can be represented in multiple ways and can
omit physical ports, reservoirs, references, or noise. V3 requires
`Sigma_B`, physical instrument grammar, and source identity independently of
`X_B` and `K_B`. Operational success selects neither Hilbert ontology nor a
field ontology.

### E6 — reset called universal erasure

Reset exports records and entropy to controllers, resonators, reservoirs, and
heat. V3 keeps all accessible destinations and claims only a reference
distribution relative to the licensed future. No information-destruction
theorem appears.

### E7 — an admission type is counted as an apparatus candidate

Writing the symbol `H_T,k` or `H_R,k` does not supply a material device. V3
therefore treats each B3 form as a contract-level admission type only. A future
physical pin must instantiate exactly one member with the complete frozen
physical grammar or omit that row before acquisition. An uninstantiated form
cannot enter scoring, the candidate poset, minimality, or necessity, and data
cannot choose whether to instantiate it.

Root result: v3 tests material interfaces without assuming a discrete lattice,
trajectory ontology, Hilbert ontology, or abstract-history ontology.

## 7. Poset, minimality, and outcome-quantifier attacks

### F1 — two incomparable minimal passes

Let `p prec x`, `p prec y`, and `x` incomparable with `y`. Let `p` validly fail
and both `x,y` pass the same commensurate duty. V3 prints

$$
\operatorname{MinPass}_G=\{x,y\}
\tag{7.1}
$$

and `MINIMAL-ANTICHAIN`. Neither member is individually necessary.

### F2 — predecessor is merely unknown

If `p` is underdetermined while `x` passes, the definition requiring every
strict predecessor to validly fail is not met. `CANDIDATE-MINIMAL[x,G]` is
blocked.

### F3 — all registered candidates contain an augmentation

If every candidate was constructed with a reference oscillator, calling it
necessary is vacuous. V3 requires at least one proper competitor without the
augmentation and requires every such competitor to validly run and fail.

### F4 — unlike duties are compared in one poset

Let one candidate use a loose external loss/margin and another a strict one.
Their pass statuses answer different questions. V3 allows minimality and
necessity only for commensurate outcomes, policies, losses, margins, power,
regimes, and resolution.

### F5 — target-built predicts while a proper candidate is unknown

One proper candidate fails, one has a straddling interval, and a target table
predicts. V3 cannot print `ONLY-TARGET-BUILT-PREDICTS`, because every proper
candidate must be valid, run, and fail. It prints the local unknown instead.

### F6 — one transfer duty fails

Let `X_D=FAIL` while `X_S=X_C=X_I=PASS`. V3 prints only
`TRANSFER-FAIL[arm,B,X_D,rho_1]`; no aggregate transfer headline exists.

### F7 — candidate duty failure called physical nonexistence

A named boundary can physically exist while failing screening at one margin.
V3 prints `DUTY-FAIL[arm,B,S,rho_0]`, not “carrier physically refuted.”

Root result: every quantifier now has a declared domain and ignorance cannot
be converted into exclusion.
