## 3. New root defects found before pinning

Root did not merely check whether v1 nouns reappeared. Five additional defects
were found in the first v2 draft and repaired before this readiness hash.

### 3.1 Literal executed history reintroduced trivial TV

The first draft scored

$$

$$

while

$$
E=(\widetilde h,\widetilde i,\widetilde f,e_C).
$$

For histories $h\ne h'$, correct execution makes the event
$\{\widetilde h=h\}$ have probabilities one and zero. The total-variation
distance is again one before any physics is examined. This was the v1 defect
in new notation.

V2 now retains literal `E` in the archive but scores a frozen common projection

$$

The projection expresses compliance and physical deviations relative to the
assignment without copying the treatment label. It may not erase a physical
record: any controller log, seed carrier, heat load, pulse residue, or trace

### 3.2 `do` was applied to nonintervenable metadata

The first draft placed apparatus identity and data-split label inside `A` and
wrote `do(A=a)`. One cannot intervene on a serial number or on the fact that a

as immutable context from the physical assignment

$$

and defines $Q_{c,a}$ by conditioning on `C=c` and intervening on `A=a`.

### 3.4 Local stochastic kernels need not define a global law

bidirectional/feedback composition and requires a target-independent global

### 3.5 A passing larger carrier does not prove it was required

## 8. Countermodel E — local kernels can be incompatible or nonunique

Let $X,Y\in\{0,1\}$.

### 8.1 No joint law

Take deterministic local specifications

$$
P(X=Y\mid Y)=1,
\qquad
P(Y=1-X\mid X)=1.
$$

No pair $(X,Y)$ satisfies both. Normalized local kernels exist, but no global
joint law realizes them.

### 8.2 Multiple joint laws

Take

$$
P(X=Y\mid Y)=1,
\qquad
P(Y=X\mid X)=1.
$$

Both $\delta_{(0,0)}$ and $\delta_{(1,1)}$, and their mixtures, realize the
local conditions. The local kernels do not select one global law.

Therefore a bidirectional physical seam cannot be predicted merely by
multiplying conditional kernels and renormalizing. V2 requires a frozen
compatibility construction and admits nonexistence, nonuniqueness, and
unresolved outcomes. Direct target data may falsify one frozen joint
prediction; it may not choose one after the fact.

With


$$

seam solutions obey

$$

$$

The following properties are logically distinct:

1. existence:
   $\operatorname{Ran}(VS_{se})\subseteq\operatorname{Ran}(K)$;
2. internal uniqueness: $\ker K=\{0\}$;
3. exterior uniqueness: $S_{es}\ker K=\{0\}$; and
4. useful conditioning under the frozen uncertainty budget.

For $K=0$, $S_{se}=0$, and $S_{es}=0$, internal amplitudes are arbitrary but
the exterior response is unique. If the right-hand side leaves the range of
`K`, no seam solution exists. An invertible but near-singular `K` can produce
an unusable prediction. A global three-module solve can also be regular while
one sequential Schur pivot is singular.

calling an algorithmic pivot failure a physical associativity failure.
