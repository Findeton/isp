## 2. Typed attempted-run law

### 2.1 Assignment, execution, and outcome

For either arm, one attempted run is

$$
\omega=(C,A,E,Y).
\tag{2.1}
$$

The immutable design context is

$$
C=(j,s,\rho,c),
\tag{2.2}
$$

where:

- `j` is the immutable apparatus/configuration instance;
- `s` identifies the immutable calibration/training/validation/held-out split;
- `rho` identifies the frozen primary or transfer regime; and
- `c` identifies the predeclared comparison cell and common outcome schema.

These are conditioning/stratification coordinates. They are not all things
one can physically intervene on. In particular the contract never writes a
`do` operation on an apparatus serial, a data-split label, or a regime label.

The assigned physical intervention is

$$
A=(h,i,f,z),
\tag{2.3}
$$

where `h` is the assigned past-history or interior preparation, `i` the
assigned boundary instrument or substitution, `f` the assigned future,
exterior, or reconnection policy, and `z` a randomized auxiliary assignment
or controller seed. The pin states whether `z` is fixed, paired, or averaged
under one predeclared assignment law. A physically retained or later
accessible seed carrier is also represented in the physical boundary/nuisance
record; calling the numerical seed an assignment coordinate does not erase
that carrier.

The execution transcript is

$$
E=(\widetilde h,\widetilde i,\widetilde f,e_C),
\tag{2.4}
$$

where tildes are executed settings and `e_C` contains compiler, controller,
acknowledgement, timing, compliance, retry, and data-routing records.

The physical observation is

$$
Y=(b,r,n,q),
\tag{2.5}
$$

where:

- `b` contains boundary-instrument outcomes and boundary-side retained
  records;
- `r` is the future/exterior scientific reader transcript;
- `n` is the registered calibration, reference, nuisance, environment, and
  drift transcript; and
- `q` is the attempt-quality state, including abort, timeout, invalid,
  noncompliance, leakage, saturation, overwritten, missing, stale-calibration,
  hardware-failure, and anomaly states.

The literal transcript `E` is retained in the immutable archive. It is not
itself placed in a comparison between two deliberately different assigned
histories. Instead the pin freezes a comparison-invariant execution projection

$$
E^\star=\kappa_{C,A}(E),
\tag{2.6}
$$

whose common coordinates include compliance status, deviations from the
assigned operation in common physical units, retries, latency, compiler and
controller acknowledgements, and routing/failure states. It does **not**
include the literal history/program label `h` or `tilde h`. Correct execution
of either member of a comparison therefore maps to the same typed
`COMPLIANT` state; a history-specific deviation maps to a predeclared common
error coordinate. Any physical consequence of the different histories
remains available in `b,r,n,q`.

This projection removes bookkeeping labels, not physical records. Any
controller log, seed carrier, pulse residue, heat load, or execution trace
that remains accessible to the licensed future must appear in `b` or `n`
under the frozen access graph. It may be omitted from the screening outcome
only with a physical inaccessibility certificate, never because an analyst
chooses not to read it.
The family `kappa` is frozen before pair selection, uses one common error
taxonomy and physical units, and may not apply history-specific binning or map
a measured deviation/failure to `COMPLIANT`. Its only treatment-dependent
role is to express execution relative to the assigned operation without
copying that operation's syntactic identity into the score.

The primary scored outcome is

$$
Y^\star=(E^\star,b,r,n^\star,q),
\tag{2.7}
$$

where `n_star` is the predeclared nuisance projection used by the primary
claim. Design and assigned-treatment labels are not scored outcomes.
Execution deviations, noncompliance, and their physical effects remain
outcomes and are never postselected away. The root hostile test is explicit:
if either `h` or `tilde h` can be reconstructed deterministically from
`Y_star` merely because its label was copied into the schema, the screening
estimand is invalid before data.

### 2.2 One common measurable outcome space

Every comparison cell `c` has one frozen measurable space

$$
(\mathcal Y_c,\mathcal F_c).
\tag{2.8}
$$

Every admitted assignment in that cell maps into the same schema. A field not
produced on a branch receives a typed sentinel such as `NOT-APPLICABLE`,
`NO-RECORD`, `TIMEOUT`, or `INACCESSIBLE`; columns are not deleted branch by
branch. The schema explicitly states whether boundary outcomes, pre-cut
records, timestamps, waveforms, seeds, and nuisance monitors occur in
`Y_star` or only in the complete archival record.

The full attempted-run parent

$$
P(dC,dA,dE,dY)
\tag{2.9}
$$

is normalized over every issued attempt. The scientific screening law is not
the marginal of an answer-selected subset.

### 2.3 Interventional kernel and estimand

The primary intention-to-treat kernel is

$$
Q_{c,a}(dy)
=
P\!\left(Y^\star\in dy\mid C=c,\operatorname{do}(A=a)\right).
\tag{2.10}
$$

It is identified by an implemented randomized or otherwise source-closed
assignment mechanism frozen before held-out acquisition. Noncompliance,
compiler substitutions, missing records, and device failure remain in
`Y_star`.

A comparison that averages auxiliary assignments uses the single frozen law

$$
Q_{c,h,i,f}(dy)
=\int Q_{c,(h,i,f,z)}(dy)\,\mu_c(dz),
\tag{2.11}
$$

not history-specific empirical seed weights. A per-protocol or compliance-
conditioned analysis may be secondary only if
its principal strata or causal assumptions are preregistered. Recording a
command is not by itself evidence that the command was executed or that an
observational conditional equals equation (2.10).

### 2.4 Standardization across nuisance blocks

If two assignments occur in different drift or calibration blocks, the
primary comparison either:

1. uses randomized interleaving within a common block;
2. transports both through one frozen, validated standardization kernel; or
3. returns underdetermined.

Within an arm and registered regime, all candidates are projections or
extensions of one frozen encompassing nuisance/reference parent. A larger
candidate may expose additional registered coordinates, but it may not receive
a separately fitted nuisance law chosen from its held-out residual.
Standardization may use only variables justified as pre-assignment or
independently exogenous for the comparison. A descendant of the assigned
history, boundary intervention, or latent physical memory cannot be regressed
away as “drift”; uncertainty about that status routes to underdetermined.

## 5. Exact presentation symmetry versus empirical matching

### 5.1 Presentation groupoid

`G_pres` contains exact arrows between descriptions of the same fixed physical
interface. Every arrow acts simultaneously on carrier labels, preparations,
instruments, readers, response coordinates, and records. It is frozen before
the candidate interiors/histories and held-out comparisons are inspected.

No empirical closeness threshold belongs to `G_pres`. A physical adapter,
active cable permutation, controller transformation, reset, or state change is
an instrument, not presentation.

### 5.2 Boundary matching is not an equivalence quotient

For histories/interiors `h,h'`, calibration/validation copies produce
boundary-reader kernels

$$
M_h(dx),\qquad M_{h'}(dx)
\tag{5.1}
$$

on one common boundary-validation outcome space. The pair is *matched at
resolution* `delta_B` when the simultaneous upper bound obeys

$$
U_B[d_B(M_h,M_{h'})]\leq\delta_B.
\tag{5.2}
$$

This is a statistical relation and need not be transitive. It is never used as
a quotient. Its selection uncertainty belongs to the global multiplicity
family.

### 5.3 Nonvacuity certificate

Every screened pair must also satisfy a predeclared separation test on an
earlier/interior record `W`:

$$
L_W[d_W(H_h,H_{h'})]>\eta_W.
\tag{5.3}
$$

Duplicating one program under two labels fails this test. If no nontrivial pair
both separates in history and matches at the boundary, screening is
underdetermined rather than vacuously true.

Destructive boundary validation uses matched copies and never the scored
continuation run.

## 6. Screening estimand and decision rule

### 6.1 Screening family

For each predeclared matched nontrivial pair `(h,h')`, boundary instrument `i`,
future/exterior policy `f`, candidate `B`, and regime `rho_0`, compare

$$
Q^{B,\rho_0}_{h,i,f}(dy)
\quad\text{and}\quad
Q^{B,\rho_0}_{h',i,f}(dy)
\tag{6.1}
$$

on the same `(Y_c,F_c)` from Section 2.2. Both are intention-to-treat kernels
under the frozen assignment mechanism.

The primary distance is total variation for a finite frozen outcome schema.
If the raw record is continuous, the pin must freeze either a measurable
coarse-graining with a physical resolution argument or a density model with
validated coverage. It may also preregister additional distances sensitive to
timing, phase, tails, or rare failures. No distance is selected after seeing a
residual.

### 6.2 Scientific margin versus measurement uncertainty

Each primary comparison has a scientific negligibility margin

$$
\epsilon^{\rm sci}_{B,h,h',i,f}.
\tag{6.2}
$$

It is derived from the maximum downstream record change compatible with the
claimed predictive use, plus an independently justified physical resolution.
Reader noise, calibration uncertainty, and drift do not enlarge
`epsilon_sci`; they widen the confidence interval for the physical distance.

A boundary with an uninformative reader is underdetermined, not sufficient.

### 6.3 Three-way local decision

Let `[L_c,U_c]` be a simultaneous confidence set for comparison `c`. Then

```text
PASS_c             iff U_c <= epsilon_sci,c
FAIL_c             iff L_c >  epsilon_sci,c
UNDERDETERMINED_c  otherwise
```

Exact equality is not required unless the pin separately justifies a zero
margin and adequate power.

### 6.4 Candidate-level decision

For a candidate and one duty family:

```text
PASS             iff every mandatory comparison is PASS_c
FAIL             iff at least one mandatory comparison is FAIL_c
UNDERDETERMINED  otherwise
INVALID          iff pin, assignment, schema, coverage, or source identity fails
```

Missing mandatory branches do not count as passes. An inaccessible branch can
be registered as infeasible only before acquisition; its absence narrows the
claim or yields underdetermination.

### 6.5 Multiplicity and adaptive candidate search

One frozen family covers:

- validation-based pair matching and history separation;
- all candidates in the registered lattice;
- all history/interior pairs;
- boundary instruments and future/exterior policies;
- composition targets and seam interventions;
- frequency/time/power bins;
- global and sequential seam calculations; and
- any stopping or sequential acquisition rule.

Candidates are evaluated in their frozen order. A later candidate may be
reported after an earlier one fails, but its confidence budget and sample split
were allocated before data. No post-hoc boundary enlargement is allowed.

## 7. General stochastic composition contract

Each independently calibrated piece `k` supplies a boundary instrument kernel

$$
\mathsf K_k(dy_k,dx_k^{\rm out}\mid
x_k^{\rm in},u_k,\lambda),
\tag{7.1}
$$

where `x_in,x_out` are boundary response coordinates, `u_k` is the assigned
local operation, and `lambda` is the shared nuisance/reference parent. A seam
kernel

$$
\mathsf V(d x_{k+1}^{\rm in},dy_V\mid x_k^{\rm out},\lambda)
\tag{7.2}
$$

models the physical connector, intermediate instrument, reset, reference, and
its records.

The frozen composition operation is the typed marginalization/constraint
composition

$$
\widehat Q_{ABC}
=
\mathsf{Comp}_{\rho_0}
(\mathsf K_A,\mathsf V_{AB},\mathsf K_B,
 \mathsf V_{BC},\mathsf K_C;\Lambda),
\tag{7.3}
$$

on the same complete outcome schema as the direct physical `ABC` attempt.
`Lambda` is one independently calibrated joint nuisance/reference law. It may
contain correlations; independence is not silently assumed.

For an acyclic temporal wiring, equation (7.3) means the ordinary iterated
kernel integral under `Lambda`; normalization then follows from the frozen
kernel domains and the Ionescu--Tulcea construction. For a bidirectional or
feedback wiring, a product of conditional kernels is not automatically a
joint probability law. The pin must instead give a target-independent global
compatibility construction and classify it as:

```text
COMP-WELL-POSED
  one normalized complete-attempt prediction exists for every admitted
  exterior assignment and nuisance stratum;

COMP-NONEXISTENT
  the frozen local kernels and seam constraints admit no joint law;

COMP-NONUNIQUE
  more than one complete joint law has the same frozen local/seam kernels;

COMP-UNRESOLVED
  existence or uniqueness cannot be certified at the registered resolution;

COMP-MODEL-INVALID
  the domain, instrument, stationarity, source, or kernel assumptions fail.
```

Only `COMP-WELL-POSED` produces a composition prediction eligible for `C`.
The direct connected target may test that prediction but may not choose among
multiple compatible joint laws, supply a normalization factor, or fit a
missing cross-seam correlation.

Composition earns `C` only when:

1. every kernel, seam, parameter-sharing rule, and nuisance family is frozen
   without the direct `ABC` target;
2. equation (7.3) is normalized over success, failure, missing, invalid, and
   anomaly outcomes;
3. a simultaneous predictive interval is classified by Section 6;
4. the direct `ABC` experiment is physically executed;
5. at least one registered seam intervention or interior substitution occurs;
6. resource growth is sub-target under the frozen criterion; and
7. no connected-target tomography, de-embedding, or residual fitting enters
   the prediction.

The two algebraic bracketings are conformance calculations on the same
physical network. They are not two empirical worlds.

### 9.2 Deterministic and stochastic response coordinates

The deterministic coordinate is

$$
b(\nu)=S_B(\nu)a(\nu).
\tag{9.3}
$$

It predicts coherent response only inside a validated linear,
time-stationary, single-mode regime.

The complete boundary coordinate is a stochastic kernel

$$
\mathsf K_B(db,dy_B,dq_B\mid a,u_B,\lambda),
\tag{9.4}

- missing/invalid/failure outcomes; and
- any correlations with the shared nuisance parent `lambda`.

No Gaussian, independent-noise, or zero-noise assumption is implicit. A
deterministic `S` result may be reported as a nested coherent-response control
but cannot by itself earn complete-law screening or composition.

### 9.4 Passive presentation groupoid

Let `p_1,...,p_m` be the fixed physical ports. A passive presentation arrow
`g` simultaneously changes coordinate names and reference phases in every
source, response, reader, and record:

$$
a\mapsto U_g a,\qquad
b\mapsto U_g b,\qquad
S\mapsto U_g S U_g^{-1}.
\tag{9.5}
$$

`U_g` is limited to the frozen representation of the same physical
cross-sections and references. The underlying serial-labelled port-to-cable
map does not change.

An active cable permutation is an assigned instrument `P_pi`. A physical

### 9.5 Interior matching and fixed-interface screening

## 10. Regional seam calculus and stochastic composition

### 10.1 Deterministic seam equations

For the combined modules, write

$$
b_e=S_{ee}a_e+S_{es}a_s,
\qquad
b_s=S_{se}a_e+S_{ss}a_s,
\qquad
a_s=Vb_s.
\tag{10.1}
$$

Define

$$
K=I-VS_{ss}.
\tag{10.2}
$$

The primary global classifier is:

```text
SEAM-REGULAR
  K invertible within the frozen conditioning budget.

SEAM-INTERNAL-NONUNIQUE-EXTERIOR-UNIQUE
  Range(V S_se) subset Range(K), Ker(K) nonzero,
  and S_es Ker(K) = {0}.

SEAM-NO-SOLUTION
  Range(V S_se) is not a subset of Range(K) for admitted inputs.

SEAM-EXTERIOR-NONUNIQUE
  solutions exist but S_es Ker(K) is nonzero.

SEAM-ILL-CONDITIONED
  the global solve exists but propagated uncertainty exceeds the
  preregistered conditioning budget.

SEAM-MODEL-INVALID
  linearity, stationarity, admitted-mode, connector, or reference-plane
  validation fails.
```

The classifier is applied in the frozen order `MODEL-INVALID`,
`NO-SOLUTION`, `EXTERIOR-NONUNIQUE`,
`INTERNAL-NONUNIQUE-EXTERIOR-UNIQUE`, `ILL-CONDITIONED`, `REGULAR`.
`ILL-CONDITIONED` is evaluated only after existence and exterior uniqueness
hold; `REGULAR` additionally requires the conditioning budget. The report may
print subordinate true properties, but exactly one primary status is used for
routing. No category is deleted after seeing the direct composite.

### 10.3 Stochastic seam composition

The module kernels in equation (9.4), connector kernels, shared source and
receiver reference, and correlated nuisance law are composed by equation
(7.3). The deterministic seam solution is the conditional coherent coordinate
inside that kernel, not the whole probability law.

Noise emitted into a seam can propagate into other modules. The prediction
must therefore include the registered joint noise-wave relation rather than
adding independent output errors after the star product. If the required seam
noise correlations cannot be independently calibrated, full stochastic
composition is underdetermined; only the coherent-response control can be
reported.

The deterministic `K=I-VS_ss` classifier does not by itself establish a
stochastic joint law. Every regional prediction therefore also prints one
`COMP-*` status from Section 7. A unique exterior mean with nonunique internal
amplitudes may coexist with nonunique noise laws; conversely a stochastic
regularization may define a unique measured distribution while invalidating
the claimed deterministic model. Neither implication is assumed. The pin
must state the finite-bandwidth state/noise variables, the joint connector
law, boundary conditions, and the theorem or numerical certificate used for
existence, uniqueness, and normalization. Target data cannot select the
certificate.

\tag{10.3}
$$

The target composite is never used to fit connector de-embedding, adapter
