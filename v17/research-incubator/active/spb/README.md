## Purpose

SPB tests a premise shared by many otherwise different physical formalisms:

> Does an independently identified physical interface screen the rest of a
> complete experiment and support held-out composition without encoding the
> target process?

It contains two separately scored arms.

1. **SPB-T** tests a temporal causal break while independently identified
   memory is read, toggled, randomized, erased, or isolated.
2. **SPB-R** tests regional interior substitution, physical reconnection, and
   three-region gluing at a fixed external interface.

The arms share five duties—descent, screening, composition, physical
intervention/readout, and no-refit transfer—but temporal sufficiency does not
imply regional sufficiency, or conversely.

## Authority boundary

The user authorized proceeding with the Sufficient Physical Boundary Gate.
That authorization opens its bounded preparation, freeze, review, and
evaluation sequence. It does not make an unavailable apparatus or missing raw
record exist, and it does not license simulated data to stand in for physical
data.

Before evaluation, the then-current exact apparatus packet, intervention
grammar, readers, nuisance model, held-outs, costs, outcome ladder, and source
bytes must be rehashed in an immutable pin. Any material platform change
requires a new differential audit and pin.

No SPB outcome may by itself establish a universal state ontology, Markovian
universe, tensor-factor ontology, chronology, spacetime, gravity, or a final
theory.
