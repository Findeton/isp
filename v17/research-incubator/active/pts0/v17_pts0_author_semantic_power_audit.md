## 2. The defining semantic correction

The rejected unconditional quantity was of the form

$$
\max_{h,h'}d_{\rm TV}(P(Y_F\mid h),P(Y_F\mid h')).
\tag{2.1}
$$

It would reject a genuine memory merely because different memory states
correctly generate different futures. PTS-0 instead tests

$$
Y_F\perp H\mid B,\operatorname{do}(\pi)
\tag{2.2}
$$

by separating two pair types.

### 2.1 `B0` test

`H-M0` and `H-M1` produce the same randomly assigned fresh `S` preparation
and different `M` states. Separate validation copies test equality of `S`.
Residual complete-future dependence then refutes the registered `S` boundary.
This is omitted-memory evidence, not indivisibility evidence.

### 2.2 `B1` test

`P-LOOP` and `P-CONTROLLER` contain different earlier histories intended to
end in the same operationally resolved `S+M` state. Nine joint reader settings
on validation copies test that premise. Scored copies receive no destructive
boundary read and no full `S+M` reset.

The design therefore does not punish `M` for retaining different pasts. It
asks whether histories remain predictive after their candidate boundary
coordinates are independently matched.

## 3. Natural sufficiency, restart, and erasure

Root attacked three possible conflations.

### 3.1 Full-boundary restart

If an author inserts `M-RESET` to make two `B1=S+M` histories equal, the
natural-state question has changed. The executable semantics routes
`ForcedRestart` to `INVALID` for natural sufficiency. Restart may be retained
as an unscored control only.

### 3.2 Isolation

An isolated `M` may no longer influence `S` and may still contain a perfect
record of the past. The first author draft incorrectly risked requiring the
complete future law to forget the history after isolation. Before package
closure, root split the obligations:

- `Y_S` tests whether the history-to-system channel is suppressed;
- `Y_M` tests whether the memory is readable; and
- `Y_FULL` retains all joint, controller, reservoir, and failure records.

No absence of an `S` effect becomes global information erasure.

### 3.3 Reset and randomization

Reset and inaccessible-seed randomization may move information into a seed,
controller, resonator, reservoir, heat record, or old-state destination. Those
records remain in the raw law and enter the registered trace bit. The absence
obligation applies only to the frozen system-future projection. This is a
bounded causal-channel statement, not Landauer erasure or destruction of
actual information.

## 4. Physical memory descent

The design does not call a fitted latent variable matter. `D_M` requires:

1. a reader that distinguishes the two written memory histories;
2. baseline history effects under exchange- and phase-sensitive policies;
3. a null memory-parked future with no system effect at resolution;
4. toggle sensitivity under both memory-sensitive policies;
5. bounded read-backaction controls;
6. suppression of the system history channel after randomize, reset, isolate,
   and reset-isolate controls;
7. a causal difference between those operations and `M-ID`; and
8. held-out transfer to `PI-PHASE`.

The operations are randomized. Direct drive leakage into `S`, controller
confounding, missing assignment cells, failed reset, missing seeds, and
unblocked carryover are not evidence of memory; they route invalid,
contradicted, or underdetermined as typed.

One confidently contradicted registered obligation makes the complete
memory-descent bundle fail. This means the *bundle* failed; its exact reason
must be printed. It does not mean no physical memory exists.

## 5. Outcome-space audit

### 5.1 Exhaustiveness and disjointness

Every issued attempt first receives one of 16 terminal codes under a fixed
priority. The code is crossed with `G=0/1`, where `G` reports whether any
predeclared controller/reference/reservoir coordinate entered a nonnominal
registered bin. The primary alphabet therefore contains 32 cells.

The initial 16-cell author draft retained controller traces only in raw
storage. Root rejected that as insufficiently complete at the registered
resolution and added the explicit cross-classification before this audit.

The raw record remains richer than the score. That is allowed only because:

- the projection and every bin are fixed before data;
- continuous raw coordinates remain stored;
- a malformed or unknown record invalidates the affected duty; and
- the maximum claim is explicitly at the registered finite resolution.

### 5.2 No syntactic distance

The assigned history, candidate label, and intervention name are not copied
into `Y_F`. Their literal execution records remain in `E` and `Y_raw` for
causal and conformance checks. Thus two histories do not become distance one
merely because their labels differ.

### 5.3 No successful-run conditioning

Preparation failures, break failures, operation failures, policy failures,
readout failures, leakage, timeout, stale records, and other registered
failures occupy ordinary score cells. The law is intention to treat.

## 6. Classifier reconstruction

### 6.1 Boundary rows

For valid nonempty intervals, exactly one of these applies:

```text
matched + screen pass;
matched + screen fail;
matched + screen unresolved;
boundary mismatch;
boundary-match unresolved.
```

Invalid procedure, malformed/empty confidence set, or forced full-boundary
restart has an earlier invalid route. Since `epsilon_pass=0.10` and
`epsilon_fail=0.25`, a valid interval cannot be both pass and fail.

### 6.2 Candidate aggregation

The ordered map is:

1. any invalid row -> `INVALID`;
2. any matched fail row -> `FAIL`;
3. every row matched pass -> `PASS`;
4. otherwise -> `UNDERDETERMINED`.

This correctly permits one matched counterexample to refute a bounded
universal. An unmatched pair cannot refute the candidate. A candidate cannot
pass while one registered pair is unmatched, unresolved, or unrun.

### 6.3 Memory aggregation

An expected-present or expected-absent distance has exactly one obligation
state: invalid, satisfied, contradicted, or unresolved. The vector map is
invalid first, contradiction second, unanimous satisfaction third, and
underdetermined otherwise.

Memory and boundary decisions are returned in different Rust types and in
different report coordinates. The checker includes a mutant in which memory
passes while the boundary remains unresolved.

## 8. Statistical and power reconstruction

### 8.1 Why the model alternatives are nonempty

The primary model is the full 32-simplex in every assigned cell. The design
also names physical circuit witnesses rather than relying on simplex geometry
alone:

- cancelling pulse histories and a parked memory populate pass interiors;
- `M0/M1` plus partial iSWAP populate the `B0` fail interior;
- direct preparation versus `U_SM U_SM^dagger` populates the `B1` pass
  interior;
- an exterior resonator/controller memory with matched `S+M` populates the
  `B1` fail interior; and
- coupled versus parked `M` modes populate memory-pass and memory-fail
  interiors.

Ideal witness distances are zero or one. Total-variation balls of radius
0.025 around the corresponding laws remain inside the 0.05/0.30 power
interiors. Both alternatives are therefore nonempty before data.

### 8.2 Why decision thresholds are not power boundaries

Uniform high probability of certifying `d<=epsilon` is not possible for all
laws exactly at `d=epsilon` with honest finite uncertainty. The user-requested
uniform power duty therefore requires interior margins. PTS-0 uses:

```text
decision pass     d <= 0.10 by upper confidence bound;
power-pass truth  d <= 0.05;
decision fail     d >= 0.25 by lower confidence bound;
power-fail truth  d >= 0.30.
```

This is a correction necessary to make the authorized power requirement
mathematically possible, not post-hoc retuning.

### 8.3 Drift and dependence

For each assigned cell, the target is its balanced randomized-schedule average
conditional law. The subset indicators centered on their conditional means
are bounded martingale differences. The finite-alphabet union bound therefore
does not require i.i.d. outcomes or stationarity.

This protection does not identify effects under confounded assignment and does
not make uncontrolled carryover harmless. Physical randomization, within-block
positivity, washout/reset, and complete attempt lineage remain validity
conditions.

### 8.4 Numeric rebuild

With 32 outcome cells, 148 cell laws, per-law TV radius `a=0.0125`, and family
error 0.01, the minimum integer satisfying

$$
2\times148\times(2^{32}-2)e^{-2na^2}\le0.01
$$

is 103,924 attempts per cell. The smallest multiple of 32 acquisition blocks
is 103,936, or 3,248 attempts per cell per block. The exact primary total is
15,382,528 issued attempts.

The resulting bound is `0.009962464798987583`. The pair-distance interval
radius is `2a=0.025`. On the simultaneous event:

- a true distance at most 0.05 has upper endpoint at most 0.10; and
- a true distance at least 0.30 has lower endpoint at least 0.25.

Uniform pass/fail power on the registered interior neighborhoods therefore
exceeds 0.99, conditional on causal validity and the finite outcome law.

The large shot count is a conservative author-side reference calculation. It
is not a claim that a particular device can sustain the schedule, and it does
not authorize reducing the count after acquisition. Device runtime,
calibration lifetime, and block feasibility belong to a future physical
packet that is presently unauthorized.
