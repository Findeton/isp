## 2. Required corrections

### F1 — the parent is not the universe

The phrase `finite-resource parent law P` can be read as a proposed fundamental
law of reality. The gate only warrants a complete operational parent packet for
one laboratory domain. Its accessed resources may be finite while a completion
is continuous or infinite-dimensional. Success cannot select discreteness,
finite ontology, or a cosmological state.

### F2 — conditional agreement needs the root law

Theorem-package Theorem 6.1 says equality of every positive-support adaptive
conditional is equivalent to equality of complete transcript measures. This is
false without equality of the initial/root marginal. Two processes can have the
same conditional kernels and different initial distributions.

The corrected third clause is:

> the root/preparation marginal and every registered positive-support adaptive
> conditional agree.

### F3 — laboratory data are not an exactly known measure

The symbol `mu_lab,e` must denote either a separately calibrated comparator
model or the data-generating law hypothesized by that model. Actual held-out data
are finite samples. Adequacy is decided by a preregistered likelihood, distance,
confidence/credible region, multiplicity rule, covariance model, stopping rule,
and missing/null-event policy. Exact equality to an empirical histogram is not
the target.

Held-out evidence must be produced after parent freeze or sequestered before
model selection. Public evidence may guide architecture selection but cannot be
reused as the promotion-bearing test.

### F4 — the finite coherence schema needs physicality hypotheses

The coherence-versus-diagonal formula is completely positive when its Gram
matrix is positive semidefinite and it admits a Kraus factorization. It is trace
preserving only when the weighted branch completeness equation holds. The input
must be a state (or the adjoint convention must be stated), and the coherent
pointer is a quantum memory rather than a classical record algebra.

The schema remains only a discriminator after the selected model derives both
physical instruments and complete readers. It cannot repair Paper 04 by calling
a coherent premeasurement a retained classical clock reading.

### F5 — stopped is task-relative

Absence of comparator-sensitive phase transport proves only that a sector is
stopped for the frozen clock task and reader family. It does not prove that the
subsystem has no dynamics or cannot clock another comparator.

### F6 — scalar redundancy is factorization, not a spelling test

An auxiliary parameter may appear in a calculation while every operational
prediction factors through a parameter-independent quotient. Conversely, a
hidden schedule can be encoded without a symbol named `lambda`.

The exact gate is therefore a factorization/noninterference claim:


with invariance under the registered reparameterization family and falsification
by interventions that would expose a hidden schedule. Preparation, readout, and
causal-slot order remain supplied laboratory context.

### F7 — the Barandes lift is a compatibility test

The endpoint pushforward in the package is what a future native packet would
need to satisfy **to reproduce this operational experiment**. It is not a
general axiom demanded by Barandes, does not construct a path measure, and does
not select a configuration ontology, clock variable, division event, or actual
trajectory.
