## 3. Frozen distinctions

### 3.1 Relative observable

A relative observable is invariant under the declared joint symmetry and refers
to relations among physical systems. It may be noncommutative with other relative
observables. Its existence does not write a record.

### 3.2 Quantum memory

A quantum memory is an admitted noncommutative carrier retaining coherent control
information. A basis label inside it is not automatically a classical outcome.

### 3.3 Operational classical record

An operational classical record is a commutative output factor with
state-independent symmetry action and an exposed normalized instrument. Its sector
projectors are central in the declared complete operational output boundary. This
classifies a usable laboratory output; it does not classify every possible native
beable.

### 3.4 Operationally stable record

An operational record is stable relative to a frozen represented future category
when every licensed future preserves its sector projectors and complete readers
recover the same value.

### 3.5 Objective record

A record is operationally objective when at least two disjoint, independently
addressable fragments carry the same perfectly or tolerance-distinguishable
classical value and can be read without perturbing one another beyond the frozen
bound.

### 3.6 Actual outcome

An actual outcome is one configuration value in an explicitly declared ontology
and contingent trajectory. A diagonal density matrix, decohered branch family, or
broadcast mixture is not by itself an actual outcome.

### 3.7 Two division notions

A Barandes division is a boundary admitted as a conditioning time for the native
first-order endpoint law. It does not require a probability distribution over all
intermediate trajectories. A strong screening division is a property of a
separately supplied path realizer: conditioned on the complete boundary, its
future-history law is independent of the earlier history. Stable or objective
record status alone establishes neither notion, and the two may not be identified
without a separate theorem.

### 3.8 Laboratory autonomy

A laboratory controller is autonomous when one fixed Hamiltonian or stochastic
law runs the protocol without externally changed gates. It may still consume a
supplied background time parameter and initial program state.

### 3.9 Background-free chronology

Background-free chronology requires directed operational precedence from the
native law without identifying source syntax, program order, Hamiltonian time, or
a comparator causal path with an emergent result.

### 3.10 Native actual variable

A native actual variable is a measurable function of the actual configuration or,
if separately declared, the actual trajectory. It may be definite while hidden,
unstable, or noncentral in a particular Hilbert/operator-algebra representation.
Definiteness does not imply operational readability.

### 3.11 Native stable record

A native record is a measurable boundary function preserved by every supported
arrow in a separately frozen native stable-future category. Its existence, future
grammar, and observation map must be constructed inside one native law; an
operationally robust pointer does not supply them.

### 3.12 Representation firewall

Noncentral in an operational representation does not mean unreal in a native
ontology. Conversely, definite in a native ontology does not mean central,
classical, or readable in every operational representation. Every native beable
requires an explicit configuration function or, when separately claimed, an
actual-trajectory function, together with a tested dictionary to the operational
theory.
