The external comparator may remain as a passive validation port during an

## 13. What the known physics contributes

The required ingredients exist separately.

### 13.1 Relational constrained dynamics

Höhn, Smith, and Lock establish an equivalence among relational Dirac
observables, Page--Wootters conditional dynamics, and reduced relational
dynamics under stated conditions, using covariant POVMs for nonideal clocks.
They explicitly distinguish a gauge-flow parameter from a physical clock
reading and describe the theory as clock-neutral rather than simply timeless.
They also stress that a global clock--system interaction-free split is not
generic.

### 13.2 Interacting and finite clocks

Smith and Ahmadi show that clock--system interaction can replace the ordinary
Schrödinger equation by a time-nonlocal equation with a self-adjoint integral
kernel. Hausmann, Schmidhuber, and Castro-Ruiz show that finite clocks make
twirled-observable and dynamically purified-measurement constructions
operationally distinct: a measurement interaction included in the constraint
can produce time-nonlocality, nonunitarity, and imperfect event ordering. These
are warnings against equating conditional labels with physical triggers.

Their discrete-time restoration is a candidate model result, not evidence that
fundamental time or the v17 ontology is discrete.

### 13.3 Autonomous ticking records

Woods gives self-timing and finite-running-memory criteria and a fixed
autonomous ticking-clock construction. Silva, Nurgalieva, and Wilming show that
a nondisturbingly readable independent clock has a classical information
sector distinct from inaccessible clockwork degrees of freedom. These results
support the clockwork/record split and explicit resource accounting. They do
not remove external time from their own dynamical descriptions.

### 13.4 Barandes inheritance

Barandes supplies a serious reason to test whether Hilbert-space structure can
be secondary to an ordinary-probability indivisible stochastic process. His
published construction is nevertheless explicitly time indexed: standalone
probabilities and transition laws are functions of target and conditioning
times. It does not by itself provide a physical internal clock, a
background-independent constraint, or gravity.

A Barandes-compatible candidate therefore has to pass the same external-time
replacement gates. Indivisibility must not be confused with Markov autonomy,
record stability, or a license to condition at every intermediate tick.

## 21. Relation to the larger v17 ontology
