The review must decide whether the U-Gen question has a mathematically literal
and scientifically neutral subject before anyone attempts to complete or rule
out a Barandes-style generator.

Reviewers do **not** decide whether stochastic ontology is desirable. They test
whether:

1. the candidate classes are genuine sets with exact membership obligations;
2. the standard quantum control passes without special pleading;
3. target-process injection and physical program specification are separated;
4. Barandes's published endpoint scope is quoted accurately;
5. gauge freedom is not mistaken for missing physical phase data or vice versa;
6. Q-Cut is imported only if terminally accepted and only at its exact
   premises; and
7. every outcome preserves the reality-identification walls.

## 4. Blindness and provenance protocol

Each seat must:

1. receive the same frozen candidate corpus;
2. inspect no sibling report before its own report freezes;
3. write an independent core reconstruction before consulting author-side
   control/audit reasoning beyond the frozen pin itself;
4. cite exact source locations for every source-scope claim;
5. separate a mathematical counterexample from a philosophical objection;
6. label every finding `STRUCTURAL`, `SEMANTIC`, `SOURCE`, `SCOPE`, or
   `EDITORIAL`;
7. state the narrowest surviving result after every finding; and
8. output a self-hash and source-manifest hash.

The seats are adversarial quality assurance. They are not genuine external
human replication merely because they are mutually blind.
