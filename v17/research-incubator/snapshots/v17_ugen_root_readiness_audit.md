## 4. Independent reconstruction of the primitive separation

Let

$$
H=\frac1{\sqrt2}
\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\1&-1\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
G=|H|^{\odot2}
=\frac12
\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\1&1\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Then

$$
H^2=I_2,
\qquad
G^2=G.
$$

The coherent rule and Markov rule agree on the one-letter primitive:

$$
\Gamma^Q_b=G=\Gamma^M_b.
$$

They disagree on the two-letter word:

$$
\Gamma^Q_{bb}=|H^2|^{\odot2}=I_2,
\qquad
\Gamma^M_{bb}=G^2=G.
$$

A second fixed-unitary coherent rule is obtained from

$$
X=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
V=e^{-i\pi X/4}
=\frac1{\sqrt2}
\begin{pmatrix}1&-i\\-i&1\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
\Gamma^V_{b^m}=|V^m|^{\odot2},
$$

so that

$$
\Gamma^V_b=G,
\qquad
V^2=-iX,
\qquad
\Gamma^V_{bb}=X.
$$

For input configuration $0$, the two final distributions are

$$
(1,0)
\quad\text{and}\quad
(1/2,1/2),
$$

whose total-variation distance is

$$
\frac12\bigl(|1-1/2|+|0-1/2|\bigr)=\frac12.
$$

The $H/V$ pair has total-variation distance $1$ on the same input. For every
stochastic $K$, $\operatorname{rank}(KG)\le1$, whereas both $I_2$ and $X$ have
rank two. Both fixed-unitary coherent completions are therefore nondivisible at
the unmeasured $bb$ boundary against every stochastic continuation on the
printed carrier. A larger hidden intermediate carrier is an additional
ontological extension and remains outside this carrier-relative lemma.
Requiring nondivision therefore does not select the calibrated $H$ rule.

If a configuration measurement is physically inserted after the first $b$,
the coherent alternatives divide and the second one-step $b$ gives the same final
mixture as the Markov rule. Thus the control distinguishes unmeasured
indivisible composition from a real division event.

All three rules are finite and uniform:

- the coherent evaluator multiplies fixed primitive matrices before taking
  modulus squares;
- the Markov evaluator multiplies the fixed stochastic matrices; and
- the $V$ evaluator powers one other fixed unitary before taking modulus
  squares.

No depth-two table is fitted. The separation therefore survives the core
author-side audit.

More generally,

$$
U_\theta=\frac1{\sqrt2}
\begin{pmatrix}1&e^{i\theta}\\e^{i\theta}&-e^{2i\theta}\end{pmatrix}
$$

has the same isolated endpoint $G$ for every $\theta$, while

$$
|U_\theta^2|^{\odot2}
=\begin{pmatrix}
\cos^2\theta&\sin^2\theta\\
\sin^2\theta&\cos^2\theta
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

This classifies a continuum of coherent endpoint-compatible completions. It is
set-theoretic; finite-effective credit requires a computable or explicitly
specified parameter.

### Exact meaning

The theorem shows that **primitive configuration endpoint matrices** do not
select the whole-program composition law. It does not show that a fixed
Barandes model lacks $\Gamma_{bb}$: his law may supply that complete transition
directly for the physical interval. The missing object is a scalable rule that
generates the full family without listing a new complete law per program.

has been found.

memory, energy, time, or geometry.

known data. No parameter-free deviation follows from an accepted new law.

### R7 — no continuum or gravity interface

laboratory precedence. Nothing here addresses QFT scaling, dynamical geometry,
