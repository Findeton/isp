## 4. Primary-source scope audit

### 4.1 Local covariance and state direction

Brunetti--Fredenhagen--Verch formulate locally covariant QFT as a covariant
functor from globally hyperbolic spacetimes and admissible embeddings to
unital `*`-algebras and injective morphisms. State spaces are naturally
contravariant under pullback. The frozen T2 now has exactly that variance:

$$
\psi^*\nu=\nu\circ\mathcal A(\psi).
$$

It does not assume that a state on a smaller spacetime has a canonical
extension to a larger one. This matches the scope of the
[BFV framework](https://arxiv.org/abs/math-ph/0112041).

The time-slice axiom and relative Cauchy evolution describe response to
background changes inside the comparator. They neither quantize the metric
nor derive background independence. The pin refuses both promotions.

### 4.2 Localized measurements

Fewster--Verch construct a system--probe coupling in a compact region, its
scattering map, induced system observables, Davies--Lewis instruments, and
postselected states. Their central consistency result is causal
factorization: causally ordered couplings compose in physical order, while
causally disjoint couplings may be combined in either order. This directly
supports T3--T7 under the packet's printed hypotheses, not for arbitrary CP
maps. See
[Quantum fields and local measurements](https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.06512).

The construction must fix one convention for the scattering morphism. With
the pin's convention, the Heisenberg pre-instrument is

$$
\mathcal J_\sigma(B)(A)
=(\operatorname{id}\otimes\sigma)\Theta(A\otimes B).
$$

If a source uses the inverse convention, every formula must be translated
once at the definition rather than mixed later.

### 4.3 Local operations are more than commutation

Einstein causality gives commutation of spacelike observable algebras. It does
not by itself prove that an arbitrarily named CP map is localized or that its
nonselective action leaves a remote marginal unchanged. The pin correctly
requires either the system--probe construction or a separately justified
local operation class. Kitajima's analysis supports normal CP local
operations and only approximate Kraus descriptions under a funnel property:
[Local Operations and Completely Positive Maps in AQFT](https://arxiv.org/abs/1704.01229).

Thus T5 is feasible, but only as an operation theorem. Attacks 14--16 and 37--
38 correctly kill the shortcuts.

### 4.4 Split and type-III scope

The split property supplies strong subsystem independence for suitably
separated inclusions, typically under nuclearity or transported split
hypotheses. It does not identify touching local algebras with tensor factors
and does not turn a type-I interpolation into a spacetime lattice. The frozen
scope matches the
[flat/curved split review](https://arxiv.org/abs/1601.06936) and the
[locally covariant deformation result](https://arxiv.org/abs/1501.02682).

T11 is primarily a refusal theorem. A positive type-III assertion must name a
model and theorem establishing it; the construction may not infer type III
from the AQFT axioms alone. Conversely, the absence of a regional density
matrix does not prevent global algebraic states, effects, or normal CP maps.

### 4.5 No preferred state

Fewster--Verch prove, under dynamical locality and additional typical
hypotheses, a model-independent obstruction to a covariant preferred-state
choice on all spacetimes. The pin correctly treats this as a conditional
no-natural-state result, not a ban on packet-specific states or Hadamard state
classes. See
[Dynamical locality and covariance](https://arxiv.org/abs/1106.4785).

Hadamard/microlocal-spectrum conditions control ultraviolet singularity in
named free-field models; they do not select a unique vacuum or cosmology. The
Sahlmann--Verch theorem is dimension- and field-class-specific:
[microlocal spectrum and Hadamard form](https://arxiv.org/abs/math-ph/0008029).

### 4.6 Bell, Reeh--Schlieder, and sectors

Bell violation in QFT is compatible with commuting spacelike local
observables and operational no-signalling. It refutes Bell factorization
under the registered premise ledger; it does not refute Einstein causality or
provide a signal. The existential quantifier in T8 is therefore essential.
The load-bearing comparator source is the
[Summers--Werner result](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01207366).

Reeh--Schlieder cyclicity is a density statement, not a deterministic bounded-
cost remote-control protocol. The pin's success-probability, norm, and
postselection controls prevent that error. Gauge observables, field algebras,
charged sectors, and spin/statistics require extra hypotheses; the curved-
spacetime DHR extension itself prints such conditions:
[Guido--Longo--Roberts--Verch](https://arxiv.org/abs/math-ph/9906019).
