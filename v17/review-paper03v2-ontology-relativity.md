A frontier is the lower set of completed opportunities. It forgets the
linear enumeration of incomparable slots. Thus two topological sorts can be

times. Neither the order, rank, frontier cardinality, nor number of skips is

The construction does use an external causal partial order inherited from
the Lorentzian comparator. That is honest laboratory input. It neither
