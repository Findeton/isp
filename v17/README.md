# ISP v17 — reality-first relational quantum gravity

v17 is a scientific reset. Its purpose is not to extend the longest surviving
v16 toy construction. Its purpose is to find the smallest ontology and law that
can account for the empirical content of quantum physics and gravitation in one
coherent world.

The motivating possibility remains serious:

> Physical reality is one actual relational history governed by an indivisible
> stochastic law; Hilbert-space quantum mechanics and classical spacetime may
> be effective representations of that history in the regimes where their
> operational assumptions hold.

That sentence is a hypothesis, not a result. v17 is organized to make it fail
cleanly if nature does not support it.

## Reality constraints

v17 treats the following as constraints on candidate theories rather than
optional aesthetic goals:

1. reproduce the full tested operational content of quantum theory in the
   declared domain, including continuous phase dependence, composition,
   entanglement, Bell correlations, contextuality, sequential instruments,
   memory, decoherence, and reversible record erasure;
2. distinguish a physical state or boundary condition from a dynamical law;
3. distinguish one actual history from a probability law over possible
   histories;
4. avoid a preferred frame, global clock, lattice, dimension, topology, or
   geometry unless independently observed or derived;
5. make matter and geometry dynamical parts of one later theory rather than
   place a quantum mechanism on a declared spacetime or a metric on a declared
   event graph;
6. recover the established low-energy content of general relativity before
   using the word gravity;
7. expose empirical differences from standard quantum theory and GR, or state
   exact empirical equivalence at the tested scope;
8. count every declared input and prevent a fitted assumption from reappearing
   as a derived conclusion.

## Scientific order

The programme order is now:

1. quantum-process adequacy;
2. ontology and representation equivalence;
3. operational clocks and removal of external time;
4. a common matter–geometry configuration and law;
5. low-energy relativistic and gravitational adequacy;
6. only then, tests of whether spacetime is fundamental or emergent;
7. empirical discriminators and phenomenology.

Dimension selection, causal-set fitting, event counting, selector laws,
curvature extraction, and GR recovery are closed until the earlier gates pass.

## Permanent honesty wall

No finite construction, exact identity, successful simulation, categorical
equivalence, or elegant interpretation is evidence that its ontology is real
without an operational distinction or an independently motivated physical
principle. Conversely, a failed toy model is not a no-go theorem against all
relational or indivisible theories.

The goal is not to make ISP survive. The goal is to learn which description of
the universe survives reality.
