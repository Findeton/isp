## 3. Repair lineage

### 3.1 The v1 failure is absent

The candidate never asks a map on $\mathcal Q$ to distinguish equal arrows.
Its lineage is

$$
\text{presentations}
\longrightarrow
\mathcal P
\xrightarrow{q}
\mathcal Q.
$$

Ontological assignments receive arrows of $\mathcal P$. Noncontextuality is
their possible descent through $q$. Thus two mixture procedures can have one
$q$ image and unequal ontic measures without a functional contradiction.

### 3.2 Presentation is narrower than operation

The groupoid removes bound names, drawings, and harmless serialization. It
does not change calibration, incidence, retained records, randomizer
correlations, boundary type, or a landmarked port. A physical exchange wiring
is a symmetry arrow, not presentation gauge. This closes the most likely
route by which the repair could have erased context a second time.

### 3.3 Procedure access is boundary typed

Complete contexts connect only to declared open ports and records. They may
not inspect a sealed apparatus label that is absent from the boundary. If
such access is later calibrated, it creates a new procedure/reader and may
refine $q$. This prevents “all possible experiment” from becoming covert
access to every microscopic device detail.

## 6. Positive contextual model audit

### 6.1 State-space and preparation lineage

The model uses normalized predictive objects rather than procedure names. At
ordinary cuts they are density operators; at declared finite-memory cuts they
are normalized conditional predictive combs. Direct preparations use point
measures. Trusted randomization uses affine mixtures of the component
measures.

For

$$
\frac12(|0\rangle\langle0|+|1\rangle\langle1|)
=\frac12(|+\rangle\langle+|+|-\rangle\langle-|)=I/2,
$$

the two measures have disjoint support. The context dependence therefore
survives deletion of every diagnostic procedure label.

### 6.2 Transformation and instrument lineage

Deterministic channels push predictive objects. A trusted channel mixture is
a mixture of pushes. An instrument branch has mass
$\operatorname{tr}[\mathcal I_k(\lambda)]$ and, on positive support, targets
the normalized postevent object. Zero-support branches contribute the zero
measure and do not create a division by zero.

Sequential iteration telescopes the normalizers and returns the standard
unnormalized instrument composition. Adaptation is branchwise. Tensor sources
use the declared product contract, while an entangled preparation is one
composite predictive object. Finite process memory uses link-product
contraction of the conditional comb.

The construction is therefore a genuine positive probability lineage. Its
cost is equally genuine: the candidate ontic object is essentially the full
quantum predictive object, potentially global and memory-bearing. The paper
does not present it as local, reductive, or fundamental.

nonselection without claiming the binary variable is physical.
