4. a claimed fiber inflation changes an admitted operational probability,
   adaptive continuation, tensor product, or physical record;

   representation morphism;

   promoted to the claim that hidden structure cannot exist;

   unpriced tensor/source-independence assumption;
8. a contextuality or Bell contradiction drops a premise required for its
   bound;
9. positivity is falsely equated with noncontextuality or Bell locality;
10. a restricted resource minimum is promoted to the universal microscopic
    size of every adequate ontology;
11. an operational record is promoted to a complete microscopic trajectory or
    an actualization mechanism;
12. the Barandes ontology is called selected, reconstructed, or refuted
    without an operational discriminator;
13. a laboratory slot, tensor factor, or latent fiber is promoted to
    chronology, space, dimension, or geometry;
14. the candidate hides a selector, fitted table, implementation convention,
    or successor theory inside an invariant definition.

A kill may preserve lower coordinates. Failure of a Borel extension, for
