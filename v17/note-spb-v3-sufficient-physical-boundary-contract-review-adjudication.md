## 2. Root reconstruction of the controlling findings

### 2.1 M3-01 — numerical gaps do not make statistical alternatives nonempty

The contract freezes

$$
0<\epsilon_c^{\rm sci}<1,
\qquad
\epsilon_c^{\rm sci}-\Delta_c^->0,
\qquad
\epsilon_c^{\rm sci}+\Delta_c^+<1,
$$

with positive gaps and error targets. It then requires pass-side and fail-side
power “under the predeclared model class.” Those inequalities prove that the
two total-variation regions are nonempty in the ambient interval `[0,1]`.
They do not prove that either region intersects the declared model class.

Root reproduces Seat M's finite counterexample. Take one binary complete
outcome, one valid randomized comparison, and

```text
epsilon_sci = 0.40
Delta_minus = 0.10
Delta_plus = 0.10
alpha_fam = 0.05
beta_pass = beta_fail = 0.10.
```

Let the predeclared screening class be the nonempty singleton

$$
\mathcal M_0
=\{Q_h=Q_{h'}=\operatorname{Bernoulli}(1/2)\}.
$$

Every printed numerical inequality holds. The exact confidence interval
`[0,0]` has coverage and pass-side power one. But

$$
\{m\in\mathcal M_0:
d_{\rm TV}(Q_h,Q_{h'})\ge 0.50\}=\varnothing.
$$

Any universally stated fail-side power bound over that set is true vacuously.
No model-valid target can exercise `LOCAL-FAIL`; the equality restriction has
encoded the answer before data. The reverse singleton, with disjoint point
masses and total variation one, makes the pass-side alternative empty.

This counterexample does not use a post-hoc threshold, target data, duplicate
programmes, a zero margin, or a failed causal design. The matching anchor is a
different requirement and does not furnish a witness in each screening power
region. The frozen law never requires nonempty model-class intersections or
pre-data witness laws on both sides.

Therefore “paired with two-sided power duties” is syntactically true, but the
submitted claim that those duties are nonvacuous is false. `V3-C5` fails.

### 2.2 M3-02 — a set of words is not an exhaustive classifier

The feedback and primary seam sections give ordered predicates. The bracketing
section does not. It states only

```text
G in {GLOBAL-DEFINED, GLOBAL-UNDEFINED, GLOBAL-UNRESOLVED,
      GLOBAL-MODEL-INVALID}

L,R in {PIVOT-DEFINED, PIVOT-SINGULAR, PIVOT-UNRESOLVED,
        PIVOT-NOT-APPLICABLE}.
```

There is no frozen global coefficient problem, no definition of the left and
right pivot problems, no predicate for a label, no priority, and no rule for
aggregating a simultaneous admissible/confidence set.

Root reproduces the finite-resolution witness. Let one otherwise valid
admissible set contain global systems with common right-hand side `(1,0)` and

$$
H_0=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&1\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
H_1=\begin{pmatrix}0&0\\0&1\end{pmatrix}.
$$

The first system has a unique solution and the second has no solution. The
frozen text permits both `GLOBAL-UNRESOLVED` (the set spans exact cases) and
`GLOBAL-UNDEFINED` (an admitted member has no solution); neither choice
violates its only explicit membership rule. It likewise supplies no unique
left/right aggregation.

The listed mutant
`(GLOBAL-DEFINED,PIVOT-SINGULAR,PIVOT-SINGULAR)` demonstrates that a desired
word is available. It does not define the map from mathematical input to that
word. Multiplicity correction cannot repair an undefined estimand or
classifier.

Thus the bracketing classifier is neither shown exhaustive nor disjoint nor
finite-resolution safe. `V3-C13` fails independently of M3-01.

### 2.3 M3-03 — an empty realized confidence set vacuously proves every seam theorem

The seam statuses quantify over every member of a simultaneous admissible
model/confidence set, but the contract neither requires the realized set to be
nonempty nor assigns the empty set to invalid or unresolved.

A proper confidence procedure can return an empty set. For example, for
`Theta={theta_0,theta_1}`, return `{theta}` with probability `1-alpha` under
true `theta` and return the empty set with probability `alpha`. Coverage is
exactly `1-alpha` for each parameter.

On the empty realization, all universal predicates in the seam decision tree
are true: uniform existence and nonexistence, exterior uniqueness and
nonuniqueness, internal uniqueness and nonuniqueness, and satisfaction and
violation of the conditioning budget. Under the frozen priority,
`SEAM-NO-SOLUTION` is selected first even though no admissible model certifies
nonexistence.

The contract routes sets that span cases to `SEAM-UNRESOLVED`; the empty set
spans none. A missing nonemptiness premise or an empty-set decision is a
semantic omission. Consequently the global exhaustiveness/disjointness claims
`V3-C6` and `V3-C13` do not survive.

### 2.4 M3-04 — the composition classifier is not lifted into the duty vector

The contract defines six exact composition statuses:

```text
COMP-PROCEDURAL-INVALID
COMP-MODEL-INVALID
COMP-NONEXISTENT
COMP-NONUNIQUE
COMP-WELL-POSED
COMP-UNRESOLVED.
```

It also says only `COMP-WELL-POSED` can earn `C`. That determines one pass
case, but it does not map the remaining statuses—or failure of the seven direct
composition conditions—to `C=INVALID`, `C=FAIL`, or
`C=UNDERDETERMINED`. No corresponding total map for `X_C` is printed.

For the compatible but nonunique binary feedback control, the exact status is
`COMP-NONUNIQUE`. Two readings remain consistent with the frozen prose:

- certified nonuniqueness validly fails the unique-composition duty; or
- absence of a target-independent selector leaves composition underdetermined.

Those readings produce different duty vectors, candidate minima, necessity
statements, transfer flags, and universal exclusions. The status classifier
itself is improved over v2; the missing object is its total lift into the
contract's advertised four-valued outcome layer.

Accordingly `V3-C7` is not established, while `V3-C15` and `V3-C16` survive
only conditionally after a duty value is independently supplied. That is not
enough for the submitted complete inference law.

### 2.5 Why these are semantic rather than editorial

Each repair would add or change a mathematical object:

- requiring witnesses in both power alternatives changes the admissible model
  and design contract;
- defining global and pivot equations plus aggregation priorities creates a
  bracketing inference law;
- requiring nonempty confidence sets or adding an empty-set route changes the
  seam classifier; and
- assigning every `COMP-*` state to a four-valued duty changes the outcome map.

None is a notation correction entailed by the frozen bytes. Removing the
affected claims or narrowing the experiment after seeing the review would also
be a semantic scope change. `SPB-D3` is therefore unavailable.

## 5. What v3 genuinely accomplished

The terminal failure should not erase the progress relative to v2.

### 5.1 Causal effects are no longer inferred from source closure

The primary estimand is an intention-to-treat interventional law. Randomized
identification requires assignment-source exogeneity, positivity, consistency,
and carryover control. Transported identification requires a frozen causal
model, intervention map, theorem, overlap/extrapolation rule, adjustment set,
uncertainty law, and falsifiable validation. “Same source” alone does not
identify a `do`-law.

### 5.2 Composition starts from a physical initial law

The v3 law begins with normalized, target-independent
`Lambda(dlambda) Pi_0(dx_0,dy_0|u_0,lambda)`, retains preparation failures, and
defines every subsequent kernel on the same complete attempted-run domain.
The v2 omitted-initial-law counterexample is repaired.

### 5.3 B3 is a material claim, not a better formula

`T-B3` and `R-B3` name only additional physical carriers with preparations,
interventions, readers, backaction, failure states, and resources. An abstract
history kernel or higher response coordinate may be a useful control, but it
cannot earn material enlargement by notation.

### 5.4 Minimality is not necessity

The candidate relation requires an explicit physical embedding and
commensurate duties. Incomparable minimal passes form an antichain. A
registered necessity statement additionally requires all relevant competitors
without the augmentation to run validly and fail, with no unknown candidate.
No family-relative result becomes fundamental necessity.

These are durable lessons for any future experimental programme. They are not
authorization to continue the SPB contract lineage.

## 7. Scientific meaning

This adjudication does **not** show that a sufficient physical boundary does
not exist. It does not favor a Markovian or indivisible ontology, select a
quantum interpretation, identify microscopic actuality, or say anything new
about chronology, spacetime, gravity, or unification.

It shows something narrower and important: the programme does not yet possess
a complete, nonvacuous, finite-resolution inference law with which the proposed
boundary experiment could be interpreted without ambiguity. Apparatus
feasibility is not enough. A real experiment whose fail alternative is empty
by model choice, or whose seam and bracketing outputs are not total functions,
cannot adjudicate ontology honestly.

The correct scientific action is therefore to preserve the question and the
sound lessons, while refusing to manufacture a result from an incomplete
contract.
