## 3. Independent reconstruction A — stable-record factorization

Let \(h\) be a stable record prefix having positive probability under the
realized prior policy, and let \(c\) be an admitted next control. Prefix
autonomy makes both the prefix law and its next-record extension independent

\[
K(r\mid h,c)=\frac{P(hr\mid c)}{P(h)}
\]

is well defined when \(P(h)>0\). Positivity is inherited and

\[
\sum_r K(r\mid h,c)
=\frac{\sum_rP(hr\mid c)}{P(h)}=1.

Repeated multiplication gives the chain rule.

At a null prefix \(P(h)=0\), the joint law supplies no ratio. Arbitrary
normalized counterfactual values can be assigned there without changing any
positive-probability record. The frozen text explicitly prints this
nonuniqueness and does not use null-prefix values as predictions.

The prior physical policy is part of the conditioning context even though the
compact notation writes \(K(r\mid h,c)\). The theorem does not assert that the
same record string erases different past controls, nor that the transcript is
a material state. C3 is correct at finite, complete, prefix-autonomous scope.
