## 2. Ontological reconstruction of the comparison

### 2.1 Predictive nomology is not actuality

The contract gives the prediction map the type

$$
\Phi_e[\mathcal N;\sigma,b,c]\longmapsto\mathcal O_e,
$$

while explicitly separating law, contingent state, control, record, and actual
sample or referent (contract lines 159--185). That is the correct separation.
A record-only law may leave `A_e` empty; a law that asserts particles,
trajectories, flashes, branches, fields, or coevents must type the asserted
referent and its relation to records (contract lines 187--195). Normalization
governs a prediction object. It does not choose one actual outcome. Decoherence
licenses record probabilities on an event algebra. It does not manufacture a
microscopic referent.

Rival C explicitly charges both the predictive and actualization laws
(contract lines 228--238), whereas Rivals A and B need not inherit that
actuality semantics. The fixed-background R1 object agrees: predictive
carriers and records do not derive one-run occurrence from normalization,
decoherence, amplification, or standard quantum theory (R1 lines 443--457).

### 2.2 Records and microscopic referents remain different types

The common empirical carrier is the complete registered profile, not an
unrecorded fine-history distribution. For each registered control context, the
profile consists of joint probabilities for retained source, gravity-sensitive,
probe, and apparatus records, or a typed non-Kolmogorov precursor together with
every licensed record probability and a comparison map (benchmark lines
98--111; pin lines 170--189).

This imposes ordinary frequency semantics only where a candidate claims a
licensed record. It does not impose an ordinary-positive measure on all fine
histories. Contract lines 193--195 expressly forbid moving record probabilities
silently onto unrecorded alternatives. The complete apparatus record `R_C` is
part of the interface, so a hidden nuisance record may not be discarded merely
because it is inconvenient to the source story.

### 2.3 Interface histories are not a history ontology

The raw carrier and gauge/presentation groupoid are comparison machinery. The
contract says verbatim that the quotient notation does not declare histories
fundamental and can receive history, process, algebraic, or actualization
descriptions (contract lines 113--130). Rival laws need agree only on complete
registered operational predictions (contract lines 240--244). Therefore a
Hilbert, Gram, pair-history, decoherence-functional, doubled-history, or
positive-record representation earns at most a prediction representation at
the mapped scope.

If the quotient or invariant comparison map does not exist for two proposed
entrants, the correct result is failure of the common-interface gate, not a
forced identification of their microscopic objects (contract lines 121--130,
369--379; pin lines 287--290).

### 2.4 Objective unravellings versus mathematical unravellings

An unravelling is not objective merely because a trajectory can be sampled.
The frozen test requires an independently physical actual referent plus a
frozen occurrence law, source functional, and reciprocal coupling (contract
lines 614--619; pin lines 233--255). Gauge, basis, notation, relabelling,
parameter choice, decoder change, coarse/refinement presentation, and a shared
answer table do not make a second entrant.

For future admission, the word `actual` is not itself evidence. Review must
exhibit a non-presentation difference in the frozen law/referent/source role.
If no such difference can be certified before outputs are unblinded, the hard
gate fails. Conversely, two frozen objective laws can be physically different
while agreeing on the bounded registered profile; that agreement is
underdetermination at scope, not proof that the laws are identical.

### 2.5 Empirical equivalence is not physical identity

For clarity, define the scope-relative mapped profile

$$
\Pi_e(\mathcal N)=\operatorname{Compare}_e(\mathcal O_e^{\mathcal N})
$$

on the common public packet, record interface, tolerances, and comparison map.
Then

$$
\mathcal N_i\equiv_e\mathcal N_j
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\Pi_e(\mathcal N_i)=\Pi_e(\mathcal N_j)
$$

within the frozen tolerance. This is an empirical equivalence relation at one
scope. It is weaker than gauge or physical identity and stronger than merely
sharing a few marginals.

The frozen text gets both directions right. Presentation/gauge duplicates are
excluded before comparison, while exact profile agreement between genuinely
distinct laws is explicitly `nonselection` (contract lines 614--636;
benchmark lines 389--418; pin lines 248--255 and 351--369). No Bayesian,
explanatory, simplicity, or ontological tie-breaker is smuggled into agreement.

`G` means the gravity-sensitive operational role, carrier, or exact operational
surrogate. It is not silently a metric, potential, quantum mediator, tensor

This neutrality is bounded by operational accountability: every entrant must

## 6. Does any permitted result shrink an empirical-equivalence class?

The answer has three distinct levels.

1. **This review: no.** There is no entrant roster, prediction evaluation,
   apparatus, or data. A favorable design verdict changes no empirical class.
2. **A future prediction comparison: sometimes a strict refinement, not an
   exclusion.** If two laws equivalent on the R1 fixed-background interface

   at least two scope-relative profile classes. That is the exact content of a
   conditional empirical wedge. Gate failure, incompleteness, or constraint
   failure only narrows a contractual admissibility set; it is not an empirical
   distinction.
3. **A future experiment: possible scope-relative shrinkage only.** Separately
   authorized data, collected under the frozen mechanical baseline, public
   packet, nuisance model, tolerances, and inference rule, could disfavor or
   exclude one mapped profile class. This would shrink the empirically viable
   class at that experiment's scope. It would not show that a survivor's
   microscopic referent is actual, that empirically equivalent survivors are
   identical, or that gravity/spacetime has a universal classical or quantum
   ontology.

Exact agreement, nonidentifiability, null witnesses, and unresolved confounds
do not shrink the equivalence class. Every stronger ontology conclusion remains
barred.
