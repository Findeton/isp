## 5. Procedure category and operational quotient

### 5.1 Objects and arrows of $\mathcal P$

$\mathcal P$ is a small typed symmetric-monoidal category equipped with
explicit trusted physical-randomization constructors. It is **not** obtained
by imposing the convex identities that hold only after operational decoding:
doing so could identify distinct mixer implementations before contextuality
is tested.

- Objects are typed laboratory boundaries.
- Preparation arrows have no incoming system port and one or more typed
  outgoing ports.
- Transformation arrows include deterministic channels and outcome-indexed
  instruments.
- Measurement arrows terminate in typed classical records.
- Complete programs contain adaptive control, ancillas, discard, retained or
  forgotten randomizer records, and compatible process slots.
- Two physically distinct implementations remain distinct arrows even when
  operationally equivalent.

Literal equality in $\mathcal P$ is equality of the complete typed physical
procedure declaration, not equality of a source string. Harmless
serialization and naming changes are quotiented by a separate presentation
groupoid before a physical procedure arrow is formed.

### 5.2 Physical randomization

A forgotten-record randomization procedure is written

$$
p=\operatorname{Mix}_{r}\bigl((\alpha_i,p_i)_i\bigr),
$$

where the independently calibrated randomizer $r$ chooses procedure $p_i$
with probability $\alpha_i$, is statistically independent of the system
before the choice, and has no undeclared interaction with it. Distinct mixer
implementations may remain distinct arrows of $\mathcal P$. Affinity is the
required decoding and system-assignment law

$$
q(p)=\sum_i\alpha_i q(p_i),
\qquad
\mathsf A_p=\sum_i\alpha_i\mathsf A_{p_i},
$$

for the applicable preparation measure, transformation kernel, instrument,
or history law $\mathsf A$; it is not literal equality of the underlying
procedure arrows. If the randomizer result is retained, the output type
carries that record and $q(p)$ is the corresponding outcome-indexed
instrument rather than the forgotten convex sum.

The sample space is the mutually exclusive randomizer outcomes $i$. The
simultaneous physical consequences inside branch $i$ are not additional
alternatives. A device advertised as a randomizer but correlated with the
system, the preparation source, or a future setting is outside this trusted
constructor until those correlations are included in the typed procedure.

### 5.3 Complete operational equivalence
