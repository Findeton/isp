## §2. The Barandes base, and the one deviation

Barandes' indivisible-stochastic formulation of quantum theory rests
on three axioms ([B3] p. 29, paraphrased with his terms):

- **Kinematical:** a fixed configuration space.
- **Dynamical:** laws consisting of a **sparse** set of indivisible
  transition probabilities, conditioned **only at division events**
  — with division events *system-centric, not global properties of
  the whole universe* ([B3] p. 10).
- **Epistemic:** a contingent standalone probability distribution.

Two further Barandes results are load-bearing for v11:

- **The interpolant test** ([B3] eqs. 22–23): given Γ(t←t₀) and
  Γ(t′←t₀), the interpolated matrix Γ̄ ≡ Γ(t←t₀)Γ(t′←t₀)⁻¹ is
  generically **pseudo-stochastic** (negative entries); a stochastic
  matrix has a stochastic inverse only if it is a permutation.
  Divisibility failure is therefore a *computable, entrywise*
  property.  **[B]**
- **The gauge doctrine** ([B3] §3.2, §3.6): Γᵢⱼ = |Θᵢⱼ|² is an
  identity, not a postulate; multiplying Θ **entrywise** by arbitrary
  time-dependent phases e^{iθᵢⱼ(t)} is a genuine gauge invariance
  (Schur-Hadamard gauge — a new result of [B3]); wave functions and
  density matrices are gauge variables (Foldy-Wouthuysen gauge,
  [B3] §3.6).  Pointwise phases are not physical; only their
  gauge-invariant composition content is.  **[B]**

**The deviation [POSIT].**  Barandes' formulation is indexed by an
external time parameter and is explicitly non-relativistic in its
current form ([B3] §5 lists relativity and gravity as future work).
v11 does not import that background.  We adopt his *law-ontology*
and replace his time axis with the object v10 spent ten versions
learning to grow: **the generated causal order.**  The transition
family is indexed cut-to-cut — Γ(cut′ ← cut), cuts being antichains
of the record's own partial order — and "later" means later in the
order the record itself built.  This is not a stretch of [B3] but
the completion of its own move: if division events are
system-centric and need no global time ([B3] p. 10), the law between
them needs none either.  **What v11 presupposes is order-background,
not spacetime-background**: the grammar, the actor alphabet, and the
bare notion of causal order.  Metric, dimension, signature, and
global time remain outputs — and v10 proved the grammar can actually
produce them (§1, verdict 1).

> **The program in one sentence: Barandes' law, on ISP's spacetime —
> his indivisibility, our emergence; his division events, our
> renewals; his time parameter deleted and replaced by the generated
> order.**  The two programs need each other: [B1–B3] supply a
> quantum ontology with no relativity; v10 supplies emergent
> geometry with — as it conclusively showed — no quantum.

refuses to cut.
